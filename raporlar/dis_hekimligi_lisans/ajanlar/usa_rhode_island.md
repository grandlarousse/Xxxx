# Rhode Island (USA) — ajan bulgusu

Düzenleyici: Rhode Island Department of Health (RIDOH), Board of Examiners in Dentistry (Office of Health Professionals Regulation, 3 Capitol Hill, Providence RI 02908) — https://health.ri.gov/licenses

**Teori-only rota:** no · **Göçmenlik engeli:** medium · **Uzaktan sürdürme:** moderate · **Güven:** medium

## Sınav yolları

- **ADEX Dental Examination (administered by CDCA-WREB-CITA) incl. periodontal portion, passed within 5 years of application** (el becerisi: evet): Primary route. 216-RICR-40-05-2.5(A)(1)(c): applicant must 'Have successfully passed the ADEX exam, including the periodontal examination portion within five (5) years from the date of application for licensure in Rhode Island'. Results must be sent directly to the Board by CDCA. Also required: CODA graduation, JCNDE National Board (INBDE) pass. The ADA Oct-2026 summary lists accepted exams as 'American Board of Dental Examiners (ADEX, CDCA-WREB-CITA), CRDTS+SRTA'. The ADEX series includes manikin hand-skills components (and from 2026 the DLOSCE as an integrated component), so this is hands-on.
- **CRDTS or SRTA regional exam** (el becerisi: evet): Listed by the ADA (updated 16 Oct 2026) as accepted alongside ADEX ('CRDTS+SRTA'). Both are hands-on regional clinical exams. The rule text itself frames acceptance as an 'examination, approved by the Board, other than a regional board that is similar...' with 75% in 'each discipline, clinical skill, procedure or knowledge area that is tested on the ADEX Examination, including the periodontal examination portion' PLUS a pass of the CDCA 'Dental Simulated Clinical Exercise (DSCE) written' comprehensive exam at 75% (2.5(A)(1)(c)(1)-(2)). Because the equivalent must cover every clinical skill/procedure of ADEX, it is necessarily hands-on.
- **Licensure by endorsement / credentials (5 years licensed elsewhere)** (el becerisi: evet): 2.5(A)(1)(c)(3): 'Hold a current license in good standing to practice dentistry in another state for five (5) years immediately prior to submitting an application for licensure in RI. Said state shall have required successful completion of a clinical exam, not part of the applicant's training program, to be considered for RI licensure.' Not available to a new graduate; and the originating state's exam must have been a clinical exam outside the training programme, so a prior hands-on exam is still unavoidable.
- **DLOSCE-only / OSCE-only route** (el becerisi: hayır): NOT available. Rhode Island is not among the DLOSCE-accepting states in the 2026 DLOSCE Candidate Guide, and neither the statute (R.I. Gen. Laws 5-31.1-6) nor the rule (216-RICR-40-05-2.5) mentions the DLOSCE or any OSCE. The rule's non-regional alternative explicitly demands a score in each 'clinical skill, procedure' tested on ADEX, so a computer-based OSCE alone cannot satisfy it.
- **PGY-1 residency in lieu of clinical exam** (el becerisi: hayır): NOT available. No residency/PGY-1 alternative appears in 5-31.1-6 or 216-RICR-40-05-2.5; the only routes are the ADEX exam, a Board-approved equivalent covering all ADEX clinical skills plus the DSCE written, or 5-year endorsement.
- **Portfolio / state-specific clinical exam** (el becerisi: hayır): NOT available. No portfolio pathway and no state-administered clinical or jurisprudence examination is provided for in the rule or on the RIDOH application form (the form only asks for National Board scores and 'Regional or state Board examination results' sent directly to RIDOH).

## Teori-only açıklaması
216-RICR-40-05-2.5(A)(1) (quoted in the ADA Rhode Island summary updated 16 Oct 2026): 'The Board requires each applicant to: a. Have graduated from a school of dentistry in accordance with 2.4.6(A)(3)...; and b. Have successfully passed the national examination of the Joint Commission on National Dental Examination (Parts I and II); and c. Have successfully passed the ADEX exam, including the periodontal examination portion within five (5) years from the date of application for licensure in Rhode Island; or (1) Have successfully passed an examination, approved by the Board, other than a regional board that is similar to the examination for which the applicant is seeking waiver, with an earned score of seventy-five percent (75%) in each discipline, clinical skill, procedure or knowledge area that is tested on the ADEX Examination, including the periodontal examination portion using the internal weighting and scoring methods the CDCA uses to score the ADEX Examination in Dentistry; and (2) Have successfully passed a comprehensive examination in applied clinical diagnosis and treatment planning (CDCA Dental Simulated Clinical Exercise {DSCE} written) with an earned score of seventy-five percent (75%); or (3) Hold a current license in good standing to practice dentistry in another state for five (5) years immediately prior to submitting an application... Said state shall have required successful completion of a clinical exam, not part of the applicant's training program'. Every branch requires either the full ADEX (manikin hand-skill components) or an equivalent that tests 'each ... clinical skill, procedure' of ADEX, or five years of licensure in a state that itself required a clinical exam. No DLOSCE/OSCE-only, residency, or portfolio alternative exists. R.I. Gen. Laws 5-31.1-6(b)(3) simply requires applicants to 'Pass in a satisfactory manner any examinations that the board requires.'

## DLOSCE notları
Rhode Island does not accept the DLOSCE in any form as a substitute for the clinical exam: it is absent from the DLOSCE Candidate Guide's list of accepting states and from 216-RICR-40-05-2.5 and the RIDOH dentist application (which asks only for 'Regional or state Board examination results'). Earlier standalone DLOSCE scores are therefore irrelevant for RI. Since the 2026 ADA/ADEX integration, a candidate who sits the full ADEX series will have the DLOSCE reported as one component, but RI's rule requires a pass of 'the ADEX exam, including the periodontal examination portion' (i.e. the whole series incl. manikin hand-skill parts) within 5 years, so an ADEX-reported DLOSCE pass alone would not suffice. No RI Board policy on an ADEX-reported DLOSCE-only pass was found.

No ITIN option, no 'no SSN' affidavit and no 'leave blank if none' instruction was found on either form; whether RIDOH would accept an ITIN or a foreign applicant without SSN is undetermined (ask the Board).

- **barrier_level:** medium
- **details:** Statutory qualifications (R.I. Gen. Laws 5-31.1-6(b)) are only: CODA-accredited graduation, other Board requirements by regulation, and passing required exams; rule 2.4.6 adds good moral character, age 18+, exams, and good standing in other states (NPDB query). No citizenship, permanent-residence, work-authorisation or lawful-presence requirement appears in the statute, rule, ADA summary or application form, and no SAVE/visa/I-94 verification is mentioned. Identity: rule 2.4.7(A)(1) requires 'A copy of the applicant's state driver's license, a state-issued identification card or such other identification papers acceptable to the Director that include birthdate and a picture' (a passport should fit 'other identification papers' but is not expressly listed). The application's home and business address blocks include 'Country, If NOT U.S.' and 'Postal Code, If NOT U.S.' fields, so a foreign address is contemplated; no RI residency or in-state address is required. The one real barrier is the SSN field: it is tied to the RIGL 5-76 tax-clearance check (SSN transmitted to the Division of Taxation) rather than to immigration status, but no alternative is printed. Hence 'medium' (ambiguous/discretionary) rather than 'high'.

## Yüz yüze adımlar
Application is a paper/mail process: form plus check or money order 'in U.S. funds only' payable to RI General Treasurer ($965), copy of a photo ID with birthdate, official transcript/verification of graduation from the dean/registrar, National Board scores sent directly by JCNDE, ADEX/regional results sent directly by CDCA, and out-of-state licence verifications. Rule 2.4.7 requires the application to be filed 30 days before the scheduled Board meeting. No fingerprinting or state criminal background check (BCI) appears on the application or in the rule; the form only requires self-disclosure of convictions (item 16). No jurisprudence exam, interview or notarisation requirement was found (grep of the form for 'notary/affidavit' returned nothing; the SSN/tax attestation is a signed, un-notarised statement). The only unavoidable physical-presence step is the ADEX clinical examination itself (manikin hand-skills at a US test site, multi-day), which can be done during a visitor-visa trip. The application checklist also lists 'BLS' (Basic Life Support for Healthcare Providers 'that includes a hands-on skill component', rule 2.6(F)) - a hands-on CPR course that can be taken in Turkey from an AHA-affiliated training centre. Everything else can be completed from abroad by mail.

## Diğer başlangıç şartları
Good moral character; age 18+; graduation from a CODA-accredited dental school (Yeditepe post-1 Feb 2024 qualifies under 5-31.1-6(b)(1) 'accredited by the American Dental Association Commission on Dental Accreditation ... and approved by the board'); JCNDE National Board (INBDE) pass; ADEX pass incl. periodontal portion within 5 years; good standing in every state of licensure incl. NPDB query; copy of photo ID with birthdate; BLS for Healthcare Providers (hands-on, AHA-conforming) per rule 2.6(F) and application checklist; RIGL 5-76 tax-status attestation with SSN; self-disclosure of criminal convictions. No jurisprudence exam, no English test (not applicable), no liability-insurance proof, no background check/fingerprints found. Timeline: file 30 days before a Board meeting; licence runs only to the next biennial expiry (30 June of even-numbered years), so a licence issued in e.g. early 2028 would need renewing by 1 May 2028.

## İlk ücretler
Dentist application fee $965.00 (non-refundable; 'includes the first license only up until the next expiration date'; 216-RICR-10-05-2 fee table: 'Dentist: Application - 5-31.1-6(d) - $965.00'); optional framed certificate $30.00; Dentist reexamination $965.00; optional permits: nitrous oxide no fee, minimal/moderate sedation/general anesthesia $70 each. Plus ADEX series fee (~$2,995, external) and INBDE fee (external).

## Lisansı sürdürme

- **renewal_period:** Biennial: licences 'expire biennially on the 30th of June of the even numbered years'; renewal application with CE attestation due before 1 May of each even-numbered year; registration not renewed by 1 July 'automatically expire[s]' (R.I. Gen. Laws 5-31.1-21; rule 2.6.1).
- **renewal_fee:** $965.00 per biennium ('Dentist: Renewal - 5-31.1-21(a) - $965.00', 216-RICR-10-05-2); Emeritus-Active renewal (age 70+) $220.00; reinstatement of an expired registration = current renewal fee plus $90.00 ('Dentist: Reinstatement (added to base renewal fee) - $90.00').
- **ce_hours:** 40 hours of continuing dental education per 2-year cycle (rule 2.16; prorated 20 h/yr for a shorter first period), 'according to the criteria established by the Rhode Island Dental Association and approved by the Board'; licensee attests at renewal and keeps records 5 years, subject to random audit. Additionally 'All dentists practicing in a dental setting shall receive a minimum of one (1) hour per year of training on the CDC Infection Control Guidelines' (2.6(E)).
- **ce_online_ok:** Yes - rule 2.16.2(2) 'Self-instructed Courses' (home study, correspondence, Internet/web-based courses with a post-test, sponsored/approved by accredited institutions, Board-recognised professional associations, post-doctoral programmes, government health agencies or teaching hospitals) may supply up to 'Dentists: forty (40) hours' per biennium, i.e. 100% of the requirement. No live/in-person component is mandated.
- **cpr_required:** Rule 2.6(F): 'All dentists practicing in a dental setting shall hold a current certificate of completion from an approved course in Basic Life Support (BLS) for Healthcare Providers that includes a hands-on skill component and conforms to the current American Heart Association Guidelines'. The hands-on skills session is explicit. The wording is limited to dentists 'practicing in a dental setting', so a licensee abroad who is not practising arguably is not subject to it, but the renewal attestation may still ask for it (unverified); an AHA BLS course can be taken outside the US.
- **practice_hours_required:** None. No minimum practice, currency or clinical-activity hours appear in 5-31.1-7, 5-31.1-21 or rule 2.6/2.16.
- **address_or_residency_required:** No RI address or residency requirement. Licensee must notify the Board of address changes; application allows non-US home and business addresses ('Country, If NOT U.S.').
- **insurance_required:** None found - no professional liability insurance requirement in the statute, rule or application.
- **inactive_status:** R.I. Gen. Laws 5-31.1-21(c): 'Dentists ... not intending to practice in this state may request, on a biennial basis, to be placed on inactive status. Those requests must be made, in writing, to the dental administrator and must be accompanied by fees as set forth in 23-1-54. Persons on inactive status may be reinstated by paying the current, annual-registration fee and must meet any requirements established' (by the Board). Open to any licensee not intending to practise in RI; requested every two years; no maximum duration stated. The dentist inactive-status fee amount and whether CE is required while inactive were not captured (unclear). Separate 'Emeritus-Active' ($220 renewal, age 70+) and a fee-waived retired 'special license' for volunteer practice (5-31.1 special license; CE still required) also exist.
- **reactivation:** From inactive: pay the current registration fee and 'meet any requirements established' by the Board (CE attestation is the obvious one); no re-examination or refresher course is written into the statute or rule. From lapse: 5-31.1-21(b)/rule 2.6.1(C): registrations not renewed by 1 July 'automatically expire/revoked'; 'The board may, in its discretion and upon the payment ... of the current registration fee, plus an additional fee ($90)', reinstate. Reinstatement is discretionary; no explicit time limit, re-exam or practice-hour condition was found, but a long lapse could trigger additional Board requirements (unclear). Note the credentials route (2.5(A)(1)(c)(3)) is not a reactivation route.
- **remote_maintainability:** moderate
- **details:** Once licensed, RI can be kept ACTIVE from abroad: biennial $965 renewal, 40 CE hours all obtainable via web-based self-instruction, no practice hours, no in-state address, no insurance. Awkward points: the $965 biennial fee is high; the BLS requirement (hands-on skills component) applies to 'dentists practicing in a dental setting' and may be demanded at renewal; the 1 h/yr CDC infection-control training (same scope); renewal/online portal mechanics for a licensee without SSN unverified; inactive-status fee/CE unverified. Rated 'moderate' rather than 'easy' because of the hands-on BLS and the SSN/tax-clearance attestation repeated at each renewal.

## Açık sorular
1) Whether RIDOH will process an application whose 'U.S. Social Security Number' field is blank or carries an ITIN; the SSN is for the RIGL 5-76 tax-clearance check, not immigration, so an exemption or ITIN may be negotiable, but nothing published says so - confirm with the Board/Division of Taxation. 2) Whether a foreign passport is accepted as 'other identification papers acceptable to the Director' (rule 2.4.7(A)(1)). 3) Dentist inactive-status fee amount under 23-1-54/216-RICR-10-05-2 and whether CE is required while inactive (not captured). 4) Whether BLS (hands-on) and CDC infection-control training are demanded at renewal from a licensee who is not 'practicing in a dental setting'. 5) Whether reinstatement after many years of lapse/inactivity would trigger Board-imposed conditions beyond fees (none in the rule). 6) Whether online renewal is possible without an SSN. 7) Board position on an ADEX-reported DLOSCE pass with failed hand-skill components (almost certainly not accepted given the 'ADEX exam, including the periodontal examination portion' wording, but no written policy found). 8) The non-regional 'examination approved by the Board' alternative (2.5(A)(1)(c)(1)-(2)) appears to be a legacy equivalence clause; no such approved exam is currently listed.
