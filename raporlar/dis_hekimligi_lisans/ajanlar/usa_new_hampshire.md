# New Hampshire (USA) — ajan bulgusu

Düzenleyici: New Hampshire Board of Dental Examiners / Office of Professional Licensure and Certification (OPLC) (licences are issued by the OPLC executive director under RSA 310:8 on criteria set by the Board) — https://www.oplc.nh.gov/board-dental-examiners

**Teori-only rota:** no · **Göçmenlik engeli:** medium · **Uzaktan sürdürme:** moderate · **Güven:** medium

## Sınav yolları

- **Licensure by examination – ADEX (CDCA-WREB-CITA) dental examination** (el becerisi: evet): Den 302.04(a): applicant 'shall have taken and passed the American Board of Dental Examiners (ADEX) examination, or other similar U.S. regional or state board clinical examination for dentist, including a clinical periodontal/scaling component', completed within the 3 years prior to completion of the NH licensing process. Den 301.02(d) requires verification 'directly to OPLC from the CDCA or other equivalent U.S. regional or state board that the applicant has taken and passed the board clinical examination or manikin examination for dentists, including a clinical periodontal scaling component'. Plus INBDE (JCNDE) grade card, CODA DDS/DMD, OPLC Universal Application + Dentist Addendum, criminal history record check with fingerprints, 3 Statements of Professional Character, BLS-HCP if applicable, at-home open-book jurisprudence exam (75%).
- **Licensure by examination – other regional/state clinical exams (CRDTS, SRTA, etc.)** (el becerisi: evet): ADA summary (updated 10 Oct 2026) lists accepted exams as 'American Board of Dental Examiners (ADEX, CDCA-WREB-CITA), CRDTS+SRTA'. Rule wording: 'other similar U.S. regional or state board clinical examination for dentists, including a clinical periodontal/scaling component' (Den 302.04(a)); all are manikin/patient hands-on exams. Same 3-year recency window.
- **DLOSCE-only / OSCE-only** (el becerisi: hayır): NOT available. New Hampshire is not among the DLOSCE-accepting states in the 2026 DLOSCE Candidate Guide, and the NH statute/rules read (RSA 317-A:9, Den 301.02, Den 302.04, Den 303) contain no reference to the DLOSCE or any OSCE; the rules expressly require a 'clinical examination or manikin examination ... including a clinical periodontal scaling component'. A DLOSCE pass reported as part of ADEX would not substitute for the ADEX hand-skill components under this wording.
- **PGY-1 residency in lieu of clinical exam** (el becerisi: evet): Not provided for in RSA 317-A or Den 300 (no residency/PGY pathway appears in the ADA summary or the rules read). Not applicable.
- **Licensure by endorsement certification (credentials)** (el becerisi: evet): Den 302.04(a) refers to 'licensure by endorsement certification as provided in Den 301.02(h), pursuant to RSA 317-A:12 III' (rules on 'experience requirements for application for license by endorsement'), processed under OPLC rule Plc 313.22; in that case the 3-year recency limit on the clinical exam does not apply, but the applicant must still have passed an ADEX/regional clinical exam at some point and hold a licence/experience elsewhere. Not usable by a new graduate with no other licence.

## Teori-only açıklaması
RSA 317-A:9: 'Applicants for a license to practice dentistry or dental hygiene shall pass an examination as determined by the board or have successfully passed a national or regional test approved by the board. Examinations may be oral, clinical, written, or any combination at the discretion of the board.' The Board's implementing rules make a hands-on clinical/manikin exam mandatory: Den 302.04(a) 'an applicant for dental licensure by examination in the state of New Hampshire shall have taken and passed the American Board of Dental Examiners (ADEX) examination, or other similar U.S. regional or state board clinical examination for dentist, including a clinical periodontal/scaling component. The examination shall be completed within the 3 year period prior to the completion of the licensing process in New Hampshire'; Den 301.02(d) 'Applicants shall have successfully passed their regional boards and provide verification directly to OPLC from the CDCA or other equivalent U.S. regional or state board that the applicant has taken and passed the board clinical examination or manikin examination for dentists, including a clinical periodontal scaling component, within the 3 years immediately prior to submitting the application.' Den 301.02(c)(7) requires 'the applicant's original grade card denoting successful completion of the examination of the Joint Commission on National Dental Examinations and the American Board of Dental Examiners (ADEX) or other equivalent examination'. No DLOSCE/OSCE, residency or portfolio alternative exists. (Source: ADA 'New Hampshire Laws & Rules', updated 10 October 2026.)

## DLOSCE notları
New Hampshire does not accept the DLOSCE in any form. It is absent from the DLOSCE 2026 Candidate Guide list of accepting states, and nothing in RSA 317-A:9, Den 301.02, Den 302.04 or Den 303 mentions the DLOSCE or any OSCE; the rules require a clinical/manikin examination 'including a clinical periodontal scaling component' verified directly by CDCA (now CDCA-WREB-CITA) or an equivalent regional/state board. Consequently neither a standalone DLOSCE score (pre-Oct 2026) nor a DLOSCE pass reported as a component of the integrated ADEX series would, on its own, satisfy NH; the full ADEX hand-skill components must be passed. (The only unusual DLOSCE-adjacent provision is Den 403.07(a)(3), which gives 100% CE credit for passing 'the written portion of the ADEX' – a CE, not licensure, matter.)

## Yüz yüze adımlar
(1) Clinical exam: the ADEX/regional hands-on exam itself must be taken in the US (manikin/patient). (2) Criminal history record check – RSA 317-A:8-a: every applicant for initial licensure 'shall submit to the office ... a criminal history record information authorization form, as provided by the New Hampshire division of state police' and 'shall submit with the release form a complete set of fingerprints taken by a qualified law enforcement agency or an authorized employee of the department of safety'; OPLC forwards them to the NH State Police for a state and FBI check; applicant bears the cost. The wording ('a qualified law enforcement agency') suggests ink fingerprint cards taken by any law-enforcement agency, which may be possible abroad or during a US visit, but OPLC practice could not be confirmed; if two sets are rejected for insufficient pattern, police clearances from every city/town of residence are accepted instead. (3) Jurisprudence exam – Den 303.03(b): 'Taken at home electronically on a secure website provided to the applicant by the OPLC or, if requested by the applicant, using pen and paper; and ... Open book'; 75% pass – can be done from abroad. (4) No interview. (5) Notarisation: none mentioned in the rules read (Statements of Professional Character are signed by licensed dentists; certified copies of passport/transcripts required) – unclear whether the Universal Application must be notarised. (6) Application is 'in writing or online' (RSA 317-A:8 I). Overall, apart from the hands-on exam, the process appears completable from abroad or during a short visit, subject to confirming the fingerprinting mechanism with OPLC.

## Diğer başlangıç şartları
CODA-accredited DDS/DMD programme of at least 2 academic years (Den 301.02(g)); INBDE/JCNDE grade card (Den 301.02(c)(7)); OPLC 'Universal Application for Initial Licensure' plus 'Dentist Addendum' (Den 301.02(a)-(b)); at least 3 'Statement of Professional Character' forms (completed by at least 2 licensed dentists in good standing if the applicant has previous dental employment), signed within 4 months of application; signed certification of graduation (or certification of completion if degree to be conferred within 3 months); official transcript (unofficial transcript + sealed diploma accepted until official is ready); identity document (birth certificate, certified copy of valid passport, or driver's licence/state ID); proof of current BLS-HCP 'if applicable'; criminal offender record check (fingerprints, NH State Police/FBI, applicant pays); certified statements from every other state dental board where licensed; verification of ADEX/regional clinical exam sent directly by CDCA (now CDCA-WREB-CITA) or equivalent, passed within 3 years; fee per Plc 1002.11; after approval, at-home online open-book jurisprudence exam on RSA 317-A, Den 100-500, ADA Code of Ethics and ADHA Code of Ethics (75% pass); after passing, OPLC issues an active licence. PDMP registration within 90 days only if the licensee holds a DEA number. Age 18+, 'good professional character'. No English-language test, no liability insurance requirement found. Timelines not published in the rules read.

## Lisansı sürdürme

- **renewal_period:** Biennial – RSA 310:8 II: 'Licenses shall be valid for 2 years from the date of issuance'; renewal application, fee and supporting documents due on or before expiration (RSA 310:8 III); CE summary due before April 1 of the renewal year (Den 403.04(a)), extension to April 30 with late fee (Den 403.04(b)).
- **renewal_fee:** Set by OPLC rule (Plc 1002.11); amount not retrieved – unclear.
- **ce_hours:** Den 403.03(b): dentists with an active NH licence 'shall complete a minimum of 40 CEUs of continuing education per biennium. At least 30 CEUs shall be earned in clinical dental care subjects, including successful completion of a BLS-HCP course ... At least 2 CEUs shall be earned in infection control in a live or online course' covering CDC guidelines. If licensee holds a DEA number/PDMP registration: additionally 3 CEUs pain management/addiction (or an online exam) and 2 hours medical emergencies (Den 403.03(c)). First biennial renewal after initial licensure is exempt from CE (Den 403.05(b)). Excess CEUs do not carry forward. 3% random audit.
- **ce_online_ok:** Not fully. Den 403.07(g): 'up to 30% of the required CEUs by participating in audio-visual or online programs which require the successful completion of a written examination'; Den 403.07(f): up to 20% by correspondence courses with a written exam. Den 403.07(a) allows 'up to 100% of the required CEUs' from education/scientific courses 'sponsored or approved by' the ADA and its constituent/component societies, AGD, dental/medical schools, hospitals, government health agencies, etc., without specifying delivery format – so live webinars from those sponsors may arguably count toward 100%, but the rule text does not say so explicitly. Infection-control CE may be 'live or online'. BLS-HCP must include a hands-on component. Practically, a licensee abroad should plan for some live/in-person CE or ADA/dental-school-sponsored live-online courses and confirm with the Board.
- **cpr_required:** Yes. Den 403.03(b) requires successful completion of a BLS-HCP course within the biennium as part of the 30 clinical CEUs, and Den 403.07(b): 'All BLS-HCP courses shall include a hands-on component' (AHA, Red Cross, NSC, ASHI, ACEP, Military Training Network or approved sponsors) – 3 CEUs credited. Hands-on skills session therefore required every 2 years (AHA/Red Cross international training sites can satisfy this abroad, subject to Board acceptance).
- **practice_hours_required:** None found in RSA 317-A or Den 400 for renewal of an active licence.
- **address_or_residency_required:** No in-state address or residency requirement. RSA 317-A:13 II: licensees 'shall notify the office ... in writing within 30 days of any change of business or residential address'. Service of documents is by certified mail to the address on file (RSA 310:10).
- **insurance_required:** No mandatory professional liability insurance requirement found in the statute or rules read.
- **inactive_status:** An inactive licence category exists – RSA 317-A:8 II refers to 'any person seeking to convert from inactive to active status', and Den 403.02(e) defines licensee as 'any active or inactive licensed ... dentist'; CE requirements in Den 403.03 apply only to licensees with an 'active' licence. Fee, maximum duration, eligibility and renewal mechanics of inactive status (OPLC rules Plc 300/1000) could not be retrieved – unclear.
- **reactivation:** RSA 317-A:8 II: anyone 'seeking to convert from inactive to active status, shall provide the office with information relating to dental competence and professional conduct, to permit the office, based on criteria established by the board, to make a decision that the applicant possesses sufficient competence and character'. Specific criteria (CE catch-up, refresher, re-examination after long inactivity) not retrieved – unclear. Lapsed licences: RSA 310:8 III – 'Holders of lapsed licenses shall not be able to renew, but shall be eligible to reinstate licenses by submitting an application and meeting the eligibility requirements as defined in statute, and rules adopted by each individual board'; RSA 317-A:8-a requires a new fingerprint criminal history check for 'reinstatement'. Whether reinstatement after a long lapse requires a new clinical exam (Den 302.04's 3-year recency rule) is unclear.
- **remote_maintainability:** moderate
- **details:** Keeping an ACTIVE NH licence from abroad is feasible but awkward: 40 CEUs/biennium with online/audio-visual CE capped at 30% (plus 20% correspondence) unless courses are sponsored by ADA/AGD/dental schools etc. (format unspecified), a hands-on BLS-HCP course every 2 years, 2 CEUs infection control (online OK), biennial renewal fee (amount unverified), no practice hours, no in-state address, no insurance mandate, address-change notice within 30 days. Inactive status exists but its fee/limits/reactivation criteria are unverified; reinstatement of a lapsed licence requires a new application and fingerprint check and may be subject to the 3-year clinical-exam recency rule.
