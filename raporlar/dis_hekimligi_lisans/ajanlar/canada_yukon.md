# Yukon (Canada) — ajan bulgusu

Düzenleyici: Government of Yukon, Department of Community Services – Professional Licensing and Regulatory Affairs (PLRA) (government licensing office; no dental college). Governing law: Dental Profession Act (RSY 2002, c. 53) and Dental Profession Regulation O.I.C. 2018/89. Contact: dental.plra@yukon.ca, 867-667-5111 (toll-free 1-800-661-0408 ext. 5111). — https://yukon.ca/en/doing-business/professional-licensing/apply-dentist-licence

## Sınav yolları

- **General resident licence via NDEB certification (graduate of an accredited program)** (el becerisi: hayır): Per the official yukon.ca 'Apply for a dentist licence' page (as returned by search-engine extracts; the page itself blocks automated reading), for a general resident licence 'You must meet 1 of the following sets of requirements': (a) hold a valid general dentist licence in another Canadian jurisdiction and be in good standing there, OR (b) have graduated from an accredited dental program and obtained NDEB certification, with the NDEB certificate/qualification issued no more than 2 years before applying. NDEB certification for graduates of CDAC/CODA-accredited programs is now the single computer-based Virtual OSCE (no hands-on component), so this route is theory-only at the examination level. A general resident licence 'lets the holder use the title of dentist' and practise without the 3-month cap. Whether a Yukon jurisprudence exam, criminal record check, CPR or insurance proof is also required could not be verified.
- **General resident licence via labour mobility (licence in good standing in another Canadian province/territory)** (el becerisi: hayır): Alternative set of requirements on the same page: a valid general dentist licence in another Canadian jurisdiction, in good standing (the search extract also mentions an NDEB equivalency/certification completed no more than 2 years before applying as the alternative branch). Hands-on status depends only on the originating jurisdiction; all Canadian jurisdictions now license accredited-program graduates on the NDEB Virtual OSCE.
- **General non-resident licence (NDEB certificate issued within 2 years)** (el becerisi: hayır): Separate licence class for dentists not based in Yukon: requires an NDEB certificate of qualification issued within the previous 2 years; it 'lets you use the title of dentist; practise in the Yukon for 3 months or less in a licence year; and work to the full extent of Yukon regulations'. Annual fee listed as $200. Renewal requires holding a current general non-resident licence and having 'met all continuing competency requirements'. No hands-on exam. Note the 2-year recency rule on the NDEB certificate for first-time applicants.
- **Specialist resident / specialist non-resident licence** (el becerisi: hayır): Open to holders of a valid specialist licence elsewhere in Canada (not relevant to a new general-dentistry graduate).
- **US regional/ADEX/CRDTS hands-on exams, DLOSCE, PGY-1 residency** (el becerisi: evet): Not recognised pathways in Yukon. Yukon licensing is built on NDEB certification; US clinical exams, the DLOSCE and PGY-1 residencies are not mentioned anywhere in the Yukon dentist licensing materials found.

## Teori-only açıklaması
Official yukon.ca 'Apply for a dentist licence' page (as extracted by search engine; direct reads blocked): for a general resident licence 'You must meet 1 of the following sets of requirements' – (1) 'holding a valid general dentist licence in another Canadian jurisdiction while in good standing there', or (2) 'graduating from an accredited dental program and obtaining NDEB certification', with the NDEB qualification issued no more than 2 years before applying. For a general non-resident licence the page asks for 'an NDEB certificate of qualification issued within the same 2-year window' and 'limits practice to 3 months or less in a licence year'. Since March 2023 NDEB certification for graduates of accredited programs (including CODA-accredited Yeditepe, graduation after 1 Feb 2024, which CDAC/NDEB treat as an accredited program) consists solely of the computer-based Virtual OSCE at Prometric centres, with no manikin or patient component. No Yukon-specific clinical exam, regional exam or residency is required. Caveat: existence/format of a Yukon jurisprudence exam and other administrative requirements (criminal record check, CPR, insurance) could not be read directly from the page; none of the search extracts of the page mention a jurisprudence exam.

## DLOSCE notları
N/A for Yukon. Yukon does not reference the DLOSCE, ADEX or any US clinical examination; the only examination credential recognised is NDEB certification (for accredited-program graduates: the computer-based Virtual OSCE, which replaced the former Written Examination + OSCE in March 2023). The 2-year recency window on the NDEB certificate for first-time Yukon applicants is the key timing constraint; nothing analogous to ADEX-embedded DLOSCE scores applies.

PGY-1 kabul: False

## Yüz yüze adımlar
Application is made 'using the online portal' on yukon.ca. No fingerprinting, in-person jurisprudence exam, interview or notarisation requirement was found in any official extract; whether a criminal record check (and in what form) is required is unverified. NDEB Virtual OSCE is taken at Prometric test centres (available internationally, so can be done without entering Canada). On present evidence the whole Yukon process appears completable from abroad, but this is unconfirmed because the application page and portal could not be read directly.

## Diğer başlangıç şartları
Verified: accredited-program graduation + NDEB certification issued within the 2 years before applying (general resident route b) or NDEB certificate within 2 years (general non-resident). Annual licence; renewal cycle starts 1 February with renewal due by 1 March. Unverified/unclear: Yukon jurisprudence exam (none mentioned in official extracts), criminal record check, CPR/BLS, professional liability insurance, English-language proof (NDEB notes regulators 'can also require extra documents, a jurisprudence test, and proof of language proficiency'), good-character declarations, processing time. Timeline driver is the 2-year NDEB-certificate recency window.

## İlk ücretler
Initial application/registration fee for a general resident licence: not found. Annual licence fees shown on the renewal page: $200 (stated for the general non-resident licence) and a $50 fee whose licence class is not clear from the extract. NDEB Virtual OSCE fee is separate (paid to NDEB).

## Lisansı sürdürme

- **renewal_period:** Annual. 'All dentists in the Yukon must: register; hold a Yukon licence; and renew their licence every year.' Renewal period begins 1 February; apply by 1 March.
- **renewal_fee:** $200 per year for a general non-resident licence; a $50 fee is also listed on the renewal page (class not clear from extract); general resident renewal fee not confirmed.
- **ce_hours:** Continuing competency guide for dentists and dentist specialists (last updated January 2022): must demonstrate at least 60 hours of continuing professional education AND at least 300 professional practice hours, counting only hours completed in the three years immediately before the application; the renewal page frames these as annual continuing competency requirements under the Dental Profession Act and Regulations ('minimum continuing education and professional practice hours').
- **ce_online_ok:** Unclear – the guide's rules on online/self-study CE and any category caps could not be read (PDF blocked to automated readers).
- **cpr_required:** Unclear – no CPR/BLS requirement found in the extracts; not verified either way.
- **practice_hours_required:** YES – at least 300 professional practice hours in the 3 years immediately before the (renewal) application, per the dentist continuing competence guide. Whether practice outside Canada counts is not stated in the extracts.
- **address_or_residency_required:** No Yukon residence required: a 'general non-resident' licence class exists for dentists practising in Yukon 3 months or less per licence year; general resident licence holders need not live in Yukon as far as the extracts show. Mailing-address/contact requirements unverified.
- **insurance_required:** Unclear – not mentioned in the extracts obtained; many Canadian regulators require it, so confirm with PLRA.
- **inactive_status:** None found. The official pages list only general resident, general non-resident, specialist resident/non-resident (and possibly temporary) licence classes; no inactive, non-practising or retired register is mentioned.
- **reactivation:** Unclear. A lapsed licence would be handled under the Dental Profession Regulation (not readable). Note that first-time applicants need an NDEB certificate issued within the previous 2 years, which suggests a recency philosophy; after years without the 300 practice hours / 60 CE hours, renewal is not possible on the face of the continuing-competency rule.
- **remote_maintainability:** hard
- **details:** The licence is annual, cheap for the non-resident class ($200) and has no Yukon residence requirement, but continuing competency demands 300 professional practice hours plus 60 CE hours in each rolling 3-year window. A dentist who is not practising anywhere cannot satisfy the practice-hour rule, and there is no documented inactive/non-practising category. If practice hours earned abroad (e.g. in Turkey) are accepted, maintenance would become moderate; that point is unverified. CE format rules, CPR, insurance and lapse/reinstatement provisions could not be verified because yukon.ca/laws.yukon.ca block automated reading.
