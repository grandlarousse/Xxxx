# Kentucky (USA) — ajan bulgusu

Düzenleyici: Kentucky Board of Dentistry (KBD), 312 Whittington Parkway, Suite 101, Louisville, KY 40222; (502) 429-7280; kbd@ky.gov — https://dentistry.ky.gov/

**Teori-only rota:** partial · **Göçmenlik engeli:** medium · **Uzaktan sürdürme:** moderate · **Güven:** medium

## Sınav yolları

- **Licensure by Clinical Examination - ADEX Dental Examination (administered by CDCA-WREB-CITA / SRTA)** (el becerisi: evet): 201 KAR 8:533 Section 2(1)(b)1 (eff. 18 Aug 2026): 'The dental examination of the American Board of Dental Examiners (ADEX) or its predecessor agencies'. All components must be passed within the 5 years preceding the application. Since Aug 2026 the ADEX exam contains the DLOSCE as its cognitive component plus manikin-based hands-on components, so this is a hands-on route. Plus all Section 1 general requirements (CODA DDS/DMD, INBDE, jurisprudence exam, CPR, fingerprint background check, English, photo, fee).
- **Licensure by Clinical Examination - CRDTS** (el becerisi: evet): 201 KAR 8:533 Section 2(1)(b)2: 'The dental examination of the Central Regional Dental Testing Service (CRDTS) or its predecessor agencies'. Hands-on regional exam; within 5 years preceding application.
- **Licensure by Clinical Examination - DLOSCE (JCNDE) standalone** (el becerisi: hayır): 201 KAR 8:533 Section 2(1)(b)3: 'The Dental Licensure Objective Structured Clinical Examination (DLOSCE) of the JCNDE' is listed on its own as a complete clinical examination (no hands-on supplement required, unlike Alaska/Iowa). The Board's licensure page and instruction sheet IDLE0124 also list 'SRTA, DLOSCE, CRDTS, or CDCA-WREB-CITA (ADEX)'. HOWEVER the standalone DLOSCE ceased after 9 Oct 2026 (ADA/ADEX agreement); the Board has published no statement on this (Jan/Mar/May 2026 minutes silent; the Aug 2026 regulation was filed in June 2026 and still names the DLOSCE). In practice this route is open only to someone who already passed the standalone DLOSCE within the 5 years preceding filing.
- **Licensure by Credentials** (el becerisi: evet): 201 KAR 8:533 Section 3: requires a prior 'state, regional, or national clinical examination used to determine clinical competency in dentistry in another state or other United States licensing jurisdiction' AND being 'licensed and actively practicing dentistry in another state or other United States licensing jurisdiction for at least five (5) of the six (6) years preceding the filing of the application'. Not available to a new graduate; underlying exam would have been hands-on (or DLOSCE in another state).
- **Licensure by International Training (non-CODA graduates only)** (el becerisi: evet): 201 KAR 8:533 Section 4: for graduates of non-CODA programmes; requires TOEFL (iBT 116) if English not primary language, two years of CODA-accredited postgraduate training (AEGD/GPR or specialty), and a Section 2(1)(b) clinical exam. Not applicable to a CODA (Yeditepe) graduate and would require a residency/visa anyway.
- **Student Limited Licence** (el becerisi: hayır): 201 KAR 8:533 Section 5: no clinical exam or INBDE required, but requires a letter of acceptance into 'a postgraduate, residency, or fellowship program in Kentucky'; practice restricted to that programme; expires on exit. Not an unrestricted licence and requires an in-Kentucky programme (i.e., a visa).
- **Faculty Limited Licence** (el becerisi: hayır): 201 KAR 8:533 Section 6: requires a faculty appointment letter from a Kentucky dental school; practice restricted to that school's programmes; expires on leaving the post. Not an unrestricted licence; requires employment (work authorisation).

## Teori-only açıklaması
The rule text expressly allows a fully computer-based route: 201 KAR 8:533 (eff. 18 Aug 2026) Section 1(10) requires that the applicant 'Successfully complete the National Board Dental Examination (NBDE) Part I and Part II or the Integrated National Board Dental Examination (INBDE)'; Section 1(5) 'Complete and pass the board's jurisprudence exam' (open-book, untimed, 50 questions, 80% pass, taken online in the application portal or on paper); and Section 2(1)(b): 'Successfully complete all components of one (1) of the following clinical examinations within the five (5) years preceding the filing of the application: 1. The dental examination of the American Board of Dental Examiners (ADEX) or its predecessor agencies; 2. The dental examination of the Central Regional Dental Testing Service (CRDTS) or its predecessor agencies; or 3. The Dental Licensure Objective Structured Clinical Examination (DLOSCE) of the JCNDE.' The DLOSCE is listed alone, with no manikin/patient supplement. No residency is required or accepted in lieu of the exam, and there is no portfolio or state-specific clinical exam. The route is only 'partial' because the standalone DLOSCE is no longer administered after 9 Oct 2026; for a new candidate the DLOSCE now exists only inside the ADEX Dental Examination (hands-on). Someone who already holds a standalone DLOSCE pass can still use it within 5 years of the pass (note Section 2(2): if applying more than 2 years after graduation, the applicant must hold a licence in another US jurisdiction or complete a board-approved CE plan). The Board has not amended the rule or issued guidance in response to the ADA/ADEX integration.

## DLOSCE notları
Kentucky accepts the DLOSCE in FULL (not partial): 201 KAR 8:533 Section 2(1)(b)3 names 'The Dental Licensure Objective Structured Clinical Examination (DLOSCE) of the JCNDE' as a complete clinical examination option; the Board's Dental Licensure page lists 'CDCA-WREB-CITA, CRDTS, DLOSCE, or SRTA' and instruction sheet IDLE0124 lists 'SRTA, DLOSCE, CRDTS, or CDCA-WREB-CITA (ADEX)'. The Board 'will look up and confirm all national board and clinical exam scores online' (no score-report request needed). Earlier standalone scores: the rule only requires completion 'within the five (5) years preceding the filing of the application', so a standalone DLOSCE passed before the Oct 2026 cessation remains usable for 5 years. DLOSCE taken as part of ADEX: the rule does not address it; a pass of the full ADEX exam (which now includes the DLOSCE as its cognitive component) satisfies Section 2(1)(b)1 anyway, but a DLOSCE-component pass without the ADEX hands-on parts would not be 'all components' of the ADEX exam and the Board has given no indication it would treat it as a standalone DLOSCE. No Board statement, minutes entry (Jan/Mar/May 2026 minutes read) or rule amendment on the ADA/ADEX integration was found; the regulation revision filed June 2026 and effective 18 Aug 2026 still lists the DLOSCE unchanged. Note the ADA Licensure-by-State page (updated 8 Oct 2026) still quotes the superseded 2024 text (CITA/CRDTS/CDCA/SRTA/WREB/DLOSCE, notarised application); the LRC version is authoritative.

The 12 general requirements in 201 KAR 8:533 Section 1 are: English at ninth-grade level 'verified by testing as necessary'; a completed and signed application 'or online equivalent' with a photo taken within six months; the fee; no disqualifying discipline; the Board's jurisprudence exam; active CPR certification; 'a nationwide state and federal criminal background check by fingerprint through the Department of Kentucky State Police'; verification of other licences; a CODA DDS/DMD; NBDE I&II or INBDE; an explanation of any NPDB hits; and one of the three pathways. TOEFL is required only for non-CODA graduates (Section 4(1)(b)). No Kentucky-wide statute requiring licensing boards to verify immigration status/lawful presence was found (only unenacted bills: 2012 HB 5, 2016 HB 523, 2017 HB 3). Address: the form asks for a home address (any country) and 'Intended Business Address (if known)'; no in-state address required and the renewal form simply asks for home/business addresses. The one practical ambiguity is the SS# field on the application/reinstatement forms (see itin_or_no_ssn_alternative), hence 'medium' rather than 'none'. The Board's fees page even anticipates foreign payment ('Check or money order originating from a Canadian financial institution: Add $15').

## Yüz yüze adımlar
Nothing requires physical presence in Kentucky. (1) Fingerprinting: 201 KAR 8:533 §1(7) requires 'a nationwide state and federal criminal background check by fingerprint through the Department of Kentucky State Police', run via the IdentoGO Universal Enrollment Platform with Board service code 27GJYG. The Board page states: 'Kentucky residents must use the live scan process at one of IdentoGO's nationwide enrollment centers. Non-Kentucky residents can either use this same process or submit a hard card (FBI FD-258 applicant fingerprint card) to IdentoGO for processing.' The IdentoGO Kentucky non-resident instructions (Mar 2026): obtain prints on an FD-258 card, pre-enrol and pay online at uenroll.identogo.com, then mail the signed confirmation page plus card to 'IdentoGO Card Scan Operations – Kentucky, 100 Salem Court, Tallahassee, FL 32301'. So it can be done from abroad by mailing an ink card (quality rejections possible) or at any US IdentoGO centre during a visit (out-of-state livescan carries an extra fee). 'Do NOT submit fingerprint cards to the Board of Dentistry office.' The Board reviews the result online. (2) Jurisprudence exam: 'an untimed, open-book exam that covers the statutes and regulations governing dental practice in Kentucky'; 50 questions, 40/50 (80%) to pass; taken inside the online application portal, or the printable PDF can be completed 'on your own time' and sent 'via mail, fax, or email'. No proctoring, no in-person sitting. (3) Notarisation: the Aug 2026 rule now requires only a 'completed and signed Application for Dental Licensure or online equivalent' (the word 'notarized' was deleted), but the current paper form AFDL0525 and the reinstatement form ARDD0124 still carry a 'Notarized affidavit to be signed in the presence of a notary'; applying online through the Thentia portal avoids notarisation. (4) No interview, no oral exam, no in-state address. (5) Official transcript must be sent by the school directly to the Board; the Board itself looks up INBDE and clinical exam scores online. (6) CPR card copy (front/back). (7) INBDE and any ADEX/CRDTS/DLOSCE sittings are at testing centres (the ADEX hands-on portions are in the US). Section 15: all requirements must be completed within six months of the Board receiving the application; incomplete packets are held for up to six months.

## Diğer başlangıç şartları
Per 201 KAR 8:533 §1 and Board instruction sheet IDLE0124: English 'with a comprehension and performance level equal to at least the ninth grade of education, verified by testing as necessary' (no TOEFL for CODA graduates; TOEFL iBT 116 only under §4 for non-CODA graduates); signed application (online or paper) with head-and-shoulders photo taken within six months; fee; not subject to disqualifying discipline; jurisprudence exam (open-book, 80%); 'active certification in cardiopulmonary resuscitation (CPR) or a more comprehensive program which meets or exceeds the American Heart Association Guidelines for CPR and ECC' (Board accepts copy of 'CPR, BLS, or ACLS card'; official sources do not say whether an online-only course qualifies); fingerprint background check via IdentoGO (code 27GJYG); verification of any other dental licence sent directly by that board; official transcript sent directly by the school (CODA DDS/DMD, degree posted; diploma copy not acceptable); INBDE (or NBDE I & II) looked up online by the Board; written explanation of any NPDB report (Board runs the NPDB query; $25 NPDB fee if previously licensed elsewhere); one clinical exam within 5 years. If applying more than two years after graduation: must hold a licence in another US jurisdiction or complete a board-approved CE plan (§2(2)). Three failed clinical exam attempts triggers a board-approved remediation plan (§2(3)). Licence issued once all requirements are complete within six months of receipt (§15); application status is posted in the 'New Applicant Spreadsheet' on the Board homepage. No malpractice insurance requirement for licensure. Licences are issued for the biennium ending 31 Dec of odd-numbered years, so a licence issued in 2026 expires 31 Dec 2027 (renewal opens Oct 2027); CE in the first cycle is halved or waived depending on graduation year (§9(1)(a)-(c)).

## İlk ücretler
201 KAR 8:520 §1: initial dental licence $325 if applied for in the first year of the biennial period (even-numbered year, e.g. 2026) or $175 in the second year (odd-numbered, e.g. 2027); NPDB query $25 (instruction sheet applies it to applicants who previously held a licence; fees page lists it generally); IdentoGO fingerprint processing fee (paid to IdentoGO at pre-enrolment; amount not stated; extra fee for out-of-state livescan); licence verification $40; Canadian-bank cheque surcharge $15; all fees non-refundable. Exam fees (INBDE, ADEX/CRDTS/DLOSCE) are paid to the testing agencies. Timeline: incomplete applications held up to six months; licence issued on completion of all requirements (no waiting for a Board meeting is mentioned).

## Lisansı sürdürme

- **renewal_period:** Biennial. KRS 313.030(2): dentist licences 'shall expire on December 31' of 'odd-numbered years' (current cycle ends 31 Dec 2027; online renewal opens Oct 2027). KRS 313.030(5): renewal 'shall be completed online' (paper only on written request before 1 Nov of the expiry year); done through the Thentia licensee portal.
- **renewal_fee:** $295 (201 KAR 8:520 §1(3)). Late/expired: $575 if reinstated 1-15 Jan, $835 16-31 Jan, $1,415 on/after 1 Feb of the year after expiry (§1(4)-(6)). Active-duty US military exempt.
- **ce_hours:** 30 hours per two-year licence period (201 KAR 8:533 §9), including: at least 10 hours 'in a live, interactive presentation format'; at least 20 hours clinical (max 10 hours in business/practice-management, charitable volunteer dentistry, or association meetings); minimum 3 hours KASPER/pain management/addiction disorders (Aug 2026 text has no DEA qualifier; the Board's CE page/chart say it applies to dentists with prescribing/DEA authority); minimum 0.5 hour Pediatric Abusive Head Trauma or Child Abuse and Neglect; minimum 1 hour on recognition/prevention of pediatric ingestion or inhalation of controlled substances (new 2026, per HB 778). New licensees: half the hours if licensed in the second year of the cycle; graduates in the renewal year owe none. Certificates kept 5 years; attestation at renewal; audits. CE Broker tracking is optional.
- **ce_online_ok:** Yes, 100% can be done remotely. The 10 'live interactive' hours do not have to be in person: Board FAQ: 'any educational setting where you are participating in a live event that allows you to ask questions and/or interact with the other participants in real time. This includes in-person classes as well as online courses conducted through a live teleconferencing platform like Zoom or Google Meet.' The remaining 20 hours may be self-study/online. No Kentucky-specific or in-state course is required (KASPER-related CE is available free online per the Board).
- **cpr_required:** Yes. §11(1)(c): renewal requires the licensee to 'Maintain an active certification in CPR or a more comprehensive program that meets or exceeds the American Heart Association Guidelines for CPR and ECC'; CPR time does not count toward the 30 CE hours; the renewal form has an attestation 'I have maintained my CPR certification which meets or exceeds American Heart Association guidelines'. The official sources read do not state whether an online-only course is acceptable; AHA-guideline BLS/CPR cards normally require a hands-on skills session, so plan on a skills session (available worldwide via AHA international training centres).
- **practice_hours_required:** No minimum hours. But §11(2): 'A licensee who has not actively practiced dentistry in the two (2) years preceding the filing of the renewal application shall complete a continuing education plan approved by the board prior to resuming the active practice of dentistry.' The renewal form asks the licensee to attest 'I have actively practiced dentistry in the previous two years' and says those who cannot should contact the Board. The rule does not make renewal itself conditional on practice; it conditions resumption of practice on a board-approved CE plan. Practice abroad would presumably count as 'actively practiced dentistry' but the rule does not define it.
- **address_or_residency_required:** No Kentucky residency or in-state address requirement. Renewal form asks for home and business address (any). Licensee must keep contact information current via the portal; renewal notices go to the last known address/email.
- **insurance_required:** No. No mandatory professional liability insurance for licensees appears in KRS 313 or 201 KAR 8:533 (HB 776 §3(4) requires the Board itself to carry liability insurance). Charitable limited licensees are covered by state malpractice coverage under KRS 304.40-075.
- **inactive_status:** 'Retirement of License' (201 KAR 8:533 §12): submit the Retirement of License Form (ROLF0124) or the portal's 'Status Change Request'; no fee listed; no CE; no time limit; you choose the effective date; the Board sends written confirmation; you agree 'not to practice in Kentucky after the effective date'; cannot retire a licence with pending discipline. A licence neither renewed nor properly retired 'shall be considered expired for reinstatement purposes' (§12(4)). There is also a one-time 'limited practice' CE waiver (§10) only for dentists with 20+ years of Kentucky practice.
- **reactivation:** Reinstatement (§13) of an expired or properly retired licence: Application to Reinstate (ARDD0124, notarised on paper, or online) with photo; fee $325 for a properly retired licence (201 KAR 8:520 §1(7); instruction sheet says $325 even year/$175 odd year plus $25 NPDB; form says $350 total); active CPR certification; verification of any licence obtained elsewhere since first Kentucky licensure; a NEW fingerprint background check via IdentoGO; NPDB explanation. 'An applicant who has not actively practiced dentistry in the two (2) years preceding the filing of the reinstatement application shall complete a continuing education plan approved by the board prior to resuming the active practice of dentistry' (content set case-by-case: 'Applicants may submit any completed or proposed CE for the Board's consideration ... Otherwise, the Board office will contact the applicant to develop a plan'). NO new clinical examination and no refresher programme requirement is written into the rule regardless of years retired. An expired (not retired) licence reinstated within 2 years pays the $575/$835/$1,415 penalties; after 2 years it pays the retired-licence fee ($325). KRS 313.030(4) confirms lapsed licences may be reinstated on payment of the original-licence fee plus late fees.
- **remote_maintainability:** moderate
- **details:** An active Kentucky licence can be kept indefinitely from abroad: online biennial renewal ($295), 30 CE hours all obtainable online (10 live-interactive hours via Zoom-type platforms are expressly accepted), no practice-hour minimum, no in-state address, no insurance, and a free no-CE retirement status with reinstatement for $325 without re-examination. The awkward parts: (a) CPR certification meeting AHA guidelines must be kept continuously active (likely a hands-on skills session every two years); (b) the renewal attestation 'I have actively practiced dentistry in the previous two years' means a non-practising licensee must contact the Board and will need a board-approved CE plan before resuming practice (renewal itself is not barred by the rule); (c) reinstatement after retirement requires a fresh fingerprint background check and (on paper) a notarised form, plus a CE plan if out of practice two years; (d) the 2026 CE list adds Kentucky-specific topics (KASPER, pediatric ingestion, PAHT) that are available online. The renewal form also asks about convictions/discipline since last renewal.

## Açık sorular
1) SSN: the paper application and reinstatement forms have an 'SS#' field with no published 'no SSN'/ITIN alternative, and the Thentia online portal's mandatory fields could not be inspected; confirm with the Board (kbd@ky.gov / 502-429-7280) whether a foreign applicant without an SSN may leave it blank. 2) DLOSCE: the rule still names the standalone DLOSCE but standalone administrations ended 9 Oct 2026; the Board has issued no guidance on whether it will (a) keep accepting pre-cessation standalone scores (rule says yes, within 5 years), (b) add another non-hands-on option, or (c) treat the DLOSCE component of ADEX separately. 3) CPR: official sources do not say whether an online-only CPR course 'meets or exceeds AHA Guidelines'; assume a hands-on skills session is needed. 4) Fingerprints from abroad: IdentoGO card-scan instructions are written for 'applicants residing outside of Kentucky'; confirm IdentoGO/KSP accept FD-258 cards rolled by a foreign police agency, and expect possible quality rejections. 5) Notarisation: the Aug 2026 rule dropped the notarisation requirement, but the current paper forms still require a notary; the online application avoids this - confirm with the Board if applying on paper from abroad. 6) KASPER CE: the Aug 2026 text of §9(3)(c) has no 'if DEA registered' qualifier while the Board's CE page/chart still say it applies only to prescribing dentists; confirm which applies to a licensee with no DEA registration. 7) Renewal while not practising: §11(2) requires a board-approved CE plan 'prior to resuming the active practice', and the renewal attestation asks about practice in the previous two years; confirm the Board renews the licence of a non-practising (or abroad-practising) dentist without conditions. 8) Whether practice abroad counts as 'actively practiced dentistry' for §11(2)/§13(2). 9) No Kentucky statewide lawful-presence/SAVE statute for professional licences was found, but a direct check of the 2025-2026 session laws was not possible after the web-search budget ran out; the KRS 313 index (updated 2 Nov 2026) shows no such section. 10) Fees: the reinstatement form says $350 total while the instruction sheet says $325 (even year)/$175 (odd year) + $25 NPDB and 201 KAR 8:520 says $325 for a properly retired licence - minor inconsistency.
