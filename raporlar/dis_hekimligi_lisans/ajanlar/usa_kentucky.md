## Sınav yolları

- **Licensure by clinical examination - ADEX dental examination (CDCA-WREB-CITA)** (el becerisi: evet): 201 KAR 8:533 s.2(1)(b)1 (current text, eff. 18 Aug 2026): 'Successfully complete all components of one (1) of the following clinical examinations within the five (5) years preceding the filing of the application: 1. The dental examination of the American Board of Dental Examiners (ADEX) or its predecessor agencies'. The ADEX series includes manikin hand-skill components, so this is hands-on. Plus all Section 1 general requirements (CODA DDS/DMD, INBDE or NBDE I+II, jurisprudence exam, CPR, fingerprint background check, English, fee).
- **Licensure by clinical examination - CRDTS dental examination** (el becerisi: evet): 201 KAR 8:533 s.2(1)(b)2: 'The dental examination of the Central Regional Dental Testing Service (CRDTS) or its predecessor agencies'. Hands-on regional exam.
- **Licensure by clinical examination - DLOSCE alone (computer-based)** (el becerisi: hayır): 201 KAR 8:533 s.2(1)(b)3: 'The Dental Licensure Objective Structured Clinical Examination (DLOSCE) of the JCNDE.' Listed as a stand-alone alternative to ADEX/CRDTS with no hands-on supplement required. The Board's own checklist (Form IDLE 0124) likewise lists 'Successful completion of the SRTA, DLOSCE, CRDTS, or CDCA-WREB-CITA (ADEX) clinical exam within the last five years.' Written into the rules, but a new candidate can no longer register for a standalone DLOSCE after Oct 2026 (see dlosce_notes), so for this candidate it is 'partial'.
- **Licensure by credentials** (el becerisi: hayır): 201 KAR 8:533 s.3 / Form IDLE 0124: for dentists with 'qualifying experience within another U.S. licensing jurisdiction' (an existing US licence plus practice history). Not available to a new graduate without a US licence; no new hands-on exam is required by Kentucky itself, but the underlying first licence will have required one.
- **Licensure by foreign training** (el becerisi: evet): 201 KAR 8:533 s.4: for graduates of non-CODA schools; requires 'two (2) years of postgraduate training in a CODA accredited advanced dental education program' AND 'a clinical examination required by Section 2(1)(b)... within five (5) years'. Not applicable to a Yeditepe (CODA-accredited) graduate, and it does not waive the clinical exam.
- **PGY-1 / residency in lieu of clinical exam** (el becerisi: hayır): NOT available. 201 KAR 8:533 contains no residency-in-lieu-of-exam pathway; postgraduate training appears only in the foreign-training pathway (and even there the s.2(1)(b) clinical exam is still required). Residents may hold a Student Limited License (s.5), which is restricted to the Kentucky training programme.
- **Student limited / faculty limited / charitable limited licences** (el becerisi: hayır): 201 KAR 8:533 s.5-7: restricted licences tied to a Kentucky residency programme, faculty post or charitable clinic; no clinical exam but not an unrestricted licence and require being in Kentucky.

## Teori-only açıklaması
Current 201 KAR 8:533 (HISTORY line: '52 Ky.R. 1739, 14; eff. 8-18-2026'; FILED WITH LRC: June 9, 2026), Section 2(1): 'An individual desiring initial licensure in Kentucky as a general dentist by clinical examination shall: (a) Complete all requirements in Section 1 of this administrative regulation; and (b) Successfully complete all components of one (1) of the following clinical examinations within the five (5) years preceding the filing of the application: 1. The dental examination of the American Board of Dental Examiners (ADEX) or its predecessor agencies; 2. The dental examination of the Central Regional Dental Testing Service (CRDTS) or its predecessor agencies; or 3. The Dental Licensure Objective Structured Clinical Examination (DLOSCE) of the JCNDE.' Section 1(10) requires the NBDE I+II or the INBDE. So on paper a CODA graduate with INBDE + DLOSCE + the Board's (mailed, take-home) jurisprudence exam needs no manikin/patient exam and no residency = theory-only. RESOLUTION OF THE VERSION CONFLICT: the ADA 'Kentucky Laws & Rules' page (updated 8 Oct 2026) still quotes the superseded 18 Jun 2024 text with six exams (CITA, CRDTS, CDCA, SRTA, WREB, DLOSCE); the engrossed markup on apps.legislature.ky.gov shows CITA, CDCA, SRTA and WREB struck out and ADEX inserted, effective 18 Aug 2026. The current list is exactly three: ADEX, CRDTS, DLOSCE, and DLOSCE is listed alone with no hands-on supplement. Section 2(2): 'An individual applying more than two (2) years after graduating with a DDS or DMD, shall: (a) Hold a license to practice dentistry in good standing in another state or other United States licensing jurisdiction; or (b) Complete a continuing education plan approved by the board.' Section 2(3): three failures of a clinical exam require a board-approved remediation plan. WHY 'partial': the standalone DLOSCE is no longer offered to new candidates (only in conjunction with the ADEX exam after 1 Aug 2026; all standalone administrations ceased after 9 Oct 2026). A candidate who already holds a standalone DLOSCE pass dated within 5 years of applying still fits the rule as written ('yes' for them). A new candidate would have to register for the full ADEX series (which bundles the DLOSCE) and then rely on the Board accepting the JCNDE-reported DLOSCE pass as 'the DLOSCE of the JCNDE' under s.2(1)(b)3 while ignoring the manikin components; the rule's wording ('all components of one (1) of the following') arguably supports that reading because DLOSCE is its own listed examination, but no Board policy, FAQ or minutes confirming it was found. The Board's forms were last revised May 2025 / Jan 2024 and predate the ADEX-DLOSCE integration.

## DLOSCE notları
Rule text accepts the DLOSCE of the JCNDE alone, within 5 years of application (201 KAR 8:533 s.2(1)(b)3, eff. 18 Aug 2026); Board checklist IDLE 0124 lists DLOSCE as one of four acceptable clinical exams. Board minutes of Nov 2023 / Jan 2024 record the Board's decision to add DLOSCE to s.2 (effective 18 Jun 2024 version) and the 2026 amendment kept it while replacing CITA/CDCA/SRTA/WREB with 'ADEX'. Earlier standalone DLOSCE scores: nothing in the rule restricts how the DLOSCE was administered, only that it be passed within the 5 years preceding the application, so a standalone pass from 2022-Oct 2026 suffices. DLOSCE taken as part of ADEX: no Board statement found (searched Board minutes/website and the KAR amendment record; no 2026 minutes or policy located). The Board 'look[s] up National Board and clinical exam scores' itself (IDLE 0124), and JCNDE score reporting for DLOSCE is separate from ADEX's hand-skills reporting, so acceptance of a DLOSCE-only pass from the integrated series is plausible but unverified; candidate should obtain written confirmation from kbd@ky.gov before paying the $2,995 ADEX fee.

## Yüz yüze adımlar
(1) Fingerprint background check: 201 KAR 8:533 s.1(7) 'Submit to a nationwide state and federal criminal background check by fingerprint through the Department of Kentucky State Police'; Board instructions: 'Schedule a digital fingerprinting session at uenroll.identogo.com ... Be sure to use service code 27GJYG ... Do NOT submit fingerprint cards to the Board of Dentistry office.' Live-scan at an IdentoGO site is required; mailed cards are refused, so this must be done in the US (Kentucky IdentoGO sites; whether an out-of-state IdentoGO site can be used under the KY service code is not verified). Can be done during a short visit. (2) Jurisprudence exam: 'Completed Jurisprudence Examination' is mailed with the application and 'The Board will grade the jurisprudence exam' - it is a take-home/open-book paper exam, not an in-person sitting; completable from abroad. (3) Notarisation: Form AFDL0525 still contains a 'Notarized affidavit to be signed in the presence of a notary' ('I, the undersigned, hereby certify under penalty of law that I am the person referred to in this application, that the attached photograph is of myself...'), although the regulation effective 18 Aug 2026 now says only 'completed and signed Application for Dental Licensure or online equivalent' (the words 'signed, and notarized' were struck). Notarisation, if still demanded, can be done abroad (US consular notary or remote online notarisation) or during the visit. (4) No interview. (5) CPR card copy, official transcript sent directly by the school, photo taken within 6 months, INBDE and clinical-exam scores looked up by the Board. (6) The DLOSCE/ADEX itself must be sat in the US (Prometric / ADEX sites). Everything except fingerprinting and the exam can be completed from abroad.

## Diğer başlangıç şartları
Per 201 KAR 8:533 s.1: English at 9th-grade level 'verified by testing as necessary' (no named test); completed application with photo taken within 6 months; fee per 201 KAR 8:520; not subject to disqualifying discipline; 'Complete and pass the board's jurisprudence exam' (paper, mailed in, graded by Board); 'active certification in cardiopulmonary resuscitation (CPR) or a more comprehensive program which meets or exceeds the American Heart Association Guidelines for CPR and ECC' (copy of CPR/BLS/ACLS card); fingerprint background check via Kentucky State Police/IdentoGO; verification of any other dental licences; CODA DDS/DMD (official transcript direct from school); NBDE I+II or INBDE; written explanation of any NPDB hits ($25 NPDB fee only if previously licensed). Clinical exam must be within 5 years before filing; if applying more than 2 years after graduation, must hold a licence in good standing in another US jurisdiction or complete a board-approved CE plan (s.2(2)). No liability-insurance requirement for licensure. Timeline: Board grades jurisprudence exam, checks background and looks up scores after receiving the complete package; processing time not published on the pages read.

## İlk ücretler
201 KAR 8:520 s.1: initial licensure fee $325 if applied for in the first year of the biennial period (even-numbered year) or $175 in the second year (odd-numbered year); plus $25 NPDB fee only if previously licensed elsewhere; IdentoGO fingerprinting fee (amount not verified); exam costs separate (ADEX series $2,995 incl. DLOSCE; INBDE fee). Licences expire 31 Dec of odd-numbered years regardless of issue date.

## Lisansı sürdürme

- **renewal_period:** Biennial: 'All dental licenses issued by the board shall expire on December 31 of odd-numbered years and shall be renewed to remain active' (201 KAR 8:533 s.11(1)).
- **renewal_fee:** $295 (201 KAR 8:520 s.1(3)). Late/expired reinstatement: $575 (1-15 Jan), $835 (16-31 Jan), $1,415 (on/after 1 Feb of the year following expiry).
- **ce_hours:** 30 hours per 2-year licensure period (201 KAR 8:533 s.9), including: minimum 10 hours 'in a live, interactive presentation format'; maximum 10 hours of practice-management/charitable/society-meeting content; minimum 3 hours KASPER/pain management/addiction; 0.5 hour Pediatric Abusive Head Trauma or Child Abuse and Neglect; 1 hour pediatric ingestion/inhalation of controlled substances. Licensees attest at renewal and are subject to audit; keep certificates 5 years. A licensee first licensed in the second year of a period is exempt for that period (per ADA summary).
- **ce_online_ok:** Mostly. Up to 20 of the 30 hours can be self-study/online; at least 10 hours must be in a 'live, interactive presentation format' with 'evidence of the method of delivery'. Whether live interactive webinars satisfy this (rather than physical attendance) is not defined in the sections read - likely yes given the 'method of delivery' wording, but unverified. The KASPER/PAHT/pediatric-ingestion topics are available online.
- **cpr_required:** Yes at every renewal: 'Maintain an active certification in CPR or a more comprehensive program that meets or exceeds the American Heart Association Guidelines for CPR and ECC' (s.11(1)(c)). AHA-equivalent certification normally requires a hands-on skills session (available at AHA international training centres abroad).
- **practice_hours_required:** None for renewal. But s.11(2): 'A licensee who has not actively practiced dentistry in the two (2) years preceding the filing of the renewal application shall complete a continuing education plan approved by the board prior to resuming the active practice of dentistry' - the licence still renews; the CE plan is a condition for resuming practice, not for renewal.
- **address_or_residency_required:** No Kentucky address or residency required; form asks for home/mailing address of any location.
- **insurance_required:** No mandatory professional liability insurance found in 201 KAR 8:533/8:520.
- **inactive_status:** 'Retirement of a Dental License' (s.12): submit a Retirement of License Form 'or online equivalent'; Board confirms in writing; cannot retire while discipline is pending. No fee, no CE and no time limit stated for retired status. Reinstatement fee for a properly retired licence is $325 (201 KAR 8:520 s.1(7)). A licence 'not properly retired or renewed shall be considered expired for reinstatement purposes'.
- **reactivation:** s.13: reinstatement of an expired or properly retired licence requires application with photo, fee ($325 if retired; $575-$1,415 if expired, and the retired-licence fee if expired more than 2 years), active CPR certification, verification of any other licences held since, fingerprint background check via Kentucky State Police (in person in the US), NPDB explanation. No clinical re-examination and no refresher course is required; but 'An applicant who has not actively practiced dentistry in the two (2) years preceding the filing of the reinstatement application shall complete a continuing education plan approved by the board prior to resuming the active practice of dentistry.'
- **remote_maintainability:** moderate
- **details:** An active Kentucky licence can be renewed every two years from abroad with the $295 fee, 30 CE hours (at least 10 live-interactive, probably satisfiable by live webinars but not confirmed) and a current AHA-standard CPR card (hands-on skills session). No practice hours, no in-state address, no insurance, no notarised renewal form found. Retired status is free and open-ended, with a $325 reinstatement plus fingerprints and CPR; return to actual practice after 2+ years without practising triggers a board-approved CE plan but no new clinical exam. Renewal may be done online (regulation refers to 'online equivalent').
