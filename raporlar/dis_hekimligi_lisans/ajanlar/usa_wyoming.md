# Wyoming (USA) — ajan bulgusu

Düzenleyici: Wyoming Board of Dental Examiners — https://dental.wyo.gov/licensing/dentists

**Teori-only rota:** no · **Göçmenlik engeli:** high · **Uzaktan sürdürme:** hard · **Güven:** medium

## Sınav yolları

- **Licensure by Examination (new graduate): INBDE + regional hands-on clinical exam (ADEX/CDCA-WREB-CITA, CRDTS, SRTA) + Wyoming jurisprudence exam** (el becerisi: evet): Wyoming Admin. Rules 034 Ch. 3 Sec. 6(b): applicant shall (i) submit completed application and fee; (ii) current BLS certification; (iii) official transcripts from a CODA accredited dental program; (iv) INBDE score report; (v) score reports documenting successful passage of a regional clinical examination that indicates competency in (A) Endodontics (access opening of a posterior tooth; access, canal instrumentation and obturation of an anterior tooth); (B) Fixed Prosthodontics (full crown and a separate bridge with two abutment preparations); (C) Periodontics, scaling on a patient or manikin in a clinical setting; (D) Restorative Dentistry, class II amalgam/composite and class III composite on a patient or manikin; slot preps not accepted. The ADA state page (updated 20 Oct 2026) lists accepted exams as ADEX (CDCA-WREB-CITA), CRDTS and SRTA. W.S. 33-15-108(a) also requires 'a written and practical examination' including 'an examination on the Wyoming Dental Practice Act and the rules' (jurisprudence). If out of clinical practice >1 year: graduation within preceding 12 months OR regional exam within 5 years plus 8 hours hands-on clinical CE per year out of practice (Sec. 6(b)(vi)).
- **DLOSCE-only / OSCE-only** (el becerisi: hayır): NOT available. Neither the statute, Rule Ch. 3 Sec. 6, nor the ADA summary mention the DLOSCE; the rule requires a regional clinical examination with manikin/patient endodontic, fixed prosthodontic, periodontal and restorative components. Wyoming is not on the JCNDE list of DLOSCE-accepting states.
- **PGY-1 / residency in lieu of clinical exam** (el becerisi: evet): NOT available for initial licensure. Residency (GPR/AEGD/specialty) appears only in Rule Ch. 3 Sec. 7 (licensure by endorsement) as one way an already-licensed dentist can demonstrate competency (alternative to 5,000 practice hours in the last 5 years); endorsement requires an active licence in another jurisdiction with equivalent requirements, so a new graduate cannot use it.
- **Licensure by Endorsement / Credentials** (el becerisi: evet): Rule Ch. 3 Sec. 7: for applicants actively licensed in another jurisdiction whose requirements are equivalent to Wyoming (which itself requires a hands-on regional exam); requires application/fee, BLS, and competency evidence (e.g., 1,000 hours active clinical practice in last 12 months, 5,000 hours in last 5 years, completion of GPR/AEGD/specialty residency, or 8 hours hands-on clinical CE per year out of practice). Not a route to initial licensure for a new graduate without another licence.
- **Volunteer License (retired dentists)** (el becerisi: evet): Board offers a 'Volunteer License' for retired dentists providing services to low-income, uninsured persons at nonprofit facilities; presupposes a prior licence; not an initial-licensure pathway.

## Teori-only açıklaması
W.S. 33-15-108(a): 'The applicant shall pass a written and practical examination in a manner satisfactory to the board. The written examination shall consist of part I and part II of the national board of dental examinations ... and an examination on the Wyoming Dental Practice Act and the rules and regulations of the board of dental examiners. The clinical practical examination shall be based on satisfactory completion of a clinical examination acceptable to the board.' Wyoming Administrative Rules 034, Chapter 3, Section 6(b)(v) (Dental Licensure by Examination): applicant shall 'Request score reports documenting successful passage of a regional clinical examination that indicates competency in: (A) Endodontics, including access opening of a posterior tooth and access, canal instrumentation and obturation of an anterior tooth; (B) Fixed Prosthodontics, a full crown procedure and a separate bridge with two (2) abutment preparations. (C) Periodontics, including scaling on a patient or non-patient (manikin) in a clinical setting; and (D) Restorative Dentistry, including a class II amalgam or composite preparation and restoration and a class III composite preparation and restoration on a patient or non-patient (manikin) in a clinical setting. Slot preps shall not be accepted.' Accepted exams per ADA page: ADEX (CDCA-WREB-CITA), CRDTS, SRTA. No DLOSCE, OSCE-only, residency, or portfolio alternative exists for initial licensure. A hands-on manikin/patient exam is unavoidable.

## DLOSCE notları
Wyoming rules and the ADA state summary (updated 20 Oct 2026) do not mention the DLOSCE at all; the required 'regional clinical examination' must demonstrate hands-on endodontic, fixed prosthodontic, periodontal and restorative competencies, so a standalone DLOSCE score (past or present) does not satisfy Sec. 6(b)(v). An ADEX-integrated DLOSCE pass would be irrelevant on its own; the ADEX series satisfies Wyoming only because of its manikin components. No published board policy on ADEX-reported DLOSCE components was found.

PGY-1 kabul: False

Although 8 U.S.C. §1621(a)(2) technically treats any 'nonimmigrant' as an eligible alien, the board form does not say so. ## Yüz yüze adımlar
(1) Regional clinical exam (ADEX/CRDTS/SRTA) must be taken in person at a US testing site with manikin/patient components. (2) Wyoming Jurisprudence Examination: per the board packet, 'When your application is received and you are eligible to complete the Jurisprudence Examination, Board staff will provide instructions for scheduling your in-person, closed-book, proctored examination' - physical presence (Cheyenne board office) required; cannot be done from abroad. (3) Paper application: forms 'are not editable or fillable. You must open and print'; 'Mail this form back to the address above' with a $300 check or money order payable to the State of Wyoming (no cash); can be mailed from abroad. (4) Lawful presence form with document copies submitted with the application (one-time). (5) No fingerprinting, Live Scan or notarisation requirement was found in the Dentist by Examination packet (W.S. 33-15-108(a) only requires a background not evidencing adverse conduct). Processing: approximately 3-4 weeks after all materials are received; board communicates only with the applicant by email. Overall a short visit could cover the jurisprudence exam, but the hands-on regional exam is the main in-person component.

## Diğer başlangıç şartları
Jurisprudence exam: in-person, closed-book, proctored, scheduled by board staff after the application is received (W.S. 33-15-108(a) requires an exam on the Wyoming Dental Practice Act and rules). BLS: 'Submit current certification in BLS' (Rule Ch. 3 Sec. 6(b)(ii)). Transcripts: official transcripts from a CODA-accredited program; NOTE W.S. 33-15-108(a) literally says a degree 'from a college or university in the United States or Canada accredited by the commission on dental accreditation' whereas the rule says 'a CODA accredited dental program' - whether the board accepts Yeditepe (CODA-accredited but in Turkey) under the statutory wording is unverified. INBDE score report. Regional clinical exam score report. Lawful presence form. No English test, no insurance requirement found. Fee $300 by check/money order. Processing about 3-4 weeks once complete. Applicants out of clinical practice >1 year need extra competency evidence (Sec. 6(b)(vi)). W.S. 33-15-108(e): after three failures, additional education required before re-examination.

## İlk ücretler
Dentist by Examination application fee: $300 (check or money order payable to the State of Wyoming; non-refundable per W.S. 33-15-108(b)). Regional clinical exam fees are separate (ADEX full series about $2,995 per known facts). Jurisprudence exam fee not stated (appears included). Relicensure after lapse: $370 (board renewals page, 2026). Renewal fee amount not captured.

## Lisansı sürdürme

- **renewal_period:** Biennial: Rule Ch. 3 Sec. 10(a)(i) 'All dental and dental hygiene licenses shall expire December 31 of every second year'; late renewal permitted 1 Jan - 31 Mar following the renewal period (Sec. 12); on 1 April any unrenewed licence lapses (Sec. 12(a)(iv)). (W.S. 33-15-109 still reads 'on or before December 31 each year' but 2021 SF0109 and the rule moved renewal to every other year.)
- **renewal_fee:** Not captured from official pages (unclear). Relicensure after lapse: $370 (dental.wyo.gov/licensing/renewals, 2026 cycle).
- **ce_hours:** 16 hours per biennium (CE period 1 Jan of even years - 31 Dec of odd years), of which 3 hours must relate to responsible prescribing of controlled substances (Rule Ch. 3 Sec. 11; W.S. 33-15-109(c)). New dentist licensed in an even year: 8 hours (3 on controlled-substance prescribing) for the first period. Excess hours cannot be carried forward. Keep records 4 years for audit.
- **ce_online_ok:** Rule Sec. 11 sets subject-matter rules only ('CE may be acquired in any jurisdiction'; practice-building/business courses unacceptable) and no online/self-study cap or live-attendance requirement was found, so 100% online appears permissible (unverified in full rule text). Hands-on clinical CE (8 hrs per year out of practice) is required only for re-entry/competency, not routine renewal.
- **cpr_required:** Yes - renewal requires 'Verify current certification in BLS' (Rule Ch. 3 Sec. 10(b)(ii)). The rule does not state whether online-only BLS is accepted; standard BLS providers require a hands-on skills session (unclear).
- **practice_hours_required:** Yes, a currency requirement: renewal applicants must '(iv) Verify active clinical practice within the preceding five (5) years' (Sec. 10(b)(iv)); '(c) Competency Requirement. After five (5) years of inactive clinical practice, an applicant shall demonstrate competency by successful completion of regional clinical examination that meets the requirements in Section 6(b)(v)' (hands-on). W.S. 33-15-109(b): a dentist who has not actively practiced or taught dentistry for the preceding five years is issued a renewal only after demonstrating maintained qualifications; board may require re-examination. No minimum hour count for renewal; the rule does not say the practice must be in Wyoming/US, so clinical practice abroad may count (unverified).
- **address_or_residency_required:** No in-state address or residency requirement found; board requires notification of address changes (Address/Name Change form).
- **insurance_required:** None found in statute, rules, or board pages.
- **inactive_status:** None. No inactive, retired or non-practising licence category exists in Rule Ch. 3 or on the board site; the only related category is a 'Volunteer License' for retired dentists serving low-income, uninsured persons at nonprofit facilities (not a maintenance status). Rule Sec. 15 'Reinstatement' applies only to revoked/surrendered/suspended/conditioned licences.
- **reactivation:** If renewal is missed by 31 March the licence lapses and 'the dentist shall apply for relicensure' (Sec. 13(a)); relicensure fee $370 and, by analogy with Sec. 6(b)(vi)/Sec. 10(c), competency evidence; after 5 years without clinical practice a full regional hands-on clinical exam is required again (Sec. 10(c)); applicants out of practice >1 year need a regional exam within 5 years plus 8 hours hands-on clinical CE per year out of practice from a CODA program or ARC-pre-approved (Sec. 6(b)(vi)).
- **remote_maintainability:** hard
- **details:** Can be renewed by mail from abroad with online CE and fees, but: (1) no inactive status; (2) every renewal requires verification of active clinical practice within the past 5 years, and after 5 years out of clinical practice a hands-on regional clinical exam must be passed again (practice abroad might satisfy this but is unverified); (3) BLS certification must be current at each renewal; (4) lapse after 31 March requires relicensure ($370). Renewal fee amount unknown.
