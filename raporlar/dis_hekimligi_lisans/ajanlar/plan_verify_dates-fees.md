## STEP 7: Expect results within about 8 weeks; plan for a possible retake (claims 6 and 7 CONFIRMED)
TIMING: March results by about 26 Apr 2027. If needed, retake 22 Nov 2027 (registration 25 May to 25 Oct 2027, 21:00 ET).
Registering page: 'Examination results are released within 8 weeks of the exam.' The Protocol says 'normally released within eight weeks'.

Attempts: the FAQ says 'The Virtual OSCE can be taken a maximum of three times.' By-law 2 s.7.5: 'A person who has failed the Virtual OSCE three (3) times shall automatically cease to be a Certification Candidate.' s.7.1.2 sets eligibility while 'not more than sixty (60) months have elapsed since the date of graduation'. s.7.4: 'A person who does not pass the Virtual OSCE while designated as a Certification Candidate pursuant to Section 7.1.2 ... shall automatically cease to be a Certification Candidate.' s.8.1 measures 60 months against 'the examination date for which they are registering'. After that, only Special Consideration applies (s.8.2).

Timing trap: 1 Mar 2027 plus 8 weeks is 26 Apr 2027. That is after both the 5 Apr 2027 application deadline and the 19 Apr 2027 registration deadline for 20 May 2027. A March candidate who fails may not learn the result in time to register for May. The realistic retake is 22 Nov 2027 (Canada only; registration opens 25 May 2027).

TIMING: After certification (earliest about May 2027 if the March exam is passed and the transcript is on file). Apply to BCCOHP within 3 years of the certificate's issue date. Applying after 1 October uses the CAD 875 half-year licence fee.
Certificate issue: 'An NDEB certificate is issued upon successful completion of the Virtual OSCE, and after proof of graduation is provided to the NDEB office.' Graduates of accredited programs outside Canada must have 'An original official transcript of marks ... sent directly to the NDEB'.

No NDEB expiry: I found no expiry clause in By-law 2 or on the certificate page. This is an absence of a rule, not an explicit statement, so 'no expiry' remains unverified.

BC 3-year rule CONFIRMED. BCCOHP Bylaws (1 Apr 2026) s.6.05: 'the eligibility standards for a full dentist licence are (a) a degree or equivalent qualification from a recognized general dentistry education program, and (b) an NDEB certificate issued no more than 3 years before the date of the applicant's application for a licence under section 6.03.' The BCCOHP web page instead says 'successful completion of the National Dental Examining Board (NDEB) examinations no more than three years before the date of your application ... If your NDEB was completed more than three years ago ... you may have to complete an equivalency determination process.'

BC fees CONFIRMED. Bylaws Schedule A, II Dentistry, full dentist licence: application fee $3,322; licence fee full year $1,749; half year $875. Note 2: 'Half year fees ... only apply to applications for a licence submitted after October 1.' Note 1: 'Application fees ... are non-refundable.' BC documents include a 'Notarized or certified copy of NDEB certificate' (not needed if NDEB confirms directly) and professional liability insurance information.

## RISKS
1. TODAY'S OPENING IS NOT USABLE YET. North American registration for 1 Mar 2027 opened 3 Nov 2026 at 10:00 ET, but only applicants with an approved application can register. The first period 'closes when all exam locations are full'. A candidate whose documents are still in transit will probably miss it and depend on the second period. That period opens 'about 6-8 weeks before the exam', roughly 4-18 Jan 2027 (inferred; NDEB has not published it), and seats are only those freed by withdrawals or added capacity.

2. RECEIVED, NOT SENT. Courier the documents from Turkey early. Canadian holidays and the 5 Jan 2027 cutoff are close together, and missing documents mean 'you will not be able to register'.

3. TRANSCRIPT TIMING IS AMBIGUOUS. The general deadline rule covers all listed documents, so get the Yeditepe sealed transcript or e-transcript delivered by 5 Jan 2027. The transcript must show the conferral date ('An interim transcript ... is not acceptable').

4. 15-DAY VERIFICATION TRAP. If Yeditepe does not answer NDEB's education verification by about 14 Feb 2027, NDEB may withdraw the candidate. That is after the registration deadline, so 'Withdrawal fees will be applied', likely 100%. Alert the Yeditepe dean's office in advance.

5. MARCH-TO-MAY RETAKE GAP. Results for 1 Mar 2027 are due within 8 weeks, i.e. by about 26 Apr 2027. The May 2027 registration deadline is 19 Apr 2027. A failed March candidate may not reach May and would wait for 22 Nov 2027, which is currently listed for Canada only.

6. NO REGION TRANSFERS. Ireland and Australia are a separate region from Canada/US. Choose a region you can get a visa for, because transfers across regions are banned. Ireland and the US each require their own visa for Turkish citizens. Visa lead times can exceed the window between registration and the exam.

7. MARCH VENUE TYPE IS UNCONFIRMED. Only May and November are stated to use Prometric. The March venue and logistics come in the registration information memo, so do not assume Prometric scheduling rules for March.

8. FEES ARE 'SUBJECT TO CHANGE'. The schedule is still dated 1 July 2025 and is reviewed annually. Withdrawal before the deadline now costs 50% (CAD 875), and a no-show costs 100% (CAD 1,750).

9. BC 3-YEAR CLOCK. The BC bylaw counts from the certificate ISSUE date, while the BC web page speaks of exam completion. Use the earlier of the two dates. The application fee (CAD 3,322) is non-refundable. Applying after 1 Oct lowers only the licence fee (CAD 875), not the application fee.

10. MISREPRESENTATION CLAUSE (by-laws in force since July 2026). Inaccurate or incomplete documents, bots used to grab seats, or password sharing (including letting an agent log in) can lead to ineligibility.

11. MINOR SOURCE INCONSISTENCY. NDEB news says the by-laws took effect 1 July 2026. The By-law 2 PDF says 'Approved July 10, 2026, Effective July 15, 2026'. This does not change the plan.

## OPEN
1. Can a 'graduated within the last 5 years' applicant be approved and register for 1 Mar 2027 if the Yeditepe transcript arrives after 5 Jan 2027, or must it be received by then? Ask NDEB at info@ndeb-bned.ca, tel. +1-613-236-5912 (transcripts: verification@ndeb-bned.ca), and quote your NDEB ID.

2. What are the exact opening dates of the second registration period for 1 Mar 2027? NDEB says only 'about 6-8 weeks before the exam'. Watch NDEBConnect or ask info@ndeb-bned.ca.

3. What venue type will the March 2027 Virtual OSCE use (NDEB-run site or otherwise), and which specific cities in the US, Canada, Ireland and Australia? These are not published and appear only in the registration information memo. Ask info@ndeb-bned.ca.

4. Does an NDEB certificate expire? No expiry clause was found in By-law 2 or on the certificate page, but no explicit 'does not expire' statement was found either. Ask info@ndeb-bned.ca. For BC purposes, the binding rule is BCCOHP Bylaw 6.05(b), the 3 years from issue.

5. Will the NDEB fees change for 2027? The page says 'Subject to Change' and fees are reviewed annually. Re-check https://ndeb-bned.ca/certification-process/fees/ before paying.

6. Does BCCOHP count the 3 years from the certificate issue date (as in the bylaw) or from the exam completion date (as on the web page)? Ask registration@oralhealthbc.ca.

7. Will Yeditepe's dean's office respond to NDEB's 'Verification of Education with University' request, and how fast? Ask the Yeditepe Faculty of Dentistry Dean's Office or Registrar (Ogrenci Isleri) directly.
