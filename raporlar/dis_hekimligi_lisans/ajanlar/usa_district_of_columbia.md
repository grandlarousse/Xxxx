## Sınav yolları

- **Licensure by examination (17 DCMR § 4204): JCNDE National Board (NBDE/INBDE) + NERB/ADEX-type regional clinical exam (CDCA/WREB/ADEX/CITA)** (el becerisi: evet): 17 DCMR § 4204.1 (2013 codified text): 'To qualify for a license by examination, an applicant shall: (a) Meet the education requirements set forth under § 4202 of this chapter; and (b) Receive a passing score on the following: (1) Part I and Part II of the examination of the Joint Commission on National Dental Examinations; (2) The Northeast Regional Board (NERB) or the American Board of Dental Examiners (ADEX) examination; and (3) The District of Columbia Dental Law Examination.' The current § 4204 (effective 3/26/2021) was amended by 2020-21 rulemakings 'Accepting nonpatient-based examinations and waiving live [patient requirements]' (N0097645, N100452) and a rulemaking clarifying that 'the requirement for dental licensure in the District includes a passing score on the periodontal portion of the Northeast Regional Board' exam (N0045581). The Board's Dentist Application Checklist (rev. 02/16/2024) item 10: 'You are required to request your National Board of Dentistry Examination (NBDE) results to be released to the DC Board of Dentistry to verify. In addition, upload a copy of your NERB (CDCA/WREB/ADEX/CITA) Dental results onto your application.' § 4204.4: NERB/ADEX results older than 10 years must be retaken. The ADEX exam (manikin-based) is hands-on. The DC Dental Law Examination is now listed as 'Dentist Take-Home Law Exam (Waived)' on the application package page.
- **Licensure by endorsement (17 DCMR § 4209) - for dentists already licensed elsewhere** (el becerisi: evet): Not available to a new graduate. Requires a licence in another jurisdiction and either passage of NERB/ADEX, or passage of another regional board exam plus evidence of having 'been actively engaged in the practice of dentistry, in another U.S. state, in good standing, for the five (5) years immediately preceding the application, and has at least eight hundred and fifty (850) hours of active dental practice' (2013 text § 4209). SSN on application also listed. Still presupposes a hands-on regional exam.
- **DLOSCE-only / OSCE-only pathway** (el becerisi: hayır): NOT AVAILABLE. Neither the DCMR chapter 42 text, the rulemaking history for § 4204, nor the 2024 Board checklist mention the DLOSCE; DC is not among the jurisdictions in the DLOSCE Candidate Guide list. No rule accepting an ADEX-integrated DLOSCE pass alone exists.
- **PGY-1 residency in lieu of clinical exam** (el becerisi: evet): NOT AVAILABLE. No residency-based pathway appears in 17 DCMR ch. 42 (§§ 4204, 4209) or the 2024 checklist; residency is mentioned only as creditable toward the 850 practice hours for endorsement.
- **Portfolio / state-specific clinical exam** (el becerisi: evet): NOT AVAILABLE. DC administers no state clinical exam and has no portfolio pathway; the only DC-specific exam was the take-home Dental Law Examination, now shown as waived.

## Teori-only açıklaması
17 DCMR § 4204.1(b) requires, besides the JCNDE national board, 'The Northeast Regional Board (NERB) or the American Board of Dental Examiners (ADEX) examination' (rulemaking N0045581 adds that this 'includes a passing score on the periodontal portion' of the NERB exam). The current Board checklist (rev. 02/16/2024) requires applicants to 'upload a copy of your NERB (CDCA/WREB/ADEX/CITA) Dental results'. The 2020-2021 emergency/final rulemakings only moved DC to 'Accepting nonpatient-based examinations and waiving live [patient]' requirements, i.e. manikin-based ADEX components are accepted, but a hands-on regional clinical exam remains mandatory. No DLOSCE-only, residency, or portfolio alternative exists in the rules or the application checklist. The DC law exam (the only purely written DC component) is listed as waived. Caveat: I could not read the verbatim 2021 codified text of § 4204, only the 2013 text, the rulemaking headings and the 2024 checklist.

## DLOSCE notları
No DC rule or Board document mentions the DLOSCE. 17 DCMR § 4204 and the 2024 checklist require NERB/ADEX (CDCA/WREB/ADEX/CITA) clinical results; DC is not in the DLOSCE Candidate Guide list of accepting jurisdictions. Because ADEX reports pass/fail per component and DC (per rulemaking N0045581) requires passing all ADEX components including the periodontal portion, an ADEX-integrated DLOSCE pass alone would not satisfy § 4204; nothing published by DC accepts earlier standalone DLOSCE scores.

- **lawful_presence_verification:** True
- **ssn_or_sin_required:** False
- **itin_or_no_ssn_alternative:** Sworn 'no SSN' affidavit accepted, but it must be accompanied by an immigration/identity document. Dentist Application Checklist (rev. 02/16/2024) item 3 'Social Security Number': 'If you do not have social security number then you must submit a Sworn Affidavit, under penalty of perjury, stating that you do not have a social security number AND one of the following: a copy of a Certificate of Citizenship or Naturalization; a Resident Alien Card; a valid foreign passport with a visa; or a work permit card from the Department of Homeland Security (I-766 or I-688B).' The DC Health application package page lists a 'Social Security Number Affidavit' form. A valid foreign passport with a (visitor) visa therefore suffices; no ITIN is mentioned. (Source: https://dchealth.dc.gov/sites/default/files/dc/sites/doh/page_content/attachments/Board%20of%20Dentistry%20Dentist%20Checklist.pdf; https://dchealth.dc.gov/service/dentistry-initial-license-application-package)
- **in_jurisdiction_address_required:** False
- **barrier_level:** low
- **details:** DC Code § 3-1205.04(d) only requires that a dentistry applicant 'is a graduate of a school of dentistry accredited by the Commission on Dental Accreditation'; the section contains no citizenship, permanent-residence, work-authorisation or lawful-presence requirement for dentists (a mailing-address/residency clause exists only for naturopathic physicians, § 3-1205.04(e)). No DC-wide SAVE/lawful-presence statute for professional licences was found. 17 DCMR § 4204.2(a)(1) (2013 text) lists 'The applicant's social security number on the application', but the Board's 2024 checklist and application package expressly provide a sworn no-SSN affidavit alternative, which must be supported by one of: Certificate of Citizenship/Naturalization, Resident Alien Card, 'a valid foreign passport with a visa', or a DHS work permit card. A Turkish passport with a B-1/B-2 visa would meet this wording (a visa-free/ESTA traveller would need to check with the Board; Turkish citizens need a visa anyway). This is a mild lawful-presence/identity check, not a status requirement. The application asks for home and business addresses (any location); no DC address or residency is required. A photocopy of a government-issued photo ID is required: 'A U.S. driver's license or U.S. passport is acceptable' (foreign passport presumably acceptable but not expressly stated).
- **citations:** DC Code § 3-1205.04 (General qualifications of applicants) https://code.dccouncil.gov/us/dc/council/code/sections/3-1205.04; 17 DCMR § 4204.2 (2013 codified text) https://doh.dc.gov/sites/default/files/dc/sites/doh/publication/attachments/dental_board_regs_3-2013.pdf; DC Board of Dentistry Dentist Application Checklist rev. 02/16/2024 https://dchealth.dc.gov/sites/default/files/dc/sites/doh/page_content/attachments/Board%20of%20Dentistry%20Dentist%20Checklist.pdf; DC Health 'Dentistry Initial License Application Package' page (lists 'Social Security Number Affidavit') https://dchealth.dc.gov/service/dentistry-initial-license-application-package

## Yüz yüze adımlar
(1) Criminal background check with fingerprinting: checklist item 11: 'CBC is completed at the time you submit your online application. A $50 payment is required online with the application. Upon completion of the payment, the applicant will receive an email with a code to schedule a fingerprint appointment.' The appointment is in-person (DC Health's fingerprint vendor); whether out-of-area or foreign ink-card submission is accepted is not stated - plan to do it during the US visit for the ADEX exam. (2) Jurisprudence: the DC 'Take-Home' Dental Law Exam is listed as 'Waived' on the application package page, so no in-person law exam. (3) No interview. (4) The no-SSN affidavit is a 'Sworn Affidavit, under penalty of perjury' - i.e. notarised; can be executed abroad before a notary/US consular officer or during the visit. (5) Passport-type photo and photocopy of government photo ID uploaded online; three moral-character references submitted online; official transcript must be sent directly by the school (e-mail to dcbod@dc.gov or mail). (6) The hands-on ADEX/CDCA-WREB-CITA exam itself must be taken in person in the US. Overall, everything except fingerprinting and the clinical exam can be done from abroad through the online licensing system.

## Diğer başlangıç şartları
Education: CODA-accredited dental school graduate (DC Code § 3-1205.04(d); 17 DCMR § 4202) - official sealed transcript showing DDS/DMD conferred, sent directly by the school. Exams: JCNDE national board (NBDE/INBDE) scores released to the Board; NERB/ADEX (CDCA/WREB/ADEX/CITA) clinical results uploaded; NERB/ADEX results must be less than 10 years old (§ 4204.4). DC Dental Law take-home exam: currently 'Waived'. Criminal background check with fingerprints ($50). One recent 2x2 passport-type photo (original), photocopy of government photo ID, SSN or sworn no-SSN affidavit plus immigration/identity document, name-change documents if applicable, three moral-character references (online form), detailed explanation for any screening-question 'yes' answers. No CPR/BLS, English test, or liability insurance requirement found for initial licensure. Application is filed through DC Health's online licensing system. No filing deadlines; processing time not published.

## İlk ücretler
Online application and licensure fee: $430 total (checklist rev. 02/16/2024: 'All pages of the online application and licensure fee of $430 are required'; 2008 fee matrix broke this down as $85 application + $319 licence + $26 law exam). Criminal background check: $50. Duplicate licence $34 (2008). ADEX/CDCA-WREB-CITA exam fees paid separately to the testing agency (ADEX full series about $2,995). INBDE fee paid to JCNDE.

## Lisansı sürdürme

- **renewal_period:** Biennial. DC Health is transitioning to a birth-month cycle: 'All credentials issued on or after June 16, 2024, will expire on the last day of the holder's' birth month, with renewals in odd or even years according to the licensee's birth year; applies to all new and renewal applicants.
- **renewal_fee:** Unclear for the current cycle; the last published dentist licence fee was $319 per two-year term (2008 fee matrix). Late renewal fee applies if CE proof is submitted within 60 days after expiry (17 DCMR § 4206.10-.12).
- **ce_hours:** 25 hours per two-year renewal period, 'which shall include current cardiopulmonary resuscitation certification for health care providers (CPR certification) and four (4) hours of infection control' (17 DCMR § 4206.4, 2013 text). Later amendments (N0073124, N0087123/N0090577) added public-health-priority content requirements; DC Health's '2025 Public Notice Identifying Public Health Issues for Continuing Education Effective October 1, 2025' applies. Licensees with less than two years on their first term during the birth-month transition need only 50% of CE.
- **ce_online_ok:** Yes in principle: 17 DCMR § 4206 was amended by Final Rulemaking N105638 (effective 3/26/2021), described as 'amending licensure requirements for Dentists allowing online courses to satisfy all continuing education requirements'. The superseded 2013 text capped internet CE at 8 CEUs per period and stated 'Internet courses and programs shall not be used to satisfy the continuing education CPR certification requirement' (§ 4206.5-.6); whether the CPR exclusion survived the 2021 amendment could not be verified.
- **cpr_required:** Yes - 'current cardiopulmonary resuscitation certification for health care providers' is part of the 25-hour CE requirement (§ 4206.4). Under the 2013 text CPR could not be done online (§ 4206.6); the 2021 amendment allowing online courses for 'all' CE requirements may have changed this - unclear. Assume a hands-on skills session (e.g. AHA BLS, available internationally) may be needed.
- **practice_hours_required:** None for renewal or for keeping an active licence. (850 hours / 5 years of practice applies only to licensure by endorsement under § 4209.)
- **address_or_residency_required:** No DC address or residency required. Licensee must keep an address of record (home and business/preferred mailing address) and notify the Board of changes; any address, including abroad, appears acceptable (not expressly stated).
- **insurance_required:** No mandatory professional liability insurance requirement found in the DC Health Occupations Revision Act or 17 DCMR ch. 42.
- **inactive_status:** DC Code § 3-1205.11: 'Upon application by a licensee ... and payment of the inactive status fee established by the Mayor, each board shall place a licensee ... on inactive status'; 'While on inactive status, the individual shall not be subject to the renewal fee and shall not practice ... in the District.' The Mayor 'may establish by rule a maximum amount of time' a licence may remain inactive; no maximum for dentists was found in ch. 42. Inactive-status fee amount: unclear. No CE required while inactive.
- **reactivation:** DC Code § 3-1205.11(c): reactivation requires paying the fee, complying 'with the continuing education and criminal background check requirements in effect' at reactivation, and establishing 'current competency to practice ... safely and effectively, which may include compliance with a re[-entry]' requirement set by the board. 17 DCMR § 4206.7: an inactive licensee seeking reactivation must show 25 hours of approved CE 'which shall include current CPR certification and four (4) hours of infection control, obtained within the two (2) year period preceding the date of the application for reactivation ... and an additional twelve (12) hours of approved continuing education credit for each additional year that the applicant was inactive status beginning with the third year.' Reinstatement of an expired licence (§ 4206.8) uses the same formula (25 h + 12 h per additional year expired from the third year) plus fees; new criminal background check. No automatic requirement to retake the clinical exam, but the Board may impose competency conditions after long absences. Note § 4204.4: for NEW applicants, NERB/ADEX results 10+ years old must be retaken.
- **remote_maintainability:** moderate
- **details:** An active DC licence can be renewed online every two years with 25 CE hours (online courses allowed since the 2021 rule), no practice-hour requirement, no DC address, no insurance. The awkward elements are the CPR certification (possibly requiring a hands-on skills component) and the fingerprint-based criminal background check that DC applies at reactivation/reinstatement (and possibly at renewal under DC Health's CBC programme - not verified). Inactive status is cheap (no renewal fee) and reactivation is CE-based (25 h + 12 h per year beyond two) rather than exam-based, but the Board retains discretion to require proof of current competency.
