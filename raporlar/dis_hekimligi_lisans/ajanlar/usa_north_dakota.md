# North Dakota (USA) — ajan bulgusu

Düzenleyici: North Dakota State Board of Dental Examiners — https://www.nddentalboard.org/

**Teori-only rota:** no · **Göçmenlik engeli:** unclear · **Uzaktan sürdürme:** moderate · **Güven:** medium

## Sınav yolları

- **Licensure by examination (regional hands-on clinical exam: ADEX/CDCA-WREB-CITA, CRDTS, SRTA)** (el becerisi: evet): NDCC 43-28-10.1: DDS/DMD from accredited school; pass the JCNDE exam (INBDE) 'or the national dental examining board of Canada'; pass 'a clinical competency examination administered by a regional dental testing service approved by the board by rule'; pass the ND laws-and-rules (jurisprudence) exam within one year of application. NDAC 20-02-01-03.1 adds: national exam and clinical exam within 5 years of application; 'Required components shall include a periodontal component, posterior composite or amalgam restoration, a class three restorative procedure, and an endodontic component, and after April 1, 2021, a fixed prosthetic component'; CPR course within 2 years; physical health/visual acuity. Board page lists approved clinical exams: CRDTS, ADEX, CDCA-WREB, CITA (all manikin/hands-on). ADA summary: 'The Board accepts all regional clinical exams however the Canadian OSCE exam is not accepted in lieu of the clinical board requirement.' Fee $540 + $75 Professional Health Program fee.
- **Licensure by credential review** (el becerisi: hayır): NDCC 43-28-15: requires 'for at least five years immediately preceding application, has been licensed in good standing and has been actively practicing dentistry in another jurisdiction where the requirements are at least substantially equivalent'; certificates of good standing from every jurisdiction; written laws-and-rules exam 'administered by the board at a meeting'; NDAC 20-02-01-03.2 adds CPR within 2 years, physical health/visual acuity, 32 hours CE within 2 years. Fee $1,490. Not available to a new graduate (needs 5 years of active practice elsewhere, and the originating licence would itself normally have needed a hands-on exam).
- **DLOSCE-only / OSCE-only** (el becerisi: hayır): NOT available. North Dakota is not among the DLOSCE-accepting states in the JCNDE 2026 Candidate Guide; the ND rule requires a 'clinical competency examination administered by a regional dental testing service' with specific manikin components (periodontal, posterior restoration, class III, endodontic, fixed prosthetic) that a computer-based OSCE cannot supply; the Board states the Canadian OSCE is not accepted in lieu of the clinical board requirement.
- **PGY-1 residency in lieu of clinical exam** (el becerisi: hayır): NOT available. Neither NDCC 43-28 nor NDAC 20-02-01 contains any provision accepting a postgraduate residency in place of the clinical competency examination (the only 'residency' reference in NDAC 20-02-01 concerns sedation/anesthesia permits).
- **Temporary / volunteer licences** (el becerisi: evet): NDAC 20-02-01-04 provides a temporary licence and the Board offers a 'Volunteer License for Dentists'; these are limited-purpose and were not researched in detail (not a route to full independent licensure for a new graduate).

## Teori-only açıklaması
NDCC 43-28-10.1(3): the applicant 'has passed a clinical competency examination administered by a regional dental testing service approved by the board by rule.' NDAC 20-02-01-03.1(2): 'The applicant has passed, within five years of application, a clinical competency examination approved by the board. Required components shall include a periodontal component, posterior composite or amalgam restoration, a class three restorative procedure, and an endodontic component, and after April 1, 2021, a fixed prosthetic component.' ADA Licensure-by-State (updated 13 Oct 2026, quoting the ND application): 'The Board accepts all regional clinical exams however the Canadian OSCE exam is not accepted in lieu of the clinical board requirement.' There is no residency, portfolio or OSCE-only alternative in the statute or rules, so a hands-on regional exam (ADEX/CRDTS/SRTA/CDCA-WREB-CITA) is unavoidable for a new graduate.

## DLOSCE notları
North Dakota does not accept the DLOSCE (standalone or otherwise) in lieu of its clinical exam: it is not on the JCNDE list of DLOSCE-accepting states, and NDAC 20-02-01-03.1 prescribes hands-on components (periodontal, posterior restoration, class III, endodontic, fixed prosthetic) that only a manikin/clinical exam can satisfy. The Board expressly rejects the Canadian OSCE. No ND policy on the ADEX-integrated DLOSCE was found; since a full ADEX series already contains the required manikin components, the DLOSCE component is irrelevant to ND eligibility (ADEX pass = eligible; DLOSCE component alone = not eligible).

PGY-1 kabul: False

## Yüz yüze adımlar
(1) Application: must be notarized OR accompanied by copies of current government ID issued within the last 5 years in lieu of notarization (can be done from abroad). (2) Criminal history record check (NDCC 43-28-11.2 via NDCC 12-60-24): fingerprint-based; the Board says 'Contact the Board office if you need the fingerprint cards and forms mailed to you' and 'Processing this information may take up to 14 business days' - so cards can be mailed and presumably inked abroad, but acceptance of prints taken by a foreign agency is unverified. (3) Jurisprudence exam: 'The applicant has passed the online Jurisprudence examination on ND laws and rules' - online, no travel. (4) Interview: 'The Board may require an interview' / 'Applicants may be required to appear at a formal meeting of the Board for an interview' (NDAC 20-02-01-03.3(11)) - discretionary; could require a visit to Bismarck. (5) Hands-on clinical exam (ADEX/CRDTS etc.) must be taken at a US test site. (6) CPR course within 2 years must be hands-on (NDAC 20-02-01-06(3)). (7) Official transcript sent directly by the dental school; verification of physical health and visual acuity. Application must be completed within six months of filing (NDAC 20-02-01-03.3; Board: application and fee no longer valid if requirements not completed within six months of postmark).

## Diğer başlangıç şartları
INBDE (or NDEB exam per NDCC 43-28-10.1(2)) within 5 years; approved regional clinical exam within 5 years; online ND jurisprudence (laws and rules) exam within 1 year of application; hands-on CPR course within 2 years; criminal history record check (fingerprint cards, applicant pays); certificates of good standing from every jurisdiction ever licensed; proof of physical health and visual acuity; official transcript direct from school; recent photograph; sworn/notarized application or ID copies; possible Board interview; $75 Professional Health Program fee (NDAC 20-05-01-01(8)(a), new rules effective 1 Apr 2026). No English test (CODA graduate). Timeline: application valid 6 months; background check up to 14 business days; Board acts at meetings.

## Lisansı sürdürme

- **renewal_period:** Biennial: licences expire 31 December of every odd-numbered year (NDCC 43-28-16.2); 60-day late window with late fee; after that the licence 'may not be renewed, and the dentist must apply for and meet the requirements for licensure' (i.e. full re-licensure including clinical exam within 5 years).
- **renewal_fee:** Set by Board rule; amount not retrieved (unclear). A $75 Professional Health Program fee was introduced April 2026 and is presumably recurring.
- **ce_hours:** 32 hours per two-year cycle (NDAC 20-02-01-06(3)), including at least 2 h ethics/jurisprudence (passing the laws-and-rules exam counts as 2 h), at least 2 h infection control, and a CPR course; max 2 h practice management.
- **ce_online_ok:** Almost entirely: 'a dentist may earn no more than sixteen (16) hours from self-study' (non-interactive); 'All other continuing education requirements may be satisfied from online education that allows for real-time interaction between attendees and the proctor' (live webinars). So 16 h self-study + 16 h live webinar = 100% online from abroad, EXCEPT 'Cardiopulmonary resuscitation courses must provide hands-on training.'
- **cpr_required:** Yes: 'All dentists must hold a current cardiopulmonary resuscitation certificate' and CPR courses 'must provide hands-on training' (NDAC 20-02-01-06(3),(5)). A hands-on CPR/BLS course taken outside the US is probably acceptable (no provider restriction stated) but unverified.
- **practice_hours_required:** None found for renewal of an active licence. Practice currency matters only for reinstatement from inactive status (clinical exam within 5 years OR evidence of clinical practice within previous 5 years, waivable).
- **address_or_residency_required:** No in-state address or residency requirement found; licensees must notify the Board of name and address changes.
- **insurance_required:** No mandatory professional liability insurance requirement found in NDCC 43-28 or NDAC 20-02-01 (not exhaustively verified - unclear).
- **inactive_status:** NDCC 43-28-17 / NDAC 20-02-01-04.3: 'Upon payment of the fee determined by the board, a dentist may place the dentist's license on inactive status. A dentist on inactive status is excused from continuing education requirements... Inactive status must be renewed annually by completing the inactive status renewal application and paying the inactive status renewal fee. A dentist on inactive status may not practice in North Dakota.' No maximum duration stated. Fee amount not retrieved (unclear).
- **reactivation:** NDAC 20-02-01-04.3: reinstatement requires (1) a clinical competency examination approved under 20-02-01-03.1 'within five years of application or provides evidence of the clinical practice of dentistry within the previous five years. The board may waive this requirement at the board's discretion'; (2) Board laws-and-rules exam within 2 years; (3) 32 h CE within 2 years; (4) CPR course within 2 years; (5) no grounds for denial. So after >5 years inactive with no practice anywhere, a new hands-on clinical exam is required unless the Board waives it. A lapsed (unrenewed >60 days) licence cannot be reinstated - full re-application is required.
- **remote_maintainability:** moderate
- **details:** An ACTIVE ND licence can be kept indefinitely from abroad: biennial renewal with fee, 32 h CE achievable fully online (16 h self-study + 16 h live-interactive webinars), no practice hours, no in-state address. The only awkward element is the mandatory hands-on CPR course each cycle (and the annual-renewal inactive status, which is cheap on CE but risks a new clinical exam on reinstatement after 5+ years without practice). Keeping the licence active (never letting it lapse past the 60-day grace) is the safe strategy.
