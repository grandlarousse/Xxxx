# New Mexico (USA) — ajan bulgusu

Düzenleyici: New Mexico Board of Dental Health Care (administered by the NM Regulation and Licensing Department, Boards and Commissions Division) — https://www.rld.nm.gov/boards-and-commissions/individual-boards-and-commissions/dental-health-care/

**Teori-only rota:** no · **Göçmenlik engeli:** low · **Uzaktan sürdürme:** moderate · **Güven:** high

## Sınav yolları

- **Licensure by examination (regional/ADEX hands-on clinical exam)** (el becerisi: evet): The only route for a new graduate. 16.5.6.8 NMAC (eff. 5/31/2023): (A) diploma from a CODA-accredited school; (B) 'successfully completed the dental national board examination' (INBDE satisfies NMSA 61-5A-12 A); (C) 'passed a board approved clinical examination which includes hands-on periodontal and restorative procedures, the results of the clinical examination are valid in New Mexico for a period not to exceed five years' (apply directly to the examining agent; scores sent directly to the board); (D) jurisprudence exam >= 75%; (E) background status report from the board-designated service (B&B Reporting). Board FAQ: 'Passed a WREB, CRDT, SRTA, NERB/ADEX clinical exam'. ADA summary (updated 13 Oct 2026) lists accepted exams as 'American Board of Dental Examiners (ADEX, CDCA-WREB-CITA), CRDTS+SRTA'. Statute 61-5A-12 B: 'Written examinations shall be supplemented by the board or its agents by administering to each applicant a practical or clinical examination that reasonably tests the applicant's qualifications to practice general dentistry' (CRDTS, NERB, SRTA, WREB 'or any other comparable practical clinical examination the board approves'). Fee $600 (includes initial licence period).
- **Licensure by credentials (out-of-state licensee)** (el becerisi: evet): Not available to a new graduate. 61-5A-12 C/D and 16.5.8.8 NMAC: must hold 'a current active license in good standing by clinical examination in another state or territory of the United States' (good standing = active for 3 consecutive years immediately preceding and minimum 5 years of licensure, 16.5.8.7), plus 'passed a clinical examination approved by the board', INBDE, 60 CE hours in past 36 months, jurisprudence exam, B&B background report. Fee $850. The underlying out-of-state licence must itself be by clinical (hands-on) examination.
- **Expedited licensure (61-5A-14 B) for dentists licensed elsewhere** (el becerisi: evet): Fee $100 application + $300 licence (16.5.5.8 M). Requires an existing licence in another jurisdiction (which itself required a clinical exam); not open to a new graduate with no licence. Not researched in detail because the candidate holds no licence.
- **Specialty licence by examination (specialty clinical exam, or diplomate status where no specialty clinical exam exists)** (el becerisi: evet): 16.5.6.9 NMAC: requires a postgraduate degree/certificate from a CODA-accredited specialty programme (i.e. a residency, which the candidate excludes) plus 'passed a specialty clinical examination approved by the board'; 'an applicant in any specialty defined above for which there is no specialty clinical examination may substitute diplomat status for the examination'. The dental public health variant is the only statutory path with no clinical-skills test, but 16.5.6.7 states 'Since the diplomat examination does not test clinical skills, dentists licensed based on diplomat status shall not practice clinical dentistry', and it still requires a CODA specialty residency. Not viable for this candidate.
- **DLOSCE-only / OSCE-only route** (el becerisi: hayır): DOES NOT EXIST in New Mexico. 16.5.6.8 C expressly requires a clinical exam 'which includes hands-on periodontal and restorative procedures'; New Mexico is not among the DLOSCE-accepting states in the JCNDE 2026 Candidate Guide (AK*, AZ, CO, IN, IA*, KY, OR, WA). No board rule or notice accepting DLOSCE (standalone or ADEX-integrated) in lieu of the hands-on exam was found.
- **PGY-1 residency in lieu of clinical exam** (el becerisi: hayır): NOT AVAILABLE. Neither NMSA 61-5A-12 nor 16.5.6 NMAC contains any residency-in-lieu-of-examination provision; the only exam alternative is licensure by credentials from another US state. (A 12-month temporary licence exists for 'student enrolled in residency program', 16.5.5.8 G(4), but that is a restricted training licence, not a pathway to full licensure.)
- **Temporary licences (48-hour, 6-month, 12-month)** (el becerisi: evet): 16.5.5.8 G lists 48-hour ($50+$50), six-month ($100+$200), twelve-month ($100+$300) temporary licences and a residency-student licence. Eligibility rules (16.5.9 NMAC) were not read in full; these are not a substitute for permanent licensure and are not relevant to a new graduate seeking a durable licence.

## Teori-only açıklaması
A hands-on clinical examination is mandatory and there is no residency, OSCE-only, DLOSCE-only, portfolio or credentials alternative for a new graduate. 16.5.6.8 NMAC 'PREREQUISITE REQUIREMENTS FOR GENERAL PRACTICE LICENSE: Each applicant for a license to practice dentistry by examination must possess the following qualifications: ... C. passed a board approved clinical examination which includes hands-on periodontal and restorative procedures, the results of the clinical examination are valid in New Mexico for a period not to exceed five years: (1) the applicant shall apply directly to a board accepted examining agent for examination; and (2) results of the clinical examination must be sent directly to the board office' [16.5.6.8 NMAC - Rp, 5/31/2023]. NMSA 1978 61-5A-12 B: 'Written examinations shall be supplemented by the board or its agents by administering to each applicant a practical or clinical examination that reasonably tests the applicant's qualifications to practice general dentistry. These examinations shall include examinations offered by the central regional dental testing service, northeast regional board of dental examiners, southern regional testing agency or western regional examining board or any other comparable practical clinical examination the board approves ... Upon an applicant passing the written and clinical examinations and payment in advance of the necessary fees, the board shall issue a license to practice dentistry.' Licensure 'without a practical or clinical examination' exists only 'by credentials ... to an applicant who is duly licensed by a clinical examination as a dentist under the laws of another state or territory of the United States' (61-5A-12 C), which a new graduate cannot satisfy. Board FAQ confirms: 'Passed a WREB, CRDT, SRTA, NERB/ADEX clinical exam'. The INBDE and the online jurisprudence exam are necessary but not sufficient.

## DLOSCE notları
New Mexico does not accept the DLOSCE as its clinical examination. The rule wording ('clinical examination which includes hands-on periodontal and restorative procedures', 16.5.6.8 C NMAC) excludes a purely computer-based OSCE, and New Mexico is absent from the JCNDE 2026 DLOSCE Candidate Guide list of accepting states. No board notice, rule amendment or FAQ entry about the DLOSCE (standalone or as a component of the ADEX Dental Examination after the April 2026 ADA/ADEX integration) was found on rld.nm.gov or in the NMAC; the ADA state summary updated 13 Oct 2026 still lists only ADEX (CDCA-WREB-CITA), CRDTS and SRTA. Consequently (a) a standalone DLOSCE score earned before Oct 2026 has no standing in New Mexico, and (b) once the DLOSCE is folded into ADEX, the candidate still has to complete the ADEX manikin/hands-on components; the DLOSCE part alone would not meet 16.5.6.8 C. Clinical exam results are valid for 5 years for NM licensure.

The current July 2026 Dentist Application Manual describes the online portal steps (Pre-screening, Basic Information/profile, Licensure Information, online Jurisprudence Exam, Questions, Upload Documents, electronic Attestation, Payment by card/e-check) and does not show an SSN or citizenship step in its text, but the 'Basic Information' profile fields are shown only as screenshots that could not be read, so whether the portal's profile has a mandatory SSN field (and whether an ITIN is accepted there) is UNVERIFIED. Statewide, Laws 2021 ch. 70 (SB 219, signed 6 Apr 2021) 'allows submission of an individual taxpayer identification number as an alternative to a social security number for certain licenses and permits' (LFC Fiscal Impact Report) and removed legal-residency proof from other professions' statutes; the dental statute was not among those because it never had such a requirement. Candidate should ask the board (505-476-4680 / dental board e-mail) whether the portal accepts an ITIN or a blank SSN field.

- **details:** New Mexico is one of the most immigration-neutral US jurisdictions. (1) Laws 2020, Chapter 53 (SB 137, signed by the Governor 6 March 2020; passed Senate 42-0, House 50-18): 'A. It is the policy of this state that a person is eligible for occupational or professional licensure or certification for which that person is qualified, regardless of the person's citizenship or immigration status. B. No administrative rule or agency procedure shall be adopted or enforced that conflicts with the policy stated in Subsection A of this section. C. This section serves as the affirmation of eligibility in this state pursuant to 8 U.S.C. Section 1621(d) for persons not lawfully present in the United States to be licensed or certified.' This means the board may not require citizenship, PR, work authorisation, lawful presence or SAVE verification. (2) The Dental Health Care Act licensure section (61-5A-12) and the rules (16.5.6 NMAC) contain no citizenship, residency, SSN, visa or lawful-presence element; the documentation list (16.5.6.10) is: application, transcripts sent directly from the school, clinical exam score card, national board score card, infection-control course proof (or graduation within 12 months), hands-on BLS/CPR card, NPDB and AADE clearinghouse verification (obtained by the board), B&B background status report, verification of any other licences. (3) Address: 16.5.1.9 NMAC only requires the licensee 'to keep the board informed of a current mailing address' and to report 'current practice address(s)'; no New Mexico residence or address is required. (4) The only residual uncertainty is an SSN field in the RLD online portal profile (see itin note); a federal child-support rule (42 USC 666(a)(13)) generally makes states collect SSNs on professional licence applications and the dental rules reference the Parental Responsibility Act (16.5.1.23 NMAC), but no NM dental rule or statute text requiring an SSN was located, and Laws 2021 ch. 70 shows the legislature's policy of accepting ITINs. A visitor on a B-1/B-2 visa can lawfully sit the clinical exam and complete the paperwork; the licence itself does not authorise employment (immigration law, not board rules).

## Yüz yüze adımlar
(1) Hands-on clinical examination (ADEX via CDCA-WREB-CITA, CRDTS or SRTA) must be taken physically at a US test site; unavoidable, and the only step that truly requires travel. (2) Jurisprudence exam: fully online, open-book, launched from inside the RLD online application ('On the Jurisprudence Exam page, select the Launch New button'; 'You are allowed 3 attempts'; pass mark 75%; $100 re-take fee under 16.5.5.8 D). No in-person component. (3) Application: online portal only ('Applications for initial licensure must be submitted through an online portal'); attestation is an electronic checkbox plus typed name; payment by Visa/Mastercard or e-check via Cybersource. No notarisation and no photograph requirement appears in the current rules or manual. (4) Background check: 'background status report from a board designated professional background service' = B&B Reporting, a commercial record-search firm (criminal, civil, identity, international screening); the rules do not require fingerprinting or Live Scan, so it is done remotely by applying/paying directly to B&B (B&B's exact data requirements, e.g. SSN, not verified). (5) NPDB: the board itself obtains NPDB and AADE Clearinghouse verification (16.5.6.10 G); the board's page also lists an NPDB self-query, which is online via ID.me identity verification (profile asks for licence info, schools, NPI/DEA etc.; ID.me verification from abroad may be awkward). (6) BLS/CPR: must be a live hands-on or hybrid-with-skills course (AHA, Red Cross, ASHI; FAQ also lists EMS Safety Services and National Safety Council); can be obtained abroad through those providers' international training centres before applying. (7) Transcripts sent directly from Yeditepe to the board. (8) No interview; the board's delegate may approve and the full board 'shall formally accept the approval of the application at the next scheduled meeting' (board meets quarterly) without applicant attendance. Net: one US trip for the clinical exam; everything else can be done from Turkey.

## Diğer başlangıç şartları
INBDE (61-5A-12 A: 'written portion of the dental examination administered by the joint commission on national dental examinations'); online open-book jurisprudence exam, 75%, 3 attempts, $100 retake; proof of infection-control course within the past 12 months OR graduation from dental school within the past 12 months (16.5.6.10 E); current BLS/CPR (AHA/ARC/ASHI) that 'cannot be a self-study course' (16.5.6.10 F); B&B Reporting background status report sent directly to the board; NPDB/AADE clearinghouse verification obtained by the board; verification of any other health-care licences held; official transcripts direct from the school. No English-language test, no liability insurance, no NM address, no citizenship. Application valid one year from receipt (16.5.6.10 A). Clinical exam scores valid 5 years. Initial licence issued for 25-36 months, expiring July 1 of the third year (16.5.11.8). Timeline: delegate review on receipt of a complete file, formal acceptance at the next quarterly board meeting.

## İlk ücretler
Application for licensure by examination $600 (non-refundable; 'includes the initial licensing period', 16.5.5.8 B). Jurisprudence re-take $100 (16.5.5.8 D). B&B Reporting background report: paid directly to B&B (amount not published in the rules). NPDB self-query $3/year subscription (plus fee for sealed mailed copy). Clinical exam fees paid separately to CDCA-WREB-CITA/CRDTS/SRTA. For comparison: licensure by credentials $850; expedited $100 + $300; duplicate licence $25. (Fee rule 16.5.5 NMAC effective 27 Dec 2022.)

## Lisansı sürdürme

- **renewal_period:** Triennial. Initial licence expires July 1 of the third year of licensure (issued for 25-36 months, 16.5.11.8); thereafter 'dental licenses expire every three years on June 30' (16.5.11.9); renewal application must be submitted by July 1 of the renewal year. Statute 61-5A-16 A: 'all licensees shall be required to renew their licenses triennially'.
- **renewal_fee:** $550 triennial renewal + $30 impaired-dentist fee = $580 per 3 years (16.5.5.8 E). Late: +$100 after July 1 through Sept 1, plus cumulative $10/day from Aug 1 (16.5.5.8 E(2)-(3)); licence may be suspended 30-60 days after July 1 and summarily revoked if not renewed by Aug 31 (61-5A-16 G-H).
- **ce_hours:** 60 hours per triennial cycle (16.5.10.8; 16.5.11.10), pro-rated at 20 hours per full year of the initial licensing period ('Initial licenses issued for less than a full year do not require continuing education for the first renewal'). Required content each renewal: current hands-on BLS/CPR; an infection-control course per renewal period (16.5.10.9 A-B); 3 hours pain management/controlled substances (16.5.10.9 D applies to DEA registrants; 16.5.1.15 E(2) states 'A dentist must complete three (3) hours related to pain management and controlled substances'). Records kept 1 year after the cycle; random audits.
- **ce_online_ok:** Not 100%. 16.5.10.8 NMAC (eff. 12/14/2019): '60 hours of continuing education, a maximum of 30 hours can be on-line, webinars or self-study'. The newer 16.5.1.15 NMAC (eff. 5/31/2023) lists 'live virtual webinars (i.e., courses conducted live, in real-time with the ability to interact with the speaker)' as an approved method separate from 'Self-study' (pre-recorded, needs post-test) and caps only self-study at 30 hours per triennium; read together, at least 30 of the 60 hours should be live (in-person or live interactive webinar), and the board's open-book jurisprudence exam gives 3 hours/year ($25). IMPORTANT EXEMPTION: 16.5.10.11 B 'Licensee practicing or residing outside the United States shall not be required to fulfill the continuing education requirements for the period of the absence. (1) The board must be notified prior to license expiration that the licensee will be outside the US, including the period of the absence. (2) Upon return to the US, the licensee shall complete the continuing education required for the years of practice within the US during the renewal cycle, or apply for an emergency deferral.' So a licensee living in Turkey who notifies the board before each expiration is excused from CE while abroad (the CPR/infection-control items sit inside the same CE part, but whether the board also waives them is not explicit).
- **cpr_required:** Yes, at initial licensure and every renewal: 'Proof of current certification in basic life support (BLS) or cardiac pulmonary resuscitation (CPR) accepted by the American heart association, the American red cross, or the American safety and health institute (ASHI); cannot be self-study course' (16.5.10.9 A). 16.5.1.15 E(1): 'Certification must be acquired through a live hands-on course or through a hybrid method where demonstration of skills is still required. Certification acquired through a self-study only course, is not allowed.' Hands-on skills session therefore required (obtainable abroad through AHA/ARC/ASHI international training centres; FAQ also accepts EMS Safety Services and National Safety Council).
- **practice_hours_required:** None. No minimum practice, currency or clinical-activity hours appear in 16.5.10, 16.5.11, 16.5.12 or 61-5A-16.
- **address_or_residency_required:** No in-state residency or address. 16.5.1.9 A: licensee must 'keep the board informed of a current mailing address' (any address); B: 'The board must be informed of current practice address(s) for all licensees'; changes reported within 30 days. Renewal application must state 'full name, business address, the date and number of the license' (61-5A-16 E).
- **insurance_required:** No mandatory professional liability insurance found in the Dental Health Care Act or 16.5 NMAC. (Only a mandatory self-reporting duty for adverse events/settlements, 16.5.3 NMAC, and the $30 impaired-dentist fee.)
- **inactive_status:** Two non-practising categories, each usable ONE TIME. (a) RETIREMENT (16.5.12.8): written request (via online portal per FAQ) before the current licence expires; exempt from renewal fees during retirement; may include CE taken since last renewal; max 3 years before reinstatement must be requested. (b) INACTIVE (16.5.12.9): application ($50, 16.5.5.8 J) before expiration of the current licence 'or the three-year eligibility of retirement status'; triennial inactive renewal fee $90 (16.5.5.8 F); no CE required while inactive; reinstatement possible 'within nine years of the date of inactivation' (or six years if the licensee signs the waiver foregoing the 3-year retirement, 16.5.12.11 G). Both may be denied if complaints/discipline are pending. Dentists with an active NM practice must also give patient notice and name a records custodian; a never-practised-in-NM licensee has nothing to notify.
- **reactivation:** From RETIREMENT within 3 years (16.5.12.10): reinstatement fee $400 + triennial renewal $550 + impairment $30; 20 hours of clinical-practice CE per year of retirement (at least 20 in the 12 months before the request); current hands-on BLS/CPR; infection-control course within 12 months; 60 CE hours for the last active triennial cycle (may include CE listed at retirement or taken during retirement); DEA and AADE clearinghouse verifications; NPDB check; verification of other licences; board review at next meeting; same licence number reassigned. From INACTIVE within 9 years (16.5.12.11): same, plus 'proof of medical emergency course during the past 12 months'. NO new clinical examination and no refresher programme is required for reinstatement within those windows. After the window (3 years retired without going inactive; 9 years inactive, or 6 with waiver): 'application for a new license must be made by examination or credentials', i.e. a new hands-on clinical exam (results only valid 5 years) or an active licence elsewhere. LAPSE: licences not renewed by July 1 are expired; renewal with late fees possible until Sept 1; FAQ: 'You may reinstate the license within the year of revocation. If the license is revoked for more than a year from the date of revocation, you must reapply for a license as a new applicant.'
- **remote_maintainability:** moderate
- **details:** Keeping the licence ACTIVE from abroad indefinitely is feasible: $580 every 3 years, no practice hours, no NM address, no insurance, online renewal, and 16.5.10.11 B excuses CE entirely while the licensee resides outside the US (with advance notice before each expiration). The awkward items are the hands-on BLS/CPR card each renewal (available abroad through AHA/ARC/ASHI international centres, but it is a physical skills session), the infection-control course each cycle (online/self-study acceptable), and, if the CE exemption is not invoked, the cap that lets only 30 of 60 hours be online/self-study. The retirement (0 fee, 3 yrs) then inactive ($90/3 yrs, up to 9 yrs total) ladder is cheap but time-limited and single-use; reinstatement needs 20 CE hrs/yr absent, hands-on CPR, infection-control and medical-emergency courses but no clinical re-examination. Beyond 9 years inactive, a new hands-on clinical exam is unavoidable, so for a multi-decade plan the licence should be kept active (using the out-of-US CE exemption) rather than parked inactive.

## Açık sorular
(1) SSN in the RLD online portal: the 'Basic Information/My Profile' fields of the portal are shown only as screenshots in the manual; it is unverified whether an SSN is mandatory there and whether an ITIN or blank entry is accepted (statewide policy under Laws 2021 ch. 70 favours ITIN acceptance; the dental statute/rules have no SSN requirement). Confirm with the board. (2) B&B Reporting's intake form: whether it demands an SSN / US address for a foreign applicant with no US record history (their site advertises international screening). (3) NPDB self-query via ID.me from Turkey (ID.me generally needs a US phone/ID or passport-based verification) - the board says it obtains NPDB/AADE verification itself (16.5.6.10 G), so a self-query may not be needed. (4) Whether the 16.5.10.11 B out-of-US CE exemption also waives the hands-on BLS/CPR and infection-control proofs at renewal, or only the 60 hours. (5) Apparent inconsistency between 16.5.10.8 (max 30 online/webinar/self-study) and the newer 16.5.1.15 (only self-study capped at 30; live webinars listed separately) - ask the board which it enforces. (6) No board statement yet on the ADEX-integrated DLOSCE; given the 'hands-on' wording of 16.5.6.8 C a rule change would be needed before any OSCE-only acceptance. (7) ADA page annotates 61-5A-16 as 'Repealed effective July 1, 2024' - this appears to reflect the Dental Health Care Act's sunset clause (extended by HB 201 (2023)); renewal rules (16.5.11) and the board FAQ still operate on the triennial cycle. (8) The older 2010 paper credentials form (dead link) reportedly required notarisation and a photo; the current online process does not, but confirm no notarised affidavit is requested at upload stage.
