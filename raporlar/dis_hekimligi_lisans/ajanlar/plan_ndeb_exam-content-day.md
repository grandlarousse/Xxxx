## STEP 2: Choose the target sitting: 20 May 2027 as the main plan, 1 Mar 2027 as a stretch
TIMING: Decide now (Nov 2026). Get the application approved before 26 Jan 2027 so you can take a May seat in first registration.
**How registration works now.** Since 1 Sep 2026, NDEB runs two registration periods. Per NDEB: 'First registration: Opens on a set date and time and closes when all exam locations are full. Second registration: Opens about 6–8 weeks before the exam. Candidates may register or transfer if seats become available because of withdrawals or increased capacity.' You can only register 'If you have an approved application'.

**1 Mar 2027 (Monday).**
- First registration opened today (3 Nov 2026, 10:00 ET = 18:00 Turkey time) for US/Canada sites. Your application will not be approved in time to use it.
- Your realistic chance is the second registration window, about 4–18 Jan 2027. It only works if your application documents are received by 5 Jan 2027, NDEB approves them quickly, and seats come free.
- Registration deadline: 18 Jan 2027, 21:00 ET (= 19 Jan 05:00 TRT).

**20 May 2027 (Thursday).**
- Documents must be received by 5 Apr 2027.
- First registration opens 26 Jan 2027, 10:00 ET (= 18:00 TRT). Be logged in at that minute if your application is already approved.
- Second window: about 25 Mar–8 Apr 2027.
- Registration deadline: 19 Apr 2027, 21:00 ET (= 20 Apr 04:00 TRT).

**22 Nov 2027 (Monday, Canada only).** This is the fallback. Registration opens 25 May 2027; deadline 25 Oct 2027.

**Transfers.** 'Candidates may transfer between exam locations only when registration is open. They may not transfer between registration periods.' There is no fee to transfer location.

## STEP 5: Match your name and ID exactly: passport vs NDEBConnect profile, including Turkish characters

Protocol rule: 'Acceptable forms of government-issued photo identification are: driver's licence, passport, or provincial photo identification card. The ID must be in English or French. Photocopies and digital images will not be accepted. Photo identification must show your name exactly as it appears in your online profile and must not be expired.'

What to do:
- Bring the original Turkish passport, valid on exam day. You also need it to enter Canada/US.
- The Turkish passport data page is labelled in Turkish/English/French, so it should meet the 'English or French' rule. Confirm with NDEB if unsure.
- Your profile name must match the passport character for character, including middle names and surname order.
- Turkish letters: the visual zone shows ş/ğ/ı/İ/ç/ö/ü, but the machine-readable line uses S/G/I/C/O/U. NDEB publishes no rule on diacritics. Before registering, email info@ndeb-bned.ca with a scan of your passport data page. - Keep the name identical across all your documents: notarized passport copy, Yeditepe transcript, NDEBConnect, Prometric.
- Do not rely on your Turkish national ID card. The Protocol's list names a passport, and Prometric's general rule (an unverified hint) is that outside your country of citizenship 'a valid passport is required'.

## STEP 6: Request test accommodations, medication or medical equipment early, if needed
TIMING: Accommodation requests: as early as possible, at the latest when you register. Medication/equipment notice: at least 15 business days before the exam.
Per NDEB: 'Candidates can request a test accommodation due to a documented disability, medical diagnosis, or for a religious reason.' Send inquiries to accommodations@ndeb-bned.ca, using the Test Accommodation Request Form plus specialist documentation.
- Learning disability: psychological assessment less than 4 years old.
- ADD/ADHD: evaluation after age 18 and within the past 4 years.
- Anxiety and other mental-health disorders: documentation less than 1 year old. NDEB states 'Exam anxiety does not lead to a test accommodation.'
- Possible accommodations include a private or semi-private room and 'Additional time, to a maximum of time and a half'.
- Snacks are allowed only by accommodation: 'Food is not permitted in the exam room at Prometric Testing Centres and a separate room is required.'

Medication or medical equipment (e.g., cushion, wrist guard, breast pump) needs no form, but 'you must notify the NDEB in writing, within 15 business days of the examination date… must be approved by the NDEB prior to the examination.'
- For 1 Mar 2027, 15 business days before is about 8 Feb 2027 (earlier if Ontario's Family Day, 15 Feb, is excluded). Send it by 1 Feb.
- For 20 May 2027, it is about 29 Apr 2027. Send it by 22 Apr.

Religious accommodation: 'A candidate unable to participate in an examination on the scheduled day due to religious doctrines, may request to participate on a different day'. A prayer space may be provided 'where available'.

## STEP 7: Learn the exam format and scoring rules, then build your answering strategy
TIMING: Week 1 of study. Re-read the Protocol in the final week.
**Format.** 'The Virtual OSCE is a computer-based examination consisting of approximately 200 questions in a single day. There are approximately 50 standard single-answer multiple-choice questions and approximately 150 case-based questions. The examination is six hours in duration with one scheduled break.' That is about 1.8 minutes per question.

**Single-answer questions** ('Select ONE correct answer'): 'There is no penalty for incorrect answers. You should always answer a question, even when you are unsure.'

**Multi-answer questions** ('Select ONE OR MORE correct answers'): 'you must select all the correct answers to receive a score of 1. If you select one or more incorrect answers, you will receive a score of 0 for the question'. Partial credit goes to a subset of correct answers with no wrong ones (Protocol example: 2 of 3 correct = 0.66). Strategy: select only the options you are sure of. One wrong click wipes out the whole item.

**Prescription questions:** 'Case-based short-answer questions will require you to complete prescriptions for a medication commonly prescribed by general dentists in Canada. These questions are worth four marks.' On repeats: 'For no repeat enter 0.' Practise complete Canadian prescriptions: drug, strength, dosage form, quantity (Disp), directions (Sig), repeats.

**Conventions:**
- FDI two-digit numbering (e.g., 2.6, 8.5).
- Probing depths over 3 mm shown in red.
- Recession positive = margin apical to the CEJ.
- Miller mobility classes.
- Furcation classes I–III.
- Endo chart: EPT maximum 80; cold/heat ++/+/-/Normal, Short/Lingering.
- Ortho chart compares the patient with the 'Average measurement for a North American patient population'.
- Images are 'displayed anatomically, as if you are looking at the patient'.

**Definitions to learn verbatim** from Protocol p.14–15: differential diagnosis (up to three), immediate management, initial treatment/management, investigations, management, most appropriate management, most likely, next step.

## STEP 8: Study plan A: about 17 weeks to the 1 Mar 2027 sitting
TIMING: 3 Nov 2026 – 28 Feb 2027. Only realistic if the application is approved and a seat opens in the January second-registration window.
Weighting follows the official blueprint:
- Operative 16±5%
- Periodontics 14±5%
- Oral Medicine & Pathology 12±5%
- Pain 12±5%
- Medical emergencies & medically complex patients 12±5%
- Endodontics 10±5%
- Prosthodontics 10±5%
- Surgery 8±5%
- Orthodontics 6±5%
Per the Protocol, radiology and dental anesthesia are spread across all categories. Pediatric patients appear in every category ('patients across all age groups as well as patients with special needs').

Routine: about 6–7 hours a day, 6 days a week. Each discipline week = reference reading + all matching Frameworks + timed questions.

Weekly schedule:
- **Wk1 (3–9 Nov):** Read the Protocol and Frameworks intro. Do a timed diagnostic on the NDEB Released Question Bank. Self-audit against the KSA list. Start the application paperwork in parallel.
- **Wk2–3 (10–23 Nov), Operative + cariology/prevention + materials:** Sturdevant's (Ritter 2026, 8th ed.), Summitt's, Craig's (2026, 15th ed.), Powers & Wataha. AAPD caries-risk and pediatric restorative guidelines. Frameworks 20–26 and 71–75 (bitewing caries/defects/calculus, recall radiograph intervals).
- **Wk4–5 (24 Nov–7 Dec), Periodontics:** 2017 AAP/EFP classification, staging and grading (Caton 2018). Newman & Carranza (14th ed.), Lindhe's. Perio chart reading. Frameworks 45–56.
- **Wk6 (8–14 Dec), Endodontics:** AAE Consensus diagnostic terminology. Cohen's Pathways (12th ed.), Torabinejad. Endo chart reading. Frameworks 1–4 and 41–44.
- **Wk7 (15–21 Dec), Pain:** Okeson TMD (9th ed.), AAOM chronic orofacial pain guide, Sharav & Benoliel. Malamed Handbook of Local Anesthesia. Analgesic regimens and prescriptions from CPS.
- **Wk8–9 (22 Dec–4 Jan), Oral Medicine/Pathology + radiology:** Neville (5th ed.), Burket's (13th ed.), White & Pharoah (Lam & Mallya 2026, 9th ed.), Langlais exercises. Frameworks 5–18 and 83–85. Make sure all application documents arrive by 5 Jan.
- **Wk10 (5–11 Jan), Medical emergencies & medically complex patients:** Malamed Medical Emergencies (8th ed.), Little & Falace (2024). AHA 2021 viridans-strep IE prophylaxis statement, AHA BP categories, AHA ACLS (2025). Write 20+ prescriptions.
- **Wk11 (12–18 Jan), Prosthodontics:** Rosenstiel (6th ed.), Shillingburg, McCracken (2026, 14th ed.), Zarb (13th ed.), Glossary of Prosthodontic Terms. Frameworks 57–70. Watch the second-registration window and the 18 Jan deadline.
- **Wk12 (19–25 Jan), Surgery:** Hupp (7th ed.), Peterson's (Miloro 2022), Fragiskos, AAOMS parameters. Fascial spaces, cranial nerves, extraction risk factors, anticoagulated patients. Frameworks 76–92.
- **Wk13 (26 Jan–1 Feb), Orthodontics + pediatrics + trauma:** Proffit (2025, 7th ed.), McDonald & Avery (11th ed.), IADT 2020 trauma guidelines (3 parts), AAPD pulp therapy/prophylaxis/xylitol policies. Cephalometrics (SNA/SNB), Moyers analysis, space maintainers. Frameworks 30–40. Send any medication notice to NDEB.
- **Wk14–15 (2–15 Feb):** Mixed case blocks by framework number. Professionalism/ethics (ACD Ethics Handbook), consent (RCDSO; CDSPI consent form), infection control (ODQ guideline), epidemiology/evidence (Fletcher). Full 6-hour mock #1: 200 questions with one break, under exam conditions with no watch.
- **Wk16 (16–22 Feb):** Weak-area review. Mock #2. Orientation video plus platform test.
- **Wk17 (23–28 Feb):** Light review only. Fly to the exam city at least 2 days before 1 Mar. Sleep.

## STEP 9: Study plan B (recommended): about 28 weeks to the 20 May 2027 sitting
TIMING: 3 Nov 2026 – 20 May 2027. Key dates: 26 Jan 2027 (register), about 22 Apr (medical notice), 20 May (exam).
**Phase 1, foundation (3 Nov – 27 Dec 2026, 8 weeks).** Same discipline order as Plan A, but slower: one discipline per week, about 4–5 hours a day.
- Read the core reference chapters.
- Make a one-page summary per Framework, listing which answer options fit which clinical picture.
- Finish all application documents so they arrive well before NDEB's deadline. The aim is approval before 26 Jan 2027.

**Phase 2, applied case practice (28 Dec 2026 – 28 Mar 2027, 13 weeks).**
- Work through all 102 Frameworks against cases. Run timed mixed blocks of 50 questions three times a week.
- Prescription drills: 2 a day (analgesics, antibiotics including penicillin-allergy alternatives, antifungals, chlorhexidine, fluoride).
- Medical-emergency algorithms every week.
- Canadian context: consent, ethics, infection control, record keeping.
- **26 Jan 2027, 10:00 ET (18:00 TRT): register the minute first registration opens**, then book Prometric when NDEB emails you.

**Phase 3, simulation (29 Mar – 16 May 2027, 7 weeks).**
- One full 6-hour, about 200-question mock each week under exam rules: no watch, water only, one break.
- Review every error and track it by blueprint category, and by error type (multi-answer over-selection vs knowledge gap).
- Send any medication or equipment notice to NDEB by about 22 Apr.
- Watch the Orientation video and test the platform in the week of 10 May.
- Travel about 16–17 May.
- 18–19 May: light review, visit the test centre, rest.

## STEP 12: Exam-day checklist (follow exactly)
TIMING: Exam day: 1 Mar 2027 (Mon) or 20 May 2027 (Thu). Pack the night before.
**Bring:**
1. Original, unexpired Turkish passport, with the name exactly as in NDEBConnect. 'Photocopies and digital images will not be accepted.'
2. Printed NDEB information memo, the Prometric confirmation (if Prometric), and any NDEB email approving medication, equipment or your name spelling.
3. A transparent water bottle with the label removed. 'No other beverages are permitted.'
4. Plain clothes in layers you can wear in the room. No hat, scarf, coat, outdoor clothing, hoodie with the hood up, or bulky clothing, unless it is religious attire.
5. Pre-approved medication or medical equipment only.

**Leave in the locker or designated area (no access during the break):**
- Phone, tablet, laptop, smartwatch, any device that records or transmits.
- 'All watches are prohibited'. Train yourself to use the on-screen clock.
- Jewellery other than a wedding ring, including 'ornate hair accessories'.
- Food. 'Food is not allowed to be consumed in the examination room.'
- Your own earplugs. 'Ear plugs will be provided.'

**At check-in:**
- Staff may inspect glasses and jewellery, possibly each time you re-enter.
- At Prometric: 'you may be scanned with a metal detector wand, fingerprinted, required to raise your pant legs above your ankles, empty and turn all pockets inside-out, and raise shirt sleeves above your wrists'.
- 'You must empty all pockets before entering or leaving the examination room.'

**In the room:**
- 'You will be required to read and agree to a Confidentiality and Non-disclosure Agreement before you can begin the examination.'
- 'You will be provided with scratch paper, a pencil, and an eraser. You cannot write on anything other than the scratch paper provided', and you hand them back at the end.
- 'You cannot leave and re-enter the examination room unattended'.
- 6 hours, about 200 questions, one scheduled break. Pace yourself at about 33 questions per hour.
- Short technical delays are already 'accounted for' in the time.
- Answer every single-answer item.
- On multi-answer items, select only the options you are sure of.
- Fill in every field of a prescription (4 marks), and enter 0 for no repeats.
- If something irregular happens, such as a system crash or noise, 'it must be immediately reported to staff at the examination centre'.

**Afterwards:** say nothing about the content. 'You are prohibited from communicating, publishing, reproducing, or transmitting any part of your examination'. NDEB investigates breaches.

## STEP 13: If you become ill or an emergency happens: withdraw correctly or appeal
TIMING: Withdraw before the exam starts. Appeals within 7 days after the exam.
**Before the exam starts.** Per the Protocol: 'If you experience a health-related issue or personal circumstance prior to the start of an examination that will prevent you from demonstrating your ability, you must withdraw. If you do not attend the examination, you will not lose an attempt. Login to your NDEBConnect account to withdraw… All withdrawals are subject to a fee.'
Withdrawal fees (Withdraw page and 13 Aug 2026 news):
- Before the registration deadline: 50% of the fee (CAD 875).
- After the registration deadline, or if you do not show up: 100% (CAD 1,750).
- 'Withdrawal for serious medical circumstances or unanticipated exceptional circumstances': 25% (CAD 437.50), with documentation per the By-laws withdrawal policy.
Withdrawal is processed instantly in NDEBConnect. You may re-register elsewhere if seats exist.

**During the exam.**
- Medical problem: you may submit a compassionate appeal 'to have the results of the examination voided… within seven days of the examination' through NDEBConnect.
- Problem with how the exam was run: report it immediately on site. Then appeal through NDEBConnect 'within seven days of the examination'.

## STEP 14: Get results, request a score check if needed, then the certificate
TIMING: Results within 8 weeks of the exam. Score verification within 1 month of release. Certificate by mail after that (timing not published).
**Results.** Per the Protocol: 'Results are normally released within eight weeks of the examination. You will receive an email notification when your results are available in your online profile. Results will be reported as a Pass/Fail. Those who receive less than 75 will also receive their test equated rescaled score. Results will not be released by telephone, email, or fax.' To view them, log in to NDEBConnect and select 'results'.
Expected release:
- 1 Mar 2027 sitting: by about 26 Apr 2027.
- 20 May 2027 sitting: by about 15 Jul 2027.

**Passing standard.** 'You must obtain a minimum test equated, re-scaled score of 75'. Per the Scoring page: 'An examinee does not need to answer 75 percent of the questions correctly… NDEB examinations are not designed to pass or fail a pre-determined percentage of examinees.' 2025 outcome: 1,576 candidates, 90.36% pass; first-time takers 1,313 of 1,441.

**If you fail.** 'you have one month from the date results are released to request a verification of your score… In no case will you have access to the examination questions or responses.' The maximum is three attempts.

**If you pass.** Per the Registering page: 'we will verify that we have received your proof of graduation. When successful completion of the Virtual OSCE and proof of graduation are confirmed you can be certified. Your certificate will be mailed to the address' in your profile. Keep your NDEBConnect mailing address current; a Turkish address works, but use a courier-safe one. 'Once certified you can apply for licensure in the province of your choice.' For BC, apply to BCCOHP within 3 years of the certificate date.

## RISKS
1. **Seats may sell out before you can register.** First registration closes when seats fill, and you can only register once your application is approved. Your realistic March 2027 route is the second window, about 4–18 Jan 2027, which only works if seats free up. The May 2027 first-registration opening (26 Jan 2027, 10:00 ET / 18:00 TRT) needs approval before that date. Missing it pushes you into the May second window, or to 22 Nov 2027 (Canada only).

2. **Name and ID mismatch can mean you are turned away.** The Protocol requires the name on your ID to match your profile 'exactly', and ID must be in English or French. Turkish characters (ş, ğ, ı/İ, ç, ö, ü) in the passport's visual zone vs ASCII spellings in NDEBConnect or Prometric are a real risk. Get written confirmation from NDEB. A name correction needs a paper form sent by mail or courier, which is slow from Turkey.

3. **Multi-answer over-selection.** One wrong option makes the whole item 0. Candidates used to 'select all that apply' partial-credit formats lose many marks this way.

4. **Prescription items (4 marks each).** These use Canadian conventions (CPS drug names, Disp/Sig/Repeats, '0' for no repeat). If you are used to Turkish e-reçete conventions, practise until it is automatic.

5. **Canadian/North American standards.** AHA 2021 infective-endocarditis prophylaxis, the 2017 AAP/EFP perio classification, AAE diagnostic terms, IADT 2020 trauma guidelines, and Canadian consent and infection-control norms. Ortho norms use the 'North American patient population' mean.

6. **No watch allowed**, and devices cannot be touched during the break. Practise timing with an on-screen clock only, at about 33 questions per hour.

7. **Late arrival** 'may be denied entry'. Prometric's general rule (not NDEB-confirmed) is 30 minutes early. Flight delays from Turkey: arrive 2 days ahead.

8. **Withdrawal costs.** After the registration deadline, withdrawing or not showing up costs 100% of the fee (CAD 1,750). The medical/exceptional rate is 25% and needs documentation. Withdraw before the exam starts; once you sit it, only a compassionate appeal (within 7 days) can void the result.

9. **Medication or medical equipment** needs written NDEB approval about 15 business days before the exam. Food in the room needs an accommodation.

10. **Recall material is prohibited.** Buying or using 'recall' or reconstructed exam questions breaks NDEB rules and can lead to sanctions. Use only official materials plus reputable commercial practice banks.

11. **Results timing affects BC.** Results take up to 8 weeks, and the certificate is mailed only after proof of graduation (the Yeditepe transcript with conferral date) is confirmed. Send the transcript early so it does not delay the certificate. BC requires the certificate to be no more than 3 years old at application.

12. **Attempts are capped at 3 in total.**

13. **Rules can change.** NDEB 'may introduce new regulations or modify existing regulations'. Re-check the Protocol and your registration's information memo in the last 2 weeks before the exam.
