# turkey_documents

TOPIC: Producing the NDEB Certification Process documents from Turkey (Yeditepe DDS graduate, accredited-programme category): passport copy notarised in English, sworn affidavits, Yeditepe transcript sent directly to NDEB and NDEB's check with the university, Turkish good-standing letter, and the criminal record needed later for BC. Step-by-step from 3 Nov 2026, aiming at the 1 Mar 2027 Virtual OSCE.


## STEP 1: Open NDEBConnect today, get your NDEB ID and set a target date for all documents
TIMING: Today, Tue 3 Nov 2026 (about 1 hour).
1) Create the NDEBConnect account using the 'CREATE ACCOUNT' link in Step 4 of https://ndeb-bned.ca/certification-process/new-how-to-apply/. Choose the category 'Received a BDS/DDS/DMD from an accredited dental program within the last 5 years' and select Yeditepe University as your university. NDEB uses that choice for its 'Verification of Education with University'.
2) Type your legal name exactly as it appears on your Turkish passport, including Turkish letters (Ş, Ğ, İ, Ç, Ö, Ü) and any second given name. NDEB: 'your document must show your name exactly as it appears on your online profile'.
3) Pay the application fee. NDEB: 'You will be required to pay the application fee before you can upload your photograph and finish setting up your account.' Then upload the photo.
4) Write down your NDEB ID. Every third party (Ministry of Health, Yeditepe) must quote it.
5) Download the Certification Process Form shown in your portal. Use the version the portal gives for the 2027 exam cycle. Do not sign it yet (see step 8).
6) Targets: the official deadline is 5 Jan 2027, by which date documents must be RECEIVED for the 1 Mar 2027 exam. Aim to have every document at NDEB by about 4 Dec 2026. Canadian holiday closures and courier delays fall in late December, and only an approved application can register for the VOSCE before 18 Jan 2027, 21:00 ET.
SOURCES: https://ndeb-bned.ca/certification-process/new-how-to-apply/

## STEP 2: Work out whether Turkey counts you as 'licensed': good-standing letter, gap affidavit, or both
TIMING: 3–6 Nov 2026 (e-Devlet check is about 15 minutes; NDEB reply usually takes days).
NDEB asks for two different things:
(a) Statements of Good Standing 'issued by all licensing bodies in which you were licensed'.
(b) A gap affidavit: 'If after completing your dental education in any country, you were not licensed for a period of twelve (12) months or more, you must submit an original sworn affidavit attesting to the fact that you were not licensed, including the reason.'
In Turkey, the basic authorisation is the Ministry of Health's diploma registration (diploma tescili). Membership of a dental chamber (dişhekimleri odası, under TDB Law 3224) is also needed to practise. No official NDEB statement was found on whether diploma tescil alone counts as being 'licensed'.
Do the following:
1) Check on e-Devlet (turkiye.gov.tr, Sağlık Bakanlığı diploma tescil / Doktor Bilgi Bankası record) whether your diploma is registered and on what date. Find your exact degree conferral date (mezuniyet tarihi).
2) Decide:
- Diploma registered (and/or a chamber registration): order the Ministry's 'İyi Hal (Good Standing) Belgesi' (step 4). If you were ever a chamber member or practised, also ask that chamber for a letter (no official procedure was found).
- Never registered and 12 months or more since conferral: a gap affidavit is required (steps 6–7).
- Registered, but 12 months or more passed between conferral and registration: do both.
- Unsure: do both. An extra affidavit costs only CAD 50 at the consulate.
3) Email verification@ndeb-bned.ca (or book a 15-minute credential-verification appointment) with your NDEB ID. Ask: 'Does registration of my diploma with the Turkish Ministry of Health count as licensure? Do I need a Statement of Good Standing from the Ministry and/or a gap affidavit?'
SOURCES: https://ndeb-bned.ca/certification-process/new-how-to-apply/ ; https://disab.saglik.gov.tr/TR-77584/iyi-hal-good-standing-belgesi.html

## STEP 3: Ask the Yeditepe Registrar to send an official English final transcript in a sealed envelope directly to NDEB
TIMING: Submit 4–6 Nov 2026. Allow about 1–2 weeks processing (estimate, not published) plus 3–7 business days DHL/UPS/FedEx courier to Ottawa. Follow up by phone after 7 days. Target arrival at NDEB by about 25 Nov 2026.
Office: Öğrenci Kayıt İşlemleri Müdürlüğü (Directorate of Registrar's Office), ground floor of the Rectorate building, Kayışdağı campus, İnönü Mah. Kayışdağı Cad. 326A, 26 Ağustos Yerleşimi, 34755 Ataşehir/İstanbul. 'Our Directorate serves on the ground floor of the Rectorate building between 08.30-17.45 on weekdays.' Phone 0216 578 00 00 (Undergraduate Registrar ext. 1294 / 1292 / 1233 / 1286 / 1227 / 1285). General email info@yeditepe.edu.tr.
No published Yeditepe online system, fee, courier rule or processing time was found for alumni transcripts sent abroad. Go in person with a written, wet-signed petition (dilekçe) and get a stamped receipt (evrak kayıt numarası).
Petition text (Turkish):
'YEDİTEPE ÜNİVERSİTESİ ÖĞRENCİ KAYIT İŞLEMLERİ MÜDÜRLÜĞÜ'NE — Üniversiteniz Diş Hekimliği Fakültesi'nden [gg.aa.yyyy] tarihinde mezun oldum (Öğrenci No: …, T.C. Kimlik No: …). Kanada Ulusal Diş Hekimliği Sınav Kurulu (NDEB) sertifikasyon başvurum (NDEB ID: …) için; derecenin verildiği tarihi (date of degree conferral) açıkça gösteren İngilizce resmi final transkriptimin (official final transcript), üniversitenizce imzalı ve mühürlü olarak, kapağı kapatılıp kapak yeri üniversitece imzalanmış/mühürlenmiş kapalı zarf içinde DOĞRUDAN üniversiteniz tarafından aşağıdaki adrese kurye ile gönderilmesini; gönderi takip numarasının tarafıma e-posta ile bildirilmesini arz ederim. Kargo/belge ücreti tarafımca ödenecektir. Adres: The National Dental Examining Board of Canada – Credential Verification, 340 Albert Street, 12th Floor, Ottawa, Ontario K1R 7Y6, Canada. Tel: +1 613 236 5912. E-posta: verification@ndeb-bned.ca'.
What NDEB checks:
- 'An original official transcript of marks be sent directly from your university to the NDEB in a sealed envelope.'
- 'A final transcript must indicate the date the degree was conferred. An interim transcript of marks or letter will not include the date of conferral and is not acceptable.'
- The transcript must be a complete record of all years with attendance dates.
Also ask:
(i) Whether Yeditepe uses MyCreds, Parchment, National Student Clearinghouse or My eQuals. These are the only electronic routes NDEB accepts, sent to verification@ndeb-bned.ca. Probably none applies.
(ii) The fee, how to pay, which courier, and the processing time.
Do NOT take the sealed envelope yourself and post it. NDEB requires it to be sent directly by the university. The Europass Diploma Supplement (free, issued with the diploma) and e-Devlet YÖK graduation documents do NOT replace the transcript. Keep them for later (BC).

## STEP 4: If your diploma is registered: ask the Ministry of Health to email its İyi Hal (Letter of Good Standing) directly to NDEB
TIMING: Post 4–6 Nov 2026 (arrives in Ankara in 1–2 days). Processing time is not published (estimate 2–6 weeks). Email disab.eh@saglik.gov.tr if no news by about 25 Nov 2026.
Issuer: T.C. Sağlık Bakanlığı – Avrupa Birliği ve Dış İlişkiler Genel Müdürlüğü (EU and Foreign Relations directorate, not SHGM). Its page (dated 09 Ekim 2026) says the letter is issued to health professionals 'diploma tescili Sağlık Bakanlığınca yapılmış' (whose diploma the Ministry has registered). The letter shows the registration details and whether any disciplinary penalty was received.
Procedure, as stated on the page:
1) A WET-SIGNED petition addressed to the General Directorate containing: 'Yurtdışında kullanılmak üzere İyi Hal Belgesi (Letter of Good Standing) talep etmekteyim'.
2) State how you want it delivered. For email, the address of the receiving institution is mandatory. Write: 'Belgenin İngilizce olarak, doğrudan verification@ndeb-bned.ca adresine, e-posta konusunda NDEB ID: [numara] yazılarak, imzalayan yetkilinin adı ve unvanı belirtilerek gönderilmesini; bir nüshasının da [kendi e-postanız] adresine iletilmesini talep ederim.'
3) Include the sentence 'Dilekçe ve eklerinde beyan ettiğim bilgi ve belgelerin doğruluğunu taahhüt ederim.'
4) Include your phone and email.
5) Attach an A4 copy of your T.C. Kimlik and an A4 copy of your diploma.
6) The directorate does not do apostilles. NDEB does not need one.
Post it by PTT/courier, with 'T.C. Sağlık Bakanlığı' written on the envelope, to: Üniversiteler Mahallesi Şehit Mehmet Bayraktar Caddesi No:3, Çankaya/Ankara 06800. Contact: disab.eh@saglik.gov.tr.
NDEB's side: the statement must come 'from an official, traceable email account, and include the name and position of the appropriate authority… Please request that your NDEB identification number be included in the subject of the email.' A @saglik.gov.tr address meets the 'official account' rule.
Note: 'Başvuru sahiplerinin, İyi Hal Belgesi … aldıktan sonra 3 ay geçmeden yeni belge talepleri dikkate alınmayacaktır.' A new request within 3 months of receiving one is ignored, so get the wording right the first time.
If you were a member of a dental chamber, ask that chamber (e.g. İstanbul Dişhekimleri Odası) for an English letter emailed from its official domain to verification@ndeb-bned.ca. No official procedure was found.
SOURCES: https://disab.saglik.gov.tr/TR-77584/iyi-hal-good-standing-belgesi.html ; https://ndeb-bned.ca/certification-process/new-how-to-apply/

## STEP 5: Book the Canadian Consulate in Istanbul (or Embassy in Ankara) for the passport copy and affidavit — safest route
TIMING: Email and book 3–5 Nov 2026. Appointment ideally by about 20 Nov 2026 (availability unknown, usually 1–3 weeks). If nothing is free before about 27 Nov, try the other mission.
Why not a Turkish notary? NDEB: 'The notary statement must be in English or French. Translated statements are not accepted.' and 'The original seal must display the notarizing officer's credentials, including full name and title… in English or French. Translated seals will not be accepted.'
Turkish notaries certify copies under Notary Law 1512, Arts. 96–99. The law works in Turkish: Art. 74 requires a sworn interpreter if the party does not know Turkish, and Art. 99 says a foreign-language paper is first translated. No provision was found that lets a noter issue the certification and seal in English. The usual Turkish fix (sworn translation of the Turkish onay şerhi plus apostille) is exactly what NDEB rejects. A Turkish lawyer is not a safe substitute either: Avukatlık Kanunu Art. 56 lets lawyers certify copies only of documents in cases they handle, for submission to courts and justice offices.
NDEB's explicit fallback: 'If you are unable to have document(s) notarized by a notarizing officer whose credentials are in English or French, you may have an Officer of a Canadian Consulate or Embassy, or a New Zealand Consulate or Embassy make a notarized photocopy of your document(s).'
How:
1) Email ISTBL-Consular@international.gc.ca (Istanbul) or ANKRA-Consular@international.gc.ca (Ankara). The official text: 'Please send your inquiries on notarial and apostille services to…'.
Suggested email: 'I am a Turkish citizen applying to the National Dental Examining Board of Canada (NDEB ID …). NDEB requires a colour photocopy of my passport certified as a true copy by an officer whose statement and seal are in English or French, and an original sworn affidavit in English. Turkish notaries certify only in Turkish, and NDEB does not accept translated statements, so no local provider can meet this requirement. NDEB's guidelines name Canadian consular officers as the alternative (link). Could you certify 2 true copies of my passport and administer the oath on my affidavit? Please confirm the fees and the documents to bring.'
Global Affairs says these services are offered 'only when no local service providers exist' and only 'if local notarial services are unavailable', so this explanation matters.
2) Book online: Istanbul https://cts-ca.anzus.solutions/?customer=embassyca&wf=appointments&action=schedule&locationId=ISTBL ; Ankara same link with locationId=ANKRA. Official text: 'Once in Türkiye you can arrange in-person consular services at the Embassy of Canada in Ankara or the Consulate of Canada in Istanbul by making an appointment online.'
3) Fees (CAD, credit card via the mission's credit card authorisation form):
- 'Certifying a true copy of a document. The fee shown is per copy.' CAD 20.00
- 'Administering an oath or receiving a statutory declaration or affirmation' CAD 50.00 per document
- 'Attesting any document bearing a signature (affidavit, declaration, affirmation, etc.)' CAD 50.00
- If they prepare a certificate under 200 words: CAD 30.00
Expected total for 2 copies plus 1–2 affidavits: about CAD 90–170.
Addresses: Consulate General of Canada, Tekfen Tower, Büyükdere Caddesi No:209, 4. Levent, İstanbul 34394. Embassy: Cinnah Caddesi 58, 06690 Çankaya, Ankara.
New Zealand Embassy Ankara (Kızkulesi Sokak No.11, Gaziosmanpaşa; NZEmbassyAnkara@mfat.govt.nz; M–F 09:00–12:30 and 13:30–16:00) is a weak backup only. Its page limits notarial help to documents 'for use under New Zealand law or within Türkiye'.

## STEP 6: Draft the English affidavit(s) yourself before the appointment, and leave them unsigned
TIMING: Draft 6–15 Nov 2026 (2–3 hours). Optionally email the draft to verification@ndeb-bned.ca for a sanity check.
Consular officials 'cannot … draft or modify legal/notarial documents', so bring a finished, typed English text and sign it only in front of the officer. NDEB definition: 'Original sworn affidavit means the applicant's written sworn statement in English or French that is legally binding, signed and sealed, or stamped by a Notary Public, lawyer, or Commissioner of Oaths. Seals or stamps must be original and must display the credentials … in English or French.'
(A) Gap-in-licensing affidavit (template): 'AFFIDAVIT. I, [FULL NAME as in passport], born [date] in [place], citizen of the Republic of Türkiye, Turkish passport No. [..], NDEB ID [..], residing at [address], MAKE OATH AND SAY (or SOLEMNLY AFFIRM): 1. I was awarded the degree of Doctor of Dental Surgery (Diş Hekimi) by Yeditepe University, Faculty of Dentistry, Istanbul, Türkiye, on [conferral date]. 2. From [conferral date] to the date of this affidavit I have not been licensed to practise dentistry in Türkiye or in any other jurisdiction [OR: my diploma was registered by the Ministry of Health of Türkiye on [date]; between [date] and [date] I was not licensed]. 3. The reason I was not licensed is: [e.g., I did not register with a dental chamber or practise because I have been preparing for the NDEB Certification Process / further study / other]. 4. I make this affidavit for submission to the National Dental Examining Board of Canada and for no improper purpose. SWORN (AFFIRMED) before me at Istanbul, Türkiye, on [date] … [signature of deponent].' Leave space for the officer's jurat, seal and signature.
(B) Name-difference affidavit, only if your name differs anywhere (e.g., transcript without the second given name, Turkish letters transliterated, surname change). NDEB requires 'your previous name in full, your current name, reason for change/difference of name, date of change/difference of name'. It can be a separate affidavit or a paragraph in (A) stating 'The names [X] and [Y] refer to one and the same person, me; the difference is due to [reason]'.
Sign exactly as in your passport. NDEB: 'You must sign ALL documentation with the NDEB in the same manner as you have signed the government issued identification.'
SOURCES: https://ndeb-bned.ca/certification-process/new-how-to-apply/ ; https://travel.gc.ca/travelling/documents/notarial-services

## STEP 7: At the consulate: get the certified passport copies and swear the affidavit(s), then check every item before leaving
TIMING: At the appointment, ideally 10–20 Nov 2026 (about 1 hour on site).
Bring:
- Original passport. It must be 'valid (not expired)', 'issued by a federal… government authority' and 'no more than 10 years since issued', and show name, date of birth, photo and signature. If the signature appears on a page other than the data page, ask for that page to be included.
- T.C. kimlik.
- The unsigned affidavit(s).
- Credit card or the completed credit card authorisation form.
- The NDEB criteria printed out.
NDEB requires:
- 'The notarizing officer must make a colour photocopy of the original document. Do not photocopy the document yourself.' Let the officer make the copy.
- 'The notarizing officer places their original seal and signature in ink on the copy of the document and state that the photocopy is a true copy of the original document seen by the notarizing officer.'
- 'No original stamps or signatures other than the notarizing officer's should be on the photocopy.'
- 'If the notarial statement is on a separate page, the Notary must be the one to attach the notarial certificate to the document.'
Before leaving, check that each copy has: (1) a statement in English that it is a true copy of the original seen by the officer; (2) the officer's printed full name and title, e.g. 'Consular Officer, Consulate General of Canada, Istanbul'; (3) an original ink signature and seal; (4) colour.
Order 2 certified copies (CAD 20 each): one for NDEB and one spare in case the courier loses the package. Copies are not reused later. BC (BCCOHP) will set its own rules.
SOURCES: https://ndeb-bned.ca/certification-process/new-how-to-apply/ ; https://www.international.gc.ca/country-pays/turkiye/istanbul-info.aspx?lang=eng

## STEP 10: Check NDEBConnect weekly, fix any problems, then register for the 1 Mar 2027 Virtual OSCE
TIMING: Weekly from mid-Nov 2026. Register as soon as approved and before 18 Jan 2027.
Each document has a status in NDEBConnect. If something is marked deficient (e.g., the transcript lacks a conferral date, or a signature does not match), act the same day. Ask the Registrar to re-issue, or book a second consular appointment.
Once the application is approved ('If you have an approved application, you can register for the Virtual OSCE'), register for the 1 Mar 2027 exam. US/Canada registration opened 3 Nov 2026 10:00 ET and closes 18 Jan 2027 21:00 ET.
Fallback: if anything misses 5 Jan 2027, the next deadlines are 5 Apr 2027 for documents and 26 Jan–19 Apr 2027 for registration, for the 20 May 2027 exam. Ask NDEB whether documents already received carry over.
Keep the visitor-visa planning separate. Apply only after a seat is confirmed.
SOURCES: https://ndeb-bned.ca/certification-process/new-how-to-apply/

## STEP 11: Later, for BC only: English criminal record (Adli Sicil Kaydı) with e-Apostil
TIMING: After NDEB certification, when preparing the BCCOHP application (BC needs the NDEB certificate to be no more than 3 years old). e-Devlet English record: about 10 minutes. e-Apostil: same day to a few days.
Not needed for NDEB. Get it only when BCCOHP asks, because freshness matters.
The Ministry of Justice issues the record from 'Cumhuriyet başsavcılıkları nezdindeki adli sicil birimlerinden, yurt dışı temsilciliklerden ve e-Devlet üzerinden… 40 dilde', English included. Barcoded e-Devlet PDFs can be checked at turkiye.gov.tr (adli sicil belge doğrulama).
Apostille: Ministry of Justice: 'e-Apostil sisteminin başlamasıyla beraber adli sicil kaydı ve mahkeme kararlarına ilişkin belgeler e-Apostil tasdikli olarak alınabiliyor' (via eapostil.gov.tr with an e-Devlet login). If BCCOHP wants a wet-signed copy, the courthouse adli sicil office plus an apostille from the Adalet Komisyonu is the paper route.
Canada is now an Apostille country: 'the entry into force of the Apostille Convention in Canada on January 11, 2024'.
Also keep for BC: the original diploma, the Europass Diploma Supplement, and the spare consular passport copy.

## RISKS
1) Turkish notary (noter) route: a standard suret onayı has a Turkish statement and a Turkish seal ('… Noteri'). Adding a sworn translation or an apostille does not fix it, because NDEB says 'Translated statements are not accepted' and 'Translated seals will not be accepted'. Using it risks rejection and a missed 5 Jan 2027 deadline. A Turkish avukat certifying the copy is also unsafe: under Avukatlık Kanunu Art. 56, lawyers' certification power covers only case documents for courts.
2) The Canadian consulate may decline, because services are offered 'only when no local service providers exist'. Your email must explain clearly why Turkish notaries cannot meet NDEB's English-credential rule, and quote NDEB's consular clause. Officials 'cannot … draft or modify legal/notarial documents', so bring a finished affidavit text. Appointment availability is unknown; book today and try both Istanbul and Ankara.
3) Copy rules: the officer must make a COLOUR copy personally. No other original stamps may be on it. If the certificate is on a separate page, the officer must attach it. Check every item before leaving the consulate.
4) Signatures: the Certification Process Form, the affidavits and later the exam must all be signed exactly like the passport signature, or NDEB will ask you to resubmit. Your name in NDEBConnect must match the passport exactly, including Turkish letters and any second given name. If Yeditepe's records differ, you need a name-difference affidavit.
5) Transcript: it must be the final transcript showing the conferral date, sent by Yeditepe itself in a sealed envelope or through MyCreds, Parchment, NSC or My eQuals. A hand-carried envelope, an interim transcript, a letter, the e-Devlet YÖK graduation document or the Diploma Supplement will not do. Yeditepe's fee, courier and processing time are unpublished, so follow up actively.
6) Good standing: the Ministry's letter exists only if your diploma is registered (tescil). Its processing time and output language are unpublished. A second request within 3 months is ignored, so the first petition must name verification@ndeb-bned.ca, the NDEB ID for the subject line, English, and the signer's name and title. A Gmail or other personal forward from you is not accepted; it must come from the Ministry's own account.
7) Licensing ambiguity: if NDEB treats diploma tescil as a licence, you need the good-standing letter. If you were unregistered for 12 months or more after conferral, you need the gap affidavit. Doing only one may leave the application incomplete. When in doubt, do both and ask NDEB in writing.
8) Verification of Education: if Yeditepe does not answer NDEB at least 15 days before the exam (14 Feb 2027 for the 1 Mar exam), you can be withdrawn and charged withdrawal fees. Brief the Dean's Office and the Registrar early.
9) Calendar: the deadline is about RECEIPT, not sending. Courier delays, Canadian holidays (late Dec to early Jan) and Turkish public holidays reduce the real window. Aim for complete receipt by about 4 Dec 2026. The VOSCE registration deadline (18 Jan 2027, 21:00 ET) needs an APPROVED application first.
10) Do not spend money on apostilles or translations for the NDEB package; none is required. Do not get the criminal record now; BC will want a recent one later.
