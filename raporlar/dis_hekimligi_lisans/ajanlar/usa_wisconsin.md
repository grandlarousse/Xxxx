# Wisconsin (USA) — ajan bulgusu

Düzenleyici: Wisconsin Dentistry Examining Board (credentialing administered by the Wisconsin Department of Safety and Professional Services, DSPS) — https://dsps.wi.gov/pages/Professions/Dentist/Default.aspx

**Teori-only rota:** no · **Göçmenlik engeli:** low · **Uzaktan sürdürme:** moderate · **Güven:** medium

## Sınav yolları

- **Licensure by Examination (INBDE + board-approved dental testing service exam: ADEX via CDCA-WREB-CITA, CRDTS, SRTA)** (el becerisi: evet): Wis. Stat. 447.04(1)(a): applicant must (3) graduate from an accredited dental school, (4) 'passed the national dental examination and the examination of a dental testing service approved by the examining board', (5) pass the board's statutes-and-rules (jurisprudence) exam, (5m) current CPR/AED proficiency from a DHS-approved provider. Wis. Admin. Code DE 2.01(1)(g): verification from a board-approved testing service of an exam 'taken within one year immediately preceding application'. DE 2.005 requires every approved testing service's exams to '(1) Test clinical knowledge. (2) Include a practical component on application of the basic principles utilizing live human patients or simulated patients.' ADA Licensure-by-State page (updated 20 Oct 2026) lists accepted services as ADEX (CDCA-WREB-CITA), CRDTS and SRTA; 'Parts I and III of ADEX are not required for WREB examinees for examinations taken on or after January 1, 2009.' All are manikin/patient hands-on exams. Applicant must have graduated from an accredited (CODA) school; Yeditepe (post 1 Feb 2024) qualifies as 'accredited dental school' but confirm with DSPS.
- **Marquette University School of Dentistry (MUSD) graduate exemption** (el becerisi: evet): On 12 July 2023 the Board approved MUSD itself as a 'dental testing service' under 447.04(1)(a)4 / DE 2.005, so MUSD graduates are exempt from a separate regional exam until 12 July 2028 (their in-school clinical assessment counts). Not available to Yeditepe graduates.
- **Licensure by Endorsement (DE 2.04 / Wis. Stat. 447.04(1)(b))** (el becerisi: evet): For a dentist 'licensed in good standing to practice dentistry in another state or territory of the United States or in another country' (statute) / 'any other jurisdiction of the United States or Canada' (DE 2.04). Requires compliance with board rules, CPR/AED, jurisprudence exam (DE 2.04(1)(f)) and the 440.05(2) fee. The full DE 2.04 eligibility text (e.g. whether a prior testing-service clinical exam or years of practice is demanded) was NOT read in this research; marked hands-on because the underlying licence will normally rest on a hands-on exam. A Canadian provincial licence obtained via NDEB Virtual OSCE could in theory be endorsed - unverified, see open questions.
- **Faculty (restricted) licence - Wis. Stat. 447.04(1)(c)** (el becerisi: hayır): Granted to a dentist already licensed in another jurisdiction who has a full-time faculty offer from a Wisconsin dental school; may require an interview; practice limited to educational facilities and ends when employment ends. Not a general licence and needs a prior licence plus US employment - not applicable to the candidate.
- **DLOSCE-only / computer-based OSCE route** (el becerisi: hayır): NOT available. Wisconsin is not on the JCNDE 2026 DLOSCE acceptance list, the ADA page lists only ADEX/CRDTS/SRTA, and DE 2.005(2) requires a practical component on live or simulated patients, which the DLOSCE lacks.
- **PGY-1 residency in lieu of clinical exam** (el becerisi: hayır): NOT available for initial licensure: 447.04(1)(a)4 requires the testing-service exam with no residency alternative. A one-year general practice residency appears only as one option for LATE RENEWAL after 5+ years (DE 2.03(5)(b)3.d) and as CE credit (DE 13.03(5)).

## Teori-only açıklaması
Wis. Stat. 447.04(1)(a)4 (quoted on the ADA page, updated 20 Oct 2026): the applicant 'Submits evidence satisfactory to the examining board that he or she has passed the national dental examination and the examination of a dental testing service approved by the examining board.' Wis. Admin. Code DE 2.005: 'A dental testing service or dental hygiene testing service may be approved if all the testing service's exams meet all of the following requirements: (1) Test clinical knowledge. (2) Include a practical component on application of the basic principles utilizing live human patients or simulated patients.' DE 2.01(1)(g): 'Verification from a board-approved testing service of successful completion of an examination taken within one year immediately preceding application.' The only approved services listed are ADEX (CDCA-WREB-CITA), CRDTS and SRTA (plus MUSD for its own graduates). A computer-only exam cannot meet DE 2.005(2), and no residency, portfolio or DLOSCE alternative exists in statute or rule, so a hands-on exam is unavoidable for a new graduate.

## DLOSCE notları
Wisconsin does not accept the DLOSCE as the clinical exam: it is absent from the JCNDE DLOSCE acceptance list and from the ADA/DSPS list of approved testing services, and DE 2.005(2) requires a practical component with live or simulated patients. No Wisconsin policy was found on the ADEX-integrated DLOSCE component; Wisconsin accepts the ADEX series as a whole (which after Aug 2026 includes the DLOSCE plus the manikin hand-skill parts), so a candidate passing only the DLOSCE component of ADEX would not satisfy DE 2.005/DE 2.01(1)(g). Earlier standalone DLOSCE scores are of no use in Wisconsin. The testing-service exam must also have been taken within one year before application (DE 2.01(1)(g)).

PGY-1 kabul: False

## Yüz yüze adımlar
(1) Hands-on testing-service exam (ADEX/CRDTS/SRTA) must be sat in person at a US (or Canadian) test site within one year before applying - feasible on a visitor visa but it is hands-on, which the candidate wants to avoid. (2) Application itself is 100% online via LicensE (license.wi.gov); upload documents, pay fees, enter 999999999 as SSN and upload form #1051 (sworn no-SSN statement - whether notarisation is needed was not verifiable because the PDF could not be read). (3) Jurisprudence exam: per DSPS credentialing sheet info512 (surfaced in search, not read directly) 'An applicant shall successfully complete an online examination on Wisconsin Statutes and Rules relating to the practice of dentistry before a license can be issued'; exam information is sent after the application is filed; appears to be completable from abroad. (4) No fingerprinting/Live Scan or criminal-history fingerprint card requirement appears in 447.04 or DE 2.01 (DSPS uses conviction questions on the application) - not independently verified. (5) No interview for the ordinary exam/endorsement routes (interview only for the faculty licence, 447.04(1)(c)1.c). (6) CPR/AED proficiency must come from a provider approved by the Wisconsin Department of Health Services (DE 2.01(1)(h)); such courses normally include a hands-on skills check but can be taken from AHA/Red Cross international providers - acceptance of a course taken abroad not verified. (7) National board (INBDE) and regional exam scores must be sent directly by the testing agencies.

## Lisansı sürdürme

- **renewal_period:** Biennial; licences expire 30 September of odd-numbered years (reporting period 1 Oct odd year - 30 Sep next odd year, e.g. 1 Oct 2025 - 30 Sep 2027). Renewal opens ~4 weeks before expiry, online via LicensE.
- **renewal_fee:** Set by DSPS under 440.03(9)(a); amount not verified in this research (DSPS 'Credential Fees' list). Late renewal within 5 years adds the 440.08(3) late fee.
- **ce_hours:** 30 credit hours per biennium related to the practice of dentistry or medicine (Wis. Stat. 447.056; DE 13.03(1)), of which not less than 25 hours must be instruction in clinical dentistry (DE 13.03) and a controlled-substances-prescribing component is required (DE 13.03(2)); CE not required for the biennium in which the licence is first issued. Credit for teaching, college courses (one 3-credit course = 6 CE hours) and 12+ months in an accredited residency (counts as all 30 hours).
- **ce_online_ok:** No live/in-person requirement and no cap on self-study/online found in 447.056 or DE 13.03 as reproduced on the ADA page; courses must be sponsored/recognised by a local, state, regional, national or international dental or medical professional organisation or be accredited college-level courses. Format restrictions, if any, not expressly verified.
- **cpr_required:** Yes. 447.05: the board 'may not renew a license ... unless the applicant for renewal attests that he or she has current proficiency in cardiopulmonary resuscitation, including the use of an automated external defibrillator' obtained from a DHS-approved instructor/organisation; DSPS renewal page: 'Attestation of CPR' and 'CPR and AED must be current to practice dentistry.' DHS-approved providers (e.g. AHA BLS) normally include a hands-on skills session; purely online CPR acceptance not verified.
- **practice_hours_required:** None for ordinary or late renewal (within 5 years). Only after a lapse of 5+ years must the dentist show an active licence elsewhere, a board-approved testing-service exam within the past year, a one-year GPR within the past year, or a board-approved clinical education program (DE 2.03(5)(b)3).
- **address_or_residency_required:** No Wisconsin address or residency required; licensee must keep a current mailing address and email on file in LicensE (Wis. Stat. 440.11). Renewal also requires completing a Department of Workforce Development survey.
- **insurance_required:** No mandatory professional liability insurance found in ch. 447 or DE rules read (Wisconsin's Injured Patients and Families Compensation Fund participation does not cover dentists) - not exhaustively verified.
- **inactive_status:** No inactive/retired/non-practising dentist category exists in ch. 447 or Wis. Admin. Code DE (none appears in the ADA digest or on the DSPS page). The only alternatives are to keep renewing actively or let the licence lapse: DE 2.03(5)(a) allows late renewal at any time within 5 years after the renewal date on payment of the renewal fee plus late fee, proof of current CPR/AED and the DE 13 CE hours.
- **reactivation:** Within 5 years of expiry: late renewal (renewal fee + late fee + current CPR/AED + CE) - no exam. After 5+ years (DE 2.03(5)(b), 'Late Renewal After 5 or More Years' on DSPS site): pay renewal + late fee, current CPR/AED, and ONE of: (a) active licence in good standing in another state; (b) board-approved testing-service (hands-on) exam within one year of renewal; (d) one-year general practice residency within one year of renewal; (e) board-approved clinical education program. DE 2.03(6) reinstatement applies to revoked/surrendered/denied licences.
- **remote_maintainability:** moderate
- **details:** Everything in the ordinary renewal cycle can be done from abroad: online LicensE renewal, fee, 30 CE hours (no in-person element found), DWD survey, and an attestation of current CPR/AED - the CPR course itself (DHS-approved provider, normally with a hands-on skills check) is the only awkward element and can likely be done with an AHA international training centre. No practice hours, no in-state address, no insurance. Because there is no inactive status, the licence must be renewed every two years; a lapse of more than 5 years forces a hands-on exam, a residency or an out-of-state active licence to get it back.
