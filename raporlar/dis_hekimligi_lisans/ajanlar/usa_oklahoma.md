# Oklahoma (USA) — ajan bulgusu

Düzenleyici: Oklahoma State Board of Dentistry (2920 N Lincoln Blvd, Ste B, Oklahoma City, OK 73105; obod.board@ok.gov; 405-522-4844) — https://oklahoma.gov/dentistry.html

**Teori-only rota:** no · **Göçmenlik engeli:** high · **Uzaktan sürdürme:** hard · **Güven:** medium

## Sınav yolları

- **Licensure by examination (regional clinical exam: ADEX via CDCA-WREB-CITA, CRDTS, SRTA)** (el becerisi: evet): Standard route for a new graduate. 59 O.S. 328.21(B)(3) requires 'a written theoretical examination and a clinical examination approved by the Board'; 328.21(D) lists mandatory dental components: fixed prosthetic (anterior all-porcelain crown + 3-unit posterior bridge preparation), 'a periodontal component on a live patient or manikin', an endodontic component, 'an anterior class III and posterior class II restorative component on a live patient or manikin', and a diagnosis and treatment planning section. ADA page (updated 13 Oct 2026) lists accepted exams as ADEX (CDCA-WREB-CITA), CRDTS and SRTA; exam must be within 5 years of application. 2026 Board application form: 'You must have taken and passed the CDCA exam or exam by component pursuant to section 328.21 (D) on a live patient or manikin.' Plus INBDE/National Board (all parts), Oklahoma jurisprudence exam (in person at Board office), BLS card, lawful-presence affidavit, $200 fee.
- **Licensure by credentials (59 O.S. 328.21(H); OAC 195:10-5)** (el becerisi: evet): Only for a dentist already licensed in another state who has practised at least 500 hours in the previous 5 years AND 'having passed a regional examination substantially equivalent to the requirements for this state'. Not available to a new graduate; still presupposes a hands-on regional exam. Fee $500; only the jurisprudence exam is required in Oklahoma.
- **DLOSCE-only / OSCE-only** (el becerisi: hayır): NOT available. Oklahoma is not on the DLOSCE-accepting list and the statute (328.21(D)) requires manikin/live-patient components. The only equivalency clause, 328.21(D)(1)(f), says 'the Board may determine equivalencies based on components of other exams for the purpose of credentialing' - i.e. for licensure by credentials, not for a first licence. The DLOSCE delivered inside ADEX can only satisfy the 'diagnosis and treatment planning section'; the five manikin components remain mandatory.
- **PGY-1 / residency in lieu of clinical exam** (el becerisi: evet): No provision found in 59 O.S. 328.21, OAC 195:10-1-2 or on the ADA summary for accepting a residency instead of the clinical exam. Residency certificates are merely listed as supporting documents for specialty applicants.
- **Portfolio / state-specific clinical exam** (el becerisi: evet): No portfolio pathway and no state-administered clinical exam exist; Oklahoma relies exclusively on the regional testing agencies listed above.

## Teori-only açıklaması
59 O.S. 328.21(B): an applicant must have 'passed a written theoretical examination and a clinical examination approved by the Board'. 328.21(D) specifies that the accepted regional exam must include 'a periodontal component on a live patient or manikin', 'an endodontic component', and 'an anterior class III and posterior class II restorative component on a live patient or manikin', plus fixed-prosthetic preparations and a diagnosis/treatment-planning section. The Board's 2026 Dentist Application repeats: 'Regional Exam Scores: You must have taken and passed the CDCA exam or exam by component pursuant to section 328.21 (D) on a live patient or manikin.' There is no DLOSCE-only, residency, or portfolio alternative; the only equivalency clause (328.21(D)(1)(f)) applies 'for the purpose of credentialing'. A hands-on manikin/live-patient exam is therefore unavoidable for a new graduate. Additional caution: OAC 195:10-1-2(8), as quoted on the ADA page, states 'The Board recognizes only those applicants who are students or graduates of Dental Schools in the United States or Canada, approved, conditionally, or provisionally approved by the Commission on Dental Accreditation' - a CODA-accredited school located in Turkey may need Board confirmation that it qualifies.

## DLOSCE notları
Oklahoma has never listed the DLOSCE as an accepted clinical exam (it is not among the eight DLOSCE states in the 2026 Candidate Guide) and nothing in 59 O.S. 328.21, OAC 195:10 or the ADA summary (updated 13 Oct 2026) mentions DLOSCE/OSCE. The statute's required 'diagnosis and treatment planning section' is satisfied by the DLOSCE component inside the full ADEX series, but the manikin prosthodontic, periodontal, endodontic and restorative components are separately mandatory ('live patient or manikin'). An ADEX-reported DLOSCE pass alone, or an earlier standalone DLOSCE score, would not suffice; the equivalency clause 328.21(D)(1)(f) only allows the Board to 'determine equivalencies based on components of other exams for the purpose of credentialing'.

Page 7 of the 2026 Dentist Application: 'All natural persons fourteen (14) years of age or older and present in the United States, applying for a license with the Oklahoma Board of Dentistry are required by the provisions of 56 O.S. Supp. 2207 [sic] Section 71, to provide the Board with verification of lawful presence in the United States by executing on[e] of the Affidavits below before Notary Public... Please note you CANNOT fill out both affidavits.' Option 1 - Verification of Citizenship: 'I am a United States Citizen.' Option 2 - 'Verifying Qualified Alien Status - Please submit a copy of your passport, green card, etc. with this application!': 'I am a qualified alien under Federal Immigration and Naturalization Act, and I am lawfully present in the United States.' Checklist item: 'Proof of Citizenship (Birth Certificate) or Qualified Alien Documentation (Passport). A Turkish citizen entering on a B-1/B-2 visitor visa is a nonimmigrant, not a 'qualified alien' (8 U.S.C. 1641 covers LPRs, asylees, refugees, parolees etc.), so he could not truthfully execute either sworn affidavit; the form's reference to work visas suggests the Board in practice accepts green-card and work-visa holders, but a visitor is not contemplated. An SSN is requested on the form with no alternative. No Oklahoma residency or in-state address requirement was found in the statute or rules (mailing address only). The affidavits must be notarised ('The Board's licensing offices are staffed with notaries who are available to provide notary service at no cost'). Note: SB 671 (2025 session) proposed amending 56 O.S. 71; its status was not verified.

## Yüz yüze adımlar
(1) Regional clinical exam (ADEX/CRDTS/SRTA) - in person in the US, hands-on manikin components; proof of malpractice insurance for the exam (OAC 195:10-1-2(17)). (2) Jurisprudence exam - taken in person at the Board office in Oklahoma City after the Board approves the application: 'The exam is offered on Thursday's between 9:00-2:00 by appointment only. You cannot schedule your exam until after your application has been approved' (statute 328.21(E): 'appear for the jurisprudence examination at the time and place to be fixed by the Board'; 70% pass per OAC). Because applications must reach the Board 30 days before a Board meeting and the exam can only be scheduled after approval, this likely requires two visits or an extended stay. (3) Notarised application and notarised lawful-presence affidavit (Board office notaries are free; a foreign notary/apostille may or may not be accepted - unverified). (4) 'Personal Interview (if requested by the Board or Investigator): Interviews are not always required but are possible and required if requested' (OAC 195:10-1-2(12)). (5) Application must be mailed as a sealed original with a check or US postal money order ('NO CASH'; older 11/2023 form said emailed PDFs are not accepted). (6) BLS card copy with the application; the Board accepts only CPR courses with a live hands-on skills portion. (7) No fingerprint-based criminal background check was found on the 2026 form or in the rules (not verified either way). (8) National Board (INBDE) scores released to Oklahoma through the ADA/JCNDE online portal - can be done from abroad.

## Diğer başlangıç şartları
Age 21+, good moral character; graduate of a CODA-accredited dental school ('Dental Schools in the United States or Canada' per OAC 195:10-1-2(8)); all parts of the National Board (INBDE) passed; regional clinical exam within 5 years; Oklahoma jurisprudence exam (in person, after Board approval, 70% pass); 2x2 colour photo; official transcript and diploma copy; verification from every state ever licensed in; current BLS/CPR card (hands-on course); malpractice insurance declarations page (new graduates exempt at application, but insurance is required for the clinical exam); lawful-presence affidavit (citizen or qualified alien) with birth certificate/passport/work-visa copy; SSN on form; notarisation; personal interview if requested. No English-language test is mentioned. Timeline: complete packet must be received 30 days before the Board meeting at which it is considered; jurisprudence exam scheduled only after approval; application closes if inactive for more than one year (328.41(K)).

## İlk ücretler
General Dentist application by exam $200 (Specialty exam $300; General Dentist credential $500; Specialty credential $500), non-refundable, check or money order only. Regional exam fees are separate (ADEX full series approx. $2,995). Jurisprudence re-examination fee set by Board (amount not verified). Annual renewal fee amount not verified in this session.

## Lisansı sürdürme

- **renewal_period:** Annual. Renewal window opens 1 October and runs to 31 December 'with no late fee'; fee due by 1 January; if unpaid within two months after 1 January the licence 'will be officially cancelled as of April 1' (59 O.S. 328.41(A),(E)). CE reporting runs on a separate two-year cycle (current cycle 1 July 2025 - 30 June 2027).
- **renewal_fee:** Not verified (set by Board rule; not shown on the pages read). Paid by check/money order with a mailed paper form or online.
- **ce_hours:** Dentists: 40 hours per two-year cycle (59 O.S. 328.41(D)(1)), which must include 2 hours of opioid/scheduled-drug prescribing, an ethics course, and a Board-approved live CPR/BLS course (hour-for-hour). New licensees are exempt for the first year after graduation; CE is prorated. Hours are logged in CE Broker (since 1 July 2025).
- **ce_online_ok:** No. Statute: 'Dentists shall complete forty (40) hours of continuing education with no more than twenty (20) hours to be completed online. Interactive classes or webinar classes may, at the discretion of the Board, count as in-person.' So at least 20 of 40 hours must be in-person or Board-approved live/interactive webinars; ethics may be taken online; CPR must have a live hands-on component.
- **cpr_required:** Yes. Board CE page: 'Live, in-person cardiopulmonary resuscitation class approved by the Board... BLS / CPR courses must have the live hands-on compression section to be accepted. Hybrid courses are accepted. NO FULL ONLINE CPR COURSES WILL BE ACCEPTED!' A BLS card is also required with the initial application.
- **practice_hours_required:** None for renewal of an active licence. 500 hours in the previous 5 years applies only to licensure by credentials (328.21(H)).
- **address_or_residency_required:** No Oklahoma residency or in-state address requirement found in the statute, rules or forms; a mailing address is required for correspondence.
- **insurance_required:** Malpractice insurance declarations page (or exemption letter) must accompany the initial application ('New graduates are exempt'), and proof of malpractice insurance is required for the clinical exam. Whether proof of insurance is required at each renewal was not verified.
- **inactive_status:** No fee-based 'inactive' licence category was found. 59 O.S. 328.41(I): a licensee in good standing who 'notifies the Board in writing of a voluntary nonrenewal of license or requests retirement status shall have a right to renew or reinstate his or her license within five (5) years from the date of notice. The Board may require any training or continuing education requirements to be met prior to reinstatement.' 328.41(J): anyone 'that has not had an active license... in excess of five (5) years shall be required to apply as a new applicant.' A separate 'Retired Volunteer' licence (for unpaid volunteer practice) has its own annual renewal form; its fee/CE and eligibility were not verified.
- **reactivation:** Within 1 year of cancellation for non-renewal: reinstatement on payment of the renewal fee plus a penalty fee (328.41(F)). After more than 1 year without renewal (and without a prior written notice of voluntary nonrenewal/retirement): must 'file an application for and take the examination or other requirements provided for in the State Dental Act' (328.41(F)). Voluntary nonrenewal/retirement with written notice: reinstatement within 5 years, Board may impose CE/training. More than 5 years without an active licence: apply as a new applicant, i.e. a regional clinical exam taken within the last 5 years (or credentials route requiring another state's active licence plus 500 practice hours).
- **remote_maintainability:** hard
- **details:** An Oklahoma licence can in principle be kept active from abroad with no practice-hour or residency requirement, but: (a) half of the 40 biennial CE hours cannot be online unless the Board exercises its discretion to count live interactive webinars as in-person; (b) CPR must include a hands-on skills session (hybrid acceptable); (c) renewal is annual and paper/check-based; (d) there is no low-cost inactive status - only a 5-year retirement/voluntary-nonrenewal window, after which a full new application with a fresh regional exam is required; (e) the lawful-presence affidavit applies at initial licensure (whether it is re-verified at renewal was not verified).
