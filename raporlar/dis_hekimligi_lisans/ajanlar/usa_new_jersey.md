# New Jersey (USA) — ajan bulgusu

Düzenleyici: New Jersey State Board of Dentistry (Division of Consumer Affairs, Office of the Attorney General) — https://www.njconsumeraffairs.gov/den

**Teori-only rota:** no · **Göçmenlik engeli:** low · **Uzaktan sürdürme:** moderate · **Güven:** medium

## Sınav yolları

- **Licensure by examination (ADEX Dental Examination incl. Periodontal Examination)** (el becerisi: evet): N.J.A.C. 13:30-1.5(b): CODA-approved dental degree transcript; notarised passport photo; 'Results from the successful completion of the ADEX dental examination, including the Periodontal Examination, administered by the Commission on Dental Competency Assessments and other entities administering the ADEX dental examination' (taken within past 5 years per ADA summary/Board application); NBDE I+II or INBDE; certificate of the free online NJ Jurisprudence orientation taken within 6 months of application; good-standing certifications from any other licensing state; NJ criminal history background check (N.J.S.A. 45:1-28 et seq.); complete employment history; $125 application fee. ADEX includes manikin hand-skill components, so this is a hands-on route. Per 13:30-1.5(d), an ADEX passer who has not practised as a licensed dentist for 5+ years before applying must re-take ADEX or a Board-approved refresher course with post-course exam.
- **Licensure by credentials / endorsement (reciprocity)** (el becerisi: evet): N.J.A.C. 13:30-1.5(e): applicant must already be licensed in another state/jurisdiction, show active practice 'in a single state or jurisdiction for at least five years prior to the date of application' (may include a dental residency), CODA degree, NBDE/INBDE, 'successful completion of a clinical examination administered or recognized by another state or jurisdiction that is comparable to the examination recognized by the North East Regional Board', online NJ Jurisprudence orientation, good-standing certifications, NJ background check, employment history, $250 fee. Not available to a new graduate (needs an existing licence plus 5 years' practice) and still requires a hands-on clinical exam comparable to NERB/ADEX.
- **PGY-1 residency in lieu of clinical exam** (el becerisi: evet): NOT available. N.J.A.C. 13:30-1.5 contains no residency-in-lieu-of-examination pathway; residency is mentioned only as countable toward the 5-year practice requirement for licensure by credentials and as a CE credit source. The ADA Licensure-by-State summary (updated 10 Oct 2026) lists only ADEX as the accepted initial examination.
- **DLOSCE-only / OSCE-only / portfolio / state-written-exam pathway** (el becerisi: hayır): NOT available. Neither the rule nor the ADA summary mentions DLOSCE, OSCE, portfolio or a state clinical exam; the only clinical examination accepted is the full ADEX series. A DLOSCE pass alone (standalone or ADEX-reported) does not satisfy 13:30-1.5(b)3.
- **Limited teaching certificate (not a full licence)** (el becerisi: hayır): N.J.S.A. 45:6-16.3 et seq. (quoted on ADA page): a limited certificate for faculty at a New Jersey dental school, renewable annually, requires a notarised application and an academic appointment; it permits only teaching-related practice and is not a route to a general dental licence. Not useful for this candidate.

## Teori-only açıklaması
N.J.A.C. 13:30-1.5(b) (quoted in ADA 'New Jersey Laws & Rules', updated 10 Oct 2026): 'To qualify as a candidate for dental licensure, an applicant shall submit a completed application to the Board, which shall contain ... 3. Results from the successful completion of the ADEX dental examination, including the Periodontal Examination, administered by the Commission on Dental Competency Assessments and other entities administering the ADEX dental examination. If an applicant fails any portion of the ADEX dental examination three consecutive times, the Board may require the applicant to sit for, and pass, a remedial course ...; 4. Results of the successful completion of either ... Parts I and II of the National Board Dental Examination; or ... The Integrated National Board Dental Examination; 5. A certificate of completion of the online New Jersey Jurisprudence orientation ...'. The Board's own application instructions state: 'There are two ways to obtain a license in New Jersey: 1. If you have taken the A.D.E.X. clinical examination ... $125. 2. If you are applying by means of endorsement (a licensee who has not taken the A.D.E.X. exam but who is currently licensed and has worked five years ...) ... $250.' The ADEX dental examination contains manikin hand-skill components, and no DLOSCE-only, residency, portfolio or written-only alternative exists in the rules. Therefore a hands-on clinical examination is unavoidable for a new graduate.

## DLOSCE notları
New Jersey's rules (N.J.A.C. 13:30-1.5) and the ADA state summary (updated 10 Oct 2026) do not mention the DLOSCE at all; the only accepted clinical examination is the complete ADEX dental examination 'including the Periodontal Examination'. New Jersey is not on the DLOSCE 2026 Candidate Guide list of accepting states. Because the DLOSCE is now a component of the ADEX series, a candidate who passes the full ADEX (including its DLOSCE component) satisfies NJ, but an ADEX-reported DLOSCE pass with failed or untaken manikin components would not meet 13:30-1.5(b)3 ('successful completion of the ADEX dental examination'); earlier standalone DLOSCE scores have no standing in NJ. No NJ Board policy on the ADEX-integrated DLOSCE was found.

- **itin_or_no_ssn_alternative:** Division of Consumer Affairs brochure 'Eligibility for Professional and Occupational Licensure in New Jersey' (ImmigrantLicense2022): 'A social security number is not required for licensure.' 'Individuals who do not have a Social Security Number (SSN) or Individual Taxpayer Identification Number (ITIN) may apply online. Please complete and submit the Certification Form for Applicants with no Social Security Number or Individual Taxpayer ID Number, which is available on the Division's website. Once submitted, the Division will issue you a Division Issued Identification Number (DIIN) which may be used to create a MyLicense account and submit an online application.' An ITIN is accepted in place of an SSN, and applicants with neither use the certification form/DIIN. CAUTION: the Board's current paper 'Application for a Dental License' PDF still carries legacy text: 'You must provide your Social Security number to the Board or Committee. Failure to do so will result in denial/nonrenewal of licensure' (citing N.J.S.A. 54:50-24, N.J.S.A. 2A:17-56.44e, SSA s.1128E(b)(2)(A), 45 CFR 60.7-60.9); the Division-wide policy and 2020 statute override this, but the applicant should use the online/DIIN route or attach the certification form.
- **in_jurisdiction_address_required:** False
- **barrier_level:** low
- **details:** New Jersey enacted S-2455/A-4225 (signed 1 Sept 2020) prohibiting Division of Consumer Affairs boards from requiring lawful presence in the United States as a qualification for a professional or occupational licence. The DCA brochure (2022) states: 'New Jersey State law allows for all individuals, regardless of citizenship status, to obtain a professional or occupational license in New Jersey if all requirements for licensure are met ... Individuals with undocumented status are eligible for licensure ... A social security number is not required for licensure.' A visitor with no US status is therefore eligible. However, the Board's paper dental application PDF (undated, pre-2020 wording) still contains Section 4 'Citizenship / Immigration Status': 'Federal law limits the issuance or renewal of professional or occupational licenses or certificates to U.S. citizens or qualified aliens. To comply with this federal law, check the appropriate box ... If you are not a U.S. citizen, attach a copy of your alien registration card (front and back) or other documentation issued by ... USCIS' with boxes 'U.S. citizen / Alien lawfully admitted for permanent residence / Other immigration status'. Under current NJ law this cannot be used to deny the licence, but the form still collects status, so expect to tick 'Other immigration status' and possibly supply passport/visa copies; no SAVE verification is mentioned anywhere. No NJ residency or in-state address requirement: the form requires an 'address of record' that may be released to the public ('One of your addresses must include a street, city, state and ZIP code' - US-centric wording; a foreign address appears permissible but unverified). Work authorisation is not required for licensure.

## Yüz yüze adımlar
(1) ADEX dental examination (manikin hand-skill sections + DLOSCE) must be sat in person at a US testing site - unavoidable. (2) Criminal history background check (N.J.S.A. 45:1-28; N.J.A.C. 13:30-1.5(b)7): the application packet's 'Certification and Authorization Form for a Criminal History Background Check' says first-time applicants 'will receive a separate mailing from the Board or Committee regarding the criminal history background process' (NJ DCA normally uses IdentoGO electronic Live Scan at New Jersey sites; whether mailed ink cards are accepted for applicants abroad could not be verified - plan to be fingerprinted during the exam trip; repeat-check fee $18.75 plus vendor fee). (3) Notarisation: the paper application must be 'signed and stamped by a Notary Public', the passport photo must be signed and notarised, and an affidavit is 'executed by the applicant before a notary public' - can be done in NJ during a visit or at a US consulate abroad (foreign notary acceptance unverified); the online MyLicense/DIIN route may reduce notarisation needs (unverified). (4) Jurisprudence: no sit-down exam; a free online NJ Jurisprudence orientation 'available 24/7 through the Internet', certificate must be dated within 6 months of application - fully remote. (5) No interview. (6) Initial licence fee is requested by letter after approval and paid by mail/online - remote.

## Diğer başlangıç şartları
Online NJ Jurisprudence orientation (free, 24/7, certificate within 6 months of application); NBDE I+II or INBDE; CODA-approved degree certified transcript; criminal history background check with fingerprinting; notarised application, photo and affidavit; complete professional employment history; good-standing certifications from any other licensing jurisdiction; ADEX results no older than 5 years; no CPR/BLS, no English test, no liability insurance, no practice hours required at initial licensure; CE not required for initial registration (N.J.A.C. 13:30-5.2(a)). Timeline not published; Board notifies by letter when application approved and then requests initial licence fee.

## İlk ücretler
Application fee $125 (ADEX route) or $250 (licensure by credentials/endorsement) per N.J.A.C. 13:30-8.1(a) and Board application instructions; initial licence fee $390 if issued in first year of biennial period, $195 if in second year (13:30-8.1(b)1.i); criminal history background check $18.75 (repeat check) plus fingerprint vendor charge (first-time amount not stated); ADEX dental examination approx. $2,995 (CDCA-WREB-CITA, from known facts); jurisprudence orientation free.

## Lisansı sürdürme

- **renewal_period:** Biennial (2 years from last expiration date), N.J.A.C. 13:30-8.6(b).
- **renewal_fee:** Active licence renewal $390 per biennium (N.J.A.C. 13:30-8.1(b)1.ii); late fee $100 (within 30 days after expiry); reinstatement fee $200 (13:30-8.1(d)1). A separate inactive-renewal fee is not listed in 13:30-8.1 (item iii 'Reserved') - amount unclear.
- **ce_hours:** 40 hours per biennium for active licensees (N.J.A.C. 13:30-5.2(a)), including 10 mandatory hours: 3 h hands-on CPR, 3 h pharmacology/internal medicine, 2 h infection control, 1 h ethics/NJ jurisprudence (the online jurisprudence orientation does not count), 1 h prescription opioids (P.L. 2017 c.28). New licensees: 20 credits if licensed in year 1 of the biennium, exempt if licensed in year 2 (but 1 opioid credit within 24 months).
- **ce_online_ok:** Not 100%. 13:30-5.2(i)1.iv: 'A maximum of 20 hours of continuing education credit shall be given for any form of written or electronic media distance learning courses. A written or electronic media distance learning course shall include a written post-test ... Webinars shall not be considered electronic media distance learning' - so self-study/online on-demand courses are capped at 20 of the 40 hours, but live webinars are not treated as distance learning and are not capped (so in practice ~37 of 40 hours can be done remotely via live webinars plus self-study). The 3 h CPR must be hands-on: 'Webinars and electronic media distance learning courses shall not satisfy this requirement.'
- **cpr_required:** Yes at every biennial renewal for active licensees: '3 hours of continuing education with practical hands-on certification for cardiopulmonary resuscitation (CPR), which meets the American Heart Association certification standards for healthcare providers ... including AED, choking, rescue breathing. Webinars and electronic media distance learning courses shall not satisfy this requirement' (13:30-5.2(e)1.i). A hands-on AHA-standard course taken abroad (e.g. AHA international training centre) should meet the standard, but Board acceptance of a foreign provider is unverified. Not required for initial licensure.
- **practice_hours_required:** None for renewal. Note 13:30-1.5(d)/(f): an ADEX passer applying for initial licensure who has not practised as a licensed dentist for 5+ years must re-take ADEX or a Board-approved refresher course with post-course exam, and the Board 'may require ... satisfactory proof of continued competency'.
- **address_or_residency_required:** No NJ residency requirement. Licensee must maintain an address of record with the Board (may be a non-residence address); foreign address appears permissible (unverified).
- **insurance_required:** No mandatory professional liability insurance found in N.J.A.C. 13:30 or N.J.S.A. 45:6 as summarised by the ADA (not verified exhaustively).
- **inactive_status:** Available at each renewal: 'A licensee or registrant may renew his or her license or registration by choosing inactive status. A licensee ... electing to renew ... as inactive shall not engage ... in the practice of dentistry ... in New Jersey until such time as the license ... is returned to active status' (13:30-8.6(c)). CE is required only of 'licensed dentists holding active licenses' (13:30-5.2(a)), so inactive renewal carries no CE/CPR requirement. No maximum duration stated. Fee for inactive renewal not listed in 13:30-8.1 (unclear).
- **reactivation:** The conditions for 'return from inactive status' and 'reinstatement of suspended license' are referenced in the title of N.J.A.C. 13:30-8.6 but the text available (ADA summary and Cornell LII copy, last amended 2011) ends at subsection (f); the specific requirements (typically CE for the inactive/suspended period, reinstatement fee $200, and - under statewide N.J.S.A. 45:1-7.1 - possible proof of competency for absences of 5+ years) could not be verified within this research. Lapse: licence not renewed within 30 days of expiry is 'suspended without a hearing' (13:30-8.6(e)); reinstatement fee $200 plus late fee $100. Risk flag: by analogy with 13:30-1.5(d), the Board may require ADEX re-take or a refresher course after 5+ years without practice - mark as unclear.
- **remote_maintainability:** moderate
- **details:** Active status can be maintained from abroad with a biennial $390 fee and 40 CE hours, of which at most 20 may be self-study distance learning (live webinars uncapped) and 3 must be a hands-on AHA-standard CPR course; records must be kept for two biennia. Inactive status is cheap in CE terms (none) with no stated time limit, but return-to-active conditions and a possible competency/re-exam requirement after 5+ years of non-practice are unverified. No practice hours, no residency, no insurance mandate, no in-state address. Renewal is online via MyLicense (DIIN account possible without SSN).
