Düzenleyici: Maine Board of Dental Practice (independent board; application processing shared with the Office of Professional and Occupational Regulation) — https://www.maine.gov/dental/

## Sınav yolları

- **Pathway I - Standard application: CODA degree + INBDE + ADEX (CDCA-WREB-CITA)** (el becerisi: evet): Rule 02-313 Ch. 6 II: (A) CODA doctoral degree; (B) passing all parts of the National Board exam (NBDE I+II or INBDE, approved Aug 2022); (C) 'passing all sections of a regional or state dental board examination approved by the Board'. June 2025 Dentist Examination Chart for ADEX: 'Didactic - Diagnostic Skills Examination - Objective Structured Clinical Examination (DSE-OSCE); and Clinical Skills - all sections required: Prosthodontic, Endodontic, Restorative (live patient cases)*, Periodontal (live patient cases)*' with CompeDont tooth (eff. 4/17/2020) and manikin periodontal (eff. 6/12/2020) approved as alternatives to live patients. Manikin hand-skill sections are mandatory, so hands-on.
- **Pathway I - Standard application with CRDTS (+SRTA) or SRTA** (el becerisi: evet): June 2025 chart lists CRDTS (operating as CRDTS + SRTA since Jan 2025): didactic Diagnosis and Comprehensive Treatment Planning plus clinical skills sections incl. Periodontal and Restorative (live patient cases; Board approved Simulated Patient alternative eff. 6/13/2025); SRTA: Clinical Skills - all sections required (Endodontics, Posterior Restorative etc.). All hands-on.
- **Pathway I - Standard application with a Board-approved state board clinical exam (Texas, California previously approved)** (el becerisi: evet): The exam chart also lists state board clinical examinations (Texas and California) previously approved by the Board; these are hands-on clinical exams.
- **DLOSCE-only / OSCE-only** (el becerisi: hayır): NOT available. The DLOSCE does not appear on the Board's June 2025 Dentist Examination Chart, Maine is not among the states listed in the DLOSCE 2026 Candidate Guide, and Rule Ch. 6 II.C requires 'all sections' of an approved regional/state exam. The ADEX DSE-OSCE is only the didactic component of ADEX; the chart states 'Clinical Skills - all sections required'.
- **PGY-1 / residency in lieu of clinical exam** (el becerisi: hayır): NOT available. Neither 32 MRS 18342, Rule Ch. 6 nor the June 2025 exam chart offers residency completion as a substitute for the clinical examination (residency appears in the rules only as an eligible continuing-education activity, Ch. 13 II). Maine issues a separate 'resident dentist' license for those in a residency, which is not a full license.
- **Pathway II - Licensure by endorsement (32 MRS 18347; Rule Ch. 11)** (el becerisi: hayır): Only for 'an applicant that is actively licensed as a Dentist in another jurisdiction' (Application Checklist, Pathway II). Not usable by a new graduate with no licence elsewhere; specific practice-time requirements not read in detail (unverified). Also requires jurisprudence exam, BLS, and the $871 fee.
- **Temporary license** (el becerisi: hayır): Six-month, non-renewable temporary licence (requires jurisprudence exam); not a route to a full, maintainable licence (details per Board checklist via search summary, not read in full).

## Teori-only açıklaması
32 MRS 18342(1): an applicant 'must provide: A. Verification of either a doctoral degree in dentistry from a dental program accredited by the American Dental Association Commission on Dental Accreditation ... and B. Verification of passing all examinations required by the board.' Board Rule 02-313 Ch. 6 II: 'A. Verification of a doctoral degree in dentistry ... from a dental school whose program is accredited by CODA ...; B. Verification of passing all parts of the National Dental Board Examination or the successor to that examination; and C. Verification of passing all sections of a regional or state dental board examination approved by the Board.' The Board's Dentist Examination Chart (June 2025) lists only ADEX (CDCA-WREB-CITA), CRDTS (+SRTA), SRTA and previously approved Texas/California state exams, and for ADEX specifies 'Clinical Skills - all sections required: Prosthodontic, Endodontic, Restorative (live patient cases)*, Periodontal (live patient cases)*' (manikin/CompeDont alternatives approved). The ADA Licensure-by-State summary (updated 29 Nov 2025) confirms: 'The Board requires candidates to take and pass all sections of the examination, including: Diagnostic Skills Examination; Prosthodontic; Endodontic, Restorative ... and Periodontal'. No OSCE-only, DLOSCE, portfolio or residency alternative exists, so a hands-on manikin examination is unavoidable.

## DLOSCE notları
Maine does not accept the DLOSCE as its clinical examination: it is absent from the Board's June 2025 Dentist Examination Chart and Maine is not in the DLOSCE 2026 Candidate Guide list of accepting states. The only OSCE recognised is the ADEX 'DSE-OSCE' didactic component, which must be accompanied by all ADEX clinical-skills sections ('all sections required'). No Board policy was found on scores from the ADEX-integrated DLOSCE; because Maine requires the full ADEX series anyway, an ADEX-reported DLOSCE pass alone would not satisfy Rule Ch. 6 II.C. Earlier standalone DLOSCE scores are therefore irrelevant in Maine.

## Yüz yüze adımlar
(1) Clinical examination: ADEX/CRDTS/SRTA must be taken in person at a US test site (manikin sections; live-patient sections optional via CompeDont/simulated patient) - this is the only unavoidable in-person US step and it is hands-on. (2) Jurisprudence examination: open-book, 10-question paper exam on Maine statutes/rules, passing score 90%, up to three attempts; 'this is not an online examination. You must print, complete and submit (upload) the required jurisprudence examination as part of your online licensure application' - can be completed anywhere and uploaded. (3) Criminal background check: Maine State Bureau of Identification criminal history record check ($21) run by the office for all applicants; no fingerprint/Live Scan requirement found (appears name-based; not fully verified). (4) BLS: 'online trainings are not accepted, unless the licensee can verify hands-on participation with the instructor as a component of the training' - a hands-on skills session is required but can be done with any provider (foreign provider acceptance unverified). (5) No interview, no notarisation, no in-person jurisprudence sitting; application is filed online; education verification may be sent by the school or as an official transcript; only non-CODA equivalency reports must be mailed (not applicable). Overall: everything except the clinical exam (and possibly BLS) can be done from abroad.

## Diğer başlangıç şartları
Jurisprudence exam (open book, 90% pass, 3 attempts, printed and uploaded with online application); current BLS certification with hands-on component (required since 10/25/2023; 32 MRS 18341(4)); Maine SBI criminal history record check ($21); CV; verification of CODA doctoral degree (official transcript or certificate-of-education form from the school); official NBDE/INBDE and regional exam scores; no English test; no insurance; applicant has 90 days after notification of missing materials to complete the file or risk denial (32 MRS 18341(3)); applications processed in order received, no published timeline.

## İlk ücretler
$871.00 total for dentist licensure (standard and endorsement pathways): $100 application fee + $750 license fee + $21 Maine criminal background fee (Application Checklist for Dentist Licensure). Plus ADEX ($2,995 full series) or CRDTS fees paid to the testing agency; INBDE fee to JCNDE. Licence runs from date of issue through the current dentist renewal term (expires 31 Dec of odd years), so a licence issued late in an odd year is short-lived.

## Lisansı sürdürme

- **renewal_period:** Biennial; 'The biennial license term begins January 1 of even numbered years and ends December 31st of odd numbered years' (Rule Ch. 13 I.A); renews on December 31 of odd years. Late renewal allowed up to 90 days after expiration with late fee.
- **renewal_fee:** Not verified (set in Board Rule Chapter 2 Fees; renewal page/fee amount not located within the tool budget). Late fee applies for renewals within 90 days after expiry.
- **ce_hours:** 40 credit hours per biennial term 'directly related to dental practice' plus current BLS (Rule Ch. 13 I.A). Required areas of study include hours on prescribing of opioid medication and, for sedation permit holders, 6 of the 40 hours in sedation (ADA summary lines).
- **ce_online_ok:** Largely yes with caveats: Ch. 13 II lists eligible activities including courses from approved sponsors, 'teaching, writing, independent study, and other distance' learning, grand rounds, writing/publication, but 'No more than ten (10) credit hours can be claimed in any one' category of certain activity types (exact category wording not fully read; a 10-hour cap on self-study/independent study is likely). Live online courses from ADA CERP/AGD PACE-type sponsors should count without cap. The hands-on BLS component is the only mandatory in-person element.
- **cpr_required:** Yes - current BLS certification is a condition of initial licensure and every renewal (32 MRS 18341(4), 18350; Rule Ch. 13 I.A) and 'online trainings are not accepted, unless the licensee can verify hands-on participation with the instructor as a component of the training' (Rule Ch. 6 I.D / Ch. 11). A hands-on skills session is therefore required each cycle (blended online + in-person skills check acceptable).
- **practice_hours_required:** None found in statute or rules for renewal of an active licence.
- **address_or_residency_required:** No in-state address or residency required; licensees must notify the Board in writing of any change of name or address within 10 days (32 MRS 18352); renewal notices are emailed to the contact information on file.
- **insurance_required:** No mandatory professional liability insurance found in statute, rules, checklist or FAQ.
- **inactive_status:** Available. Board FAQ: 'Board Rule, Chapter 13, Section IV (C) outlines the process for requesting Inactive status. The process is simply to write to the Board and request inactive status with a statement that you will not provide services for the remainder of the license term. The Board will review the request and will either grant the request or deny the request.' Fee, CE obligations while inactive, and maximum duration not verified (Ch. 13 IV(C) text not retrieved). The license-types page lists 'Request an Inactive License' as a standard service. Inactive status is granted for the remainder of a licence term, implying it is re-requested each biennium (unverified).
- **reactivation:** From inactive: 'submit a written request to the Board and the Board will determine if any additional information, training or examination is required' (FAQ, Rule Ch. 13 IV(C)) - discretionary; no automatic re-examination. From lapse: up to 90 days late = renewal + late fee; 91 days to 2 years expired = reinstatement application, 'must pay the required fees and meet all qualifications for initial licensure. The Board may, giving due consideration to the protection of the public, waive the examination requirements' (Rule Ch. 11 III.C; 32 MRS 18349(3)); more than 2 years expired = new initial licensure application meeting all qualifications, i.e. a current clinical (hands-on) exam would be required again.
- **remote_maintainability:** moderate
- **details:** An active Maine licence can be kept from abroad with 40 CE hours per biennium (mostly online), biennial fee, and a BLS certificate that includes a hands-on skills component; there are no practice-hour, residency, address or insurance conditions. Inactive status exists but is discretionary, requested in writing per term, and return to active may involve Board-imposed training or examination; letting the licence lapse beyond 2 years forces a full new application including a hands-on regional exam. The SSN/tax-reporting link (36 MRS 175) could affect renewal only if the State Tax Assessor flags unpaid Maine taxes, which is unlikely for a non-practising non-resident.
