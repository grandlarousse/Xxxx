## Sınav yolları

- **NDEB certificate route (Dental Register Part One, general dentist) - Act s.5(2)(a) second limb** (el becerisi: hayır): Act s.5(2): 'A person is eligible to be registered in Part One of the Dental Register if he or she pays the prescribed fees and produces evidence satisfactory to the Registration Committee that he or she (a) is registered or eligible to be registered to practise dentistry in a province or territory or holds a certificate of qualification from the National Dental Examining Board of Canada; and (b) is of good character.' s.7(1): the Registration Committee 'shall register' a person who meets the requirements. Requirements sheet NWT9043/0924 item 4: 'Copy of National Dental Examining Board of Canada Certificate (NDEB)'. For a graduate of an accredited programme (CDAC treats CODA-accredited programmes outside the US, e.g. Yeditepe after 1 Feb 2024, as accredited) the NDEB certificate is obtained through the computer-based Virtual OSCE only. No NWT jurisprudence exam, no clinical/manikin exam, no residency is listed in the Act, Regulations or the requirements sheet.
- **Labour-mobility route - registered or eligible to be registered in another Canadian province/territory (Act s.5(2)(a) first limb; s.24.1/24.2 register on application)** (el becerisi: hayır): Same section allows registration of a person who 'is registered or eligible to be registered to practise dentistry in a province or territory'. Requirements sheet: applicants without NDEB can be licensed if they hold (or show eligibility for) a full unrestricted licence elsewhere in Canada. Hands-on only if the other jurisdiction's route needs it (all provinces use NDEB, so normally no).
- **Temporary Register (Act ss.21-23; Reg s.2(a)(iv)) - 3-month temporary licence** (el becerisi: hayır): Short-term licence for dentists practising general dentistry 'for a period of three months' (may be extended once per year by three months per Regulations); fee $128. Same qualification evidence as Part One; not a route to a permanent licence.
- **Dental Register Part Two (specialist) - Act s.6** (el becerisi: hayır): Requires completion of a CDAC-accredited specialty programme and the National Dental Specialty Examination / RCDC certification (requirements sheet item 5). Not relevant to a new general-dentistry graduate.
- **Student Register (Act s.24.1)** (el becerisi: hayır): Only for a student 'promoted to the final year of dental studies' working under direct supervision of a licensee; not a licensure pathway for a graduate. Former 'Dental Register Part Three' (sponsored graduates of non-Canadian schools) was repealed by R-076-2014 (Reg s.2(a)(iii) 'Repealed').

## Teori-only açıklaması
Dental Profession Act (NWT) s.5(2): 'A person is eligible to be registered in Part One of the Dental Register if he or she pays the prescribed fees and produces evidence satisfactory to the Registration Committee that he or she (a) is registered or eligible to be registered to practise dentistry in a province or territory or holds a certificate of qualification from the National Dental Examining Board of Canada; and (b) is of good character.' s.7(1): 'The Registration Committee shall register' such a person. The NWT 'Requirements for Registration / Licensure as a Dentist' (form NWT9043/0924) lists only: application form, photo ID, dental degree/transcripts, 'Copy of National Dental Examining Board of Canada Certificate (NDEB)', certificates of standing, CV, citizenship or immigration/work-permit documentation, and fees. No jurisprudence exam, no clinical/manikin exam, no residency, no CPR, no criminal record check appear in the Act, the Dental Profession Regulations or the requirements sheet. Since March 2023 NDEB certification for graduates of accredited programmes is the computer-based Virtual OSCE only, so the whole route is theory/computer-based. Caveats: (1) requirements sheet 'Note: Currency: to be eligible for registration, applicants must have practiced within the 3 years immediately prior to applying' (a new graduate's recent clinical training should normally satisfy this but it is not stated); (2) the immigration-document requirement in item 8 (see immigration section).

## DLOSCE notları
N/A for Canada. The NWT Act recognises only the NDEB certificate of qualification (or registration/eligibility in another Canadian province/territory). The DLOSCE, ADEX, CRDTS and other US exams are not mentioned anywhere in the Act, Regulations or the requirements sheet and are not accepted in lieu of NDEB certification. The relevant computer-based exam is the NDEB Virtual OSCE.

## Yüz yüze adımlar
None identified in the NWT process itself. The requirements sheet states 'All items, including the application and supporting documentation, may be emailed to professional_licensing@gov.nt.ca'; the form ends with a simple signed 'Declaration' (no notarisation or commissioner of oaths mentioned); there is no fingerprinting or criminal-record-check requirement (only self-declaration questions about convictions, discipline, refusals and liability claims); no NWT jurisprudence exam; no interview. Certificates of standing must be sent directly by the other regulators. Within ten days of registration the registrant must 'satisfy the Registrar that they have appropriate malpractice insurance' (Reg s.3(2)) - documentary. The only exam, the NDEB Virtual OSCE, is computer-based at Prometric centres (availability outside Canada not verified here). Processing: 'allow up to 15 business days (3 weeks) for decision and processing' once the file is complete; Regulations require the Registrar to refer applications to the Committee within 30 days.

## Diğer başlangıç şartları
Per NWT9043/0924: (1) completed application form; (2) copy of valid government photo ID; (3) copy of dental degree from an accredited post-secondary institution (or transcripts sent directly); (4) copy of NDEB certificate; (5) specialists only: CDAC-accredited specialty programme / RCDC; (6) certificates of standing from every jurisdiction worldwide where currently or previously (15 years) registered, sent directly by the regulators; (7) current CV; (8) birth certificate/citizenship documentation (Canadian citizen) or 'valid immigration or work permit' (non-citizen); (9) fees (cheque, money order or credit-card authorisation). Currency note: 'applicants must have practiced within the 3 years immediately prior to applying'. Good character (Act s.5(2)(b)). Mandatory malpractice insurance evidenced within 10 days of registration (Regulations). No jurisprudence exam, no CPR/BLS, no English test (self-declared fluency), no background check listed. Timeline: up to 15 business days after complete file. 'Applicant must be fully licensed before beginning work.'

## İlk ücretler
Per application form (NWT9043/0924): Annual Registration $128 + Licence $255 = $383 CAD (licence year April 1 - March 31); Temporary permit (3 months) $128. Renewal page: licence renewal $255; $128 where registered after Dec 31; reinstatement (late renewal) $255. Regulations fee schedule (s.14, indexed annually from the 2020-21 base) also lists a reinstatement fee of $215 'in addition to Annual Individual Licence fee', student licence, certificate of standing and corporate licence fees. NDEB fees are separate (paid to NDEB). Malpractice insurance premium separate.

## Lisansı sürdürme

- **renewal_period:** Annual. Act s.20(2): licence 'is valid from April 1 of the year specified on the licence to March 31 of the following year'. Registrar emails the NWT Licence Renewal Application Form in mid-January (Regulations: renewal notices by Feb 15); completed form due 'before March 1st of the new calendar year'.
- **renewal_fee:** $255 CAD (licence renewal April 1 - March 31); $128 if registered after Dec 31; reinstatement (late renewal) $255 per the renewal page (Regulations schedule: reinstatement $215 plus the annual licence fee, indexed). Payable by credit-card authorisation, cheque or money order to the Government of the Northwest Territories.
- **ce_hours:** None found. The Dental Profession Act and the Dental Profession Regulations contain no continuing-education/continuing-competency requirement for dentists (the Act's only 'continuing' provisions concern jurisdiction and offences), and the dentist renewal page lists only the renewal form and fee. (By contrast the NWT requires an annual continuing competency record from dental hygienists.) Not fully verified because the renewal form itself is only emailed to registrants.
- **ce_online_ok:** No CE requirement identified, so nothing to complete; if the Registrar introduces a continuing-competency record (as for hygienists) its format is unknown.
- **cpr_required:** No CPR/BLS requirement found in the Act, Regulations, requirements sheet or renewal page.
- **practice_hours_required:** No practice-hour requirement for renewal found in the Act, Regulations or renewal page. However the Registrar applies a currency rule to NEW applications ('must have practiced within the 3 years immediately prior to applying'), which would likely be applied to a re-application after a lapse. Whether the annual renewal form asks about practice is unknown (form not public).
- **address_or_residency_required:** No NWT address or residency requirement. Form asks for home/business/mailing address (Province/State field) and 'Intended practice location in the NWT (or Virtual)'; correspondence goes to the mailing address 'unless advised otherwise'.
- **insurance_required:** Yes. Dental Profession Regulations s.3(2): 'Within ten days of registration, registrants must satisfy the Registrar that they have appropriate malpractice insurance.' Whether proof is demanded at every renewal is not stated on the public renewal page.
- **inactive_status:** None. The Regulations (s.2(a)) establish only Dental Register Part One (general), Part Two (specialists), Temporary Register Parts One and Two (3-month licences) and the Student Register; Part Three was repealed (R-076-2014). There is no non-practising/inactive/retired category and no reduced fee. The only way to hold the licence is to pay the full annual fee.
- **reactivation:** Act s.18(2): if the licence fee is not paid 'on or before March 31 in each year, the registration of that person is cancelled.' s.19(1): the person 'shall be reinstated ... on payment ... of the prescribed licence fee for that year and any other prescribed fee required to be paid for reinstatement' (reinstatement $255 per renewal page / $215 + licence fee per Regulations). s.19(3): 'Where a person allows his or her registration to lapse for more than six months, the Registration Committee may refuse to reinstate that person' - after that, a new application is needed and the 3-year currency rule and immigration-document rule would apply again; no new clinical exam is prescribed (NDEB certificate is permanent).
- **remote_maintainability:** moderate
- **details:** Keeping the licence active from abroad requires only: paying $255 by March 1 each year on the emailed renewal form (no CE, no CPR, no practice hours, no NWT address found) and maintaining 'appropriate malpractice insurance' (a Canadian professional liability policy, which may be awkward or costly for someone not practising in Canada). Risks: no inactive category; a missed payment cancels registration and a lapse of more than six months lets the Committee refuse reinstatement; the Registrar's currency expectation (practice within 3 years) could be applied on any re-application; the non-public renewal form may ask about practice status or insurance.
