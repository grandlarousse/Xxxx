# New York (USA) — ajan bulgusu

Düzenleyici: New York State Education Department (NYSED), Office of the Professions – State Board for Dentistry (licences are issued by the Department under the Board of Regents) — https://www.op.nysed.gov/professions/dentists

**Teori-only rota:** no · **Göçmenlik engeli:** high · **Uzaktan sürdürme:** hard · **Güven:** medium

## Sınav yolları

- **PGY-1 residency pathway (the ONLY route to initial licensure for a new graduate)** (el becerisi: evet): Education Law §6604(3): experience 'shall consist of satisfactory completion of a clinically-based postdoctoral general practice or specialty dental residency program, of at least one year's duration, in a hospital or dental facility accredited for teaching purposes by a national accrediting body approved by the department [CODA]... [which] shall include a formal outcome assessment evaluation of the resident's competence to practice dentistry'. 8 NYCRR 61.18(b)(1): residency must be 'completed successfully by the applicant prior to the submission to the department of the application for licensure'; 61.18(b)(3) requires the program director's written attestation of competence. Written exam: §6604(4) + 8 NYCRR 61.2(b) – NBDE Parts I and II (INBDE is the current successor; NYSED page snippet: 'you must obtain passing scores on both Parts I and II of the National Board Dental Examination... verification... directly from the Joint Commission on National Dental Examinations'). No clinical exam of any kind is accepted. The residency is a 12-month clinical, patient-care job in New York (residents register with NYSED within 60 days, §6605(5)/61.18(d)), which requires physical presence and a work-authorising visa – excluded by the candidate's profile.
- **Regional / ADEX / CDCA / CRDTS hands-on clinical examination** (el becerisi: evet): NOT ACCEPTED. ADA Licensure-by-State (updated 13 Oct 2026): 'Beginning January 1, 2007, all applicants for initial licensure must complete an approved clinically-based dental residency program of at least one year's duration. A clinical (practical) examination such as NERB/CDCA will not be accepted for initial licensure after December 31, 2006. Source: 8 NYCRR §61.18.' 8 NYCRR 61.2(a) (the old Part III clinical exam / NERB route) applies only to persons who had completed education and applied on or before 31 Dec 2006.
- **DLOSCE / OSCE-only (computer-based clinical exam) pathway** (el becerisi: hayır): DOES NOT EXIST in New York. Neither Education Law Art. 133 nor 8 NYCRR Part 61 mentions the DLOSCE or any OSCE; §6604(4)/61.2(b) require only the written National Board examination, and §6604(3)/61.18 make the residency mandatory. The DLOSCE therefore cannot substitute for anything in New York, whether taken standalone (ended Oct 2026) or inside the ADEX exam.
- **Licensure by endorsement of another US state or Canadian provincial licence (8 NYCRR 61.4 / 59.6; Education Law §6506(6))** (el becerisi: evet): Requires an existing licence from 'another jurisdiction of the United States or a Canadian province', 'satisfactory professional experience of at least two years following initial licensure' (59.6) and 'lawful and reputable practice in dentistry of not less than eight months during the two years next preceding the filing of the application' (61.4(a)(2)); applicant must not have failed a NY-used exam unless later passed. Statutory conditions in §6506(6) include '(f) Citizenship or immigration status: be a United States citizen or a noncitizen lawfully admitted for permanent residence in the United States'. This is the only route that bypasses the NY residency, but it needs two years of actual (hands-on) practice under another licence.
- **Restricted dental faculty licence (Education Law §6604-B)** (el becerisi: evet): Only for a full-time faculty member at an approved New York dental school; practice limited to the school's facilities. Needs, within the last five years, 'two years of satisfactory practice as a dentist or... an advanced education program in general dentistry or in a dental specialty' accredited by CODA, and §6604-B(7): 'must be a United States citizen or a noncitizen lawfully admitted for permanent residence... provided, however, that the department may grant a three year waiver'. Fee $300 + $300 per registration. Not a general licence.
- **Limited permit (Education Law §6605)** (el becerisi: hayır): Not a licence. 'The department may issue a limited permit to a graduate of a dental college who meets the educational qualifications for admission to the licensing examination in dentistry for employment in a hospital or dental facility approved by an appropriate agency, while under the direction or supervision of a licensed dentist. No such permit shall be issued or renewed unless such graduate has a bona fide offer of a position'. Valid one year, renewable once (or annually during a residency); fee $105; 'Nothing in this section shall be construed to authorize such unlicensed dentist to engage in the private practice of dentistry.' Requires US employment, i.e. work authorisation.
- **Regents' discretionary waiver (Education Law §6506(5))** (el becerisi: evet): The Board of Regents may 'Waive education, experience and examination requirements for a professional license... provided the board of regents shall be satisfied that the requirements of such article have been substantially met'. Purely discretionary; no published process for waiving the residency for a new graduate; not a realistic route.

## Teori-only açıklaması
New York has no examination-based route at all for initial licensure: a one-year clinical residency is a statutory requirement in addition to the written exam. Education Law §6604: 'To qualify for a license as a dentist, an applicant shall fulfill the following requirements: ... 3. Experience: have experience satisfactory to the board and in accordance with the commissioner's regulations, provided that such experience shall consist of satisfactory completion of a clinically-based postdoctoral general practice or specialty dental residency program, of at least one year's duration, in a hospital or dental facility accredited for teaching purposes by a national accrediting body approved by the department, provided, further that any such residency program shall include a formal outcome assessment evaluation of the resident's competence to practice dentistry acceptable to the department; 4. Examination: pass a written examination satisfactory to the board and in accordance with the commissioner's regulations'. 8 NYCRR 61.18(b)(1): 'The residency program shall be a postdoctoral dental residency program in either general dentistry, or a specialty of dentistry..., of at least one year's duration in a hospital or facility accredited for teaching purposes by an acceptable national accrediting body, which is completed successfully by the applicant prior to the submission to the department of the application for licensure.' ADA summary of §61.18 (updated 13 Oct 2026): 'PGY1 - Beginning January 1, 2007, all applicants for initial licensure must complete an approved clinically-based dental residency program of at least one year's duration. A clinical (practical) examination such as NERB/CDCA will not be accepted for initial licensure after December 31, 2006.' The written exam (61.2(b)(2): 'The department may accept grades acceptable to the State Board for Dentistry on an examination of the National Board Dental Examinations as meeting the requirements of Parts I and II of the licensing examination') is necessary but not sufficient. Hence the candidate, who refuses any residency, cannot be licensed in New York by INBDE/DLOSCE/jurisprudence alone, and even the residency route would require US work authorisation and (per §6604(6)) citizenship/permanent residence or a Regents shortage-area waiver.

## DLOSCE notları
New York's statute and regulations do not reference the DLOSCE, any OSCE, or any clinical examination for post-2006 applicants. 8 NYCRR 61.2(b)(1): 'The examination shall consist of two parts designed to sample the knowledge from all areas related to dentistry' (i.e. NBDE I & II / INBDE only); the clinical competence requirement is met exclusively by the PGY-1 residency's 'formal written outcome assessment' (61.18(b)(3)). Consequently: (a) a standalone DLOSCE score (pre-Oct 2026) has no value in NY; (b) a DLOSCE taken as part of the ADEX exam has no value in NY (ADEX itself is not accepted); (c) the ADA/ADEX integration is irrelevant to NY. The ADA 2026 DLOSCE Candidate Guide does not list New York as an accepting state (per the KNOWN FACTS list).

Provided, however that pursuant to 8 USC § 1621(d), no otherwise qualified alien shall be precluded from obtaining a professional license under this Title if an individual is not unlawfully present in the United States, including but not limited to individuals granted Deferred Action for Childhood Arrivals relief or similar relief from deportation.' A tourist with no status to declare does not fit the form, the governing statute still says citizen/LPR, and – decisively – the mandatory PGY-1 residency is paid US employment requiring a work-authorising visa (J-1/H-1B), so no licence can be obtained on a visitor visa. Pending 'Empire State Licensing Act' bills (e.g. S1086/2025, S5964/2023) to delete the citizenship requirement were not confirmed as enacted. No state-wide SAVE/lawful-presence statute beyond the above was found; the Department's own status form is the verification mechanism. ADDRESS: no New York address or residency requirement anywhere in §6604 or Part 61; §6502(5) only requires notifying the Department of any change of name or mailing address within 30 days.

## Yüz yüze adımlar
The dominant in-person step is the residency itself: 12 months of full-time clinical work in a CODA-accredited New York (or other US) hospital/dental facility, with NYSED residency registration within 60 days of entry (Education Law §6605(5); 8 NYCRR 61.18(d)); this cannot be done from abroad or on a visitor visa. Paperwork: NYSED applications are filed by mail/online and 'may, in lieu of being certified or sworn under oath, be subscribed by the applicant and affirmed by the applicant as true under penalties of perjury' (Education Law §6501-B), so no notarisation is needed; no fingerprinting or criminal-background check appears in Education Law Art. 133, Art. 130 or 8 NYCRR Part 61 (good moral character is 'as determined by the department' from application disclosures); there is no jurisprudence examination – instead a one-time 3-hour NY dental jurisprudence-and-ethics CE course (§6604-A(6), 8 NYCRR 61.15(c)(1)(v)), which residents may take during residency; no interview. Pre-licensure coursework in identification and reporting of child abuse (NYSED-approved providers, available online per NYSED page snippet) and infection-control training every four years (Education Law §6505-B) are course-based and can be done online. The INBDE is taken at Prometric centres (including abroad). Net: everything except the residency could be done remotely or during a short visit, but the residency makes the route impossible for this candidate.

## Diğer başlangıç şartları
Age 21+ (§6604(5)); good moral character (§6604(7)); doctoral dental degree from a program 'registered by the department or accredited by an accrediting organization acceptable to the department' (8 NYCRR 61.1(a)(1) – a CODA-accredited program such as Yeditepe qualifies; foreign unaccredited graduates must instead do two years in an accredited school, 61.1(b)); passing NBDE Parts I & II / INBDE verified directly by JCNDE (61.2(b)); completed CODA-accredited PGY-1 with written outcome assessment and program-director attestation (61.18); NYSED-approved child-abuse identification/reporting coursework (NYSED page snippet); infection-control coursework every four years, documented at registration (§6505-B); one-time 3-hour jurisprudence & ethics CE course within the first CE-required registration period (§6604-A(6)). No English-language test, no CPR certificate and no liability insurance are required for licensure (the insurance requirement in 61.2(a)(5) applied only to the pre-2007 Part III clinical exam). Statement of Citizenship/Immigration Status form (Form 1IS) and child-support certification (GOL §3-503) are filed with the application. Timeline: INBDE + residency (12 months) + NYSED processing; licence is issued only after the residency is completed (61.18(b)(1)).

## İlk ücretler
Statutory fees, Education Law §6604(8): '$220 ... for admission to a department conducted examination and for an initial license', '$115 for each reexamination', '$135 for an initial license for persons not requiring admission to a department conducted examination', and '$210 for each triennial registration period'; §6604-A(5): mandatory CE fee $45 per triennium (first-period licensees exempt from CE). NYSED's current published licence + first-registration fee for dentists could not be read from the NYSED site in this session (expected to be the $220 figure – verify). Residency registration fee: 'reasonable and shall not exceed the limited permit fee' of $105 per year (§6605(4)-(5)). Limited permit $105; restricted faculty licence $300 + $300 per registration (§6604-B(6)). INBDE fee is paid separately to JCNDE. Late registration: $10 per month (§6502(3)); bad-cheque fee $25 (§6502(7)).

## Lisansı sürdürme

- **renewal_period:** Triennial (every 3 years) registration; the licence itself 'shall be valid during the life of the holder unless revoked, annulled or suspended' (Education Law §6502(1)).
- **renewal_fee:** $210 triennial registration (§6604(8)) + $45 mandatory continuing-education fee (§6604-A(5); 8 NYCRR 61.15(i)(1), payable only by licensees 'engaged in the practice of dentistry in New York State') = $255 per 3 years; late filing $10 per month (§6502(3)) unless the licensee has told the Department they are not practising in NY (§6502(4)); conditional registration (CE deficiency) costs an additional full registration fee (61.15(i)(2)).
- **ce_hours:** 60 hours of 'acceptable formal continuing education' per triennial registration period (§6604-A(2); 8 NYCRR 61.15(c)(1)(iii)); exempt in the registration period in which first licensed (§6604-A(1)(b)); one-time 3 hours NY dental jurisprudence & ethics (§6604-A(6)); one-time 2 hours tobacco (61.15(c)(1)(iv)); the regulation text quoted by the ADA also states 'A dentist must also complete three (3) credit hours of CPR/ACLS/PALS education and three (3) hours of mandated reporting of child abuse and maltreatment'; infection-control coursework every 4 years (§6505-B). CE must come from NYSED-approved sponsors (ADA CERP / NYSDA-approved sponsors are deemed approved, 61.15(h)).
- **ce_online_ok:** NO, not entirely. §6604-A(2): 'a maximum of eighteen hours of which may be self-instructional coursework as approved by the department'; 8 NYCRR 61.15(c)(1)(iii) as quoted on the ADA page: 'no more than 18 hours of such continuing education shall consist of self-study courses, and 42 must be in-person course credits.' Whether NYSED counts live, interactive webinars as 'in-person' could not be verified (NYSED CE page unreachable); if not, 42 hours per triennium must be attended physically.
- **cpr_required:** The quoted 61.15 text requires 'three (3) credit hours of CPR/ACLS/PALS education' as part of CE; the text does not state that a hands-on skills session is required, and no CPR card is required for licensure or registration. Verify with the NYSED dentistry CE page.
- **practice_hours_required:** None. Neither §6604-A nor 61.15 contains any minimum practice or currency-hours requirement for registration.
- **address_or_residency_required:** None. No in-state address or residency requirement; §6502(5) only requires notifying the Department of changes of name or mailing address within 30 days (any address, including abroad).
- **insurance_required:** None. No mandatory professional-liability insurance for licensure or registration (the only insurance clause, 61.2(a)(5), related to the pre-2007 clinical exam).
- **inactive_status:** New York has no formal 'inactive licence' category because the licence is lifetime; the equivalent is simply not registering. Education Law §6502(4): 'Any licensee who is not engaging in the practice of his profession in this state and does not desire to register shall so advise the department. Such licensee shall not be required to pay an additional fee for failure to register at the beginning of the registration period.' 8 NYCRR 61.15(b)(2)(i)(b) exempts from CE 'licensees who are not engaged in the practice of dentistry, as evidenced by not being registered to practice in New York State'; §6604-A(1)(c) likewise exempts a dentist 'not engaged in public practice' on filing a statement. Fee: $0; CE: none; no maximum duration; anyone may use it.
- **reactivation:** No clinical re-examination or refresher course. 8 NYCRR 61.15(c)(2): a licensee returning after a lapse 'shall submit an application for renewal of registration ... and evidence of acceptable continuing education': (i) if 'lawfully practicing in another jurisdiction', 'three years of acceptable continuing education completed within the three years immediately preceding the submission of such application' (i.e. 60 hours, at 1 2/3 hours per month); (ii) if 'not ... practicing in another jurisdiction', 'three years of acceptable continuing education completed within the 12 months immediately preceding the submission of such application'. Plus the registration fee + $45 CE fee, current infection-control training (§6505-B), and 'No licensee resuming practice after a lapse of registration shall be permitted to practice without actual possession of the registration certificate' (§6502(3)). Late fees apply only if the licensee failed to notify the Department (§6502(4)). The 18-hour self-study cap presumably applies to the catch-up hours too.
- **remote_maintainability:** hard
- **details:** The licence itself is the easiest in the US to 'park': it never expires, non-registration costs nothing, requires no CE, has no time limit, and reactivation needs only 60 hours of CE (no exam) plus fees. Keeping an ACTIVE registration from abroad, however, is impeded by the regulatory text that 42 of the 60 triennial CE hours 'must be in-person course credits' (only 18 self-study), which implies travel to live courses roughly every 18-36 months unless NYSED accepts live webinars as in-person (unverified). There are no practice-hour, address, insurance or hands-on-CPR card requirements for renewal. Because of the in-person CE component the active-status rating is 'hard' under the task's definition; a licensee content to hold the lifetime licence unregistered and reactivate when needed would find it 'easy'. All of this is academic for the candidate, since initial licensure is unattainable without a US residency and citizenship/PR (or a shortage-area waiver).
