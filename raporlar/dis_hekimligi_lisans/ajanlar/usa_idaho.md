# Idaho (USA) — ajan bulgusu

Düzenleyici: Idaho Board of Dentistry (administered by the Idaho Division of Occupational and Professional Licenses, DOPL) — https://dopl.idaho.gov/bod/

**Teori-only rota:** no · **Göçmenlik engeli:** high · **Uzaktan sürdürme:** moderate · **Güven:** medium

## Sınav yolları

- **Licensure by examination (INBDE + Board-approved regional clinical exam + Idaho jurisprudence exam)** (el becerisi: evet): Idaho Code 54-915(5) and 54-918 require passing the examinations 'conducted by the board or its agent'; IDAPA 24.31.01.100.02.a requires the INBDE and 100.02.b requires 'a Board-approved clinical examination upon such subjects as specified by the Board'. Per the ADA Licensure-by-State summary (updated 24 Oct 2025, citing the 'Idaho Dentist Application Licensure by Examination'), accepted exams are ADEX (CDCA-WREB-CITA) and CRDTS+SRTA, and 'Examinations taken after 04/01/2022 must include the following clinical components: Patient-based or Manikin operative section; Patient-based or Manikin periodontal section; Simulated endodontic section; Simulated prosthodontic section; A written competency examination.' Clinical exam results are valid for licensure by examination for 5 years. Also required: written jurisprudence exam (75% pass, 54-918(1)), proof of current BLS certification (rule 100.01.b), CODA graduation (rule 100.01.a), application fee.
- **DLOSCE-only / computer-based OSCE route** (el becerisi: evet): NOT AVAILABLE. Idaho is not among the states listed in the DLOSCE Candidate Guide, and neither the statute, IDAPA 24.31.01 nor the ADA summary mention DLOSCE/OSCE; the required clinical exam must include manikin/patient operative and periodontal sections plus simulated endo and prostho. No theory-only clinical pathway exists.
- **PGY-1 residency in lieu of clinical exam** (el becerisi: evet): NOT AVAILABLE. No provision in Idaho Code 54-915/54-918 or IDAPA 24.31.01 allows a postgraduate residency to substitute for the Board-approved clinical examination.
- **Licensure by credentials (Idaho Code 54-916B)** (el becerisi: evet): Not open to a new graduate: requires an active licence in good standing in another state, at least 5 years of clinical practice immediately preceding application with a minimum of 3,500 hours, CODA graduation, National Board exams AND 'successfully completed a board-approved clinical examination' (so a hands-on exam is still required).
- **Specialty licence (IDAPA 24.31.01.100.04)** (el becerisi: evet): Requires a CODA-accredited postdoctoral advanced education programme of at least 2 full-time academic years plus a general licensure exam acceptable to the Board, a specialty exam, or American Board certification. Not applicable to the candidate (no residency intended).

## Teori-only açıklaması
IDAPA 24.31.01.100.02 (effective 7-1-24): 'a. Written Examination. Applicants for dentistry and dental hygiene are required to pass the INBDE or NBDHE.' 'b. Clinical Examination. Applicants for general dentistry, dental hygiene or dental therapy are required to pass a Board-approved clinical examination upon such subjects as specified by the Board. ... Clinical examination results will be valid for licensure by examination for a period of (5) five years from the date of successful completion of the examination.' Idaho Code 54-918(2): 'In lieu of conducting clinical examinations, the board may require and accept the results of clinical examinations administered by national or regional testing organizations approved by the board.' The ADA summary of the Board's own application states the accepted exams are 'American Board of Dental Examiners (ADEX, CDCA-WREB-CITA), CRDTS+SRTA' and that 'Examinations taken after 04/01/2022 must include the following clinical components: Patient-based or Manikin operative section; Patient-based or Manikin periodontal section; Simulated endodontic section; Simulated prosthodontic section; A written competency examination.' There is no DLOSCE, OSCE, portfolio or residency alternative, so a hands-on manikin/patient clinical exam is unavoidable.

## DLOSCE notları
Idaho does not accept the DLOSCE in fulfilment of its clinical examination requirement: it is absent from the DLOSCE Candidate Guide's list of accepting states, and the Board's rules/application (as summarised by the ADA, updated 24 Oct 2025) require a Board-approved clinical exam containing operative, periodontal, simulated endodontic and simulated prosthodontic sections plus a written competency exam. Under the 2026 ADEX integration, the DLOSCE is merely the written/competency component of the full ADEX series; an ADEX-reported DLOSCE pass without the manikin components would not satisfy Idaho's required sections. Earlier standalone DLOSCE scores are likewise not recognised. No Idaho Board policy on the ADEX-integrated DLOSCE was found (the DOPL Board of Dentistry page makes no mention of DLOSCE).

## Göçmenlik / kimlik

- **citizenship_or_pr_required:** False
- **work_authorization_required:** False
- **lawful_presence_verification:** True
- **ssn_or_sin_required:** False
- **itin_or_no_ssn_alternative:** Idaho Code 73-122 (applies to 'any application for a professional, occupational or recreational license'): '(2) The requirement that an applicant provide a social security number shall apply only to applicants who have been assigned a social security number. (3) An applicant who has not been assigned a social security number shall: (a) Present written verification from the social security administration that the applicant has not been assigned a social security number; and (b) Submit a birth certificate, passport or other documentary evidence issued by an entity other than a state or the United States; and (c) Submit such proof as the department may require that the applicant is lawfully present in the United States.' So there is a no-SSN alternative (SSA 'no number assigned' letter + passport), but it is explicitly tied to proof of lawful presence; an ITIN is not mentioned. Source: https://legislature.idaho.gov/statutesrules/idstat/Title73/T73CH1/SECT73-122/
- **in_jurisdiction_address_required:** False
- **barrier_level:** high
- **details:** No citizenship, permanent residence or work-authorisation requirement appears in Idaho Code 54-915/54-916 or IDAPA 24.31.01. However two state-wide statutes bite: (1) Idaho Code 67-7903(1): 'each agency ... of this state shall verify the lawful presence in the United States of each natural person eighteen (18) years of age or older who applies for state or local public benefits' (professional licences are state public benefits under 8 U.S.C. 1621, and 67-7903(7) expressly says an agency 'may accept as prima facie evidence of an applicant's lawful presence ... the information required in subsection (4) ... when issuing a professional license or a commercial license'). Verification under 67-7903(4) is by electronic means, or by listed documents (Idaho/other-state driver's licence or ID, US passport, immigration-court/USCIS/DOS/CBP documents showing the individual 'may lawfully enter and remain in the United States', etc.), 'Requiring the applicant to provide a valid social security number', and an attestation under penalty of perjury that the applicant '(i) ... is a United States citizen or legal permanent resident; or (ii) ... is otherwise lawfully present in the United States pursuant to federal law'; aliens who attest lawful presence are verified through the federal SAVE program (67-7903(6)). (2) Idaho Code 73-122 requires the SSN on every professional-licence application, and the no-SSN alternative requires an SSA letter plus proof of lawful presence. A Turkish citizen holding a B-1/B-2 visa and a valid I-94 is arguably 'lawfully present pursuant to federal law' while physically in the US, and SAVE can verify nonimmigrants, but the Board/DOPL has not published guidance on whether a visitor-status applicant (especially one applying from abroad, i.e. not present in the US) satisfies the lawful-presence test; this is discretionary. Obtaining the SSA 'no number assigned' verification letter requires an in-person SSA visit in the US. The actual DOPL dentist application form was not read (tool budget), so the exact form fields/attestation wording are unverified.
- **citations:** Idaho Code 73-122 (Social security number) https://legislature.idaho.gov/statutesrules/idstat/Title73/T73CH1/SECT73-122/ ; Idaho Code 67-7903 (Verification of lawful presence) https://legislature.idaho.gov/statutesrules/idstat/Title67/T67CH79/SECT67-7903/ ; Idaho Code 54-915, 54-916, 54-918 (as quoted in ADA Idaho_Licensure summary) ; IDAPA 24.31.01 https://adminrules.idaho.gov/rules/current/24/243101.pdf

## Yüz yüze adımlar
(1) Hands-on clinical exam (ADEX/CRDTS/SRTA) at a US testing site - unavoidable, in person. (2) Written jurisprudence exam 'conducted by the board' (Idaho Code 54-918(1), 75% pass) - delivery format (online vs in-person) not verified on the DOPL site. (3) BLS certification (IDAPA 24.31.01.100.01.b) - normally requires a hands-on skills session; can be done during a US visit or with a provider abroad (American Heart Association international). (4) Criminal history/'investigation' under 54-916 - whether fingerprint-based and whether mailed cards are accepted was not verified (not stated in the rule text read). (5) SSA 'no SSN assigned' letter under Idaho Code 73-122(3)(a) - requires an in-person visit to a US Social Security office. (6) Lawful-presence verification under 67-7903 (attestation + documents/SAVE) - could be done during a visit, but applicability to visitor status is unclear. (7) Notarisation - not mentioned in the rules read; unverified. DOPL applications are submitted through its online services portal.

## Diğer başlangıç şartları
Proof of graduation from a CODA-accredited programme at time of graduation (rule 100.01.a); INBDE pass (rule 100.02.a); Board-approved hands-on clinical exam with results no older than 5 years (rule 100.02.b); written jurisprudence exam, 75% pass (Idaho Code 54-918(1)); proof of current BLS certification (rule 100.01.b); no disqualifying criminal conviction per Idaho Code 67-9411(1) (54-915(1)); application 'filed with the board at a sufficient time to permit the board to investigate the applicant' (54-916); SSN or Idaho Code 73-122 no-SSN package (SSA letter + passport + proof of lawful presence); lawful-presence verification under Idaho Code 67-7903. No English test, no liability insurance requirement found. Processing timelines not published on the pages read.

## İlk ücretler
DOPL fee schedule effective 1 July 2026 (https://dopl.idaho.gov/bod/): Dentist/Dental Specialist application fee $300; initial licence fee $280; renewal fee $0 (DOPL 'Fee Holiday' for the Board of Dentistry since 1 Jan 2026); late fee $50. Statutory caps: application fee max $300 (54-916); biennial licence fee max $400 active / $200 inactive (54-920). External costs: INBDE, ADEX series (~$2,995, includes DLOSCE), BLS course, jurisprudence exam fee (not published on pages read), SSA letter (free).

## Lisansı sürdürme

- **renewal_period:** Biennial. Idaho Code 54-920(3): dental licences renew 'prior to September 30 of every even-numbered calendar year'. DOPL note: 'As of August 1st, 2026, DOPL has begun transitioning the Board of Dentistry to biennial licensure.'
- **renewal_fee:** Currently $0 for dentists under the DOPL Fee Holiday (fee table effective 1 July 2026). Statutory maximum (54-920(2)) $400 biennial for active status, $200 for inactive status. Late fee $50 (within 30 days of expiry); after that the licence is cancelled.
- **ce_hours:** 30 hours per biennial renewal for an active-status licence: IDAPA 24.31.01.100.06 'A licensee renewing an active status license shall report 30 oral health/health-related continuing education hour credits to the Board of verifiable CE or volunteer practice.' No CE requirement is stated for inactive-status renewal.
- **ce_online_ok:** The current rule (7-1-24) contains no restriction on online/self-study CE and no live/in-person component is specified; 'volunteer practice' may also count. Board audit/approval practice not verified - treat as likely all-online but unconfirmed.
- **cpr_required:** Yes for practising licensees: IDAPA 24.31.01.100.01.b 'Practicing licensees must maintain current BLS certification.' BLS normally needs a hands-on skills session (AHA offers these worldwide). The wording 'practicing licensees' suggests inactive-status holders are not required to keep BLS, but this is not explicit.
- **practice_hours_required:** None for renewal of an active licence. (3,500 hours/5 years applies only to licensure-by-credentials applicants, 54-916B.)
- **address_or_residency_required:** No Idaho residency or in-state address requirement found. 54-920(3) only refers to the 'licensee's address of record on file with the board'.
- **insurance_required:** No mandatory professional liability insurance found in Idaho Code title 54 ch. 9 or IDAPA 24.31.01.
- **inactive_status:** Idaho Code 54-920(6)(b) and (7)(b): 'The board may issue a license with inactive status to any qualified applicant or qualified licensee who fulfilled the licensure requirements but does not practice in the state of Idaho. Renewal of a license with inactive status requires compliance with requirements specified in rule.' Fee capped at $200 biennial (currently $0 renewal under the fee holiday, confirmation for inactive not verified). No CE is specified for inactive renewal in the current rule and no maximum duration is stated.
- **reactivation:** Not specified in the current (7-1-24) rule text read; the slimmed-down IDAPA 24.31.01 no longer contains an explicit reactivation section, so the Board's practice (CE catch-up? new clinical exam after long inactivity?) is unclear. Lapse: 54-920(4) - licence expires if not renewed; if not renewed with the $50 late fee within 30 days it is cancelled. DOPL general FAQ: a licence lapsed less than five years may be reinstated with a renewal application, renewal fees, a reinstatement fee and proof of CE; longer lapses require re-application. Clinical exam results are only valid for 5 years for licensure by examination.
- **remote_maintainability:** moderate
- **details:** An Idaho dental licence can in principle be kept from abroad: biennial renewal, currently $0 fee, 30 CE hours with no stated in-person component, no practice-hour, residency, address or insurance requirement, and an inactive status with no stated CE and a low capped fee. The awkward parts are the BLS requirement for practising licensees (hands-on skills session) and the absence of published reactivation rules for inactive/lapsed licences, which creates uncertainty about whether a new clinical exam would be demanded after many years inactive.
