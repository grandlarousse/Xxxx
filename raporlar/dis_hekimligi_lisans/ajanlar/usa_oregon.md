# Oregon (USA) — ajan bulgusu

Düzenleyici: Oregon Board of Dentistry (OBD) — https://www.oregon.gov/dentistry/Pages/apply-license.aspx

**Teori-only rota:** partial · **Göçmenlik engeli:** low · **Uzaktan sürdürme:** moderate · **Güven:** medium

## Sınav yolları

- **Licensure by Examination – regional/national hands-on clinical exam (ADEX via CDCA-WREB-CITA, CRDTS, SRTA, Delaware Practical Board exam)** (el becerisi: evet): OAR 818-021-0010(3): 'An applicant must pass a Board examination consisting of a clinical portion administered by the Board, or any clinical Board examination administered by any state, or regional testing agency, national testing agency or other Board-recognized testing agency and a jurisprudence portion administered by the Board. Clinical examination results will be recognized by the Board for five years.' ADA summary (updated 8 Oct 2026) lists ADEX (CDCA-WREB-CITA), CRDTS, SRTA 'performed on manikins or patients' or the Delaware Practical Board exam. Also required: CODA graduation, INBDE or NDEB written certification (818-021-0010(1)(c)), jurisprudence exam, 1-hour Oregon pain-management course (818-021-0010(5)). Three failures of a clinical exam trigger mandatory remedial training (818-021-0010(4)). Fees: $445 application + $50 PDMP + $490 biennial licence fee.
- **Licensure by Examination – DLOSCE (JCNDE computer-based OSCE) as the 'national testing agency' clinical exam** (el becerisi: hayır): The rule text accepts a clinical exam from a 'national testing agency'; JCNDE is a national testing agency. OBD Board minutes of 12 Dec 2025 record the JCNDE representative stating that 'the Oregon Board of Dentistry currently accepts the Dental Licensure Objective Structured Clinical Examination (DLOSCE) for dental licensure' and the 2026 DLOSCE Candidate Guide lists Oregon. No separate OBD rule names the DLOSCE; acceptance rests on the 'national testing agency' wording. Standalone DLOSCE administrations ended 9 Oct 2026; a standalone pass is usable for 5 years from the exam date. For new candidates the DLOSCE is now only obtainable inside the ADEX series (five manikin exams + DLOSCE), and no OBD policy was found accepting an ADEX-reported DLOSCE pass alone.
- **Licensure by Examination – NDEB Virtual OSCE as 'other Board-recognized testing agency' exam** (el becerisi: hayır): Not an established pathway. NDEB appears in OAR 818-021-0010(1)(c) only as an alternative to the INBDE written exam ('Certification of having passed the dental examination administered by the Joint Commission on National Dental Examinations or Canadian National Dental Examining Board Examination'). ORS 679.070(3)(b)(B) says the board 'May accept the results of board-recognized testing agencies' (discretionary). No board decision recognising the NDEB Virtual OSCE as a clinical exam was found in the 2025–2026 minutes/packets reviewed.
- **Licensure Without Further Examination (by credentials) – OAR 818-021-0011** (el becerisi: evet): Requires an active, unrestricted licence in another state AND 'Having passed the dental clinical examination conducted by a regional testing agency, by a state dental licensing authority, by a national testing agency or other Board-recognized testing agency', plus either that exam within 5 years or 3,500 hours of licensed practice in the preceding 5 years + 40 CE hours, jurisprudence exam, pain-management course. Fee $890 application + $50 PDMP + $490 licence fee. Not available to a new graduate without another state licence; a prior clinical exam is still required (a standalone DLOSCE pass would satisfy it in principle).
- **PGY-1 / residency in lieu of clinical exam** (el becerisi: evet): NOT available. Neither ORS 679.070 nor OAR 818-021-0010/0011 allow a postgraduate residency to substitute for the clinical examination. A 2-year CODA postdoctoral General Dentistry residency is mentioned in 818-021-0011(1)(b) only as an educational-equivalency route for non-US/Canadian graduates applying by credentials, and still requires a passed clinical exam.
- **Portfolio / state-administered Board clinical exam** (el becerisi: evet): The rule mentions 'a clinical portion administered by the Board' but the OBD does not currently administer its own clinical exam and has no portfolio pathway (none on the OBD apply-license page).

## Teori-only açıklaması
Rule text (OAR 818-021-0010(3), quoted on the OBD apply-license page and in the ADA summary updated 8 Oct 2026): 'An applicant must pass a Board examination consisting of a clinical portion administered by the Board, or any clinical Board examination administered by any state, or regional testing agency, national testing agency or other Board-recognized testing agency and a jurisprudence portion administered by the Board. Clinical examination results will be recognized by the Board for five years.' ORS 679.070(3)(b): to satisfy the clinical examination the board '(A) Shall accept the results of regional and national testing agencies or clinical board examinations administered by other states; and (B) May accept the results of board-recognized testing agencies.' Because the DLOSCE is a 'national testing agency' (JCNDE) exam, a theory-only route (INBDE + DLOSCE + open-book jurisprudence + online pain-management course) exists on paper, and OBD minutes of 12 Dec 2025 record that 'the Oregon Board of Dentistry currently accepts the DLOSCE for dental licensure.' However, standalone DLOSCE administrations ceased 9 Oct 2026; new candidates can only take the DLOSCE as a component of the ADEX series, which includes manikin hand-skill exams, and ADEX states 'Separate registration of the DLOSCE component is NOT an option.' No OBD rule, minute or policy was found accepting an ADEX-reported DLOSCE component pass by itself, and the Board's 2026 'ADEX merger' rulemaking (June 12, 2026 minutes; Sept 9, 2026 Licensing, Standards & Competency Committee packet) concerns updating agency names, not creating a DLOSCE-only route. Hence: yes for someone who already holds a standalone DLOSCE pass dated within the last 5 years; otherwise currently impractical = partial.

## DLOSCE notları
OAR 818-021-0010(3) accepts a clinical exam of a 'national testing agency' (JCNDE) and ORS 679.070(3)(b)(A) says the board 'Shall accept the results of regional and national testing agencies'. OBD Board Meeting Minutes, 12 Dec 2025 (JCNDE report): 'Ms. Rowley stated that the Oregon Board of Dentistry currently accepts the Dental Licensure Objective Structured Clinical Examination (DLOSCE) for dental licensure and will accept the DHLOSCE for dental hygiene licensure.' Results are recognised for five years, so an earlier standalone DLOSCE pass (before 9 Oct 2026) remains usable until 5 years after the exam date. The ADA Oregon summary (8 Oct 2026) lists only ADEX/CRDTS/SRTA/Delaware in its header, which suggests the ADA no longer treats DLOSCE-alone as a live option. Nothing in the June 12, 2026 Board minutes (ADEX report by Stephen Prisby; referral of ADEX-merger amendments to OAR 818-035-0072 and 818-042-0095) or the Sept 9, 2026 LSC Committee packet ('ADEX Merger ... CDCA-WREB-CITA into ADEX. The committee discussed removing testing agency-[specific references]... The motion passed') addresses accepting the DLOSCE component of ADEX alone or the NDEB Virtual OSCE. The October 23, 2026 Board minutes were not yet posted. Whether the OBD would license on an ADEX score report showing DLOSCE pass but manikin failures is unresolved; ADEX reports pass/fail per component, and the OBD's rule speaks of passing the 'clinical Board examination' of the agency, which for ADEX means the whole series.

## Göçmenlik / kimlik

- **itin_or_no_ssn_alternative:** Statutory: ORS 25.785 (title: 'Issuing entities to require Social Security number or other identification', as amended 2025 c.99 §3): '(2) If an individual does not have a Social Security number, a federal individual taxpayer identification number or identification issued by the federal government, a state agency, board or commission described in subsection (1) of this section may accept a written statement from the individual to fulfill the requirement in subsection (1) of this section. (3) An individual may not submit ... a written statement ... knowing the statement to be false' (false statement = Class A misdemeanour, ORS 25.990(3)). The statute thus treats an ITIN or other federally issued identification as satisfying the requirement and makes a written no-SSN statement discretionary ('may accept'). OBD-specific: the OBD Privacy Act Notification (form, cites ORS 25.785 and 42 USC 666(a)(13)) still says 'You are required to provide your Social Security Number to the OBD... Failure to provide your Social Security Number will be a basis to refuse to issue your license' and the OBD application has a mandatory SSN field; no ITIN/no-SSN procedure is published on oregon.gov/dentistry. Comparison: the Oregon Medical Board online application states 'If you have not been issued a Social Security Number, please contact the Board at 971-673-2700 or licensing@omb.oregon.gov to provide a federally issued iden[tification number]...' i.e. a sister board under the same statute accepts a federal ID number (ITIN) plus statement. The Oregon Health Licensing Office requires an ITIN if no SSN (search result, not read). Expect the OBD to follow ORS 25.785 on request (Information@obd.oregon.gov) but this is unconfirmed.
- **in_jurisdiction_address_required:** False
- **barrier_level:** low
- **details:** No citizenship, permanent-residence, work-authorisation or lawful-presence requirement appears in ORS 679.060 (application requirements: fee, conviction details, CODA diploma), OAR 818-021-0010, the OBD apply-license page or the OBD applicant FAQ (greps for citizen/lawful/immigration/visa returned nothing). Oregon has no state-wide SAVE/lawful-presence statute for professional licences and none is referenced by the OBD. The only identity hurdle is the SSN field: ORS 25.785 as amended in 2025 now contemplates an ITIN/other federal identification and allows a written statement from a person with none; the OBD's 2013-era privacy notice has not been updated and the online application's SSN field behaviour for ITINs could not be verified. An ITIN is obtainable by a non-resident Turkish citizen (IRS Form W-7). Mailing address of record can be foreign (renewal notice 'to the last mailing address on file'); no Oregon address is required.

## Yüz yüze adımlar
(1) Fingerprint-based criminal background check: the OBD packet states 'Live Scan fingerprints can only be transmitted electronically. Once the Oregon Board of Dentistry receives your application and application fee, we will send you the Request for Transmission for Live Scan Fingerprints form' (wording from the OBD Dental Therapy licensure packet; the dentist online application is expected to use the same Live Scan process but this was not separately verified). Live Scan requires attending an Oregon-approved electronic fingerprint vendor in person; no ink-card option for applicants abroad is published, so plan a US visit (the same visit used for exams/notarisation). (2) Jurisprudence examination: 'open book' and, per the OBD packet, mailed to the applicant and returned by mail (for the online dentist application it is handled through the application portal) — can be completed from abroad. (3) Oregon one-hour pain-management course (OHA Pain Management Commission) is an online module — from abroad. (4) Notarisation: the paper OBD application packets must be 'completed in full, notarized'; the dentist application is online, so notarisation of the main form is not confirmed as required; any notarised supporting affidavits can be done at a US notary during a visit or (if accepted) via a US consulate/online notary — unverified. (5) No interview, no in-person jurisprudence sitting, no in-state residency. (6) The hands-on clinical exam (ADEX etc.), if taken, requires attending a US exam site; a standalone DLOSCE was at Prometric centres (available internationally) but is no longer offered. Processing: 'On average, it takes approximately four weeks from the time you submit your application/fees for a license to be issued'; the Board does not expedite.

## Diğer başlangıç şartları
Graduation from a CODA-accredited dental school (OAR 818-021-0010(1)(a)); certification of passing the JCNDE exam (INBDE) or the NDEB written exam (818-021-0010(1)(c)); clinical exam within 5 years (see pathways); Board jurisprudence examination (open book; unlimited retakes, must be passed within 5 years of the clinical exam per 818-021-0010); one-hour Oregon-specific pain-management course from the OHA Pain Management Commission before initial licensure (818-021-0010(5)); evidence of any convictions (ORS 679.060(2)(b)) via Live Scan fingerprint background check; English: exam is in English, no separate English test for CODA graduates; no CPR certificate listed as an application item but licensees must maintain BLS for Healthcare Providers (see maintenance); no liability-insurance requirement found. Timeline: about four weeks after a complete application and fees.

## İlk ücretler
Licensure by Examination: Application fee $445.00 + Prescription Drug Monitoring Program fee $50.00 + Biennial Licensure fee $490.00 = $985 (OBD apply-license page). Licensure Without Further Examination: $890.00 application + $50 PDMP + $490 licence fee = $1,430. Optional permits: nitrous oxide $40, minimal sedation $75, moderate sedation $200, general anaesthesia $400. Fingerprint vendor fee, clinical exam fee (ADEX series $2,995) and pain-management course are extra.

## Lisansı sürdürme

- **renewal_period:** Biennial. OAR 818-021-0070(1): dentists submit the renewal fee and online renewal application by March 31 every other year (licensed in odd year -> renew in odd years; even -> even). OAR 818-021-0080: renewal notice sent to the last mailing address on file; renewal is online and must be completed before expiry.
- **renewal_fee:** $490 biennial licensure fee (figure from the OBD apply-license page; the renewal-fee rule itself was not read). Late/penalty fees for lapsed licences not verified.
- **ce_hours:** 40 hours every two years (OAR 818-021-0060(1)), 'directly related to clinical patient care or the ...'. Mandatory content: at least 3 hours medical emergencies in a dental office; at least 2 hours infection control; at least 2 hours cultural competency; 1-hour Oregon pain-management course (OHA) each renewal (818-021-0060(5)); 7 hours implant CE if placing/restoring implants. Caps: max 4 hours practice management/patient relations; max 12 hours teaching/scientific sessions; max 6 hours for pro bono/authorship/board service etc.
- **ce_online_ok:** Yes. OAR 818-021-0060(3)(c) counts 'Correspondence courses, videotapes, distance learning courses or similar self-study course, provided that the course provides a certificate of completion' with no cap on this category; the pain-management module is online. Nothing requires live or in-person CE, so all 40 hours can be done online from abroad (keep certificates; audits at renewal).
- **cpr_required:** Yes in practice: the OBD requires licensees to maintain current 'BLS for Healthcare Providers' certification (the Board's disciplinary guidelines in the 21 Aug 2026 packet list 'FAILURE TO MAINTAIN BASIC LIFE SUPPORT (BLS) FOR HEALTHCARE PROVIDERS' as a violation and specify 'BLS for Healthcare Providers (not CPR/AED)'; sedation/anaesthesia permit rules also require it). The specific base rule number was not read. BLS-HCP certification normally requires a hands-on skills check (AHA/Red Cross blended online + in-person skills session), which may be obtainable abroad from AHA international training centres.
- **practice_hours_required:** None for renewal of an active licence (no practice-hour requirement in 818-021-0060/0070/0080). 3,500 hours in 5 years appears only as an alternative to a recent clinical exam for licensure by credentials (818-021-0011).
- **address_or_residency_required:** No. Renewal is online and notices go to the 'last mailing address on file'; no Oregon address or residency requirement found; correspondence by email.
- **insurance_required:** No mandatory professional liability insurance requirement found in ORS 679 / OAR 818 materials reviewed.
- **inactive_status:** Oregon has a 'Retired' licence status (OBD agendas list 'Request for Reinstatement of a Retired License' and the renewal page references inactive/expired/retired licences), but the governing rule text (believed to be OAR 818-021-0025) was not read, so fee, CE and duration limits are unclear. Expired licences require Board-approved reinstatement (Aug 2026 Board approved individual 'reinstatement of expired license' requests).
- **reactivation:** Unclear. Reinstatement of an expired or retired licence is handled case-by-case by Board motion (June/Aug 2026 minutes show individual approvals). The reinstatement rule (believed OAR 818-021-0085) with its time limits, back fees, CE make-up and any re-examination requirement was not read. Safest course: keep the licence active continuously via online CE + BLS + $490 biennial fee.
- **remote_maintainability:** moderate
- **details:** Active status can be maintained from abroad: biennial online renewal, $490 fee (plus PDMP $50 if also charged at renewal — unverified), 40 hours CE that can be entirely distance-learning/self-study, no practice hours, no in-state address, no insurance. The awkward element is the BLS for Healthcare Providers certificate, which involves a hands-on skills session (available at AHA international training sites, including Turkey). Retired/inactive and reinstatement rules not verified.
