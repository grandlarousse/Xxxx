# Oregon (USA) — ajan bulgusu

Düzenleyici: Oregon Board of Dentistry (OBD) — https://www.oregon.gov/dentistry/

**Teori-only rota:** partial · **Göçmenlik engeli:** medium · **Uzaktan sürdürme:** moderate · **Güven:** medium

## Sınav yolları

- **Licensure by Examination via ADEX Dental Examination (CDCA-WREB-CITA, now merged into ADEX)** (el becerisi: evet): OAR 818-021-0010(3): applicant must pass 'any clinical Board examination administered by any state, or regional testing agency, national testing agency or other Board-recognized testing agency' plus the Board jurisprudence exam; results recognised for five years. ADEX series = five manikin hand-skill components (perio, endo, prostho, anterior & posterior restorative) + the computer-based DLOSCE; $2,995 + typodont/facility fees. Candidate must also hold INBDE (or NDEB) pass, CODA degree, fingerprint background check, 1-hr Oregon pain-management course, BLS.
- **Licensure by Examination via CRDTS/SRTA or another state's clinical board exam (e.g. Delaware Practical Board)** (el becerisi: evet): Same rule (OAR 818-021-0010(3)); the ADA Oct-2026 summary lists ADEX, CRDTS+SRTA 'performed on manikins or patients' or the Delaware Practical Board exam as accepted clinical exams. All are hands-on.
- **Licensure by Examination using the DLOSCE alone (JCNDE/ADA computer-based OSCE) as the 'national testing agency / Board-recognized testing agency' clinical exam** (el becerisi: hayır): Oregon is listed in the JCNDE 2026 DLOSCE Candidate Guide (updated 4/2026) as accepting the DLOSCE in FULL fulfilment of its clinical exam requirement (no asterisk), and OBD minutes of 12 Dec 2025 record that 'the Oregon Board of Dentistry currently accepts the Dental Licensure Objective Structured Clinical Examination (DLOSCE) for dental licensure'. There is no OAR text naming the DLOSCE; acceptance rests on the 'national testing agency / other Board-recognized testing agency' wording of OAR 818-021-0010(3) and ORS 679.070(3)(b). HOWEVER the standalone DLOSCE is no longer offered to new candidates (standalone administrations ceased after 9 Oct 2026; ADEX FAQ: 'Separate registration of the DLOSCE component is NOT an option'), so this route is only usable by someone who already holds a standalone DLOSCE pass (valid for 5 years under 818-021-0010(3)) or if OBD agrees to accept the DLOSCE component of the ADEX series on its own (unconfirmed).
- **PGY-1 / residency in lieu of clinical examination** (el becerisi: evet): NOT available in Oregon. Neither ORS 679.060-679.090 nor OAR 818-021-0010/0011 contains any provision substituting a postgraduate residency for the clinical examination. Residency hours only count as 'licensed clinical practice' hours toward Licensure Without Further Examination (818-021-0011(1)(e)), which still requires a passed clinical exam and an active licence elsewhere.
- **Licensure Without Further Examination (by credentials), OAR 818-021-0011** (el becerisi: evet): Requires (c) having passed a dental clinical exam of a regional/state/national/Board-recognized testing agency, (d) an active unrestricted licence in another state, (e) 3,500 hours of licensed clinical practice in the 5 years before application (teaching at a CODA school counts), (f) 40 CE hours in the prior 2 years, plus jurisprudence exam and pain-management course. Not open to a new graduate; hands-on unless the earlier licence was obtained on a DLOSCE-only basis. Fee $890 + $50 PDMP + $490 licence.
- **Specialty licence by examination (OAR 818-021-0017) / Dental Faculty licence** (el becerisi: evet): Specialty route requires a CODA-accredited post-graduate specialty programme and a CDCA/ADEX specialty or American Board exam; Dental Faculty licence (fee category in OAR 818-001-0087) is tied to employment at an Oregon CODA programme. Neither is relevant to a new general-dentistry graduate.

## Teori-only açıklaması
Rule text (OAR 818-021-0010(3), quoted on the ADA Oregon page updated 8 Oct 2026 and on the OBD apply-license page): 'An applicant must pass a Board examination consisting of a clinical portion administered by the Board, or any clinical Board examination administered by any state, or regional testing agency, national testing agency or other Board-recognized testing agency and a jurisprudence portion administered by the Board. Clinical examination results will be recognized by the Board for five years.' Statute ORS 679.070(3)(b): to satisfy the clinical examination the board '(A) Shall accept the results of regional and national testing agencies or clinical board examinations administered by other states; and (B) May accept the results of board-recognized testing agencies.' The JCNDE 2026 DLOSCE Candidate Guide lists Oregon (without the 'partial' asterisk) among states where the DLOSCE is 'accepted ... in full or partial fulfillment of clinical examination requirements', and OBD minutes of 12 Dec 2025 record that 'the Oregon Board of Dentistry currently accepts the Dental Licensure Objective Structured Clinical Examination (DLOSCE) for dental licensure'. So a written/computer-only route (INBDE + DLOSCE + open-book jurisprudence exam + online pain course) is written into Oregon practice with no residency and no hands-on exam. It is rated 'partial' because the only vehicle, the standalone DLOSCE, has been withdrawn: per the ADA press release of 14 Apr 2026 the DLOSCE 'will no longer be offered or administered as a standalone examination to new DLOSCE candidates, except in conjunction with the ADEX Dental Examination, after August 1, 2026. All standalone administrations of the DLOSCE will cease after October 9, 2026', and ADEX's FAQ states 'Separate registration of the DLOSCE component is NOT an option' and that 'All candidates will challenge five hand-skill components and the ADA's Dental Licensure OSCE'. For a candidate who has not already passed a standalone DLOSCE, the theory-only route therefore depends on OBD agreeing to accept the DLOSCE component of the ADEX series by itself (ADEX reports every component pass/fail to boards, but OBD has not published a position). The ADA Oregon summary now lists only ADEX, CRDTS+SRTA and the Delaware practical exam as the clinical exams it knows of.

## DLOSCE notları
No OAR or ORS text names the DLOSCE; acceptance is by Board recognition under the 'national testing agency / other Board-recognized testing agency' language of OAR 818-021-0010(3) and ORS 679.070(3)(b)(B). Evidence of acceptance: (1) JCNDE 2026 DLOSCE Candidate Guide (updated 4/2026) lists Oregon as accepting the DLOSCE in full fulfilment (Alaska and Iowa carry the 'partial, with a hands-on assessment' asterisk; Oregon does not); (2) OBD Board meeting minutes 12 Dec 2025: 'the Oregon Board of Dentistry currently accepts the Dental Licensure Objective Structured Clinical Examination (DLOSCE) for dental licensure' (statement by the ODHA representative, recorded without correction; the same meeting noted the ADA/ADEX joint statement and that rule changes related to the CDCA-WREB-CITA/ADEX merger would go to the Licensing, Standards and Competency Committee). Earlier standalone scores: OAR 818-021-0010(3) recognises clinical exam results for five years, so a standalone DLOSCE passed on or before 25 Sep 2026 (JCNDE: results via DENTPIN for exams 'on or before September 25, 2026') remains usable until 5 years after the pass date. DLOSCE taken as part of ADEX: ADEX FAQ says 'State Boards will accept the DSE OSCE and DLOSCE as valid components of the ADEX Dental Examination series' and ADEX reports all historical pass/fail results per component to boards, but 'Separate registration of the DLOSCE component is NOT an option' (the $2,995 package includes the five manikin exams; the DLOSCE is scheduled at Prometric from Aug 2026). OBD has published nothing on whether it would license on the ADEX-DLOSCE component alone without the hand-skill components; the Dec 2025/Feb 2026 minutes show merger-related rule changes still pending. The ADA Oregon summary (8 Oct 2026) no longer mentions the DLOSCE. Treat DLOSCE-only as unconfirmed for anyone who has not already passed the standalone exam.

## Göçmenlik / kimlik

- **citizenship_or_pr_required:** False
- **work_authorization_required:** False
- **lawful_presence_verification:** False
- **ssn_or_sin_required:** True
- **itin_or_no_ssn_alternative:** Board's own Privacy Act Notification (Rev. 12/2013, still linked from the apply-license page): 'As part of your application for an initial professional license, you are required to provide your Social Security Number to the Oregon Board of Dentistry (OBD). This is a mandatory requirement under ... ORS 25.785 and under Federal Law USC section 666(a)(13)(a). Failure to provide your Social Security Number will be a basis to refuse to issue your license.' No ITIN or no-SSN affidavit is published by OBD for dentists. However the statute it cites, ORS 25.785(2), states: 'A state agency, board or commission described in subsection (1) of this section may accept a written statement from an individual who has not been issued a Social Security number by the United States Social Security Administration to fulfill the requirement in subsection (1)' (other Oregon boards, e.g. the Veterinary Medical Examining Board and OHA Health Licensing Office, use such statements/ITINs). So the alternative exists in statute but is discretionary ('may') and not advertised by OBD; the online application has an SSN field. HB 3830 (2025), which would have barred licensing boards from denying a licence 'solely on the basis of the individual's immigration or citizenship status' and regulated ITIN use, died in the House Committee on Commerce and Consumer Protection (no chapter number).
- **in_jurisdiction_address_required:** False
- **barrier_level:** medium
- **details:** ORS 679.060 (application) and ORS 679.065 (qualifications: 18+, CODA graduate) and OAR 818-021-0010 contain no citizenship, permanent-residence, work-authorisation or lawful-presence requirement, and OBD rules contain no SAVE/immigration-status verification; Oregon has no state-wide lawful-presence law for professional licences (the 2025 bill on the subject failed). The fee schedule (OAR 818-001-0087(1)(b)(N) 'Non-Resident Dental Background Check - $100.00') and the renewal rule (mailing address plus business or home address, any location) show that non-residents are expected. The only identity hurdle is the SSN: OBD's notice says it is mandatory and a ground for refusal, while ORS 25.785(2) lets the Board accept a written no-SSN statement at its discretion. A visitor with no US status is therefore not barred by rule, but must obtain OBD's agreement to the ORS 25.785(2) written statement (or an ITIN) before relying on this jurisdiction.
- **citations:** ORS 679.060, 679.065 (https://oregon.public.law/statutes/ors_679.060; ADA Oregon summary); OAR 818-021-0010 (https://oregon.public.law/rules/oar_818-021-0010); OBD Privacy Act Notification (https://www.oregon.gov/dentistry/Documents/Form_Privacy_Act_Notification.pdf); ORS 25.785 (https://oregon.public.law/statutes/ors_25.785); OAR 818-001-0087 fees (https://oregon.public.law/rules/oar_818-001-0087); OAR 818-021-0080 renewal (https://oregon.public.law/rules/oar_818-021-0080); HB 3830 (2025) overview (https://olis.oregonlegislature.gov/liz/2025R1/Measures/Overview/HB3830).

## Yüz yüze adımlar
(1) Fingerprint-based state and FBI background check is compulsory for every dental applicant (OAR 818-021-0026(1): 'The Board requires fingerprints of all applicants'; refusal = incomplete fitness determination). OBD instruction sheets state: 'Live Scan fingerprints can only be transmitted electronically. Once the Oregon Board of Dentistry receives your application and application fee, we will send you the Request for Transmission for Live Scan Fingerprints form. Electronic fingerprints are now available throughout the United States.' i.e. prints are taken at a Live Scan vendor anywhere in the US (not only Oregon) during a visit; a 'Non-Resident Dental Background Check' fee of $100 applies (OAR 818-001-0087). Whether ink cards from abroad are accepted is not published (OSP pages unavailable); the Feb 2026 minutes show OSP pressing boards to make fingerprinting compulsory, so expect Live Scan in the US. A new background check is also required on reinstatement of a retired/expired (>60 days) licence. (2) Jurisprudence examination: Board-administered, 'open book', fee $0; OBD sheets say it is sent to the applicant after the application/fee are received and returned by mail; since the move to the online portal it is presumably delivered through the portal/email (format unverified) and needs no in-person attendance. (3) No interview. (4) Notarisation: the pre-portal paper application had to be 'completed in full, notarized'; the current application is submitted through the online licensing portal (FAQ: 'Applications for dental ... licensure are completed through our online application portal'); whether a notarised affidavit is still uploaded is unconfirmed. (5) Pain-management course: 1-hour online OPMC module with online post-test and certificate. (6) BLS card: photocopy/upload. (7) Official transcript sent by the school; INBDE score via JCNDE. (8) The clinical exam itself (ADEX manikin series at a US site, DLOSCE at a Prometric centre) requires US presence. Net: one US trip (any state) for Live Scan prints plus the exam(s); all other steps can be completed from abroad.

## Diğer başlangıç şartları
Written exam: 'Certification of having passed the dental examination administered by the Joint Commission on National Dental Examinations or Canadian National Dental Examining Board Examination' (OAR 818-021-0010(1)(c)); INBDE accepted. Jurisprudence exam (Board, open book, $0). One-hour Oregon-specific pain-management course from the Oregon Pain Management Commission before initial licensure (818-021-0010(5)); free online module. BLS for Healthcare Providers 'or its equivalent' must be held at all times (OAR 818-012-0030(18)); copy submitted with application. Fingerprint background check (OAR 818-021-0026); disclosure of all arrests/charges/convictions. Registration with the Oregon Prescription Drug Monitoring Program ($50 fee collected with application). No English test for graduates of a CODA school (English proficiency is required only under (1)(b) for graduates of schools outside the US/Canada who complete a 2-year CODA programme). No liability-insurance requirement for dentists (dental therapists must show it). Age 18+. Application valid 180 days (OAR 818-021-0120) and the exam must be taken within that period. Timelines: FAQ says 'approximately four weeks from the time you submit your application/fees for a license to be issued'; older sheets say 6-8 weeks, background checks 'can take several weeks'. Dean's certification allows application before graduation (818-021-0010(2)). Three failures of a clinical exam require remedial training at a CODA school.

## İlk ücretler
OBD apply-license page (2026): Application fee $445 + Prescription Drug Monitoring Program fee $50 + biennial licensure fee $490 = $985 mandatory (jurisprudence exam $0; optional nitrous $40 / minimal sedation $75 / moderate $200 / GA $400). Non-Resident Dental Background Check $100 plus Live Scan vendor charges (OAR 818-001-0087 as mirrored May 2025 still shows older $345 application / $390 licence; the Board website figures are the current ones). Licensure Without Further Examination application $890. Exam costs: ADEX Dental Examination series $2,995 + $150 typodont per procedure + facility fee (DLOSCE retake $695); a standalone DLOSCE was about $575 and is no longer available. Fees are non-refundable (ORS 679.120(8)).

## Lisansı sürdürme

- **renewal_period:** Biennial: renew online by 31 March every other year (dentists licensed in odd years renew in odd years, even in even) - ORS 679.120(5)-(6), OAR 818-021-0080. Licence year runs 1 April - 31 March.
- **renewal_fee:** Biennial dental licence fee $490 per OBD website (OAR 818-001-0087 mirror from 2025 still lists $390 - the rule has since been raised); retired $0; volunteer $0; late penalties $50 (<=30 days), $100 (31-59 days), $150/$250 + back fees + $500 reinstatement thereafter (OAR 818-021-0085). Paid online by US-issued card or e-check.
- **ce_hours:** 40 hours every two years, 'directly related to clinical patient care or the practice of dental public health' (OAR 818-021-0060(1)); must include >=3 h medical emergencies, >=2 h infection control, >=2 h cultural competency, and the 1-hour Oregon pain-management course at each renewal; max 4 h practice management/patient relations, max 12 h teaching/scientific sessions, max 6 h in the pro-bono/authorship/board-service category; implant placers +7 h. Records kept 4 years; audited on request.
- **ce_online_ok:** Yes in substance: OAR 818-021-0060(3)(c) expressly counts 'Correspondence courses, videotapes, distance learning courses or similar self-study course, provided that the course provides a certificate of completion' with no cap on such hours; the pain-management hour is an online module; medical-emergency, infection-control and cultural-competency hours are available online. No live/in-person CE component is mandated. (Only the pro-bono-in-Oregon category is location-bound and it is optional.)
- **cpr_required:** Yes. OAR 818-012-0030(18) makes it unprofessional conduct to 'Fail to maintain at a minimum a current BLS for Healthcare Providers certificate or its equivalent'; the Board's renewal page says 'During the renewal process you will be required to upload a profile picture and your Healthcare Provider BLS', and its retirement article says BLS must be kept current 'even if you are no longer practicing'. AHA BLS Provider (and most 'equivalents') require an in-person skills session; AHA international training centres (including in Turkey) can supply it, but a fully online-only CPR card is unlikely to be accepted.
- **practice_hours_required:** None for renewal of an active licence (renewal statement covers CE, jurisdictions practised in, discipline/convictions). 3,500 practice hours in 5 years are required only for Licensure Without Further Examination applicants and for specialty endorsements.
- **address_or_residency_required:** No Oregon address or residency. Renewal must list a mailing address and a business address 'or if the licensee has no business address, licensee's home address' (OAR 818-021-0080(3)); change of address within 30 days (ORS 679.120(4)). Non-resident status is contemplated by the fee schedule.
- **insurance_required:** No professional-liability insurance requirement for dentists in ORS 679/OAR 818 (proof of liability insurance is required only for dental therapists; volunteer licensees are offered reduced-rate cover).
- **inactive_status:** 'Retired' licence (OAR 818-021-0090; ORS 679.120(3)(a)): fee $0, no CE, no BLS; BUT available only to 'A dentist ... who no longer practices in any jurisdiction' (practising abroad disqualifies; the Board then only lets the licence expire). Request a retirement form by email before expiry; signed form must reach OBD within 60 days after expiry. Maximum useful duration 4 years: 'If the dentist ... fails to reinstate her or his license within four years from retiring the license, the dentist ... must apply for licensure under the current statute and rules of the Board.' Volunteer licence ($0) is an active licence requiring 80 h pro-bono work in Oregon per cycle plus CE and BLS - not usable from abroad. Expired licence can likewise be reinstated only within 4 years.
- **reactivation:** Retired <4 years (OAR 818-021-0090(2)): $500 reinstatement fee; pass the Board's jurisprudence exam; 'Pass any other qualifying examination as may be determined necessary by the Board after assessing the applicant's professional background and credentials'; good-standing letters from all states where licensed; CE for the period; new fingerprint background check. ORS 679.120(3)(b): 'if more than one year has expired since the license was retired or inactivated, satisfactory evidence of operative competence must be submitted to the board' - what evidence suffices is not specified in rule. Expired licence: tiered penalties (OAR 818-021-0085) - within 1 year $150 penalty + back fees + $500 + CE + background check; 1-4 years $250 penalty + back fees + $500 + jurisprudence exam + any qualifying exam the Board requires + good-standing letters + CE + background check. After 4 years retired or expired the licence is gone and a new application under current rules is required, which for someone without 3,500 practice hours means a clinical exam passed within the previous 5 years.
- **remote_maintainability:** moderate
- **details:** An ACTIVE Oregon dental licence can be kept indefinitely from abroad: online renewal every 2 years (about $490), 40 CE hours that can all be distance-learning/self-study with certificates (including the free online Oregon pain-management hour), no practice-hour requirement, no in-state address, no insurance. The awkward parts are (a) a continuously current BLS for Healthcare Providers card (hands-on skills session, obtainable at AHA international centres), (b) a US-issued card/e-check for payment, and (c) the fact that the $0 retired status is only open to those not practising anywhere and lasts at most 4 years before the licence is lost; reinstatement after >1 year retired requires 'evidence of operative competence' and possibly a qualifying exam at the Board's discretion, with a fresh fingerprint check. Practical strategy for a dentist working in Turkey: keep the licence active (retirement is not permitted while practising elsewhere).
