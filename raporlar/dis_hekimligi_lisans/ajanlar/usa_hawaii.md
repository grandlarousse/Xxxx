# Hawaii (USA) — ajan bulgusu

Düzenleyici: Hawaii Board of Dentistry (Department of Commerce and Consumer Affairs, Professional and Vocational Licensing Division) — https://cca.hawaii.gov/pvl/boards/dentist/

**Teori-only rota:** no · **Göçmenlik engeli:** high · **Uzaktan sürdürme:** moderate · **Güven:** high

## Sınav yolları

- **Examination route: INBDE/NBDE + full ADEX dental examination (CDCA-WREB-CITA) - the ONLY route to a full dentist licence** (el becerisi: evet): HRS 448-9 requires a CODA-accredited diploma plus proof of passing the National Board Dental Examination (INBDE accepted as the successor); HRS 448-9.4: 'An applicant shall take and pass the ADEX examination, and neither the state examination nor any regional examination shall be accepted.' HAR 16-79-9(a): applicant must (1) be 18+, (2) pass the NBDE, (3) pass the ADEX dental examination, (4) be a US citizen/national/permanent resident/alien authorized to work in the US, (5) be a CODA graduate. Board application instructions (DT-01, rev 08/2024): 'Dentist licensure applicants must still pass all sections of the ADEX exam in their entirety. The ADEX full manikin based dental licensure exam taken on or after June 5, 2020 is acceptable.' The ADEX series includes manikin-based endodontic, fixed prosthodontic and restorative (CompeDont) sections plus periodontal, so a hands-on component is unavoidable. Application form question 5: 'Have you taken and passed all 5 parts of the ADEX dental examination?'
- **DLOSCE-only / OSCE-only route** (el becerisi: hayır): NOT available. Hawaii is not on the JCNDE list of DLOSCE-accepting states and HRS 448-9.4 / HAR 16-79-9(a)(3) require passage of the ADEX dental examination in full; the board accepts no other clinical exam. A DLOSCE pass (standalone or ADEX-integrated) alone does not satisfy the rule.
- **PGY-1 residency in lieu of clinical exam** (el becerisi: evet): NOT available. No residency alternative exists in HRS 448 or HAR 16-79; the only residency-related provision is a Temporary Dentist (DTT) licence for post-doctoral residents (form DT-09b), which is not a full licence and still requires work authorization/SSN.
- **Licensure by credentials / endorsement / reciprocity** (el becerisi: evet): NOT available. The ADA summary's 'License by Credential/Endorsement' section cites only HAR 16-79-11 (documentation: official NBDE verification, official ADEX verification, certified dental degree). Hawaii does not waive the ADEX exam for dentists licensed elsewhere; every applicant must have passed the ADEX (HRS 448-9.4). Only a Community Service Dentist licence (HRS 448-9.5, form DT-16; practice limited to approved community health settings) and Temporary licences (DT-09/DT-09b, military spouse) exist as alternatives, none of which is a full unrestricted licence and all still require US work authorization and an SSN.
- **Portfolio / state-administered clinical exam** (el becerisi: evet): NOT available. HRS 448-9.4 expressly states that 'neither the state examination nor any regional examination shall be accepted' (the former Hawaii state clinical exam was abolished in favour of ADEX). No portfolio route exists.

## Teori-only açıklaması
HRS 448-9.4 (as quoted on the ADA Hawaii Laws & Rules page, updated 7 Oct 2026): 'American Board of Dental Examiners (ADEX) examination; regional examinations. An applicant shall take and pass the ADEX examination, and neither the state examination nor any regional examination shall be accepted.' HAR 16-79-9(a) (HAR 16-79, compilation 08/2016, read directly): 'A person applying for licensure to practice dentistry shall comply with the following requirements: (1) Be eighteen years of age or more; (2) Pass the National Board Dental Examination; (3) Pass the ADEX dental examination; (4) Be a United States citizen, United States national, permanent resident of the United States, or alien authorized to work in the United States; and (5) Be a graduate from a dental school accredited by CODA.' Board application instructions: 'Dentist licensure applicants must still pass all sections of the ADEX exam in their entirety. The ADEX full manikin based dental licensure exam taken on or after June 5, 2020 is acceptable for a dentist license.' The ADEX series necessarily includes manikin-based hand-skill sections, so no computer/written-only route exists, and there is no residency, credentials or portfolio alternative.

## DLOSCE notları
Hawaii does not accept the DLOSCE (standalone or otherwise) as its clinical examination; it is not among the DLOSCE-accepting states and HRS 448-9.4 / HAR 16-79-9(a)(3) require the full ADEX dental examination. Nothing in the board's rules, application instructions (rev 08/2024) or FAQs (rev 07/2025) mentions the DLOSCE. Because Hawaii requires 'all sections of the ADEX exam in their entirety', a candidate taking the 2026+ ADEX will have to pass the integrated DLOSCE/DSE component AND every manikin section; an ADEX-reported DLOSCE pass alone is worthless here. Earlier standalone DLOSCE scores are irrelevant to Hawaii.

PGY-1 kabul: False

- **barrier_level:** high
- **details:** Hawaii is an absolute bar for this candidate. HAR 16-79-9(a)(4) requires every dentist applicant to 'Be a United States citizen, United States national, permanent resident of the United States, or alien authorized to work in the United States.' The board's application instructions state: 'Pursuant to section 436B-10, Hawaii Revised Statutes, all applicants shall be a United States ("U.S.") citizen, U.S. national, or an alien authorized to work in the United States in addition to meeting the academic, clinical and examination requirements for licensure. Therefore, even if an applicant meets the academic, clinical training and examination requirements for licensure, that applicant shall not be issued a license if that applicant is not a U.S. citizen, a U.S. national, or an alien authorized to work in the U.S. However, the Board will issue the applicant a conditional approval that signifies that the applicant has met the educational, experience, and examination requirements for licensure; provided that this conditional approval shall not be considered a license to engage in the profession and shall not authorize the applicant to work in our State. To obtain authorization to work in our State, the applicant shall be required to contact the U.S. Citizenship and Immigration Services (USCIS)...' The licence is issued only once the applicant provides proof of work authorization and a Social Security Number (and within a deadline, failing which re-application may be required). The application form itself asks (Q2): 'Are you a U.S. citizen, a U.S. national, or an alien authorized to work in the United States? YES/NO' and has a mandatory Social Security No. field. Verification is by the board's own review of the applicant's status/work-authorization evidence and SSN (no SAVE programme mentioned). A visitor-visa holder cannot be licensed; at best they could obtain a non-licence 'conditional approval'. No Hawaii residency or in-state address requirement was found (the form asks for a residence address, which may be anywhere).

## Yüz yüze adımlar
No fingerprinting, criminal background check, jurisprudence exam or interview is required for initial dentist licensure (none appears in HRS 448 excerpts, HAR 16-79, the application instructions or the FAQs; the application header lists 'NPDB', suggesting a National Practitioner Data Bank self-query report is submitted - obtainable online). Notarisation is not mentioned. The application is a paper form mailed with a cheque payable to 'Commerce & Consumer Affairs' to the PVL Licensing Branch, 335 Merchant Street, Honolulu; supporting verifications (NBDE/INBDE, ADEX, degree/transcript, other-state licence verifications) must be sent directly by the issuing bodies. All of this could be done from abroad. The only physical-presence step is the ADEX dental examination itself (manikin-based hand-skill sections at a CDCA-WREB-CITA test site, which need not be in Hawaii). Processing takes a minimum of 45-60 business days; applicants must complete the process within one year of filing. In practice the immigration/SSN bar makes these steps moot: the licence will not be issued without US work authorization and an SSN.

## Diğer başlangıç şartları
Age 18+; CODA-accredited dental degree (certified copy of degree/certificate or official transcript sent directly to the board); official verification of passing the NBDE/INBDE; official verification of passing all sections of the ADEX dental examination (CDCA-WREB-CITA); verification of any other dental licences held; completed DT-01 application with SSN and citizenship/work-authorization declaration plus proof of US work authorization; no jurisprudence exam, no English test, no CPR requirement at initial licensure (BLS is a renewal CE requirement), no liability insurance requirement, no state-residency requirement. Applications are accepted on an ongoing basis; minimum processing 45-60 business days; application file destroyed if licensure not completed within 1 year. Three failures of ADEX require a one-semester postgraduate course in operative/prosthetic dentistry before re-taking (HAR 16-79-114).

## İlk ücretler
Dentist licence: $448 if issued in an even-numbered year (Application $100 non-refundable + License $120 + Compliance Resolution Fund $148 + 1/2 renewal for the second year of the biennium $80) or $294 if issued in an odd-numbered year (Application $100 + License $120 + CRF $74). Plus ADEX examination fees paid to CDCA-WREB-CITA (approx. $2,995 for the full series per the ADEX FAQ) and INBDE fees. Temporary dentist licence $75; anesthesia permit $100.

## Lisansı sürdürme

- **renewal_period:** Biennial; all licences expire December 31 of each odd-numbered year regardless of issuance date (HAR 16-79-144; application instructions 'BIENNIAL RENEWAL'). Next renewal: by 31 Dec 2027.
- **renewal_fee:** Not stated as a single figure in the sources read; implied by the fee breakdown as Renewal $160 + Compliance Resolution Fund $148 = approx. $308 per biennium for an active licence (the application shows '1/2 renewal ... $80' and 'CRF $74/$148'). Online renewal via MyPVL. Inactive-status renewal requires the renewal plus an 'inactive license fee' (amount not found).
- **ce_hours:** 32 CE hours per biennium (16 if first licensed in the first year of the biennium; none if licensed in the second year) - HAR 16-79-144. Composition (HAR 16-79-141): more than half must be clinical courses directly related to patient care; a continuously current BLS for Healthcare Providers course with a hands-on component (AHA/American Red Cross), max 4 hrs credit; ethics training of at least 3 hours per year (HAR 16-79-144(1)(D); the HAR 16-79-141(a)(3)(B) text as summarised by the ADA reads 'six hours per year' - inconsistency, verify); volunteer, instructor and convention attendance hours are capped. Courses must come from approved sponsors listed in HAR 16-79-142 (AGD PACE, ACCME, ADA/ADA CERP providers, AHA, ARC, CODA programmes, state dental associations etc.).
- **ce_online_ok:** NOT under the standing rule: HAR 16-79-141(d) allows CE 'through computer, correspondence courses, or courses presented via other media, such as audio and video tape recording; provided that those courses do not comprise more than eight CE hours' per biennium. However, the Board voted on 26 Jan 2026 to extend a blanket pandemic-hardship waiver (HAR 16-79-147(b)(5)) accepting ALL CE completed through virtual means for the 1 Jan 2026 - 31 Dec 2027 biennium only, 'The blanket waiver does NOT include the skills-portion of Basic Life Support (BLS), Advanced Cardiac Life Support (ACLS), or Pediatric Advanced Life Support (PALS) courses. Therefore, the skills-portion of BLS, ACLS, and PALS courses must be taken in-person.' So at least the BLS skills session is always in person, and absent future waivers ~24 of 32 hours must be live (not necessarily in Hawaii - any approved sponsor's live course qualifies).
- **cpr_required:** Yes. HAR 16-79-141(a)(2): BLS courses 'Shall be completed, continuously current, and include a hands-on component'; must be a Basic Life Support for Healthcare Providers course sponsored by the American Heart Association or American Red Cross (or board-approved sponsor). The 2026 virtual-CE waiver notice confirms the BLS skills portion must be taken in person. Required for active-status renewal only; not required while on inactive status (FAQ Q4: CEs are not required while inactive).
- **practice_hours_required:** None to renew or hold an active licence. BUT restoration of a FORFEITED licence more than 2 years after expiration requires evidence of active clinical practice of at least 1,000 hours per year for the 3 years immediately preceding the request plus certification from the other jurisdiction's board (HAR 16-79-3.1(b)(2)).
- **address_or_residency_required:** No Hawaii residency or in-state address requirement found. The application requires a residence address and mailing address (can be foreign). Licensees must keep the board informed of their current address (standard HRS 436B requirement).
- **insurance_required:** No mandatory professional liability insurance requirement found in HRS 448, HAR 16-79, the application instructions or FAQs.
- **inactive_status:** Available: HAR 16-79-12.7 - 'Upon written request by a licensee during the licensure period or at renewal, and upon payment of an inactive license fee, the board shall place the licensee's active license on an inactive status. (b) A licensee may continue and renew on inactive status for the biennial period.' Board page: 'Inactive status licenses must be renewed by the renewal deadline to remain on inactive status.' FAQ Q4: 'You may not practice on inactive status. Inactive licenses must still be renewed by the renewal deadline; however, CEs are not required while on inactive status.' No maximum duration is stated (can apparently be renewed on inactive status indefinitely). Inactivation is requested on a hard-copy renewal form / 'Inactivation' application; the inactive licence fee amount was not found. Anyone holding an active licence qualifies.
- **reactivation:** From inactive (HAR 16-79-12.7(e)): at any time by completing a reactivation application, fulfilling all requirements in effect at the time including payment of an activation fee and other fees, meeting the CE requirements (i.e. the biennial 32-hour CE including hands-on BLS and ethics), and providing fitness-to-practise information (sanctions, pending discipline, convictions). No re-examination or practice-hour requirement for reactivation from inactive status. Lapse/forfeiture (HAR 16-79-3.1; FAQ Q3): failure to renew on time automatically forfeits the licence; restoration within 2 years of expiration on written application, renewal + penalty + CRF fees and full CE; restoration between 2 and 5 years additionally requires 3 years of active clinical practice of at least 1,000 hours/year with certification from the other jurisdiction, CE of 32 hours per biennium, an NPDB self-query and possibly a board-approved background check, failing which the person 'may be required to reapply as a new applicant, take and pass the licensure examination'; after more than 5 years the person 'shall be required to reapply as a new applicant, take and pass the licensure examination [ADEX], submit a self-query report from NPDB, and submit a report from an independent background check service'. Restoration forms are hard copy, mailed with payment.
- **remote_maintainability:** moderate
- **details:** Moot for this candidate because the licence cannot be issued at all without US work authorization and an SSN. For a licence-holder living abroad: active status is awkward - 32 CE hours per biennium of which, under the standing rule, only 8 may be computer/correspondence based (waived for the 2026-2027 biennium only) and a hands-on BLS skills session is always required; CE may come from any approved sponsor (ADA CERP, AGD PACE, ACCME providers) so live courses abroad can count. Inactive status is the practical option: renew every odd-numbered year, pay renewal + inactive fee, no CE, no stated time limit, and reactivation needs only an application, fees and the current biennium's CE (incl. in-person BLS) - no exam. Never let the licence forfeit: after 2 years restoration needs 1,000 practice hours/year for 3 years, and after 5 years the ADEX must be retaken. No practice-hour, residency, address or insurance requirement for active or inactive renewal. Renewal is online via MyPVL (inactivation and CE-waiver requests require hard-copy forms by mail).
