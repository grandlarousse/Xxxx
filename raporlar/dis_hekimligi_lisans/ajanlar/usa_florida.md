# Florida (USA) — ajan bulgusu

Düzenleyici: Florida Board of Dentistry (Florida Department of Health, Division of Medical Quality Assurance) — https://floridasdentistry.gov/

**Teori-only rota:** no · **Göçmenlik engeli:** high · **Uzaktan sürdürme:** moderate · **Güven:** medium

## Sınav yolları

- **Examination route: INBDE + Florida Laws and Rules Examination + ADEX Dental Licensing Examination (American Dental Licensing Examination) administered in Florida** (el becerisi: evet): The only initial-licensure route for a new graduate. s. 466.006(2)(c) F.S. requires the JCNDE exam (INBDE); s. 466.006(4) requires BOTH (a) a written Laws and Rules exam (registered through CDCA-WREB-CITA at adextesting.org) and (b) 'a practical or clinical examination, which must be the American Dental Licensing Examination produced by the American Board of Dental Examiners, Inc.' ADEX includes typodont/manikin hand-skill sections (ADA page: 'Typodont manikin-based or patient examination'). Candidate must also be 18+, graduate of a CODA-accredited school (Yeditepe post-1 Feb 2024 qualifies), supply CPR certification, transcripts, INBDE scores, Livescan fingerprints; fees $100 application + $305 initial licensure.
- **ADEX scores from another jurisdiction (taken within 365 days)** (el becerisi: evet): s. 466.006(4)(b)1.: 'As an alternative to such practical or clinical examination, an applicant may submit scores from an American Dental Licensing Examination previously administered in a jurisdiction other than this state after October 1, 2011.' Same hands-on ADEX exam, just taken at a non-Florida site; Laws and Rules exam still required.
- **ADEX scores older than 365 days (Florida's de-facto 'licensure by credentials')** (el becerisi: evet): s. 466.006(4)(b)2.: out-of-state ADEX scores older than 365 days are valid only if the applicant graduated from a CODA school, 'currently possesses a valid and active dental license in good standing ... from another state or territory of the United States', discloses NPDB reports, and proves consecutive full-time practice (minimum 1,200 hours/year) in a US jurisdiction for the 5 years preceding application (or since initial licensure), with tax returns for each year. Not available to a new graduate without a US licence; still ADEX-based.
- **MOBILE Act licensure by endorsement (s. 456.0145)** (el becerisi: evet): Requires an active, unencumbered licence from another US state/DC/territory plus at least 2 years of active practice in the preceding 4 years and no pending discipline; fee $305. Not an initial pathway for a new graduate and presupposes a hands-on-exam-based US licence.
- **DLOSCE-only / OSCE-only** (el becerisi: hayır): NOT AVAILABLE. Florida statute, board website and the ADA Florida page (updated 7 Oct 2026) contain no reference to the DLOSCE or any OSCE; s. 466.006(4)(b) mandates that the clinical examination 'must be the American Dental Licensing Examination produced by the American Board of Dental Examiners'. Florida is not on the DLOSCE 2026 Candidate Guide list of accepting states.
- **PGY-1 residency in lieu of clinical exam** (el becerisi: hayır): NOT AVAILABLE. No residency-in-lieu-of-exam provision in Ch. 466. Florida issues resident/intern permits (s. 466.025) and teaching permits only for practice inside the programme/dental school; they are not licences and do not waive ADEX.
- **Portfolio / state-specific clinical exam** (el becerisi: hayır): NOT AVAILABLE. s. 466.006(1)(a) states the legislative intent 'to use the American Dental Licensing Examination ... in lieu of an independent state-developed practical or clinical examination'; no portfolio pathway exists.

## Teori-only açıklaması
s. 466.006(4), F.S. (quoted on the ADA Florida page, updated 7 Oct 2026): 'Notwithstanding any other provision of law in chapter 456 pertaining to the clinical dental licensure examination or national examinations, to be licensed as a dentist in this state, an applicant must successfully complete both of the following: (a) A written examination on the laws and rules of the state regulating the practice of dentistry. (b) A practical or clinical examination, which must be the American Dental Licensing Examination produced by the American Board of Dental Examiners, Inc., or its successor entity, if any, which is administered in this state ... 1. As an alternative to such practical or clinical examination, an applicant may submit scores from an American Dental Licensing Examination previously administered in a jurisdiction other than this state after October 1, 2011.' The ADA summary lists the initial licensure examination as 'American Board of Dental Examiners (ADEX, CDCA-WREB-CITA) (Typodont manikin-based or patient examination)'. The board's own page lists required exams as (1) National Board (INBDE), (2) ADEX Dental Licensing Examination, (3) Florida Laws and Rules Examination. No OSCE, DLOSCE, residency, portfolio or credentials alternative exists for a new graduate, so a hands-on manikin/patient exam is unavoidable.

## DLOSCE notları
Florida statute (Ch. 466), the Board of Dentistry website and the ADA Florida summary make no mention of the DLOSCE or any OSCE; Florida is not among the DLOSCE-accepting states in the 2026 Candidate Guide. Because s. 466.006(4)(b) requires the ADEX exam itself, a Florida candidate taking ADEX from Aug 2026 onward will sit the integrated DLOSCE as one ADEX component, but the manikin hand-skill components must also be passed; nothing in Florida law or board guidance treats an ADEX-reported DLOSCE pass (or any earlier standalone DLOSCE score) as satisfying the clinical examination on its own. Standalone DLOSCE scores are irrelevant in Florida.

PGY-1 kabul: False

## Göçmenlik / kimlik

- **citizenship_or_pr_required:** False
- **work_authorization_required:** False
- **lawful_presence_verification:** False
- **ssn_or_sin_required:** True
- **itin_or_no_ssn_alternative:** None (no ITIN, no 'no-SSN' affidavit). s. 456.013(1)(a) F.S.: 'The application shall require the social security number and date of birth of the applicant, except as provided in paragraphs (b) and (c).' The only exception, s. 456.013(1)(b): 'If an applicant has not been issued a social security number by the Federal Government at the time of application because the applicant is not a citizen or resident of this country, the department may process the application using a unique personal identification number. If such an applicant is otherwise eligible for licensure, the board ... may issue a temporary license to the applicant which shall expire 30 days after issuance unless a social security number is obtained and submitted in writing to the department ... Upon receipt of the applicant's social security number, the department shall issue a new license, which shall expire at the end of the current biennium.' The 'unique personal identification number' is an internal DOH processing identifier, not an ITIN. The dental application form DH-MQA 1182 (section '2. SOCIAL SECURITY DISCLOSURE') has a single field 'U.S. Social Security Number: ____' and states SSNs are collected under s. 456.013(1)(a) and ss. 409.2577 and 409.2598 (Title IV-D child support) and 'must also be recorded on all professional and occupational license applications'; no alternative field is offered. Source: https://www.flsenate.gov/Laws/Statutes/2025/456.013 ; https://floridasdentistry.gov/Applications/dentistry-licensure-app.pdf
- **in_jurisdiction_address_required:** False
- **barrier_level:** high
- **details:** Florida imposes no citizenship, permanent-residence, work-authorisation or lawful-presence test for a dental licence: Ch. 466 and s. 456.013 contain no such requirement, the application form (DH-MQA 1182) asks no citizenship or immigration-status question, and no SAVE/affidavit-of-lawful-presence step was found. The practical barrier is the SSN: under s. 456.013(1)(a) and (12) (federal PRWORA/Title IV-D child-support enforcement) every applicant must give a US Social Security number. A non-citizen without an SSN can at most be processed under a department-assigned identifier and, at the board's discretion ('may'), receive a TEMPORARY licence that 'shall expire 30 days after issuance unless a social security number is obtained'. A Turkish visitor on a B-1/B-2 visa is generally not eligible for an SSN (SSA issues SSNs to non-citizens only with DHS work authorisation or for narrowly defined non-work reasons), so a permanent Florida licence is effectively unobtainable without work-authorised status. Address: the form asks for a mailing address (where the licence is sent) and a physical location (only required if the mailing address is a P.O. Box; posted on the DOH website); no Florida residency or in-state address requirement was found.

## Yüz yüze adımlar
(1) ADEX Dental Licensing Examination: in-person hands-on manikin (and optional patient) sections at a US test site; registration via CDCA-WREB-CITA (adextesting.org). (2) Florida Laws and Rules Examination: board page states 'The Florida Department of Health has contracted with The Commission on Dental Competency Assessments (CDCA) to provide the Laws and Rules examination ... All applicants must visit adextesting.org to register'; it is a written/computer exam, but delivery format (test-centre vs remote) was not verified in this research; can be taken at any time, including on the same US trip. (3) Fingerprinting: 'Florida passed House Bill 975 following the 2024 legislative session, which requires this profession to complete electronic fingerprinting'; 'Applicants for initial licensure must use a Livescan service provider to submit a set of fingerprints to the Florida Department of Law Enforcement (FDLE)' with the correct ORI number; 'You must print this form and take it with you to a Livescan service provider.' This is done in person at an FDLE-approved vendor (easily done on a Florida visit); whether an approved vendor will accept ink cards mailed from abroad was not verified. (4) CPR/BLS certification with in-person hands-on skills (blended online + in-person skills accepted; fully online rejected) - can be obtained from AHA/Red Cross-equivalent providers abroad. (5) No interview. (6) Application can be filed online at flhealthsource.gov or on paper; no notarisation requirement was found on form DH-MQA 1182. Transcripts and INBDE scores must be sent directly by the school/ADA. All in-person items can be completed during one or two short US visits (ADEX dates permitting).

## Diğer başlangıç şartları
Age 18+; CODA-accredited degree with final official transcripts sent directly by the registrar (embossed seal, matriculation and graduation dates); INBDE scores sent directly from the ADA/JCNDE; Florida Laws and Rules Examination (via CDCA/adextesting.org) and ADEX; current CPR certification at basic life support level (one- and two-rescuer adult/child/infant, AED, ambu-bag; AHA, American Red Cross or equivalent; must include in-person hands-on skills); electronic Livescan fingerprint background screening through FDLE/Care Provider Background Screening Clearinghouse (HB 975, 2024); health-history questions with physician letters if applicable; disclosure of prior discipline; disqualifying-offence screening under s. 456.0635; SSN (see immigration). No English-language test (CODA graduate). Application and application fee valid for one year (s. 456.013(1)(a)). No liability-insurance proof at application. Initial licence expires 28 February of the following even-numbered year (biennium 1 March - 28 February of even years). Timeline: board states initial review of new applications in about 10 business days; licensure after all documents/exam scores received.

## İlk ücretler
Board of Dentistry: Application fee $100 (non-refundable) + initial licensure fee $305 = $405 total; the initial licensure fee is halved for applicants applying in the second year of the biennium. Plus ADEX full series approx. $2,995 (known fact), Florida Laws and Rules exam fee payable to CDCA (amount not verified), Livescan fingerprint vendor fee (varies, not verified), CPR course, transcript/INBDE score transmission fees.

## Lisansı sürdürme

- **renewal_period:** Biennial; licensure biennium runs 1 March - 28 February of even-numbered years (all initial licences expire 28 February of the following even-numbered year). Renewal online via flhealthsource.gov; CE tracked in CE Broker (not verified in this research).
- **renewal_fee:** Active biennial renewal fee for dentists set by Rule 64B5-15.006 F.A.C.; the board's fee pages show the dentist licensure fee as $305 (half of this is charged to second-year initial applicants), so the biennial renewal is understood to be approx. $305 - exact current renewal amount not verified. Inactive renewal fee: Rule 64B5-15.008 (amount not verified; by s. 456.036(3) it may not exceed the active fee). Retired status fee: Rule 64B5-15.0122 (statutory cap $50). Delinquency fee: Rule 64B5-15.011 (cap = active renewal fee). Change-of-status fee: Rule 64B5-15.012.
- **ce_hours:** 30 hours per biennium for dentists (Rule 64B5-12.013(1)), which must include a board-approved 2-hour prevention of medical errors course; a 2-hour domestic violence course every third biennium; a 2-hour HIV/AIDS course no later than the first renewal (home study expressly permitted); a 2-hour course on safe and effective prescribing of controlled substances where applicable (s. 466.0135/456.0301); CPR certification is additional and does not count toward the 30 hours. Credit accepted from board-approved providers, ADA/AGD/NDA and affiliates, CODA-accredited schools, accredited universities, AHA/Red Cross, and 'board-approved individual study pursuant to Rule 64B5-12.018'.
- **ce_online_ok:** Largely yes but not fully verified. Rule 64B5-12.013(6) sets provider-based criteria, not delivery-format criteria, and online courses from board-approved/ADA CERP/AGD PACE providers are routinely accepted; home study is expressly permitted for the HIV/AIDS course. Whether Rule 64B5-12.018 (individual study) caps home-study/self-study hours was not verified. The one unavoidable live component is CPR: 'CPR training and certification shall be taken in-person or through a blended learning course ... Online training without hands-on training completed in person for certification shall not be accepted by the board' (64B5-12.013(2)(b)).
- **cpr_required:** Yes, every biennium and for reactivation: BLS-level CPR (one- and two-rescuer adult/child/infant, FBAO, AED, ambu-bag) certified by AHA, American Red Cross or an entity with equivalent requirements; must be in-person or blended (online theory + in-person hands-on skills). Fully online CPR is rejected. Can be done abroad through AHA/Red Cross-equivalent international training centres.
- **practice_hours_required:** None for renewal of an active licence. Practice hours (1,200 h/yr for 5 years) are relevant only to the out-of-state-ADEX-scores-older-than-365-days pathway and MOBILE endorsement (2 of last 4 years).
- **address_or_residency_required:** No Florida residency or in-state address requirement. Licensee must keep a mailing address (and a physical practice location if the mailing address is a P.O. Box, which is published on the DOH website) on file and report changes; a foreign address appears permissible but was not expressly confirmed.
- **insurance_required:** Not required for licensure or renewal as a condition in the documents reviewed. Florida does have a financial-responsibility rule for practising dentists (Rule 64B5-17.011 F.A.C., professional liability coverage or letter of credit, with exemptions for licensees not practising in Florida and inactive/retired licensees) - existence and current terms NOT verified in this research; treat as open.
- **inactive_status:** Available at any renewal (s. 456.036(2): licensee may choose active, inactive or retired status). Inactive renewal fee per Rule 64B5-15.008 (may not exceed active fee); change-of-status fee if switched mid-cycle. No CE needed while inactive and no stated maximum duration - inactive status can be renewed indefinitely. Retired status also available (fee capped at $50, Rule 64B5-15.0122) but reactivation after more than 5 years retired may require full re-examination (s. 456.036(10)). Practising on an inactive/retired licence is a disciplinary violation (s. 456.036(1)).
- **reactivation:** Inactive to active 'at any time, if the licensee meets all requirements for active status': pay the active fee difference/reactivation fee (Rule 64B5-15.009) and change-of-status fee; s. 456.036(11): 'Before reactivation, an inactive status licensee ... must meet the same continuing education requirements, if any, imposed on an active status licensee for all biennial licensure periods in which the licensee was inactive' (i.e. 30 h per missed biennium, plus the 2-h medical errors course and current hands-on CPR per 64B5-12.013(2)). s. 456.036(9): if inactive for more than two consecutive bienniums the board may 'impose reasonable conditions, excluding full reexamination but including part of a national examination or a special purpose examination to assess current competency'. Lapse: a licence not renewed becomes delinquent for the following cycle; failure to reactivate within that cycle renders the licence 'null' and the person must re-apply as a new applicant (s. 456.036(6)), i.e. ADEX again. Retired more than 5 years: board may require full re-examination (s. 456.036(10)).
- **remote_maintainability:** moderate
- **details:** Once held, a Florida licence can in principle be kept active from abroad: 30 h biennial CE (online courses from approved providers generally acceptable), no practice-hour requirement, no in-state address, inactive status renewable indefinitely with CE back-fill on reactivation and no full re-exam unless the licence lapses to null or is retired >5 years. The awkward parts are the mandatory hands-on CPR every biennium (obtainable abroad via AHA international training centres), possible financial-responsibility paperwork if practising in Florida, and the unverified home-study cap. The decisive obstacle remains obtaining a non-temporary licence at all without an SSN.
