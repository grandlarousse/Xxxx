# Missouri (USA) — ajan bulgusu

Düzenleyici: Missouri Dental Board (Division of Professional Registration, Department of Commerce and Insurance) — https://pr.mo.gov/dental.asp

**Teori-only rota:** no · **Göçmenlik engeli:** medium · **Uzaktan sürdürme:** easy · **Güven:** medium

## Sınav yolları

- **Licensure by examination (regional/state hands-on competency exam + INBDE + jurisprudence)** (el becerisi: evet): 20 CSR 2110-2.010: DDS/DMD from an accredited school + National Board (INBDE) + 'a state or regional entry-level competency examination' passed within 5 years + Missouri jurisprudence exam (80%) + current hands-on AHA BLS. Per 2.010(5), effective 1 Jan 2021 the competency exam must be administered by CRDTS, CDCA, WREB, SRTA, CITA (now ADEX via CDCA-WREB-CITA) or an individual state dental board; 'The tested procedures are to be patient based, manikin based, or a combination of both' and must include >=75% in six clinical-skills areas (periodontics, endodontics, posterior class II, anterior class III, anterior single-unit crown prep, posterior three-unit bridge abutment preps) PLUS written/didactic testing in removable prosthetics, diagnosis/treatment planning and oral surgery. ADEX (five manikin exams + DLOSCE) or CRDTS/SRTA satisfy this; all are hands-on.
- **Licensure without examination by credentials (RSMo 332.211)** (el becerisi: hayır): Board 'shall grant without examination' a licence to a dentist 'who has been licensed in another state for at least five consecutive years immediately preceding his applying' with qualifications at least equivalent to Missouri's; board may require the jurisprudence exam, and a practical exam only if the applicant's licence was ever disciplined for incompetency or he failed a practical exam. NOT available to a new graduate (needs 5 years of licensure elsewhere first).
- **DLOSCE / computer-based OSCE alone** (el becerisi: evet): Not a pathway. Missouri is not among the DLOSCE-accepting states and 20 CSR 2110-2.010(5)(A) requires patient- or manikin-based clinical-skills testing in six areas. A DLOSCE pass (standalone or ADEX-reported) could at most satisfy the 2.010(5)(B) written/didactic component; the manikin hand-skill exams remain mandatory.
- **PGY-1 residency in lieu of clinical exam** (el becerisi: hayır): Not provided for in RSMo Chapter 332 or 20 CSR 2110-2. A residency does not substitute for the competency examination in Missouri (residents/fellows appear only as a CE-exemption category in 20 CSR 2110-2.240).
- **Limited Teaching License** (el becerisi: hayır): Fee $230 (20 CSR 2110-2.170). Restricted to faculty practice at an accredited Missouri dental school; requires a faculty appointment, so not a route to general licensure and not usable from abroad.

## Teori-only açıklaması
20 CSR 2110-2.010(1)(C) requires that each applicant 'Have passed a state or regional entry-level competency examination ... within the previous five (5) years', and 2.010(5) states: 'Effective January 1, 2021, competency examinations shall be administered by any of the following: the Central Regional Dental Testing Service (CRDTS), the Commission on Dental Competency Assessments (CDCA), the Western Regional Examining Board (WREB), the Southern Regional Testing Agency (SRTA), the Council of Interstate Testing Agencies (CITA), or by an individual state dental board. The tested procedures are to be patient based, manikin based, or a combination of both. (A) Applicants for licensure shall obtain a passing score of seventy-five percent (75%) or above in each of the following areas: 1. Periodontics clinical skills testing; 2. Endodontics clinical skills testing; 3. Posterior class II amalgam or posterior class II composite preparation and restoration clinical skills testing; 4. Anterior class III composite preparation and restoration clinical skills testing; 5. Anterior tooth preparation for a single unit crown; and 6. Posterior tooth preparations for use as abutments for a three unit bridge.' Hand-skills testing is therefore unavoidable for a new graduate; the only exam-free route (RSMo 332.211 credentials) requires five consecutive years of licensure in another state. In addition, 2.010(1)(E) requires hands-on BLS: 'Online-only courses will not be accepted to satisfy the BLS requirement.' (ADA Missouri Licensure summary updated 9 Oct 2026; 20 CSR 2110-2 as published by the Missouri Secretary of State.)

## DLOSCE notları
Missouri's rules do not mention the DLOSCE or any OSCE, and Missouri is not on the JCNDE list of DLOSCE-accepting states. 20 CSR 2110-2.010(5)(A) requires patient- or manikin-based clinical-skills testing in six named areas, so a DLOSCE pass alone (standalone pre-Oct-2026 score or an ADEX-reported DLOSCE component) cannot satisfy the competency-examination requirement. Within the integrated ADEX series the DLOSCE replaces the former DSE OSCE and would count toward 2.010(5)(B) ('written or didactic competency testing' in removable prosthetics, diagnosis/treatment planning, oral surgery), but the five ADEX manikin hand-skill exams must still be passed; the board receives pass/fail per component and 2.010(5)(A) demands >=75% in each clinical-skills area. Competency scores must be less than 5 years old at application.

Statewide lawful-presence law: RSMo 208.009.3 provides that 'at the time of application for any state or local public benefit, an applicant who is eighteen years of age or older shall provide affirmative proof that the applicant is a citizen or a permanent resident of the United States or is lawfully present in the United States', proof including 'any document issued by the federal government that confirms an alien's lawful presence'; 208.009.6: 'An applicant who is an alien shall not receive any state or local public benefit unless the alien's lawful presence in the United States is first verified by the federal government' (SAVE). 208.009.2's definition of 'public benefit' lists grants, contracts, loans and retirement/welfare/health/disability/housing/food benefits and refers to 8 U.S.C. 1621(b); it does not expressly name professional licences (8 U.S.C. 1621(c) does), so applicability to dental licensure rests on the board's practice, which could not be confirmed from the form. If applied, a Turkish citizen admitted as a B-1/B-2 visitor is 'lawfully present' and could document it with passport/visa and I-94, subject to SAVE verification; a visitor without any status would not satisfy it. The application must be sworn (RSMo 332.141: 'made under oath or affirmation'). The SSN requirement is the most likely hard obstacle for a person with no SSN; the fee rule's authority also cites RSMo 43.543, indicating a fingerprint criminal-history check.

## Yüz yüze adımlar
(1) Clinical competency exam (ADEX/CRDTS/SRTA) in person at a US test site, manikin-based, scheduled by the testing agency. (2) Missouri jurisprudence exam: 20 CSR 2110-2.010(1)(D) says applicants 'will be scheduled to take the jurisprudence examination at the board office, at the office of one (1) of the members of the board, or at such times deemed necessary by the board' (i.e., in Jefferson City, in person; whether an online option now exists was not verifiable). RSMo 332.151 also provides that the applicant 'shall appear before the board at the time and place in or out of the state of Missouri specified by the board'. (3) Hands-on AHA BLS course 'by an instructor who is physically present with the students'; 'Online-only courses will not be accepted' (can be done in Turkey through an AHA international training centre or during the US visit). (4) Sworn/notarised application (RSMo 332.141) - can be notarised abroad. (5) Fingerprint criminal background check appears to apply (fee rule cites RSMo 43.543; Missouri uses the MACHS/IdentoGO system); whether mailed fingerprint cards are accepted for out-of-state applicants was not confirmed. (6) Application plus transcripts, INBDE score report and competency scores sent directly by the issuing bodies; passport photo. All steps can plausibly be completed during one or two visitor-visa trips, but the application must be completed within one year of submission (2.010(2)(I)).

## Diğer başlangıç şartları
Jurisprudence exam on Missouri dental laws/rules, pass mark 80% (written, scheduled by the board; re-take fee $100). Current AHA BLS for Healthcare Providers (hands-on, instructor physically present). Fingerprint-based criminal background check (RSMo 43.543 cited in the fee rule; details unverified). No English-language test, no liability-insurance requirement, no interview found in the rules. Good moral character; sworn application. Official transcript sent directly by Yeditepe; INBDE certification sent by JCNDE; competency scores sent by the testing agency (less than 5 years old); verification of any other licences. Application remains active for one year (RSMo 332.181.2); it becomes void if not completed within that period. Newly licensed dentists receive an initial licence valid until the end of the current two-year renewal period (20 CSR 2110-2.071) and are CE-exempt for the remainder of that time block (2.240(2)(D)).

## Lisansı sürdürme

- **renewal_period:** Biennial; licences renew every two years (RSMo 332.181.4), renewal forms due November 30 of the renewal year (20 CSR 2110-2.240(2)(A) references the 30 Nov cycle).
- **renewal_fee:** $150 biennial dentist renewal (20 CSR 2110-2.170(1)(C)); renewal penalty fee $100 if late (2.170(1)(D)).
- **ce_hours:** 50 hours per two-year period (RSMo 332.181.4; 20 CSR 2110-2.240(2)); at least 40 of the 50 must be 'directly related to the updating and maintaining of knowledge and skills in the treatment, health, and safety of the individual dental patient'; up to 25 excess hours may be carried forward; records kept 6 years; board may audit. Board-approved sponsors required (ADA CERP/AGD PACE-type sponsors listed in 2.240(1)(C)). Diplomate status earned in the period = deemed 50 hours.
- **ce_online_ok:** No cap on online/self-study hours was found in 20 CSR 2110-2.240 as read (the rule regulates sponsors and subject matter, not delivery format; credit is '1 hour per 50-60 minutes of contact (either academic or clinical) instruction'). Online courses from board-approved sponsors appear acceptable for all 50 hours, but this should be confirmed with the board. Attendance at open board meetings (in person) gives optional credit only. The board may also grant a CE waiver/extension for 'foreign residency' on written request at least 45 days before the renewal date (2.240(3); RSMo 332.181.4).
- **cpr_required:** Hands-on AHA BLS (instructor physically present; online-only not accepted) is required for INITIAL licensure (20 CSR 2110-2.010(1)(E)). No BLS requirement for biennial renewal was found in RSMo 332.181 or 20 CSR 2110-2.071/2.240 as read - unverified.
- **practice_hours_required:** None. No practice/currency hours are required to renew or keep an active licence (RSMo 332.181; 20 CSR 2110-2.071).
- **address_or_residency_required:** None. No Missouri address or residency required; licensee must keep the board informed of legal name, mailing address and telephone number (20 CSR 2110-2.175) and display the licence in any Missouri office where practising.
- **insurance_required:** None found in Chapter 332 or 20 CSR 2110-2.
- **inactive_status:** No inactive or retired licence category exists in RSMo 332.181 or 20 CSR 2110-2; the CE rule merely defines a 'Retired licensee' (one who neither practises nor holds out as practising) as a licensee category without reducing the 50-hour requirement. The only alternative to active renewal is letting the licence expire (see reactivation).
- **reactivation:** Expired licence may be renewed within four years of expiry by submitting the renewal form, the $150 renewal fee plus $100 penalty fee, and proof of CE: 50 hours if renewed in the first two-year cycle after expiry, 100 hours in the second cycle, all earned within the four years before the renewal application (RSMo 332.181.5; 20 CSR 2110-2.071(8)-(9)). A licence not renewed within four years 'shall be void' and the dentist 'shall pay the same fees and be examined in the same manner as an original applicant' - i.e., a new hands-on competency exam (unless eligible by credentials under 332.211, which needs 5 years of licensure in another state).
- **remote_maintainability:** easy
- **details:** Keeping the licence active from abroad requires only the $150 biennial fee and 50 hours of CE from board-approved sponsors (no delivery-format cap found), with no practice hours, no in-state address, no insurance and no hands-on CPR at renewal identified; the board can even waive CE for foreign residency. Caveats: no inactive status exists, so the licence must be renewed every cycle; lapses beyond four years mean full re-examination; confirm with the board that 100% online CE is accepted and that BLS is not required at renewal.
