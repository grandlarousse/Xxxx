# Montana (USA) — ajan bulgusu

Düzenleyici: Montana Board of Dentistry (Department of Labor & Industry, Business Standards Division / Professional Boards & Licensing) — https://boards.bsd.dli.mt.gov/dentistry/

**Teori-only rota:** no · **Göçmenlik engeli:** low · **Uzaktan sürdürme:** easy · **Güven:** medium

## Sınav yolları

- **Licensure by examination (standard route for new graduates)** (el becerisi: evet): ARM 24.138.502(1): applicant must have (a) passed the JCNDE written exam (INBDE), (b) 'passed a board approved regional clinical practical examination' (valid 5 years), and (c) graduated from a CODA-accredited dental school. ARM 24.138.502(2): the board-approved clinical practical exam 'must include' (a) a patient-based or, from 12 Mar 2020, simulated patient-based operative exam (class II composite plus a second restoration), (b) patient-based/simulated periodontal exam, (c) non-patient-based endodontics (anterior access, posterior access, obturation) and (d) non-patient-based prosthodontics exam. ADA summary (updated 9 Oct 2026) lists accepted exams as ADEX (CDCA-WREB-CITA), CRDTS and SRTA; the board's 2020 application form lists CRDTS, SRTA, WREB. All are manikin/hands-on. Plus Montana jurisprudence exam (open book, 75%), CPR/ACLS/PALS card, NPDB query, fees.
- **Nonroutine application review of a non-conforming clinical exam** (el becerisi: evet): ARM 24.138.502(5): 'The board may review applications from applicants who have taken a clinical practical examination that does not meet the above criteria as nonroutine for licensure.' Purely discretionary board review at a board meeting (ARM 24.138.304). The DLOSCE is not named anywhere in Montana rules and contains none of the mandated operative/perio/endo/prosth components, so a DLOSCE-only application would at best be a long-shot nonroutine request; no published policy accepting it.
- **Licensure by credentials (ARM 24.138.505)** (el becerisi: evet): Board licenses 'without additional examination, except a jurisprudence examination' if the dentist holds a licence elsewhere obtained after passing a board-approved clinical examination or state practical exam, is currently engaged in clinical direct-patient-care dentistry (documented active practice; residency hours can count toward the practice requirement), has 60 hours of clinical CE in the preceding 3 years, passes the jurisprudence exam and pays the credentialing fee ($500 per 2021 ARM 24.138.402; $375 on the 2020 form). Not available to a brand-new graduate and the underlying licence still rests on a hands-on exam.
- **PGY-1 residency in lieu of clinical exam** (el becerisi: evet): No such pathway exists in MCA 37-4-301 or ARM 24.138.502/505. Residency hours are only credited toward the active-practice requirement under the credentials route (24.138.505(e)(vii)).
- **DLOSCE / computer-based OSCE only** (el becerisi: hayır): NOT accepted. Montana is not among the DLOSCE-accepting states in the ADA 2026 DLOSCE guide, and ARM 24.138.502(2) mandates simulated/patient-based operative and periodontal exams plus non-patient endo and prosthodontic exams, none of which a computer OSCE provides.

## Teori-only açıklaması
ARM 24.138.502(1): 'Applicants for dentist licensure must have: (a) passed the written examination administered by the Joint Commission on National Dental Examinations ...; (b) passed a board approved regional clinical practical examination. Examinations shall be valid for the purpose of initial licensure for a period of five years from the date of passage of the examination; and (c) graduated from a dental school accredited by the American Dental Association Commission on Dental Accreditation'. ARM 24.138.502(2): 'A board-approved clinical practical exam must include the following components: (a) patient-based or, beginning March 12, 2020, a simulated patient-based operative exam, to include one direct posterior class II composite and any one of the following: (i) direct posterior class II composite; or (ii) direct posterior class II amalgam; or (iii) direct anterior class III composite; (b) patient-based or, beginning March 12, 2020, a simulated patient-based periodontal exam; (c) non-patient-based endodontics exam ...; and (d) non-patient-based prosthodontics exam.' (quoted in ADA Montana Laws & Rules, updated 9 Oct 2026). MCA 37-4-301 likewise requires a certificate of graduation plus examination. There is no residency, portfolio or OSCE-only alternative; the only escape hatch is discretionary nonroutine review under 24.138.502(5), which has no published DLOSCE policy. A hands-on manikin exam (ADEX/CRDTS/SRTA) is therefore unavoidable.

## DLOSCE notları
Montana rules and the board application never mention the DLOSCE/OSCE; the ADA DLOSCE 2026 guide does not list Montana as accepting it. The required clinical exam must contain simulated/patient-based operative and periodontal components and non-patient endo and prosth components (ARM 24.138.502(2)), which the DLOSCE lacks. The DLOSCE taken as a component of the ADEX series is simply part of the accepted ADEX exam; there is no provision for accepting an ADEX-reported DLOSCE pass alone, and earlier standalone DLOSCE scores would only be considerable through the board's discretionary 'nonroutine' review (24.138.502(5)) with no precedent found. Treat the DLOSCE-only route as closed in Montana.

Montana DLI/BSD form 'Request for Exemption from Social Security Number Requirement': 'Montana Code Annotated 37-1-307 requires applicants for a Montana professional or occupational practice license to provide a United States Social Security Number (SSN). However, pursuant to Policy Interpretation Question (PIQ) number 99-05 issued by the U.S. Dept. of Health and Human Services in 1999, the State of Montana, Department of Labor & Industry, Business Standards Division (BSD) may issue a license to an applicant who does not have a SSN if the applicant submits this form truthfully attesting that he or she has not been assigned a SSN.' If later assigned an SSN it must be reported before renewal; form filed once per licence. The board's dentist application form item 7 reads 'Social Security Number / FOREIGN ID NUMBER', i.e. a foreign ID number may be entered in place of an SSN. Source: https://nasba.org/files/2011/02/Montana_SSNAffidavit.pdf (copy of the Montana BSD form) and https://boards.bsd.dli.mt.gov/_docs/den/dds_app---FINAL-FILLABLE-10-15-2020.pdf
- **in_jurisdiction_address_required:** False
- **barrier_level:** low
- **details:** The dentist application form (10-15-2020) and ARM 24.138.550 contain no citizenship, permanent-residence, work-authorisation, visa/I-94 or lawful-presence question (grep of the full form for citizen/lawful/immigra/visa returned nothing); only identity (name, DOB, SSN or foreign ID number), education, exam, NPDB self-query, CPR and fee items. Montana has no SAVE-type lawful-presence statute applied to professional licences (the 2012 LR-121 immigration-status law was struck down by the Montana Supreme Court in 2016 - background knowledge, not verified in this session). SSN is required by MCA 37-1-307 (child-support enforcement) but BSD issues licences to applicants who have never had an SSN on filing the exemption affidavit; the form warns a licence 'will not be renewed or issued to a person who refuses to provide his or her SSN' if one exists. Address: since HB 276 (2025) 'All licensees and applicants are required to provide the department with their current mailing address and any change of address within 30 days' - any mailing address (foreign allowed; renewal form explicitly contemplates Canadian/foreign payments) - no Montana residency or in-state address requirement. The application form has separate Business Address and Home Address fields.

## Yüz yüze adımlar
Only the hands-on clinical exam (ADEX/CRDTS/SRTA at a US test site) and the INBDE (Prometric) require physical presence. No fingerprint or criminal background check appears in ARM 24.138.550 or the application (the department itself obtains a National Practitioner Data Bank query; applicant also submits an NPDB self-query, done online). Jurisprudence exam: 'This is an untimed, self-proctored, open "book" examination ... pass with a score of 75% or better' (application form p.3) - can be completed anywhere, $85. No interview. No notarisation requirement found on the form. Applications are filed online via ebiz.mt.gov/pol or by mail with cheque/money order; communication is by e-mail. CPR/ACLS/PALS card: 'Internet courses will be accepted if a hands on evaluation of clinical competency is also included' (ARM 24.138.550(1)(d)) - a blended course with an in-person skills check can be done abroad through AHA/ERC international training centres. Licence verifications from other jurisdictions must be sent directly by those boards.

## Diğer başlangıç şartları
Jurisprudence exam (open book, self-proctored, 75%, $85 fee). Current CPR/ACLS/PALS card with hands-on skills component. NPDB self-query and department NPDB query. Verification of INBDE pass from JCNDE and of clinical exam pass from the testing agency (exam valid 5 years). Dental-school certificate of graduation (CODA-accredited). No English test, no fingerprinting, no insurance requirement for licensure. Nonroutine applications (e.g. non-conforming clinical exam, disciplinary history) go to a board meeting (ARM 24.138.304); routine applications are processed by staff - the board FAQ notes statutory processing timelines; expect several weeks to a few months.

## İlk ücretler
ARM 24.138.402 (version in force 30 Jun 2021): original application fee $100; jurisprudence examination fee $85; credentialing fee for dentists $500 (credentials route only; the 2020 paper form still shows $375). The 2020 application form: 'Application Fee - $100.00, Jurisprudence Exam Fee - $85.00' for the exam route (total $185). Exam costs are extra: ADEX full series $2,995 (per task facts), INBDE fee, NPDB self-query (~$4). Montana Prescription Drug Registry (MPDR) fee $30/year is collected at renewal.

## Lisansı sürdürme

- **renewal_period:** Annual; licence expires 1 March each year (board renewal form: 'Your Montana dental license and/or Anesthesia Permit will expire on March 1'). CE is counted over a three-year cycle.
- **renewal_fee:** ARM 24.138.402: active renewal fee for dentists $306; inactive renewal fee $153 (2021 schedule). Plus $30/yr MPDR (prescription registry) fee for prescribers, payable 'whether or not the licensee is physically' in Montana. The board's paper renewal form (undated, probably a fee-reduction year) quotes $183 ($153 + $30 MPDR); late renewal after 1 March incurs a penalty of 100% of the renewal fee ('increasing the total to $612.00'). Current-year fee should be confirmed in the ebiz portal.
- **ce_hours:** 60 hours per three-year cycle for dentists (ARM 24.138.2104 in the 2021 compilation; ADA page cites the same requirement at ARM 24.138.2110, updated Oct 2026). Dentists with sedation permits need 20 (deep/GA) or 12 (moderate) anesthesia-specific hours within the 60. Not acceptable: personal development, legislative/political, unproven modalities, basic science courses, basic life support/CPR.
- **ce_online_ok:** No cap on online/self-study CE was found in the portions of ARM 24.138.2101-2105 read; CE must be in acceptable subject matter (24.138.2102) from approved providers and is audited (24.138.2105). Verification that 100% distance learning is accepted was not obtained - treat as 'probably yes, unverified'.
- **cpr_required:** A current CPR/ACLS/PALS card is required at initial licensure, for conversion from inactive to active (24.138.514) and for reactivation of an expired licence (24.138.525). The renewal form does not list CPR as a renewal requirement (not verified in the rules). Where CPR is required, internet courses count only if a hands-on skills evaluation is included (24.138.550(1)(d)).
- **practice_hours_required:** None to renew an ACTIVE licence. However, to convert an INACTIVE licence back to active the licensee must show 'evidence that the applicant has actively and competently practiced in this or another jurisdiction within the last five years' (24.138.514(3)(b)); if out of practice longer than five years, must complete 'a clinical competency course(s) or skills assessment analysis approved by the board; or a board approved regional or state examination within the most recent five years' (24.138.514(4)).
- **address_or_residency_required:** No Montana residency or in-state address. Must keep a current mailing address (any country) and e-mail on file and report changes within 30 days (HB 276, 2025).
- **insurance_required:** No mandatory professional liability insurance found in the rules or renewal form.
- **inactive_status:** ARM 24.138.514(1): 'Licensees may place their license on inactive status upon written request to the board.' Inactive renewal fee $153/yr (24.138.402(9)); inactive licence does not permit practice. No maximum duration stated. Whether CE must be accrued while inactive is not stated, but 60 hours for the three most recent renewal years must be produced on conversion to active.
- **reactivation:** Inactive -> active (24.138.514(3)): completed form, proof of active competent practice within the last 5 years, licence verifications from all jurisdictions, 60 CE hours for the three most current renewal years, current CPR/ACLS/PALS card, 'any other information the board may require for evidence of operative competency', and payment of the difference between inactive and active fees. If out of practice > 5 years: additionally a board-approved clinical competency course/skills assessment OR a board-approved regional/state clinical exam within the last 5 years (i.e. hands-on again). Expired licence (24.138.525): meet MCA 37-1-141 (late renewal/lapse rules), current CPR card, licence verifications if expired > 6 months, 60 CE hours in the most recent 3 years; after the statutory lapse period a new application is required (period not verified here).
- **remote_maintainability:** easy
- **details:** Keeping the licence ACTIVE from abroad requires only the annual fee ($306 + $30 MPDR per the 2021 schedule), 60 CE hours per 3-year cycle (no self-study cap found), a foreign mailing address is acceptable, no practice hours, no insurance, no in-person step. The trap is INACTIVE status: a dentist who goes inactive (or does not practise anywhere) for more than five years can only return to active status after a board-approved hands-on competency course/skills assessment or a fresh regional clinical exam. The SSN exemption affidavit must be on file before renewal, and an SSN must be reported if one is ever issued. Fee schedule read is the 2021 ARM compilation; rule numbers for CE were renumbered (2104 -> 2110) by Oct 2026.
