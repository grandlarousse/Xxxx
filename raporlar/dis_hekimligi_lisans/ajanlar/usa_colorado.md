## Sınav yolları

- **OSCE in lieu of clinical examination (original licensure) - includes the Canadian/NDEB OSCE and, historically, the standalone DLOSCE** (el becerisi: hayır): Board policy (as summarised by the ADA Colorado Laws & Rules page, updated 7 Oct 2026, policy amended 10/9/2026): the Board accepts the exams developed by ADEX (CDCA/CITA), Delaware, CRDTS, SRTA, WREB 'and all OSCE's', and 'accepts successful completion of a PGY-1, portfolio, or OSCE requirement in lieu of a clinical examination for dentists.' Only the PGY-1 and portfolio routes carry the extra condition of first being fully licensed in the jurisdiction where they were completed; the OSCE route does not. The 2016 CDA report on the Board's Policy 1A revision states: 'Successful completion of the OSCE examination will be accepted for dentist candidates applying for original licensure' and the ADA New Dentist Blog (2016) confirms candidates may 'take the Canadian OSCE to apply for initial licensure in Colorado'. Statutory basis: C.R.S. 12-220-403(1)(b) and Rule 1.6(B)(1)(c) - 'an examination or other methodology, as determined by the Board, designed to test the applicant's clinical skills and knowledge'. Plus INBDE (JCNDE) required under 12-220-403(1)(a). No hands-on component when the OSCE used is the NDEB Virtual OSCE (computer-based). Standalone DLOSCE is also on the Board's accepted list (JCNDE 2026 guide) but standalone administrations ended 9 Oct 2026.
- **ADEX Dental Examination (CDCA-WREB-CITA), including its DSE-OSCE component** (el becerisi: evet): Accepted; 'All parts of a clinical examination are required to be successfully completed and no part of an examination is considered optional for purposes of Colorado licensure'; 'Periodontal testing for dentists is required'. Manikin-based hands-on sections are mandatory, so the DLOSCE taken as part of ADEX does not avoid hands-on testing.
- **CRDTS, SRTA, WREB (legacy), Delaware Practical Exam** (el becerisi: evet): All U.S. dental clinical examinations 'performed on mannikins or patients' that provide adequate assurance of competency are accepted (ADA page). All are hands-on.
- **PGY-1 residency in lieu of clinical exam** (el becerisi: evet): Accepted, but 'Dentists submitting successful completion of a PGY-1 or portfolio model for consideration are required to be first fully licensed (current and active) in the state/jurisdiction where the PGY-1 or portfolio was completed, or in another jurisdiction where a state board has reviewed and accepted it towards licensure before applying for licensure in Colorado.' Requires a US residency (visa) - not suitable for this candidate.
- **Portfolio (e.g., California hybrid portfolio) in lieu of clinical exam** (el becerisi: evet): Accepted only after first being licensed in the jurisdiction where the portfolio was completed (same condition as PGY-1). Portfolio is patient-based within a dental school.
- **Licensure by endorsement (Occupational Credential Portability Program)** (el becerisi: hayır): C.R.S. 12-220-404 and Rule 1.6(C): requires an active license in good standing in another US state/territory plus the same education/INBDE/clinical-methodology requirements of Rule 1.6(B)(1), and currency (1 year active practice under a license, 5 of 7 years teaching, military, clinical exam within 1 year, or Board-approved evaluation). Not available to a new graduate with no US license.

## Teori-only açıklaması
Statute: C.R.S. 12-220-403(1): 'An applicant for dental licensure must submit to the board proof that the applicant successfully passed the following: (a) The examination administered by the Joint Commission on National Dental Examinations; and (b) An examination or other methodology, as determined by the board, designed to test the applicant's clinical skills and knowledge, which may include residency and portfolio models.' Rule 1.6(B)(1)(c) (3 CCR 709-1, eff. 8/30/2026) repeats this. The Board's examination policy, as quoted on the ADA 'Colorado Laws & Rules' page (updated 7 October 2026, policy 'Amended 10/9/2026'): 'The Colorado Dental Board accepts all U.S. dental clinical examinations ... which currently includes the exams developed by ADEX and administered by CDCA and CITA; and the exams developed and administered by Delaware, CRDTS, SRTA, WREB and all OSCE's. ... The Colorado Dental Board accepts successful completion of a PGY-1, portfolio, or OSCE requirement in lieu of a clinical examination for dentists. Dentists submitting successful completion of a PGY-1 or portfolio model ... are required to be first fully licensed ... in the state/jurisdiction where the PGY-1 or portfolio was completed' - note the prior-licensure condition is attached to PGY-1 and portfolio only, not to the OSCE. The Colorado Dental Association's 2016 account of the Policy 1A revision: 'Successful completion of the OSCE examination will be accepted for dentist candidates applying for original licensure (given that the exam uses a pass/fail methodology...)' and the ADA New Dentist Blog: candidates may 'Take the Canadian OSCE to apply for initial licensure in Colorado.' Therefore a CODA-programme graduate with INBDE + NDEB (Virtual) OSCE + 1 hour of (on-demand) jurisprudence education + fingerprints can be licensed with no manikin/patient exam and no residency. Caveats: (i) I could only read the Board's policy text as quoted by the ADA (Oct 2026) and the CDA (2016), not the policy PDF itself; (ii) the policy says 'all OSCE's' but does not expressly name the NDEB 'Virtual' OSCE format introduced after 2016 - confirm with the Board; (iii) Rule 1.6(B)(3) currency: a graduate applying more than 12 months after graduation with no practice must show e.g. 'Passed a Board approved clinical examination within one year' - whether the OSCE satisfies this is not stated.

## DLOSCE notları
Colorado is listed in the JCNDE DLOSCE candidate guide as accepting the DLOSCE in full fulfilment of the clinical exam requirement, consistent with the Board policy accepting 'all OSCE's' in lieu of a clinical examination. Nothing in the statute, Rule 1.6, or the ADA summary (Oct 2026) addresses DLOSCE scores earned as part of the ADEX exam; because the Board requires that 'All parts of a clinical examination are required to be successfully completed and no part of an examination is considered optional', a DLOSCE/DSE-OSCE taken inside ADEX would only count together with the full ADEX (hands-on) exam. Earlier standalone DLOSCE passes should remain acceptable (the policy has no expiry on exam results beyond the Rule 1.6(B)(3) currency rules: new graduate within 12 months, or clinical exam within one year of application, etc.). Since standalone DLOSCE administrations ended 9 Oct 2026, the practical non-hands-on OSCE for a new candidate is the NDEB OSCE. Confirm directly with the Board (dora_dentalboard@state.co.us) that the NDEB Virtual OSCE is accepted.

## Yüz yüze adımlar
(1) Fingerprint-based CBI/FBI background check is required for dentists (DPO Fingerprinting page lists 'Dentist - 2TFVHY' (IdentoGO) and 'Dentist - 8750DENI' (American BioIdentity)). Electronic capture is done at vendor sites in Colorado, but both vendors offer non-resident processing: American BioIdentity - 'Applicants will register online, select "out-of-state applicant" and pay for their order online ... Once the order has been placed the applicant will receive a receipt with instructions on where to send their fingerprint cards to Colorado Fingerprinting for processing'; IdentoGO has 'Non-Resident Processing Instructions - Colorado'. So fingerprint cards can be taken abroad (e.g., by local police) and mailed - no US visit strictly required. 'DPO must receive the results of both background checks prior to issuing a license.' (2) Application is entirely online via DPO Online Services (no paper form); the statute says the application is 'verified by the oath of the applicant' but this is done by online attestation - no notarisation step is published. (3) Jurisprudence: no exam; Rule 1.6(A)(17) requires an attestation of 'at least one (1) hour of jurisprudence education prior to applying for initial licensure ... This education may be live and interactive or on-demand' - can be completed online from abroad. (4) No interview. (5) Official transcript must be sent by the school; INBDE results via JCNDE; OSCE results from the testing agency (NDEB). (6) The NDEB Written/Virtual OSCE are taken at NDEB-designated sites (outside Colorado's control). Overall: licensure can be completed from abroad or during a short visit; no in-state live-scan is mandatory.

## Diğer başlangıç şartları
Age 21+ (Rule 1.6(A)(16); C.R.S. 12-220-401(1)(a)). DDS/DMD from a school CODA-accredited at the time of graduation, official transcript (Rule 1.6(B)(1)(a)). INBDE (JCNDE) pass. Clinical methodology (OSCE / exam / PGY-1 / portfolio). Currency (Rule 1.6(B)(3)): graduated within the 12 months before the application is received, OR 1 year active practice in last 5, OR teaching, OR military, OR 'Passed a Board approved clinical examination within one year of the date the application is received', OR Board-approved evaluation. Jurisprudence: 1 hour of education (online/on-demand OK), attestation only - no exam. Fingerprint CBI/FBI background check. Professional liability insurance $500,000/$1,500,000 'prior to practicing as a licensed dentist in this state' OR a Rule 1.5 exemption (Rule 1.5(E): 'Holds an active dental license, but does not engage in any patient care within Colorado'). Disclosure of discipline/malpractice history; 10-year malpractice coverage-history verification if previously insured. Healthcare Professions Profile Program (HPPP) profile. No English-language test. Opioid/substance-use training applies at renewal (Rule 1.6(L)); BLS required of all licensees. All application documents must be received within one year of the application (Rule 1.6(A)(13)). Timeline: not published; background check results must arrive before issuance. New licensees issued within 120 days of the 28 Feb even-year expiry get the following expiry date.

## Lisansı sürdürme

- **renewal_period:** Biennial: 'All Dentist ... licenses expire on February 28 of even-numbered years (February 29 during leap years)'; renewal opens 4-6 weeks before expiry; 60-day grace period after expiry (C.R.S. 12-20-202(1)(e)); Rule 1.6(A)(6): renewal every two years applies to active, inactive and retired status.
- **renewal_fee:** Not verified (DPO fee schedule not retrievable); retired status fee capped at $50 by C.R.S. 12-220-410(2)(b).
- **ce_hours:** 30 hours per 2-year period for active licenses (C.R.S. 12-220-308; Rule 1.6(H)(1)); 0 hours for first renewal if licensed <=12 months, 15 hours if licensed 12-24 months. At least 16 of the 30 must be clinical/science-based; at least 2 hours infection control (CDC guidelines); OSHA bloodborne-pathogen training; 1 hour opioid/substance-use training per period (exempt if the dentist 'Attests to the Board that they do not prescribe opioids'); BLS counts for max 2 hours.
- **ce_online_ok:** Mostly yes. 'At least 50% of the required CE hours must be live and interactive. This may include web-based courses. Courses meeting this requirement include the opportunity and ability for course participants to ask questions in real-time, and for the instructor to answer participant questions in real-time' (DPO Dental CE page; Rule 1.6(H)(9)). So up to 50% self-study and the rest live webinars - all completable from abroad. Accepted providers: ADA CERP, AGD PACE, AMA PRA Cat 1, CODA institutions, ACPE, CDC/ANSI. Exception: BLS has a hands-on skills component.
- **cpr_required:** Yes. 'Maintaining current Basic Life Support (BLS) for healthcare providers is required for all dental licenses' (Rule 1.6(H)(7)). Courses must follow ILCOR/AHA guidelines; 'Initial training must include a minimum of 3 hours of training, including skills practice and skills testing. Renewal courses must include a minimum of 2 hours of training, including skills practice and testing.' - a hands-on skills session is required (AHA-type courses are available internationally). The rule does not state BLS is waived for inactive/retired status, but CE requirements generally do not apply to inactive/retired licensees (Rule 1.6(H)(2)).
- **practice_hours_required:** None to renew an active license. Practice hours only matter on reactivation/reinstatement after 2+ years (300 hours in a 12-month period within the preceding 5 years, or alternatives) - Rule 1.6(I)(2).
- **address_or_residency_required:** No Colorado residency. Rule 1.6(A)(5): licensee must keep a business address, email and 'preferred physical mailing address' current (30-day notice); DPO keeps 'one physical mailing address and electronic mail address for each licensee'. Email is the primary communication channel.
- **insurance_required:** Statutorily yes for active licensure ($500,000 per incident / $1,500,000 aggregate; C.R.S. 13-64-301(1)(a)(I)(A), 12-220-307), BUT Rule 1.5 exempts a dentist who 'Holds an inactive license', 'Holds a retired license', or 'Holds an active dental license, but does not engage in any patient care within Colorado or any of the acts constituting the practice of dentistry ... including but not limited to the prescribing of medications, diagnosis, and development of a treatment plan'. A dentist living abroad and not treating patients in Colorado therefore needs no Colorado malpractice policy.
- **inactive_status:** Inactive (C.R.S. 12-220-409; Rule 1.6(A)(6),(8)): apply by paper form 'Dentist - Change Status to Inactive', affidavit that the licensee will not practise dentistry in Colorado, pay the license fee (amount not verified); no CE while inactive ('This requirement does not apply to a licensee placing the licensee's license into inactive or retired status or renewing such status'); must still renew every two years; no maximum duration. Retired (C.R.S. 12-220-410): affidavit that the licensee 'will not practice dentistry ... will no longer earn income as a dentist ... administrator or consultant' (anywhere, not just Colorado); fee 'must not exceed fifty dollars'; no CE; may volunteer for the indigent; renew biennially. Academic licensees are not eligible for inactive/retired.
- **reactivation:** Rule 1.6(I) and (H)(3): inactive/retired/expired for LESS than two years - submit application, fee, insurance or Rule 1.5 exemption, disclosures, substance-use training attestation, and 'proof of having completed the required thirty hours of continuing education credit for the previous renewal period'. TWO OR MORE years inactive/retired/expired - 'an applicant is required to demonstrate continued clinical competency. An applicant who ... has not practiced at least 300 hours in a twelve-month period during the five years immediately preceding the application ... must demonstrate to the Board how the applicant has maintained professional ability, knowledge, and skills' via (case-by-case) postgraduate training/residency, research/teaching, military/public health service, 'Passed a Board approved clinical examination within one year', or a Board-approved evaluation by a CODA institution; the Board may add probationary practice or courses. Practice abroad is not excluded from the 300-hour test but is reviewed case by case. Expired licenses: 60-day grace, then reinstatement; >2 years expired triggers the competency demonstration under C.R.S. 12-20-202(2)(c)(II) as well.
- **remote_maintainability:** moderate
- **details:** Keeping the license ACTIVE from abroad indefinitely is feasible: biennial online renewal, 30 CE hours (50% live-interactive webinars + 50% self-study, plus 2 h infection control, 1 h opioid training or attestation of not prescribing opioids), current BLS (hands-on skills session - the only awkward element), no practice-hour requirement, no Colorado address/residency, and no malpractice insurance while not treating patients in Colorado (Rule 1.5(E)). Alternatively, inactive status costs only the fee and requires no CE, but reactivation after 2+ years requires proof of 300 practice hours in a 12-month window within 5 years or an alternative competency demonstration (possibly a clinical exam), so inactive status is only cheap if the dentist keeps practising somewhere. Best strategy for this candidate: keep the license active. The DPO communicates by email; fees are not verified.
