## Sınav yolları

- **ADEX Dental Examination (CDCA-WREB-CITA) and other U.S. regional clinical exams (CRDTS+SRTA, Delaware Practical Exam, WREB)** (el becerisi: evet): Board Policy 1.A: 'The Colorado Dental Board accepts all U.S. dental ... clinical examinations, performed on mannikins or patients ... which currently includes the exams developed by ADEX and administered by CDCA and CITA; and the exams developed and administered by Delaware, CRDTS, SRTA, WREB and all OSCE's.' All parts must be passed; periodontal testing for dentists is required. The online application lists this as 'Passed an examination designed to test your clinical skills/knowledge administered by a regional testing agency.'
- **Canadian OSCE (NDEB) in lieu of a clinical examination** (el becerisi: hayır): The current DPO 'Dentist (DEN) Initial Application Preview' (official online-application question list) offers, as one of the options for demonstrating clinical skills and knowledge: 'Completion of the Canadian Objective Structured Clinical Examination (OSCE)', alongside regional exam, PGY-1 and 'Hybrid Portfolio'. Board Policy 1.A: 'The Colorado Dental Board accepts successful completion of a PGY-1, portfolio, or OSCE requirement in lieu of a clinical examination for dentists.' The pre-licensure requirement to be 'first fully licensed' in the jurisdiction where the method was completed applies only to PGY-1 and portfolio, not OSCE. Caveat: no source names NDEB's post-March-2023 'Virtual OSCE' specifically; Policy 1.A says 'all OSCE's'.
- **DLOSCE (ADA/JCNDE) as an 'OSCE requirement'** (el becerisi: hayır): Policy 1.A accepts 'all OSCE's' and the DLOSCE 2026 Candidate Guide lists Colorado as accepting it. No Colorado rule or policy text names the DLOSCE expressly or addresses ADEX-integrated DLOSCE component scores. Standalone DLOSCE administrations ended Oct 2026, so this sub-route is now impractical unless the Board accepts an ADEX-reported DLOSCE pass alone (no published policy).
- **PGY-1 residency** (el becerisi: evet): Accepted in lieu of clinical exam (C.R.S. 12-220-403(1)(b); Rule 1.6(B)(1)(c); Policy 1.A), but 'Dentists submitting successful completion of a PGY-1 or portfolio model for consideration are required to be first fully licensed (current and active) in the state/jurisdiction where the PGY-1 or portfolio was completed, or in another jurisdiction where a state board has reviewed and accepted it towards licensure before applying for licensure in Colorado.' Requires a residency visa; not suitable for the candidate.
- **Portfolio ('Hybrid Portfolio', e.g. California)** (el becerisi: evet): Accepted in lieu of clinical exam under the same condition as PGY-1 (must first be fully licensed in the state where completed). Portfolio is completed on live patients during dental school in the portfolio state.
- **Licensure by Endorsement (credentials)** (el becerisi: evet): Rule 1.6(C): applicant must hold an active license in another state/jurisdiction and demonstrate current clinical competency (practice 1 of last 5 years, teaching, military, Board-approved clinical exam within one year, or Board-approved evaluation). Not available to a new graduate without another licence. (Also a 'Telehealth Method' licence exists for out-of-state licensees.)

## Teori-only açıklaması
Statute C.R.S. 12-220-403(1): applicant must pass '(a) The examination administered by the Joint Commission on National Dental Examinations; and (b) An examination or other methodology, as determined by the board, designed to test the applicant's clinical skills and knowledge, which may include residency and portfolio models.' Rule 3 CCR 709-1, 1.6(B)(1)(c) repeats this. Board Policy 1.A (Acceptance of Clinical Examinations; DPO-posted version shows 'Amended 5/4/2023', ADA page shows 'Amended 10/9/2026' with identical operative text): 'The Colorado Dental Board accepts all U.S. dental, dental therapist and dental hygiene clinical examinations, performed on mannikins or patients ... which currently includes the exams developed by ADEX and administered by CDCA and CITA; and the exams developed and administered by Delaware, CRDTS, SRTA, WREB and all OSCE's. ... The Colorado Dental Board accepts successful completion of a PGY-1, portfolio, or OSCE requirement in lieu of a clinical examination for dentists.' Critic point (1) resolved: although the first sentence is prefaced by 'U.S.', the official, current DPO 'Dentist (DEN) Initial Application Preview' (the question set of the live online application) expressly lists 'Completion of the Canadian Objective Structured Clinical Examination (OSCE)' as one of the four ways to demonstrate clinical skills and knowledge ('Passed an examination ... administered by a regional testing agency', 'Completion of a PGY-1 residency', 'Completion of a Hybrid Portfolio', 'Completion of the Canadian Objective Structured Clinical Examination (OSCE)'). So a new graduate of a CODA-accredited programme (Yeditepe post-Feb-2024) can satisfy 12-220-403 with INBDE + the NDEB OSCE, with no hands-on exam and no residency. Residual caveats: (a) no Colorado document uses the term 'Virtual OSCE' (NDEB's current format since March 2023); the application wording is generic 'Canadian OSCE', and Policy 1.A says 'all OSCE's', so acceptance of the Virtual OSCE is implied, not expressly stated; (b) currency rule 1.6(B)(3) requires, for applicants more than 12 months after graduation, one of: 1 year of practice in last 5, teaching, military, 'Passed a Board approved clinical examination within one year of the date the application is received', or a Board-approved evaluation. Whether an NDEB OSCE pass counts as a 'Board approved clinical examination' for this currency purpose is not stated; safest is to apply within 12 months of graduation (Rule 1.6(B)(3)(a)); (c) Board meeting minutes of 7 Jan, 30 Apr and 13 May 2026 contain no discussion of OSCE/NDEB/DLOSCE/ADEX integration.

## DLOSCE notları
Colorado names no exam called 'DLOSCE'; Policy 1.A accepts 'all OSCE's' and an 'OSCE requirement in lieu of a clinical examination', which is why the JCNDE DLOSCE Candidate Guide lists Colorado as accepting the DLOSCE. The online application question list does not mention DLOSCE (it names the 'Canadian OSCE'). No Colorado rule, policy, application text or 2026 Board minutes address whether a DLOSCE component pass reported as part of the ADEX series (after standalone DLOSCE ended Oct 2026) would be accepted on its own; under Policy 1.A 'All parts of a clinical examination are required to be successfully completed and no part of an examination is considered optional', so an ADEX candidate who passed only the DLOSCE component would most likely NOT be treated as having completed the ADEX exam. Earlier standalone DLOSCE scores (pre-Oct 2026) presumably remain acceptable under 'all OSCE's'; recency only matters via the 12-month currency rule 1.6(B)(3). Practically, the NDEB (Canadian) OSCE is now the only operating theory-only route.

## Lisansı sürdürme

- **renewal_period:** Biennial: all dentist licences expire on February 28 of even-numbered years (Feb 29 in leap years); renewal online via DPO Online Services.
- **renewal_fee:** Not published on the pages read (set in the DPO online portal); unclear. Inactive licences must also be renewed each cycle (fee not stated).
- **ce_hours:** 30 hours of Board-approved CE per two-year renewal period (Rule 1.3(H)(1); C.R.S. 12-220-308). At least 16 of the 30 must be clinical or science; at least 50% must be 'live and interactive' (Rule 1.3(G)(9)/(H)(9)); BLS course credited up to 2 hours; presenter credit up to 6 hours. First renewal: 0 hours if licensed <=12 months before expiry, 15 hours if 12-24 months.
- **ce_online_ok:** Partly. 'At least fifty percent of the required hours must be live and interactive' (rule text on ADA page); Board Policy 1.HH 'Continuing Education Clarification Policy' defines what the Board treats as 'live and interactive' (text not fully read; the definition typically covers real-time online webinars with presenter interaction, but this was not verified). Remaining 50% may be self-study/online. So fully asynchronous CE is NOT permitted; synchronous online CE likely satisfies the live requirement - confirm with Policy 1.HH.
- **cpr_required:** Yes. Rule 1.3(H)(7): 'Current Basic Life Support (BLS) for healthcare providers is required of all licensees'; BLS courses 'shall be consistent with the most current science and ... guidelines for CPR and Emergency Cardiovascular Care (ECC)'; initial training min 3 hours, renewal min 2 hours. Hands-on skills session not expressly mandated in the rule text, but AHA-consistent BLS-for-healthcare-providers courses normally include an in-person skills check; BLS courses are available worldwide (AHA international training centres). Not required while inactive.
- **practice_hours_required:** None to renew an active licence. 300 practice hours in a 12-month period (within last 5 years) is one of the options only when REACTIVATING a licence inactive for 2+ years.
- **address_or_residency_required:** No in-state address or Colorado residency requirement found; licensees must keep contact information updated via the DPO online account.
- **insurance_required:** Professional liability insurance is required before practising dentistry in Colorado (Rule 1.6(B)(2)(a); C.R.S. 12-220); the application/reactivation forms use an attestation 'that I have obtained or will obtain professional liability insurance prior to practicing'. An active licensee not practising in Colorado therefore attests rather than buys cover; not required at all while inactive ('during the time my license is INACTIVE, I do not have to comply with any continuing education, insurance, or financial responsibility requirements').
- **inactive_status:** Inactive status: 'No Fee' to change status (DPO form 'Change Status to Inactive'); 'You must continue to renew your Inactive license to maintain the status'; no CE, insurance or financial-responsibility requirements while inactive; no maximum duration stated; available to any licensee not currently in a renewal period. A separate 'Retired' status also exists (form on DPO site; details not read).
- **reactivation:** Reactivation application, fee $444, online/mailed form: detail practice since inactivation, attest to liability insurance before practising. If inactive <2 years: proof of the 30 CE hours for the previous period (Rule 1.3(H)(3)). If inactive 2+ years: demonstrate continued clinical competency through ONE of: active practice >=300 hours in a 12-month period within the preceding 5 years (anywhere; Board may request documentation); OR a Board-approved evaluation/refresher at a CODA institution with recommendation and verification of clinical experience; OR 'Passed a Board approved clinical examination within one year of the date this application is received' (DENTPIN). Expired (non-renewed) licences use a Reinstatement application with similar competency rules.
- **remote_maintainability:** moderate
- **details:** Keeping the licence ACTIVE from abroad requires: biennial online renewal and fee, 30 CE hours per cycle of which 50% 'live and interactive' (synchronous online likely acceptable, to be confirmed under Policy 1.HH), current BLS for healthcare providers (renewal course min 2 h, usually with a skills check, obtainable abroad), and an insurance attestation only before actually practising in Colorado. No practice-hour minimum, no in-state address, no notarised forms. Alternatively the licence can sit in INACTIVE status indefinitely for no status-change fee (renewal of the inactive licence each cycle still required), but reactivation after 2+ years inactive needs 300 practice hours in a 12-month period (which practice abroad can supply, subject to Board documentation) or a Board-approved clinical exam within the last year. Decades-long remote maintenance in active status is therefore feasible but needs ongoing live/interactive CE and BLS; inactive status is cheap but reactivation after a long gap is burdensome.
