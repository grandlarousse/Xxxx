# Michigan (USA) — ajan bulgusu

Düzenleyici: Michigan Board of Dentistry, administered by the Michigan Department of Licensing and Regulatory Affairs (LARA), Bureau of Professional Licensing (applications via the MiPLUS portal) — https://www.michigan.gov/lara (Bureau of Professional Licensing; dentist application via www.michigan.gov/miplus; current Dentist Licensing Guide: https://www.michigan.gov/documents/lara/Dentist_Final_654119_7.pdf)

## Sınav yolları

- **Licensure by examination (R 338.11201) - INBDE + full ADEX (CDCA-WREB-CITA) clinical examination** (el becerisi: evet): Rule 1201 (eff. Oct 2, 2023): applicant must (a) graduate with a DDS/DMD from a program complying with R 338.11301 (CODA standards), (b) 'Pass all parts of the NBDE, or the INBDE if the INBDE replaces the NBDE', (c) 'Pass all parts, written and clinical, of the ADEX examination that is conducted by the CDCA-WREB-CITA, or by another regional testing agency', (d) hold current BLS/ACLS for healthcare providers 'with a hands-on component' earned within 2 years before licensure. ADA Oct-2026 summary: the exam must include the computer-based DSE OSCE PLUS endodontic, fixed prosthodontic, periodontic (live patient or manikin) and restorative anterior+posterior (live patient or manikin) portions. The 2026 LARA guide repeats: pass 'all parts, written and clinical' of ADEX 'conducted by CDCA-WREB-CITA, or another regional testing agency'. This is the only route for a new graduate; manikin/clinical portions are unavoidable.
- **Licensure by endorsement (licensed in another US state) - not available to a new graduate** (el becerisi: evet): Requires an existing dental licence in another state in good standing. Licensed <5 years: must still show passage of 'all parts, written and clinical' of ADEX or an exam 'deemed substantially equivalent to the ADEX examination by the Board of Dentistry' (CRDTS, SRTA, WREB, state clinical exams are cited as examples). Licensed >=5 years in a state that required a regional/state clinical exam: must show completion of that exam (ADA summary). The 2021 guide contains a clause about applicants 'not required to complete a regional examination as part of the initial licensing process' in their original state, which may waive the exam for >=5-year licensees from a no-exam state, but this was not fully verified and in any case needs five years of prior licensure, so it is not an initial route for a new graduate.
- **Licensure by endorsement for dentists licensed in Canada (province)** (el becerisi: evet): The licensing guide has a separate endorsement category for applicants licensed in a Canadian province; per the 2021 guide text the applicant must still 'take and pass all parts of a clinical examination offered by the CDCA, OR offered by a regional testing agency, state, or other entity that is substantially equivalent', and the 2026 guide refers to ADEX 'conducted by the CDCA-WREB-CITA or another regional testing agency' and credentials 'substantially equivalent to the requirements for licensure by examination'. Not a theory-only route (NDEB Virtual OSCE alone is not listed as equivalent).
- **DLOSCE-only / standalone OSCE route** (el becerisi: hayır): NOT AVAILABLE. Michigan is not among the states that accept the DLOSCE in lieu of the clinical exam; the rules require 'all parts, written and clinical' of ADEX. The DSE OSCE/DLOSCE is simply the required computer-based component of the ADEX series and does not substitute for the endo/prostho/perio/restorative hand-skill parts.
- **PGY-1 residency in lieu of clinical exam** (el becerisi: hayır): NOT AVAILABLE. Neither MCL 333.16174, the Board rules (R 338.11201-11202) nor the LARA licensing guide offer a residency-based or portfolio pathway for dentist licensure.

## Teori-only açıklaması
Michigan Administrative Rule R 338.11201 (Licensure by examination; History ... 2023 MR 19, Eff. Oct. 2, 2023): 'an applicant for dentist licensure by examination shall ... (b) Pass all parts of the NBDE, or the INBDE if the INBDE replaces the NBDE, that is conducted and scored by the JCNDE, to qualify for the licensing examination in subdivision (c) of this rule. (c) Pass all parts, written and clinical, of the ADEX examination that is conducted by the CDCA-WREB-CITA, or by another regional testing agency.' ADA Licensure-by-State (updated 9 Oct 2026): 'applicants by examination must pass all parts, written and clinical, of the American Board of Dental Examiners, Inc (ADEX) clinical examination offered by the CDCA-WREB-CITA ... The examination must include ... DSE OSCE; Endodontic portion; Fixed prosthodontic portion; Periodontic/scaling - live patient OR manikin; Restorative (operative) posterior AND anterior portion - live patient OR manikin.' There is no DLOSCE-only, residency, portfolio or credential route for a new graduate, so a hands-on (manikin/live-patient) component is unavoidable.

## DLOSCE notları
Michigan does not accept the DLOSCE in full or partial fulfilment of the clinical examination (it is absent from the JCNDE DLOSCE acceptance list and from the Michigan rules). The rules (R 338.11201(c)) require 'all parts, written and clinical' of the ADEX examination; the ADA summary explicitly lists the 'Computer-based diagnostic skills examination objective structured clinical examination (DSE OSCE)' as ONE of the required ADEX parts alongside endodontic, fixed prosthodontic, periodontic and restorative hand-skill portions. Therefore a DLOSCE pass - whether taken standalone before Oct 2026 or as the ADEX-integrated DSE OSCE/DLOSCE component - only satisfies the 'written' component of ADEX; the manikin components must still be passed. Michigan has published no policy accepting an ADEX-reported DLOSCE pass alone. The 'substantially equivalent' clause applies only to applicants by endorsement (already licensed elsewhere), not to new graduates.

Michigan Dentist Licensing Guide (Revision Date 1/26/2026, p.1-2): 'Social Security Number - An individual applying for licensure is required to provide his or her social security number at the time of application. If exempt under law from obtaining a SSN or do not have a SSN, the SSN affidavit form will be required to be uploaded at the time the application is submitted.' (identical wording in the 08/2021 guide). No ITIN is requested.
- **in_jurisdiction_address_required:** False
- **barrier_level:** low
- **details:** MCL 333.16174 (Public Health Code, general licensure requirements) requires only: age 18+, good moral character, the prescribed education/experience, 'a working knowledge of the English language', payment of fees, disclosure/consent regarding disciplinary history, and (since 1 Oct 2008) submission of fingerprints to the Michigan State Police/FBI for a criminal history check. There is no citizenship, permanent-residence, work-authorisation, lawful-presence or SAVE requirement in the statute, the Board rules read, or the 2026 LARA licensing guide; the guide asks only for the SSN or, for someone who 'do[es] not have a SSN', an SSN affidavit form uploaded with the MiPLUS application. No Michigan address or residency is required; the CE rules even credit renewal of a licence 'held in another state ... if the applicant resides and practices in another state', so out-of-state (and out-of-country) licensees are contemplated. English proficiency is satisfied if the dental program was taught in English, or by 60 college credits from an English-speaking school, or an approved English test (Yeditepe's English-medium DDS should qualify, subject to LARA acceptance). No general Michigan lawful-presence-for-professional-licences statute was found; this was not exhaustively verified.

## Yüz yüze adımlar
(1) Fingerprint-based criminal history check is mandatory for initial licensure (MCL 333.16174(3): applicant 'shall submit his or her fingerprints to the department of state police ... and request that the department of state police forward his or her fingerprints to the federal bureau of investigation'). The guide says that after the online MiPLUS application is submitted LARA emails an 'Application Confirmation letter containing instructions to complete the Criminal Background Check'; Michigan normally uses Live Scan at in-state vendors, and LARA's general practice is to allow out-of-state applicants to use mailed ink fingerprint cards, but the out-of-state/abroad card option was not verified in the pages read (open question). (2) The ADEX clinical examination itself (manikin hand-skill parts) must be taken in person at a CDCA-WREB-CITA site in the US. (3) BLS/ACLS 'with a hands-on component' - can be done anywhere with an AHA-equivalent provider. (4) No Michigan jurisprudence exam, no interview, no notarisation; the application is fully online via MiPLUS, transcripts are sent by the school (emailed to bpldata@michigan.gov or mailed). A short US visit would suffice for Live Scan fingerprinting if cards cannot be used from abroad.

## Diğer başlangıç şartları
INBDE (all parts); full ADEX exam (approx. $2,995 via CDCA-WREB-CITA); current BLS or ACLS for healthcare providers with a hands-on component earned within the 2-year period before licensure (R 338.11201(d)); one-time human-trafficking-identification training meeting R 338.11123 before licensure (required since 6 Jan 2022); implicit-bias training under R 338.7004 is believed to apply to initial applicants (not verified in pages read); fingerprint criminal background check (Michigan State Police/FBI); good moral character questions; English proficiency (program taught in English / 60 English-medium college credits / approved English test per MCL 333.16174(1)(d)); official transcript with DDS/DMD (certified English translation if not in English); SSN or SSN affidavit; no jurisprudence exam; no liability insurance requirement; controlled-substance licence is optional and separate ($259.10). Timeline: LARA typically issues once exam scores, background check and documents are on file (no published processing time read).

## İlk ücretler
Dentist by Exam or Endorsement application fee $322.40 (LARA guide rev. 1/26/2026; paid online by Visa/Mastercard/Discover); statutory components per MCL 333.16323: application processing fee $20.00 and licence fee $90.00 per year (3-year licence); Dentist relicensure $342.40; optional Controlled Substance License $259.10; dental specialty licence $20 processing + $15/yr; temporary licence $20. Plus third-party costs: fingerprint/background check fee (not read), INBDE fee, ADEX full series approx. $2,995.

## Lisansı sürdürme

- **renewal_period:** 3 years (R 338.11701 refers to the licensee being 'licensed for the 3-year period immediately preceding the expiration date'; dental specialty licence renews concurrently). A 60-day late-renewal grace period with late fee applies (MCL 333.16201(2)).
- **renewal_fee:** Statutory licence fee is $90.00 per year (MCL 333.16323(c)(i)), i.e. about $270 per 3-year renewal, plus any late fee if renewed within the 60-day grace period; exact LARA online renewal amount not verified.
- **ce_hours:** 60 hours of board-approved CE per 3-year cycle (R 338.11701(2)-(3)), including at least 3 hours pain and symptom management, 3 hours implicit bias (1 per year), 1 hour human trafficking, 1 hour infection control, 1 hour dental ethics/jurisprudence (cited in relicensure CE list), 3 hours opioid/controlled-substance awareness if DEA-registered, at least 20 hours in delivery of care/materials/pharmacology, and 20 hours in the specialty if a specialty licence is held.
- **ce_online_ok:** Largely yes in practice - R 338.11703 credits ADA CERP/AGD PACE-approved programs without stating an in-person requirement, and no separate live/in-person CE quota was found in the rule text read; however the rule sets category maxima and a possible cap on self-study/online hours was NOT verified (unclear). The only mandatory hands-on element is the BLS/ACLS certification. Credit is also given for renewing a licence in another state in which the licensee 'resides and practices' (up to 60 hours).
- **cpr_required:** Yes. Each renewal requires proof of 'current certification in BLS or ACLS for healthcare providers with a hands-on component from an agency or organization that grants certification pursuant to standards equivalent to those established by the AHA' (R 338.11701; AHA 2020 guidelines adopted by reference in R 338.11705(4)). Fully online CPR is not acceptable; a hands-on skills session is needed (can be done abroad with an AHA-equivalent provider).
- **practice_hours_required:** None. No minimum practice or currency hours are required to renew or keep the licence active.
- **address_or_residency_required:** None. No Michigan address or residency required; the CE rules expressly contemplate a licensee who 'resides and practices in another state'. Licensee must keep a current mailing/e-mail address on file for renewal notices (MCL 333.16201(1)).
- **insurance_required:** No. No mandatory professional liability insurance for dental licensure/renewal in Michigan was found in the statute, rules or guide.
- **inactive_status:** No inactive/retired/non-practising dentist licence category exists (only a 'special retired volunteer dentist' licence for retired dentists providing volunteer care, which has its own requirements). The only alternative to active renewal is letting the licence lapse: it becomes 'null and void' 60 days after expiry (MCL 333.16201(3)).
- **reactivation:** Relicensure after lapse (MCL 333.16201(3)-(4); R 338.11263/relicensure rules; LARA guide): lapsed <=3 years - apply for relicensure ($342.40), proof of hands-on BLS/ACLS within prior 2 years and 60 hours of board-approved CE from the prior 3 years (incl. 3 h pain mgmt, 1 h ethics/jurisprudence, implicit bias etc.); no new background check if expired within last 3 years. Lapsed >3 but <5 years - additionally must EITHER retake and pass the full ADEX clinical examination (CDCA-WREB-CITA or another regional agency) within the prior 2 years OR show a valid, unrestricted licence in another state/Canada held within the prior 3 years. Lapsed >=5 years - must apply as a new applicant meeting all current licensure-by-examination requirements, including retaking and passing ADEX within the prior 2 years (the guide states such applicants 'are not eligible to take another, substantially equivalent exam'). So a long lapse forces a new hands-on exam.
- **remote_maintainability:** moderate
- **details:** The licence can be kept ACTIVE indefinitely from abroad: 3-year renewal online via MiPLUS, ~$270 fee, 60 CE hours (ADA CERP/PACE online courses accepted; specific topic hours required), no practice hours, no in-state address, no insurance. The awkward elements are (a) a BLS/ACLS certification with a hands-on component every cycle and (b) the absence of any inactive status - if the licence lapses more than 3 years the dentist must either hold another US/Canadian licence or re-sit the full hands-on ADEX exam; after 5 years ADEX is mandatory. Whether 100% of CE may be self-study/online is not confirmed.
