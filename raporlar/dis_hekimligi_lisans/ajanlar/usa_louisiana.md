# Louisiana (USA) — ajan bulgusu

Düzenleyici: Louisiana State Board of Dentistry (LSBD) — https://www.lsbd.org/

**Teori-only rota:** no · **Göçmenlik engeli:** high · **Uzaktan sürdürme:** hard · **Güven:** high

## Sınav yolları

- **Licensure by Examination (LBE) - ADEX dental examination (CDCA-WREB-CITA)** (el becerisi: evet): The ONLY route to initial licensure for a new graduate. LSBD LBE application (Rev. 2025): 'The only acceptable clinical licensure examination is the ADEX examination (regardless of testing agency). You must have completed all portions of the ADEX examination (DSE, endodontics, prosthodontics, restorative, and periodontics)' within 5 years of the date the board receives the application (LAC 46:XXXIII.1709.C; R.S. 37:761(C) still says three years). LAC 46:XXXIII.1709.C lists the accepted exams: pre-2012 CITA/CRDTS/NERB/SRTA/WREB, post-2012 CITA, and 'the American Board of Dental Examiners (ADEX) dental examination'. LAC 46:XXXIII.1713.B.1 further requires that an approved clinical exam 'shall include procedures performed on human subjects as part of the assessment of restorative and periodontal clinical competencies' - i.e. a hand-skills component is mandatory. Also required: INBDE/NBDE, written jurisprudence exam (100 T/F-MCQ, 75 to pass), CPR/BLS card, opioid-management course, fingerprint background check, notarized application, proof of citizenship/PR/work authorisation, $350 fee. Max three failures of any clinical exam (1709.F).
- **DLOSCE-only / OSCE-only** (el becerisi: hayır): NOT available. Neither the Louisiana Dental Practice Act, LAC 46:XXXIII Chapter 17, the ADA Louisiana summary (updated 7 July 2026) nor the LSBD LBE application mention the DLOSCE or any OSCE. Louisiana is not on the JCNDE list of DLOSCE-accepting states. The DLOSCE component of ADEX is irrelevant because every ADEX part (five hand-skills exams) must be passed.
- **PGY-1 / residency in lieu of clinical exam** (el becerisi: evet): Not accepted for initial licensure. A one-year accredited post-doctoral programme only serves to overcome the three-failure bar after still passing an accepted clinical exam (LAC 46:XXXIII.1709.F.2). A two-year GPR or specialty residency is relevant only to licensure by credentials, which additionally requires a licence in another state and a prior hand-skills clinical exam (§306.A.4.c-d and A.5).
- **Licensure by Credentials (LBC) - regular or Louisiana Resident LBC** (el becerisi: evet): Only for dentists already holding a nonrestricted licence in another US state: 3 years active practice at >=1,000 h/yr (or 3 yrs full-time teaching, or a 2-year GPR / specialty residency applied for within 180 days), AND must have 'successfully completed an initial clinical licensure examination in a United States jurisdiction that included a hand skills assessment' (LAC 46:XXXIII.306.A.5), plus jurisprudence exam, all R.S. 37:761 requirements (citizenship/work authorisation), possible personal interview, three affidavits, $2,050 fee. Reciprocity is expressly prohibited (R.S. 37:768). Not a new-graduate route.
- **Licensure by credentials for DDS/DMD + MD (LAC 46:XXXIII.308)** (el becerisi: evet): Narrow route for dual-degree dentists licensed in another state AND licensed to practise medicine in Louisiana, with completed residency; still subject to R.S. 37:761. Not applicable.

## Teori-only açıklaması
LAC 46:XXXIII.1709.B: 'To be licensed as a dentist in this state, an applicant for initial licensure must successfully complete the following: 1. a written examination on the jurisprudence and ethics of the state regulating the practice of dentistry; and 2. the Louisiana state Board of Dentistry approved clinical examination.' §1709.C names the accepted exams (CITA post-2012 and 'the American Board of Dental Examiners (ADEX) dental examination'). §1713.B.1: 'The clinical examination shall include procedures performed on human subjects as part of the assessment of restorative and periodontal clinical competencies and shall have included evaluations in at least four of the following subject matter areas...'. LSBD LBE application: 'The only acceptable clinical licensure examination is the ADEX examination (regardless of testing agency). You must have completed all portions of the ADEX examination (DSE, endodontics, prosthodontics, restorative, and periodontics)'. R.S. 37:761(C) likewise requires completion of a 'clinical dental licensing examination approved by the board'. There is no DLOSCE/OSCE, portfolio or residency substitute, so a hands-on exam is unavoidable.

## DLOSCE notları
Louisiana does not accept the DLOSCE in any form. It is absent from the Dental Practice Act, LAC 46:XXXIII (Chapters 1, 3, 17), the ADA Louisiana Licensure summary (updated 7 July 2026) and the LSBD LBE application (which says only the ADEX exam, all portions including DSE, endo, prostho, restorative and perio, is acceptable). Louisiana is not among the DLOSCE-accepting states in the 2026 JCNDE candidate guide. The DLOSCE taken inside the ADEX series does not change anything, because the board verifies in the ADEX results portal that all five hand-skills portions were passed; earlier standalone DLOSCE scores are of no use here.

PGY-1 kabul: False

- **details:** R.S. 37:761(A)(1): 'The board shall require that every applicant for a dental license shall: (1) Be a citizen or possess valid and current legal authority to reside and work in the United States duly issued by the United States Citizenship and Immigration Services or its successor.' This applies to every dental licence (LBE and LBC, via LAC 46:XXXIII.306.A.1). LSBD LBE application, eligibility item 5: 'Eligibility to work in the U.S.: you must be a U.S. citizen, permanent resident, or have legal authorization to live and work in the U.S.' Section 'PROOF OF U.S. CITIZENSHIP OR PERMANENT RESIDENCY STATUS OR LEGAL AUTHORITY TO LIVE AND WORK IN THE U.S.': 'You must show documentation that you have current, valid authority to live and work in the United States. All documentation must be an original or certified true copy and mailed to the board office... U.S. citizens must submit... U.S. birth certificate... or U.S. naturalization certificate. U.S. permanent residents must submit... current U.S. permanent resident card. If you are NOT a U.S. citizen or permanent resident, please call the board office directly to determine what documentation you should submit.' Form item 5 asks 'Citizen or permanent resident of the U.S. OR do you possess valid and current legal authority to live and work in the U.S.? Yes/No'. A B-1/B-2 visitor visa confers no authority to work, so a Turkish citizen with no US status cannot meet R.S. 37:761(A)(1); this is a statutory requirement, not board discretion. Citizenship or PR as such is not required (an EAD/work visa holder qualifies). The board verifies status by document inspection (originals/certified copies); no SAVE check or separate lawful-presence affidavit was seen. No Louisiana residence or in-state address is required for LBE (a mailing address is required; the 'Louisiana Resident LBC' is merely an optional credentials variant).

## Yüz yüze adımlar
(1) Fingerprint criminal background check: 'All applicants for a full Louisiana license must submit to a criminal fingerprint background check. You may schedule your background check after your license application and fee have been received' (lsbd.org/licenseinfo.htm). Scheduled online through IdentoGO (Louisiana service centres) AFTER the board has the application and fee; results received before the application are NOT accepted; a second print at a service centre may be required at extra cost; background check fee $100 (LAC 46:XXXIII fee schedule). Whether out-of-state/foreign ink cards are accepted was not confirmed - assume an in-state Live Scan visit is needed. (2) Jurisprudence exam: 100 T/F and MCQ, 75 to pass, scheduled with the board office only after the application and fee are received; scores valid one year. The delivery format (board office in Metairie vs remote) was not stated in the sources read - assume in person. (3) Application must be signed before a notary (US or foreign notary acceptable? not stated) and original or certified true copies of status documents must be MAILED. (4) ADEX itself is an in-person manikin exam at a US dental school site. (5) LBC applicants may be required to appear for a personal interview before the board (§306.A.17); not required for LBE. A visitor could in principle sequence ADEX, mailing of the application, then one trip for fingerprints and the jurisprudence exam - but the statutory work-authorisation requirement makes the whole exercise moot.

## Diğer başlangıç şartları
Per LSBD LBE application (Rev. 2025): graduate of CODA-accredited school (LAC 46:XXXIII.103; Yeditepe post-1 Feb 2024 qualifies); pass ADEX (all portions) within 5 years; pass INBDE/NBDE; current BLS-for-healthcare-providers CPR card (AHA or Red Cross or equivalent; R.S. 37:761(A)(5)); be a citizen or hold USCIS authority to reside and work in the US; pass board jurisprudence exam (100 questions, 75 to pass, based on Dental Practice Act); fingerprint background check ($100); notarized application; approved opioid-management course (3 h, once in career); recent passport-type photo; NPDB-HIPDB self-query; $350 non-refundable fee by check/money order. No English test (CODA graduate). No liability insurance required for licensure. Board issues the licence after all items are in; the LSBD says LBE 'involves minimal paperwork'. Jurisprudence score valid 1 year; ADEX scores valid 5 years (rule) / 3 years (statute wording).

## İlk ücretler
LBE application $350 (non-refundable); criminal background check $100 (plus IdentoGO service cost); ADEX full series approx. $2,995 (CDCA-WREB-CITA); INBDE fee separate; CPR course cost; opioid course cost. Licensure by credentials: $2,050. Retired volunteer dental licence $50. Renewal: $590 biennial per lsbd.org/fees.htm (LAC 46:XXXIII fee rule text still reads $540); reinstatement of a licence lapsed by non-renewal $500 plus delinquency fee.

## Lisansı sürdürme

- **renewal_period:** Biennial; all nonrestricted licences renewed biennially (LAC 46:XXXIII), expiring 31 December of even years (current cycle expires 31 Dec 2026; 2026-2027 renewal open online or by paper).
- **renewal_fee:** $590 biennial dental licence renewal fee per lsbd.org/fees.htm (the LAC 46:XXXIII fee schedule text reads $540); delinquency fee added for late renewal; reinstatement of a licence lapsed by non-renewal $500.
- **ce_hours:** 30 hours per biennial renewal period (LAC 46:XXXIII.1611.A): 20 hours of 'personally attended clinical courses pertaining to the actual delivery of dental services to patients' + 10 hours of clinical courses that 'may be done in person, online or via correspondence' (online courses must end with a written exam); 3 of the 30 must be an opioid-management course (once per career, or exemption certification if no controlled substances prescribed). First renewal after initial licensure is exempt from CE except opioid course and CPR (§1607.A.3). CE rules 'also apply to all dentists... licensed to practice in Louisiana, but are practicing outside of Louisiana' (§1603.A).
- **ce_online_ok:** No. Only 10 of the 30 hours may be online/correspondence; 20 hours must be 'personally attended' clinical courses (§1611.A.1). Whether a live course attended abroad from an approved sponsor (§1615) counts was not verified; live webinars are not expressly recognised as 'personally attended'.
- **cpr_required:** Yes. R.S. 37:761(A)(5) requires a current AHA healthcare-provider CPR / Red Cross Professional Rescue (or equivalent) certificate; §1611.F: on audit 'a photocopy of the CPR card evidencing successful completion of the course for each year shall be appended'. AHA/Red Cross BLS provider cards require a hands-on skills session (blended learning allowed). CPR cannot be waived even for retired volunteer licensees.
- **practice_hours_required:** None to renew an active licence. BUT rules (LAC 46:XXXIII, reinstatement/inactive provisions, partially read): when a licence has been inactive or the licensee 'has not practiced for one year or greater' the licensee must pass the jurisprudence (Practice Act) examination within 120 days of reinstatement or return to active practice; when inactive for one year or more a new fingerprint background check is required; when inactive for about three years the board may require an evaluation by 'an entity of the board's choosing' at the licensee's cost (exact wording/section to be confirmed).
- **address_or_residency_required:** No Louisiana residence or in-state address required; a current mailing address and telephone number must be kept on file with the board. CE obligations apply equally to licensees practising outside Louisiana.
- **insurance_required:** Not required for licensure or renewal (only LBC applicants must explain malpractice payments and show insurance never revoked, §306.A.19-20).
- **inactive_status:** No general inactive/non-practising category for dentists. The only reduced category is the 'retired volunteer dental license' (R.S. 37:761.1; fee $50, no fee to convert from active): limited to dentists who have practised in good standing and who provide volunteer care; exempt from CE (except opioid course and CPR) only if working fewer than 250 h/yr (§1607.A.5). Otherwise a licence not renewed simply lapses; reinstatement of a licence lapsed by non-renewal costs $500 plus delinquency fee and triggers the inactive-licence requirements (jurisprudence re-exam, new fingerprints, possible evaluation).
- **reactivation:** Lapsed by non-renewal: reinstatement fee $500 + delinquency fee, outstanding CE (must be completed by August of following year under §1605.B), new fingerprint background check if inactive >= 1 year, jurisprudence/Practice Act exam within 120 days if inactive or not practising >= 1 year, and a board-chosen competency evaluation at the licensee's expense after roughly 3 years of inactivity (rule text partially read - verify LAC 46:XXXIII §112-114). No new ADEX exam is explicitly required for reinstatement, but the evaluation can include whatever areas 'the board feels are necessary to have evaluated'.
- **remote_maintainability:** hard
- **details:** Keeping a Louisiana licence active from Turkey would require: $590 every two years; 30 CE hours of which 20 must be personally attended (in-person) clinical courses and 3 opioid hours; a current hands-on BLS card every renewal; mailing/online renewal with an attestation of CE on the board form. There is no inactive status to park the licence; a lapse costs $500 to cure plus jurisprudence re-exam, fingerprints and possible board-ordered evaluation after prolonged non-practice. Combined with the 20-hour in-person CE rule this is rated 'hard' even before the initial-licensure barriers.
