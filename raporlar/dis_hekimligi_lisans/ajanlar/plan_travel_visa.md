# travel_visa

TOPIC: Travel and visas for a Turkish citizen to sit the NDEB Virtual OSCE (Canada, USA, Ireland, Australia), with a recommended sitting and fallbacks, starting 3 Nov 2026


## STEP 1: Pick the target sitting and country today
TIMING: Today, 3 Nov 2026 (decide within 1-2 days)
Plan A: sit the 1 March 2027 Virtual OSCE in Canada. Apply for the Canadian visitor visa (TRV) now, in parallel with the NDEB Certification Process application. Plan A-bis (still March): an Irish venue on a short-stay 'C' visa. The Embassy of Ireland in Ankara lists 'Exam or Interview' as a visit purpose, and the fee is EUR 60. Plan B: 20 May 2027 at a Prometric centre in Canada (same TRV) or the US (B1/B2). Plan C: 22 Nov 2027, Canada only (NDEB application deadline 18 Aug 2027). Australia is not recommended because the official information could not be verified, and the trip is long and expensive.

Why: NDEB lets you register only with an APPROVED application ('If you have an approved application, you can register for the Virtual OSCE'). It also warns: 'It is not unusual for examinations to fill-up prior to the registration deadline.' Your seat and your visa must therefore line up. Location transfers are free in NDEBConnect before the registration deadline ('Location transfers can be done through your NDEBConnect account prior to the registration deadline. There are no fees for transferring your location.').
SOURCES: https://ndeb-bned.ca/certification-process/registering-for-the-exam/ ; https://www.ireland.ie/en/turkiye/ankara/services/visas/visas-for-ireland/

## STEP 2: Assemble the core visa evidence pack (used for every country)
TIMING: 3-10 Nov 2026 (about 1 week; bank letter and employer letter take 1-3 working days)
1) Passport valid well beyond the travel dates. Renew now if it expires before about Sept 2027, because the Canadian visa is tied to the passport. 2) Bank statements for the last 3-6 months, plus a bank letter in English showing enough funds for flights, hotel and the CAD 1,750 exam fee. If a parent sponsors you, add the sponsor letter, the sponsor's statements and proof of relationship. 3) Ties to Türkiye: employment or clinic letter in English (role, salary, approved leave, return date), property or vehicle deeds, family registry (vukuatlı nüfus kayıt örneği). 4) Proof of purpose: Yeditepe diploma and transcript copies; the NDEB application confirmation and receipt from NDEBConnect; once registered, the NDEB registration confirmation and information memo (NDEB: 'The information memo posted in your registration will have a schedule, exact location, and other important details about your exam'); a printout of the NDEB dates page. 5) A cover letter explaining the trip: exam date, about 5-7 days' stay, return ticket, intention to return. 6) Travel history: copies of earlier Schengen, UK and US visas. 7) A refundable hotel booking and flight reservation, not a purchased ticket.

Ireland requires documents 'in English or accompanied by a notarised translation' and says 'It is not sufficient to send in copies of your documents.'
SOURCES: https://www.canada.ca/en/immigration-refugees-citizenship/services/visit-canada/apply-visitor-visa.html ; https://www.ireland.ie/en/turkiye/ankara/services/visas/visas-for-ireland/ ; https://ndeb-bned.ca/certification-process/registering-for-the-exam/

## STEP 3: Confirm you cannot use a Canadian eTA (a visa is mandatory)
TIMING: Today (5 minutes)
Türkiye is NOT on Canada's eTA-X list of visa-required countries whose citizens may use an eTA instead of a visa. A valid US visa does not help. The canada.ca eTA-X page (page details dated 2026-10-30) lists Antigua and Barbuda, Argentina, Brazil, Costa Rica, Mexico, Morocco, Panama, Philippines, St Kitts and Nevis, St Lucia, St Vincent and the Grenadines, Seychelles, Thailand, Trinidad and Tobago and Uruguay. Indonesia and Malaysia were added on 26 May 2026. Even for listed countries the conditions are 'You're flying to or transiting through a Canadian airport' AND you have 'held a Canadian visitor visa (temporary resident visa) in the past 10 years' or 'currently hold a valid United States (US) nonimmigrant visa (NIV)'. A Turkish citizen therefore needs a visitor visa (TRV). Do not pay for an eTA (CAD 7): it is not valid for Turkish passports.
SOURCES: https://www.canada.ca/en/immigration-refugees-citizenship/services/visit-canada/eta/eligibility/eta-x.html ; https://www.canada.ca/en/immigration-refugees-citizenship/services/visit-canada/eta/apply.html

## STEP 4: Apply for the Canadian visitor visa (TRV) online this week
TIMING: Submit by about 7-10 Nov 2026; expect a decision about mid-Jan 2027 (63 days as of 29 Oct 2026, not guaranteed)
Where: the IRCC Portal or an IRCC secure account (canada.ca, 'Apply for a visitor visa'). IRCC states: 'You must apply online.' Paper is allowed only in exceptional cases, such as disability. Fees: processing CAD 100 (individual) plus biometrics CAD 85, paid online by card. In the online questionnaire choose purpose 'Visit / Other' and describe it as 'To sit the NDEB Virtual OSCE dental certification examination, 1 March 2027'. Upload the evidence pack from step 2 and the cover letter.

Current processing time for applications from Türkiye: 63 days. This is the IRCC processing-times data, last updated 29 Oct 2026 (for comparison: Greece 40 days, Ireland 21 days, US 18 days). 63 days counted from about 10 Nov 2026 points to a decision around mid-January 2027. Passport submission and return add about 1-2 weeks. This is feasible for 1 March 2027 ONLY if you apply now. If you wait for NDEB approval and registration (January), 63 days runs past 1 March.

Tip: IRCC does not require an exam registration to apply. Use the NDEB application confirmation now, then send the registration confirmation through the IRCC webform or account as soon as you have it.
SOURCES: https://www.canada.ca/en/immigration-refugees-citizenship/services/visit-canada/apply-visitor-visa.html ; https://www.canada.ca/content/dam/ircc/documents/json/data-ptime-en.json ; https://www.canada.ca/en/immigration-refugees-citizenship/services/application/check-processing-times.html

## STEP 5: Give biometrics at the Canada Visa Application Centre (VFS Global) in Ankara or Istanbul
TIMING: Within 1-2 weeks of paying (by about 20 Nov 2026); passport stage about mid/late Jan 2027
After you pay, IRCC issues a Biometric Instruction Letter (BIL). Book an appointment on VFS Global Türkiye (visa.vfsglobal.com/tur/en/can). VFS says a prior appointment is mandatory and that you should book 'only after you have received your Biometric Instruction Letter (BIL)'. The canada.ca VAC list shows centres in Ankara (Regnum Sky Tower, Çankaya) and Istanbul. Izmir and Gaziantep could not be confirmed for Canada.

IRCC says to 'book your biometrics collection appointment as soon as possible to avoid processing delays'. IRCC's usual instruction is to give biometrics within 30 days of the BIL (not confirmed on the page read). Biometrics for temporary residence are valid for 10 years, so a May or November 2027 trip will not need new ones. Bring the BIL, your passport and the VFS appointment letter. VFS charges its own service fees.

After approval, IRCC sends a passport request. Submit the passport at the same VFS centre and allow about 1-2 weeks for the visa counterfoil and courier return.
SOURCES: https://www.canada.ca/en/immigration-refugees-citizenship/corporate/contact-ircc/offices/find-visa-application-centre ; https://visa.vfsglobal.com/tur/en/can/book-an-appointment ; https://www.canada.ca/en/immigration-refugees-citizenship/services/visit-canada/apply-visitor-visa.html

## STEP 6: Register for the exam the moment NDEB approves your application, and choose the venue that matches your visa
TIMING: Immediately after NDEB approval (target by mid-Dec 2026; hard deadline 18 Jan 2027 21:00 ET)
NDEB steps (verbatim): '1. Login to your NDEBConnect account 2. Select "registration" 3. Select "new registration" 4. Select a location to take the exam. Only locations with space available will be displayed. 5. Pay the registration fee.' The Virtual OSCE fee is CAD 1,750. For 1 Mar 2027, registration for US/Canada venues opened 3 Nov 2026 10:00 ET and for Australia/Ireland opens 10 Nov 2026; the deadline is 18 Jan 2027 21:00 ET. One NDEB mirror page shows 19 Jan 2027, so verify in NDEBConnect.

NDEB does not publish venue cities for March. Its general statement is 'The Virtual OSCE is administered at locations across Canada and at some locations in the USA, Ireland and Australia.' Only venues with seats appear at registration, and the exact address is in the information memo. For May and November: 'the Virtual OSCE will be held at Prometric test centres in Canada and the US ... you will need to schedule your seat time with Prometric after you have registered.'

After registering, download the confirmation and information memo and upload them to IRCC (or attach them to the Irish or US application). Book a flight arriving 2 days before the exam, refundable if possible.
SOURCES: https://ndeb-bned.ca/certification-process/registering-for-the-exam/ ; https://ndeb-bned.ca/en/certification-process/virtual-osce ; https://ndeb-bned.ca/equivalency-process/dates-and-locations/

## STEP 7: Parallel March option: Ireland short-stay 'C' (Visit - Exam) visa or the UK-visa waiver
TIMING: Register for the Ireland venue when NDEB approves (Ireland registration opens 10 Nov 2026); lodge the AVATS application about 1-15 Dec 2026
Who applies: the Embassy of Ireland in Ankara handles applicants 'legally resident in the Türkiye, Azerbaijan or Georgia'. Steps (verbatim): '1. Complete the online application form' (AVATS, visas.inis.gov.ie); '2. Refer to the checklists ... and gather your supporting documentation'; '3. Submit your signed application form and supporting documents.' Submit at an Irish VFS centre (Istanbul, Ankara, Izmir, Antalya or Gaziantep). VFS helpline: info.irelandtr@vfshelpline.com, 0212 373 5802. The checklist category is 'Visit (Including Tourist / Event, Visit Family / Friend, Performance, Exam or Interview)'. Fees in Ankara: single journey EUR 60, multiple EUR 100, plus the VFS service fee; 'This fee cannot be refunded if your application is refused'. Documents must be original, verifiable and in English, and any earlier refusal by any country must be declared.

Processing: Ankara publishes target times and a Weekly Decision Report. The current figure could not be retrieved (estimates of 4-8 weeks are unverified). Ireland usually lets you apply up to 3 months before travel (not confirmed here), so for 1 Mar 2027 lodge in early December 2026.

Waiver alternative: Türkiye IS on Ireland's Short-Stay Visa Waiver Programme list. It applies only if you hold a valid UK short-stay visa (not a UK ETA) and first 'landed and gained lawful entry to the UK on foot of your current UK visa, prior to undertaking the journey to Ireland'. Your Irish visit must end before your UK leave ends. A UK visit visa costs extra and must be obtained first.
SOURCES: https://www.ireland.ie/en/turkiye/ankara/services/visas/visas-for-ireland/ ; https://www.irishimmigration.ie/coming-to-visit-ireland/short-stay-visa-waiver-programme/

## STEP 8: May 2027 fallback in the US: start the B1/B2 process now because interview waits are long
TIMING: DS-160 and fee by mid-Nov 2026 if you want this fallback; interview likely Feb-Apr 2027; target sitting 20 May 2027 (NDEB application deadline 5 Apr 2027; registration 26 Jan - 19 Apr 2027)
Complete the DS-160 online (ceac.state.gov). Pay the MRV fee of USD 185. A further USD 250 'Visa Integrity Fee' was legislated in July 2025, but whether it is collected in Türkiye is unclear. Book the interview at the US Embassy Ankara or the Consulate General Istanbul through the official appointment system, then attend the interview with the evidence pack and NDEB documents.

Wait times: the official travel.state.gov Global Visa Wait Times tool was blocked (403) from this environment. Third-party trackers citing the 17 Sept 2026 State Department update give Istanbul about 4.5 months to the next B1/B2 appointment and Ankara about 2.5-4 months. Other guides say about 175 days for Ankara and 188 for Istanbul. Applying now therefore puts the interview around Feb-Apr 2027: too late for 1 March, plausible for 20 May 2027. If the wait is long, ask the post about an expedited appointment for the exam (exam travel is not a guaranteed expedite ground).

Is sitting an exam a permitted B activity? Licensing and board exams (for example USMLE) are commonly taken on B-1/B-2. This could not be confirmed in 9 FAM 402.2 because fam.state.gov was unavailable. A US venue only exists in May and November at Prometric (US venues in March are 'some locations'), and you schedule the Prometric seat after NDEB registration. Since the Canadian TRV covers Canadian Prometric centres, the US route is mainly useful if the TRV is refused.
SOURCES: https://travel.state.gov/content/travel/en/us-visas/visa-information-resources/global-visa-wait-times.html ; https://www.scholarshipsads.com/us-visa-wait-times/istanbul ; https://www.scholarshipsads.com/us-visa-wait-times/ankara ; https://www.nbcnews.com/news/us-news/visa-integrity-fee-what-to-know-rcna219760 ; https://ndeb-bned.ca/certification-process/registering-for-the-exam/

## STEP 9: Australia (March only): low-priority option
TIMING: Only if needed; lodge by about 15 Nov 2026 for 1 Mar 2027
Turkish citizens need a Visitor visa (subclass 600, Tourist stream, applied for outside Australia) lodged online through ImmiAccount. Turkish passports are not eligible for eVisitor or the ETA. The official Home Affairs pages are JavaScript-rendered or blocked (403) here, so the fee and processing time are unverified. Third-party sources show a fee of about AUD 190-250 (fees rose on 1 July 2026) and 90% of tourist-stream applications finalised in about 33-54 days. Biometrics may be requested at an Australian Visa Application Centre in Türkiye. Use this only if NDEB's Australian venue is the only one with seats and the Canada and Ireland visas fail. Lodge in mid-November 2026 at the latest.
SOURCES: https://immi.homeaffairs.gov.au/visas/getting-a-visa/visa-listing/visitor-600/tourist-stream-overseas ; https://asiapacificgroup.com/blog/australian-tourist-visa-subclass-600-processing-time/

## STEP 10: Decision checkpoint: visa versus seat in mid/late January 2027
TIMING: 18 Jan 2027 (NDEB registration deadline) and again about 15 Feb 2027
About 18 Jan 2027: if you are registered (Canada venue) and the TRV is approved or at the passport stage, keep the booking. If the TRV is still pending, contact IRCC through the webform with the NDEB registration confirmation showing the exam date (an urgent-processing request is at IRCC's discretion). If the Irish visa came through, you can transfer to an Irish venue at no fee, but only before the registration deadline: 'Location transfers can be done through your NDEBConnect account prior to the registration deadline. There are no fees for transferring your location.' After the deadline, if no visa arrives in time, withdraw ('Withdraw can now be processed instantaneously on your NDEBConnect profile') and register for 20 May 2027 at a Canadian Prometric centre. Check the refund terms on NDEB's Withdraw page first. Note that the NDEB application for May must be received by 5 Apr 2027 unless your March approval carries over (ask NDEB).
SOURCES: https://ndeb-bned.ca/certification-process/registering-for-the-exam/

## STEP 11: Travel, exam day and afterwards
TIMING: Late Feb 2027 travel; results by about late April 2027; certificate thereafter
Fly to arrive 1-2 days before 1 March 2027. Carry your passport (the ID used for NDEB), the NDEB information memo and the visa-pack copies for the border officer: return ticket, hotel, funds, NDEB letter. Results: 'Examination results are released within 8 weeks of the exam.' Certification: 'When successful completion of the Virtual OSCE and proof of graduation are confirmed you can be certified. Your certificate will be mailed to the address in your online profile and your name will be added to the National Register.'

Then apply to BCCOHP. The certificate must be no more than 3 years old at application, which is not a constraint here. A visitor visa does not allow you to work. Practising in BC later needs a separate IRCC work permit or PR, even though BCCOHP licensing has no citizenship requirement.
SOURCES: https://ndeb-bned.ca/certification-process/registering-for-the-exam/

## RISKS
1) Order of steps for March 2027. NDEB registration needs an APPROVED application, and the application documents have to be received by 5 Jan 2027. Those documents include the notarized passport copy and the transcript sent by Yeditepe in a sealed envelope, and NDEB must also verify the degree with the university. Approval may therefore only come in mid/late December or January. The Canadian visa takes 63 days (as of 29 Oct 2026) plus passport handling, so it must be applied for NOW, before you have an exam registration. Applying only after registering in January will almost certainly miss 1 March.
2) Seats. Seats can sell out before the deadline ('It is not unusual for examinations to fill-up'), and you cannot reserve one before approval. If the Canadian venues are full when you are approved, you may be offered only Ireland, Australia or the US, which need different visas. Holding an Irish visa application in reserve reduces this risk.
3) No eTA shortcut. Türkiye is not on Canada's eTA-X list, so even a valid US visa does not exempt you. Do not buy an eTA.
4) Processing times are estimates, not guarantees. The 63-day figure changes weekly, and biometrics delays or requests for more documents add time. A previous visa refusal anywhere must be declared; Ireland explicitly refuses applications that conceal one.
5) The visa decision depends on proving you will go home. A fresh graduate without a job is a higher-risk profile. Strong ties (job or residency position, family, property), sufficient funds and a clear one-week itinerary matter. Never say you intend to work in Canada during this trip. Mention the BC licensing goal only as a future, separate step.
6) US route. B1/B2 interview waits of about 3-6 months make March impossible. The State Department wait-time tool could not be checked. The USD 250 Visa Integrity Fee may or may not be charged. The FAM basis for an 'exam' as a B activity was not verified.
7) Irish route. You must be legally resident in Türkiye to apply via Ankara. Documents must be originals in English. The fee is non-refundable. The current target processing time was not retrievable. The UK-visa waiver needs an actual UK visa (not an ETA) and a first entry into the UK.
8) Venues. NDEB publishes no March venue cities; the exact venue appears only in the information memo after registration. Book refundable flights and hotels until the memo is out.
9) Deadline mismatch. The task data says the 1 Mar 2027 registration deadline is 18 Jan 2027 21:00 ET, but an NDEB mirror page shows 19 Jan 2027 and also mentions a 6 Feb 2027 session. Treat 18 Jan as the binding date until you check NDEBConnect.
10) Withdrawal costs. Withdraw refund rules were not read, so check the NDEB 'Withdraw' page before booking.
11) After certification. A visitor visa does not allow work. Practising in BC needs a separate IRCC work permit or PR, even though BCCOHP licensing has no citizenship requirement.
