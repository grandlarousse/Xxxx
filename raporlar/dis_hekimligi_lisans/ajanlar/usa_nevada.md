# Nevada (USA) — ajan bulgusu

Düzenleyici: Nevada State Board of Dental Examiners (Board of Dental Examiners of Nevada, NSBDE) — https://dental.nv.gov/

**Teori-only rota:** no · **Göçmenlik engeli:** high · **Uzaktan sürdürme:** hard · **Güven:** high

## Sınav yolları

- **General license by examination – ADEX Dental Examination (CDCA-WREB-CITA)** (el becerisi: evet): NRS 631.240(1)(b)(1): applicant 'must successfully pass a clinical examination approved by the Board and the American Board of Dental Examiners'. NAC 631.090 spells out what that exam must contain: 'Pass the Dental Simulated Clinical Examination ...; Demonstrate proficiency in endodontics ...; fixed prosthodontics ...; restorative dentistry ...; periodontics ...; and perform such other procedures as the Board requires' – i.e. the full manikin hand-skills series. Board guide (6/2026): 'Passed all required sections of the ADEX or WREB clinical examination'; scores forwarded via adextesting.org. Application fee $1,200 (NAC 631.029). Also requires INBDE/National Board certificate (NRS 631.240(1)(a)), online jurisprudence exam, fingerprints, CPR card, notarized application, proof of US citizenship/work authorization.
- **General license by examination – WREB clinical examination (legacy, now part of CDCA-WREB-CITA)** (el becerisi: evet): NRS 631.240(1)(b)(2): alternatively 'present to the Board a certificate granted by the Western Regional Examining Board which contains a notation that the applicant has passed a clinical examination administered by the Western Regional Examining Board'. Board website: 'The Board accepts two clinical examinations ADEX (after October 1, 2007) or WREB.' Hands-on (manikin/patient) exam.
- **DLOSCE-only / OSCE-only route** (el becerisi: evet): NOT available. Nevada is not on the JCNDE list of DLOSCE-accepting states; NRS 631 and NAC 631 contain no reference to the DLOSCE or any OSCE; NAC 631.090 makes the manikin demonstrations (endo, fixed pros, restorative, perio, DSE) mandatory components of the accepted clinical exam, and the Board's 2026 guide requires 'all required sections of the ADEX or WREB clinical examination'. A pass on the ADEX-integrated DLOSCE component alone would be a partial ADEX result and would not satisfy NRS 631.240(1)(b). No theory-only route exists.
- **PGY-1 / residency in lieu of clinical exam** (el becerisi: evet): NOT accepted for a general license. The only residency-related licence is the Limited License (NRS 631.271) issued 'without a clinical examination' to a dental intern/resident or faculty member who holds a contract with an accredited Nevada program; it must be renewed annually ($200), 'Once the residency or instruction contract has ended, the license expires' (Board guide), and it does not convert into a general license without passing ADEX/WREB. It also requires US work authorisation and a visa-sponsored position, which the candidate excludes.
- **Restricted geographical license / restricted license (NRS 631.274, 631.275) – clinical-exam waiver for underserved-area employment** (el becerisi: hayır): Board may issue 'without a clinical examination required by NRS 631.240' a restricted geographical license when a board of county commissioners, or the director of a federally qualified health center or nonprofit clinic, requests a waiver for a specific applicant who will practise under contract in that area (NRS 631.274(1)(a)-(c)); the holder may later petition for an unrestricted license without a clinical examination (NRS 631.274(7)). Fee $600; annual renewal; licence tied to the employment contract. Not realistic for the candidate: it requires an actual Nevada employer, physical practice in Nevada and therefore US work authorisation.
- **Licensure by endorsement / credentials (existing out-of-state licensee)** (el becerisi: hayır): Nevada form offers 'General License by: ... Endorsement' ($1,200) and 'Specialty License by Cred.' ($1,325); NAC 631.030(2) requires additional information for licensure by endorsement (certification of good standing from every state of practice, etc.). This is for dentists already licensed and practising in another US state/territory/DC; not open to a new graduate with no prior US licence. Detailed criteria (years of practice) not verified in this research.
- **Military reciprocity** (el becerisi: hayır): Expedited license for active/retired military personnel and military spouses holding another state's licence (Board website). Not applicable to the candidate.

## Teori-only açıklaması
NRS 631.240(1): 'Any person desiring to obtain a license to practice dentistry in this State ... (a) ... must present to the Board a certificate granted by the Joint Commission on National Dental Examinations ...; and (b) Except as otherwise provided in this chapter, must: (1) Successfully pass a clinical examination approved by the Board and the American Board of Dental Examiners; or (2) Present to the Board a certificate granted by the Western Regional Examining Board which contains a notation that the applicant has passed a clinical examination administered by the Western Regional Examining Board.' NAC 631.090: 'an applicant taking the clinical examination approved by the Board and the American Board of Dental Examiners or the clinical examination administered by the Western Regional Examining Board must: 1. Pass the Dental Simulated Clinical Examination or a comparable examination ...; 2. Demonstrate proficiency in endodontics ...; 3. ... fixed prosthodontics ...; 4. ... restorative dentistry ...; 5. ... periodontics ...; and 6. Perform such other procedures as the Board requires.' The only statutory exceptions to the clinical exam (NRS 631.271 limited license for residents/faculty; NRS 631.274/631.275 restricted licences for underserved-area employment; endorsement for existing licensees) all require a Nevada employment contract or a prior US licence. There is no DLOSCE/OSCE, portfolio or PGY-1 substitute. Hence a hands-on manikin examination (ADEX or WREB) is unavoidable for a new graduate.

## DLOSCE notları
Nevada does not accept the DLOSCE in any form. Neither NRS 631 nor NAC 631 mentions the DLOSCE or an OSCE; Nevada is absent from the JCNDE 2026 list of accepting states; NAC 631.090 requires the manikin hand-skill demonstrations as mandatory components of the accepted ADEX/WREB exam; the Board's Initial License Application Guide (dated 6/2026) requires 'Passed all required sections of the ADEX or WREB clinical examination' with scores sent through adextesting.org. Because ADEX reports pass/fail per component, a candidate who passed only the ADEX-integrated DLOSCE component would have an incomplete ADEX result and would not qualify (NAC 631.140 treats missing sections as a failed/incomplete exam requiring re-examination). Earlier standalone DLOSCE scores have no standing in Nevada.

PGY-1 kabul: False

## Göçmenlik / kimlik

- **citizenship_or_pr_required:** False
- **work_authorization_required:** True
- **lawful_presence_verification:** True
- **ssn_or_sin_required:** False
- **itin_or_no_ssn_alternative:** ITIN accepted by statute: NRS 622.238(3) – 'Notwithstanding the provisions of NRS ... 631.225 ..., an applicant for a license who does not have a social security number must provide an alternative personally identifying number, including, without limitation, an individual taxpayer identification number'. However the Board's own application form (rev. 6/8/2026) still shows '1. *Social Security Number' as a required (*) field and NRS 631.225(1)(a) still says the applicant 'shall include the social security number of the applicant in the application'; the ITIN alternative therefore has to be invoked with the Board under NRS 622.238.
- **in_jurisdiction_address_required:** False
- **barrier_level:** high
- **details:** Citizenship/PR is not required, but proof of US work eligibility is. NAC 631.030(1)(f) requires every applicant to provide: 'If he or she is not a natural born citizen of the United States, a copy of his or her certificate of naturalization or other document attesting that he or she is legally eligible to reside and work in the United States'. The Board's Initial License Application Guide (6/2026) lists for the licence-by-exam checklist: '7. Proof of U.S citizenship or authorization to work in the U. S. (See pg. 19 for details)' and its eligibility summary says '2. Have proof of U.S citizenship or legal authorization to work in the U.S.'; the application form has a mandatory field '*United States Work Authorization Status: US Citizen / Legal Resident / Naturalized Resident ...' (remaining options truncated in extraction). A Turkish citizen on a B-1/B-2 visitor visa has no document attesting legal eligibility to work, so cannot satisfy this as written. Lawful presence is verified by document copy, not via SAVE; Nevada has no state SAVE statute for professional licences. Counter-point: NRS 622.238(2) (added 2019, amended 2021/2023) provides that 'a regulatory body shall not deny the application of a person for the issuance of a license pursuant to this title based solely on his or her immigration or citizenship status', and subsection (1)(b) declares that 'each resident of this State, regardless of his or her immigration or citizenship status, is eligible to receive the benefit of applying for a license' under 8 U.S.C. 1621(d). This statute could be argued to override NAC 631.030(1)(f), but it is framed around Nevada residents, the Board's 2026 forms still demand work-authorisation proof, and no Board policy applying NRS 622.238 to non-resident visitors was found – so the practical barrier remains high. No Nevada address or residency is required (form asks for home/mailing/practice addresses; NAC 631.150 only requires keeping the Board informed of current addresses).

## Yüz yüze adımlar
(1) ADEX/WREB manikin clinical exam: must be taken in person at a US test site (unavoidable). (2) Fingerprints: NRS 631.220(1)(c) requires 'a complete set of fingerprints' for FBI/Nevada DPS check. Board website: applicants outside Nevada 'will receive two fingerprint cards by mail' after submitting the online Fingerprint Packet Request Form; 'Electronic (Live Scan) submissions cannot be accepted for applicants who are fingerprinted outside Nevada', but a live-scan agency may print onto the Board cards; Live Scan is available for applicants physically in Nevada ('Applicants do not need to be Nevada residents to use Live Scan'). So fingerprinting can be done from abroad on ink cards (a local police/fingerprint service rolling the Board-issued cards), results take 2-6 weeks and are valid only 90 days, so timing must be coordinated with the complete application. (3) Application must be notarized ('you must have the application properly notarized before submission'; 'APPLICANT NOTARY' block; applications 'not notarized will be returned') – doable during a US visit or, subject to Board acceptance, before a foreign notary/US consulate (not verified). (4) Jurisprudence exam: NRS 631.240(2) 'The Board shall examine each applicant in writing on the contents and interpretation of this chapter and the regulations of the Board' – administered as an 'Online jurisprudence exam' (registration provided by the Board office), so no travel needed. (5) No interview. (6) Timelines: Board reviews within 14 business days of a complete packet; licence issued/collected within 30 days of approval; certain items (e.g. jurisprudence) may be submitted within 90 days of approval for some licence types. Overall, apart from the hands-on exam, the paperwork can be completed during one short visit or largely from abroad.

## Diğer başlangıç şartları
Age 21+, graduate of an accredited dental school, good moral character (NRS 631.230); National Board (INBDE) certificate (NRS 631.240(1)(a)); ADEX or WREB clinical exam passed (all sections); online jurisprudence examination on NRS/NAC 631 (NRS 631.240(2)); copy of current CPR certification (NAC 631.030(1)(o)); recent photograph; complete fingerprint set/FBI background check (NRS 631.220(1)(c)); child-support statement (NRS 631.225(1)(b)); SSN (or ITIN via NRS 622.238); proof of US citizenship or work authorisation (NAC 631.030(1)(f)); certification from the dean of the dental college (Board form); HIPAA form; disclosure of other licences, discipline, claims; notarized application; application fee $1,200 (3% surcharge if paid by card); optional laser-proficiency course (6 h) if lasers will be used. No English test, no liability insurance, no interview. Processing: 14 business days after complete packet; fingerprints 2-6 weeks, valid 90 days.

## İlk ücretler
General dental license by exam (ADEX/WREB) or endorsement: $1,200 application fee (NAC 631.029; Board form). Plus: fingerprint/background-check vendor fee (set by vendor/NV DPS, amount not stated), online jurisprudence exam (registration via Board; fee not stated), NRS/NAC booklets $3 each (optional), 3% credit-card surcharge. Exam costs separate (ADEX full series $2,995 per known facts). Limited license (resident/faculty) $125; restricted geographical $600; specialty by credential $1,325.

## Lisansı sürdürme

- **renewal_period:** Biennial: general dentist licences expire June 30 of each odd-numbered year (NRS 631.330(1)-(2); Board website). Failure to renew by June 30 'automatically suspends the license', reinstatable on payment of the reinstatement fee and compliance (NRS 631.330(2)(b)); NRS 631.330(3) provides further consequences (revocation) if not reinstated within 12 months (text not fully read).
- **renewal_fee:** Active general dentist $600 biennial; inactive dentist $200 biennial; retired/disabled dentist $50 biennial (NAC 631.029 / 2025-27 non-active renewal form; statutory caps NRS 631.345: inactive max $400, retired max $100); reinstatement of suspended licence $300; revoked $500; reactivation from inactive/retired to active $300 (form 'License Reactivation ($300)'); CE audit (2nd+) $200; 3% card surcharge.
- **ce_hours:** 40 hours per biennium for dentists (NAC 631.173(1): 'biennially complete at least 40 hours'); of which at least 30 hours in approved clinical subjects (NAC 631.175(2)) and 4 hours infection control per biennium (NAC 631.175(4)); plus statutory topics under NRS 631.342: one-time 4-hour bioterrorism/WMD course, 2 hours SBIRT (screening, brief intervention, referral to treatment), 2 hours on persons with disabilities / controlled-substance prescribing if registered under NRS 453.231, and other specified topics; newly licensed dentists need only the annual (20 h) share for the partial first cycle.
- **ce_online_ok:** No, not all. NAC 631.176(c): 'For courses completed via home study, on-line study, self-study or journal study through correspondence, webinar, compact disc or digital video disc, not more than 50 percent of the number of hours of continuing education required by subsection 1 or 2 of NAC 631.173' may be credited. So at least 20 of the 40 biennial hours must come from courses 'conducted by an approved instructor' (in-person) or other live categories (dental society meetings max 3 h, group study 3 h, volunteer nonprofit services max 6 h). Webinars count as the capped online category.
- **cpr_required:** Yes. NAC 631.173(4): 'each dentist ... must maintain current certification in administering cardiopulmonary resuscitation ... Any course taken pursuant to this subsection must be taught by a certified instructor.' Proof of current CPR certification must be submitted at renewal (NAC 631.177(1)(d)). Rule does not say 'hands-on' explicitly, but requires an instructor-taught course; whether an online-only CPR certificate is accepted is unverified (likely not). Inactive/retired licensees are exempt from CE (NRS 631.343), which includes this.
- **practice_hours_required:** No minimum hours, but an active licence cannot be kept while not practising in Nevada: NRS 631.335(1) 'The license of a person who does not actively practice in this State for 1 year automatically reverts to inactive status at the time the license renewal fee is next payable.' A dentist living abroad will therefore hold an INACTIVE Nevada licence after the first renewal.
- **address_or_residency_required:** No Nevada address or residency required for initial licensure or renewal; licensees must keep the Board informed of current home/mailing/practice addresses and report changes within 30 days (NAC 631.150; renewal form). Foreign address appears acceptable (form has free-text address fields).
- **insurance_required:** None found. NRS 631 / NAC 631 contain no mandatory professional liability insurance requirement for licensure or renewal.
- **inactive_status:** Available and cheap. Licensee may request inactive or retired status in writing before the licence expires (NAC 631.170(1)); licence also reverts to inactive automatically after 1 year of not practising in Nevada (NRS 631.335(1)). Inactive dentist renewal $200 per biennium (retired/disabled $50); 'A holder of an inactive license, or the holder of a license who is retired or has a disability, is exempt from the requirement of continuing education' (NRS 631.343). No maximum duration of inactive status is stated; renew biennially by June 30 using the Non-Active Biennial Renewal Application (online or PDF).
- **reactivation:** Depends on what the licensee did meanwhile (NAC 631.170; NRS 631.335). (a) If the licensee 'has maintained an active license and practice outside this State' (e.g. practising in Turkey) the Secretary-Treasurer may reinstate administratively on: payment of fees (reactivation $300 + renewal), a list of employment while inactive, disclosure of claims/discipline/peer review, 'proof of ... completion of an amount of continuing education, prorated as necessary, for the year in which the license is restored to active status', certification of good standing from each jurisdiction of current practice, good moral character – no new clinical exam. (b) If NOT practising elsewhere and inactive < 2 years: fees, a signed and notarized petition for reinstatement, prorated CE, employment list. (c) If NOT practising elsewhere and inactive/retired 2 years or more: all of (b) plus 'pass such additional examinations for licensure as the Board may prescribe' (NAC 631.170(4)(b)) – the Board can require a clinical re-examination. Retired status reinstatement requires a motion of the Board (NRS 631.335(2)). Lapsed (suspended) licence: reinstate with $300 fee and compliance; revoked after extended lapse ($500 + petition).
- **remote_maintainability:** hard
- **details:** Keeping the licence ACTIVE from abroad is not possible: NRS 631.335(1) automatically converts the licence to inactive at the next renewal once the dentist has not practised in Nevada for 1 year, and even before that half of the 40 biennial CE hours must be non-online and an instructor-taught CPR certificate is needed. The realistic long-term option is INACTIVE status: $200 every two years, no CE, no CPR, no address requirement, no stated time limit, and administrative reactivation ($300 + prorated CE + good-standing letters) for a dentist who has kept practising elsewhere; the risk is NAC 631.170(4) (possible re-examination) if the licensee has not practised anywhere for 2+ years. Renewal (active or inactive) can be done online with card payment.
