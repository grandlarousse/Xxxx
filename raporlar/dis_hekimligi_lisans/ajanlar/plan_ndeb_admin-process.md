# ndeb_admin-process

TOPIC: NDEB Certification Process (accredited-programme route, Virtual OSCE) for a Turkish Yeditepe DDS graduate living in Türkiye, from today (3 Nov 2026) to the NDEB certificate, ready for a BCCOHP licence application


## STEP 1: Lock in your eligibility basis and your deadlines before you pay anything
TIMING: Today, 3 Nov 2026 (about 1 hour)
1) Eligibility category: "Received a BDS/DDS/DMD from an accredited dental program within the last 5 years" (how-to-apply). By-law 2 s.7.1.2 says you are eligible if you are "a graduate of an Accredited Program ... and not more than sixty (60) months have elapsed since the date of graduation". This depends on your diploma (conferral) date being ON or AFTER Yeditepe's CODA/CDAC recognition date (1 Feb 2024). Check the conferral date printed on your Yeditepe transcript before you apply.
2) Your 60-month clock: graduation date + 60 months is the last moment you can still pass as an ordinary candidate. By-law 2 s.7.4: "A person who does not pass the Virtual OSCE while designated as a Certification Candidate pursuant to Section 7.1.2 ... shall automatically cease to be a Certification Candidate." After that, s.8 Special Consideration applies.
3) BC clock: BCCOHP requires the NDEB certificate to be issued no more than 3 years before the BC application, so plan the BC application soon after certification.
4) Sittings you can realistically aim for, with Türkiye times (TRT = UTC+3; ET is EST until 14 Mar 2027, then EDT):
- 1 Mar 2027 (US/Canada). Registration opened TODAY, 3 Nov 2026, at 10:00 ET (18:00 TRT). Deadline 18 Jan 2027 21:00 ET (19 Jan 05:00 TRT). Documents must be received by 5 Jan 2027.
- 1 Mar 2027 (Australia/Ireland). Opens 10 Nov 2026, 18:00 TRT, same deadline.
- 20 May 2027 (US/Canada, Prometric). Opens 26 Jan 2027, 18:00 TRT. Deadline 19 Apr 2027 21:00 ET (20 Apr 04:00 TRT). Documents by 5 Apr 2027.
- 22 Nov 2027 (Canada only). Opens 25 May 2027, 17:00 TRT. Deadline 25 Oct 2027 21:00 ET (26 Oct 04:00 TRT). Documents by 18 Aug 2027.
You cannot register until your application is APPROVED, so registration opening today does not help you yet.

## STEP 2: Create your NDEBConnect account and pay the CAD 450 application fee
TIMING: This week (3–7 Nov 2026), about 1 hour. Do it first because your NDEB ID is needed on every later document.
Where: ndeb-bned.ca, How to Apply, Step 4, "CREATE ACCOUNT". Login page: ndeb-bned.ca/login-online-profile/.

What you need (stated on the NDEB Equivalency how-to-apply page; the Certification page gives no separate list): "a current photograph in JPEG or PNG format", "access to a desktop or laptop computer", "a valid credit card". No pixel size or background rules are published, so use a recent passport-style colour photo.

Order of steps (official): "You will be required to pay the application fee before you can upload your photograph and finish setting up your account."
Fee: "Application Fee (one-time fee) $ 450" (CAD, non-refundable).
Payment: only "a valid credit card" is documented. Card brands are not published, so use a card enabled for international online payments in CAD.

Fields and choices:
- Legal name exactly as on your passport. "your document must show your name exactly as it appears on your online profile". Treat Turkish characters (ş, ğ, ı, İ, ç, ö, ü) carefully: the exam ID check needs the name to match "exactly".
- Email: "use an email address that you check regularly. All communication about your application and registration will be sent to this email". It is also your username.
- University: "Your degree and graduation date/expected graduation date are verified directly with the university you have selected during application." Select Yeditepe University. If Yeditepe is not in the list, stop and email info@ndeb-bned.ca before submitting. Do not choose a different school.
- Mailing address: your certificate goes there by regular mail. Use a reliable Turkish address with a postal code and keep it updated.

Write down your NDEB ID: "you will need to include this in all correspondence with the NDEB." It goes on the Form and in the subject line of good-standing emails.

Rules: do not share your password and do not use bots. Both are misrepresentation under By-law 2 s.5 (July 2026).

## STEP 3: Order your official transcript from Yeditepe and alert the Dean's office about NDEB's education check
TIMING: Request by about 10 Nov 2026 so it arrives in Ottawa well before 5 Jan 2027. Verification of Education must be confirmed by 14 Feb 2027 (March sitting), 5 May 2027 (May) or 7 Nov 2027 (November).
Proof of Graduation (official): "An original official transcript of marks be sent directly from your university to the NDEB in a sealed envelope." Electronic copies are accepted only via MyCreds, Parchment, National Student Clearinghouse or My eQuals, sent to verification@ndeb-bned.ca.
The transcript must show the conferral date: "A final transcript must indicate the date the degree was conferred. An interim transcript of marks or letter will not include the date of conferral and is not acceptable."
It must be a complete record of all courses and grades from all dental schools attended, with the dates you attended.

At Yeditepe Öğrenci İşleri, ask for:
(a) an English-language official transcript showing the mezuniyet/conferral date;
(b) the university to mail it in a sealed, stamped envelope DIRECTLY to: NDEB, 340 Albert Street, 12th Floor, Ottawa, Ontario, Canada K1R 7Y6, with your NDEB ID on the cover;
(c) tracked courier if possible.
Never send it yourself. If only a Turkish version exists, ask NDEB how to handle the translation, because the transcript must come sealed from the university.

Verification of Education: NDEB contacts the university itself (By-law 2 s.7.2: verification "shall be provided by the Dean or a Dean's Designate"). "If your graduation information is not confirmed at least 15 days prior to the examination date, you will be notified and may be withdrawn. Withdrawal fees will be applied." Tell the Dekanlık to expect NDEB's request and to answer quickly.

When the transcript is due: see Step 8. The Form says it is not needed before the exam, but the how-to-apply page lists it for your category. Send it now so it cannot become a blocker.

## STEP 4: Get a notarized colour photocopy of your passport that meets NDEB's English/French rule
TIMING: Book this week; get it done by about 20 Nov 2026.
The ID must be government-issued and show legal name, date of birth, photo and signature. It must be "valid (not expired)", issued by a government authority, and "no more than 10 years since issued".

Notarization rules (official):
- "The notarizing officer must make a colour photocopy of the original document. Do not photocopy the document yourself."
- The officer "places their original seal and signature in ink on the copy" and states it is a true copy.
- "The notary statement must be in English or French. Translated statements are not accepted."
- "The original seal must display the notarizing officer's credentials, including full name and title ... in English or French."
- "No original stamps or signatures other than the notarizing officer's should be on the photocopy."
- If the statement is on a separate page, the notary must attach it.
- "A statement indicating that you are swearing that the document is a true copy ... will not be accepted."

A standard Turkish noter onay statement and seal are in Turkish, so they will fail. NDEB's fallback: "you may have an Officer of a Canadian Consulate or Embassy, or a New Zealand Consulate or Embassy make a notarized photocopy of your document(s)." In practice, book the notarial service at the Embassy of Canada in Ankara or the Consulate in Istanbul (fee and availability not verified here). Alternatives are a notary or lawyer whose statement and seal are natively in English (not translated).

Sign every NDEB document exactly as you signed your passport. Mismatched signatures must be re-submitted.

## STEP 5: Arrange a Statement of Good Standing and a gap affidavit, only if they apply to you
TIMING: Start in November 2026. Everything must be received by 5 Jan 2027 for the March cycle (or 5 Apr / 18 Aug 2027 for later sittings).
Statement of Good Standing: needed if you are or were ever licensed or registered to practise anywhere (for example, Turkish Ministry of Health diploma registration and practice registration with the regional dental chamber).
- It must come from "all licensing bodies in which you were licensed".
- The licensing body sends it to verification@ndeb-bned.ca "from an official, traceable email account, and include the name and position of the appropriate authority". Gmail, Yahoo and Hotmail are refused.
- "Please request that your NDEB identification number be included in the subject of the email."
- World Document Services is also accepted.
- Not needed for "an academic license or a license for internships only".

Gap affidavit: "If after completing your dental education in any country, you were not licensed for a period of twelve (12) months or more, you must submit an original sworn affidavit attesting to the fact that you were not licensed, including the reason."
- "Original sworn affidavit means the applicant's written sworn statement in English or French that is legally binding, signed and sealed, or stamped by a Notary Public, lawyer, or Commissioner of Oaths". The seal must show credentials in English or French.
- The same Canadian embassy/consulate visit can usually handle this.
- If you were never licensed and 12+ months have passed since graduation by the time you submit, include it.

Name difference (for example, a different spelling on the Yeditepe transcript): send a notarized marriage or name-change document, or an original sworn affidavit explaining the difference.

## STEP 6: Fill in and sign the 2026 Certification Process Form
TIMING: Late November 2026, at the same time as Step 4.
Download: https://ndeb-bned.ca/wp-content/uploads/2025/09/2026_Certification-Process-Form.pdf (2 pages). "Carefully read, sign, and submit this page with your first submission of required documents."

Sections and fields:
(1) Header: Legal Name; NDEB ID Number.
(2) Document Checklist (tick boxes). Documents from you: this Form, completed and signed; notarized valid government-issued photo ID; proof of name change if required; sworn affidavit on gaps in licensing if required. Documents from the licensing body: Statement of Good Standing. Documents from the university after graduation: proof of graduation. The form states: "This is not required to be submitted before taking the examination."
(3) Disclosure. Tick ONE of: "I have never been licensed to practice dentistry in any country"; licence never suspended or revoked; licence suspended or revoked; licence currently under probation.
(4) Licence table (only if ever licensed): Province or State and Country; Profession; First date of registration (DD/MM/YY); End of registration (DD/MM/YY); Reason for ending registration. "This information may be verified directly with the dental regulator."
(5) Consent, three statements: you have read the requirements; you authorize release of academic information from all universities; you authorize release of licensure information from all regulators.
(6) Applicant's Signature and Date [DD/MM/YYYY].

There is NO witness, notary or guarantor box. Only your own signature is needed, and it must match your passport signature.
"All documents submitted to the NDEB become the property of the NDEB and will not be returned."

## STEP 8: Track document status in NDEBConnect and wait for the approval email
TIMING: About 2–3 weeks after your package arrives (5 business days to log, 10 business days to decide), so roughly mid-December 2026 if it arrives in early December.
Who reviews: NDEB staff (credential verification). If you are ineligible, the Registrar may deny (By-law 2 s.5.1).

Times (2025 Service Standards):
- "Documents received in the NDEB office will normally be entered into your online profile within five business days of receipt."
- Certification Process: "Upon receipt of your documents, you will normally receive a decision by email within 10 business days."
- Email enquiries are normally acknowledged "within one business day".

Status: each document's status updates on your NDEBConnect application page, including the "Verification of Education with University" line. "You will receive an email when your application is approved."

Which documents are needed for approval and registration: the mailed items (ID, Form, affidavit if any) and any good-standing statement must be in by the application deadline. For the transcript, two official sources conflict:
- The 2026 Form says proof of graduation is "not required to be submitted before taking the examination".
- By-law 2 s.6.1.2 ties proof of graduation formally to certification for expected graduates.
- But the how-to-apply page lists the transcript as a required document for your within-5-years category.
To be safe, have the transcript arrive before approval, and confirm with verification@ndeb-bned.ca.

If something is missing or wrong you get an email. Fix it immediately. After approval, "Approved" appears and "registration" becomes available.

## STEP 9: Register for the Virtual OSCE and pay CAD 1,750
TIMING: March 2027: second period about early-to-mid January 2027, only if approved by then. May 2027 (recommended): first period 26 Jan 2027, 18:00 TRT. November 2027: 25 May 2027, 17:00 TRT.
Official steps: "1. Login to your NDEBConnect account 2. Select 'registration' 3. Select 'new registration' 4. Select a location to take the exam. Only locations with space available will be displayed. 5. Pay the registration fee."
Fee: "Virtual OSCE $1750" (CAD, fees effective 1 Jul 2025, subject to change). Payment is online in NDEBConnect; a credit card is the documented method. Afterwards see "my registrations". The information memo there has "a schedule, exact location, and other important details". "Location memos are usually posted ... within two weeks of the registration deadline date."

Two registration periods (changed 13 Aug 2026, in force from 1 Sep 2026):
- "First registration: Opens on a set date and time and closes when all exam locations are full."
- "Second registration: Opens about 6–8 weeks before the exam. Candidates may register or transfer if seats become available because of withdrawals or increased capacity."
- Estimated second windows: about 4–18 Jan 2027 (March), about 25 Mar–8 Apr 2027 (May), about 27 Sep–11 Oct 2027 (November). NDEB announces the exact dates.

If seats are full: "You can only register for examinations when registration is open and if spaces are available ... It is not unusual for examinations to fill-up prior to the registration deadline." There is no waitlist; use the second period or the next sitting.

Regions:
- North America opens one week before Australia/NZ (Ireland is grouped with Australia for March 2027).
- "No Transfers Between Regions". Choose a region you can get a visa for.
- For you, Canadian sites are best (Canadian visitor visa only). Ireland or Australia would need those countries' visas.

Transfers: "Location transfers can be done through your NDEBConnect account prior to the registration deadline. There are no fees for transferring your location." But "Candidates may transfer between exam locations only when registration is open. They may not transfer between registration periods."

Withdrawal fees (all non-refundable portions):
- Before the registration deadline: 50% (CAD 875).
- After the deadline or no-show: 100% (CAD 1,750).
- Approved serious medical or unforeseen exceptional circumstance: 25% (CAD 437.50). Withdraw in NDEBConnect before the exam starts, then email a Refund Request with documents within 7 days.
- A no-show or withdrawal does not cost an attempt: "If you do not attend the examination, you will not lose an attempt."

Strategy: your approval will almost certainly come after today's first March opening (3 Nov 2026), so March 2027 is a stretch that depends on second-period seats. The realistic primary target is 20 May 2027 (Prometric). Be logged in, approved and ready at 18:00 TRT on 26 Jan 2027, when first registration opens.

## STEP 10: Ask for test accommodations if you need them
TIMING: Immediately after you register.
Grounds: a documented disability, a medical diagnosis, or a religious reason. Use the Test Accommodation Request Form, completed by a licensed health care professional.

Deadlines (Exam & Appeal Policies 2026):
- s.9.4: if the need is known when you apply, you "must submit an Accommodation Request immediately following registration for their first Examination".
- s.9.5: new needs "no later than eight weeks prior to the Examination date". That is 4 Jan 2027 for March, 25 Mar 2027 for May, 27 Sep 2027 for November.
- Medication or medical equipment: "you must notify the NDEB in writing, within 15 business days of the examination date", with NDEB approval before the exam.
- Holy-day conflict: "Virtual OSCE ... rescheduled on Monday after regular scheduled date, where available."

NDEB does not accept accommodation letters from universities or other organizations.

## STEP 14: Get your results; request a score check or retake if you fail
TIMING: Within 8 weeks of the exam. Verification request within 30 days of release.
Release: "Examination results are released within 8 weeks of the exam. You will receive an email indicating your results are available. To view your results login to your NDEBConnect account and select 'results' from the main menu." Results are never given by phone, email or fax.
- Reported as Pass/Fail. The passing standard is an equated, rescaled 75. If you score under 75 you also get your score.
- Expected latest release: about 26 Apr 2027 (March sitting), 15 Jul 2027 (May), 17 Jan 2028 (November).

Score verification (fail only): NDEBConnect, then Service Requests, then New Service Request.
- Fee CAD 200, paid at submission, within 30 days of the score release.
- The outcome is emailed within about 6 weeks (2026 Policies). The 2025 service standard said no more than 4 weeks.
- "The Candidate's Examination score after verification is final". You never see the questions or your answer sheet.
- If the verification turns your result into a pass, any later registration is withdrawn and refunded.

Retakes:
- "The Virtual OSCE can be taken a maximum of three times." After 3 fails you automatically stop being a Certification Candidate (By-law 2 s.7.5) and would need the Equivalency Process.
- You must also pass within 60 months of graduation (s.7.1.2 and s.7.4).
- Register for the next sitting in its normal registration period.

2025 statistics: 1,576 candidates, 90.36% passed; first-time takers 1,313 of 1,441 passed.

## STEP 16: Move on to BCCOHP licensing and keep your NDEB account in order
TIMING: Right after certification; BC application must be within 3 years of the certificate date.
"The provincial Dental Regulatory Authorities (DRAs) are responsible for issuing dental licenses. Once certified, contact the DRA in the province you wish to practice." NDEB certification "does not automatically give" a licence, and the DRA may require more documents, jurisprudence tests and language proof.

BC: apply to BCCOHP within 3 years of the certificate's issue date. BCCOHP can see the National Register. Ask BCCOHP whether they also want an NDEB "Proof of certification" letter (CAD 30), ordered via NDEBConnect Service Requests.

Account housekeeping:
- After 24 months with no exam activity your NDEBConnect account goes inactive, and reactivation costs a fee (Policies s.5).
- Annual tax receipts are in the "miscellaneous" tab.
- Name changes need the Request for Name Change Form, sent by mail or courier.

## RISKS
1) Seats fill fast. March 2027 US/Canada first registration opened today (3 Nov 2026, 18:00 TRT). It closes when all locations are full, and you cannot register until approved. Realistically, March depends on second-period seats (about early-to-mid January 2027). Treat 20 May 2027 as the main target, and be approved before 26 Jan 2027, 18:00 TRT, when first registration opens.
2) A Turkish notary will probably be rejected. A standard noter statement and seal are in Turkish, and translated statements or seals are refused. Use the Canadian Embassy in Ankara or the Consulate in Istanbul, or a notary whose credentials are natively in English. You may not photocopy the passport yourself, and no other stamps may appear on the copy.
3) Name and signature mismatch. Use the exact passport spelling, including Turkish characters, everywhere: NDEBConnect, the Form, the transcript, and the ID shown at the exam. Sign exactly like your passport. A mismatch forces re-submission, and at the exam it can mean being turned away.
4) Transcript problems. An interim transcript, a missing conferral date, a transcript you send yourself, or an unsealed envelope will each be rejected. A diploma dated before Yeditepe's 1 Feb 2024 CODA recognition would make you ineligible for this route.
5) The university's education check is late. If Yeditepe does not answer NDEB's verification at least 15 days before the exam, you can be withdrawn and charged withdrawal fees.
6) Withdrawal costs. 50% (CAD 875) before the registration deadline and 100% (CAD 1,750) after it. No transfers between regions or between registration periods. Pick a region whose visa you can realistically get: Canada for you, not Ireland or Australia unless you also hold those visas.
7) Prometric scheduling is a separate step. Registering with NDEB for May or November does not book a Prometric seat. Schedule as soon as NDEB emails you, and read Prometric's rules.
8) Visa timing. NDEB issues no visa letters. Apply for the Canadian visitor visa as soon as you are registered; a late visa leads to a 100% fee loss if you withdraw after the deadline.
9) The certificate goes by regular mail to Türkiye and could be lost (a replacement costs CAD 75). Keep your address current. BCCOHP reads the National Register anyway, but check whether it also wants a CAD 30 proof-of-certification letter.
10) Time limits. Maximum 3 attempts. Pass within 60 months of graduation. Your account goes inactive after 24 months without exam activity. BC requires the certificate to be no more than 3 years old when you apply.
11) Misrepresentation (By-law 2 s.5, in force 1 Jul 2026). Bots, password sharing, sharing exam content or incorrect documents can void results and end eligibility. Appeals of administrative measures must be filed within 10 business days.
12) Conflicting official sources. The Form says proof of graduation is not needed before the exam, but the how-to-apply page lists the transcript for your category. Score-verification turnaround is 6 weeks in the 2026 Policies versus 4 weeks in the 2025 Service Standards. The FAQ elsewhere mentions an older USD-style figure; the fees page (CAD 1,750) is current. Plan for the stricter reading.
13) Time zones. NDEB times are Eastern Time. Openings are at 18:00 TRT in winter and 17:00 TRT in summer; deadlines at 21:00 ET are 04:00 or 05:00 TRT the NEXT day.
