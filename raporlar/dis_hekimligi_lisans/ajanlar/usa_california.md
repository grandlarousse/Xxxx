# California (USA) — ajan bulgusu

Düzenleyici: Dental Board of California (DBC), Department of Consumer Affairs — https://www.dbc.ca.gov/

**Teori-only rota:** no · **Göçmenlik engeli:** low · **Uzaktan sürdürme:** moderate · **Güven:** medium

## Sınav yolları

- **Licensure based on passing the ADEX Dental Examination (B&P Code §1632(c)(2))** (el becerisi: evet): DBC page (2026): applicant must submit proof of having passed, within 5 years preceding the application, ALL of these ADEX sections: 'Diagnostic Skills Examination / Dental Licensure Objective Structured Clinical Examination (OSCE) (computer based). Prosthodontic (manikin based). Endodontic (manikin based). Restorative – Anterior and Posterior (live patient or manikin based using CompeDont tooth). Periodontal (live patient, or manikin based using CompeDont tooth).' DBC note: 'The DLOSCE ... has replaced the DSE OSCE as of August 1, 2026. Candidates registered for the ADEX AFTER June 1, 2026, will now take the DLOSCE.' Plus INBDE (§1632(a)), California Law and Ethics Examination (§1632(b)), fingerprints, $400 application fee. This is the only exam route open to a new graduate; four of five components are manikin/patient hands-on.
- **Licensure based on passing the WREB examination (B&P Code §1632(c)(1))** (el becerisi: evet): ADA page: 'The WREB examination is no longer offered as of December 31, 2022. All required sections of the WREB examination must have been taken prior to this date for licensure eligibility in California.' Effectively closed for a 2024+ graduate.
- **Licensure by residency (B&P Code §1634.1)** (el becerisi: hayır): No clinical exam, but requires 'a clinically based advanced education program in general dentistry or an advanced education program in general practice residency that is, at minimum, one year in duration and is accredited by either the Commission on Dental Accreditation ... or a national accrediting body approved by the board', with a board-approved 'certification of clinical residency program completion' issued within two years before application. Requires a US residency (visa) so excluded by the candidate's constraints; no hands-on EXAM but a full year of clinical training.
- **Portfolio examination (former B&P §1632(c)(1); §1632.5, §1632.6; 16 CCR Form 33A-22P)** (el becerisi: evet): The portfolio was a live-patient competency examination completed inside a California (board-approved) dental school during the final year of the programme. The current text of §1632(c) as amended by SB 1453 (effective 1 Jan 2025) lists only WREB and ADEX; the portfolio provisions (§1632.5/1632.6 and the 16 CCR application regulations) remain on the books but the portfolio is only accessible to students of a California dental school, so it is not available to a Yeditepe graduate.
- **Licensure by credential (B&P Code §1635.5)** (el becerisi: hayır): For dentists already licensed in another US state with documented clinical practice (5,000 hours in the preceding 5 years, or completion of a residency, plus 50 CE units within 2 years, etc.). Not available to a new graduate with no other licence.

## Teori-only açıklaması
B&P Code §1632(c) (as amended by Stats. 2024, Ch. 483 (SB 1453), eff. 1 Jan 2025, quoted on the ADA page updated 7 Oct 2026): 'Except as otherwise provided in Section 1632.5, the board shall require each applicant to have taken and received a passing score on one of the following: (1) A clinical and written examination administered by the Western Regional Examining Board within five years prior to the date of their application ... (2) The clinical and written examination developed by the American Board of Dental Examiners, Inc., within five years prior to the date of their application for a license under this section.' §1632(e): 'The board may determine the testing format, as related to patients, for the examination provided pursuant to paragraph (2) of subdivision (c)' (i.e., manikin instead of patient, but still hands-on). The DBC application page requires the full ADEX series: OSCE (computer based) PLUS 'Prosthodontic (manikin based). Endodontic (manikin based). Restorative ... (live patient or manikin based using CompeDont tooth). Periodontal (live patient, or manikin based using CompeDont tooth).' The only non-exam alternative is §1634.1 (one-year CODA-accredited AEGD/GPR residency). California is not on the JCNDE list of states accepting the DLOSCE in fulfilment of the clinical exam, and no rule allows the OSCE/DLOSCE component alone. Therefore a computer-only route does not exist.

## DLOSCE notları
California never accepted a standalone DLOSCE; the statute names only 'the clinical and written examination developed by the American Board of Dental Examiners' (ADEX) or WREB. The DBC ADEX page states: 'NOTE: The DLOSCE (DENTAL LICENSURE OBJECTIVE STRUCTURED CLINICAL EXAM) has replaced the DSE OSCE as of August 1, 2026. Candidates registered for the ADEX AFTER June 1, 2026, will now take the DLOSCE.' So the ADEX-integrated DLOSCE is accepted only as the computer-based component of the five-part ADEX series; the four manikin/patient components remain mandatory. No published DBC policy addresses whether an earlier standalone (JCNDE) DLOSCE pass can substitute for the ADEX OSCE/DLOSCE component; the ADA/ADEX agreement (DLOSCE delivered 'in conjunction with ADEX') suggests ADEX reporting would be required. Treat as unclear and ask DBC/CDCA-WREB-CITA.

## Yüz yüze adımlar
(1) ADEX examination itself: in-person, hands-on at a US test site (unavoidable). (2) Fingerprinting: DBC fingerprint page: 'Live Scan is available only in California, for either residents or visitors'; otherwise 'Ink on Cards Method: Hard Cards ... Requires a $49 fingerprint fee ... If you cannot be fingerprinted in California, you may go to a law enforcement agency to have fingerprints made. The two (2) cards will be provided by the Board' — i.e., can be done abroad at a police agency with Board-supplied cards, but since April 2025 the Board must first approve the application and send the Live Scan form/cards; 'Reports on some cards are received within a month after submission. Others may take many months'. A licence is not issued until DOJ and FBI clearance is received. (3) California Law and Ethics Examination (B&P §1632(b)): separate application + $125 fee; board-developed exam administered through a testing vendor at test centres (exact format/locations not verified in this research; assume a short US visit may be needed). (4) No interview, no notarisation requirement found; application can be filed online via BreEZe or by mail; dean's certification letter and passport-style photo are submitted by the school/applicant. (5) §1628(c) requires evidence of financial responsibility/liability insurance for patient injuries during the examination (relevant to ADEX patient-based sections).

## Diğer başlangıç şartları
Age 18+ (§1628). INBDE/National Board written exam (§1632(a)). California Law and Ethics Examination developed and administered by the board, separate application and $125 fee, dean's certification is the only other prerequisite, passing result valid 2 years (§1632(b)). ADEX sections passed within 5 years before application. Dean's letter or 'Certification of Dean of Dental College Granting Degree' form. Colour passport-style photo. Classifiable fingerprints (Live Scan in CA or hard cards, $49) and DOJ/FBI clearance before issuance. No CPR/BLS requirement for initial licence found (BLS is a renewal CE requirement). No English test (CODA graduate). No liability insurance for the licence, only for exam patients (§1628(c)). Timeline: the board must notify within 30 days whether an application is complete and decide within 30 days after a complete application (16 CCR processing-time rule quoted on ADA page); fingerprint hard-card clearance can take months.

## İlk ücretler
ADEX application processing fee $400 (non-refundable); Law and Ethics Examination application $125 (non-refundable); fingerprint fee $49 for hard cards (Live Scan vendor fee instead, no $49 to Board); ADEX full series approx. $2,995 (known fact, CDCA-WREB-CITA); initial licence issuance fee not captured in this research (check 16 CCR §1021 fee schedule); first renewal active $680 incl. $15 CURES fee.

## Lisansı sürdürme

- **renewal_period:** Biennial (every two years); licences expire at the end of the two-year period; 30-day grace before delinquency fee.
- **renewal_fee:** Dentist License ACTIVE $680.00 (includes annual $15 CURES fee); INACTIVE $650.00; Reduced-fee ACTIVE $355 / INACTIVE $325 (only for dentists who have practised in California 20+ years); delinquency fee $325 (30 days after expiration).
- **ce_hours:** Active status: '50 hours of CE (including applicable mandatory courses) every two years' (DBC renewal page; B&P §1645; 16 CCR §1016). Mandatory courses each cycle: infection control, California Dental Practice Act, and certification in Basic Life Support. Inactive status: 'CE is not required.'
- **ce_online_ok:** Mostly but not entirely. The BLS requirement must be met by an AHA/ARC/ASHI/CERP/PACE-approved BLS course that includes 'a live, in-person skills practice session, a skills test and a written examination' (16 CCR §1016). Infection control and Dental Practice Act courses are available online. 16 CCR §1016 also caps certain non-live/self-study categories (the ADA page shows a '20% of total required units' cap for one category) - the exact maximum share of self-instruction CE was not verified in this research.
- **cpr_required:** Yes for ACTIVE renewal: BLS certification is a mandatory CE course and the course must include a live, in-person skills practice session and skills test (16 CCR §1016(b)(1)(C)). AHA or American Red Cross BLS courses qualify, and AHA BLS is available through international training centres outside the US (e.g., in Turkey). Not required for INACTIVE renewal.
- **practice_hours_required:** None. No minimum practice or currency hours to renew or keep an active licence.
- **address_or_residency_required:** No California address or residency requirement; licensee must keep an address of record with the Board (change-of-address notification). Out-of-state/out-of-country addresses are accepted.
- **insurance_required:** No mandatory professional liability insurance for holding the licence (only financial responsibility/insurance for patient injury during the licensing exam, §1628(c)).
- **inactive_status:** At each renewal the dentist chooses active or inactive status (DBC: 'When renewing a Dentist License, you must choose active or inactive status'). Inactive: fee $650 per two years, no CE, cannot practise or prescribe. No maximum duration found. Reduced-fee inactive ($325) only after 20 years of California practice. A retired-dentist CE concession (B&P §1645(c)) applies to retired dentists providing uncompensated care.
- **reactivation:** DBC renewal page: 'To change a Dentist License from inactive to active status, you must certify completion of the required continuing education courses' (i.e., the 50-hour active CE package incl. BLS, infection control, Dental Practice Act). No new clinical examination, refresher course or practice-hour requirement was found for inactive-to-active. Lapsed licences: renewable with delinquency fee; under the Dental Practice Act a licence not renewed within five years of expiration generally cannot be renewed and the dentist must reapply (not verified in this research - confirm B&P §1718/§1718.3).
- **remote_maintainability:** moderate
- **details:** Can be kept ACTIVE indefinitely from abroad with 50 CE hours per 2 years and $680, the only awkward part being an in-person BLS skills session (available via AHA international training centres) and possibly limits on self-study CE. Alternatively keep it INACTIVE for $650 per 2 years with zero CE and reactivate by completing the CE package - no exam. No practice hours, no address, no insurance requirements. Fees are high relative to other states.
