# Ohio (USA) — ajan bulgusu

Düzenleyici: Ohio State Dental Board (OSDB) — https://dental.ohio.gov

**Teori-only rota:** no · **Göçmenlik engeli:** high · **Uzaktan sürdürme:** easy · **Güven:** medium

## Sınav yolları

- **Regional/ADEX clinical examination (ADEX via CDCA-WREB-CITA, CRDTS, SRTA; statute also lists NERB, CDCA, CITA, WREB) - all components passed** (el becerisi: evet): ORC 4715.10(D)(1): 'Have taken an examination administered by any of the following regional testing agencies and received a passing score on the examination as determined by the administering agency: the central regional dental testing service, inc., northeast regional board of dental examiners, inc., the commission on dental competency assessments, the southern regional dental testing agency, inc., the council of interstate testing agencies, inc., or the western regional examining board'. OAC 4715-5-01.1(A)(5): 'Provides evidence of successfully passing all components based on a conjunctive scoring method of one of the following regional board examinations'. OAC 4715-5-03(A): 'each applicant ... must provide evidence of a passing score on each component of the regional examination as established by the agency for the component'; (B) if the agency sets no component passing score, each component must be passed at 75%. ADA summary (13 Oct 2026): 'ADEX (CDCA-WREB-CITA), CRDTS+SRTA (must pass all components)'. The board's own checklist item 3(a) asks for 'Regional Board Scores (Copy of Scores)' from 'ADEX (formerly CDCA-WREB-CITA)...'. All of these exams contain manikin/hand-skill components, so this route is hands-on.
- **CODA-accredited residency (PGY-1 / GPR / AEGD etc.) in lieu of clinical exam** (el becerisi: evet): ORC 4715.10(D)(3): 'Have completed a dental residency program accredited or approved by the commission on dental accreditation and administered by an accredited dental college or hospital.' OAC 4715-5-01.1(A)(7) requires attestation by the program director on a board form; board checklist item 3(b): 'Residency - Copy of Certificate of Completion (Diploma)'. Residency is clinical work in the US (and requires a visa), so not a theory-only option.
- **Examination administered by the State Dental Board itself** (el becerisi: evet): ORC 4715.10(D)(2): 'Have taken an examination administered by the state dental board and received a passing score as established by the board.' The board does not currently administer its own clinical exam (its application checklist offers only regional board scores or residency); this is a dormant statutory option, and any board-run exam would be a clinical exam, not a theory-only one.
- **Licensure by endorsement/credentials (ORC 4715.10(F) and Chapter 4796; OAC 4715-5-01.1(A)(6))** (el becerisi: evet): Requires an existing dental licence from another US state plus active practice: OAC 4715-5-01.1(A)(6) '...license in good standing from another state for five years and has actively engaged in the legal and reputable practice of dentistry ... for five years immediately preceding application'; the ORC 4796.03 route needs a substantially similar out-of-state licence held at least one year with active practice (board checklist: 'Licensed in another state, and actively practicing for at least one (1) of the five (5) years immediately preceding date of application (Time in a residency program is not accepted)'). Not available to a new graduate; the underlying out-of-state licence itself would have required a hands-on exam or residency.
- **DLOSCE-only / OSCE-only route** (el becerisi: hayır): NOT AVAILABLE in Ohio. No reference to the DLOSCE, any OSCE, or any non-regional computer-based clinical exam appears in ORC 4715.10, OAC 4715-5-01.1, OAC 4715-5-03, the ADA Ohio summary (updated 13 Oct 2026) or the board's dentist licensing page (grep for DLOSCE/OSCE returned nothing). Ohio is not among the states listed in the 2026 DLOSCE Candidate Guide. Listed here only to record that it was checked.

## Teori-only açıklaması
A new graduate of a CODA-accredited programme must satisfy ORC 4715.10(C) (age 18, accredited graduate, 'passed parts I and II of the examination given by the national board of dental examiners' [INBDE], written jurisprudence exam, fee) AND one of ORC 4715.10(D): '(1) Have taken an examination administered by any of the following regional testing agencies and received a passing score ... (2) Have taken an examination administered by the state dental board and received a passing score as established by the board; (3) Have completed a dental residency program accredited or approved by the commission on dental accreditation'. OAC 4715-5-01.1(A)(5) narrows (D)(1) to 'successfully passing all components based on a conjunctive scoring method of one of the following regional board examinations' and OAC 4715-5-03(A) requires 'evidence of a passing score on each component of the regional examination as established by the agency for the component'. Every regional/ADEX examination includes manikin hand-skill components, so a hands-on component (or a residency) is unavoidable. No rule or statute mentions the DLOSCE or any OSCE, and the ADA summary (13 Oct 2026) lists only 'ADEX (CDCA-WREB-CITA), CRDTS+SRTA (must pass all components) OR PGY-1 from a CODA-accredited program'.

## DLOSCE notları
Ohio's statute and rules are silent on the DLOSCE/OSCE: ORC 4715.10(D)(1) names only the regional agencies (CRDTS, NERB, CDCA, SRTA, CITA, WREB) and OAC 4715-5-01.1(A)(5)/4715-5-03(A) require passing EVERY component of the regional examination under conjunctive scoring. Consequently a standalone DLOSCE score (earlier administrations) is not accepted at all, and an ADEX-reported DLOSCE pass alone would not satisfy the rule because the manikin components of the ADEX series must also be passed. Ohio does not appear in the 2026 DLOSCE Candidate Guide list of accepting states; the ADA Ohio summary (13 Oct 2026) and the board's dentist page (read 2 Nov 2026) contain no DLOSCE language. No board policy on the ADEX-integrated DLOSCE was found.

PGY-1 kabul: True

Address: the application 'will require you to enter a mailing and a public address. A fingerprint-based BCI and FBI criminal records check (ORC 4776.02) is required - identity verification, not immigration verification.

## Yüz yüze adımlar
(1) Criminal records check: fingerprint-based Ohio BCI and FBI checks required for every initial licence and for reinstatement (ORC 4776.02; board checklist 'Criminal Records Check'). The board does not take fingerprints itself; in Ohio this is done electronically (WebCheck) at a vendor; the board's page refers to an out-of-state fingerprint-service option (about $46 covering both checks) which uses mailed fingerprint cards - whether a foreign national abroad can use the mailed-card route was not verified; the check must be completed shortly before applying (results from other purposes not accepted). (2) Jurisprudence exam: ORC 4715.10(C)(4) 'written jurisprudence examination'; the board states it is open-book on ORC/OAC 4715, with the materials posted on the dentist page and completed as part of the online eLicense application - no proctored in-person sitting identified. (3) Application 'verified by oath' (ORC 4715.10(B)) - handled by electronic attestation in eLicense; no notarisation is listed on the board's checklist. (4) No interview. (5) Proof of Hepatitis B immunity/immunisation is uploaded. (6) The clinical exam itself (ADEX/CRDTS/SRTA) is hands-on and must be taken in person in the US. Apart from fingerprinting and the clinical exam, the process is online; a single short US visit could cover exam + fingerprinting.

## Diğer başlangıç şartları
Age 18; graduate of a CODA-accredited dental college (transcript or certification letter/board form with school seal); INBDE/NBDE scores (JCNDE screenshot or release); regional board scores (all components passed) or CODA residency certificate; open-book online jurisprudence exam on ORC/OAC 4715 (OAC 4715-5-01.1(B)); proof of Hepatitis B immunity or immunisation; BCI + FBI fingerprint criminal records check (ORC 4776.02); background questions (personal statement if any criminal/disciplinary history); list of all health licences ever held with verifications; mailing/public (practice) address; employment history; good moral character (OAC 4715-5-01.1(A)(2)); SSN on the application (ORC 3123.50). No English-language test for accredited graduates (TOEFL applies only to unaccredited foreign graduates). No liability insurance requirement found. Paper applications are no longer accepted - everything goes through eLicense Ohio. Processing timelines are not published on the page read.

## İlk ücretler
Initial dentist licence application fee $454 (board page; ORC 4715.13 sets $267 in odd-numbered years and $454 in even-numbered years because the fee is prorated to the biennium - per search summary of the statute, not read directly). Plus fingerprint/background-check costs (board page cites about $46 for the out-of-state fingerprint service covering BCI+FBI; Ohio WebCheck vendors charge similar amounts). Plus the regional/ADEX exam fee paid to the testing agency (ADEX full series about $2,995) and INBDE fee. Payment by MasterCard/Visa in eLicense.

## Lisansı sürdürme

- **renewal_period:** Biennial - 'A license expires on the date that is two years from the date of issuance' (board page); renewal window opens 90 days before expiry; renewed online via eLicense Ohio.
- **renewal_fee:** $312 total ($272 registration + $40 automatically routed to the Ohio Department of Health dentist loan repayment fund per ORC 4715.14(E)); late fee $127 after expiration.
- **ce_hours:** 30 hours per two-year registration period (ORC 4715.141(A): 'a minimum of thirty (30) hours of continuing dental education'); recent graduates may be exempted in their first renewal period (OAC 4715-8-04(C)(1)(a)). Category caps apply (max 5 h practice-management, 3 h papers, 3 h teaching, 2 h posters, up to 1/3 volunteer) but 'Dental education and scientific courses' has no maximum.
- **ce_online_ok:** Yes. OAC 4715-8-01(A) accepts 'Supervised self-instruction' including '(c) Graded internet, online, or webinar computer courses' (needs a graded test with >=75%) as well as live 'directly interactive' formats (which can also be live webinars); no cap on self-instruction hours is stated in the rule as read. Courses must come from board-approved sponsors, which include ADA CERP and AGD PACE providers, accredited universities and dental associations (OAC 4715-8-02), so all 30 hours can be done online from abroad. No specific mandatory topic (e.g. opioid/infection control) was seen in the text read, but this was not exhaustively checked.
- **cpr_required:** Not found for a general dentist licence. ORC 4715.251 (read) imposes current BLS certification (ARC/AHA/ASHI course) at each renewal only on dental hygienists; BLS-HCP is required for holders of anesthesia/sedation permits (board page). No dentist-specific CPR renewal statute or rule was identified - treat as 'apparently not required for a plain dentist licence, unverified'.
- **practice_hours_required:** None. No minimum practice or currency hours appear in the renewal statute/rules or on the board's renewal page.
- **address_or_residency_required:** No in-state residency. The application asks for a mailing and a public address ('should be your main practice address'); licensees must report address changes within 10 days (ORC 4715.24(C) for hygienists, 'whether a resident or not'; dentist parallel assumed in ORC 4715.14). Whether a non-US address is accepted in eLicense is unverified.
- **insurance_required:** No mandatory professional liability insurance requirement found in ORC/OAC 4715 or on the board's licensing page.
- **inactive_status:** An inactive/retired category exists: the board's dentist page lists a reinstatement application for 'Inactive/Retired Status' ($312) distinct from 'Inactive/Expired Status' ($693). The mechanics of electing inactive/retired status (fee while inactive, any CE while inactive, maximum duration) were not on the section read and remain unverified (the hygienist analogue, ORC 4715.241 'temporarily retired', has no time limit).
- **reactivation:** Reinstatement (from inactive/retired or from expired/lapsed) is by online application with: proof of at least 30 hours of continuing dental education during the two-year period immediately preceding reinstatement; the open-book jurisprudence exam; proof of Hepatitis B immunity/immunisation; a new BCI + FBI criminal records check; fee $312 (inactive/retired) or $693 (inactive/expired). No clinical re-examination, refresher course or practice-hour requirement is listed, and no maximum lapse period is stated on the page read. An expired licence is automatically suspended for non-renewal (standard renewal procedure ORC 4745.01-.03) until reinstated.
- **remote_maintainability:** easy
- **details:** Once licensed, the licence can be kept active from abroad with online renewal every two years ($312), 30 hours of CE obtainable entirely through graded online/webinar courses from ADA CERP/AGD PACE sponsors, no practice hours, no Ohio residency, no insurance, and (for a plain dentist licence) no identified CPR skills session. Caveats: ORC 3123.50 requires the SSN on every renewal application as well; CE records must be kept 4 years for audit (OAC 4715-8-04); an 'inactive/retired' category exists with cheap reinstatement ($312 + 30 CE hours + jurisprudence + fingerprint check) but its entry conditions were not verified. Reinstatement requires a fresh fingerprint background check, which may need a US visit or mailed cards.

## Açık sorular
1) SSN: ORC 3123.50 is categorical, but it is unknown whether the eLicense Ohio dental application allows an applicant to proceed without an SSN (some Ohio boards accept a no-SSN attestation) - ask licensing@den.ohio.gov. 2) The board page sections on 'Criminal Records Check' and 'Jurisprudence Exam' were not read in full: confirm the mailed fingerprint-card option for applicants abroad and the exact exam format (the search summary of the official page says open-book). 3) Whether eLicense accepts a non-US 'main practice address'. 4) Entry conditions, fee and CE obligations of the dentist 'inactive/retired' status, and any maximum lapse period before reinstatement needs more than 30 CE hours (ORC 4715.14 / OAC on dentist retirement not read). 5) Whether any CPR/BLS requirement applies to general dentists at renewal (none found; only hygienists and anesthesia-permit holders identified). 6) Whether the board has or will adopt any policy on the ADEX-integrated DLOSCE - nothing published as of 13 Oct 2026 (ADA) / 2 Nov 2026 (board page). 7) Processing timelines are not published. 8) Exact ORC 4715.13 fee schedule ($267 odd year / $454 even year) was taken from a search summary, not read directly.
