# Massachusetts (USA) — ajan bulgusu

Düzenleyici: Massachusetts Board of Registration in Dentistry (Department of Public Health, Bureau of Health Professions Licensure) — https://www.mass.gov/orgs/board-of-registration-in-dentistry

**Teori-only rota:** no · **Göçmenlik engeli:** high · **Uzaktan sürdürme:** moderate · **Güven:** medium

## Sınav yolları

- **Licensure by examination (M.G.L. c.112 s.45; 234 CMR 4.03) - INBDE/NBDE + Board-approved regional/state clinical exam + MA Dental Ethics & Jurisprudence Exam** (el becerisi: evet): 234 CMR 4.03(5) requires 'Documentation of a passing score on each of the following exams: (a) Parts I and II of the ADA National Board Examination; (b) The CDCA or other state or regional examination approved by the Board; and (c) Massachusetts Dental Ethics and Jurisprudence Examination'. The Board's 'Regional and State Clinical Exam Requirements' list (as reproduced on the ADA Massachusetts page updated 9 Oct 2026) accepts: ADEX as administered by CDCA-WREB-CITA; CRDTS (if taken before 1 July 2009, otherwise CDCA DSE + CRDTS); SRTA (only 1 Jan 2013-9 Aug 2015, otherwise CDCA DSE + SRTA). 'Successful completion of patient-based periodontal examination is required for all exams; minimum score of 75% in each section'. So a live-patient periodontal component plus manikin components are unavoidable. DLOSCE is not on the Board's list. Other requirements: physician's health statement within 1 year, current BLS or CPR/AED certification, passport photo, disciplinary disclosure, good-moral-character proof (two licensed-dentist signatures per Board page), tax/child-support attestation (c.62C s.49A, c.119A s.16), c.94C s.18(e) prescriber training, MassHealth ORP (ordering/referring, non-billing) enrollment attestation (M.G.L. c.112 s.45).
- **Licensure by credentials (M.G.L. c.112 s.48; 234 CMR 4.04)** (el becerisi: hayır): Only for a dentist 'lawfully in practice for at least five years in another state'. 234 CMR 4.04 requires five years of practice (private practice, residency programs, etc. may count), transcript from a CODA school, letters of standing, BLS/CPR, the MA jurisprudence exam, etc.; no new clinical exam. Not available to a new graduate; the underlying out-of-state licence would itself have required a hands-on exam.
- **Limited registration (dental intern / limited dental faculty, M.G.L. c.112 s.45A)** (el becerisi: hayır): Not a full licence: restricted to practice within a Board-approved residency/advanced education programme or faculty appointment, renewable annually with 20 CEUs/year. Requires enrolment/employment in Massachusetts (and therefore a visa). The statutory alien clause of s.45 expressly does not apply to limited registration under s.45A. Not a route to unrestricted licensure by itself.
- **DLOSCE-only / OSCE-only route** (el becerisi: hayır): NOT available. The Board's approved clinical examination list contains only ADEX (CDCA-WREB-CITA), CRDTS and SRTA combinations, and states that a patient-based periodontal examination is required for all exams. The DLOSCE is not listed and no Massachusetts rule or Board notice accepting the DLOSCE (standalone or as the ADEX-integrated component) alone was found.
- **PGY-1 residency in lieu of clinical exam** (el becerisi: hayır): NOT available. Neither M.G.L. c.112 s.45 nor 234 CMR 4.03 provides a residency substitute for the clinical examination; a residency only counts toward the 5-year practice requirement for licensure by credentials (234 CMR 4.04).

## Teori-only açıklaması
234 CMR 4.03(5): 'Documentation of a passing score on each of the following exams: (a) Parts I and II of the ADA National Board Examination; (b) The CDCA or other state or regional examination approved by the Board; and (c) Massachusetts Dental Ethics and Jurisprudence Examination or a Board-approved successor examination.' The Board's Regional and State Clinical Exam Requirements (quoted on the ADA Massachusetts Laws & Rules page, updated 9 October 2026): 'Successful completion of patient-based periodontal examination is required for all exams; minimum score of 75% in each section of the exam: ADEX as administered by CDCA-WREB-CITA; CRDTS if taken prior to July 1, 2009; SRTA if taken between Jan. 1, 2013 and Aug. 9, 2015; CDCA DSE and CRDTS if taken on or after July 1, 2009; CDCA DSE and SRTA if taken prior to Jan. 1, 2013 or after Aug. 9, 2015.' The only alternative, licensure by credentials under M.G.L. c.112 s.48 / 234 CMR 4.04, requires five years of lawful practice in another state. A live-patient periodontal exam plus manikin ADEX components are therefore unavoidable for a new graduate.

## DLOSCE notları
Massachusetts does not list the DLOSCE among accepted examinations (it is not in the 2026 DLOSCE Candidate Guide's list of accepting states, and the Board's own exam list names only ADEX/CDCA, CRDTS and SRTA with a mandatory patient-based periodontal section). Nothing in 234 CMR 4.03 or on the Board's exam-requirements document addresses the DLOSCE, standalone or ADEX-integrated; an ADEX-reported DLOSCE pass would simply be one component of the full ADEX series, which Massachusetts requires in full (minimum 75% in each section). Note that under 234 CMR 8.05(1)(c) completion of the 'CDCA Diagnosis, Oral Medicine, Radiology and Comprehensive Treatment Planning examination ... or other Board approved clinical competency examination' earns CE credit, but that is for renewal only, not initial licensure.

- **details:** Statutory alien clause still on the books and still reproduced in the ADA's 9 Oct 2026 summary: M.G.L. c.112 s.45: 'The board shall examine an applicant who is an alien only if he presents to it a certificate from the court in which he shall have filed his declaration of intention to become a citizen of the United States, or from the Immigration and Naturalization Service of the United States, showing that he has declared his intention to become such a citizen, or a copy of such declaration of intention, certified by the clerk of such court. In case the applicant is subsequently registered, unless he shall present to the board, within five years following the filing of the certificate or certified copy hereinbefore referred to, his completed naturalization papers showing that he is a citizen of the United States his certificate of registration shall be revoked and his registration cancelled. The foregoing provisions of this paragraph shall not apply to limited registration under [s.45A].' A 'declaration of intention' (USCIS Form N-300) can only be filed by a lawful permanent resident, so on its face the statute bars a Turkish visitor with no US status from even sitting the Board's examination, and would revoke a licence not followed by naturalisation within five years. Alienage conditions of this kind have been held unconstitutional for professional licences (In re Griffiths, 413 U.S. 717 (1973)), and the Board's current online checklist (per search snippets; page not readable) does not list citizenship or a declaration of intention among required documents, so actual enforcement is UNVERIFIED. No separate Massachusetts statewide SAVE/lawful-presence statute for professional licences was identified. Work authorisation is not a stated requirement, but mandatory MassHealth ordering/referring-provider enrollment (M.G.L. c.112 s.45; 234 CMR 4.14(1)) as a condition of initial licensure AND every renewal is a practical identity/tax-ID hurdle for a non-resident. No Massachusetts address or residency requirement was found in c.112 or 234 CMR 4.00.

## Yüz yüze adımlar
(1) Clinical examination: ADEX (CDCA-WREB-CITA) with a mandatory patient-based periodontal section must be taken at a US test site; this requires travel and (for the patient-based section) a patient. (2) Application must be 'signed and sworn to' (M.G.L. c.112 s.45) and the 2017 regulation required a notarised affidavit; a CORI (criminal record) acknowledgment form must be signed in person, witnessed either by a Board/BHPL employee at the Board's Boston office or by a notary who verifies identity (per the Board's application checklist as summarised in search results; page itself not readable). No fingerprinting/Live Scan requirement was found. (3) Massachusetts Dental Ethics and Jurisprudence Examination: requested from dentistry.admin@mass.gov and sent by email, answered on an answer sheet (take-home style, as described for the Board's other licence types) - can be done from abroad. (4) Physician's statement made after an examination within one year of application - can be done abroad. (5) MassHealth ORP (non-billing ordering/referring provider) enrollment is mandatory before applying and at each renewal (vendor DentaQuest from 1 Feb 2026) - online but normally needs an NPI and a US tax identifier. (6) Diploma/transcript originals with college seal (photocopies not accepted) must be sent. Overall: everything except the ADEX clinical exam and the notarised/witnessed forms can be done from abroad; the notary step could be done during the exam visit or at a US consulate.

## Diğer başlangıç şartları
Jurisprudence: Massachusetts Dental Ethics and Jurisprudence Examination (emailed by the Board; open-book answer-sheet format, not a proctored on-site exam as far as the Board's pages indicate). CPR/BLS: 'Documentation of current BLS certification or CPR/AED certification' (234 CMR 4.03(6)). Background: good-moral-character proof (two licensed-dentist signatures on the application), disclosure statement of all disciplinary/civil/criminal actions, CORI acknowledgment form (in-person/notary witnessed). Health: physician's statement within one year. Prescriber training: proof of completion of training required by M.G.L. c.94C s.18(e) (234 CMR 4.03(11)). MassHealth: must apply to participate as an ordering/referring non-billing provider before initial licensure and each renewal (M.G.L. c.112 s.45). Tax/child-support attestation under penalties of perjury (234 CMR 4.03(10)). No English-language test for CODA graduates. No liability insurance requirement found. Timeline: Board page states applications remain open only one year from the initial application date; failing applicants are entitled to two re-examinations within three years (c.112 s.45).

## Lisansı sürdürme

- **renewal_period:** Biennial; dentists renew 'no later than March 31st of even-numbered years' (234 CMR 4.14(1)).
- **renewal_fee:** Set by the Executive Office of Administration and Finance; amount not verified (mass.gov pages unreadable). Late renewal fee plus all intervening cycle fees apply after expiry (234 CMR 4.14(8)-(9)).
- **ce_hours:** 40 CEUs during the 24 months immediately preceding March 31 of even-numbered years (234 CMR 8.02(1)); must include CDC Guidelines/Infection Control and 'continuous certification in CPR/AED, or BLS' (234 CMR 8.03(1)); c.94C s.18(e) opioid/prescriber training only if holding an MCSR/DEA registration or otherwise prescribing. New graduates are exempt for the licensing period in which they graduate (8.02(6)). CEUs cannot be carried over (8.02(9)).
- **ce_online_ok:** Partly. 234 CMR 8.05(2) 'Self-instruction. A dentist may earn a maximum of 20 CEUs ... per renewal cycle by completing an individual study course (home study, on-line, correspondence, audio or video). All such courses must include a test'. The remaining 20 CEUs must come from 8.05(1) 'Educational and scientific courses sponsored or approved by' accredited institutions, professional associations/societies, accredited postdoctoral programs, government health agencies or teaching hospitals, or from teaching/research/publication categories (also capped). The rule does not state that 8.05(1) courses must be in-person; whether live interactive webinars count as 'courses' rather than 'self-instruction' is not stated in the regulation and was not verifiable on the Board's site. Expect at least some live (possibly live-online) component.
- **cpr_required:** Yes: 'continuous certification in CPR/AED, or BLS' is a required CE element at every renewal (234 CMR 8.03(1)(b)) and current BLS/CPR-AED certification is required at initial licensure (4.03(6)). Whether an online-only (no skills session) course is accepted is not addressed in the regulation; the Board's CE guidance could not be read. Assume a hands-on skills component is needed (AHA/Red Cross standard).
- **practice_hours_required:** None for routine renewal. However, 234 CMR 4.14(9)(c) (renewal of a licence expired more than two cycles): 'The Board may, in its discretion, require a licensee to be re-examined for competency when a dentist has not practiced dentistry in the Commonwealth within five years from the date of initial licensure, or if a dentist does not maintain a current license for a period of five consecutive years.' This discretionary re-examination risk arises only on reinstatement of a long-expired licence, not on timely renewal.
- **address_or_residency_required:** No Massachusetts address or residency requirement found in M.G.L. c.112 or 234 CMR 4.00/8.00. Renewal does require an attestation under penalties of perjury that the dentist 'participates in, or had applied to participate in, MassHealth' as a provider or as a non-billing ordering/referring provider (234 CMR 4.14(1)), which must be maintained from abroad.
- **insurance_required:** No mandatory professional liability insurance requirement found in c.112 or 234 CMR.
- **inactive_status:** Not verified. 234 CMR 4.00 (as reproduced by the ADA) contains no 'inactive' licence category for dentists; Board minutes (May 2023) refer to a 'retired' licence status that can be rescinded by Board vote on petition, and the Board publishes a 'License Reactivation Guide' (mass.gov/info-details/board-of-registration-in-dentistry-license-reactivation-guide, unreadable - HTTP 403). Fee, CE and time limits for retired status are unknown. Simply letting the licence expire is the de facto alternative (see reactivation).
- **reactivation:** Expired less than two renewal cycles (234 CMR 4.14(8)): file renewal application, pay renewal fee for each intervening cycle plus late fee, submit CE certificates for each missed cycle, and an explanation for the failure to renew. Expired more than two cycles (4.14(9)): same, plus the Board may require additional documentation, may 'require a licensee to be re-examined for competency' if the dentist has not practised in the Commonwealth within five years of initial licensure or has not maintained a current licence for five consecutive years, and may impose 'such other conditions as the Board may require'; the Board may in its discretion decline to renew/reactivate (4.14(10)). Reinstatement of CE: CEUs equal to the number of cycles expired (8.02(11)). Reactivation from 'retired' status requires a Board petition (details unverified).
- **remote_maintainability:** moderate
- **details:** Keeping the licence active from Turkey is feasible on paper: biennial fee, 40 CEUs (max 20 pure self-study/online; the rest from sponsored courses, which may or may not accept live webinars), continuous CPR/BLS (likely needs a hands-on skills session), MassHealth ORP enrollment attestation each renewal, and no practice-hour, address or insurance requirement. Awkward elements: the 20 non-self-study CEUs, hands-on CPR, and MassHealth enrollment maintenance. The main structural risk is the statutory alien clause (licence 'shall be revoked' if naturalisation papers are not presented within five years of the declaration of intention) if the Board enforces it, and the discretionary competency re-examination for a dentist who never practises in Massachusetts if the licence ever lapses more than two cycles. Renewal cycle: 31 March of even-numbered years.

## Açık sorular
1. Does the Board actually enforce the M.G.L. c.112 s.45 alien clause (declaration of intention to naturalise; revocation if not naturalised within five years)? The clause is almost certainly unconstitutional under In re Griffiths but remains in the statute and in the ADA's Oct 2026 summary; written confirmation from dentistry.admin@mass.gov is needed. 2. Does the dentist application require an SSN, and is an ITIN or 'no SSN' affidavit accepted (mass.gov blocked all page/PDF fetches)? 3. Can mandatory MassHealth ORP enrollment (prerequisite to licensure and every renewal) be completed by a non-resident without SSN/US tax ID? 4. Current initial and renewal fee amounts for dentists. 5. Whether live interactive webinars count as 8.05(1) courses (so that all 40 CEUs could be earned online) and whether online-only CPR/BLS is accepted. 6. Details of the 'retired' licence status (eligibility, fee, CE, reactivation petition) and the Board's reactivation guide. 7. Whether the CORI acknowledgment/notarised affidavit is required of every applicant or only those answering 'yes' to moral-character questions, and whether a foreign notary or US consular notarisation is acceptable.
