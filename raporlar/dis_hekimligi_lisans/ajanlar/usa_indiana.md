# Indiana (USA) — ajan bulgusu

Düzenleyici: Indiana State Board of Dentistry (administered by the Indiana Professional Licensing Agency, IPLA) — https://www.in.gov/pla/professions/dentistry-home/

**Teori-only rota:** no · **Göçmenlik engeli:** high · **Uzaktan sürdürme:** moderate · **Güven:** high

## Sınav yolları

- **Licensure by examination: INBDE + ADEX Dental Examination (CDCA-WREB-CITA)** (el becerisi: evet): IC 25-14-1-3(a): 'Every applicant must pass an examination administered by an entity approved by the board and may not take the examination more than three (3) times.' 828 IAC 1-1-3 requires (1) all sections of the national board exam, (2) a clinical examination, (3) the Indiana law exam. IPLA 'Dentist by Examination' instructions (read 2 Nov 2026): 'An applicant must pass all parts of one (1) of the following examinations ... Commission on Dental Competency Assessment (CDCA), North East Regional Board (NERB), Western Regional Examining Board (WREB), and Council of Interstate Testing Agency (CITA) Website: cdcaexams.org [now adextesting.org]'. Clinical exam must have been passed within the 5 years before the board receives the application. ADEX includes manikin-based hands-on sections (the DLOSCE is only its computer-based component). Board must receive official score reports directly from the agency.
- **Licensure by examination: INBDE + CRDTS (SRTA has merged into CRDTS)** (el becerisi: evet): Same statutory basis; IPLA lists 'Central Regional Dental Testing Service Examination (CRDTS) www.crdts.org' and 'Southern Regional Testing Agency (SRTA) has merged with CRDTS'. 828 IAC 1-1-7(1) (readopted 2019) lists CRDTS, NERB, SRTA, WREB. Hands-on manikin/patient components. 2026 board minutes show the board routinely licensing applicants with ADEX, CDCA or CRDTS and denying an applicant under the three-attempt limit (Feb 2026 minutes).
- **Board-administered examination (828 IAC 1-1-7(2))** (el becerisi: evet): 828 IAC 1-1-7(2): 'Have taken an examination administered by the board and received a passing score as established by the board.' Theoretical only: the board does not currently administer its own clinical exam; IPLA instructions list only CDCA/ADEX and CRDTS/SRTA.
- **DLOSCE-only (computer OSCE) - NOT in Indiana's rules or application instructions; standalone DLOSCE discontinued Oct 2026** (el becerisi: hayır): The JCNDE 2026 DLOSCE Candidate Guide (updated 4/2026) lists 'Indiana' (no asterisk, i.e. full fulfilment) among states accepting the DLOSCE 'in full or partial fulfillment of clinical examination requirements ... Please refer to each state board's website for specific details'. However, NO Indiana official source confirms this: 828 IAC 1-1-7 lists only CRDTS/NERB/SRTA/WREB or a board exam; the IPLA Dentist-by-Examination checklist and 'Exams & Testing' section (read 2 Nov 2026) list only CDCA/ADEX and CRDTS/SRTA; State Form 42127 (R15/3-25) exam checkboxes are NBDE, State Constructed Exam, CRDTS, CDCA, SRTA, NERB, WREB, Canadian Provincial Clinical Licensing Exam; the ADA 'Indiana Laws & Rules' page (updated 8 Oct 2026) lists only ADEX and CRDTS+SRTA. Since standalone DLOSCE administrations ended (JCNDE: results via DENTPIN only for exams taken on or before 25 Sept 2026; now 'All administrations are conducted through ADEX'), this is not a usable pathway.
- **Licensure by endorsement / credentials (IC 25-14-1-16; 828 IAC 1-3-1.1)** (el becerisi: evet): Not available to a new graduate: requires a licence in another state or Canadian province with substantially equal requirements, 'active practice of dentistry for not less than two (2) years out of the three (3) years immediately preceding', 20 CE hours in the prior 2 years, proof of NBDE or 'National Dental Examining Board of Canada Written Examination', and proof the applicant 'satisfactorily completed a national, regional, state, or provincial clinical licensing examination in any other state or Canadian province', three letters from practising dentists, BLS/ACLS, Indiana law exam, possible board appearance (IC 25-14-1-16(d)). Also requires US SSN (828 IAC 1-3-1.1(c)) and the IC 12-32-1 citizenship/work-authorisation affidavit.
- **PGY-1 / residency in lieu of clinical exam** (el becerisi: evet): Not provided for. Neither IC 25-14-1-3 nor 828 IAC 1-1-7 allows a residency to substitute for the clinical examination. A 'Dental Residency Permit' exists only for persons enrolled in an Indiana University School of Dentistry residency and is a permit, not a licence.

## Teori-only açıklaması
828 IAC 1-1-3: 'In order to obtain an Indiana license to practice dentistry, each applicant must pass an examination that includes the following: (1) All sections of the national dental board examination. (2) A clinical examination. (3) A written examination covering Indiana law...'. 828 IAC 1-1-7: 'To be eligible for licensure by examination, an applicant must meet any one (1) of the following clinical examination requirements: (1) Have passed all parts of one (1) of the following examinations within the five (5) year period immediately before the date of the board's receipt of the applicant's application: (A) The Central Regional Dental Testing Service (CRDTS) examination. (B) The North East Regional Board (NERB) examination. (C) The Southern Regional Testing Agency (SRTA) examination. (D) The Western Regional Examining Board (WREB) examination. (2) Have taken an examination administered by the board and received a passing score as established by the board.' IPLA application instructions (2 Nov 2026) implement this as CDCA/ADEX or CRDTS/SRTA only. All of these are hands-on regional exams. The only suggestion of a computer-only route is JCNDE's own listing of Indiana as a DLOSCE-accepting state, which is contradicted by every Indiana official source and by the ADA's Oct 2026 Indiana page, and standalone DLOSCE is no longer offered. Therefore a new graduate cannot be licensed in Indiana without a hands-on clinical examination.

## DLOSCE notları
JCNDE 2026 DLOSCE Candidate Guide lists Indiana (without the 'partial' asterisk) as accepting the DLOSCE, but the Indiana board's rule (828 IAC 1-1-7), the IPLA Dentist-by-Examination checklist, the 'Exams & Testing' page and State Form 42127 (R15/3-25) make no mention of the DLOSCE; they list CDCA/ADEX, CRDTS and SRTA only. The ADA 'Indiana Laws & Rules' summary (updated 8 Oct 2026) likewise lists only ADEX (CDCA-WREB-CITA) and CRDTS+SRTA. No board minutes available online (2026) mention the DLOSCE. The JCNDE page now states 'All administrations are conducted through ADEX' and the 2026 guide 'is for historical reference purposes only'; the last standalone DLOSCE window (retake-only) ran 21 Apr-26 Sept 2026. Indiana publishes nothing about how DLOSCE scores earned inside the ADEX Dental Examination are treated; in practice the board accepts the ADEX Dental Examination as a whole (which includes the DLOSCE as its computer-based component plus manikin sections). Whether an earlier standalone DLOSCE score alone would be accepted is unverified and, given the rule text, unlikely; applicants should confirm with the board (pla8@pla.in.gov, 317-234-2054).

- **citizenship_or_pr_required:** False
- **work_authorization_required:** True
- **lawful_presence_verification:** True
- **ssn_or_sin_required:** True
- **itin_or_no_ssn_alternative:** None. IPLA Dentistry Licensing Information page: 'Mandatory Disclosure of U.S. Social Security Number: Your social security number is being requested by this state agency in accordance with Ind. Code 4-1-8-1 and 25-1-5-11(a). Disclosure is mandatory, and this record cannot be processed without it. Failure to disclose your U.S. social security number will result in the denial of your application.' 828 IAC 1-1-2(d): 'All applicants must submit the applicant's United States Social Security number in order to be eligible for licensure.' IC 25-1-5-11(e): 'An applicant or a licensee shall provide the applicant's or licensee's Social Security number to the agency.' IC 4-1-8-1(a)(10) expressly exempts the 'Indiana professional licensing agency' from the general prohibition on compelling SSNs. No ITIN option, no 'no-SSN' affidavit was found on the IPLA site or State Form 42127 (the SSN field is marked mandatory).
- **in_jurisdiction_address_required:** False
- **barrier_level:** high
- **details:** Indiana's statewide public-benefits law applies to professional licences. IC 12-32-1-3: 'state or local public benefit' has the meaning in 8 U.S.C. 1621 (which includes 'any ... professional license'). IC 12-32-1-5(a): an agency 'shall verify, in the manner required under section 6 of this chapter, the eligibility of any individual who (1) is at least eighteen (18) years of age; and (2) applies for state or local public benefits'. IC 12-32-1-6: the agency shall 'Require the individual to execute a verification stating under penalty of perjury that the individual: (A) is a United States citizen; (B) is a qualified alien (as defined under 8 U.S.C. 1641); or (C) ... is authorized by the federal government to work in the United States as provided under section 6.5 ... executing the verification only for the purpose of applying for a professional or occupational license'. IC 12-32-1-6.5(c) (added 2018): 'An individual who is authorized by the federal government to work in the United States is eligible for a professional or occupational license ... if the individual meets all the requirements, other than the requirement under 8 USC 1621(a), to obtain or renew the professional or occupational license.' IC 12-32-1-7: a knowingly false verification is a Level 6 felony. The board's application, State Form 42127 (R15 / 3-25), implements this verbatim: 'Pursuant to IC 12-32-1-5 and IC 12-32-1-6, I swear under the penalty of perjury that: (Please select ONLY ONE of the following.) [ ] I am a United States Citizen. [ ] I am a qualified alien (as defined under 8 USC 1641). [ ] I am authorized by the Federal government to work in the United States.' A Turkish citizen entering on a B-1/B-2 visitor visa is none of these three (a visitor is a nonimmigrant, not a 'qualified alien', and has no federal work authorisation), so the verification cannot truthfully be executed and the application cannot be completed. Verification is by sworn affidavit on the application; no SAVE query or copy of visa/I-94 is described in the statute sections or IPLA instructions read. Separately, a US SSN is mandatory with no alternative (see itin field). No Indiana residency or in-state address is required (form asks for 'Address of applicant' with city/state/ZIP; IPLA mails renewal notices to the address of record), and the licence 'shall be properly displayed at all times in the office of the person named as the holder' only matters if practising (IC 25-14-1-10(b)). Citizenship/PR as such is not required (work-authorised non-citizens such as EAD holders qualify), but for this candidate the combined SSN + IC 12-32-1 affidavit requirements are an absolute bar.

## Yüz yüze adımlar
(1) Fingerprint-based national criminal history background check (IC 25-1-1.1-4(c): applicant 'shall submit to a national criminal history background check at the cost of the individual'). Done through IdentoGO/IDEMIA with the Dentist service code 24YBGH, and only AFTER IPLA emails confirmation that the application was received ('CBCs conducted prior to the application received date ... will not be considered'). In-state: Live Scan at an Indiana IdentoGO site. Out of state: the IPLA 'Out of State Card Scan Processing' programme lets applicants 'that reside outside the State of Indiana' register online, select 'Pay for Ink Card Submission', and mail an FD-258 hard card (ink-rolled or electronically captured and printed, taken by 'a local law enforcement agency or other entity that provides fingerprinting services') with the confirmation page to IdentoGO Cardscan Dept, Brentwood TN; results go to Indiana State Police/FBI then IPLA. Nothing in the flyer prohibits cards taken outside the US, so this can plausibly be done from abroad, but IPLA's flyer is written for US out-of-state residents (unverified for foreign residents). CBC is not required for renewal or reinstatement. (2) Jurisprudence exam: 'the applicant will be sent a jurisprudence exam by email ... fourteen (14) days ... to complete the examination and return the required information'; 30 true-false/multiple-choice questions, 75% pass (828 IAC 1-1-6(b)); fully remote. (3) No interview normally; the board may request an appearance (IC 25-14-1-16(d) for endorsement; reinstatement/inactive reactivation 'you may be scheduled to appear before the Board'; board meetings are also held via Zoom). (4) Notarisation: only for name-change documents and criminal-conviction statements (828 IAC 1-1-2(c)); the application itself is submitted online under penalty of perjury, no notary. (5) Hands-on steps unavoidable: ADEX or CRDTS clinical exam at a US testing site, and an AHA/Red Cross CPR/BLS course completed within the prior year (828 IAC 1-1-1(2)) with a current BLS/ACLS card uploaded. (6) Official transcripts, dean/registrar Certificate of Completion (State Form 'Certificate of Completion'), and INBDE and clinical scores must be sent directly to the board by the issuing bodies.

## Diğer başlangıç şartları
Graduation from a CODA-accredited dental school (828 IAC 1-1-1(1); IC 25-14-1-3(a)); official transcript showing degree conferral date plus Certificate of Completion signed by dean and registrar (828 IAC 1-1-2(b)); INBDE/NBDE passing scores sent directly from JCNDE; clinical exam (ADEX or CRDTS) passed within 5 years, maximum three attempts (IC 25-14-1-3(a)); CPR: 'certification of having completed, within the prior year, an American Red Cross or American Heart Association cardiopulmonary resuscitation course' (828 IAC 1-1-1(2)) - IPLA asks for a current BLS or ACLS card; Indiana jurisprudence exam by email, 75% pass; fingerprint national criminal background check after application receipt; two passport photos per rule (not on the current online checklist); verification of any other health licences sent directly by the issuing board (State Form 7143, which itself requires SSN) and NPDB self-query if previously licensed elsewhere; US SSN mandatory; IC 12-32-1 citizenship/qualified-alien/work-authorisation affidavit; positive-response documentation for any arrests/discipline; English test (TOEFL) is required only for graduates of unaccredited (foreign non-CODA) schools, not for CODA graduates. Application abandoned if all requirements are not submitted within one year of filing. No liability insurance requirement found.

## İlk ücretler
Application for dental licence: $250.00 (828 IAC 0.5-2-3; non-refundable, paid by card online). Criminal background check: paid by the applicant to IdentoGO/IDEMIA (amount not stated on IPLA pages). Jurisprudence exam: no separate fee listed. Plus third-party costs: INBDE, ADEX/CRDTS exam fees, CPR course, transcript/score-report fees. Renewal: $100.00 per biennium (IPLA fee schedule) plus a $20 dental compliance fund fee at renewal (IC 25-14-1-10(a)). Timelines: application auto-abandoned after one year if incomplete; jurisprudence exam emailed only after all other items are approved, 14 days to return; IPLA publishes no processing-time estimate.

## Lisansı sürdürme

- **renewal_period:** Biennial; all dentist licences expire 1 March of even-numbered years (IC 25-14-3-6; IPLA). Renewal notices sent ~90 days before expiry by email (or mail if no email on file). Renew online via the IPLA/Access Indiana account or by paper form.
- **renewal_fee:** $100.00 (IPLA fee schedule) plus $20 compliance fee deposited in the dental compliance fund (IC 25-14-1-10(a)). Renewal of a licence expired more than 3 years: $350.00. Reinstatement of inactive licence: $350.00 (plus the renewal fee per IPLA instructions).
- **ce_hours:** 20 credit hours per two-year licence period (IC 25-14-3-8(a)), including 2 hours covering ethics, professional responsibility and Indiana dental statutes/rules (828 IAC 1-5-6); max 5 hours practice management; BLS training counts for no more than 2 hours. Not required during the first licence period (dentist who has held an initial licence for less than 2 years, IC 25-14-3-1(1)). CE certificates retained 3 years; random audit of 1-10% of licensees; renewal is a sworn statement of compliance.
- **ce_online_ok:** Partly. IC 25-14-3-8(a): 'At least half of the required minimum credit hours must be from live presentations or live workshops.' IPLA guidance: 'Online on pre-recorded, self-study with no live interactions, can only be counted as one-half the continuing education requirement ... as distance learning. Any online webinars where you interact with the presenter will count for live hour requirements.' So 100% can be completed from abroad online provided at least 10 hours are live interactive webinars from approved organisations (ADA, AGD, accredited institutions, etc.); no physical attendance is required.
- **cpr_required:** Not listed as a dentist renewal requirement on the IPLA page (dental hygienists must complete a BLS course each period; dentists are not so listed). CPR/BLS within the prior year is required at initial licensure (828 IAC 1-1-1(2)), and a 'copy of current CPR card' is required to reinstate an inactive or expired licence. AHA/Red Cross CPR courses have a hands-on skills component.
- **practice_hours_required:** None for renewal of an active licence. (Two of the last three years of practice are required only for licensure by endorsement.)
- **address_or_residency_required:** No Indiana residency or in-state address requirement found. Licensee must keep contact information current in the online account; renewal notice goes to the address/email of record. IC 25-14-1-10(b) requires the licence to be displayed in the holder's office, relevant only if practising in Indiana.
- **insurance_required:** No mandatory professional liability insurance requirement found in IC 25-14 or 828 IAC or on the IPLA site.
- **inactive_status:** IC 25-14-1-27.1(a): 'The board may classify a license as inactive if the board receives written notification from a licensed dentist stating that the dentist will not practice as a dentist in Indiana.' IPLA: request inactive status at renewal by marking 'yes' on the renewal form; 'There is no renewal fee or continuing education required to be placed on inactive status.' CE chapter does not apply to inactive licensees (IC 25-14-3-1(4)). No maximum duration stated. Anyone holding an Indiana licence qualifies.
- **reactivation:** From inactive (IC 25-14-1-27.1(b)-(c); IPLA): pay the renewal fee plus the $350 reinstatement fee, submit copy of current CPR card, 'proof of half the required continuing education needed since your license was inactivated' including 2 hours of Indiana ethics/jurisprudence, a statement of practice describing activities while inactive, and 'you may be scheduled to appear before the Board. The Board will determine if you will need to complete such remediation and/or additional training as deemed appropriate.' Statute: 'The board may require a licensee whose license has been inactive for more than three (3) years to participate in remediation or pass an examination administered by an entity approved by the board.' Lapsed (not renewed): licence is invalidated automatically (IC 25-14-1-10(c)); 3 years or less delinquent: reinstated on renewal application + current renewal fee + IPLA reinstatement fee + sworn CE statement (IC 25-1-8-6(c)); more than 3 years: renewal fee + reinstatement fee equal to the application fee ($350 'expired more than three years' on the fee schedule) + proof of all CE since expiry incl. 2 hrs Indiana ethics + CPR card + statement of practice + 'such remediation and additional training as deemed appropriate by the board given the lapse of time' (IC 25-1-8-6(d)); board may require the holder to appear and explain the failure to renew (IC 25-14-1-10(f)). No automatic re-examination requirement, but discretionary.
- **remote_maintainability:** moderate
- **details:** For a dentist who already holds the licence, Indiana is relatively easy to maintain from abroad: biennial online renewal ($120 incl. compliance fee), 20 CE hours of which at least 10 must be live but live interactive webinars count, no practice-hour requirement, no in-state address, no insurance, no CPR at renewal, and a free, CE-free inactive status with no time limit. Rated 'moderate' rather than 'easy' because (a) IC 12-32-1-6.5(c) applies the citizenship/qualified-alien/work-authorisation verification to applications 'to obtain or renew' a professional licence, so a licensee who is not a citizen, qualified alien or work-authorised may be unable to execute the renewal attestation (the renewal form itself could not be retrieved to confirm the wording); (b) reactivation from inactive after more than 3 years is discretionary and may involve a board appearance, remediation or an examination; (c) CPR card (hands-on course) is needed to reactivate. For this candidate the question is moot because initial licensure is barred by the SSN and IC 12-32-1 requirements.

## Açık sorular
1) DLOSCE discrepancy: JCNDE lists Indiana as accepting the DLOSCE in full, but no Indiana rule, instruction, form or minute confirms it; the board should be asked directly whether a DLOSCE score (standalone, pre-Oct 2026, or earned inside the ADEX exam) is accepted without ADEX/CRDTS manikin sections. Given 828 IAC 1-1-7's wording, acceptance appears unlikely. 2) The dentist renewal form could not be retrieved; whether the IC 12-32-1 citizenship/work-authorisation affidavit is repeated at each renewal (IC 12-32-1-6.5 says 'obtain or renew') is unverified. 3) Whether IPLA's online application (Access Indiana) accepts a foreign mailing address, and whether IdentoGO's card-scan programme accepts FD-258 cards taken outside the US, were not confirmed in writing. 4) Whether IPLA has any unpublished practice for applicants lacking an SSN (none found; the published wording says the application will be denied). 5) Current IdentoGO CBC fee amount not stated by IPLA. 6) Web-search budget was exhausted near the end of the research; older (2020-2021) board minutes that might record a DLOSCE policy were not located.
