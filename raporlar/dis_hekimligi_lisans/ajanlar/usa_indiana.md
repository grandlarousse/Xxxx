# Indiana (USA) — ajan bulgusu

Düzenleyici: Indiana State Board of Dentistry (staffed by the Indiana Professional Licensing Agency, IPLA) — https://www.in.gov/pla/professions/dentistry-home/

**Teori-only rota:** no · **Göçmenlik engeli:** high · **Uzaktan sürdürme:** moderate · **Güven:** medium

## Sınav yolları

- **Licensure by examination (standard route for CODA graduates): INBDE + hands-on regional clinical exam + Indiana jurisprudence exam** (el becerisi: evet): 828 IAC 1-1-3: applicant must pass (1) all sections of the national dental board examination, (2) a clinical examination, (3) a written examination on Indiana dental law. 828 IAC 1-1-7 (readopted 11/25/2019; listed for readoption without change on the Board's 9 Oct 2026 agenda): 'To be eligible for licensure by examination, an applicant must meet any one (1) of the following clinical examination requirements: (1) Have passed all parts of one (1) of the following examinations within the five (5) year period immediately before the date of the board's receipt of the applicant's application: (A) The Central Regional Dental Testing Service (CRDTS) examination. (B) The North East Regional Board (NERB) examination. (C) The Southern Regional Testing Agency (SRTA) examination. (D) The Western Regional Examining Board (WREB) examination. (2) Have taken an examination administered by the board and received a passing score as established by the board.' IPLA 'Dentist by Examination' instructions (Oct 2026) list only: CDCA/NERB/WREB/CITA (i.e. ADEX via CDCA-WREB-CITA), CRDTS, SRTA; 'An applicant must pass all parts of one (1) of the following examinations... Only official score reports can be accepted.' ADA Licensure-by-State page (updated 8 Oct 2026) summarises Indiana as accepting ADEX (CDCA-WREB-CITA), CRDTS, SRTA. All are manikin/hands-on exams. DLOSCE is not mentioned anywhere in the rule, the fee rule or the IPLA instructions.
- **Board-administered clinical examination (828 IAC 1-1-7(2))** (el becerisi: evet): The rule still allows 'an examination administered by the board', but the Board no longer administers its own clinical exam; the IPLA instructions offer only the regional/ADEX exams. Historically a hands-on exam. Not a practical pathway.
- **Licensure by endorsement / credentials (IC 25-14-1-16; 828 IAC 1-3-1.1)** (el becerisi: evet): Requires (IC 25-14-1-16(b)) a licence in another state or a province of Canada with substantially equal requirements, practice of dentistry for at least 2 of the 3 years preceding application, the Indiana law exam, CE (20 h in previous 2 years, max 2 h BLS) and, per 828 IAC 1-3-1.1, proof of passing the NBDE/INBDE or 'National Dental Examining Board of Canada Written Examination' plus 'satisfactorily completed a national, regional, state, or provincial clinical licensing examination in any other state or Canadian province'. Not available to a new graduate (needs 2 years of licensed practice elsewhere) and 828 IAC 1-3-1.1(c) also requires a US Social Security number.
- **PGY-1 / residency in lieu of clinical exam** (el becerisi: hayır): NOT accepted. Neither 828 IAC 1-1-7 nor the IPLA instructions allow a residency to replace the clinical examination. The only residency reference is in the foreign-trained (non-accredited graduate) route under IC 25-14-1-4.5, which requires a 2-year clinical training programme (e.g. GPR) IN ADDITION to the national board and the regional clinical exam. Not relevant to a CODA graduate.
- **DLOSCE-only / computer-based OSCE route** (el becerisi: hayır): Not written into Indiana law. JCNDE's 2026 DLOSCE candidate guide lists Indiana as accepting the DLOSCE in full, but no Indiana source read (828 IAC 1-1-7, 828 IAC 1-3-1.1, 828 IAC 0.5-2-3, IPLA Dentist-by-Examination instructions, ADA Oct 2026 summary) mentions the DLOSCE or any OSCE. Would require a Board policy/discretionary acceptance that could not be located; treat as unavailable unless the Board confirms in writing (pla8@pla.in.gov).

## Teori-only açıklaması
828 IAC 1-1-3: 'In order to obtain an Indiana license to practice dentistry, each applicant must pass an examination that includes the following: (1) All sections of the national dental board examination. (2) A clinical examination. (3) A written examination covering Indiana law...'. 828 IAC 1-1-7 (current text, readopted 2019, on the Oct 2026 readoption agenda without changes) defines the clinical examination exclusively as CRDTS, NERB, SRTA, WREB (today: ADEX via CDCA-WREB-CITA, CRDTS, SRTA) or 'an examination administered by the board'. IPLA's Dentist-by-Examination instructions: 'Clinical Examination: An applicant must pass all parts of one (1) of the following examinations' - CDCA/NERB/WREB/CITA, CRDTS, SRTA. Every listed exam has manikin hand-skill components; 'all parts' must be passed, so an ADEX-reported DLOSCE pass alone would not satisfy the rule. No residency alternative exists. The JCNDE claim that Indiana accepts the DLOSCE could not be substantiated in any Indiana rule, form or instruction.

## DLOSCE notları
DLOSCE/OSCE does not appear in 828 IAC 1-1-7 (clinical examination), 828 IAC 1-3-1.1 (endorsement), 828 IAC 0.5-2-3 (fees) or the IPLA 'Dentist by Examination' checklist (which names only CDCA/NERB/WREB/CITA, CRDTS and SRTA and requires passing 'all parts' of one of them). The ADA Licensure-by-State summary (8 Oct 2026) likewise lists only ADEX (CDCA-WREB-CITA), CRDTS and SRTA. JCNDE's listing of Indiana as a full-acceptance DLOSCE state therefore appears to rest on an unpublished Board policy (possibly a COVID-era accommodation) that is not reflected in current rules or instructions. Because the standalone DLOSCE ended 9 Oct 2026 and ADEX refuses separate registration of the DLOSCE component, the only way a new candidate now obtains a DLOSCE score is inside the full ADEX series, and Indiana's rule requires 'all parts' of that series to be passed - so the DLOSCE is irrelevant to Indiana in practice. Scores from an earlier standalone DLOSCE: no Indiana rule addresses them; acceptance would be discretionary and unverified. Board minutes were not reviewed (the 9 Oct 2026 agenda only lists 828 IAC 1-1 for readoption without changes).

PGY-1 kabul: False

## Göçmenlik / kimlik

- **citizenship_or_pr_required:** False
- **work_authorization_required:** True
- **lawful_presence_verification:** True
- **ssn_or_sin_required:** True
- **itin_or_no_ssn_alternative:** None found. 828 IAC 1-1-2(d) and 828 IAC 1-3-1.1(c): 'All applicants must submit the applicant's United States Social Security number in order to be eligible for licensure.' IPLA dentistry licensing page: 'Mandatory Disclosure of U.S. Social Security Number: Your social security number is being requested by this state agency in accordance with Ind. Code 4-1-8-1 and 25-1-5-11(a). Disclosure is mandatory, and this record cannot be processed without it.' No ITIN or no-SSN affidavit option is mentioned on the dental pages or in the dental rules (text of IC 25-1-5-11 itself not read - see open questions).
- **in_jurisdiction_address_required:** False
- **barrier_level:** high
- **details:** Indiana applies a state-wide lawful-presence verification law to professional licences. IC 12-32-1-5(a): 'Notwithstanding any other provision of law and except as otherwise provided under federal law and subsections (b), (d), (e), and (f), an agency or a political subdivision shall verify, in the manner required under section 6 of this chapter, the eligibility of any individual who: (1) is at least eighteen (18) years of age; and (2) applies for state or local public benefits or federal public benefits that are provided by the agency' (professional licences are 'state or local public benefits' under 8 U.S.C. 1621(c), and IPLA applies the statute to every licence application). IC 12-32-1-6 (as amended by P.L.172-2018): the agency shall '(1) Require the individual to execute a verification stating under penalty of perjury that the individual: (A) is a United States citizen; (B) is a qualified alien (as defined under 8 U.S.C. 1641); or (C) meets the following: (i) The individual is authorized by the federal government to work in the United States as provided under section 6.5 of this chapter. (ii) The individual is executing the verification only for the purpose of applying for a professional or occupational license'. IPLA's standard application wording (seen on an IPLA licence form, forms.in.gov id 5089): 'Pursuant to IC 12-32-1-5 and IC 12-32-1-6, I swear under the penalty of perjury that: [ ] I am a United States Citizen [ ] I am a qualified alien (as defined under 8 U.S.C. 1641) [ ] I am authorized by the Federal Government to work in the United States.' Indiana does NOT adopt the broader federal category of 'nonimmigrant under the INA' (8 U.S.C. 1621(a)(2)); the only options are citizen, qualified alien (LPR, refugee, asylee, parolee >= 1 yr, etc.) or federal work authorisation (EAD, the 2018 DACA fix). A Turkish citizen on a B-1/B-2 visitor visa with an I-94 is none of these and cannot truthfully execute the verification; a false statement is a felony (IC 12-32-1-7). Verification is by sworn attestation (perjury), not SAVE, but the attestation itself cannot be made. In addition the SSN is mandatory with no alternative identified. No Indiana address or residency requirement was found (out-of-state licensees are routine). Conclusion: a visitor with no US status cannot be licensed in Indiana.
- **citations:** IC 12-32-1-5 (https://www.lawserver.com/law/state/indiana/in-code/indiana_code_12-32-1-5); IC 12-32-1-6 as amended by P.L.172-2018 (https://www.lawserver.com/law/state/indiana/in-code/indiana_code_12-32-1-6); IPLA application wording citing IC 12-32-1-5/-6 (https://forms.in.gov/Download.aspx?id=5089); 828 IAC 1-1-2(d) as quoted on the ADA Indiana page; 828 IAC 1-3-1.1(c) (https://www.law.cornell.edu/regulations/indiana/828-IAC-1-3-1.1); IPLA mandatory SSN disclosure notice citing IC 4-1-8-1 and IC 25-1-5-11(a) (https://www.in.gov/pla/professions/dentistry-home/dentistry-licensing-information/)

## Yüz yüze adımlar
(1) Hands-on clinical exam (ADEX/CRDTS/SRTA) must be taken in person at a US test site - unavoidable. (2) Criminal background check: IPLA requires a criminal background check for all dentist applicants (item 2 of the checklist); IPLA uses a fingerprint-based national check through its vendor - whether out-of-country applicants may use mailed fingerprint cards was not verified (unclear). (3) Jurisprudence (Indiana dental law) exam: 'Once all above items have been reviewed and approved, the applicant will be sent a jurisprudence exam by email. All applicants for dental licensure are required to pass a jurisprudence examination' (passing score 75 under 828 IAC 1-1-6; retake after 30 days, $25) - can be completed from abroad. (4) No notarisation: application is online via IPLA/myLicense and sworn 'under oath or affirmation, subject to the penalties for perjury' (828 IAC 1-3-1.1(b)(9)); the lawful-presence verification is likewise an affirmation. (5) Board appearance: IC 25-14-1-16(d) 'An applicant shall, at the request of the board, make an appearance before the board' - discretionary; Board meets via Zoom. (6) BLS/ACLS card: 'Applicants are required to submit a copy of your current BLS and/or ACLS certification card' - obtainable abroad but AHA BLS normally has a hands-on skills session.

## Diğer başlangıç şartları
Per IPLA 'Dentist by Examination' checklist: proof of DDS/DMD from an accredited (CODA) school (IC 25-14-1-3: board may recognise CODA-accredited schools); criminal background check; official INBDE/NBDE score report sent directly by JCNDE; official clinical-exam score report (all parts passed within 5 years before Board receipt of application); copy of current BLS or ACLS card; NPDB/HIPDB self-query if licensed in another health profession; emailed Indiana jurisprudence exam (score >= 75); mandatory US SSN; IC 12-32-1-6 citizenship/qualified-alien/work-authorisation verification; all statements under oath. No English test for CODA graduates (the TOEFL/English proficiency item applies only to the IC 25-14-1-4.5 foreign-trained route, waivable by the Board). No liability insurance requirement. Timeline: jurisprudence exam is sent only after all other items are approved; licence issued after passing it.

## İlk ücretler
828 IAC 0.5-2-3 (as amended, filed 3/27/2025): application for licensure $250; repeat law examination $25; licence verification to another state $10. Plus: ADEX full series $2,995 (known fact); criminal background check vendor fee (amount not verified); INBDE fee. Biennial renewal $100 + $20 dental compliance fee (IC 25-14-1-10(a)).

## Lisansı sürdürme

- **renewal_period:** Biennial. IC 25-14-3-6: 'license period' = 'the two (2) year period beginning on March 2, 1992, and every two (2) years thereafter' (licences expire 1 March of even-numbered years; IC 25-14-1-10(h) 'Biennial renewal of licenses is subject to IC 25-1-2').
- **renewal_fee:** $100 biennially (828 IAC 0.5-2-3(3)) plus a $20 compliance fee per renewal (IC 25-14-1-10(a)) = $120 per 2 years.
- **ce_hours:** 20 credit hours per 2-year licence period (IC 25-14-3-8(a)); at least 2 hours in ethics/professional responsibility; max 5 hours practice management; max 2 hours BLS training counted; at least half (10 h) 'must be from live presentations or live workshops'. CE exemption for the first 2 years after initial licensure (IC 25-14-3-1(1)) and for inactive licensees (IC 25-14-3-1(4)). Renewal requires a sworn statement of CE compliance (IC 25-14-1-10(i)).
- **ce_online_ok:** Partly. IC 25-14-3-9 allows self-study credit where 'Subject matter of the course may be presented by written, audio, or video materials', but IC 25-14-3-8(a) says 'At least half of the required minimum credit hours must be from live presentations or live workshops.' The statute does not define 'live'; whether a live interactive webinar counts as a 'live presentation' is not stated in the sources read (unclear - commonly treated as acceptable by Indiana CE providers, but verify with the Board). Therefore up to 10 h recorded self-study + 10 h live (webinar or in person).
- **cpr_required:** Current BLS or ACLS card is required at initial application. No CPR/BLS requirement for renewal was found in IC 25-14-3 or IC 25-14-1-10; BLS training may count for up to 2 CE hours. Hands-on skills session: AHA BLS normally includes a skills check - unclear whether a fully online card would be accepted.
- **practice_hours_required:** None for renewal of an active licence. (2 of the last 3 years practising is required only for licensure by endorsement under IC 25-14-1-16.)
- **address_or_residency_required:** None. No Indiana address or residency requirement found for initial licensure or renewal; address of record may be out of state/abroad.
- **insurance_required:** No mandatory professional liability insurance found in IC 25-14 or 828 IAC.
- **inactive_status:** IC 25-14-1-27.1(a): 'The board may classify a license as inactive if the board receives written notification from a licensed dentist stating that the dentist will not practice as a dentist in Indiana.' Inactive licensees are exempt from CE (IC 25-14-3-1(4)). No separate inactive fee was found in 828 IAC 0.5-2-3 (whether the biennial renewal fee continues to apply to inactive licences was not verified). No maximum duration stated, but remediation/exam may be imposed after >3 years inactive.
- **reactivation:** IC 25-14-1-27.1(b): board may issue a licence to the holder of an inactive licence if the applicant '(1) pays the renewal fee set by the board; (2) pays the reinstatement fee set by the board [$250, 828 IAC 0.5-2-3(13) 'Reinstatement of inactive license']; (3) meets continuing education requirements set by the board; and (4) meets competency standards set by the board.' (c): 'The board may require a licensee whose license has been inactive for more than three (3) years to participate in remediation or pass an examination administered by an entity approved by the board.' Lapsed (unrenewed) licences: IC 25-14-1-10(c) licence is invalidated automatically; reinstatement within 3 years under IC 25-1-8-6(c) (fees + CE); after more than 3 years under IC 25-1-8-6(d) (board may require evidence of competency/examination), and the Board may require the holder to appear and explain (IC 25-14-1-10(f)).
- **remote_maintainability:** moderate
- **details:** If a licence were ever obtained, keeping it active from abroad is feasible: $120 every 2 years, 20 h CE with 10 h allowed as recorded self-study and 10 h 'live' (live webinars probably acceptable but not defined), no practice hours, no in-state address, no insurance, no renewal CPR requirement found. Inactive status with CE exemption exists; reactivation costs $250 + renewal fee + CE and, after >3 years inactive, possibly remediation or an exam. Caveat: renewal is an 'application' and IPLA renewals also carry the IC 12-32-1-6 citizenship/qualified-alien/work-authorisation attestation, so lawful-presence status would be re-attested at each renewal (not verified for the dental renewal form). The decisive barrier is initial licensure, not maintenance.

## Açık sorular
(1) The dentist application itself is submitted online through IPLA's portal; the IC 12-32-1-6 attestation wording was verified on another IPLA licence form (id 5089) and in the statute, not on a dentist-specific form - assumed identical since the statute binds all IPLA boards. (2) Text of IC 25-1-5-11 (SSN) was not read; it is cited by IPLA as making SSN disclosure mandatory, but whether it contains any exception for applicants without an SSN (e.g. ITIN) is unverified - the dental rules themselves say the SSN is required 'in order to be eligible for licensure'. (3) JCNDE lists Indiana as accepting the DLOSCE in full, yet no Indiana rule, fee schedule, instruction or ADA summary mentions it; Board minutes were not reviewed. Email pla8@pla.in.gov to ask whether a DLOSCE score (standalone or ADEX-reported) can satisfy 828 IAC 1-1-7 - but even if so, the SSN and lawful-presence requirements remain. (4) Fingerprinting method for applicants living abroad (mailed cards vs in-state LiveScan) not verified. (5) Whether 'live presentations' in IC 25-14-3-8 includes live webinars, and whether an inactive licence still pays the biennial renewal fee, were not verified. (6) IC 12-32-1-5(e) exemption text was not read in full; it does not appear to help visitor-visa holders given IPLA's three-option attestation.
