# Kansas (USA) — ajan bulgusu

Düzenleyici: Kansas Dental Board — https://dental.ks.gov/

**Teori-only rota:** no · **Göçmenlik engeli:** unclear · **Uzaktan sürdürme:** moderate · **Güven:** medium

## Sınav yolları

- **Licensure by examination – ADEX Dental Examination (CDCA-WREB-CITA)** (el becerisi: evet): Accepted regional clinical exam (ADA Kansas Licensure summary, updated 29 Nov 2025). Board policy quoted by ADA: 'On and after January 1, 2023, all applicants seeking a license to practice dentistry in Kansas must take and successfully complete all sections of a clinical licensure exam that is patient-based or manikin-based. The sections required include the Objective Structured Clinical Exam (OSCE), Endodontic, Periodontic, Prosthodontic, and Restorative.' The ADEX series includes five manikin hand-skill exams plus the DLOSCE; all sections must be passed. Plus INBDE, K.A.R. 71-1-9 Kansas dental-law (jurisprudence) exam, $200 application fee, age 21, good moral character, CODA-standard school graduation (K.S.A. 65-1426).
- **Licensure by examination – CRDTS clinical exam** (el becerisi: evet): CRDTS listed as accepted by ADA summary; must still cover all five required sections (OSCE, Endo, Perio, Prostho, Restorative) of a patient- or manikin-based exam.
- **Licensure by examination – SRTA clinical exam** (el becerisi: evet): SRTA listed as accepted by ADA summary; same all-sections patient-/manikin-based requirement applies.
- **DLOSCE / OSCE-only (standalone computer-based)** (el becerisi: hayır): NOT accepted in Kansas. Kansas is not among the DLOSCE-accepting states in the JCNDE 2026 Candidate Guide, and the Board requires the OSCE section AND the Endodontic, Periodontic, Prosthodontic and Restorative hands-on sections of a patient-based or manikin-based exam. K.S.A. 65-1428(a): 'The examination shall be both theoretical and clinical'. The DLOSCE/OSCE component only counts as one section of a full ADEX pass.
- **PGY-1 residency in lieu of clinical exam** (el becerisi: evet): No residency-in-lieu-of-exam provision exists in K.S.A. 65-1426/65-1428 or the Board's stated requirements; a clinical exam is mandatory. (Residency itself would in any case be hands-on and require a visa.)
- **Licensure by credentials (K.S.A. 65-1434, 'licensure without examination')** (el becerisi: evet): Requires a national board exam AND 'a clinical examination, administered by any state or clinical dental testing agency, of equivalent merit to the clinical examination accepted by the board', plus having 'held a license to practice dentistry in one or more other states of the United States for the five-year period immediately preceding the date of application' and five years' active practice. Not available to a new graduate.
- **Temporary 14-day volunteer license (K.S.A. 65-1426(d))** (el becerisi: evet): Only for dentists already licensed in another US state providing gratuitous services under K.S.A. 75-6102; not an initial licensure route for a new graduate.

## Teori-only açıklaması
K.S.A. 65-1428(a) (quoted on the ADA Kansas Licensure page, updated 29 Nov 2025): 'All examinations provided for in this act shall be conducted in a fair and wholly impartial manner. The examination shall be both theoretical and clinical, and shall thoroughly test the qualifications of each applicant to practice dentistry.' Kansas Dental Board policy (ADA summary citing the Board's 'Dentists Licensing by Examination' page): 'On and after January 1, 2023, all applicants seeking a license to practice dentistry in Kansas must take and successfully complete all sections of a clinical licensure exam that is patient-based or manikin-based. The sections required include the Objective Structured Clinical Exam (OSCE), Endodontic, Periodontic, Prosthodontic, and Restorative.' Only ADEX (CDCA-WREB-CITA), CRDTS and SRTA are accepted. No DLOSCE-only, residency, or portfolio alternative exists; licensure by credentials (K.S.A. 65-1434) also demands a clinical exam plus five years of prior US licensure/practice.

## DLOSCE notları
Kansas does not accept the DLOSCE as a standalone clinical exam (not on the JCNDE 2026 list; Board requires OSCE + Endodontic + Periodontic + Prosthodontic + Restorative sections of a patient-/manikin-based exam). The OSCE requirement is satisfied by the OSCE/DLOSCE component inside a full ADEX (or CRDTS/SRTA) pass; an ADEX-reported DLOSCE pass alone would not satisfy the other four hands-on sections. No Board statement found about earlier standalone DLOSCE scores; since Kansas never accepted DLOSCE alone, the Oct 2026 end of standalone DLOSCE administrations changes nothing for Kansas.

## Yüz yüze adımlar
(1) Hands-on clinical exam (ADEX/CRDTS/SRTA) must be taken in person at a US/accepted test site – unavoidable and a visitor could sit it, but it is hands-on. (2) K.A.R. 71-1-9 Kansas dental-law examination: 'All candidates for a Kansas dental license ... will be required to pass a satisfactory examination on the pertinent provisions of the Kansas dental law' – format (online/open-book vs in-person) could not be verified because the Board site blocked access. (3) Application must be 'verified by the oath of the applicant' (K.S.A. 65-1426(a)) i.e. notarised; a foreign notary/apostille or US consulate notarisation may be accepted – unverified. (4) Recent photograph required. (5) Fingerprint-based criminal background check: no statutory requirement found in K.S.A. 65-1426; Board practice unverified. (6) No interview requirement found. Overall: apart from the hands-on exam, the remaining steps appear completable by mail/online or during a short visit, but this is partly unverified.

## Lisansı sürdürme

- **renewal_period:** Biennial (K.S.A. 65-1431(a): 'shall be renewed on a biennial basis'; staggered system, renewal due in December, late window Feb 1–Mar 1, licence cancelled if not renewed by March 1).
- **renewal_fee:** Fixed by the Board under K.S.A. 65-1447; amount not verified (Board fee page inaccessible). Late penalty up to $500 (K.S.A. 65-1431(f)(2)).
- **ce_hours:** 60 hours per biennial renewal period, at least 2 in ethics (K.A.R. 71-4-1(b)). Waived for the first year after graduation/licensure (K.A.R. 71-4-2(d)).
- **ce_online_ok:** Home study/online credit is allowed: K.A.R. 71-4-2(g) 'Credit for programs of home study shall be allowed for eligible courses based upon the hours ... established by the sponsor ... subject to prior review and determination of the allowable hours of credit by the board.' No explicit cap on home-study hours was found in 71-4-2, but credit is subject to Board review; courses from ADA/AGD/CERP-type sponsors 'both within and without the state of Kansas' qualify (71-4-2(b)). Up to 6 hours may be earned by gratuitous care (K.S.A. 65-1431(d)). Whether 100% self-study is accepted in practice is unverified.
- **cpr_required:** Yes. K.A.R. 71-4-1(a): each licensee must submit with the renewal application 'a current "basic cardiac life support for the health care provider" certificate from the American heart association or a current certificate deemed equivalent by the board'. AHA BLS Provider certification normally includes a hands-on skills session (blended learning possible; AHA courses are available internationally).
- **practice_hours_required:** None found in K.S.A. 65-1431 or K.A.R. 71-4.
- **address_or_residency_required:** No Kansas residency or in-state address requirement found; renewal application reports an 'office address' (K.S.A. 65-1431(c)).
- **insurance_required:** No mandatory professional liability insurance requirement found for licensure/renewal.
- **inactive_status:** Retired/disabled waiver (K.S.A. 65-1431(h)-(i)): the Board 'may waive the payment of biennial fees and the continuing education requirements' for a licensee who 'has retired from such practice' and files a certificate that the licensee 'is not engaged ... in the provision of any dental service ... as defined by the statutes of the state of Kansas'; no fee, no CE, no time limit ('shall continue so long as the retirement ... exists'). Caveat: performing any dental service, 'including consulting service', is deemed resumption of practice, so a dentist actively practising abroad may not qualify; Board interpretation for foreign practice unverified. No separate 'inactive' licence category found.
- **reactivation:** Return from retirement: fees and CE reimposed from the date of return (K.S.A. 65-1431(i)); K.A.R. 71-4-2(i) requires make-up CE before returning to active practice: 1 year 30 h, 2 years 60 h, 3 years 70 h, 4 years 80 h, 5+ years 100 h. No new clinical exam or refresher course is required by these provisions. Reinstatement after cancellation for non-renewal (after March 1): rules not verified (Board page inaccessible); K.S.A. 65-1431 provides cancellation, and re-licensure may require a new application.
- **remote_maintainability:** moderate
- **details:** Active licence can be kept from abroad with 60 h biennial CE (home study permitted subject to Board review), a current AHA BLS-for-healthcare-provider certificate (hands-on skills component, obtainable abroad), and the biennial fee; no practice hours, no in-state address, no insurance. Retired status is free and unlimited but requires certifying no provision of dental services, which likely excludes someone practising dentistry abroad. Main obstacle is initial licensure, not maintenance.
