# bc_licensure

TOPIC: British Columbia (BCCOHP) Full Dentist licence after the NDEB certificate, step by step, for a non-resident Turkish citizen (Yeditepe DDS, CODA-accredited). Covers the online application, the documents to mail and notarize, fees and the half-year rule, the criminal record check from abroad, the CAD 3M liability insurance, timing against the 3-year rule, and what renewal requires each year. All amounts are in CAD.


## STEP 1: Confirm in writing that BCCOHP accepts a Yeditepe CODA diploma, and ask the open questions
TIMING: Now: Nov 2026, in parallel with the NDEB application (deadline 5 Jan 2027). Allow 1 to 3 weeks for a reply.
BC's bylaws recognise CODA international programmes. Schedule D 'Recognized General Dentistry Education Programs' of the BCCOHP Bylaws (April 1, 2026) lists 'CODA Accredited Dental Programs (US and international)'. Bylaw 6.05 sets the Full dentist licence standard: (a) a degree from a recognized general dentistry education program and (b) 'an NDEB certificate issued no more than 3 years before the date of the applicant's application'. BCCOHP's public 'Dentists coming from another country' page still only names Canada/US/Australia/NZ/Ireland, so get written confirmation now. Email licensing@oralhealthbc.ca (registration@oralhealthbc.ca is printed on the forms; phone 672-202-0448, or 1-888-202-0448 toll-free within Canada). Suggested questions: (1) is a Yeditepe (CODA international, diploma after 1 Feb 2024) graduate eligible for Full Dentist under Schedule D; (2) may a Turkish notary (noter) notarize the Statutory Declaration and the Photo ID Notarization Form, and must the notarial wording be in English; how should a non-Canadian notary fill the field 'in the province of'; (3) which secondary photo ID they accept from a Turkish citizen, for example the T.C. kimlik kartı, a Canadian visitor visa (TRV) counterfoil or a Yeditepe student card; (4) can an applicant with no Canadian address or practice address apply; (5) which professional liability insurers they accept besides CDSPI, and whether a 'per claim' policy satisfies the '$3,000,000 per occurrence' rule; (6) whether any 'mandatory vaccinations' must be shown (bylaw 6.03(1)(a)(ii)(E)).

## STEP 2: Prepare the Turkish-side documents (education proof, ID, and Turkish registration status)
TIMING: Nov 2026 to Feb 2027: clarify only. Order 60-day documents 3 to 6 weeks before submitting the BC application.
(a) Education. BCCOHP requires a 'Notarized or certified copy of education degree/diploma'. Its footnote says: '†If confirmation of successful completion is provided to BCCOHP by your educational institution or the NDEB directly, notarization of these documents is not required during submission.' Ask Yeditepe's Öğrenci İşleri now whether it can send an English graduation confirmation (transcript with conferral date) directly to BCCOHP when you ask later. It is the same kind of document you are already ordering for the NDEB. (b) ID. The Photo ID form needs 'two pieces of photo ID (one primary and one secondary, or two primary)'. Primary examples include 'Valid passport'. Secondary examples include 'School Identification Card (student card)', 'Foreign passport', 'Student, work, visitor or temporary resident permit' and 'Picture employee ID card'. Keep the Turkish passport valid well past the application date. (c) Turkish registration status. If you were ever registered or licensed to practise in Turkey (TDB / Istanbul Dişhekimleri Odası membership, or Ministry of Health diploma registration with practice), BCCOHP will need a Certificate or Letter of Standing. It must be 'delivered directly to BCCOHP from the licensing/regulating body in a sealed envelope' and 'is valid for up to 60 days from the date that it was signed'. Under bylaw 6.03(1)(a)(iv), anyone who has practised outside Canada also needs records 'dated no more than 60 days prior to the date of the applicant's application ... comparable to a criminal record check'. In practice that means a Turkish adli sicil kaydı (e-Devlet). Do not order these now: they expire after 60 days, so order them in step 6.

## STEP 3: Pass the Virtual OSCE, receive the NDEB certificate, and record its issue date (this starts the 3-year BC clock)
TIMING: Exam 1 Mar 2027. Result and certificate date not verified, probably spring 2027. Last day to submit to BC = certificate issue date + 3 years (for example, a certificate dated May 2027 gives a deadline of about May 2030).
BCCOHP says: 'Once you've successfully completed the appropriate NDEB process and received your NDEB certificate, please visit our Dentist Licence section to find the appropriate BCCOHP application package for you.' Bylaw 6.05(b) requires 'an NDEB certificate issued no more than 3 years before the date of the applicant's application'. The online application asks for 'National Dental Examination Board (NDEB) certification information', so in practice you apply after the certificate exists. To skip notarizing the certificate, ask the NDEB to send confirmation directly to BCCOHP (footnote †). The NDEB fee and procedure for this are not verified here; check the NDEB 'certificate verification' service. If you passed more than 3 years earlier, or are not licensed elsewhere in Canada: 'you may have to complete an equivalency determination process.'

## STEP 4: Decide when to submit (timing strategy)
TIMING: Decide by about Apr to May 2027. Earliest submission: right after the certificate. Half-year option: on or after 2 Oct 2027. Latest: certificate issue date + 3 years.
Facts: the licence year runs April 1 to March 31. 'A practising licence expires on the March 31 immediately following the date it was issued or last renewed' (bylaw 6.20). Licence fee for a Full dentist: '$1,749' full year, '$875' half year. 'Half year fees ... only apply to applications for a licence submitted after October 1.' Non-practising status no longer exists: 'the non-practising status will no longer exist as of April 1, 2026.' Every licence therefore costs the full renewal fee plus CAD 3M insurance every year, even if you are not working. A licence does not give you the right to work in Canada. You also need IRCC work authorisation (work permit or PR); a visitor cannot practise. This is based on IRCC general rules, not BCCOHP text, and was not fetched (canada.ca was unreachable). Options: (A) A job and work permit are already in progress: apply as soon as the certificate arrives and pay the full-year fee, about CAD 5,099 in total. (B) No work authorisation yet, but you want the licence early (some employers or LMIA/PR streams want proof of licensure): submit after 1 Oct 2027 for the half-year fee of $875 (total about $4,225). The licence then expires 31 Mar 2028 and must be renewed. (C) Keep the certificate 'fresh' and apply only when the work permit is near, but always within 3 years of the certificate issue date. Whatever you choose, factor in the proposed 5% fee increase effective 1 Apr 2027 (not yet final). It gives approximately: application $3,488, licence $1,836 full year / $919 half year, renewal $1,836.

## STEP 5: Arrange CAD 3,000,000 per-occurrence professional liability insurance before filling in the application
TIMING: Start 4 to 8 weeks before the planned submission date. The policy must be in force by the time the licence is issued and kept continuously.
Bylaw 8.14(2): a full dentist licensee 'must be insured against liability arising from an error, omission, or negligent act in the provision of health services included in the practice of dentistry in an amount of at least $3,000,000 per occurrence'. Bylaw 8.14(5): the insurance must 'be in a form that is satisfactory to the college'. The online application asks for 'Professional liability insurance information', so have the insurer name, policy number, effective dates and limit ready. The usual route is CDSPI (Aviva-underwritten). Its online application covers 'Malpractice Insurance (Excluding Ontario and Alberta)'. The 2026 member premiums for '$3,000,000 per claim (maximum $9-million per calendar year)' are $1,685.39 ($1,000 deductible), $1,627.08 ($2,500) and $1,553.84 ($5,000). '(add 25% on all premium rates for Non-Members)' gives about $2,107 / $2,034 / $1,942. 'Provincial taxes are extra.' BCDA membership is not required to buy CDSPI cover (non-members pay +25%). You cannot join the BCDA before licensure anyway: BCDA Full membership requires an active BCCOHP licence ('Dentists holding an active Full Dentist BCCOHP Licence are eligible'). After licensure, join BCDA 'First Year in Practice' ('New graduate dentists ... that register for BCDA membership within one (1) year of graduation') to get member pricing. Risk: the CDSPI form says 'If you are a citizen or permanent resident outside of Quebec, please proceed with the online application'. A Turkish non-resident must therefore confirm eligibility with CDSPI Advisory Services (1-800-561-9401, insurance@cdspi.com), along with the effective date, whether coverage can start on the licence date, and the short-term option ('Short Term Malpractice Member Minimum Premium: $250.00'). No alternative insurer named by BCCOHP was found. Proof: BCCOHP's FAQ says 'Proof of liability insurance is not required unless requested or if you are changing licensure to a Full Dentist or Restricted to Specialty licence.' Expect to upload the CDSPI certificate of insurance.

## STEP 6: Order the 60-day documents (only if you were ever registered or practised in Turkey)
TIMING: 3 to 6 weeks before submission. Licensing takes about 8 weeks in total, so a certificate can expire mid-process; confirm with staff before ordering.
Certificate or Letter of Standing from every regulator (TDB / Oda / Ministry, whichever holds your registration), sent directly to BCCOHP in a sealed envelope. Mailing address: BCCOHP, 110 - 1765 8th Ave W, Vancouver, BC V6J 5C6. 'The certificate is valid for up to 60 days from the date that it was signed. If your licensing process is not completed within 60 days ... a new certificate will be required.' If you practised in Turkey, also get the adli sicil kaydı (with a translation if needed) dated within 60 days (bylaw 6.03(1)(a)(iv)(A)). If you never registered or practised, these are not needed; state that in the application.

## STEP 7: Create the BCCOHP web account and complete the online application in one sitting
TIMING: The chosen submission day (see step 4). Takes about 20 minutes plus preparation.
From the 'Dentist initial licence' page, open 'Full Dentist Licence' and click 'Apply now >>' (https://apps.oralhealthbc.ca/registration-application.aspx). Step 1: 'If you have not been licensed with BCCOHP, you will need to create a new account ... You will be required to enter your contact information.' Rules: 'Your application must be completed in one attempt.' 'If you logout or close your web browser, your partially completed application will not be saved.' 'The online application process may take up to 20 minutes.' Step 2 needs: 'Contact information – home and practice addresses, phone numbers, and email addresses (if applicable)'; 'BC Driver's Licence number or other form of government issued photo identification' (use the Turkish passport number); 'Dental education – institution, graduation/conferral date, degree received'; 'National Dental Examination Board (NDEB) certification information'; 'Names of other jurisdiction(s) you have been registered with, along with dates'; 'Professional liability insurance information'. The bylaws also require (6.03) a criminal record check authorization, the Schedule C declaration, proof of insurance compliance, and confirmation of mandatory vaccinations 'required by or under an enactment'.

## STEP 8: Pay the application fee and criminal record check fee immediately (non-refundable)
TIMING: The same session as step 7.
Step 3: 'Once the application is completed, you will be prompted to pay the application and criminal record check fees (non-refundable).' Full dentist application fee: '$3,322' (Schedule A; 'Application fees specified in this Schedule are non-refundable'). CRC: '$28 for new applicants'. Payment: 'Credit Card (VISA, Mastercard)' or 'Cheque/money order (mail)'. Use a card, because mailing a cheque from Turkey is slow. Pay at once: 'BCCOHP will send your CRC to the Ministry on your behalf for processing after you submit your initial payment' and your notarized ID copies.

## STEP 9: Download, sign and notarize the Statutory Declaration and Photo Identification Notarization Form, then courier the originals
TIMING: Notarize within days of submitting online. Courier transit Turkey to Vancouver is about 3 to 7 days.
Step 4 list: 'Notarized Statutory declaration (available for download during the application process)'; 'Notarized Photo Identification Notarization form, along with notarized photocopy of two pieces of photo ID (one primary and one secondary, or two primary)'; 'Notarized or certified copy of education degree/diploma*†'; 'Notarized or certified copy of NDEB certificate*†' († means no notarization is needed if the school or NDEB confirms directly); 'Certificates or Letters of Standing'; 'Name change documents (if required)'. 'For all documents that require notarization, the original must be received by the BCCOHP office. Please submit your documents via mail.' 'For documents that require notarization, please only upload these documents after they are notarized.' Form rules (April 2026 forms): 'APPLICATION MUST BE COMPLETE, WITH PHOTO ATTACHED, PRIOR TO NOTARIZATION.' 'THIS FORM MUST BE SIGNED AND STAMPED WITH THE NOTARY SEAL.' On the Photo ID form, the 'Notary stamp and/or seal must be directly on photocopy attached to this form. The same stamp and/or seal should be affixed in the box below.' Also: 'Seal or stamp required on notary signature and on attached photocopy of ID Signature alone is not sufficient.' Notary wording: the Statutory Declaration reads 'DECLARED before me at the city of ____, in (country) ____', so Istanbul, Türkiye works. The Photo ID form reads 'DECLARED before me at the city of ____, in the province of ____'. A Turkish notary can write 'İstanbul' and 'İstanbul Province, Republic of Türkiye', but confirm this with BCCOHP. Who may notarize: the Commissioner for Oaths Information Sheet lists BC commissioners (lawyers, notaries public, and others) and adds: 'Note: For persons outside of British Columbia, persons or agencies equivalent to the above in other provinces or states may provide legal notarization of BCCOHP application documents.' A Turkish noter is therefore the equivalent of a notary public. BCCOHP states no language rule (unlike the NDEB). Turkish notaries normally certify in Turkish, so ask the noter for an English or bilingual annotation (or a sworn translation), or use the notarial services of the Canadian Embassy in Ankara or the Consulate in Istanbul (availability and fees not verified). Apostille is not mentioned by BCCOHP (not verified). Courier the originals with tracking to BCCOHP, 110 - 1765 8th Ave W, Vancouver, BC V6J 5C6, and upload scans to the portal.

## STEP 10: Complete the BC Ministry criminal record check (CRRP) from abroad
TIMING: Starts once the fee is paid and the notarized ID is received. Usually 4 weeks; longer if fingerprints are requested.
BCCOHP submits the CRC to the Ministry of Public Safety and Solicitor General. You cannot use your own check: 'The Act does not permit us to accept CRCs that have been undertaken through another agency (e.g., the RCMP).' Processing time: 'approximately 4 weeks after the information is submitted'. Fingerprints are needed only if the Ministry writes to you (a vulnerable-sector match on date of birth and gender). 'If you reside outside of Canada, the Ministry of Public Safety and Solicitor General will ask you to fill out a form indicating the date that you will return to Canada and attend for fingerprinting.' 'You will have 90 days from the date of the initial fingerprint request letter to complete the VS check requirement.' Fingerprint results take 2 to 8 weeks; tell BCCOHP if you receive a letter. Status enquiries: 1-855-587-0185 (option 2) or criminalrecords@gov.bc.ca.
SOURCES: https://oralhealthbc.ca/licensure/criminal-record-checks/ ; https://oralhealthbc.ca/licensure/dentist-licensure-renewal/dentists/

## STEP 11: Pay the licence fee when BCCOHP confirms the file is complete; licence issued
TIMING: About 8 weeks after a complete submission.
'A separate payment of the licence fee will be required once BCCOHP staff have confirmed application completion. This takes place closer to the end of the application process.' Full dentist licence fee: $1,749 for a full year, or $875 for a half year if the application was submitted after October 1 (2026/27 Schedule A; 5% increase proposed from 1 Apr 2027). Processing: 'The general application processing time is eight weeks. Please note that incomplete applications or insufficient information may delay your application process.' Total initial cost: $3,322 + $28 + $1,749 = $5,099, or $4,225 with the half-year fee. Insurance is extra.

## STEP 12: After licensure: BCDA membership (optional), insurance at member rates, and no practice without IRCC work authorisation
TIMING: From the date the licence is issued.
Join BCDA 'First Year in Practice' if you are within 1 year of graduation. Membership gives 'CDSPI Malpractice Insurance Member Pricing' (avoiding the 25% non-member load), legal expense insurance and similar benefits. Keep the CAD 3M insurance continuous: you declare on the Statutory Declaration 'I will ensure that I maintain professional liability insurance coverage as required by the BCCOHP for the entire period of my licence.' Notify BCCOHP within 14 days of any change in business contact information (bylaw 8.15). Report charges or convictions (Criminal Records Review Act s.17). Do not treat patients in Canada until you hold a work permit or PR; the BC licence alone does not authorise work (IRCC rule; not fetched).

## STEP 13: Renew every year by March 31 and complete the QAP
TIMING: Every Feb 1 to Mar 31 for as long as you hold the licence. The first renewal is due the March 31 after issue (for example, a licence issued in Dec 2027 is renewed by 31 Mar 2028).
Renewal: 'BCCOHP's licensing year runs from April 1 each year until March 31 the following year.' The registrar notifies you 'On or before February 1 of each year' (6.21). Renew online with: the Schedule C declaration; CRC authorization every 5 years (6.22: when the previous one was submitted 'more than 4 years before'); insurance attestation ('You must attest that you have the required professional liability insurance to renew'); mandatory-vaccination confirmation; and fees. 2026/27 fees: Full Dentist $1,749 + $5.60 CRC = $1,754.60 (5% increase proposed for 2027/28). If you miss March 31: 'You will no longer be able to practice as of April 1'. Reinstatement 'on or before the May 30' (within 60 days) costs a $332 late fee plus the renewal fee and CRC ($2,086.60 in total). Later reinstatement costs $2,581 plus the $28 CRC ($2,609) and must happen 'within 3 years of the date on which the former licensee was last a licensee' (6.26(2)). QAP (mandatory, annual, April 1 to March 31; the first cycle ran 'April 13, 2026, to March 31, 2027'). Component A: practice self-inventory, standards self-reflection, 'Set a minimum of 2 professional goals', a CPD plan, and annual CPD self-reporting. Component B: online standards modules ('no more than three modules ... each year'). Component C: individualised support if selected. It applies to everyone, including those 'newly licensed in British Columbia'. Unfinished QA requirements can block renewal: 'If you are not able to complete your remaining QA requirements by March 31, 2026, you will not be eligible to renew your licence.' Contact: qualityassurance@oralhealthbc.ca.

## RISKS
1) Eligibility wording. Schedule D of the Bylaws recognises 'CODA Accredited Dental Programs (US and international)', but BCCOHP's public 'Dentists coming from another country' page names only CA/US/AU/NZ/IE. Get written confirmation for Yeditepe (diploma after 1 Feb 2024).
2) The 3-year clock runs from the NDEB certificate issue date, not the exam date. Missing it can trigger an 'equivalency determination process'.
3) No non-practising status exists after 1 Apr 2026. Applying early without a work permit costs roughly CAD 1,750 plus CAD 1,700 to 2,100 of insurance every year for nothing. Use the half-year fee (submit after 1 Oct) or delay, but stay within 3 years.
4) The licence does not give the right to work in Canada. Practising on a visitor visa is not allowed; you need an IRCC work permit or PR.
5) The application fee ($3,322) and the CRC fee are non-refundable. Do not pay until you are sure of your eligibility and timing.
6) The online form cannot be saved: have every item ready (passport number, Yeditepe conferral date, NDEB certificate details, the full insurance policy, and any jurisdictions you were registered in).
7) Notarization. The form must be complete with photo attached before the notary signs. The seal must be on the notary's signature and on the ID photocopy; a signature alone is rejected. 'In the province of' on the Photo ID form and the language of Turkish notarial annotations are both uncertain: ask BCCOHP, or use a bilingual or English notarization or Canadian consular notarial services.
8) 60-day validity of the Certificate of Standing and the foreign criminal record documents. With 8-week processing they can expire, so order late and coordinate with staff.
9) CDSPI eligibility for a foreign national who is not resident in Canada is unclear: the form says 'citizen or permanent resident outside of Quebec'. CDSPI quotes 'per claim' limits while the bylaw requires 'per occurrence'. Note also the non-member 25% load, and the (third-party, unverified) claim that coverage applies only to actions brought in Canada.
10) If the Ministry requests fingerprints, you must return to Canada within 90 days. A visitor visa is needed for that trip, so plan travel.
11) A proposed 5% fee rise from 1 Apr 2027 is not final.
12) The Schedule-A PDF linked on the dentist page (2025/02) is stale and now redirects. Use the April 1, 2026 Bylaws.
13) The forms quoted are from the April 2026 Limited (Academic) package. The Full Dentist Statutory Declaration is downloaded inside the portal and may differ slightly.
