# Tennessee (USA) — ajan bulgusu

**Teori-only rota:** no · **Göçmenlik engeli:** high · **Uzaktan sürdürme:** moderate · **Güven:** medium

## Sınav yolları

- **Licensure by examination (regional/ADEX hands-on clinical exam + INBDE + TN Ethics & Jurisprudence exam)** (el becerisi: evet): Rule 0460-02-.05(1): the Board 'adopts as its licensure examination and requires ... successful completion of all of the following examination components as a prerequisite for licensure: (a) An examination must include a prosthetic component, a periodontal component, an endodontic component, and a live human patient or non-patient based anterior and posterior restorative component. (b) The National Board if the applicant graduated ... after 1972. (c) The Tennessee Board of Dentistry Ethics and Jurisprudence examination.' Application instructions PH-3168 (item 2) list the accepted agencies: SRTA, CDCA, CRDTS, WREB, CITA; 'The ADEX examination is accepted. CITA examination is accepted if it was the ADEX examination that was administered.' ADA Oct-2026 summary: ADEX (CDCA-WREB-CITA), CRDTS, SRTA. This is the only route open to a new graduate with no prior licence; the restorative component must be performed on a live patient or a manikin, so it is hands-on. Fee: $310 application + state regulatory fee (Rule 0460-01-.02(1)) + regional exam fees (ADEX ~$2,995) + background check.
- **DLOSCE-only / computer-based OSCE route** (el becerisi: hayır): NOT AVAILABLE. The DLOSCE is not mentioned anywhere in T.C.A. 63-5-110/-111, Rules 0460-02-.01/.05 (Sept 2023 revision), the ADA Oct-2026 Tennessee summary or the PH-3168 application; Tennessee is not on the JCNDE 2026 DLOSCE acceptance list. Rule 0460-02-.05(1)(a) requires a 'live human patient or non-patient based ... restorative component', which a computer-based OSCE cannot supply. A DLOSCE pass reported as part of the ADEX series is simply one component of the accepted ADEX series; the manikin components would still have to be passed.
- **PGY-1 / residency in lieu of clinical exam** (el becerisi: evet): NOT AVAILABLE. Neither T.C.A. 63-5-110 nor Rule 0460-02-.05 provides a residency alternative to the clinical examination; the only references to residencies in the rules are for specialty certification (Rule 0460-02-.10). A residency would in any case still require the ADEX/CRDTS/SRTA exam for general licensure.
- **Licensure by criteria (reciprocity / credentials) - T.C.A. 63-5-110(b), Rule 0460-02-.01(3)** (el becerisi: hayır): Only for dentists who already hold a licence in another US state and who (d) have practised dentistry in another state for at least 5 years, or (e) taught in an ADA-accredited institution for 5 years, or (f) a combination; with 2 years' practice/teaching the applicant must additionally pass a regional testing agency exam or another state's exam (Rule 0460-02-.05, 0460-02-.01(3)(g)-(i)). Must also 'demonstrate intent to actively practice or teach in Tennessee by submitting proof of employment as a dentist or by submitting proof of starting a private dental practice' (Rule 0460-02-.01(3)(b)). Fee $422.50 (PH-3802). Not available to a new graduate; and the 5-year route requires a hands-on exam to have been passed elsewhere at the time of original licensure.
- **Educational limited licence / dual-degree licence / limited licence** (el becerisi: hayır): Rule 0460-02-.05 exempts 'educational limited licensure applicants and dual degree licensure applicants' (only the Ethics & Jurisprudence exam) and 'limited licensure applicants' (National Board + Jurisprudence only). These are restricted licences tied to faculty employment at a Tennessee dental school or similar institutional practice (Rule 0460-02-.02), not general practice licences; they require a Tennessee employer and so a work visa. Eligibility details were not read in full - treat as not applicable to this candidate.

## Teori-only açıklaması
Rule 0460-02-.05 EXAMINATIONS: 'All persons intending to apply for licensure as a dentist in Tennessee must successfully complete the examinations provided by this rule ... (1) The Board adopts as its licensure examination and requires, with the previously noted exceptions, successful completion of all of the following examination components as a prerequisite for licensure: (a) An examination must include a prosthetic component, a periodontal component, an endodontic component, and a live human patient or non-patient based anterior and posterior restorative component. (b) The National Board if the applicant graduated from a dental college, school or university after 1972. (c) The Tennessee Board of Dentistry Ethics and Jurisprudence examination.' Statute T.C.A. 63-5-111(a)(2) likewise lets the board accept 'the results of its own board examination or the result[s]' of regional/other-state exams; the ADA summary (updated 20 Oct 2026) states 'An examination may be conducted on a live human patient or a non-patient-[based simulation]'. There is no DLOSCE, OSCE, residency or portfolio alternative in the statute, rules or application form, so a new graduate cannot be licensed on INBDE + jurisprudence alone.

## DLOSCE notları
Tennessee does not accept the DLOSCE in any form as its clinical examination. The DLOSCE is absent from T.C.A. 63-5-110/-111, from Rules 0460-02-.01 and 0460-02-.05 (Sept 2023 revision), from the ADA 'Tennessee Laws & Rules' summary updated 20 Oct 2026 (which lists only ADEX (CDCA-WREB-CITA), CRDTS and SRTA), and from application instructions PH-3168; Tennessee is not on the JCNDE 2026 DLOSCE acceptance list. The rule requires a 'live human patient or non-patient based' restorative component, so neither an earlier standalone DLOSCE score nor a DLOSCE pass reported within the ADEX series can satisfy the clinical-exam requirement by itself; the full ADEX series (manikin components) is what the Board accepts. The Board publishes a 'Policy Statement on acceptance of the American Board of Dental Examiners, Inc. Examination (ADEX)' on its website (not read); nothing seen suggests it carves out the DLOSCE component.

## Göçmenlik / kimlik

- **citizenship_or_pr_required:** False
- **work_authorization_required:** False
- **lawful_presence_verification:** True
- **ssn_or_sin_required:** True
- **itin_or_no_ssn_alternative:** None offered. PH-3168 (Instructions for Licensure as a Dentist by Examination): 'You must write your social security number on the application for it to be complete. State law requires social security numbers on this application. TCA § 36-5-1301(a), as authorized by 42 U.S.C. § 405(c)(2)(C)(i). The number will be used to verify your identity and for any other purpose allowed by state or federal law.' No ITIN, affidavit or blank-field option is mentioned anywhere in the instructions or rules.
- **in_jurisdiction_address_required:** False
- **barrier_level:** high
- **details:** Statute T.C.A. 63-5-110(a)(2): 'The applicant shall be at least eighteen (18) years of age, a citizen of the United States or Canada or legally entitled to live within the United States and of good moral character and reputation.' Rule 0460-02-.01(1)(e): 'An applicant shall submit proof of United States or Canadian citizenship or evidence of being legally entitled to live in the United States. Such evidence may include copies of birth certificates, naturalization papers, or current visa status.' PH-3168 item 9: 'Attach proof of U.S. or Canadian citizenship or evidence of being legally entitled to live in the U.S. (e.g. copy of birth certificate, voter's registration card, U.S. passport, naturalization papers, or current visa status.)' PH-3168 item 15: 'All applicants must complete, sign and have notarized the Declaration of Citizenship form [PH-4183] and submit the documents required by the Declaration of Citizenship form' - this is the Department of Health's state-wide lawful-presence verification form used for all health-related-board licences (form content not read; it is the mechanism by which the Department verifies lawful presence for non-citizens). The application form itself asks 'Social Security Number: ___ U.S. Citizen: Yes / No'. Citizenship/PR is therefore NOT required and work authorisation is not expressly required, but (1) the applicant must prove being 'legally entitled to live within the United States' - a B-1/B-2 visitor status is 'current visa status' but it is doubtful a tourist visa establishes entitlement to LIVE in the US; this is a Board/Department judgement call; and (2) an SSN is mandatory by state law (T.C.A. 36-5-1301, child-support enforcement) with no stated alternative, and a visitor with no work authorisation cannot obtain an SSN. The combined effect is a high barrier for the candidate profile. Address: only a mailing address is required; a practice address must be reported within 30 days of obtaining one; no Tennessee residency requirement for the exam route (the criteria/reciprocity route separately requires proof of intent to practise in Tennessee).
- **citations:** T.C.A. § 63-5-110(a)(2) (quoted in ADA Tennessee summary, updated 20 Oct 2026): https://www.ada.org/~/media/Project/ADA%20Organization/ADA/ADA-org/Files/Education/Licensure%20by%20State/Tennessee_Licensure ; Rule 0460-02-.01(1)(e) (Rules of the Tennessee Board of Dentistry, Chapter 0460-02, Sept 2023 revision: https://publications.tnsosfiles.com/rules/0460/0460-02.20230919.pdf); PH-3168 Instructions for Licensure as a Dentist by Examination or Dual Degree, items 9 and 15 and SSN notice citing T.C.A. § 36-5-1301(a): https://www.tn.gov/content/dam/tn/health/documents/PH-3168.pdf ; Declaration of Citizenship form PH-4183: https://www.tn.gov/content/dam/tn/health/healthprofboards/PH-41833.pdf (not read).

## Yüz yüze adımlar
(1) Hands-on clinical exam (ADEX/CRDTS/SRTA) must be taken in person at a US test site - feasible on a visitor visa but unavoidable. (2) Criminal background check with fingerprints through IDEMIA/IdentoGO (https://tn.state.identogo.com/workflow/28TYXY). Board FAQ: 'For applicants living out of state, please note that results are typically received quicker if fingerprints are electronically scanned; therefore, if you are going to be in Tennessee anytime during the application process or live close to the Tennessee border, it may be to your benefit to schedule a fingerprint scan rather than' [mail fingerprint cards] - i.e. mailed ink cards are accepted for out-of-state applicants; whether cards taken abroad are accepted was not confirmed. (3) Tennessee Board of Dentistry Ethics and Jurisprudence examination: a 'Jurisprudence Exam' link is posted under the Applications tab of the Board website and is submitted with the application; its exact format (take-home/open-book vs proctored) was not verified. (4) Declaration of Citizenship form must be notarized; a photograph signed on the front must accompany the application; notarization can be done abroad (US consulate or local notary with apostille not required by the form as far as seen). (5) No interview requirement found. (6) CPR/BLS certification (BLS Healthcare Provider or CPR/AED for the Professional Rescuer or equivalent with AED training) - typically a hands-on skills session. (7) Application is paper, mailed with cheque/money order; an online initial-application portal is being rolled out per the website notice. Overall: one short US visit for the regional exam (and possibly fingerprint scan/CPR) could cover the in-person steps, but the SSN requirement cannot be satisfied by a visit.

## Diğer başlangıç şartları
INBDE (National Board) certificate; ADEX/CRDTS/SRTA clinical exam certificate; Tennessee Board of Dentistry Ethics and Jurisprudence examination (Rule 0460-02-.01(1)(k)); current CPR certification (BLS for Healthcare Providers, CPR/AED for Professional Rescuers or equivalent course with AED training - copy of card front and back); criminal background check (fingerprints via IdentoGO); Practitioner Profile Questionnaire (online); notarized Declaration of Citizenship form PH-4183 plus proof of citizenship or 'current visa status'; SSN on the application (mandatory per T.C.A. 36-5-1301); two letters attesting to character from dental professionals on letterhead; signed photograph; dental school transcript/diploma from accredited school; disclosure of criminal/disciplinary history; statement of physical capability; non-refundable fees. No English-language test, no liability insurance requirement found. Timelines: Board says mail renewals take 10-14 days; initial application processing times not stated (licensure decisions governed by Rule 0460-01-.04, Board staff/consultant review).

## İlk ücretler
Licensure by examination: $310 non-refundable application fee (PH-3168, 2023 revision) plus the state regulatory fee under Rule 0460-01-.02(1) (amount not read; historically $10). Licensure by criteria: $422.50 (PH-3802). Plus regional exam fees (ADEX series ~$2,995), INBDE fee, IdentoGO background-check fee (not read), CPR course, notarization. Duplicate score request fees extra.

## Lisansı sürdürme

- **renewal_period:** Biennial - licences are renewed every two years (T.C.A. 63-5-117(d) alternative system: 'licenses or renewals ... are valid for twenty-four (24) months and shall expire on the' last day of the month); CE cycle is the two calendar years (1 Jan odd year - 31 Dec even year) preceding the renewal year. Renewal can be completed online.
- **renewal_fee:** Not read on an official page in this session (Rule 0460-01-.02 sets it; reactivation of a retired licence requires 'the licensure renewal fees and state regulatory fee'). Believed to be roughly equal to the $310 application fee plus the state regulatory fee - UNVERIFIED.
- **ce_hours:** 40 clock hours per two-calendar-year cycle (T.C.A. 63-5-108(c)(1): min 24, max 60 set by Board; Rule 0460-01-.05(1)(a): 40 hours), of which at least 2 hours must pertain to prescribing of controlled substances (incl. TN Chronic Pain Guidelines); exempt during the two-year cycle of initial licensure. Random monthly audits; certificates kept and produced only on audit.
- **ce_online_ok:** Yes - 100% online is permitted for dentists. Board FAQ: 'How many continuing education hours may I get online each year? There is no limit for dentists and dental assistants but dental hygienists can have no more than 50% online or web-based.' T.C.A. 63-5-108(c)(1) applies the 50% online cap only to dental hygienists, and treats a 'live, interactive webinar' as in-person CE. No live/in-person component required for dentists.
- **cpr_required:** Yes, at every renewal while the licence is active: T.C.A. 63-5-108(c)(1) 'all applicants shall provide proof of current CPR certification by an organization approved by the board unless such requirement is waived by the board in its discretion'. Rule 0460-01-.05 defines CPR as 'successful completion of a BLS for Healthcare Providers, or CPR/AED for Professional Rescuers, or an equivalent course, which provides training for healthcare professionals in CPR and the use of an AED'. Board website: 'If your license is active in this state (not in a retired status), whether you practice or not, you are required to have C.P.R. recertification and continuing education.' Hands-on: AHA BLS Provider courses include an in-person skills session; whether an online-only course is accepted was not verified (the PH-3168 sentence 'The course must be...' was cut off). Waivers must be requested in writing and be based on conditions beyond the licensee's control (Rule 0460-01-.05(5)).
- **practice_hours_required:** None for renewal. However T.C.A. 63-5-108(c)(1) gives the Board 'discretion to require completion of continuing education courses of a type and number of hours satisfactory to the board in cases involving license renewal of dentists ... who have not actively practiced or have been retired from practice for more than two (2) years' - discretionary, not routinely applied as far as seen.
- **address_or_residency_required:** No in-state residency. Must keep the Board informed of mailing address changes ('IT'S THE LAW! If you change your mailing address, you must notify the Board's Administrative Office') and report a practice address within 30 days of obtaining one; a foreign mailing address appears permissible (not expressly addressed).
- **insurance_required:** No mandatory professional liability insurance requirement found in the statute, rules or application.
- **inactive_status:** 'Retired' licence: file the Affidavit of Retirement From Practice in Tennessee (form PH-3460) by mail; while retired no CE or CPR is required (website: CE/CPR obligations apply only if the licence is 'active ... (not in a retired status)'). Fee for retiring: none seen (not verified). No stated maximum duration; reactivation requirements escalate at 2 and 5 years. A separate 'Inactive Pro Bono Dental License' (PH-3892) exists for volunteer practice only.
- **reactivation:** Rule 0460-01-.05(6) and website FAQ: submit the Reinstatement/Reactivation Application (PH-3870), pay the licensure renewal fee and state regulatory fee, and show CE as follows: retired <2 years - one-half of the biennial CE (20 hours); retired 2 to <5 years - 24 hours of Board-approved dental CE earned in the 12 months before application, including at least one course that 'focuses on and serves as a clinical (in the mouth) refresher', and, at the Board's/consultant's discretion if competency concerns arise, an evaluation of current competency at an approved dental school; retired 5+ years - 24 hours of CE including a clinical refresher course earned in the preceding 12 months AND (per the rule text, lines partially read) an evaluation of current competency at an approved school of dentistry 'is required before reinstatement will be considered'. Reactivation CE cannot be counted toward the next biennial renewal. No new regional clinical exam is required for reactivation. Lapsed (not retired) licence: T.C.A. 63-5-117 - failure to renew within 60 days after the due date lapses the licence; reinstatement is at the Board's discretion 'upon good cause being shown' on payment of all past-due renewal fees plus a penalty (ADA summary lines 771-786).
- **remote_maintainability:** moderate
- **details:** Keeping the licence ACTIVE from abroad is feasible in principle: biennial online renewal, 40 CE hours that may be 100% online/webinar, no practice-hour requirement, no in-state address, no insurance mandate. The recurring obstacles are (a) proof of current CPR/BLS at each renewal (AHA-style courses normally include a hands-on skills check; waiver only in writing for circumstances beyond the licensee's control), and (b) the Board's discretionary power to demand extra CE from dentists who have not actively practised for more than two years. The retired status is cheap (no CE/CPR) but reactivation after 5+ years requires a dental-school competency evaluation in the US, and after 2-5 years a clinical 'in the mouth' refresher course. Renewal fee amount and the retirement fee were not verified.
