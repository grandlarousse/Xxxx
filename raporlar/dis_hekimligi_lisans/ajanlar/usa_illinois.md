# Illinois (USA) — ajan bulgusu

Düzenleyici: Illinois Department of Financial and Professional Regulation (IDFPR), Division of Professional Regulation (advised by the Illinois State Board of Dentistry) — https://idfpr.illinois.gov/profs/dentist.html

**Teori-only rota:** no · **Göçmenlik engeli:** low · **Uzaktan sürdürme:** moderate · **Güven:** medium

## Sınav yolları

- **Licensure by examination (INBDE + regional hands-on clinical exam: CDCA-WREB-CITA/ADEX, CRDTS or SRTA)** (el becerisi: evet): 225 ILCS 25/9(e): applicant must have 'passed the integrated National Board Dental Examination administered by the Joint Commission on National Dental Examinations and has successfully completed an examination conducted by one of the following regional testing services: ... CRDTS, SRTA, WREB, CDCA, or CITA'. 68 Ill. Adm. Code 1220.120(a) (amended eff. 10 Sept 2024): 'The Division shall accept the following examinations for licensure if administered and passed in their entirety (which includes passage of the restorative, periodontal, prosthodontic and endodontic portions of the examination): 1) CDCA-WREB-CITA Examination ...; 2) CRDTS Examination ...; or 3) SRTA Examination'. Scores must be sent directly by the testing agency; exam must have been completed within the 5 years before application if never licensed elsewhere (1220.120(c),(d)). Other requirements: age 21, good moral character, graduation from a CODA-accredited dental school (Sec. 9(b),(c)(1)), fee $250 (1220.415(a)(1)). This is the ONLY route for a new graduate; every accepted exam has manikin hand-skill components.
- **DLOSCE / OSCE-only** (el becerisi: hayır): NOT available. Neither 225 ILCS 25/9(e) nor 68 Ill. Adm. Code 1220.120 mentions the DLOSCE or any OSCE; the rule requires one of the three regional exams passed 'in their entirety' including the restorative, periodontal, prosthodontic and endodontic (manikin) portions. The ADA Licensure-by-State summary for Illinois (updated 24 Oct 2025) lists only ADEX (CDCA-WREB-CITA), CRDTS and SRTA as accepted initial licensure exams.
- **PGY-1 / residency in lieu of clinical exam** (el becerisi: evet): NOT available as a licensure route. The Act contains no provision substituting a residency for the regional exam. The only residency-related licences are the 'temporary training license' (for persons accepted into a CODA-accredited specialty/residency program; valid only for the program) and a 'pre-license practice allowance' for residents who have applied for a general licence; both still require the general-licence exam requirements for full licensure (225 ILCS 25/11).
- **Licensure by credentials (licensed in another state)** (el becerisi: evet): 225 ILCS 25/19: applicant 'lawfully licensed to practice dentistry ... in another state or territory' whose licensing requirements were 'substantially equivalent' and 'who has been lawfully engaged in the practice of dentistry ... for at least 2 years immediately preceding the filing of his or her application'. 'Substantially equivalent' is defined as graduation from an ADA-accredited school, passing the National Board, 'and successfully completed an examination conducted by a regional testing service'. Fee $750 (1220.415(a)(5)). Not usable by a new graduate and still presupposes a hands-on regional exam.
- **Restricted faculty licence / temporary training licence / visiting-dentist temporary permit** (el becerisi: hayır): Limited licences tied to employment at an Illinois dental school, enrolment in an Illinois CODA residency, or short-term free dental care events. None is a general licence, none can be held indefinitely from abroad, and the faculty/training licences require a US employer/program (and thus a visa), so they do not fit the candidate.

## Teori-only açıklaması
A hands-on regional clinical exam is unavoidable. 225 ILCS 25/9(e) (quoted on the ADA Illinois Licensure page, updated 24 Oct 2025): the applicant must 'present satisfactory evidence that the applicant has passed the integrated National Board Dental Examination ... and has successfully completed an examination conducted by one of the following regional testing services: the Central Regional Dental Testing Service, Inc. (CRDTS), the Southern Regional Testing Agency, Inc. (SRTA), the Western Regional Examining Board (WREB), the Commission on Dental Competency Assessments (CDCA), or the Council of Interstate Testing Agencies (CITA).' 68 Ill. Adm. Code 1220.120(a) (amended 48 Ill. Reg. 14138, eff. 10 Sept 2024): 'The Division shall accept the following examinations for licensure if administered and passed in their entirety (which includes passage of the restorative, periodontal, prosthodontic and endodontic portions of the examination): 1) CDCA-WREB-CITA Examination, with a passing score established by the testing entity; 2) CRDTS Examination ...; or 3) SRTA Examination ...'. The restorative, periodontal, prosthodontic and endodontic portions are the manikin/hand-skill parts of ADEX/CRDTS/SRTA, so the rule expressly requires the hands-on components. No DLOSCE, OSCE, residency, portfolio or state-written-exam alternative exists in the Act or rules; there is no jurisprudence exam either (nothing in the Act, rules or ADA summary).

## DLOSCE notları
Illinois does not accept the DLOSCE in any form: the DLOSCE is not mentioned in 225 ILCS 25/9(e) or 68 Ill. Adm. Code 1220.120, and the ADA summary lists only ADEX (CDCA-WREB-CITA), CRDTS and SRTA. Because 1220.120(a) requires the accepted exam to be 'administered and passed in their entirety (which includes passage of the restorative, periodontal, prosthodontic and endodontic portions)', an ADEX-reported pass of the DLOSCE component alone would not satisfy Illinois; the full ADEX series (five manikin hand-skill exams plus the DLOSCE) must be passed. Earlier standalone DLOSCE scores are irrelevant to Illinois licensure.

## Yüz yüze adımlar
(1) The regional clinical exam (ADEX via CDCA-WREB-CITA, CRDTS or SRTA) must be taken in person at a US test site on manikins; this is the only unavoidable physical-presence step and can be done on a short visit (visa suitability is the testing agency's/consulate's matter, not researched here). (2) Application: new dental applications are filed through IDFPR's online CORE portal (IDFPR dentist page: 'New applications for Dental professions are now available in CORE'); transcripts/education certification, INBDE results (JCNDE) and regional exam scores must be sent directly to IDFPR by the school/testing agencies, all of which can be arranged from abroad. (3) No jurisprudence exam exists for Illinois dentists (none in the Act, rules or ADA summary). (4) No fingerprint-based criminal background check was found in the Dental Practice Act, Part 1220 or on the IDFPR dentist page (nothing matching 'fingerprint'); the application relies on self-disclosure questions. (5) The SSN/ITIN affidavit is signed under penalty of perjury; no notarisation block appears on the form. (6) Applicants have 3 years from the date of application to complete the process or the application is denied and the fee forfeited (225 ILCS 25/19, and generally). Overall, apart from the hands-on exam, everything can be completed from abroad.

## Diğer başlangıç şartları
Age 21 and good moral character (225 ILCS 25/9(b)); graduation from a CODA-accredited dental school (Sec. 9(c)(1), satisfied by Yeditepe post-Feb 2024 per the known facts); INBDE passed with certificate mailed to IDFPR by JCNDE (1220.110(d)); regional exam (CDCA-WREB-CITA/ADEX, CRDTS or SRTA) passed in its entirety within the 5 years preceding application, scores sent directly by the agency (1220.120); application fee $250; no jurisprudence exam; no English-language test for CODA graduates; no CPR/BLS for initial issuance (BLS is a renewal requirement under Sec. 16); no liability-insurance requirement; SSN/ITIN or the IDFPR Social Security/ITIN affidavit. Timeline: an initial licence issued during an open renewal period takes the next expiration date (Sec. 16); 3 years to complete the application.

## İlk ücretler
Application for initial dental licence by examination: $250 (68 Ill. Adm. Code 1220.415(a)(1)); licensure by credentials from another jurisdiction: $750 (1220.415(a)(5)); dental specialist $300; sedation permit $300; certification of record $20. Plus third-party costs: INBDE (JCNDE) and the full ADEX series (about $2,995 per the known facts) or CRDTS/SRTA fees, and travel to a US exam site. First 3-year renewal: $300 but no CE required for the first renewal after original issuance (1220.440(a)(4)).

## Lisansı sürdürme

- **renewal_period:** 3 years (225 ILCS 25/16: 'The renewal period for each license issued under this Act shall be 3 years'); dental licences expire 30 September of the renewal year (the prerenewal period is 'the 36 months preceding September 30 of the year of the renewal', 1220.440(a)(3)); last cycle renewed in 2024, so next expiry 30 Sept 2027. May renew within 90 days after expiry with a late fee, retroactive to the expiration date (Sec. 16).
- **renewal_fee:** $300 per 3-year cycle ($100 per year) (68 Ill. Adm. Code 1220.415(b)(1)); restoration of a lapsed (not inactive) licence: $50 plus all lapsed renewal fees (1220.415(c)(1)).
- **ce_hours:** 48 hours per 3-year prerenewal period (225 ILCS 25/16.1; 1220.440(a)(1)); none required for the first renewal after original licensure (1220.440(a)(4)); BLS training counts for no more than 4 hours (Sec. 16). CE used for another state may be applied to Illinois (1220.440(a)(7)).
- **ce_online_ok:** Not 100% self-study: 1220.440(b)(3) 'CE credit may be earned for completion of an individual study course (correspondence, audio or video course) sponsored by an approved sponsor ... No more than 50% of the required CE credit hours during a prerenewal period may be acquired through correspondence courses.' Live, moderated online formats are allowed: (b)(4) 'CE credit may be earned from teleconferencing courses with a moderator present given by an Illinois approved sponsor', so the remaining hours can plausibly be done via live webinars from approved sponsors (ADA CERP/AGD PACE providers etc. are approved sponsors) without travelling; no in-person attendance is mandated. Specific mandated topics applying to all IDFPR CE professions (e.g. sexual-harassment-prevention and implicit-bias training) are reported but were not verified in this research.
- **cpr_required:** Yes at renewal: 225 ILCS 25/16: 'A dentist or dental hygienist shall provide proof of current Basic Life Support (BLS) certification intended for health care providers at the time of renewal as provided by rule' (exemption by rule only for physical disability). Health-care-provider BLS courses (e.g. AHA) normally include a hands-on skills session; these can be obtained abroad through international training centres. Whether a fully online BLS card is accepted was not verified.
- **practice_hours_required:** None for ordinary renewal of an active licence. Practice only becomes relevant for restoration after more than 5 years expired/inactive: Sec. 16 requires 'proof acceptable to the Department of taking continuing education and of his fitness to have the license restored, including sworn evidence certifying to active practice in another jurisdiction', and if the person 'has not maintained an active practice satisfactory to the department', the Department 'may require the person to complete a period of evaluated clinical experience and may require successful completion of a practical examination'.
- **address_or_residency_required:** No Illinois address or residency required; 1220.440(a)(6): 'Dentists or dental hygienist licensed in Illinois but residing in other states shall comply with the CE requirements set forth in this Section'. Only an address of record (any country) is needed.
- **insurance_required:** No mandatory professional liability insurance found in the Dental Practice Act, Part 1220 or on the IDFPR dentist page.
- **inactive_status:** 225 ILCS 25/11(e): 'Any person who holds one of the licenses ... may elect, upon payment of the required fee, to place his or her license on an inactive status and shall, subject to the rules of the Department, be excused from the payment of renewal fees until he or she notifies the Department in writing of his or her desire to resume active status.' No renewal fee or CE while inactive; no stated maximum duration; may not practise in Illinois while inactive. Restoration from inactive within 5 years of expiry: pay the current renewal fee ($300) and show compliance with current CE requirements (Sec. 16; Sec. 11(e)). Inactive for more than 5 years triggers the stricter restoration process below.
- **reactivation:** Within 5 years of expiration/inactive: pay the required fee and show 'proof of compliance with current continuing education requirements' (Sec. 16); no new clinical exam. Expired or inactive for more than 5 years: apply for restoration, file 'proof acceptable to the Department of taking continuing education and of his fitness to have the license restored, including sworn evidence certifying to active practice in another jurisdiction, and ... paying the required restoration fee'; if the Department finds the person has not maintained an active practice satisfactory to it, it determines fitness 'by an evaluation process established by rule' and 'may require the person to complete a period of evaluated clinical experience and may require successful completion of a practical examination'. Lapsed-licence restoration fee $50 plus lapsed renewal fees. Military/other-service exceptions exist (Sec. 16).
- **remote_maintainability:** moderate
- **details:** An Illinois dental licence can be kept ACTIVE indefinitely from abroad: $300 every 3 years, 48 CE hours (up to 50% correspondence/self-study, the rest via live moderated online courses from approved sponsors), a current health-care-provider BLS card (hands-on skills session, obtainable abroad), no practice-hour requirement, no in-state address, no insurance, and the IDFPR online renewal portal. The awkward elements are the hands-on BLS card and the 50% cap on correspondence CE. Inactive status is cheap (no renewal fee, no CE) but, if it lasts more than 5 years, restoration requires sworn evidence of active practice elsewhere and may require evaluated clinical experience or a practical exam, so staying active (or restoring before the 5-year mark) is the safer strategy.
