## Sınav yolları

- **Examination route: INBDE + regional hands-on clinical exam (ADEX via CDCA-WREB-CITA, CRDTS, SRTA) + WV Dental Law Examination** (el becerisi: evet): W. Va. Code §30-4-8(a)(4): applicant must have 'passed a national board examination as given by the Joint Commission on National Dental Examinations and a clinical examination administered by the Commission on Dental Competency Assessments, the Central Regional Dental Testing Service, the Council of Interstate Testing Agencies, the Southern Regional Testing Agency, or the Western Regional Examining Board, or the successor to any of those entities, which demonstrates competency, and passed each individual component with no compensatory scoring in: (A) Endodontics, including access opening of a posterior tooth and access, canal instrumentation, and obturation of an anterior tooth; (B) Fixed prosthodontics, including an anterior crown preparation and two posterior crown preparations involving a fixed partial denture factor; (C) Periodontics, including scaling and root planing; and (D) Restorative, including a class II amalgam or composite preparation and restoration and a class III composite preparation and restoration.' Rule 5CSR1-6.1 designates JCNDE for the written exam and 'any state and/or regional clinical examination'. Rule 5CSR9-3 adds: transcript sent directly from school, JCNDE scores sent directly, regional/state clinical board results sent directly, pass the West Virginia Dental Law Examination, letters of good standing from every health-care licence ever held, two professional letters of recommendation (not family). ADA summary (updated 20 Oct 2026) lists accepted exams as ADEX (CDCA-WREB-CITA), CRDTS, SRTA. The named components are manikin/patient hand-skill procedures, so this route is hands-on.
- **Licensure of a dentist from out of state (licensure by credentials, Rule 5CSR9-4)** (el becerisi: evet): Not available to a new graduate with no other licence in practice, and in any event Rule 5CSR9-4.5 still requires 'evidence of the applicant's successful completion of an examination by a regional and/or state clinical board approved by the Board sent directly from the regional or state board', plus JCNDE (4.4), the WV Dental Law Examination (4.6), good-standing letters and two professional references. No practice-years-in-lieu-of-exam provision appears.
- **DLOSCE-only / OSCE-only route** (el becerisi: hayır): NOT AVAILABLE. §30-4-8(a)(4) requires a clinical examination with passed individual components in endodontics, fixed prosthodontics, periodontal scaling/root planing and restorative procedures; a computer-based OSCE cannot satisfy these. Neither the statute nor Rules 5CSR1-6 / 5CSR9-3 mention DLOSCE or any OSCE. West Virginia is not on the JCNDE 2026 list of DLOSCE-accepting states. Only sub-paragraph (E) gives discretion: 'The board may consider clinical examinations taken prior to July 1, 2019, or individual state clinical examinations as equivalent which demonstrates competency' - this covers other states' hands-on exams, not a theory-only exam.
- **PGY-1 / residency in lieu of clinical exam** (el becerisi: evet): NOT AVAILABLE as a licensure pathway. §30-4-8 contains no residency alternative; §30-4-14 only creates a 'Dental intern, resident, or teaching permit' (a limited permit to work in a programme, not a licence pathway). A residency would itself require a US visa in any case.
- **Portfolio / state-specific clinical exam** (el becerisi: evet): No portfolio pathway exists. West Virginia does not administer its own clinical exam; §30-4-8(a)(4)(E) merely lets the board treat another state's own clinical examination as equivalent (still hands-on).

## Teori-only açıklaması
W. Va. Code §30-4-8(a)(4) (quoted on the ADA 'West Virginia Laws & Rules' page updated 20 October 2026) requires the applicant to have 'passed a national board examination as given by the Joint Commission on National Dental Examinations and a clinical examination administered by the Commission on Dental Competency Assessments, the Central Regional Dental Testing Service, the Council of Interstate Testing Agencies, the Southern Regional Testing Agency, or the Western Regional Examining Board, or the successor to any of those entities, which demonstrates competency, and passed each individual component with no compensatory scoring in: (A) Endodontics, including access opening of a posterior tooth and access, canal instrumentation, and obturation of an anterior tooth; (B) Fixed prosthodontics, including an anterior crown preparation and two posterior crown preparations involving a fixed partial denture factor; (C) Periodontics, including scaling and root planing; and (D) Restorative, including a class II amalgam or composite preparation and restoration and a class III composite preparation and restoration.' Rule 5CSR9-3.5: 'Each applicant shall submit evidence of the applicant's successful completion of an examination by a regional and/or state clinical board approved by the Board sent directly from the regional or state board to the Board's office.' Each listed component is a manikin/patient procedure, so a hands-on regional exam is unavoidable; there is no DLOSCE, OSCE, residency or portfolio alternative in the statute or rules.

## DLOSCE notları
West Virginia does not accept the DLOSCE (standalone or ADEX-integrated) as the clinical examination; it is not on the JCNDE 2026 DLOSCE acceptance list and the word 'DLOSCE'/'OSCE' does not appear in §30-4-8, Rule 5CSR1-6 or Rule 5CSR9. The statute names specific hand-skill components (endo, fixed prosth, perio SRP, restorative) that must each be passed with no compensatory scoring, which only the manikin/patient parts of ADEX/CRDTS/SRTA deliver. A DLOSCE pass reported within an ADEX series would therefore be irrelevant to WV eligibility: the board looks at the hand-skill component results. No board statement on ADEX-integrated DLOSCE scores was located (board website unreachable on 2 Nov 2026).

## Yüz yüze adımlar
(1) The hands-on regional clinical examination (ADEX/CRDTS/SRTA) must be taken in person at a US exam site - this is the unavoidable physical-presence step and the one that defeats the candidate's goal. (2) West Virginia Dental Law Examination (Rule 5CSR9-3.6): format (online/open-book vs in-office) could not be verified because the board website was down; no rule text requires it to be sat in person. (3) No fingerprinting or criminal-background-check step appears in §30-4-8 or Rule 5CSR9-3 (only a statutory bar for certain convictions under §30-1-24, presumably self-disclosed on the application); unverified at form level. (4) Supporting documents must be sent directly to the board by third parties: official transcript from the school, JCNDE (INBDE) score report, regional-board results, good-standing letters from any licensing body, and two professional letters of recommendation 'by way of mail or facsimile from the persons writing them' - all can be arranged from abroad. (5) No interview requirement found. (6) Notarisation requirement on the application form unknown (form not accessible).

## Lisansı sürdürme

- **renewal_period:** W. Va. Code §30-4-12(a): 'All persons regulated by this article shall annually or biannually, renew his or her board authorization'; the Board's actual dentist cycle (understood to be annual) and expiry date could not be confirmed because the board website was unreachable. CE reporting is biennial: Rule 5CSR11-3.4.5 - 'submit to the Board by February 1, 2026, and every two years thereafter documentation of satisfactory completion of the required hours'.
- **renewal_fee:** Set by Board fee schedule (§30-4-12(b) requires a renewal fee plus a late fee); amount not verified.
- **ce_hours:** 35 hours biennially for dentists (Rule 5CSR11-3.1.1), including at least 2 hours on drug diversion / oral effects of tobacco / oral effects of substance abuse (3.5.3), 2 hours infection control (3.5.4), current BLS/CPR certification each period (3.5.5, may be counted as CE). Initial licensees: 3 extra hours on drug diversion/opioid prescribing within one year of licensure (3.2.1). One hour = 50 minutes; no credit for identical courses in the same period.
- **ce_online_ok:** Mostly. Rule 5CSR11-3.7: 'Educational and scientific courses - 100% of the requirement may be obtained by Educational and scientific courses given by permanent or Board approved biennial sponsors' (format not restricted; live webinars from approved sponsors such as ADA/AGD/dental schools appear to qualify, but the rule does not expressly say 'online'); 'Supervised self-instruction - 80% of the requirement may be obtained in this category' (graded audio/video, correspondence and computer courses with a sponsor-graded test); 'Non-supervised self-instruction - A maximum of 10%' (journals, home study, non-graded audio/video). So at least 80% can clearly be done online from abroad; the balance depends on whether an approved sponsor's online course counts as an 'educational and scientific course'. Separate hard limit: Rule 3.5.6 - 'The Board may not accept courses or re-certifications completed on the Internet for basic life support courses unless the course includes a skills examination with a certified instructor.'
- **cpr_required:** Yes, every CE period: current health-care-provider BLS certification covering adult/child/infant resuscitation, two-rescuer, barrier/bag ventilation, FBAO and AED, via AHA, American Red Cross 'or an equivalent course' (3.5.5). Online-only BLS is NOT accepted - an in-person skills examination with a certified instructor is required (3.5.6). Can be done abroad through an AHA/Red Cross international training centre or an 'equivalent course'.
- **practice_hours_required:** None found in W. Va. Code 30-4 or Rules 5CSR9/5CSR11 for renewal.
- **address_or_residency_required:** None found; no in-state address or residency requirement appears in the statute or rules read.
- **insurance_required:** No mandatory professional liability insurance requirement appears in the statute or rules read.
- **inactive_status:** Unclear. W. Va. Code Chapter 30 Article 4 has no inactive/retired licence section (§30-4-15 provides only a 'special volunteer dentist' licence for indigent care). Whether the Board offers an inactive status by rule or on the renewal form could not be verified (board website down).
- **reactivation:** W. Va. Code §30-4-17(b): 'A licensee who does not complete annual renewal, as specified herein and by the board by rule, and whose license has lapsed for one year or longer, shall make application for reinstatement as specified by the board by rule.' §30-4-17(c): the board 'at its discretion and for cause, may require an applicant for reinstatement to undergo a physical and/or mental evaluation'. Rule-level reinstatement conditions (back fees, CE make-up, any re-examination after long lapse) not verified. Rule 5CSR11-3.4.4.b lets the Board allow a licensee to make up a CE deficiency and assess a late fee.
- **remote_maintainability:** moderate
- **details:** If licensed, the licence can in principle be kept active from abroad: no practice hours, no in-state address, no insurance mandate, CE largely online (80% graded self-instruction explicitly permitted). The awkward items are a hands-on BLS skills session every two years, an unverified annual renewal fee/form, random CE audits (records kept 6 years), and the absence of a confirmed inactive category. Statute §30-4-12(a) lets the board choose annual or biennial renewal; the dentist cycle appears to be annual. All maintenance detail from the Board's own renewal pages remains unverified because www.wvdentalboard.org was unreachable on 2 Nov 2026.
