# Texas (USA) — ajan bulgusu

Düzenleyici: Texas State Board of Dental Examiners (TSBDE) — https://tsbde.texas.gov/

**Teori-only rota:** no · **Göçmenlik engeli:** medium · **Uzaktan sürdürme:** easy · **Güven:** medium

## Sınav yolları

- **Licensure by Examination (22 TAC §101.2(a); Tex. Occ. Code §§256.002-256.003)** (el becerisi: evet): For graduates of a CODA-accredited DDS/DMD programme: (1) CODA degree; (2) pass the JCNDE examination (INBDE or NBDE I+II); (3) 'Has taken and passed the appropriate general dentistry live patient or hands-on simulation clinical examination administered by a regional examining board designated by the Board.' Designated boards (Rule §101.2(c)): CDCA-WREB-CITA (ADEX) from 1 Aug 2022 and CRDTS-SRTA from 6 Jan 2025; results accepted for 7 years; exams taken after 1 Jan 2019 must include passing the periodontics and prosthodontics sections (Rule 101.2(e); 2026 application: 'required to have successfully passed all examination components including Periodontics and Prosthodontics'). Plus general qualifications of Rule 101.1(c): age 21, professional fitness, current BLS course (hands-on skills demonstration), TSBDE jurisprudence assessment within 1 year, fees, fingerprints, HHSC-approved human trafficking prevention course, NPDB self-query. TSBDE electronically validates ADEX and CRDTS-SRTA score reports.
- **Licensure by Credentials (22 TAC §101.3; Tex. Occ. Code §256.101) - fee $2,945** (el becerisi: evet): Not available to a new graduate: requires a current licence in good standing in another US state/DC/territory 'provided that such licensure followed successful completion of a general dentistry live patient or hands-on simulation clinical examination administered by another state or regional examining board', plus practice for 3 of the 5 years preceding application (or 5 years as a CODA dental educator), JCNDE exam, endorsement by the current board, background checks and 12 h CE within 12 months. A Temporary Licensure by Credentials ($895) exists for non-profit employers and requires proof of Texas residency - also not applicable.
- **Foreign (non-CODA) graduate route (22 TAC §101.2(b); Occ. Code §256.002(a)(2)(B)) - fee $360** (el becerisi: evet): Not relevant to a CODA graduate (Yeditepe is CODA-accredited since 1 Feb 2024). Non-CODA graduates must complete a 2-year CODA-accredited ADA specialty programme and still pass the JCNDE exam AND a regional live-patient/hands-on simulation clinical exam.
- **DLOSCE / OSCE-only pathway** (el becerisi: hayır): DOES NOT EXIST in Texas. Neither the Dental Practice Act, 22 TAC Chapter 101, the ADA Texas summary (updated 20 Oct 2026), the TSBDE licensure-by-exam web page nor the 2026 application form mention the DLOSCE or any OSCE; the rule text requires a 'live patient or hands-on simulation clinical examination'.
- **PGY-1 / residency in lieu of clinical exam** (el becerisi: hayır): DOES NOT EXIST in Texas rules; no residency-based waiver of the regional clinical examination is provided (only specialty training is relevant, and only to make non-CODA graduates eligible to sit the regional exam).

## Teori-only açıklaması
22 TAC §101.2(a) (Licensure by Examination): 'an applicant for licensure by examination who is a graduate of an accredited school must present proof that the applicant: (1) Has graduated and received either the "DDS" or "DMD" degree from a dental school accredited by [CODA]; (2) Has taken and passed the examination for dentists given by the American Dental Association Joint Commission on National Dental Examinations; and (3) Has taken and passed the appropriate general dentistry live patient or hands-on simulation clinical examination administered by a regional examining board designated by the Board.' Rule §101.2(c): designated regional boards are CDCA-WREB-CITA (ADEX, 1 Aug 2022) and CRDTS-SRTA (6 Jan 2025); results accepted for seven years. Tex. Occ. Code §256.003(c): 'The board shall contract with one or more independent or regional testing services for any required clinical examination.' The 2026 application further states: 'All applicants who have taken a regional clinical examination after January 1, 2019 are required to have successfully passed all examination components including Periodontics and Prosthodontics.' Licensure by credentials (§101.3) likewise requires that the out-of-state licence 'followed successful completion of a general dentistry live patient or hands-on simulation clinical examination'. There is no OSCE, DLOSCE, portfolio or residency alternative, so a hands-on regional examination (ADEX via CDCA-WREB-CITA, or CRDTS-SRTA) is unavoidable.

## DLOSCE notları
Texas does not accept the DLOSCE (standalone or ADEX-integrated) in fulfilment of its clinical examination requirement. The DLOSCE/OSCE is not mentioned in Occ. Code ch. 256, 22 TAC ch. 101, the ADA 'Texas Laws & Rules' summary (updated 20 Oct 2026), the TSBDE licensure-by-exam page or the 2026 application. Rule 101.2(a)(3) expressly requires a 'live patient or hands-on simulation clinical examination'. Because TSBDE validates ADEX score reports electronically and requires that 'all examination components including Periodontics and Prosthodontics' be passed for exams taken after 1 Jan 2019, a DLOSCE pass reported as one component of the ADEX series would not substitute for the manikin hand-skill components; the candidate would need to pass the full ADEX (or CRDTS-SRTA) series. Earlier standalone DLOSCE scores have no standing in Texas.

- **itin_or_no_ssn_alternative:** None published. The 2026 TSBDE Dental Licensure Application has a field 'Social Security #*' and the asterisk footnote reads: '* Pursuant to Sec. 59.001 of the Dental Practice Act, the social security number of an applicant for or holder of a license ... that is provided to the licensing agency is confidential and not subject to disclosure under Chapter 552, Government Code.' No ITIN option, 'no SSN' affidavit or 'leave blank if none' instruction appears on the form, the online-application page or the rules. (Texas Family Code §231.302(c) generally requires licensing agencies to collect applicants' SSNs for child-support enforcement - not read in this session.) Whether TSBDE will process an application from a non-resident alien with no SSN must be confirmed with the Board (open question).
- **in_jurisdiction_address_required:** False
- **barrier_level:** medium
- **details:** Statutory minimum qualifications (Occ. Code §256.002): age 21, CODA graduation (or non-CODA + 2-yr CODA specialty), application fee, passing the examination. Rule 101.1(c) adds professional fitness, BLS, jurisprudence, fees, fingerprints, human-trafficking course and NPDB self-query. Nothing in the statute, rules, ADA summary, application form or TSBDE web page requires US citizenship, permanent residence, work authorisation, lawful presence/SAVE verification or a visa/I-94 copy; Texas has no general lawful-presence statute for professional licences that was found to apply to TSBDE. Identity proof is 'Copy of driver's license, naturalization papers, or passport', and the TSBDE page defines an acceptable passport to include '(ii) an unexpired passport issued by the government of another [country]', so a Turkish passport suffices. A Texas address is not required: the form asks only for a 'Mailing Address'; licensees must notify a change of address within 60 days; the rules expressly contemplate licensees 'who reside outside of the United States' (22 TAC §104.1(1)(D); Occ. Code §257.004(c)). The one real obstacle is the apparently mandatory SSN field with no published alternative; also the ADEX/CRDTS hands-on exam must be taken in the US (visitor status is sufficient for examination purposes).

## Yüz yüze adımlar
(1) Regional clinical exam (ADEX via CDCA-WREB-CITA or CRDTS-SRTA): in-person, hands-on, in the US - unavoidable. (2) Fingerprinting: 'Fingerprint Session is required for all methods of licensure. Once the Texas State Board of Dental Examiners is in receipt of your application, you will be notified via email of the agency's service code. This code is required in order for you to schedule a fingerprint session with IdentoGo.' This is a Texas IdentoGO (Live Scan) session; whether IdentoGO's out-of-state/overseas mailed fingerprint-card option is available to TSBDE applicants was not verified - plan to do it during a US visit after the application is received. (3) Jurisprudence assessment: 'administered by the Board or an entity designated by the Board', valid 1 year before application and repeated every 4 years; TSBDE lists it as a separate 'Jurisprudence Assessment' item; format (reported as an online open-book assessment) was not verified in this session. (4) Notarisation: the paper application carries a 'Notary Signature' block and any disclosure must be by 'NOTARIZED affidavit'; the current process is an online application with uploads, with sealed transcripts and NPDB self-query mailed to Austin. (5) BLS/CPR certificate 'must include hands on demonstration of skills' - obtainable in Turkey from an AHA-affiliated centre or during the US visit. (6) No interview. (7) After approval the licence is issued in 'Expired' status; the applicant pays a prorated initial renewal online to activate it (approval letter authorises practice for 30 days). Processing: 'Allow four (4) weeks from the date the required documentation was mailed to our office before checking your status.' Realistically one US trip for the exam plus a second (or an extended one) for fingerprinting; everything else can be done from abroad.

## Diğer başlangıç şartları
Age 21+; CODA DDS/DMD transcript in sealed envelope; INBDE (TSBDE has electronic access - ask ADA DTS to release scores to Texas); regional clinical exam within 7 years with all components incl. periodontics/prosthodontics passed; TSBDE Jurisprudence Assessment (dentists without sedation) within 1 year before application; current BLS/CPR with hands-on skills demonstration; HHSC-approved human trafficking prevention course (online); NPDB self-query report in sealed envelope (valid 60 days); 2x2 passport photo; copy of passport; fingerprint background check (IdentoGO); verification of any prior licences/registrations; professional fitness/disclosure questions (notarised affidavit if disclosing); application and initial prorated renewal fee. No English test for CODA graduates; no liability insurance requirement found. First licence valid 18-30 months, then annual renewal.

## Lisansı sürdürme

- **renewal_period:** Annual renewal (22 TAC §104.3(a): 'Each licensee and registrant shall attest during the annual renewal process'); first licence valid 18-30 months; CE requirement is counted per biennial period (24 h per 2 years).
- **renewal_fee:** Not captured (fee table in 22 TAC §102.1 is an attached graphic; see https://tsbde.texas.gov/licensing/fee-schedule/). Late: expired <=90 days = 1.5x renewal fee; >90 days <1 year = 2x; >=1 year cannot renew (Occ. Code §257.002).
- **ce_hours:** 24 hours per biennial renewal period (22 TAC §104.1): >=16 h technical/scientific clinical; <=8 h risk management; HHSC human trafficking prevention course each period (within the 24 h); 4 h opioid/pain-management per biennium for dentists 'whose practice includes direct patient care'; jurisprudence assessment every 4 years (in addition to the 24 h); CE must come from providers in §104.2 (ADA CERP, AGD PACE etc.). CPR/OSHA/practice-finance hours do not count.
- **ce_online_ok:** Yes for a licensee living abroad. Normally only 'Up to 8 hours of coursework may be self-study' (§104.1(2)(D)), but §104.1(1)(D): 'A licensee who resides outside of the United States may, without prior approval of the Licensing Committee, complete all required hours of coursework by self-study' (correspondence, video, audio, reading courses from §104.2 providers); on audit the licensee 'must be able to demonstrate residence outside of the United States for all periods of time for which self-study hours were submitted'. No live/in-person component for foreign residents.
- **cpr_required:** At renewal a dentist must attest completion of a current CPR course 'that includes a demonstration of skills and a written evaluation' (hands-on) - Occ. Code §257.004(a). EXEMPTION §257.004(c): a dentist 'who resides in a country other than the United States on the renewal date of the person's license and has not practiced dentistry ... in the United States during the license period preceding the renewal date is exempt ... if the person submits proof of foreign residence with the person's renewal application.'
- **practice_hours_required:** None to renew or keep an active licence.
- **address_or_residency_required:** None. Mailing address only; notify change of address within 60 days (TSBDE page); custodian-of-records designation form at renewal. Rules explicitly contemplate licensees residing outside the US.
- **insurance_required:** No mandatory professional liability insurance found in the Dental Practice Act, Board rules or renewal instructions.
- **inactive_status:** Retired status (22 TAC §101.7; Occ. Code §256.102): holder of a valid, current licence applies on a Board form before the licence expires; denied if complaints/discipline pending. Retired dentists may practise only volunteer charity care (with Board letter of authorization, 6 h CE/yr if doing so). Retired-status fee not captured (see fee schedule). No maximum duration stated. Occ. Code §257.002(d-2) also lets a licence expired >=1 year be renewed onto retired status (charity care only) without re-examination.
- **reactivation:** Reinstatement of a retired licence (§101.7(b)): application + fees ($214 application fee per 2020 form), verification of licensure/discipline from all boards, Texas jurisprudence assessment within 1 year, current BLS course, fingerprints again, and CE: if practising elsewhere or practised within the preceding 2 years - 12 h CE within 12 months; if NOT actively practised for 2+ years - '24 hours of continuing education, of which a minimum of 12 hours must be clinical (hands-on)' within the 12 months before application. No new clinical examination. Lapse: a licence expired 1 year or more cannot be renewed; a new licence must be obtained 'by complying with the requirements and procedures, including the examination requirements, for obtaining an original license' (Occ. Code §257.002(d)) unless licensed and practising in another state for 2 years (d-1, reexamination waived, fee 2x renewal) or renewed onto retired/charity status (d-2).
- **remote_maintainability:** easy
- **details:** Texas is unusually accommodating for licensees living abroad: annual online renewal with fee; 24 h CE per 2 years that a foreign resident may complete 100% by self-study without prior approval; CPR exempt for foreign residents who did not practise in the US; jurisprudence assessment every 4 years (online, unverified); no practice hours, no Texas address, no insurance. Key risk: never let the licence lapse >=1 year (that forces a new application incl. the hands-on exam); if the licence is not needed, convert to retired status before expiry and reinstate later with CE (12 h clinical hands-on if >2 years without practice), jurisprudence, BLS and fingerprints but no clinical exam.

## Açık sorular
1) SSN: the application has a mandatory-looking 'Social Security #*' field with no published ITIN/'no SSN' alternative - TSBDE must be asked whether a non-resident alien without an SSN can apply (Texas Family Code §231.302 SSN-collection duty not read). 2) Exact application fee for licensure by examination, the annual renewal fee and the retired-status fee were not captured (fee table is a graphic on the TSBDE fee schedule page). 3) Jurisprudence Assessment delivery format (online/remote vs proctored) and fee not verified. 4) Whether IdentoGO/TSBDE accept mailed fingerprint cards from abroad instead of a Texas IdentoGO session. 5) Whether a foreign (Turkish) notary is acceptable for the affidavit, or a US consular notarisation is needed. 6) Texas has no DLOSCE provision at all, so no change is expected from the ADEX-DLOSCE integration; confirmed that the full hands-on regional exam is required - the candidate's 'no hands-on exam' criterion cannot be met in Texas.
