# Alaska (USA) — ajan bulgusu

Düzenleyici: Board of Dental Examiners of Alaska (Alaska Department of Commerce, Community & Economic Development, Division of Corporations, Business and Professional Licensing) — https://www.commerce.alaska.gov/web/cbpl/ProfessionalLicensing/BoardofDentalExaminers.aspx

**Teori-only rota:** no · **Göçmenlik engeli:** low · **Uzaktan sürdürme:** moderate · **Güven:** medium

## Sınav yolları

- **Licensure by Examination Level III (new graduate, licensed 0-90 days) via WREB-equivalent regional clinical exam: ADEX (CDCA-WREB-CITA) or CRDTS+SRTA** (el becerisi: evet): 12 AAC 28.940(b)(8)(B): certificate showing the applicant passed 'the clinical examination conducted by WREB on or after February 1, 2019 or an equivalent examination' within the 5 years preceding application. The exam MUST include: (i) standardization/calibration of examiners and anonymity; (iii) 'constructive response or objective structured clinical examination testing that includes diagnosis and treatment planning, periodontics, restorative dentistry, oral pathology, medical considerations, prescription writing and prosthodontics'; (iv) 'endodontics testing, on a mannequin or live patient, to include access and obturation of an anterior tooth and access of a multi-canalled posterior tooth'; (v) 'prosthetics testing, on a mannequin or live patient, to include a crown prep or a bridge prep'; (vi) 'a patient based or a mannequin based operative examination that includes one class II posterior alloy or composite procedure, and one additional operative procedure'. ADA summary (7 Oct 2026) lists ADEX (CDCA-WREB-CITA) and CRDTS+SRTA as accepted. Plus INBDE (or NBDE I+II), jurisprudence exam, interview.
- **PGY-1 residency in lieu of clinical exam (GPR/AEGD)** (el becerisi: hayır): 12 AAC 28.940(b)(8)(D): certificate 'showing satisfactory completion of a clinically-based postdoctoral general practice residency (GPR) or advanced education in general dentistry program (AEGD), of at least one year's duration, in a hospital or dental facility accredited for teaching purposes by the Commission on Dental Accreditation of the American Dental Association; the residency program must include a formal evaluation of the resident's competence to practice dentistry'. No exam, but requires a US residency (visa) - excluded by candidate profile.
- **Two-year+ CODA-approved postgraduate dental specialty training program in lieu of clinical exam** (el becerisi: hayır): 12 AAC 28.940(b)(8)(C): certificate 'showing successful completion of a two-year or more postgraduate dental specialty training program approved by the Commission on Dental Accreditation of the American Dental Association'. Requires a US/Canadian residency programme - excluded by candidate profile.
- **DLOSCE / OSCE-only route** (el becerisi: evet): NOT available as a stand-alone route. The rule text never names the DLOSCE; the JCNDE guide lists Alaska as only 'partial' acceptance because a DLOSCE can at most satisfy component (iii) (constructive response/OSCE) of 12 AAC 28.940(b)(8)(B), while components (iv)-(vi) expressly require endodontic, prosthetic and operative testing 'on a mannequin or live patient'. No theory-only pathway exists.
- **Licensure by Credentials (12 AAC 28.951 / AS 08.36.234) and Examination Level II** (el becerisi: hayır): Credentials: for dentists 'licensed for more than 5 years' holding 'an active license from a board of dental examiners established under the laws of a state or territory of the United States issued after thorough examination' plus continued competence (ADA page: 20 hours continuous clinical practice per week for previous 5 years). Level II: licensed 90 days-5 years, still needs WREB/equivalent clinical exam. Neither is open to a new graduate with no prior licence.

## Teori-only açıklaması
AS 08.36.110(a)(2) requires the applicant to 'pass, to the satisfaction of the board, written, clinical, and other examinations administered or approved by the board'. 12 AAC 28.940(b)(8)(B) defines the acceptable clinical exam as WREB (post-1 Feb 2019) 'or an equivalent examination' that 'must include the following subject areas, components, or characteristics: ... (iv) endodontics testing, on a mannequin or live patient, to include access and obturation of an anterior tooth and access of a multi-canalled posterior tooth; (v) prosthetics testing, on a mannequin or live patient, to include a crown prep or a bridge prep; (vi) a patient based or a mannequin based operative examination that includes one class II posterior alloy or composite procedure, and one additional operative procedure, either anterior class III or posterior class II'. The only alternatives to this hands-on exam are completion of a CODA-accredited 1-year GPR/AEGD or a 2-year+ specialty residency (12 AAC 28.940(b)(8)(C),(D)), i.e. a US residency. Application instructions 08-4159 (rev. 02/13/2025), item 6 'Clinical Experience', repeat the same three options. Therefore a hands-on exam or a residency is unavoidable.

## DLOSCE notları
The Alaska rules (12 AAC 28.940, as reproduced on the ADA Licensure-by-State page updated 7 Oct 2026) and the application instructions (08-4159, rev. 02/13/2025) do not mention the DLOSCE by name or any ADEX-integrated DLOSCE score. The rule's component (iii) 'constructive response or objective structured clinical examination testing' is the reason the JCNDE lists Alaska as 'partial' acceptance: a DLOSCE pass could only cover that OSCE component, and the candidate would still need the mannequin/live-patient endodontic, prosthetic and operative components (iv)-(vi), which in practice means taking the ADEX series (which now embeds the DLOSCE). Whether the board would combine an earlier stand-alone DLOSCE score with ADEX manikin-only components is not addressed in any official text; the exam must have been passed within the five years preceding application. Bottom line: DLOSCE alone, whether stand-alone or ADEX-reported, does not satisfy Alaska.

## Yüz yüze adımlar
(1) Hands-on clinical exam (ADEX via CDCA-WREB-CITA, or CRDTS+SRTA) must be taken at a US test site during a visit. (2) Application: 'A completed application, signed and notarized (#08-4159, pages 1-7)' with a 'Part XV Notarized Signature' page ('Notary Public for ___'); certified true copies also require a notary. Can be done abroad before a notary/US consular officer, but the form does not say whether foreign notaries are accepted (unclear). (3) No fingerprinting or criminal history check appears in the dentist application instructions (08-4159 rev. 02/13/2025). (4) Jurisprudence exam: 'The applicant must complete the written Alaska jurisprudence examination authorized under AS 08.36.110 and pass with a score of at least 70 percent. This exam will be sent to each applicant after the initial review of their application' - a take-home written questionnaire, no in-person sitting. (5) Interview: 'Upon completion of your application, applicants will be required to have a personal interview by a member of the board. The completed application and ALL supporting documents must be received and processed before the interview can be scheduled.' AS 08.36.110(b) says the applicant 'may be interviewed in person by the board or by a member of the board ... The interview must be recorded.' Whether phone/video is permitted is not stated (unclear; historically often by telephone). (6) CPR course must have a hands-on component ('Online courses are not acceptable unless there's a hands-on component'); AHA/Red Cross-equivalent training abroad appears acceptable. (7) Verification of DEA Registration Status form (08-4159b) must be sent to the DEA even if not registered. Processing: 4-6 weeks; licence issued 5-7 weeks after a complete application.

## Diğer başlangıç şartları
Level III (licensed 0-90 days) checklist per 08-4159 (rev. 02/13/2025): notarised application; fees; Authorization for Release of Records (08-4159a); official transcripts sent directly from the dental school verifying the degree and CODA accreditation at time of graduation (12 AAC 28.938); INBDE (or NBDE I+II) certificate; clinical experience (WREB/equivalent regional exam within 5 years, or 2-yr specialty programme, or 1-yr GPR/AEGD); current CPR certification 'based upon training equivalent to that required for the completion of a CPR course certified by the American Heart Association or American Red Cross. Online courses are not acceptable unless there's a hands-on component'; Verification of DEA Registration Status (08-4159b) 'required even if you are not currently registered with the DEA'; if DEA-registered, 2 hours pain management/opioid education within prior 2 years; affidavits (not impaired; adherence to ethical standards 12 AAC 28.905(b); list of all licences ever held); written Alaska jurisprudence exam, 70% pass, sent after initial review; personal interview by a board member (recorded). No English test, no fingerprints, no insurance, no state residency. Timelines: 4-6 weeks processing (longer Jan-Feb of odd years), licence issued 5-7 weeks after complete application.

## İlk ücretler
Nonrefundable application fee $600.00 + permanent licence fee $450.00 = $1,050.00 payable to 'State of Alaska' (form 08-4159, rev. 02/13/2025; 12 AAC 02.190). Plus exam costs (ADEX ~$2,995 per known facts), CPR course, notary, transcripts.

## Lisansı sürdürme

- **renewal_period:** Biennial: 'Licenses are issued for a two-year period and expire on February 28 of odd-numbered years, regardless of the date of issuance' (form 08-4132 rev. 04/01/2026). 'There is no grace period - it is illegal to work if your license has lapsed.'
- **renewal_fee:** $450.00 full-term biennial renewal; $225.00 prorated renewal (form 08-4132, 12 AAC 02.190). Late renewal/reinstatement adds unpaid renewal fees plus a penalty fee under AS 08.01.100(b).
- **ce_hours:** 32 contact hours per biennium (12 AAC 28.400(a)(1)); +2 hours pain management/opioid use if the licensee holds a DEA registration; first renewal: 'Licenses initially issued after March 1, 2024, are not required to provide evidence of continuing education for this renewal only' (12 AAC 28.405). Limits: CPR training max 4 hours (28.410(c)); practice/risk management max 3 hours (28.410(g)); volunteer service max 2 hours (28.410(h)).
- **ce_online_ok:** Largely yes. 12 AAC 28.410(i): 'Not more than eight hours of continuing education taken through the Internet in a 24-hour period may apply' - i.e. internet CE is permitted with no overall cap other than 8 h/day. 28.410(a) restricts acceptable providers to a listed set (incl. (4) 'self-study programs offered by a dental or dental hygiene college or university, the AGD, or the ADA that have been approved by the board'); full provider list (a)(1)-(3) not read. The only live/hands-on element is the CPR certification.
- **cpr_required:** Yes, at every renewal: licensee must certify 'I hold a current CPR certification that meets the requirements of AS 08.36.070 and 12 AAC 28.920' and the renewal form adds 'I further certify the course was NOT an online course.' Initial application: 'Online courses are not acceptable unless there's a hands-on component.' So a hands-on skills session is required (AHA/Red Cross-equivalent training, which can be obtained abroad).
- **practice_hours_required:** None for renewal (renewal form 08-4132 contains no practice-hour attestation). A 20-hours-per-week-for-5-years clinical practice currency requirement applies only to licensure by credentials (new applicants from other states), not to renewal.
- **address_or_residency_required:** No Alaska or US address required; only a mailing address/email of record which must be kept current (12 AAC 02.900; renewal form 'Changes to legal names, email addresses and/or mailing addresses').
- **insurance_required:** No mandatory professional liability insurance found in AS 08.36, 12 AAC 28 excerpts, application or renewal forms.
- **inactive_status:** None found for dentists. The renewal form 08-4132 (rev. 04/01/2026) offers no inactive/retired option (no occurrence of 'inactive' or 'retire'), and AS 08.36.250 provides only renewal, lapse (forfeiture for non-payment) and reinstatement. Treat as 'no inactive status' (unclear only insofar as the full board website could not be read).
- **reactivation:** AS 08.36.250(c): 'A licensed dentist who does not pay the license renewal fee forfeits the dentist's license. The board may reinstate the license without examination within two years after the date on which payment was due upon written application, proof of continued professional competence, and payment of all unpaid renewal fees and any penalty fee established under AS 08.01.100(b).' Late/lapsed renewal form requires listing all licences held in other jurisdictions during the lapse with verifications. After the 2-year window no reinstatement route is stated; the dentist would have to apply anew (Level II/credentials require another jurisdiction's active licence and, for credentials, 20 h/week clinical practice for 5 years; otherwise a new clinical exam) - unclear.
- **remote_maintainability:** moderate
- **details:** Can be kept active from abroad indefinitely by paying $450 every two years (Feb 28, odd years), completing 32 CE hours (internet CE allowed, max 8 h/day, from approved provider types) and renewing a hands-on CPR certification; SSN exemption must remain on file at renewal (AS 08.01.100 wording on the renewal form). Downsides: no inactive or retired status, so the full fee and CE are due every cycle, a lapse of more than two years cannot be cured by simple reinstatement, and CPR cannot be online-only.
