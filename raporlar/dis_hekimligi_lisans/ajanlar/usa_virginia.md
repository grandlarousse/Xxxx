# Virginia (USA) — ajan bulgusu

Düzenleyici: Virginia Board of Dentistry (Virginia Department of Health Professions, DHP) — https://www.dhp.virginia.gov/Boards/Dentistry/

**Teori-only rota:** no · **Göçmenlik engeli:** high · **Uzaktan sürdürme:** moderate · **Güven:** medium

## Sınav yolları

- **Licensure by examination (ADEX only)** (el becerisi: evet): Code of Virginia § 54.1-2709(B) requires proof the applicant (ii) graduated from an accredited dental school, (iii) passed all parts of the JCNDE examination (INBDE) and (iv) 'has successfully completed a clinical examination acceptable to the Board'. 18VAC60-21-210(A)(1)(b): 'Passed a dental clinical competency examination that is accepted by the board.' Board Guidance Document 60-25 (revised 8 Dec 2023, effective 15 Feb 2024): 'Effective January 1, 2023, the Board only accepts the ADEX Exam from dental applicants who apply for licensure by examination ... regardless of the dates portions of the examination were taken.' The guidance defines an acceptable clinical competency exam as one 'which includes live patient and mannequin-based testing methods to demonstrate the skills needed to safely provide care and treatment of patients', requires a minimum passing score of 75% on EACH listed ADEX component and prohibits compensatory scoring. The Oct 2026 application instructions repeat: original score report from the testing agency (CDCA-WREB-CITA / adextesting.org); 'Canadian exams are not accepted'; certificates not accepted. Clinical exam passed 5+ years before application may require retake/clinical CE/evidence of practice (18VAC60-21-210(A)(3)). Education: 18VAC60-21-200 accepts a diploma from a program accredited by CODA or CDAC, so the Yeditepe CODA-accredited degree qualifies. Fee $400.
- **Licensure by credentials (endorsement)** (el becerisi: evet): 18VAC60-21-210(B) / § 54.1-2709(C): must have passed the National Boards, 'have successfully completed a clinical competency examination acceptable to the board', hold a current unrestricted license in another US jurisdiction, and have been in continuous clinical practice in another US jurisdiction or federal service for 5 of the 6 years immediately preceding application (one year = minimum 600 hours; intern/residency/public-health volunteer practice may count). Not available to a new graduate; still requires a hands-on clinical exam passed somewhere. Fee $500.
- **DLOSCE-only / OSCE-only route** (el becerisi: hayır): NOT available. Virginia is not among the DLOSCE-accepting states; Guidance 60-25 accepts only the ADEX exam and defines the required exam as including live-patient and mannequin-based methods. No Board policy mentions the DLOSCE at all.
- **PGY-1 residency in lieu of clinical exam** (el becerisi: hayır): NOT available. Residency appears in the rules only (a) as an alternative EDUCATION credential (18VAC60-21-200: a 12-month CODA post-doctoral AEGD or 24-month specialty program) and (b) as creditable practice toward the 5-of-6-years requirement for licensure by credentials. The clinical-examination requirement in § 54.1-2709(B)(iv) still applies in every case.
- **Temporary permits / faculty / restricted volunteer / temporary resident's licence** (el becerisi: hayır): Limited-purpose authorisations (§§ 54.1-2713, 54.1-2714, 54.1-2715, 54.1-2712.1; 18VAC60-21-230) tied to teaching appointments, residency programs or volunteer work in Virginia; not an unrestricted licence and tied to a Virginia institution/employer, so not relevant to a visitor. Fees $25-$400.

## Teori-only açıklaması
Code of Virginia § 54.1-2709(B): application must show the applicant '(iii) has passed all parts of the examination given by the Joint Commission on National Dental Examinations; (iv) has successfully completed a clinical examination acceptable to the Board'. 18VAC60-21-210(A)(1): 'All applicants for licensure by examination shall have: a. Successfully completed all parts of the National Board Dental Examination ...; and b. Passed a dental clinical competency examination that is accepted by the board.' Board Guidance Document 60-25 (Acceptable Clinical Examinations Effective January 1, 2023; revised 8 Dec 2023, effective 15 Feb 2024): 'Effective January 1, 2023, the Board only accepts the ADEX Exam from dental applicants who apply for licensure by examination. This policy applies to all examinations completed in calendar year 2023 and thereafter, regardless of the dates portions of the examination were taken.' It further states the Board 'will only accept a clinical competency exam that is' one 'which includes live patient and mannequin-based testing methods to demonstrate the skills needed to safely provide care and treatment of patients', requires 'a minimum passing score of 75% for each of the following' ADEX components, and rejects compensatory scoring. The ADEX exam includes five manikin hand-skill components, so a hands-on examination is unavoidable; no residency, portfolio, OSCE-only or DLOSCE-only alternative exists in statute, regulation or Board guidance (ADA Licensure-by-State page, updated 20 Oct 2026, confirms the same).

## DLOSCE notları
Virginia's rules and Guidance 60-25 do not mention the DLOSCE at all; Virginia is not on the JCNDE list of DLOSCE-accepting states. The Board accepts only the ADEX Dental Examination, with every component passed at 75% or better and no compensatory scoring. Consequently neither a standalone DLOSCE score (now discontinued, Oct 2026) nor the DLOSCE component reported as part of the ADEX series can substitute for the manikin components; at most the ADEX-integrated DLOSCE would become one additional component that must also be passed (the Board has not published a policy on the ADEX-integrated DLOSCE as of the Oct 2026 instruction revision). 'Canadian exams are not accepted' (Guidance 60-25 and application instructions), so the NDEB Virtual OSCE is useless here.

PGY-1 kabul: False

## Göçmenlik / kimlik

- **citizenship_or_pr_required:** False
- **work_authorization_required:** False
- **lawful_presence_verification:** False
- **ssn_or_sin_required:** True
- **itin_or_no_ssn_alternative:** None acceptable to this candidate. Code of Virginia § 54.1-116(A): 'Every applicant for a license ... issued by the Commonwealth pursuant to this title, and every applicant for renewal thereof, shall provide on the application either his social security number or control number issued by the Department of Motor Vehicles pursuant to § 46.2-342. An initial application or renewal application which does not include either identifying number shall not be considered or acted upon by the issuing entity, and no refund of any fees paid with the application shall be granted.' The only alternative is a Virginia DMV control number (requires a Virginia DMV credential, i.e. Virginia residency), not an ITIN. § 54.1-116(B): a DHP health regulatory board 'may issue a temporary license or authorization to practice, effective for not longer than 90 days, to an otherwise qualified applicant ... who is a foreign national and cannot provide a social security number or control number at the time of application' - a 90-day stopgap only, not a full licence.
- **in_jurisdiction_address_required:** False
- **barrier_level:** high
- **details:** No citizenship, permanent-residence or work-authorisation requirement appears in § 54.1-2709, 18VAC60-21-190/200/210, Guidance 60-25 or the Oct 2026 application instructions, and no lawful-presence/SAVE verification language was found for DHP boards (statute and instructions grepped for citizen/lawful/immigration/visa: no hits). The decisive barrier is statutory: § 54.1-116 makes an SSN (or Virginia DMV control number) mandatory for BOTH the initial application and EVERY annual renewal, with no ITIN or affidavit alternative; a foreign national without either number can only receive a temporary authorisation of up to 90 days. A visitor with no US status cannot obtain an SSN, and obtaining a Virginia DMV control number requires Virginia residency documentation. Address: the application requires an 'Address of Record' (made public under § 54.1-2400.02 unless a second publicly disclosable address is given); nothing requires it to be in Virginia. Transcript, NBDE/INBDE score and ADEX score report must be sent directly by the issuing bodies; NPDB self-query report (dated within 6 months) required.
- **citations:** Code of Virginia § 54.1-116 (https://law.lis.virginia.gov/vacode/title54.1/chapter1/section54.1-116/); Code of Virginia § 54.1-2709 (quoted on ADA page); 18VAC60-21-190, -200, -210 (https://law.lis.virginia.gov/admincodefull/title18/agency60/chapter21/partv/); Dentist Online Application Instructions, Revised October 2026 (https://www.license.dhp.virginia.gov/apply/Forms/Dentistry/Dentist_InstrForms.pdf); Guidance Document 60-25 (https://www.dhp.virginia.gov/media/dhpweb/docs/dentistry/guidance/60-25.pdf)

## Yüz yüze adımlar
(1) ADEX clinical exam: hands-on manikin components at US test sites - physical presence unavoidable. (2) Application itself is online (license.dhp.virginia.gov) with card payment; transcript, INBDE score report, ADEX score report and licence verifications are mailed/emailed directly by the institutions, which can be arranged from abroad. (3) No fingerprinting or criminal-background check requirement was found for dentists (only an NPDB self-query report, obtainable online); no Board jurisprudence examination exists; no interview. (4) The Oct 2026 instruction packet contains one form that 'MUST BE COMPLETED BEFORE A NOTARY PUBLIC' (page 8 of the packet; which applicant category it applies to could not be confirmed within budget - likely the credentials chronology/attestation form). Notarisation, if required, could be done at a US consulate or a US notary during a visit. (5) The statutory SSN/DMV-control-number requirement cannot be satisfied from abroad or on a visitor visa (see immigration).

## Diğer başlangıç şartları
Good moral character (§ 54.1-2709(B)(i)); final certified transcript from the CODA/CDAC-accredited school sent directly; verifiable evidence of passing all parts of the JCNDE exam (INBDE) - original score report, copies not accepted; original ADEX score report (each component passed at 75%+, no compensatory scoring, Canadian exams/certificates not accepted); original NPDB (and HIPDB) self-query report dated within 6 months; licence verifications from every jurisdiction ever licensed; SSN or Virginia DMV control number (§ 54.1-116). No jurisprudence exam, no English test, no insurance requirement, no fingerprinting found. CPR/BLS is not listed in the initial application instructions but must be maintained for renewal (18VAC60-21-250(A)(1)). The $400 fee is valid for one year from receipt; the Board grants approval to sit for the ADEX exam to 'otherwise eligible' applicants who name their testing agency on the application. Processing time not published in the sources read.

## Lisansı sürdürme

- **renewal_period:** Annual, in the licensee's birth month (18VAC60-21-240(A)); applies to both active and inactive licences.
- **renewal_fee:** Active dental licence $285/year; inactive $145/year; late fee $100 (active) / $50 (inactive); licence may be renewed up to one year after the deadline with late fee; after >1 year expired a reinstatement application and $500 reinstatement fee are required (18VAC60-21-40(B)-(D), 18VAC60-21-240(E)-(F)).
- **ce_hours:** 15 hours per annual renewal (exempt for the first renewal after initial licensure), including 2 hours related to pain management (18VAC60-21-250(A); 18VAC60-21-106 for prescribers); up to 15 excess hours may carry forward; up to 3 hours may be earned through uncompensated care in a Virginia free clinic (optional).
- **ce_online_ok:** Yes in substance: 18VAC60-21-250(C) - 'Continuing education credit may be earned for verifiable attendance at or participation in any course, to include audio and video presentations, that meets the requirements in subsection B ... and is given by a sponsor approved by the board.' No live/in-person minimum is stated. Only the CPR/BLS certification has a hands-on element.
- **cpr_required:** Yes. 18VAC60-21-250(A)(1): 'A dentist shall maintain current training certification in basic cardiopulmonary resuscitation with hands-on airway training for health care providers or basic life support' - i.e. a hands-on skills component is required for an active licence (online-only CPR does not satisfy it).
- **practice_hours_required:** None for renewal of an active or inactive licence. (Practice hours - 600 h/year for 5 of 6 years - apply only to licensure by credentials; and 18VAC60-21-210(A)(3) only to initial applicants whose clinical exam is 5+ years old.)
- **address_or_residency_required:** An Address of Record (public unless a separate publicly disclosable address is given) must be kept; no requirement that it be in Virginia or the US was found. No residency requirement.
- **insurance_required:** No mandatory professional liability insurance requirement found in the statute, regulations or instructions.
- **inactive_status:** Statutory inactive licence (§ 54.1-2709(D); 18VAC60-21-220(A)): 'Any dentist who holds a current, unrestricted license in Virginia may, upon a request on the renewal application and submission of the required fee, be issued an inactive license.' Renewal fee $145/year (late fee $50); no maximum duration stated; may not practise. CE while inactive: not explicitly required - the reactivation rule requires catch-up CE 'equal to the requirement for the number of years in which the license has been inactive, not to exceed a total of 45 hours', which implies no CE accrues while inactive (not explicitly confirmed).
- **reactivation:** 18VAC60-21-220(B): submit reactivation application with 'evidence of continuing clinical competence' and pay the difference between the inactive and active renewal fees ($140). The board 'shall consider (i) hours of continuing education that meet the requirements of 18VAC60-21-250; (ii) evidence of active practice in another state or in federal service; (iii) current specialty board certification; (iv) recent passage of a clinical competency examination that is accepted by the board; or (v) a refresher program offered by a program accredited by [CODA]'. CE hours equal to the years inactive (max 45; at least 15 in the last 12 months, remainder within 36 months) 'must be included with the application'; the board may deny reactivation to anyone 'unable to demonstrate continuing competence' (discretionary, but the rule text allows CE alone as the evidence). Lapse >1 year: reinstatement application + $500 fee + same continuing-competence evidence (18VAC60-21-240(F)). Note: every renewal/reactivation/reinstatement application must again carry an SSN or DMV control number (§ 54.1-116).
- **remote_maintainability:** moderate
- **details:** Assuming a licence were obtained, it could in principle be held indefinitely from abroad: annual online renewal, $285 (active) or $145 (inactive), 15 h CE that can be audio/video courses from approved sponsors, no practice-hour, insurance or in-state-address requirement, and an open-ended inactive status with a CE-based reactivation path. The awkward parts are (a) the hands-on BLS certification needed to keep the licence active, (b) the Board's discretion over continuing-competence evidence on reactivation after many years inactive, and (c) the statutory SSN/DMV-control-number requirement on every renewal, which for this candidate is the real obstacle (hence overall practical rating would be 'hard' for someone without an SSN).
