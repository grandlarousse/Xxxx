# South Carolina (USA) — ajan bulgusu

Düzenleyici: South Carolina Board of Dentistry (SC Department of Labor, Licensing and Regulation, LLR) — https://llr.sc.gov/bod/

**Teori-only rota:** no · **Göçmenlik engeli:** high · **Uzaktan sürdürme:** hard · **Güven:** medium

## Sınav yolları

- **Licensure by examination - ADEX Dental Examination (CDCA-WREB-CITA)** (el becerisi: evet): Board's 'Dental Licensure by Examination Requirements' (Rev. 04/2021): CODA graduation + National Board (INBDE) + 'a clinical examination acceptable to the Board... The Board accepts CRDTS, ADEX and SRTA approved clinical examinations.' ADEX includes manikin hand-skill sections (the DLOSCE component is only one part); also CPR, SC jurisprudence exam (online), good moral character, interview if requested.
- **Licensure by examination - CRDTS clinical exam** (el becerisi: evet): Accepted regional hands-on (manikin/patient) clinical exam under the same requirements document; ADA Laws & Rules page lists 'ADEX (CDCA-WREB-CITA), CRDTS + SRTA' as accepted initial licensure exams.
- **Licensure by examination - SRTA clinical exam** (el becerisi: evet): Accepted regional hands-on clinical exam. Board explicitly 'does not accept state clinical examinations nor the WREB clinical examination.'
- **DLOSCE / computer-based OSCE only - NOT AVAILABLE** (el becerisi: evet): South Carolina is not among the states listed in the 2026 DLOSCE Candidate Guide, and no SC board document read mentions the DLOSCE or any OSCE as satisfying the clinical exam requirement. Only CRDTS/ADEX/SRTA full clinical exams are accepted, so a hands-on component is unavoidable.
- **PGY-1 residency in lieu of clinical exam - NOT AVAILABLE** (el becerisi: evet): No residency/PGY-1 alternative appears in SC Code 40-15-140, Reg. 39-1 or the Board's requirements document. (A separate 'Dental Resident Limited License' exists only for residents in SC training programmes; it is not a full licence.)
- **Licensure by credentials (SC Code 40-15-275) / reciprocity (40-15-270)** (el becerisi: evet): Only for dentists already licensed in another US state; requires having 'satisfactorily passed a state or regional clinical board examination approved by the board and a jurisprudence examination', practice of 1,200 hours/year in clinical settings, 70 CE hours in past 5 years, etc. Reg. 39-1(A): 'The South Carolina Board of Dentistry has no reciprocal licensure arrangement with any other jurisdiction.' Not usable by a new graduate and still rests on a hands-on exam.

## Teori-only açıklaması
Board's 'Dental Licensure by Examination Requirements and Application Process Overview' (Rev. 04/2021, llr.sc.gov/bod): '3. You must have successfully passed a clinical examination acceptable to the Board for licensure. The Board accepts CRDTS, ADEX and SRTA approved clinical examinations. The Board does not accept state clinical examinations nor the WREB clinical examination.' SC Code 40-15-140: 'Each applicant must satisfactorily pass the examination prepared by the board on subjects and operations pertaining to dentistry... The examination must be given either orally or in writing, or by requiring a practical demonstration of the applicant's skill, or by any combination of such methods as the board may in its discretion require.' SC Reg. 39-1(C): 'The Board shall require each applicant to successfully complete an examination before such applicant is licensed... Each applicant shall furnish their own patient on the exam.' The Board exercises that discretion by accepting only the three hands-on regional exams; there is no DLOSCE, OSCE-only, portfolio or PGY-1 alternative. Hence INBDE + online jurisprudence exam alone cannot produce a licence.

## DLOSCE notları
Nothing in the SC statute (Title 40 Ch. 15), Reg. 39-1 or the Board's licensure-by-examination documents mentions the DLOSCE or any OSCE; SC is not a DLOSCE-accepting state in the JCNDE 2026 Candidate Guide. The DLOSCE is relevant in SC only as the component now embedded in the ADEX Dental Examination, which the Board accepts as a whole (hand-skill manikin sections included). No SC policy accepting an ADEX-reported DLOSCE pass alone, or earlier standalone DLOSCE scores, was found; standalone DLOSCE scores would not substitute for a CRDTS/ADEX/SRTA clinical exam.

PGY-1 kabul: False

## Göçmenlik / kimlik

- **citizenship_or_pr_required:** False
- **work_authorization_required:** False
- **lawful_presence_verification:** True
- **ssn_or_sin_required:** True
- **itin_or_no_ssn_alternative:** None found. Required documents list: 'Copy of Social Security Card'. Application form (Rev. 03/2015, p.4): 'Failure to provide your social security number for these mandatory purposes will result in the denial of your licensure application. Social security numbers may also be disclosed to other governmental... Your social security number will not be released for any other purpose not provided for by law.' No ITIN, 'no SSN' affidavit or blank-field option is mentioned in any document read (unverified whether Board staff grant exceptions informally).
- **in_jurisdiction_address_required:** False
- **barrier_level:** high
- **details:** (1) Lawful presence: under SC Code 8-29-10 et seq. LLR 'must verify that any person who applies for a South Carolina license is lawfully present in the United States.' Applicant must file a NOTARIZED 'Verification of Lawful Presence in the United States - Affidavit of Eligibility' checking one box: '1. I am a United States citizen; or 2. I am a Legal Permanent Resident...; or 3. I am a Qualified Alien or non-immigrant under the Federal Immigration and Nationality Act... eighteen years of age or older, and lawfully present in the United States; 4. Other'. Boxes 2-4 require 'a copy of your immigration documents'; accepted documents include 'I-94 (Arrival/Departure Record) in Unexpired Foreign Passport', I-551, I-766 EAD, I-20, DS-2019, etc. A Turkish visitor admitted on a B-1/B-2 visa is a 'non-immigrant... lawfully present' while in the US and holds an I-94, so citizenship/PR/work authorisation is NOT required; but the affidavit adds: 'the representations made in this Affidavit shall apply through any license(s) or renewals issued, and that I shall have an affirmative duty to immediately advise [LLR] of any change of my immigration or citizenship status' - how LLR treats a licensee who is no longer present in the US is unclear. 'The information provided is subject to verification' (likely SAVE). (2) SSN: a Social Security card copy and SSN are mandatory and failure to provide = denial (no ITIN alternative stated). A B-visa visitor cannot obtain an SSN, so this is the decisive barrier. (3) No SC residence or in-state address required for licensure (Reg. 39-5(A): keep Board informed of current mailing address), but SC Code 40-15-170 deems the licence inactive after six successive years of neither residing nor practising in SC.
- **citations:** SC Code 8-29-10 et seq. (Illegal Immigration Reform Act - lawful presence verification for public benefits/licences), quoted in LLR 'Verification of Lawful Presence in the United States - Affidavit of Eligibility' (Rev. 02-02-2015) inside https://llr.sc.gov/bod/PDF/Examination-Licensure__to_Practice_Dentistry_Req_and_Instructions_Online.pdf ; SSN wording in https://llr.sc.gov/bod/PDF/Applications/Application_to_Practice_Dentistry_Exam.pdf (Rev. 03/2015, p.4); SC Code 40-15-170 and Reg. 39-5 quoted on https://www.ada.org/~/media/Project/ADA%20Organization/ADA/ADA-org/Files/Education/Licensure%20by%20State/South_Carolina_Licensure

## Yüz yüze adımlar
(a) Clinical exam (ADEX/CRDTS/SRTA) must be taken in person at a US test site - hands-on. (b) SC Jurisprudence Examination: ONLINE - 'Once the completed application is approved, you will be e-mailed instructions with a UserId to take the exam online. A score of 70 or higher is considered a passing score' - can be done from abroad. (c) Two notarised affidavits: the 'Dentistry Signature Affidavit' (Rev. 1/23, which also authorises 'a criminal background investigation') and the 'Verification of Lawful Presence' affidavit; the forms have a 'Notary Public for the State of' line, implying a US notary (a US consular notarisation or remote online notarisation might be accepted - unverified). (d) No fingerprint card / Live Scan requirement appears in the application instructions; the background check appears to be a name-based investigation authorised by the signature affidavit (unverified whether SLED/FBI fingerprinting is requested in practice). (e) 'You must agree to appear for a personal interview if requested by the Board' (Columbia, SC) - discretionary. (f) Identification: copy of passport/driver's licence AND copy of Social Security card; copy of immigration documents (I-94 in passport) for the lawful-presence affidavit - the I-94 only exists while admitted to the US, so this is effectively done during a US visit. (g) Supporting documents (transcript, INBDE scores from ADA, NPDB self-query, one reference letter from a licensed dentist, CPR certificate) can be arranged from abroad. Application is submitted online via LLR portal; status checked online.

## Diğer başlangıç şartları
Graduation from a CODA-approved dental programme (Yeditepe post-1 Feb 2024 qualifies; Reg. 39-1(B)(3) allows a notarised copy of the diploma or dean's sworn statement; official transcript sent directly by the school); passed National Board Examination (INBDE scores sent directly by ADA); passed CRDTS/ADEX/SRTA clinical exam; current CPR certification (course taken within two years of application); good moral character with one original reference letter from a licensed dentist on letterhead dated within 6 months; NPDB self-query report; DEA verification if applicable; personal history questions with written explanations; SC Jurisprudence Examination online (pass 70); notarised Signature Affidavit authorising criminal background investigation; notarised lawful-presence affidavit; copy of ID/passport and Social Security card; minimum age 21 (Reg. 39-1(B)(1)); agree to interview if requested. No English test (CODA graduate), no insurance requirement found. Application valid one year from receipt; older instructions cited a 6-8 week processing time (not directly verified). New licensees by examination are exempt from CE for their first renewal period.

## İlk ücretler
Application by examination fee: $300 (non-refundable, non-transferable; returned-check fee up to $30). Jurisprudence exam: no separate fee stated. Clinical exam paid to the testing agency (e.g. full ADEX series $2,995 per ADEX FAQ). NPDB self-query, transcripts, notary and CPR course at cost. Reinstatement after lapse: $75 application + $500 dentist reinstatement fee ($575). Timeline: application file valid 1 year; licence issued after all documents, clinical scores and online jurisprudence exam are received.

## Lisansı sürdürme

- **renewal_period:** Biennial. Dentist licences renew by 31 December of even-numbered years (renewal window opens on or around 15 October of the even year); late renewal allowed 1 Jan-31 Jan of the odd year at double fee and 1 Feb-1 Mar of the odd year at double fee + $5/day; after 1 March a reinstatement application is required ('There is no late renewal period' in the ordinary sense; next window on or around 15 Oct 2026 - 1 Mar 2027).
- **renewal_fee:** Renewal page (read 2 Nov 2026) shows biennial fee rows of $280 (double $560 if renewed 1-31 Jan of odd year; $560 + $5/day 1 Feb-1 Mar) and $320 ($640 / $640 + $5/day); the row headers were not captured so the dentist figure is most likely $280 or $320 per two years - verify on https://llr.sc.gov/bod/renewals.aspx. Reinstatement after lapse: $75 + $500.
- **ce_hours:** Reg. 39-5(F): dentists 14 CE hours per year or 28 hours over the two-year renewal cycle; new licensees by examination exempt for the first relicensure period; CPR course counts up to 4 hours; at least 2 hours on prescribing/monitoring controlled substances for dentists who prescribe them (SC Code 40-15-145); 1 hour biennially on bloodborne-pathogen/infection-control topics for those exposed to body fluids (per Reg. 39-5(F)(1)(c), partially read); teaching credit capped at 25%.
- **ce_online_ok:** Effectively yes. Reg. 39-5(F): 'Fifty percent (50%) of the required continuing education hours must be obtained via live, in-person attendance. Interactive webinars are considered live or in-person continuing education hours. The remaining fifty percent (50%) of the required continuing education hours can be earned via online computer seminars.' So 14 h via interactive (live) webinars + 14 h self-study online satisfies the rule from abroad.
- **cpr_required:** Yes. Reg. 39-5(F)(3): 'All dentists and dental hygienists must have completed an approved CPR course within two (2) years of licensure or renewal. Thereafter, all dentists and dental hygienists must be recertified in CPR once every two (2) years.' Whether a hands-on skills session is required (vs online-only CPR) is not specified in the rule - unclear; CPR certificate copy is required at initial application.
- **practice_hours_required:** No minimum practice hours for ordinary renewal. BUT SC Code 40-15-170 (quoted in Reg. 39-5(D)): 'The license of a dentist or dental hygienist who does not either reside or practice in South Carolina for a period of six successive years shall be deemed inactive... Relicensing after an absence of over six years can be made at the discretion of the Board upon proof of high professional fitness and moral character.' Practice hours (1,200/yr) matter only for licensure by credentials.
- **address_or_residency_required:** No in-state address or residency required to obtain or renew (Reg. 39-5(A): 'Every licensed dentist... shall keep the Board informed of their current mailing address'; a valid email is needed for the online portal). However, the six-successive-year non-residence/non-practice rule in 40-15-170 deems the licence inactive.
- **insurance_required:** None found in statute, regulation, application or renewal pages.
- **inactive_status:** No voluntary inactive/retired licence category is listed on the Board's renewal page or in the documents read. The only 'inactive' concept is statutory: a licence is 'deemed inactive' once the dentist has neither resided nor practised in SC for six successive years (40-15-170), with relicensing discretionary. A licence not renewed by 1 March of the odd year lapses and requires reinstatement ($75 + $500, jurisprudence exam, CE, CPR).
- **reactivation:** Reinstatement of a lapsed/expired licence: application ($75) + $500 dentist fee, pass the SC Dental Practice Act (jurisprudence) examination, CE hours taken to reinstate cannot be reused for the next renewal, other-state licences must be in good standing, application valid one year. 'If your SC license/registration has been inactive or expired for six (6) or more years, you may be required to' [complete re-entry requirements / appear before the Board] (document truncated); Reg. 39-5(C): 'the Board may require a reexamination of the person whose license or certificate has expired or may require the person to appear before the Board'; 40-15-170 relicensing after 6+ years' absence is at Board discretion 'upon proof of high professional fitness and moral character' (hygiene re-entry programme lists 14 CE hours + current CPR; dentist equivalent not read).
- **remote_maintainability:** hard
- **details:** Ordinary renewal from abroad is feasible: online renewal portal, proxy payment allowed, 28 CE hours per biennium all achievable online (half via interactive webinars), biennial CPR, no practice hours, no in-state address, no insurance. The obstacle is SC Code 40-15-170: a dentist who neither resides nor practises in South Carolina for six successive years is 'deemed inactive' by statute and can be relicensed only at the Board's discretion, so a licence cannot be kept active indefinitely while living in Turkey. Additionally the lawful-presence affidavit imposes an 'affirmative duty to immediately advise' LLR of any change in immigration status, and the portal/password recovery references the SSN (last 5 digits), reinforcing the SSN dependency.
