# Alabama (USA) — ajan bulgusu

Düzenleyici: Board of Dental Examiners of Alabama — https://www.dentalboard.org/

**Teori-only rota:** no · **Göçmenlik engeli:** high · **Uzaktan sürdürme:** moderate · **Güven:** medium

## Sınav yolları

- **Licensure by examination – regional/ADEX clinical exam (ADEX via CDCA-WREB-CITA, CRDTS, SRTA)** (el becerisi: evet): ADA Alabama summary (updated 7 Oct 2026): 'The Board of Dental Examiners of Alabama currently accepts the following regional exams: American Board of Dental Examiners (ADEX, CDCA-WREB-CITA), CRDTS+SRTA. Both mannequin and patient-based portions are accepted. In order to apply for this method of licensure, you must have successfully passed a regional examination within the five (5) years immediately preceding the date on your application.' Prerequisites (Rule 270-X-2-.01): graduate of a Board-approved (CODA) dental school; NBDE I+II or INBDE. Code § 34-9-10(b)(1): application to take an examination must be received 'within 18 months of graduation from dental school or completion of an accredited or approved post-doctoral residency program'; (b)(2) those who passed a board-approved (non-board-administered) exam must apply within five years of notification of passing. Written/theory parts: § 34-9-11 lets the board 'recognize any written parts of an examination given by the Joint Commission on National Dental Examinations in lieu of' board written exams, but a clinical (hands-on) examination remains required.
- **DLOSCE-only / OSCE-only** (el becerisi: hayır): NOT available. Alabama does not appear in the 2026 DLOSCE Candidate Guide list of accepting states, and neither the ADA Alabama summary nor Administrative Code Chapter 270-X-2 (revised 4/13/26) mentions DLOSCE/OSCE; the only accepted clinical exams are ADEX/CDCA-WREB-CITA, CRDTS and SRTA, all with manikin/patient components.
- **PGY-1 / residency in lieu of clinical exam** (el becerisi: evet): NOT available as a substitute. Residency appears in § 34-9-10(b)(1) only to extend the 18-month application window ('within 18 months of graduation from dental school or completion of an accredited or approved post-doctoral residency program'); no rule accepts completion of a residency instead of a regional clinical exam. (Not relevant to candidate in any case.)
- **Licensure by credentials (endorsement from another US state)** (el becerisi: evet): Code § 34-9-10(c)-(d) and Rule 270-X-2-.19: for dentists already licensed in another US state 'having examination standards regarded by the Board ... as an equivalent to the Alabama standards' and engaged in active clinical practice (rule requires a period of active practice; the ADA summary cites five years); requires NBDE/INBDE, verification of good standing, NPDB/AADB checks, written statement agreeing to be interviewed, 'must successfully pass a written jurisprudence examination', DEA certification, two affidavits from licensed dentists practising in the applicant's area, transcript, no felony etc. Board may also require questioning, peer review, substance testing and 'Background checks for criminal or fraudulent activities'. Underlying licence would itself have required a hands-on exam; not usable by a new graduate.
- **Special purpose licence / teaching permit** (el becerisi: hayır): § 34-9-10 special purpose licence and Rule 270-X-2-.10 teaching/special teaching permits exist for dentists licensed elsewhere or affiliated with institutions; not a general practice licence for a new graduate and not a route to full licensure.

## Teori-only açıklaması
A hands-on regional clinical examination is unavoidable. ADA 'Alabama Laws & Rules' (updated 7 October 2026): 'The Board of Dental Examiners of Alabama currently accepts the following regional exams: American Board of Dental Examiners (ADEX, CDCA-WREB-CITA), CRDTS+SRTA. Both mannequin and patient-based portions are accepted.' Code of Alabama § 34-9-11: 'All examinations provided for in this chapter shall be approved by the board and shall be of the type and character as to test the qualifications of the applicant to practice dentistry. Provided, however, the board may recognize any written parts of an examination given by the Joint Commission on National Dental Examinations in lieu of or subject to the board examinations as the board may approve.' Rule 270-X-2-.05 refers to 'examinations, either written, clinical or laboratory procedures'. Nothing in § 34-9-10/-11 or Chapter 270-X-2 (rev. 4/13/26) accepts DLOSCE, an OSCE, a residency or a portfolio in place of the clinical exam.

## DLOSCE notları
Alabama is not among the DLOSCE-accepting states in the 2026 DLOSCE Candidate Guide (Alaska*, Arizona, Colorado, Indiana, Iowa*, Kentucky, Oregon, Washington). The ADA state summary (7 Oct 2026) and Admin Code Chapter 270-X-2 contain no reference to DLOSCE or any OSCE; the board accepts only ADEX (CDCA-WREB-CITA), CRDTS and SRTA, with 'both mannequin and patient-based portions accepted'. Because Alabama never recognised the standalone DLOSCE, the April 2026 ADA/ADEX integration (DLOSCE delivered only within the full ADEX series after 1 Aug 2026; standalone ended 9 Oct 2026) changes nothing: a candidate would have to pass the complete ADEX dental examination including its manikin hand-skill sections; an ADEX-reported DLOSCE pass alone has no standing in Alabama.

- **in_jurisdiction_address_required:** False
- **barrier_level:** high
- **details:** Statute: Code of Alabama § 34-9-10(a): 'every person as a prerequisite to licensure shall be at least 19 years of age, of good moral character, a citizen of the United States or, if not a citizen of the United States, a person who is legally present in the United States with appropriate documentation from the federal government, and a graduate of a dental school or college accredited by the American Dental Association Commission on Dental Accreditation and approved by the board'. Citizenship/PR and work authorisation are NOT required per se, but lawful presence in the US 'with appropriate documentation from the federal government' is a statutory prerequisite, and § 34-9-11 says those found qualified 'consistent with Section 34-9-10(a)' are granted a licence. A B-1/B-2 visitor physically in the US with a valid I-94 is 'legally present' at that moment, so the statute may be satisfiable during an exam/paperwork visit, but (i) a person applying from Turkey is not 'present in the United States', and (ii) Alabama's Beason-Hammon Taxpayer and Citizen Protection Act (Ala. Code § 31-13-7, Act 2011-535) requires state agencies to verify lawful presence (declaration of citizenship/lawful presence plus federal SAVE verification for non-citizens) before granting 'public benefits', which include professional licences — the statute text could not be fetched in this session (Justia/FindLaw/ALISON blocked), so the exact verification mechanism used by the dental board (declaration form, copy of visa/I-94, SAVE) is unverified. No in-state address or residency requirement found; Rule 270-X-2-.09 only requires notifying address changes. Barrier rated 'high' under the rubric because lawful presence is a statutory condition; whether the board would license a visitor-visa holder is discretionary/untested.
- **citations:** Code of Alabama § 34-9-10(a) (quoted in ADA 'Alabama Laws & Rules', updated 7 Oct 2026: https://www.ada.org/~/media/Project/ADA%20Organization/ADA/ADA-org/Files/Education/Licensure%20by%20State/Alabama_Licensure); Code § 34-9-11; Ala. ## Yüz yüze adımlar
(1) Hands-on regional clinical exam (ADEX/CDCA-WREB-CITA, CRDTS or SRTA) must be taken in person at a US test site; passing scores valid five years before application. (2) Written jurisprudence examination: explicitly required for licensure by credentials (Rule 270-X-2-.19(2)(f)); for licensure by examination the format/requirement is not stated in the rules read; whether it is online or in person is unverified. (3) Fingerprinting/criminal background check: Chapter 270-X-2 only mentions 'Background checks for criminal or fraudulent activities' as a discretionary item for credentials applicants; the current examination application packet (dentalboard.org > Applications & Forms > State Licensure And Permit Applications) was not retrieved, so whether Live Scan vs mailed cards is used is unclear. (4) Board may require applicants to 'appear for examination at a time and place to be fixed by the board' (§ 34-9-11) and credentials applicants must agree to be interviewed at the Board's request. (5) Annual registration is by form/online; the CE rule requires uploading documentation to CE Broker. Lawful-presence documentation would in practice require being in the US with a valid visa/I-94 at application time. Short-visit feasibility: exam and any interview can be done during visits; the remaining steps depend on the application packet (unverified).

## Diğer başlangıç şartları
Age 19+, good moral character, CODA-accredited dental degree approved by the Board (Yeditepe post-1 Feb 2024 should qualify as CODA-accredited; board approval of a non-US CODA school not confirmed), NBDE I+II or INBDE (Rule 270-X-2-.01(3)); application with all credentials at least 30 days before the exam date with examination and certificate fees (Rule 270-X-2-.02); application for exam within 18 months of graduation (§ 34-9-10(b)(1)) or licensure application within five years of passing a board-approved exam (§ 34-9-10(b)(2)); CE Rule 270-X-4-.04(4)(e): 'An applicant applying for a dental or dental hygiene license shall, at a minimum, provide documentation of completion of BLS and Infectious Disease Control training' (BLS must be live/hands-on training per 2(a)(2)); written exams must be in English (Rule 270-X-2-.05(5)); no English test, no liability-insurance requirement found; jurisprudence exam explicitly for credentials applicants, unverified for exam applicants. Fees are 'determined by the Board' (Rule 270-X-2-.04) and capped by § 34-9-16; fee schedule not retrieved.

## Lisansı sürdürme

- **renewal_period:** Annual. Registration year runs 1 October–30 September; renewal form/fee due by 30 September each year (Rule 270-X-2-.07). Licence converts to 'expired' on 1 October if not renewed; may be reinstated 1 Oct–31 Dec by meeting renewal requirements and paying the fee; automatically suspended on 1 January.
- **renewal_fee:** Annual registration fee set by the Board (§ 34-9-15, § 34-9-16); amount not retrieved (Board 'Fee Schedule' page). Late: statutory penalty plus all accrued annual fees up to five years.
- **ce_hours:** 20 hours per year (Rule 270-X-4-.04(2)(a)) including: minimum 10 hours 'live training'; current BLS certification (live training, 2–4 hours); 1 hour controlled-substance prescribing; 1 hour infectious disease control; 1 hour ethics; up to 4 hours via pro bono work in Alabama. Documentation uploaded to CE Broker.
- **ce_online_ok:** No, not 100% online for a dentist practising in Alabama: 'A minimum of ten (10) hours shall be live training' where live training means 'the primary presenter or instructor is physically present at the same location as the student'; BLS also live. HOWEVER Rule 270-X-4-.04(3)(c): 'A dentist or dental hygienist licensed in the State of Alabama but practicing outside the State of Alabama is excused from the requirements of 2(a) or 2(b). If the dentist or dental hygienist returns to the State of Alabama to practice, they shall submit proof of continuing education for the number of years they practiced outside the State of Alabama up to a maximum of five (5) years.' A dentist practising in Turkey could therefore renew without CE; a licensee not practising anywhere may not fit the 'practicing outside the State' wording (unclear). Live training taken abroad might be accepted (courses by dental associations/accredited schools are 'approved'; others can be pre-approved), unverified.
- **cpr_required:** Yes for in-state practitioners: 'A dentist shall maintain a current certification in Basic Life Support (BLS) training through the American Heart Association, American Red Cross, or an equivalent program. This program shall be completed through live training and certified for a minimum of two (2) hours and a maximum of four (4) hours.' Live (hands-on) training required. Excused under 3(c) if practising outside Alabama. BLS also required at initial application.
- **practice_hours_required:** None for renewal of a licence obtained by examination. (Active clinical practice is required only to qualify for licensure by credentials.)
- **address_or_residency_required:** No in-state address/residency requirement found. Rule 270-X-2-.09 requires licensees to report changes of address; board mails notices to 'the last address of record'.
- **insurance_required:** No mandatory professional liability insurance found in Chapter 270-X-2 or the CE rule (credentials applicants only disclose insurance history).
- **inactive_status:** An inactive status exists by request (CE Rule 270-X-4-.04(4)(g) refers to a licensee 'having requested their license be placed inactive') and § 34-9-15(d)(1) requires the board to 'waive the annual payment of fees ... and issue a current annual registration certificate to any licensee ... who has retired from the practice of dentistry ... because of age or physical disability'. Fee, CE and maximum duration for voluntary inactive status were not found in the rules read (unclear). 'Expired' (1 Oct–31 Dec) and 'suspended' (from 1 Jan) statuses follow non-renewal.
- **reactivation:** Rule 270-X-4-.04(4)(g): a dentist reinstating after voluntary inactive status or expiry 'shall show continuing education equal to 2(a) ... up to a maximum of five (5) years. If the license has been inactive or expired for more than five (5) years, the dentist ... shall be required to show continuing education equal to 2(a) and additional continuing education as' [text cut off in source]. Rule 270-X-2-.07(5): a licence automatically suspended for less than five years may be reinstated by meeting all reinstatement requirements, paying the annual fee for all intervening years (max five) and the statutory maximum reinstatement penalty (§ 34-9-16). Treatment after more than five years' suspension is not stated (possibly new application/exam — unclear). No re-examination requirement found for inactive/expired reinstatement within five years.
- **remote_maintainability:** moderate
- **details:** Annual renewal (fee + CE attestation) can be done remotely. The 20-hour CE requirement with 10 live hours and live BLS is the main obstacle for someone abroad, but Rule 270-X-4-.04(3)(c) expressly excuses licensees 'practicing outside the State of Alabama' from CE, with catch-up CE (max five years) only on return to practise in Alabama. No practice-hour, residency, address or insurance requirements found. Annual fee amount and voluntary-inactive terms unverified. Rated moderate rather than easy because of annual (not multi-year) renewal, the live-CE/BLS requirement if the practising-abroad exemption does not apply, and the undocumented inactive-status terms.
