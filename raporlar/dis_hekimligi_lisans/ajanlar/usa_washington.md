# Washington (USA) — ajan bulgusu

Düzenleyici: Washington State Dental Quality Assurance Commission (DQAC), credentialing administered by the Washington State Department of Health (DOH) — https://doh.wa.gov/licenses-permits-and-certificates/professions-new-renew-or-update/dentist

**Teori-only rota:** partial · **Göçmenlik engeli:** none · **Uzaktan sürdürme:** easy · **Güven:** medium

## Sınav yolları

- **Licensure by examination - regional/ADEX hands-on practical exam** (el becerisi: evet): WAC 246-817-120 (as amended effective 9 Aug 2026, WSR 26-15-046): written exam (INBDE, NBDE I+II, or NDEB written) PLUS a practical exam containing at least Restorative, Endodontic, Periodontal, Prosthodontic and Comprehensive treatment planning/diagnostic skills, from WREB, CRDTS, CDCA, SRTA, CITA, or ADEX ('formerly known as CDCA-WREB-CITA'). Manikin or patient-based accepted. All sections from one agency (or max two agencies coordinating). Results valid only if taken within 5 years before application. Scores sent directly from the agency or a notarized copy. The California portfolio exam is expressly NOT accepted.
- **Licensure by examination - DLOSCE (JCNDE computer-based OSCE) as the practical exam** (el becerisi: hayır): WAC 246-817-120(2)(a)(viii) lists 'The Joint Commission on National Dental Examinations dental licensure objective structured clinical examination (DLOSCE)' as an accepted practical examination 'provided the testing agency offers at least the sections listed in subsection (1)(b)'. The DOH Licensing Requirements page and the Sept 2026 application packet (DOH 646-137 / 646-010) still list DLOSCE as a stand-alone tick-box option with no additional hands-on assessment. INBDE + DLOSCE + online jurisprudence exam would therefore be a fully computer-based route in the rules. Practical constraint: per the known facts, stand-alone DLOSCE administrations ended 9 Oct 2026 and the DLOSCE is now delivered only with the (hands-on) ADEX exam; WA has published nothing on the ADEX-integrated DLOSCE. A stand-alone DLOSCE passed before Oct 2026 remains usable for 5 years from the exam date (WAC 246-817-120(4)).
- **Licensure by examination - NDEB of Canada 'complete clinical examination' (Written + Virtual OSCE) for graduates of an approved school** (el becerisi: hayır): WAC 246-817-120(2)(b): 'The commission will accept the complete National Dental Examining Board (NDEB) of Canada clinical examination as meeting its standards if the applicant is a graduate of an approved dental school defined in WAC 246-817-110 (2)(a).' NDEB certification for accredited-programme graduates is the Written Examination + Virtual OSCE (both computer-based). Two uncertainties: (i) WAC 246-817-110(2)(a) defines approved schools as CODA-approved schools 'in the United States or Canada' (the ADA summary paraphrases this as 'graduate of an Accredited Canadian Dental School'), so a CODA-accredited school in Turkey may not qualify on a literal reading; (ii) whether DQAC regards the current Virtual OSCE as the 'complete NDEB clinical examination' covering the five required sections is not stated anywhere official. Needs written confirmation from DQAC.
- **Licensure by examination - another U.S. state/territory's own state-board clinical examination** (el becerisi: evet): WAC 246-817-120(2)(a)(vii): 'U.S. state or territory with an individual state board clinical examination' is accepted if it contains the five required sections. In practice these are hands-on exams; the California portfolio pathway is explicitly rejected by DQAC.
- **PGY-1 residency in lieu of the practical examination** (el becerisi: evet): RCW 18.32.040(3)(c) and WAC 246-817-110(8)(b): a CODA-accredited general practice residency, AEGD or pediatric residency of at least one year, located IN WASHINGTON STATE, in a program that serves predominantly low-income patients, is accepted in lieu of the practical exam (written exam still required). Clinical residency = hands-on patient care and would require a visa, so not suitable for this candidate.
- **Licensure without examination (by credentials) - RCW 18.32.215 / WAC 246-817-135** (el becerisi: hayır): Only for dentists holding an active unrestricted license in another U.S. state/territory who are 'currently engaged in the practice of dentistry' (federal service, dental-school employment, residency, or practicing at least 20 hours/week for the four consecutive years preceding application) plus CODA graduation (or 4 years practice + 1-year residency for non-approved schools), jurisprudence exam, photo, background. Not available to a new graduate; the underlying first license would itself have required a practical exam or residency.
- **Dentist and Dental Hygienist Compact privilege** (el becerisi: hayır): Rules to implement ESHB 1576 (compact) are still only at CR-101 stage (WSR 24-21-097, filed Oct 2024) - not yet available. Note RCW 18.415.010 (immigration-status non-discrimination) expressly excludes interstate compacts, and the compact itself requires an existing qualifying state license.

## Teori-only açıklaması
Washington's rule text contains two non-hands-on practical-exam options. WAC 246-817-120(2)(a) (amended effective 9 Aug 2026): 'The commission accepts the following practical examinations provided the testing agency offers at least the sections listed in subsection (1)(b) of this section: ... (viii) The Joint Commission on National Dental Examinations dental licensure objective structured clinical examination (DLOSCE)'; and (2)(b): 'The commission will accept the complete National Dental Examining Board (NDEB) of Canada clinical examination as meeting its standards if the applicant is a graduate of an approved dental school defined in WAC 246-817-110 (2)(a).' Combined with the written exam ((1)(a): INBDE accepted) and the jurisprudence exam, which DOH describes as 'Free, Online, Open book, and Multiple choice', a licence could be obtained with computer-based exams only and no residency (RCW 18.32.040(3)(c) residency is an alternative, not a requirement). The DOH September 2026 application packet (DOH 646-137) still offers DLOSCE as a stand-alone tick-box. Why only 'partial': (1) the stand-alone DLOSCE ceased after 9 Oct 2026 (known fact) and WA has issued no guidance on the ADEX-integrated DLOSCE, so unless the candidate already holds a stand-alone DLOSCE pass (valid 5 years, WAC 246-817-120(4)) this option is not currently obtainable; (2) the NDEB option is limited to graduates of an 'approved dental school defined in WAC 246-817-110(2)(a)', which reads: 'DQAC recognizes only those applicants who are students or graduates of dental schools in the United States or Canada, approved, conditionally or provisionally, by the Commission on Dental Accreditation of the American Dental Association ... (b) Other dental schools which apply for DQAC approval and which meet these adopted standards to the DQAC's satisfaction may be approved, but it is the responsibility of a school to apply for approval'. A CODA-accredited school in Turkey is not literally 'in the United States or Canada'; if DQAC treats Yeditepe as 'nonaccredited', WAC 246-817-160 would additionally require 'at least two additional predoctoral or postdoctoral academic years of dental education ... in a dental program in the United States or Canada, approved ... by the Commission on Dental Accreditation and include clinical training' - which is impossible on a visitor visa. The DOH Licensing Requirements web page states the education requirement more broadly ('graduate from a Commission on Dental Accreditation (CODA)-accredited dental school'), so DQAC's treatment of CODA-accredited international programmes must be confirmed in writing before relying on Washington.

## DLOSCE notları
DLOSCE is accepted in full as the practical examination: WAC 246-817-120(2)(a)(viii) (current text adopted by WSR 26-15-046, effective 9 Aug 2026, which merely renamed agencies and kept DLOSCE), the DOH 'Dentist - Licensing Requirements' page ('The Joint Commission on National Dental Examinations Dental Licensure Objective Structured Clinical Examination (DLOSCE)' listed among acceptable clinical examinations with no supplementary hands-on requirement), and the DOH application form 646-010 / instructions 646-137 (September 2026) which has a DLOSCE tick-box and 'Date of exam' line. Conditions: the exam must cover the five required sections (restorative, endodontic, periodontal, prosthodontic, treatment planning/diagnosis); all sections must be passed with the same agency (or two agencies coordinating); results must be from within the 5 years preceding the application; verification must come directly from JCNDE or be a notarized copy. Washington has published nothing about the ADA/ADEX integration or about DLOSCE scores earned 'as part of' ADEX; since the ADEX exam itself is an accepted hands-on exam, an ADEX pass makes the question moot, and a candidate holding only the DLOSCE component of ADEX (without the ADEX manikin sections) would need DQAC to confirm whether that score counts as the 'DLOSCE' under (2)(a)(viii) - unknown. Earlier stand-alone DLOSCE scores (taken before Oct 2026) satisfy the rule until five years from the exam date.

PGY-1 kabul: True

A state agency, regulatory authority, or disciplining authority shall not deny an application for a professional license ... solely on the basis of a person's immigration or citizenship status if the person has met all other qualifications.' It applies to all Title 18 professions (dentistry is chapter 18.32 RCW) except interstate compacts and private investigators/security guards/bail bond recovery agents. RCW 43.17.425(2)(c) separately requires DOH to ensure 'Agency employees may not condition services or request information or proof regarding a person's immigration status, citizenship status, or place of birth.' Address of record may be outside the US (WAC 246-12-310 only requires a current address on file). No Washington residency, in-state address or work authorization is required by RCW 18.32.040, RCW 18.32.100, WAC 246-817-110 or the form. The only identity-related process is the criminal background check (all applicants via WSP database; fingerprint-based WSP/FBI check only if DOH notifies you - see in-person steps).

## Yüz yüze adımlar
(1) Application: online via Secure Access Washington (SAW) / DOH Online Services or paper by mail; applicant signs with 'Original Signature' - no notarization of the application itself. (2) Exam score verification: 'sent electronically or directly from an approved examination organization or a notarized copy of the original examination scores' (DOH 646-137) - direct transmission avoids notarization; transcript/dean's list must be sent directly by the school. (3) Photograph: a recent 2x2 original photo, signed in ink across the bottom and dated - can be mailed from abroad. (4) Jurisprudence examination: 'Free, Online, Open book, and Multiple choice', must score 100%, can be taken any time during the application process; results transmitted electronically to DOH - fully doable from abroad. (5) Background check: 'A background check is processed for every applicant' (WSP name-based database). A fingerprint-based WSP/FBI check 'may be required if you have lived in another state or if you have a criminal record in Washington State' (DOH 646-145); the DOH Fingerprint Background Check page says it targets 'applicants from out-of-state' because they are 'unlikely to have criminal history information in the database maintained by the Washington State Patrol' and 'Most applicants for health profession licenses will not undergo this criminal background check.' If required, DOH mails a fingerprint card; 'Fingerprints must be taken on the fingerprint card provided by the department. Fingerprint services may be obtained at local law enforcement offices'; fee $36 (from 1 Oct 2026) plus the cost of fingerprinting; 'Do not pay for or schedule a background check until you receive an official email notification from the Department of Health.' Because the card is a mailed ink card, it can in principle be completed abroad or during a short US visit, but DOH does not state whether a foreign-resident applicant is treated as 'out-of-state' or whether foreign police fingerprinting is accepted - confirm with DOH (360-236-4700). (6) No interview, no oral exam, no in-person jurisprudence exam, no in-person appearance before the Commission for routine applications. (7) The clinical exams themselves (ADEX/CRDTS etc.) are in the US; the DLOSCE (when it existed stand-alone) was at Prometric centres; NDEB exams are run by NDEB. (8) BLS/CPR is not an application requirement.

## Diğer başlangıç şartları
Written exam: INBDE (or NBDE I+II, or NDEB written) with original/certified scorecard. Jurisprudence exam: free online open-book, 100% pass, any time before issuance. Education: official transcript with degree posted sent directly from the school, or for recent graduates a verified list of graduating students from the dean. Complete chronology of education and practice history since graduation. Verification of any other dental licences held (sent directly by that board) and DEA verification (if any). 'Proof of malpractice insurance if available' - insurance is not mandatory. Personal data questions (health, substance use, convictions, discipline) with explanations/certified documents for 'yes' answers. Authorization for NPDB/DEA inquiries. 2x2 signed/dated photo. Criminal background check (fingerprint-based only if DOH notifies). No English-language test, no CPR/BLS at application, no citizenship/immigration documentation. Practical exam results must be within 5 years of application. Fees $500 (non-refundable). Timelines: DOH does not publish an initial-application processing target; it urges online filing and prompt replies to emails; renewals are processed within about 7 business days (mail about 2 weeks). After licensure the first 3-year CE period starts on the licensure date; suicide-prevention (3 h one-time) and opioid-prescribing (3 h one-time, only if you want to prescribe opioids) trainings are due by the end of the first full CE reporting period.

## İlk ücretler
Initial application by examination: $500 (WAC 246-817-990; DOH fee page, fee on/after 1 Jan 2026 - non-refundable). Licensure without examination: $500 application + $500 initial license = $1,000. Fingerprint-based FBI check if required: $36 (from 1 Oct 2026) plus local fingerprinting cost. Jurisprudence exam: free. Exam fees (INBDE, DLOSCE/ADEX, NDEB) and score-verification/transcript fees are paid separately to those bodies.

## Lisansı sürdürme

- **renewal_period:** Annually, on the licensee's birthday (WAC 246-817-990(1); chapter 246-12 WAC). Renewable up to 90 days before expiry, online (SAW / DOH Online Services) or by mail. CE attestation every third renewal.
- **renewal_fee:** Active renewal $435 ($365 renewal + $70 impaired-dentist / Washington Physician Health Program surcharge, fees on/after 1 Jan 2026); late penalty $185; expired licence reissuance $300. Inactive renewal $195 ($125 + $70), late $50. Retired-active renewal $220 ($150 + $70), late $75.
- **ce_hours:** 63 hours every three years (WAC 246-817-440), attested on the renewal in the third year; random audit of up to 25% of dentists. Must include: the online jurisprudence exam once every 3 years (1 h), 2 h commission-approved health equity training every 3 years, 3 h in opioids and pain management, 3 h suicide prevention (one-time, from the DOH model list), 3 h opioid-prescribing CE (one-time, only to prescribe opioids). Keep documentation 4 years (WAC 246-12-200). Specialty board certification/recertification or AGD Fellowship/Mastership in the period counts as 62 h.
- **ce_online_ok:** Yes - 100% can be done from abroad. WAC 246-817-440(5)(a) gives full credit for 'live interactive webinars' with no cap; (5)(b)(i) gives full credit for 'live or recorded interactive webinars'; DOH FAQ: 'Full credit is allowed for live Webinars' and 'Full credit is allowed for recorded interactive webinars', 'No limit'. Other online/mail self-study counts at half rate (30 min per hour) with no cap. The jurisprudence exam is online-only. Health-equity training is available online (counts at half rate if self-study). No live/in-person component is required. BLS course hours are optional CE (max 3 h).
- **cpr_required:** BLS is NOT a renewal condition. WAC 246-817-720 requires a current health-care-provider BLS certification only for 'Dental staff providing direct patient care in an in-office or out-patient setting' and, when it applies, 'Initial and renewal certification must include both didactic and hands-on components' with an 'in-person skills assessment'. A licensee living abroad and not treating patients in Washington does not need it to renew.
- **practice_hours_required:** None to renew an active licence (no currency/practice-hour requirement in RCW 18.32 or WAC 246-817). Practice history matters only for (a) licensure by credentials (20 h/week for 4 years) and (b) returning from inactive status after more than 3 years, where 'actively practicing in another United States jurisdiction' within the last 3 years avoids re-examination (WAC 246-817-220(4)-(5)).
- **address_or_residency_required:** None. Only a current address of record (any country; form has a 'Country' field) and email must be kept up to date (WAC 246-12-310). No Washington residency or in-state practice address.
- **insurance_required:** Not mandatory. Application and reactivation rules ask only for 'proof of malpractice insurance if available'.
- **inactive_status:** Inactive licence available (RCW 18.32.185; WAC 246-817-220; WAC 246-12-090): must hold an active Washington licence in good standing and 'will not practice in Washington'; request in writing; renew annually on birthday at $195 (incl. $70 surcharge); 'You're not required to submit continuing education to renew an inactive dentist license'; no maximum duration. Retired-active status (WAC 246-817-230; WAC 246-12-120 to -140): $220/yr, 63 h CE per 3 years, may practise only in emergent or intermittent/non-permanent circumstances and may not receive compensation; must declare at each renewal that practice was only intermittent/emergency.
- **reactivation:** From inactive, 3 years or less: written request, declaration of CE for the two most recent years (DOH FAQ: 42 hours in previous two years), active renewal fee + surcharge - no exam. From inactive, more than 3 years AND actively practising in another US jurisdiction within the last 3 years: application, licence certification from that jurisdiction, proof of practice, CE declaration, fees - no exam. From inactive, more than 3 years AND NOT practising in another US jurisdiction (the likely scenario for someone living abroad): must provide 'Proof of successful completion of: (i) An approved practical/practice examination under WAC 246-817-120; or (ii) A qualifying residency program', plus licence certifications, CE declaration (two most recent years), jurisprudence exam within the past year, malpractice info 'if available', and fees (WAC 246-817-220(5)) - i.e. a new practical exam (hands-on unless a non-hands-on option such as DLOSCE/NDEB is accepted at that time). From retired-active: written request, declarations, 21 h CE in the previous year, active renewal fee - no exam (WAC 246-12-140; DOH FAQ). Expired licence: 1 cycle or less - late fee $185 + renewal + CE compliance; more than 1 cycle but under 3 years - abbreviated application, late fee, renewal, $300 reissuance fee, declarations incl. CE for two most recent years; 3 years or more - same plus 'Satisfy other competency requirements of the regulatory entity, if required' and comply with current statutory conditions (WAC 246-12-040(3); WAC 246-817-210) - DQAC may impose exam/competency conditions.
- **remote_maintainability:** easy
- **details:** An ACTIVE Washington dental licence can be kept indefinitely from abroad: pay $435 each year on your birthday (online), attest every 3 years to 63 CE hours (all obtainable via live/recorded interactive webinars at full credit, plus the free online jurisprudence exam and an online health-equity course), keep a current foreign address/email on file. No practice hours, no BLS unless treating patients in a Washington office, no insurance, no in-state address, no notarised renewal forms, no immigration documentation. The key strategic point: do NOT let the licence go inactive or expire for more than 3 years while not practising in a US jurisdiction, because returning to active status then requires a new practical examination or qualifying residency (WAC 246-817-220(5)) or discretionary competency requirements (WAC 246-12-040(3)(c)). Inactive status ($195/yr, no CE) is cheap but only safe for up to 3 years. Note the $70 surcharge increase from 1 Jan 2026 and that WAC 246-12 was comprehensively re-filed effective 14 June 2026 (WSR 26-11-023) without changing these mechanics.
- **practice_hours_note:** see practice_hours_required
