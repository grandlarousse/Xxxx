# Connecticut (USA) — ajan bulgusu

Düzenleyici: Connecticut Department of Public Health (DPH), Practitioner Licensing and Investigations Section (licensing authority); Connecticut State Dental Commission (advisory, approves exams under CGS 20-108) — https://portal.ct.gov/DPH/Practitioner-Licensing--Investigations/Dentist/Dentist-Licensure-Requirements

**Teori-only rota:** no · **Göçmenlik engeli:** medium · **Uzaktan sürdürme:** easy · **Güven:** medium

## Sınav yolları

- **ADEX Dental Examination via CDCA-WREB-CITA (regional board exam)** (el becerisi: evet): DPH approved list: 'Commission on Dental Competency Assessments (CDCA-WREB-CITA)'. Statute CGS 20-108 allows the Dental Commission to accept 'clinical or practical examinations' in lieu of the PGY-1 required by 20-107(a)(3); since 1 July 2021 such exams 'shall not require the participation of patients' (manikin OK, still hands-on). DPH: 'Candidates must successfully complete all components of a regional board examination; an overall passing score is not acceptable.' So the full ADEX series incl. the five manikin hand-skill components is needed; an ADEX-reported DLOSCE pass alone does not satisfy the 'all components' rule.
- **CRDTS, SRTA or WREB clinical exam + diagnostics component of CDCA or CITA clinical test** (el becerisi: evet): DPH: 'Candidates who have successfully completed a clinical performance examination offered by the Central Regional Testing Service, the Southern Regional Testing Agency or the Western Regional Examining Board must also pass the diagnostics component of the clinical performance test offered by the Commission on Dental Competency Assessments (formerly NERB) or the Council of Interstate Testing Agencies.' All are hands-on regional exams.
- **PGY-1 residency exemption (statutory default route, CGS 20-107(a)(3))** (el becerisi: evet): Statute requires 'at least one year of a clinically based postdoctoral general practice or specialty dental residency program accredited by the Commission on Dental Accreditation'. DPH: applicants from an ADA-accredited program who have not taken a regional exam 'may be exempt from the regional examination provided the applicant completed not less than one year of graduate dental training as a resident dentist in a program accredited by [CODA], provided that the supervising dentist attests to the resident dentist's competency in all areas tested on the [CDCA]. This exemption does not apply to foreign educated dentists.' A Yeditepe (CODA-accredited) graduate should count as ADA-accredited-program graduate, but a residency requires a US visa/clinical work, which the candidate excludes. Not an exam but involves live-patient clinical practice.
- **DLOSCE / computer-based OSCE only** (el becerisi: hayır): NOT accepted. Connecticut is not on the JCNDE DLOSCE 2026 list of accepting states; neither the ADA Connecticut summary (updated 7 Oct 2026) nor the DPH approved-exam list mentions DLOSCE; DPH requires all components of an approved regional board exam. The Dental Commission could in theory approve another 'clinical or practical examination' under CGS 20-108, but no such approval is published.
- **Licensure without examination / endorsement (CGS 20-110)** (el becerisi: hayır): 'The Department of Public Health may, upon receipt of an application and a fee of five hundred sixty-five dollars, issue a license without examination to a practicing dentist in another state or territory who (1) holds a current valid license in good professional standing issued after examination by another state or territory that maintains licensing standards which, except for the practical examination, are commensurate with the state's standards, and (2) has worked continuously as a licensed dentist in an academic or clinical setting in another state or territory for a period of not less than one year immediately preceding the application.' Not available to a new graduate; requires one year of continuous US practice (i.e. a licence elsewhere plus work authorisation). Notably the other state's exam standards need only be commensurate 'except for the practical examination', so a licence from a non-hands-on state plus one year of practice there could later convert to Connecticut.

## Teori-only açıklaması
CGS 20-107(a): no license shall be issued unless the applicant presents '(1) a diploma ... from a dental college ... accredited by [CODA]; (2) evidence of satisfactory completion of a written examination or examinations given by the Joint Commission on National Dental Examinations ...; and (3) evidence of satisfactory completion of at least one year of a clinically based postdoctoral general practice or specialty dental residency program accredited by [CODA].' CGS 20-108: 'The State Dental Commission may ... accept and approve the results of clinical or practical examinations ... in lieu of the clinically based postdoctoral general practice or specialty dental residency program ... On and after July 1, 2021 ... such clinical or practical examinations shall not require the participation of patients.' DPH implementation: 'Successfully complete an acceptable Regional Board Examination approved by the Connecticut State Dental Commission and Department of Public Health. The following regional examinations are approved: Commission on Dental Competency Assessments (CDCA-WREB-CITA), Southern Regional Testing Agency, Central Regional Dental Testing Service, Western Regional Examining Board. Candidates must successfully complete all components of a regional board examination; an overall passing score is not acceptable.' Every approved exam has manikin hand-skill components, and the only alternative is a one-year clinical residency; no OSCE-only or written-only option exists.

## DLOSCE notları
DLOSCE is not mentioned anywhere in the Connecticut statute (Ch. 379), the ADA Connecticut summary (updated 7 Oct 2026) or the DPH approved-exam list, and Connecticut is absent from the JCNDE DLOSCE 2026 list of accepting states. DPH's rule that candidates 'must successfully complete all components of a regional board examination; an overall passing score is not acceptable' means that once DLOSCE is a component of ADEX, a candidate must pass the DLOSCE AND all five manikin components; an ADEX-reported DLOSCE pass alone, or an earlier standalone DLOSCE score, would not satisfy Connecticut. CGS 20-108 lets the Dental Commission approve additional 'clinical or practical examinations' (patient-free since 1 July 2021), but no approval of DLOSCE as a standalone exam has been published.

## Yüz yüze adımlar
Connecticut's dentist application lists NO fingerprinting or criminal background check (the only check is the $4.75 National Practitioner Data Bank query), NO jurisprudence exam, NO interview and NO CPR course for initial licensure. The application is 'only accepted online' (DPH eLicense) and supporting documents (official transcript, National Board/INBDE score report, regional exam results, licence verifications) are sent directly by the issuing bodies, so the paperwork can be completed entirely from abroad. The only step requiring physical presence is the hands-on regional/ADEX examination itself at a US test site (possible on a B-1/B-2 visitor visa, but it is a manikin hands-on exam). For maintenance, the CE exemption for non-practising licensees requires 'a notarized application for exemption on a form prescribed by the department' (CGS 20-126c(e)); whether a foreign or consular notarisation is accepted was not verified.

## Diğer başlangıç şartları
(1) Diploma from a CODA-accredited program (official transcript sent directly by the school); (2) National Board (INBDE/NBDE) score report sent directly to DPH; (3) all components of an approved regional board exam (ADEX/CDCA-WREB-CITA, or CRDTS/SRTA/WREB plus the CDCA/CITA diagnostics component) OR a CODA PGY-1 with supervising-dentist attestation (not for foreign-educated dentists); (4) verification of every licence ever held in any state/territory; (5) online application and fee of $569.75. No jurisprudence exam, no CPR/BLS, no background check/fingerprints, no English test, no liability insurance, no state residency. Processing timeline not published on the page. Note: Connecticut's PGY-1 default and exam-in-lieu structure mean the state has no written-only track for anyone.

## İlk ücretler
Initial licence by examination: $569.75 (includes $4.75 NPDB query), applications online only. Licensure without examination (out-of-state, CGS 20-110): statutory fee $565 (DPH likely adds the $4.75 NPDB charge). Statutory examination fee under CGS 20-109: $565. Plus the cost of the regional exam itself (ADEX full series about $2,995 per the ADEX FAQ) and INBDE.

## Lisansı sürdürme

- **renewal_period:** Annual, in the licensee's birth month (CGS 20-113a: 'Licenses issued under this chapter shall be renewed annually in accordance with the provisions of section 19a-88'; DPH: 'Most licenses expire in the first birth month following issuance and are renewable annually thereafter'). Online renewal is mandatory for dentists; renewal notices are sent by email only.
- **renewal_fee:** Set by CGS 19a-88; believed to be $570 per year for dentists, but the figure was not verified on a page read in this session (cga.ct.gov fetch failed; DPH renewal page does not state it).
- **ce_hours:** 25 contact hours (50 min each) every 24 months, including at least one contact hour in each of any three of the ten Commissioner-prescribed mandatory topics, plus at least one hour in infection control in a dental setting and one hour in prescribing controlled substances and pain management (CGS 20-126c(b)). Up to 10 hours per 24 months may be substituted by volunteer practice at a public health facility (8 volunteer hours = 1 contact hour). First renewal after initial licensure is exempt.
- **ce_online_ok:** Yes. DPH: 'Internet-based, on-line and other distance learning opportunities are acceptable.' Statute lists 'courses, including on-line courses' as qualifying. No live or in-person component is required.
- **cpr_required:** No. CPR/BLS is not a renewal requirement. 'Medical emergencies in the dental office (including current training in ...)' is merely one of the ten optional mandatory-topic choices (a licensee picks any three), so a hands-on CPR course can be avoided entirely.
- **practice_hours_required:** None. No minimum practice or currency hours to renew or to keep the licence active.
- **address_or_residency_required:** None. No Connecticut address or residency required; licensee must simply keep an address of record and email current (dentists receive renewal notices 'by email only').
- **insurance_required:** No mandatory professional liability insurance appears in the licensing or renewal requirements (not separately verified in practice-act regulations).
- **inactive_status:** Connecticut DPH has no formal inactive/retired licence category (DPH renewal information: there is no inactive licence status; a licence is either renewed or lapses). Functional equivalent: a licensee 'who is not engaged in active professional practice in any form during a registration period shall be exempt from the continuing education requirements', on filing before expiry 'a notarized application for exemption on a form prescribed by the department' (CGS 20-126c(e); DPH 'Dentist CEU Waiver/Exemption' affidavit, attesting the dentist did not/will not practise in Connecticut during the period). The full annual renewal fee is still paid; no time limit on repeated exemptions is stated. The exemption statement warns the licensee 'may not engage in professional practice until the licensee has met the continuing education requirements'.
- **reactivation:** After CE exemption: simply complete the 25-hour CE requirement before resuming practice; no exam, no refresher, no practice hours. After lapse: a 90-day grace period after expiry (per DPH renewal information) after which the licence becomes void under CGS 19a-88; reinstatement under CGS 19a-14 requires an application plus 'evidence documenting successful completion of twelve contact hours of continuing education within the one-year period immediately preceding application for reinstatement' (CGS 20-126c(g)). The dentist-specific reinstatement page returned 404, so any additional requirements after a long void period (e.g. verification of other licences, competency review) could not be verified.
- **remote_maintainability:** easy
- **details:** Licence can be kept active indefinitely from abroad: annual online renewal and fee, email notices, 25 hours of CE per two years that may be entirely online, no CPR mandate, no practice hours, no address/residency requirement, no insurance. If the licensee is not practising at all anywhere, a notarized CE-exemption affidavit removes even the CE burden while the licence stays active (fee still payable). The main awkward items are the notarized exemption form (only if claiming exemption) and the unverified exact renewal fee.

1) How the DPH eLicense online dentist application handles an applicant with no SSN (blank field, ITIN, or explanation) – the AG's 2022 opinion says the law does not condition licensure on an SSN, but DPH's form practice was not verified; contact DPH Dental Licensing (410 Capitol Ave, Hartford). 2) Exact annual renewal fee under CGS 19a-88 (believed $570) – not verified on a read page. 3) Whether the State Dental Commission would ever approve a DLOSCE/ADEX-reported DLOSCE pass alone under CGS 20-108 – nothing published; current rule requires all components of ADEX. 4) Whether a foreign/consular notarisation is accepted for the CE-exemption affidavit. 5) Reinstatement requirements after a long void period (dentist reinstatement page returned 404). 6) Whether Connecticut treats a Yeditepe CODA-accredited graduate as 'ADA accredited program' for the PGY-1 exemption (DPH says the exemption 'does not apply to foreign educated dentists'; CODA accreditation should place the candidate in the accredited category, but DPH confirmation is advisable if that route were ever relevant). Bottom line for this candidate: Connecticut imposes no citizenship/immigration barrier and is very easy to maintain from abroad, but it has no theory-only route – a hands-on manikin regional exam (full ADEX) or a clinical residency is unavoidable; a later endorsement under CGS 20-110 would additionally need one year of US practice.
