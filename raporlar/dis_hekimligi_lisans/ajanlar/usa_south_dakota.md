# South Dakota (USA) — ajan bulgusu

Düzenleyici: South Dakota State Board of Dentistry — https://www.sdboardofdentistry.com/

**Teori-only rota:** no · **Göçmenlik engeli:** unclear · **Uzaktan sürdürme:** moderate · **Güven:** medium

## Sınav yolları

- **Licensure by examination (Application for New License) - board-approved hands-on clinical competency psychomotor exam: ADEX (CDCA-WREB-CITA), CRDTS, SRTA** (el becerisi: evet): SDCL 36-6A-44: applicant must (2) pass a national comprehensive exam (INBDE / NBDE I & II per ARSD 20:43:03:01(3)); (3) have 'graduated from an American Dental Association Commission on Dental Accreditation accredited United States dental school' (DDS/DMD); (4) 'within five years preceding the date of application ... passed a clinical regional or national examination approved by the board'; (5) pass a state laws-and-rules exam (written, cut score 70%); (6) no pending discipline; (7) references of good moral character. ARSD 20:43:03:01(4) requires 'verification of the applicant's passage, within five years ..., of a patient-based, simulation-based, or manikin-based clinical competency psychomotor examination approved by the State Board of Dentistry pursuant to 20:43:03:02' (three failures = ineligible). ARSD 20:43:03:02 requires the exam to allow 'direct observation by a board designee' and include periodontal, restorative, prosthodontic (simulation/manikin), endodontic (simulation/manikin) components and a remediation policy. ADA Oct-2026 summary lists ADEX (CDCA-WREB-CITA), CRDTS and SRTA as the accepted exams. All are hands-on manikin/patient exams.
- **Licensure by credential verification (SDCL 36-6A-47 / ARSD 20:43:03:04)** (el becerisi: evet): Only for dentists currently licensed in another state who have 'completed a minimum of three thousand dental clinical hours within the five years immediately preceding the date of application', graduated from a CODA-accredited United States dental school, passed the national exam; Board accepts CRDTS, WREB, CDCA, CITA or SRTA as the clinical exam. Application fee $600. Not available to a new graduate without another licence and 3,000 practice hours.
- **Temporary registration (SDCL 36-6A-49)** (el becerisi: evet): For a dentist or dental hygienist already licensed in another state; not an initial-licensure route for a new graduate.
- **Petition for review of substantial equivalence (SDCL 36-1D-1, universal-recognition style review)** (el becerisi: evet): Board webpage: 'An applicant who would like to petition for a review by the Board to determine if the requirements outlined in SDCL 36-1D-1 have been satisfied must submit an application, applicable fees, and documentation for review. For any South Dakota requirement you do not meet, please submit documentation that demonstrates substantial equivalence.' This is discretionary and still presupposes licensure elsewhere/equivalent clinical competency; not a theory-only route.
- **PGY-1 residency in lieu of clinical exam** (el becerisi: evet): NOT provided for in SDCL 36-6A-44 or ARSD 20:43:03; no residency substitute exists. (Residency would in any case require a visa.)
- **DLOSCE / OSCE-only** (el becerisi: hayır): NOT accepted. South Dakota is not among the DLOSCE-accepting states in the JCNDE 2026 guide, and ARSD 20:43:03:02 requires a 'psychomotor' exam with 'direct observation by a board designee' and manikin/simulation prosthodontic and endodontic components, which a computer-based OSCE cannot satisfy.

## Teori-only açıklaması
SDCL 36-6A-44(4): 'Within five years preceding the date of application, the applicant has passed a clinical regional or national examination approved by the board or a state examination or examinations that the board deems equivalent.' ARSD 20:43:03:01(4): 'Verification of the applicant's passage, within five years preceding the date of application, of a patient-based, simulation-based, or manikin-based clinical competency psychomotor examination approved by the State Board of Dentistry pursuant to § 20:43:03:02.' ARSD 20:43:03:02: the approved exam must 'allow direct observation by a board designee; and include: (1) A patient-based or an equivalent simulation-based or manikin-based periodontal component; (2) A patient-based or an equivalent simulation-based or manikin-based restorative component; (3) A simulation-based or manikin-based prosthodontic component; (4) A simulation-based or manikin-based endodontic component; and (5) A remediation policy'. No residency, portfolio or OSCE-only alternative exists in statute or rule. Additional structural problem: 36-6A-44(3) and 20:43:03:01(2) require graduation from a 'CODA accredited United States dental school'; Yeditepe (Turkey) is CODA-accredited but not a US school, so eligibility would need Board interpretation or a 36-1D-1 equivalence petition.

## DLOSCE notları
No DLOSCE/OSCE acceptance in SDCL 36-6A or ARSD 20:43:03, on the Board's application pages, or in the ADA Oct-2026 state summary (accepted exams: ADEX (CDCA-WREB-CITA), CRDTS, SRTA). The rule explicitly demands a psychomotor exam with manikin/simulation prosthodontic and endodontic components observed by a board designee, so a standalone DLOSCE score (pre-Oct 2026) or an ADEX-reported DLOSCE component alone would not satisfy 20:43:03:01(4); only a full passed ADEX series (which now includes DLOSCE plus the five manikin hand-skill exams) would count. Board states 'Verification from the testing agency that the exam meets all requirements may be requested.'

## Yüz yüze adımlar
(1) Fingerprint-based state and federal criminal background check (SDCL 36-6A-44.1; ARSD 20:43:03:01(9)): after receiving a completed application the Board MAILS the applicant a packet with the agency-specific fingerprint cards which 'must be used'; ink cards can normally be rolled at any police agency abroad and mailed back (Board does not mention Live Scan), so this appears doable from abroad though mailing to Turkey adds time. (2) State laws-and-rules (jurisprudence) exam: 'a written examination administered by the State Board of Dentistry on the relevant administrative rules and statutes with a cut score of at least seventy percent' (ARSD 20:43:03:01) - delivery format (online vs. at the Board office) not verified. (3) 'If requested by the State Board of Dentistry, an applicant must appear for a personal interview conducted by the board on a date set by the board' - discretionary. (4) Applications are decided at Board meetings; 'a complete application, fee, and all supporting documentation must be received by the board at least thirty days before the board meeting'. (5) The clinical exam itself (ADEX/CRDTS/SRTA) must be taken in person at a US test site. Notarisation requirements not verified (form not retrieved). Mailing address for documents: SD State Board of Dentistry, PO Box 42, Black Hawk, SD 57718.

## Diğer başlangıç şartları
INBDE or NBDE I & II; board-approved hands-on clinical exam passed within 5 years (max 3 failures); written SD laws-and-rules exam (70% cut score); copy of birth certificate or equivalent; recent photograph; current CPR card (AHA Healthcare Provider / ARC Professional Rescuer or Board-approved equivalent - Board requires a course that 'includes a hands-on skills assessment'); completed fingerprint cards for DCI/FBI check (fee payable); verification of any other licences; references of good moral character; no pending discipline. Degree must be DDS/DMD from a 'CODA accredited United States dental school' (eligibility question for Yeditepe graduates). No English test, no insurance requirement found. Timeline: Board acts at scheduled meetings; file at least 30 days before the meeting; background-check packet mailed after application receipt.

## İlk ücretler
Application for licence by credential verification: $600 (ARSD 20:43:03:04(1)). Application-by-examination fee set in ARSD 20:43:03:01(1) (amount not captured; expect similar order of magnitude). Fingerprint background-check fee charged to applicant (SDCL 36-6A-44.1). Annual registration fee $200 + $20 CE fee (ARSD 20:43:03:06). Clinical exam fees separate (ADEX full series about $2,995).

## Lisansı sürdürme

- **renewal_period:** Annual - renew by July 1 each year (SDCL 36-6A-52; ARSD 20:43:03:06); CE measured over a five-year licensure cycle.
- **renewal_fee:** $200 annual registration fee plus $20 continuing-education fee = $220/year (ARSD 20:43:03:06).
- **ce_hours:** 100 hours per five-year cycle, of which 50 must be 'academic' (course physically at a CODA school, presenter affiliated with a CODA school, ADA CERP-approved provider, or AGD PACE-approved provider). Caps per 5-year cycle: nutrition 15 h, practice management 10 h, home study 30 h, CPR 15 h (must be live, in-person). Extra 25 h anesthesia CE for sedation permit holders.
- **ce_online_ok:** Mostly yes, with a cap on non-interactive formats: 'Home study' (on-demand online courses, videos without instructor interaction) is limited to 30 of the 100 hours per 5-year cycle. However the Board's CE page states that 'live, interactive courses' delivered 'through Zoom, a webinar, or similar technology' where you can interact with the instructor count under the regular categories (Clinical etc.), so the remaining 70 hours can be earned via live webinars from abroad provided the academic-hour provider criteria (ADA CERP / AGD PACE / dental-school affiliated) are met. Only CPR CE credit must be live and in person.
- **cpr_required:** Yes. Every dentist 'shall maintain a current cardiopulmonary resuscitation card' (ARSD 20:43:03:06) from AHA Healthcare Provider, ARC Professional Rescuer or Board-approved equivalent; Board's CE page: the course must meet AHA BLS/CPR-ECC guidelines 'and include a hands-on skills assessment'. So a hands-on CPR skills session (available worldwide via AHA/ERC-equivalent providers) is needed to keep the card current, typically every two years.
- **practice_hours_required:** None for renewal of a licence obtained by examination. (3,000 clinical hours in 5 years applies only to the credential-verification route.)
- **address_or_residency_required:** No in-state address or residency requirement found; SDCL 36-6A-58 requires notifying the Board within ten days of any change of residence, name, employment or business address.
- **insurance_required:** No mandatory professional liability insurance found in SDCL 36-6A or ARSD 20:43.
- **inactive_status:** No inactive, retired or non-practising licence category found in SDCL 36-6A, ARSD 20:43:03 (as summarised by the ADA) or on the Board's licensing/renewal pages. The only option appears to be continuous active renewal or letting the licence lapse.
- **reactivation:** Not specified in the sources read. SDCL 36-6A-52: failure to renew by July 1 leads to lapse (exact consequence text truncated). SDCL 36-6A-57: a licensee who fails to meet CE requirements 'may, at the discretion of the board, be reexamined to determine his or her competency' and be suspended until competency is shown. A dentist whose licence has lapsed for years would most likely have to re-apply (clinical exam within the past 5 years under 36-6A-44, or credential route needing another state licence and 3,000 hours) - unverified.
- **remote_maintainability:** moderate
- **details:** Keeping the licence active from abroad is feasible: annual $220 fee, no practice hours, no in-state address, no insurance, and CE can be done remotely if at least 70 of the 100 hours per 5 years are live interactive webinars (home study capped at 30 h) from ADA CERP/AGD PACE/dental-school providers. The awkward parts are the current CPR card requiring a hands-on skills assessment and the absence of any inactive status (so a lapse likely means re-qualifying, including a new clinical exam within 5 years). Renewal is done online through the licensee account on the Board site.
