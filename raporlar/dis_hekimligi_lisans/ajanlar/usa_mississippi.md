# Mississippi (USA) — ajan bulgusu

## Sınav yolları

- **Licensure by examination (Miss. Code § 73-9-23; Board Regulation 14) – ADEX (CDCA-WREB-CITA) or SRTA clinical exam** (el becerisi: evet): For a graduate of a CODA-accredited dental school: age 21+, good moral character, diploma, INBDE (National Board Parts I & II 'or its successor'), successful completion of a 'Board-approved licensure examination' (ADA summary, updated 9 Oct 2026: 'American Board of Dental Examiners (ADEX, CDCA-WREB-CITA), SRTA'; Board text: ADEX, NERB, CITA or SRTA, accepted for a maximum of 5 years from passing; after 1 year the candidate must show active practice of at least 90 days per year). Board on 13 Jan 2023 'approved acceptance of the hybrid of either manikin or patient-based clinical examinations on a permanent basis' – i.e. a hands-on manikin or patient exam is still required. Reg. 14.4.b adds that if the clinical exam lacks a 'written/computerized comprehensive examination on applied clinical diagnosis and treatment planning' the candidate must ALSO pass the ADEX computerized exam (now the DLOSCE component) – it is an add-on, never a substitute. Plus Mississippi jurisprudence exam (75% pass) within 90 days of applying, notarized application with photo, references from two citizens of the applicant's state of residence, application fee ($250 per the 24 Apr 2026 fee schedule, previously $300 – extraction ambiguous).
- **DLOSCE-only / computer-based-only route** (el becerisi: hayır): NOT AVAILABLE. Mississippi is not among the states listed in the 2026 DLOSCE Candidate Guide as accepting the DLOSCE, and Reg. 14.4 only recognises 'clinical licensure examinations administered by other state and regional testing agencies'. The ADEX written/computerized component is required only as a supplement under Reg. 14.4.b. No board policy accepts an ADEX-reported DLOSCE pass alone.
- **PGY-1 residency in lieu of clinical exam** (el becerisi: hayır): NOT AVAILABLE. Neither § 73-9-23 nor Reg. 14 provides a residency pathway. Residency is mentioned only (a) to extend the INBDE-completion deadline for candidates in an ADA-accredited residency and (b) under Reg. 47, where an approved residency may count toward the five-year practice requirement for licensure by credentials.
- **Licensure by credentials (Miss. Code § 73-9-24; Board Regulation 47)** (el becerisi: evet): For dentists already licensed elsewhere: requires a minimum five-year period of active practice (residency may count toward it), having 'passed a state or regional clinical licensure examination', endorsement from all states of licensure, written statement agreeing to appear for interviews at the Board's request, jurisprudence exam, and a $2,500 application fee; the Board may also consider background checks, continuing education, and an 'agreement to initiate practice in the credentialing jurisdiction within a [stated period]'. Not open to a new graduate and still presupposes a hands-on clinical exam.
- **Institutional / teaching / provisional licence (Mississippi Code Title 73 Ch. 9; fee $250)** (el becerisi: hayır): Limited licence tied to employment at a Mississippi institution or dental school ('less than complete resident licensure'); not a general practice licence and requires presence/employment in Mississippi. Not useful for the candidate's purpose; details not fully verified.

## Teori-only açıklaması
Miss. Code § 73-9-23(1): 'No person who desires to practice dentistry ... in the State of Mississippi shall be licensed until that person has passed an examination by the board.' § 73-9-23(7): 'the board may by its rules and regulations prescribe reasonable professional standards for oral, written, clinical and other examinations given to applicants, and, if deemed necessary by the board, include a requirement that licensure examinations of applicants be conducted utilizing live human subjects. Each applicant shall appear before the board and be examined to determine his or her learning and skill in dentistry.' Board Regulation 14.4.a: 'For the purpose of licensure by examination, the Board may from time-to time recognize clinical licensure examinations administered by other state and regional testing agencies (hereinafter referred to as "Board-approved licensure examination").' The Board's accepted list is ADEX (CDCA-WREB-CITA) and SRTA, and on 13 Jan 2023 it 'approved acceptance of the hybrid of either manikin or patient-based clinical examinations on a permanent basis' – both are hands-on. Reg. 14.4.b (effective 1 Jan 2014) makes the ADEX computerized/written diagnosis-and-treatment-planning exam an additional requirement when the clinical exam lacks it, not a replacement. No DLOSCE-only, residency, or portfolio pathway exists.

## DLOSCE notları
The DLOSCE is not accepted as a clinical exam in Mississippi (Mississippi is absent from the 2026 DLOSCE Candidate Guide list; Board Reg. 14 recognises only regional/state 'clinical licensure examinations'). Reg. 14.4.b: candidates whose Board-approved clinical exam 'does not contain a written/computerized comprehensive examination on applied clinical diagnosis and treatment planning, aside from the written/computerized examinations administered by the Joint Commission on National Dental Examinations, will be required to successfully complete the American Board of Dental Examiners (ADEX) computerized/written comprehensive examination on applied clinical diagnosis and treatment planning prior to applying for licensure by examination' – i.e. the ADEX-integrated DLOSCE satisfies this supplemental written requirement for ADEX candidates, but the manikin hand-skill components remain mandatory. Standalone DLOSCE scores (taken before Oct 2026) have no standalone value here; nothing in the rules addresses them. No published Board policy on accepting an ADEX-reported DLOSCE pass alone.

## Yüz yüze adımlar
(1) Hands-on clinical exam (ADEX or SRTA) must be taken in person at a US exam site – unavoidable. (2) Application must be 'completed, signed, and notarized' with an unmounted bust photo attached; the form has three notary blocks (applicant and two character references). Whether a foreign (Turkish) notary or US consular notarization is accepted is not stated – unclear. (3) Jurisprudence exam: must be passed with 75% within 90 days of applying; based on the Dental Practice Act and Board regulations; the FAQ does not state the delivery format (online vs paper vs at Board office) – unclear. (4) Fingerprinting / criminal background check: no fingerprint or background-check item appeared in the exam application form or Reg. 14 text I searched; background checks are listed only as a discretionary factor for licensure by credentials – treat as 'probably none for exam route, unverified'. (5) § 73-9-23(7) says 'Each applicant shall appear before the board and be examined'; in practice the Board accepts regional exam results, but a personal appearance/interview could be requested (credentials route explicitly requires agreeing to interviews). (6) Reactivation after 3+ years without practice may require an in-person 'clinical competency assessment' administered by the Board (Reg. 49 ¶5), offered at least twice a year.

## Diğer başlangıç şartları
INBDE (National Board 'Parts I and II ... or its successor commission'); age 21; high-school education; good moral character; diploma from CODA-accredited school; Board-approved clinical exam passed within the last 5 years (and, if more than 1 year ago, proof of 90+ days/year active practice); ADEX written/computerized diagnosis & treatment-planning exam if the clinical exam lacked one (Reg. 14.4.b); Mississippi jurisprudence exam (75%, within 90 days of application; format unverified); notarized application + photo; references from two citizens of the applicant's state of residence (notarized letters acceptable); endorsements from all states where ever licensed; application checklist also lists 'Proof of Liability Insurance' and 'Proof of CPR' (CPR certification per Board Reg. 45); no English test for CODA graduates (English proficiency is required only for non-accredited foreign graduates under § 73-9-23(3)). Timeline: licence is issued after jurisprudence exam and Board review; Board meets periodically; scores of the clinical exam expire if INBDE not completed by 31 Dec of the same year.

## İlk ücretler
Application for dental license by examination: $250 per Board Reg. 37 fee schedule dated 24 Apr 2026 (document shows the old $300 figure struck through and $250 – text extraction rendered it '$30250.00', so confirm). Licensure by credentials: $2,500. Institutional/teaching/provisional: $250. Clinical exam paid separately to the agency (ADEX full series $2,995 per known facts). Jurisprudence exam fee: not stated. Annual dental license renewal fee $200 (collected biennially, i.e. ~$400 per two-year cycle) plus a mandatory impaired-licensee (PHN) assessment whose amount was not verified; a new licensee who pays within 90 days before 1 Sept is covered for the current and the upcoming renewal period.

## Lisansı sürdürme

- **renewal_period:** Biennial for dentists. Reg. 37: fees collected biennially (Board defines the biennial cycle as two consecutive one-year renewal periods); renewal opens 1 September with a two-month penalty phase from 1 November. CE reporting period per Reg. 41: 1 January of even year through 31 December of odd year.
- **renewal_fee:** 'Annual dental license renewal ... $200.00' (Reg. 37, 24 Apr 2026) collected for the biennial period (so about $400 per cycle) plus the mandatory impaired-licensee/PHN assessment ('The payment of the renewal fee for impaired licensees is mandatory for all currently licensed dentists'); amount of the assessment not verified. Board FAQ (per search summary): no reduced fee for inactive or retired status.
- **ce_hours:** 40 hours of Board-approved CDE per biennial renewal period (Reg. 41.2.B), including 3 hours of opioid management/prescribing. Exempt: first biennial renewal period after initial licensure; licensees in full-time postgraduate specialty training; licensees inactive for the whole renewal period. CPR hours count up to 8 hours (ACLS/PALS up to 16).
- **ce_online_ok:** Unclear. Reg. 41 lists approved providers and activity types (meetings, ADEX examiner calibration, CPR); I did not find or read any explicit cap on online/self-study hours – verify with Reg. 41 section 4/5. Nothing read requires live in-person CE.
- **cpr_required:** Yes – Board Regulation 45 requires CPR certification; proof of CPR is on the initial application checklist and is required when changing from inactive to active status (Active/Inactive FAQ: 'you must supply the appropriate proof of continuing education and certification in Cardiopulmonary Resuscitation'). Whether a hands-on skills session is mandated by Reg. 45 was not verified (AHA-style courses normally include one).
- **practice_hours_required:** YES – this is the decisive obstacle. Miss. Code § 73-9-19 ¶5: 'any licensed dentist ... who has actively practiced his or her profession for at least three (3) months of the immediately preceding license renewal period shall be considered in active practice'; Reg. 49 ¶3: the dentist 'must actively practice dentistry ... in the State of Mississippi a minimum of one (1) day per month for any three (3) months during each of the one-year license renewal periods ... to remain on active status with the Board ... Otherwise, dentists ... will be listed as inactive.' Only practice at a VA hospital, federal facility or residency programme in another state counts as active. Practice in Turkey does not keep the licence active.
- **address_or_residency_required:** No in-state address or residency requirement; § 73-9-19 maintains post-office/residential addresses for active and inactive licensees and requires notice of address change within 30 days. The inactive list expressly covers licensees 'registered but not actively practicing in this state'.
- **insurance_required:** 'Proof of Liability Insurance' appears on the Board's application checklist row of the exam application form; whether liability insurance is mandatory for renewal/maintenance was not verified – unclear.
- **inactive_status:** Statutory 'inactive list' (§ 73-9-19): a licensee 'registered but not actively practicing in the State of Mississippi at the time of making application for renewal, shall be registered on the inactive list and shall not be authorized to practice'; the Board must notify the licensee and offer a hearing before moving them to inactive. Inactive licensees must still renew every biennium and pay the full renewal fee and PHN assessment (no reduced inactive/retired fee per Board FAQ), but are exempt from CE for periods spent inactive. No maximum duration stated.
- **reactivation:** § 73-9-19: inactive licensee may return to active status on (a) written application stating the reasons for inactivity plus any other information the Board requires, or (b) evidence of active practice in good standing in another state. Reg. 41.8: 20 hours of Board-approved CDE within the 12 months before the request (CPR hours do not count) and pass the Board's jurisprudence exam; FAQ also requires CPR certification. Reg. 49 ¶5: if the dentist 'has not practiced dentistry ... for a period of three (3) or more years preceding the request ... the Board may require the applicant to submit to a clinical competency assessment administered either by the Board or other institution', given at least twice a year (e.g. during the annual licensure examinations), at the applicant's cost. Whether practice in Turkey counts as 'actively practiced ... in another state' is unclear. Lapsed/voided licence (Reg. 23, Reg. 41.9): reinstatement needs 20 CDE hours within 12 months and the jurisprudence exam, plus fees.
- **remote_maintainability:** hard
- **details:** The licence can be kept on the books indefinitely from abroad as an INACTIVE licence by paying the biennial renewal fee (about $400 + PHN assessment) with no CE, but it cannot be kept ACTIVE without physically practising in Mississippi at least one day a month in three months of every year. Returning to active after 3+ years without US practice may trigger a Board-administered clinical competency assessment (hands-on) in addition to 20 CE hours, jurisprudence exam and CPR.
