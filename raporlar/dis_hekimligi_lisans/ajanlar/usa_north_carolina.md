# North Carolina (USA) — ajan bulgusu

Düzenleyici: North Carolina State Board of Dental Examiners (NCSBDE) — https://www.ncdentalboard.org

**Teori-only rota:** no · **Göçmenlik engeli:** high · **Uzaktan sürdürme:** moderate · **Güven:** medium

## Sınav yolları

- **Licensure by examination: INBDE + ADEX clinical exam (CDCA-WREB-CITA) + Board jurisprudence and sterilization exams** (el becerisi: evet): Standard route for a new graduate of a CODA-accredited programme. 21 NCAC 16B .0303(b): applicants 'shall achieve passing scores on the examination administered by the Joint Commission on National Dental Examinations and clinical examinations administered by Board approved testing agencies'; .0303(a): at least 80 percent on the Board's sterilization and jurisprudence examinations. ADA Licensure-by-State page (updated 13 Oct 2026): 'The only acceptable clinical exam is the ADEX exam taken after 9/25/13. The optional perio scaling portion of ADEX is required in NC.' G.S. 90-30 requires the clinical exam to 'include procedures performed on human subjects as part of the assessment of restorative clinical competencies'; rule .0303(d)(1) allows 'an alternative method that simulates human subjects, including manikins'. Application fee $395 (21 NCAC 16M .0101(a)(1)); ADEX full series approx. $2,995 (known fact). Fingerprint-card SBI/FBI criminal history check (G.S. 90-30(b); 21 NCAC 16B .0301), photo, signed release form, sealed official transcripts.
- **Board-conducted written/oral and clinical examination** (el becerisi: evet): G.S. 90-30 authorises the Board 'to conduct both written or oral and clinical examinations'; 21 NCAC 16M .0101(a)(13) lists a 'Board conducted examination processing fee $805.00'. In practice the Board accepts only ADEX (ADA page); any Board-conducted exam would be clinical/hands-on. Not a theory-only route.
- **DLOSCE / OSCE-only** (el becerisi: hayır): NOT available. Neither G.S. 90-30 nor 21 NCAC 16B .0303 mentions the DLOSCE or any OSCE; the ADA page says ADEX is the only acceptable clinical exam. Rule .0303(d) requires an approved clinical exam to include restorative procedures on human subjects or manikins, clinical periodontics, and conjunctive scoring per section, which a computer-based OSCE cannot meet. (Listed only to record that it is closed.)
- **PGY-1 / residency in lieu of clinical exam** (el becerisi: hayır): NOT accepted. No statute or rule provision allows a postgraduate residency to substitute for the clinical licensing examination; the renewal form merely asks whether a licensee is 'enrolled in or completed a residency program' (21 NCAC 16R .0101(a)(12)). Intern permit ($150, 21 NCAC 16M .0101(a)(5)) exists only for trainees in NC programmes.
- **Licensure by credentials (G.S. 90-36; 21 NCAC 16B .0501)** (el becerisi: hayır): Only for dentists already licensed in another state with documented clinical practice ('not including post graduate training, residency programs or an...' per .0501), notarized application, $2,000 fee (16M .0101(a)(10)), malpractice-carrier verification, written Jurisprudence and Sterilization/Infection Control exams; Board 'may conduct examinations and interviews'. No hands-on exam but unavailable to a new graduate with no prior US licence/practice.
- **Licensure by endorsement (21 NCAC 16B .1001-.1002; fee $395)** (el becerisi: hayır): Referenced in 21 NCAC 16B .0201/.0301 and 16M .0101(a)(14) for applicants licensed elsewhere; rule text not read (Board site unreachable). Presumed to require an existing licence in another jurisdiction, so not open to a new graduate. Mark unclear.
- **Instructor's license / provisional license / limited volunteer license** (el becerisi: hayır): Instructor's license ($140) for faculty of NC dental schools; provisional ($100) and limited volunteer ($100) licences are restricted-practice categories tied to NC institutions or volunteer settings (G.S. 90-21.107), not general licensure for a new graduate living abroad.

## Teori-only açıklaması
G.S. 90-30 (quoted on ADA NC page, updated 13 Oct 2026): 'The North Carolina State Board of Dental Examiners is authorized to conduct both written or oral and clinical examinations or to accept the results of other Board-approved regional or national independent third-party clinical examinations that shall include procedures performed on human subjects as part of the assessment of restorative clinical competencies and that are determined by the Board to be of such character as to thoroughly test the qualifications of the applicant'. 21 NCAC 16B .0303(b): 'All applicants for dental licensure shall achieve passing scores on the examination administered by the Joint Commission on National Dental Examinations and clinical examinations administered by Board approved testing agencies.' .0303(d): 'the clinical examination administered by a testing agency shall: (1) include procedures performed on human subjects or an alternative method that simulates human subjects, including manikins, as part of the assessment of restorative clinical competencies; (2) include evaluations in clinical periodontics and at least three of the following subject matter areas... (3) provide ... (D) conjunctive scoring'. ADA summary: 'The only acceptable clinical exam is the ADEX exam taken after 9/25/13. The optional perio scaling portion of ADEX is required in NC.' No DLOSCE/OSCE, portfolio or PGY-1 alternative exists in the statute or rules, so a hands-on (manikin) ADEX clinical exam is unavoidable.

## DLOSCE notları
North Carolina's statute and rules contain no reference to the DLOSCE or any OSCE (grep of the full ADA laws-and-rules compilation found none). The Board accepts only the ADEX clinical examination (post-9/25/13) including the perio scaling section. Because the DLOSCE is now integrated as one component of the ADEX series, an NC applicant will in practice take it as part of ADEX, but it carries no independent weight: a DLOSCE pass alone (standalone pre-Oct-2026 score or ADEX-reported component) cannot satisfy 21 NCAC 16B .0303(d), which requires hands-on restorative, periodontal and other clinical-skills sections with conjunctive scoring. No Board policy accepting an ADEX-reported DLOSCE alone was found.

PGY-1 kabul: False

## Göçmenlik / kimlik

- **citizenship_or_pr_required:** False
- **work_authorization_required:** False
- **lawful_presence_verification:** True
- **ssn_or_sin_required:** True
- **itin_or_no_ssn_alternative:** None found. G.S. 93B-14 (as amended by S.L. 2025-94 s.27): 'Every occupational licensing board shall require applicants for licensure to provide to the Board the applicant's social security number, and the Board shall verify the authenticity of the applicant's social security number' (released 'to the Social Security Administration for the purpose of verifying the authenticity'). The statute offers no ITIN or no-SSN affidavit alternative. The NC Medical Board implements the same law by requiring an ink-signed, mailed SSA-89 consent form for every applicant after 21 May 2026; the Dental Board's own implementation could not be read (site unreachable via proxy) and should be confirmed directly with the Board.
- **in_jurisdiction_address_required:** False
- **barrier_level:** high
- **details:** No citizenship or permanent-residence requirement appears in the Dental Practice Act or 21 NCAC 16B for initial licensure, and nothing requires work authorisation per se. However (1) G.S. 93B-14 makes an SSN mandatory for every applicant to any NC occupational licensing board and, since S.L. 2025-94, requires the board to verify it with the SSA; a visitor without US status cannot obtain an SSN, so this is a de-facto bar unless the Board grants an accommodation not found in the statute. (2) The annual renewal rule 21 NCAC 16R .0101(a)(7) requires the renewal application to include 'citizenship or immigration status, with verifying documentation' - i.e. the Board verifies immigration status every year at renewal. (3) The initial application form's exact SSN/citizenship wording could not be read because www.ncdentalboard.org failed TLS verification through the proxy; the ADA compilation of the initial-licensure rules (16B .0301) contains no citizenship question. No general SAVE/lawful-presence statute for professional licences was found beyond these provisions. No in-state address or residency is required: G.S. 90-31 renewal asks for 'name, address and the county in which he has practiced during the preceding year' and 16R .0101 asks for a mailing address only.

## Yüz yüze adımlar
(1) ADEX clinical examination: hands-on manikin exam at a US test site - cannot be done from abroad (several days on site). (2) Criminal history check: G.S. 90-30(b) and 21 NCAC 16B .0301 require a 'Fingerprint Record Card, and such other form(s) required to perform a criminal history check at the time of the application. The form and card are available from the Board office' - ink fingerprint cards are mailed, so they can in principle be rolled by a police agency abroad and mailed back (not confirmed with the Board). (3) Board sterilization and jurisprudence examinations (80% pass, 21 NCAC 16B .0303(a)): delivery format (online vs. at the Board office in Morrisville) not verified; treat as possibly in-person. (4) Application: Board-furnished forms, photograph taken within six months affixed, signed substance-abuse release form, official transcripts mailed in sealed envelopes; the credentials application must be notarized (.0501) and the by-exam form very likely also (unverified); notarisation can be done at a US consulate or by a Turkish notary with apostille depending on Board acceptance. (5) SSN verification: if the Dental Board mirrors the Medical Board's implementation of S.L. 2025-94, an SSA-89 form must be physically signed in ink and mailed - doable from abroad but requires an SSN. (6) Renewal is fully electronic (21 NCAC 16R .0101(a)), but requires uploading 'citizenship or immigration status, with verifying documentation'. No interview is required for licensure by examination (interviews are discretionary only for licensure by credentials under G.S. 90-36).

## Diğer başlangıç şartları
Good moral character, at least 18 years old, graduate of a Board-recognised accredited dental school (G.S. 90-30); INBDE pass; ADEX clinical exam (post-9/25/13) including perio scaling; Board sterilization and jurisprudence exams at 80% (reexamination allowed per 16B .0317); SBI/FBI fingerprint criminal history check via NC Department of Public Safety (DPS 'may charge each applicant a fee'); photograph; signed release re substance-abuse treatment within last 10 years; sealed official undergraduate and dental-school transcripts; verification of any other state licences; SSN and SSA verification (G.S. 93B-14); 'All persons practicing dentistry in North Carolina shall maintain unexpired CPR certification at all times' (21 NCAC 16B .0201(c)) - CPR is required to practise, and the renewal asks whether the licensee holds unexpired CPR certification; no English-language test for a CODA graduate; no liability-insurance requirement for licensure by examination. Incomplete applications are returned; address changes must be reported within 10 business days. Processing timelines not published in the material read.

## İlk ücretler
Application for general dentistry license $395 (21 NCAC 16M .0101(a)(1)); Board-conducted examination processing fee $805 (only if the Board itself examines); DPS/SBI/FBI criminal background check fee (amount set by DPS, not stated); ADEX dental examination series approx. $2,995 paid to CDCA-WREB-CITA (known fact); INBDE fee to JCNDE; licensure by credentials $2,000 and endorsement $395 (not applicable to a new graduate). ## Lisansı sürdürme

- **renewal_period:** Annual: renewal application must be submitted electronically before midnight on January 31 each year (G.S. 90-31; 21 NCAC 16R .0101(a)).
- **renewal_fee:** $289 renewal of general dentistry license + $40 NC Caring Dental Professionals assessment = $329/year; late renewal fee $50; reinstatement of license $225 (21 NCAC 16M .0101).
- **ce_hours:** 15 clock-hours of continuing education each calendar year (21 NCAC 16R .0201(a)); 1 of the 15 must address controlled-substance prescribing if the licensee prescribes controlled substances; a 6-hour professionalism and ethics course (within the 15) in the first CE year for licensees issued after 1 Jan 2019 who are 'actively engaged in the practice or teaching of dentistry in North Carolina'. CE must relate to clinical patient care (courses on practice management/personal development do not count). CE is reported on the renewal form and subject to audit (16R .0203).
- **ce_online_ok:** Yes. 21 NCAC 16R .0201(d): 'Any or all of the hours may be acquired through self-study courses, provided that the self-study courses are related to clinical patient care and offered by a Board-approved sponsor listed in Rule .0202 of this Section. The dentist shall pass a test following every self-study course and obtain a certificate of completion.' Approved sponsors include ADA CERP-recognised providers. No live/in-person component required. CPR courses do not count toward CE (.0201(e)).
- **cpr_required:** CPR certification must be maintained 'at all times' by all persons practising dentistry in NC (21 NCAC 16B .0201(c)); the renewal form asks whether the licensee holds unexpired CPR certification (16R .0101(a)(13)). Whether a hands-on skills session is mandatory is not specified in the rules read (the Board's CPR policy page could not be accessed). Semi-retired Class I/II dentists must keep CPR, but 'A retired dentist who does not practice any dentistry shall be exempt from all continuing education and CPR certification requirements' (16R .0204(a)(3)).
- **practice_hours_required:** None. No minimum practice or currency hours are required to renew; the renewal only asks the number of hours worked in an average week and employment type (16R .0101(a)(10)).
- **address_or_residency_required:** No NC residency or in-state address required; the renewal collects a mailing address and the county practised in during the preceding year (G.S. 90-31). Address changes must be reported within 10 business days.
- **insurance_required:** Not required for licensure by examination or renewal (no provision found); malpractice-carrier verification is required only for the licensure-by-credentials pathway (16B .0501).
- **inactive_status:** No formal inactive/retired licence category in the rules read; instead the Board grants CE exemptions on written evidence (21 NCAC 16R .0204(a)): (1) semi-retired Class I (practises not more than 250 clock hours/yr) - exempt from all CE, must keep CPR; (2) semi-retired Class II (not more than 1,000 hrs/yr) - half CE, keep CPR; (3) 'A retired dentist who does not practice any dentistry shall be exempt from all continuing education and CPR certification requirements'; (4) disability variance. The licence itself still must be renewed every year at the full fee ($329) with the full renewal form, including 'citizenship or immigration status, with verifying documentation'. No time limit on the exemption is stated.
- **reactivation:** 21 NCAC 16R .0204(b): 'In those instances where continuing education is waived and the exempt individual wishes to resume practice, the Board shall require continuing education courses in accordance with Paragraph (a) of this Rule when reclassifying the licensee. The Board may require those licensees who have not practiced dentistry for a year or more to undergo a bench test before allowing the licensee to resume practice if there is evidence that the licensee is unable to practice dentistry competently, such as a failing score on a dental licensing examination, a written report of a licensed physician...' - i.e. CE catch-up required; a bench (clinical) test only where there is evidence of incompetence. Lapse: if renewal is not received by 31 Jan a $50 late fee applies and practising without renewal is an offence (G.S. 90-31); reinstatement fee $225 (16M .0101(a)(8)); 21 NCAC 16B .0900 provides an extension period for eligible licensees to pay fees, obtain CPR and complete CE - details not read.
- **remote_maintainability:** moderate
- **details:** Mechanically the licence is easy to keep from abroad: fully online annual renewal (~$329), 15 CE hours entirely by online self-study with a post-course test, or a complete CE+CPR exemption as a non-practising 'retired dentist' (requires written evidence/request), no practice hours, no in-state address, no insurance. The complication is that every renewal application must include 'citizenship or immigration status, with verifying documentation' (21 NCAC 16R .0101(a)(7)); what documentation the Board accepts from a non-resident foreign national living abroad is unknown, and the initial SSN requirement must already have been met. Rated moderate on mechanics; the immigration documentation is the uncertain element.
