## Sınav yolları

- **NDEB certificate + ODQ diploma-equivalence recognition leading to a permit (graduates of CDAC/CODA-accredited programmes)** (el becerisi: hayır): ODQ page 'Diplômés au Canada': 'Si vous détenez un certificat du Bureau national d'examen dentaire (BNED) du Canada et un diplôme délivré par une faculté dentaire canadienne ou américaine agréée par la Commission de l'agrément dentaire du Canada, vous pouvez déposer une demande de reconnaissance d'équivalence de diplôme conduisant à la délivrance d'un permis d'exercice de l'Ordre des dentistes du Québec.' Steps: (1) file 'Demande de reconnaissance d'équivalence de diplôme' with university transcript (courses, credits, results), certified copy of dental diploma(s), certified copy of NDEB certificate or proof of success, CV (work experience and CE), translations if documents are not in French or English, plus an administration fee; applications are assessed every two months. (2) Then file Form A1 'Demande d'obtention de permis d'exercice, de certificat de spécialiste ou de réinscription' with the required documents (incl. Certificate(s) of professional conduct, Form C2, and a copy of driver's licence, health-insurance card or passport for identity verification) at least 15 business days before the monthly Comité d'admission meeting. (3) Prove ability to work in French (Charter of the French Language) - OQLF exam or exemption, or a temporary permit (s.37) valid max 1 year renewable up to 3 times. (4) Oath-taking (prestation de serment) organised by the ODQ, pay permit + tableau fees and subscribe to the FARPODQ liability fund. The NDEB certificate for accredited-programme graduates is obtained through the computer-based Virtual OSCE only; no ODQ clinical/hands-on exam is mentioned for NDEB-certificate holders. CAVEAT: the page wording refers to a 'faculté dentaire canadienne ou américaine agréée par la CDAC'; whether the ODQ treats CODA-accredited Yeditepe (Turkey) as such is not stated and must be confirmed with the ODQ (admission@odq.qc.ca, 514 875-8511 ext. 2231).
- **Canadian mobility (Règlement sur les autorisations légales d'exercer la profession de dentiste hors du Québec)** (el becerisi: hayır): A dentist already licensed in another Canadian province/territory signatory to the mobility agreement can obtain an ODQ permit on proof of that legal authorisation plus identity, diploma and NDEB certificate; French requirement still applies. Not a first-licence route by itself.
- **ODQ examination (historical / non-NDEB holders)** (el becerisi: evet): Older regulation (1997 Gazette) refers to an 'examen de l'Ordre' for candidates who have not passed the NDEB; not relevant to an NDEB-certificate holder and its current existence/format was not verified.
- **France-Quebec mutual recognition arrangement (ARM)** (el becerisi: hayır): Separate route for French-trained dentists only (page 'Diplômés en France'); not applicable to this candidate.
- **PGY-1 residency in lieu of exam** (el becerisi: evet): Not a Quebec pathway. 'Membre résident' status exists only for dentists enrolled in a Quebec university postgraduate/residency programme; it does not replace the NDEB certificate.

## Yüz yüze adımlar
No fingerprinting or criminal-record check is mentioned on the ODQ permit pages (good-conduct is covered by Form C2 certificates from regulators/universities). Certified copies ('copie certifiée conforme') of diploma and NDEB certificate are required (can be prepared abroad). Applications go to the Comité d'admission, which meets once a month; file at least 15 business days before. New dentists take part in an oath-taking session ('prestation de serment') organised by the ODQ - format (in person in Montreal vs. remote) not stated. The French-language proof is the main in-person element: the OQLF French examination is normally sat in Quebec (written and oral components) unless an exemption applies; a s.37 temporary permit allows practice while learning French but requires the OQLF exam after each renewal and expires after a maximum of 4 years. No ODQ jurisprudence/ethics examination appears on the pages read (3 UFC of ethics CE per 3 years applies after licensure). Everything else (forms, documents, fees) can be sent by email/mail.

## Diğer başlangıç şartları
French: 'Conformément aux exigences de la Charte québécoise de la langue française, les candidats de tous les ordres professionnels du Québec doivent démontrer leur capacité à travailler en français' (ODQ); exemptions under s.35 for persons with 3+ years of full-time French-language secondary/post-secondary education or recent Quebec French secondary-school exams; otherwise OQLF exam or s.37 temporary permit (max 1 year, renewable up to 3 times). Certificate(s) of professional conduct (Form C2) from every jurisdiction where previously registered. Mandatory subscription to the Fonds d'assurance-responsabilité professionnelle de l'ODQ (FARPODQ) on registration, unless an exemption (e.g. employer insurance) is granted. Translation of non-French/English documents. Oath. CPR (RCR-DEA with practical component) is a CE obligation (3 UFC per 3-year period) rather than a documented initial-licence condition. Timelines: equivalence requests assessed every two months; admission committee monthly; file 15 business days ahead.

- **address_or_residency_required:** No Quebec residency requirement found. Members declare their place(s) of practice (lieux d'exercice) to the Order; an inactive member declares that they do not practise. 'Déménagement à l'extérieur du Québec' is listed as a reason for resigning from the tableau, and 'Séjour temporaire à l'étranger' as a reason for inactive status - the ODQ frames living abroad as temporary.
- **insurance_required:** Yes for active members: 'Tout membre actif doit souscrire au Fonds d'assurance-responsabilité professionnelle de l'Ordre des dentistes du Québec (FARPODQ), sauf s'il fournit une preuve indiquant qu'une assurance lui est offerte par son employeur.' Inactive member declares to the ODQ and FARPODQ that he/she does not practise (premium not payable / refunded pro rata on resignation).
- **inactive_status:** 'Membre inactif': declares to the Order and the FARPODQ that he/she does not practise dentistry; accepted motives listed include period of disability/illness, maternity or parental leave, prolonged studies, temporary stay abroad ('Séjour temporaire à l'étranger'). Keeps: reduced dues at 15% of the active regular cotisation, right to the title 'Docteur/Docteure', voting rights, access to publications. CE obligations continue to apply to inactive members ('tous les membres inscrits au tableau ... que ce soit à titre de membre actif ou inactif, doivent répondre aux obligations' of the CE regulation), though a dispense (exemption) can be requested for listed motives with supporting proof. No maximum duration stated on the page. Alternative: 'membre démissionnaire' (resignation, e.g. moving outside Quebec) - no dues, cannot practise or use the title; reinstatement on request with fees.
- **reactivation:** Inactive to active: 'Avant de reprendre vos activités professionnelles, vous devrez communiquer avec l'Ordre ... pour l'étude de votre dossier' - discretionary file review; no automatic re-examination stated, but conditions (refresher, supervision, inspection) are possible and not specified. After resignation: 'Réinscription au tableau' by written request, fees apply; Form A1 covers reinscription; conditions after long absence not specified on the pages read (Professional Code allows the Order to impose refresher training). French: a temporary permit holder must pass the OQLF exam within the max 4-year window.
- **remote_maintainability:** moderate
- **details:** Keeping an ODQ registration from abroad is possible in principle through inactive status (15% dues, no FARPODQ premium, no practice-hour rule found), but inactive members remain bound by the 90-UFC/3-year CE regulation including 3 UFC of hands-on CPR (online-only not recognised) and 30 UFC from ODQ-recognised providers, unless a dispense is granted; the inactive-status motive is worded as a temporary stay abroad; and reactivation is a discretionary file review. Fee amounts and the maximum duration of inactive status were not verified.
