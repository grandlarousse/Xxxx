# Pennsylvania (USA) — ajan bulgusu

Düzenleyici: Pennsylvania State Board of Dentistry (Department of State, Bureau of Professional and Occupational Affairs); applications filed through PALS (www.pals.pa.gov) — https://www.pa.gov/agencies/dos/department-and-offices/bpoa/boards-commissions/dentistry

**Teori-only rota:** partial · **Göçmenlik engeli:** medium · **Uzaktan sürdürme:** moderate · **Güven:** medium

## Sınav yolları

- **Standard examination route: INBDE/NBDE + regional/ADEX clinical examination** (el becerisi: evet): 49 Pa. Code § 33.103(a): 'Candidates for licensure shall pass the National Board Dental Examination (written examination) and the dental clinical examination administered by one of the following: (1) NERB [now CDCA-WREB-CITA/ADEX] (2) SRTA (3) WREB (4) CRDTS (5) CITA.' The Board's Oct-2026 snapshot lists ADEX, CDCA, CRDTS, CITA, SRTA, WREB. Clinical scores are accepted for 5 years from the date reported (§ 33.103(d)); after 5 years only if the candidate has been in postgraduate training or practising elsewhere. All listed agencies administer manikin/patient-based hands-on exams. Education: diploma from a CODA-accredited (or CDAC-accredited, per the Board snapshot) school (§ 33.102(a)(1)). Yeditepe graduates after 1 Feb 2024 qualify as CODA graduates.
- **DLOSCE-only / computer-based OSCE route** (el becerisi: evet): NOT available. Neither 49 Pa. Code § 33.103 nor the Board snapshot mentions the DLOSCE or any OSCE; Pennsylvania is not among the DLOSCE-accepting states in the 2026 DLOSCE guide. A DLOSCE pass (standalone or as the ADEX-reported component) does not meet the § 33.103 requirement for 'the dental clinical examination administered by' one of the listed agencies.
- **PGY-1 / residency in lieu of clinical exam** (el becerisi: evet): NOT available. No residency alternative exists in 63 P.S. § 122 or 49 Pa. Code §§ 33.102-33.107. Postgraduate training is mentioned only to extend the 5-year validity of clinical-exam scores (§ 33.103(d)).
- **Licensure by criteria approval (credentials) incl. NDEB-certified applicants** (el becerisi: hayır): 63 P.S. § 122(f) empowers the Board 'to accept and endorse ... licenses to practise dentistry ... issued by another state or territory or of Canada, and to accept as properly qualified any applicant who has been examined and certified by the National Dental Examining Board ... Provided, That the standards of such other state, territory or of Canada or of such national board are, in the opinion of the board, equal to the standards of this Commonwealth.' Implementing rule 49 Pa. Code § 33.107: dentists 'who are currently licensed ... in another state or territory or in Canada may be granted licensure ... if the credentials submitted meet the requirements of the act and of this subchapter. Licensure ... by criteria approval will be granted only to applicants whose licensing boards confirm that they reciprocate with the Commonwealth'; letters of good standing from every state of practice; Board may require a personal interview. Theoretically an NDEB-certified dentist (Virtual OSCE only) holding a current Canadian provincial licence could use this, but (i) a current Canadian licence is a prerequisite, (ii) the regulation's cross-reference to 'this subchapter' arguably imports the § 33.103 clinical-exam requirement, and (iii) equivalence/reciprocity is at the Board's discretion. No published Board policy confirms acceptance of NDEB certification without a regional clinical exam. Treat as speculative.
- **Licensure by endorsement (Act 41 of 2019, 63 Pa.C.S. § 3111; 49 Pa. Code § 33.107a, eff. 26 July 2025) and provisional endorsement licence (§ 33.107b)** (el becerisi: hayır): Requires a current licence in good standing from a jurisdiction with substantially equivalent standards (applicant must submit that jurisdiction's law/rules, translated if needed) AND active licensed practice for at least 2 of the 5 years immediately preceding the application. Not usable by a new graduate.
- **Limited licence for teaching (63 P.S. § 122(c.1)) / temporary volunteer licence** (el becerisi: hayır): Niche categories without examination for faculty appointments or volunteer events; not a general practice licence and not relevant to the candidate.

## Teori-only açıklaması
For a direct applicant the answer is effectively NO: 49 Pa. Code § 33.103(a) requires passing 'the National Board Dental Examination (written examination) and the dental clinical examination administered by one of the following: NERB [CDCA-WREB-CITA/ADEX], SRTA, WREB, CRDTS, CITA' -- all hands-on exams; there is no DLOSCE, OSCE, portfolio or PGY-1 alternative, and the Board's own snapshot (read 2 Nov 2026) repeats '2 Examinations Required'. The only theory-only text in Pennsylvania law is the credentials clause in 63 P.S. § 122(f): the Board may 'accept as properly qualified any applicant who has been examined and certified by the National Dental Examining Board [of Canada] ... Provided, That the standards ... are, in the opinion of the board, equal to the standards of this Commonwealth'. Under 49 Pa. Code § 33.107 this is available only to a dentist 'currently licensed ... in Canada' whose licensing board confirms reciprocity, and the credentials must 'meet the requirements of the act and of this subchapter' (which contains § 33.103). So a theory-only path is written into the statute but needs something extra (a Canadian provincial licence obtained via NDEB Virtual OSCE, plus a discretionary equivalence finding) and is unverified in practice -- hence 'partial', not 'yes'.

## DLOSCE notları
Pennsylvania does not accept the DLOSCE in any form. 49 Pa. Code § 33.103 (quoted in the ADA 'Pennsylvania Laws & Rules' summary updated 13 Oct 2026) and the Board's Dentist Licensure Snapshot list only the NBDE/INBDE plus a clinical examination administered by ADEX/CDCA, CRDTS, CITA, SRTA or WREB. No rule or Board notice addresses the DLOSCE, the ADEX-integrated DLOSCE component, or standalone DLOSCE scores. Because the rule requires 'the dental clinical examination administered by' those agencies, a Board would read that as the full ADEX series (manikin components included); an ADEX-reported pass on the DLOSCE component alone would not satisfy § 33.103.

PGY-1 kabul: False

## Yüz yüze adımlar
(1) Clinical exam: the ADEX/CDCA-WREB-CITA, CRDTS, SRTA, CITA or WREB exam must be taken in person at a US test site (manikin components) -- unavoidable under § 33.103. (2) INBDE: computer-based at Prometric; can be taken before travelling. (3) No jurisprudence exam exists in Pennsylvania (only '2 Examinations Required'); no oral interview for standard applicants (the Board 'may grant a personal interview' only for criteria-approval applicants, § 33.107). (4) Criminal history: Pennsylvania's dental application historically requires a Criminal History Record Check from each state/country where the applicant lived, worked or trained in the past 5 years (documents, not Live Scan); for a Turkey-resident applicant that is a Turkish criminal record certificate -- obtainable from abroad. Act 79 of 2024 introduced fingerprint-based checks for some Department of State licences; whether it now covers dentists was not verified. (5) Notarisation: the initial application is online (PALS) with document uploads; a notarised affidavit is required only for duplicates (§ 33.104(c)) and for reactivation of a lapsed licence (§ 33.106(a)) -- can be done at a US consulate or by remote online notarisation. (6) 3-hour child-abuse recognition course (Act 31) and 8-hour DEA/MATE training (only if a DEA registration is sought) are available online. Net: apart from the hands-on clinical exam, everything can be completed from abroad or during a short visit.

## Diğer başlangıç şartları
Age 21+, good moral character, no Controlled Substance Act felony (63 P.S. § 122(c)). Graduation from a CODA- or CDAC-accredited school (§ 33.102; snapshot states CDAC is recognised alongside CODA). INBDE + regional/ADEX clinical exam (§ 33.103), clinical scores valid 5 years. 3 hours of DHS-approved child abuse recognition and reporting training for initial licensure (49 Pa. Code § 33.256(a); online providers exist). No jurisprudence exam, no English test, no CPR proof at initial application (CPR is a renewal condition), no insurance at application (63 P.S. § 122.2 liability-insurance requirement applies to practising dentists). DEA/MATE Act 8-hour training only if applying for DEA registration. Criminal history checks per Board practice (see in-person steps). Timeline: PALS processing timelines are published separately (not read); the licence expires 31 March of the next odd-numbered year regardless of issue date (§ 33.104(a)); CE is waived for the biennium in which the licensure exam was passed (§ 33.401(g)(1)).

## İlk ücretler
Initial dentist application/licence fee $260 (effective 1 July 2025; 49 Pa. Code § 33.3 shows a stepped schedule $235 -> $260 -> $282 for later bienniums). Biennial renewal $366 (1 Apr 2025 - 31 Mar 2027). Exam fees extra: INBDE (JCNDE) and ADEX full series (~$2,995 per known facts) or other regional exam; child-abuse course (~free-$50); criminal history checks (varies). Processing time not verified.

## Lisansı sürdürme

- **renewal_period:** Biennial; every licence expires 31 March of odd-numbered years regardless of issue date (49 Pa. Code §§ 33.104(a), 33.105(a)). Next cycle ends 31 Mar 2027.
- **renewal_fee:** $366 per biennium (Board snapshot, effective 1 Apr 2025 - 31 Mar 2027; § 33.3 schedule indicates further stepped increases).
- **ce_hours:** 30 hours per biennium for dentists (63 P.S. § 122(j.2)(2)(i); 49 Pa. Code § 33.401(a)(1)), including 2 hours of approved child abuse recognition and reporting CE (§ 33.401(h), § 33.256(b)). Exempt for the biennium in which the licensure exam was passed (§ 33.401(g)(1)).
- **ce_online_ok:** Not 100% self-study. § 33.401(c): 'At least 50% of the required credit hours shall be taken in lecture or clinical presentations.' § 33.401(d): 'A maximum of 50% of the required credit hours ... may be taken through individual study' (defined as a course from an approved sponsor that lets the participant learn without direct instructor interaction). The rule does not say whether a live interactive webinar counts as 'lecture'; if the Board accepts live-streamed lectures (common practice, unverified), all 30 hours can be done from abroad; otherwise ~15 hours must be in-person lectures. The 2-hour child-abuse course is available online from DHS-approved providers (electronic reporting required).
- **cpr_required:** Yes. Condition of every renewal: 'current certification in infant, child and adult cardiopulmonary resuscitation', courses 'substantially similar in content to the CPR courses offered by the American Heart Association and the American Red Cross and have a similar renewal period' (63 P.S. § 122(j.1); 49 Pa. Code § 33.105(b)). AHA/ARC-equivalent courses include a hands-on skills check; online-only cards are unlikely to be accepted (not expressly addressed). AHA courses are available in Turkey.
- **practice_hours_required:** None. No currency/practice-hour requirement for renewal in the Dental Law or 49 Pa. Code ch. 33.
- **address_or_residency_required:** No in-state address or residency. Licensees must notify the Board in writing within 10 days of an address change (49 Pa. Code ch. 33).
- **insurance_required:** 63 P.S. § 122.2 (Dental Law § 3.2) requires dentists to maintain professional liability insurance (referenced in 49 Pa. Code § 33.107a); whether a licensee who does not practise in Pennsylvania must carry it (renewal attestation typically allows 'not practising in PA') was not verified.
- **inactive_status:** Pennsylvania licences may be allowed to lapse or be placed on inactive status (Department of State practice; no fee and no CE while inactive -- not verified from a page in this session). Reactivation is governed by 49 Pa. Code § 33.106 (see reactivation). No maximum duration, but lapse >5 years triggers possible re-examination.
- **reactivation:** 49 Pa. Code § 33.106(a): applicant pays the current biennial renewal fee, provides 'evidence of current certification in cardiopulmonary resuscitation', submits 'a notarized affidavit identifying the period of time in which the applicant did not practice in this Commonwealth', and must 'complete the continuing education requirements for the renewal periods during which the license ... [was lapsed]' (text partially truncated in the read). § 33.106(b): 'an applicant for reactivation who has failed to renew for more than 5 years may be subject to reexamination under section 3.1(b) of the act (63 P.S. § 122.1(b))' -- i.e. the hands-on clinical exam could be required again. § 33.106(c): $5/month late fee plus back renewal fees if the person practised in PA while lapsed.
- **remote_maintainability:** moderate
- **details:** Keeping the licence ACTIVE from abroad for decades is feasible: biennial fee ($366), 30 CE hours (≥50% lecture/clinical presentation -- live webinars likely but unverified; ≤50% self-study), 2 h online child-abuse CE, AHA/ARC-type CPR card (hands-on skills session, available in Turkey), no practice hours, no in-state address, online renewal via PALS. Awkward points: the lecture-format CE rule, hands-on CPR, liability-insurance attestation. Do NOT let it lapse: after 5 years lapsed the Board may require re-examination (§ 33.106(b)); reactivation also needs a notarised affidavit and back CE.
