## Sınav yolları

- **Licensure by examination (INBDE + regional/ADEX hands-on clinical exam)** (el becerisi: evet): 49 Pa. Code s.33.103(a): 'Candidates for licensure shall pass the National Board Dental Examination (written examination) and the dental clinical examination administered by one of the following: (1) NERB [now CDCA/ADEX] (2) SRTA (3) WREB (4) CRDTS (5) CITA.' The Board's 2026 Dentist Licensure Snapshot says '2 Examinations Required' and lists ADEX, CRDTS, SRTA, CITA, NERB, WREB; the ADA page (updated 13 Oct 2026) lists 'ADEX (CDCA-WREB-CITA), CRDTS+SRTA'. All are manikin/patient hands-on exams. Scores recognised for 5 years (s.33.103(d)); after 5 years only if candidate has been in postgraduate training or practice elsewhere. Application fee $260 (from 1 Jul 2025; $282 from 1 Jul 2027), plus 3 hours child-abuse training (Act 31) and liability-insurance attestation.
- **DLOSCE / OSCE-only** (el becerisi: hayır): NOT available. The DLOSCE is not mentioned anywhere in 49 Pa. Code Ch. 33, the Dental Law, the Board snapshot or the ADA summary, and Pennsylvania is not on the JCNDE list of DLOSCE-accepting states. s.33.103 requires 'the dental clinical examination administered by' a regional agency, i.e. the full hands-on exam.
- **PGY-1 residency in lieu of clinical exam** (el becerisi: hayır): NOT available. Neither 63 P.S. s.122 nor 49 Pa. Code s.33.103 provides a residency alternative; the ADA summary lists only ADEX/CRDTS/SRTA for initial licensure.
- **Licensure by criteria approval (reciprocity) - 49 Pa. Code s.33.107 / Dental Law s.3(f), 63 P.S. s.122(f)** (el becerisi: evet): For dentists 'currently licensed ... in another state or territory or in Canada'. Granted 'only to applicants whose licensing boards confirm that they reciprocate with the Commonwealth'; letters of good standing from every jurisdiction practised in; Board 'may grant a personal interview'. Statute s.3(f) also lets the Board 'accept as properly qualified any applicant who has been examined and certified by the National Dental Examining Board' PROVIDED the standards of the other jurisdiction 'or of such national board are, in the opinion of the board, equal to the standards of this Commonwealth' AND that jurisdiction 'shall recognize and endorse ... licenses issued by this Commonwealth'. No fixed practice-years in s.33.107. In practice it is NOT a theory-only route for this candidate: Pennsylvania's standard includes a hands-on clinical exam, so an NDEB Virtual-OSCE-only certification / BC or Saskatchewan licence is unlikely to be deemed 'equal', and BC/SK do not reciprocally endorse Pennsylvania licences (they require NDEB certification), so the reciprocity proviso fails. Fee $260 ($282 from 1 Jul 2027).
- **Licensure by endorsement (Act 41 of 2019, 63 Pa.C.S. s.3111; 49 Pa. Code s.33.107a)** (el becerisi: evet): Requires a current licence in a jurisdiction 'whose licensure standards are substantially equivalent to or exceed' 63 P.S. s.122(c),(d),(d.1) and ss.33.102-33.103 (which include the hands-on clinical exam), AND 'actively engaged in the licensed practice of their profession ... for at least 2 of the 5 years immediately preceding the filing of the application', plus no discipline, liability insurance (63 P.S. s.122.2), 3 h child-abuse training; Board may require a personal interview (video for good cause). Applicant must submit the other jurisdiction's licensing law (translated if needed). A BC/SK licence obtained via NDEB alone would need the Board to find a no-hands-on OSCE 'substantially equivalent' (unlikely) and the candidate would need 2 years of actual licensed practice there, impossible without Canadian work authorisation.
- **Restricted faculty licence / temporary volunteer licence** (el becerisi: hayır): Not general licensure: restricted to teaching at a PA dental school or to supervised volunteer work; not a route to an unrestricted licence (Dental Law s.3(c.1), Board forms).

## Teori-only açıklaması
49 Pa. Code s.33.103(a) (amended 2010, current through 56 Pa.B. 5370, Aug 2026): 'Candidates for licensure shall pass the National Board Dental Examination (written examination) and the dental clinical examination administered by one of the following: (1) The North East Regional Board of Dental Examiners, Inc. (NERB). (2) The Southern Regional Testing Agency, Inc. (SRTA). (3) The Western Regional Examining Board (WREB). (4) The Central Regional Dental Testing Service, Inc. (CRDTS). (5) The Council of Interstate Testing Agencies, Inc. (CITA).' The Board's 2026 snapshot: '2 Examinations Required - Candidates for licensure as a Dentist shall pass the National Board Dental Examination (written examination) and the dental clinical examination administered by one of the following: 1. The American Board of Dental Examiners (ADEX) ... 6. The Western Regional Examining Board (WREB).' Every listed clinical exam is hands-on; no DLOSCE/OSCE, residency, portfolio or state exam alternative exists in the statute (63 P.S. s.122(c),(e)) or rules. The only non-exam routes (s.33.107 criteria approval under s.3(f) of the Dental Law, and s.33.107a Act 41 endorsement) presuppose an existing licence from a jurisdiction whose standards the Board judges 'equal'/'substantially equivalent' to Pennsylvania's hands-on standard, reciprocity by that jurisdiction, and (for s.33.107a) 2 of the last 5 years in active licensed practice. For a new graduate holding at most an NDEB-based Canadian registration without the right to work in Canada, none of these conditions can be met, so the answer is 'no', not 'partial'.

## DLOSCE notları
Pennsylvania does not accept the DLOSCE in any form: it is absent from 49 Pa. Code s.33.103, the Dental Law, the Board's 2026 Dentist Licensure Snapshot and the ADA summary (updated 13 Oct 2026), and Pennsylvania is not on the JCNDE 2026 list of DLOSCE-accepting states. Because s.33.103 requires the full 'dental clinical examination administered by' ADEX/CDCA-WREB-CITA, CRDTS or SRTA, a pass of only the DLOSCE component of the ADEX series (or an earlier standalone DLOSCE score) does not satisfy the rule; the Board needs an overall ADEX (or CRDTS/SRTA) clinical pass. No Pennsylvania policy on ADEX-reported DLOSCE sub-scores exists.

However the Board's paper forms (e.g. Temporary Volunteer Dentist application, 04/2016, and the volunteer licence application) state 'U.S. Social Security Number: ... *ETIN [ITIN] or SIN cannot be accepted' and that SSN disclosure is mandatory under the federal child-support provisions; no published 'no-SSN affidavit' procedure for the PALS online dentist application could be verified within this research budget. Expect to need to contact the Board/PALS for a no-SSN workaround; ITIN is expressly not accepted.
- **in_jurisdiction_address_required:** False
- **barrier_level:** medium

Code Ch. 33; no SAVE or immigration-status verification is required by the rules read and Pennsylvania has no general lawful-presence statute for professional licences that I found. No Pennsylvania address or residency is required (renewal notices go to the licensee's e-mail). The practical barrier is the SSN: statute only requires it 'of an individual who has one', but Board forms say ITIN/SIN cannot be accepted and the PALS online application's handling of applicants with no SSN is unverified, hence 'medium' (ambiguous/discretionary).

## Yüz yüze adımlar
(1) Hands-on clinical examination (ADEX/CDCA-WREB-CITA, CRDTS or SRTA) must be taken in person at a US exam site - unavoidable. (2) No Pennsylvania jurisprudence exam exists (Board snapshot: '2 Examinations Required' = INBDE + clinical only). (3) No fingerprinting/Live Scan for dentists; Pennsylvania boards use criminal history record check reports (state police / equivalent for each state or country lived or worked in during the past 5 years) that can be obtained by mail/online from abroad - dentist-specific checklist wording not verified in this budget. (4) Application is filed online via PALS (www.pals.pa.gov) and the licence can be processed from abroad; no interview for the exam route, but the Board 'may grant a personal interview' for criteria approval (s.33.107) and 'may require' one for endorsement (s.33.107a(b), video allowed for good cause). (5) 3-hour child-abuse recognition course (Act 31) is available online. (6) Reactivation after lapse needs a 'notarized affidavit identifying the period of time in which the applicant did not practice in this Commonwealth' (s.33.106(a)) - notarisation can be done at a US consulate or by a notary abroad (acceptability of foreign notaries not verified). (7) CPR certification required at renewal - AHA/Red Cross-type courses normally include an in-person skills session (online-only acceptance not verified).

## Diğer başlangıç şartları
INBDE pass; hands-on regional clinical exam pass within 5 years (s.33.103(d)); DDS/DMD from a CODA-accredited program (s.33.102); age 21+, good moral character, no disqualifying felony drug conviction (63 P.S. s.122(c)); 3 hours of Board-approved child abuse recognition and reporting training for initial licensure (Act 31; s.33.256); professional liability insurance requirement under Dental Law s.3.2 (63 P.S. s.122.2) referenced in s.33.107a(a)(6) - exact coverage and any exemption for dentists not practising in PA not read; criminal history record checks and disclosure of all licences/criminal matters (Board forms); no jurisprudence exam; no English test; no CPR requirement at initial licensure (CPR required at renewal). Applicants are exempt from CE for the biennium in which they passed the licensing exam (s.33.401(g)(1)). Processing: online via PALS; Board publishes processing-time guides (not read).

## İlk ücretler
Licence application (by examination) $260 effective 1 Jul 2025 ($282 from 1 Jul 2027) - 49 Pa. Code s.33.3(a) and Board snapshot ('$260.00 Initial Fee'); criteria-approval application $260/$282; biennial renewal $366 (1 Apr 2025-31 Mar 2027), $432 from 1 Apr 2027 (s.33.3(b)). Exam fees extra (ADEX series $2,995; INBDE). Child-abuse course and CHRC report fees extra.

## Lisansı sürdürme

- **renewal_period:** Biennial; licences expire 31 March of every odd-numbered year (49 Pa. Code s.33.105(a)); renewal online via PALS, notices e-mailed ~60 days before expiry.
- **renewal_fee:** $366 for the 1 Apr 2025-31 Mar 2027 biennium; $432 for 1 Apr 2027 onward (s.33.3(b)); Board snapshot shows '$366.00 Renewal Fee'.
- **ce_hours:** 30 credit hours per biennium (s.33.401(a)(1)), including 2 hours child abuse recognition and reporting (s.33.401(h)) and, for DEA-registered dentists, 2 hours opioid/pain management; CPR hours do not count toward the 30.
- **ce_online_ok:** Not entirely self-study: s.33.401(c) 'At least 50% of the required credit hours shall be taken in lecture or clinical presentations' and (d) 'A maximum of 50% ... may be taken through individual study'. Whether live online webinars count as 'lecture' is not stated in the rule (likely acceptable from ADA CERP/AGD PACE sponsors under s.33.403, but unverified). No in-person component is expressly required for dentist CE.
- **cpr_required:** Yes - s.33.105(b): licensees 'shall maintain current certification in infant, child and adult cardiopulmonary resuscitation' as a condition of biennial renewal; courses must be 'substantially similar in content to the CPR courses offered by the American Heart Association and the American Red Cross and have a similar renewal period' (63 P.S. s.122(j.1)). AHA/Red Cross-style certification normally includes a hands-on skills check; Board acceptance of online-only CPR not verified.
- **practice_hours_required:** None for renewal. Practice/training is relevant only to exam-score validity (>5 years) and reactivation after >5 years lapse.
- **address_or_residency_required:** None; no Pennsylvania address or residency requirement in the Dental Law or Ch. 33; renewal notices are e-mailed.
- **insurance_required:** Dental Law s.3.2 (63 P.S. s.122.2) imposes a professional liability insurance requirement (referenced in s.33.107a(a)(6)); its text and any exemption for licensees not practising in Pennsylvania were not read - unclear.
- **inactive_status:** Board renewal page: 'If your license is INACTIVE or EXPIRED, you must submit a reactivation application through the online process.' A licensee may let the licence lapse/go inactive (no renewal fee or CE while inactive); dentists who fail to renew 'are prohibited from practicing their profession in this Commonwealth' (s.33.105(c)). No separate fee-bearing 'retired' category found; no maximum duration, but after more than 5 years un-renewed the Board 'may' require re-examination.
- **reactivation:** 49 Pa. Code s.33.106: pay the current biennial renewal fee, provide evidence of current CPR certification, submit a notarized affidavit identifying the period of non-practice in Pennsylvania, and complete the CE for the renewal periods during which the licence was not renewed; '(b) ... an applicant for reactivation who has failed to renew for more than 5 years may be subject to reexamination under section 3.1(b) of the act (63 P.S. s.122.1(b))'. Late fees ($5/month) apply only if the person practised in PA while unlicensed.
- **remote_maintainability:** moderate
- **details:** The licence can be kept ACTIVE indefinitely from abroad by paying $366-432 every two years, completing 30 CE hours (at least half 'lecture or clinical presentations' - most practically live online courses from approved sponsors; up to half pure self-study), 2 h child-abuse CE, and holding current AHA/Red Cross-type CPR (likely a hands-on skills session every 2 years). No practice hours, no PA address, no in-person step. Caveats: liability-insurance rule (63 P.S. s.122.2) not verified for non-practising licensees; CPR online-only acceptance not verified. Letting the licence lapse is cheap but reactivation after >5 years can trigger discretionary re-examination, so keeping it active is advisable.
