# Utah (USA) — ajan bulgusu

Düzenleyici: Utah Division of Professional Licensing (DOPL), Department of Commerce, acting with the Utah Board of Dentists and Dental Hygienists — https://dopl.utah.gov/dentist/

**Teori-only rota:** no · **Göçmenlik engeli:** medium · **Uzaktan sürdürme:** moderate · **Güven:** medium

## Sınav yolları

- **Standard initial licensure: CODA degree + INBDE + regional hands-on clinical exam (ADEX via CDCA-WREB-CITA, CRDTS, SRTA, or legacy WREB/CDCA/CITA)** (el becerisi: evet): Utah Code 58-69-302(1): applicant shall (c) hold an earned doctor's degree in dentistry from a CODA-accredited dental school; (d) 'pass the National Board Dental Examinations as administered by the Joint Commission on National Dental Examinations' (INBDE); (e) 'pass any regional dental clinical licensure examination approved by division rule'. Rule R156-69-302b: 'an applicant for licensure as a dentist shall pass the periodontics, endodontics, operative, class 2 restoration, class 3 restoration, and prosthodontics sections of any of the following regional dental clinical licensure examinations: (1) the WREB examination...; (2) the CDCA examination...; (3) the SRTA examination...; (4) the CRDTS examination...; or (5) the CITA examination'. The application form requires 'an official score report or certificate be released to Utah from WREB, CDCA, CITA, CRDTS, or SRTA showing a passing score on the regional examination'. All six named sections are hands-on (manikin/patient) components; the DLOSCE/OSCE component is not what Utah requires. ADA summary (updated 20 Oct 2026) lists 'ADEX (CDCA-WREB-CITA), CRDTS+SRTA' as the accepted exams.
- **DLOSCE-only / OSCE-only route** (el becerisi: hayır): NOT AVAILABLE. Utah is not among the DLOSCE-accepting states, and neither statute 58-69-302 nor rule R156-69-302b mentions the DLOSCE, any OSCE, or any computer-based substitute for the six hands-on sections. The ADA summary and the DOPL application form list only WREB/CDCA/CITA/CRDTS/SRTA score reports.
- **PGY-1 residency in lieu of clinical exam** (el becerisi: hayır): NOT AVAILABLE. No residency/PGY-1 alternative appears in Utah Code 58-69-302, rule R156-69-302b, the ADA summary, or the DOPL application form.
- **Licensure by endorsement (58-69-302(2) / 58-1-302)** (el becerisi: hayır): Only for dentists already licensed in another state who 'document having been engaged in lawful professional practice of dentistry for not less than 6,000 hours in the five years immediately preceding the date of application' (application fee $100). Not open to a new graduate; and the original state licence would itself have required a clinical exam, so this does not avoid hands-on testing.
- **Faculty exemption (58-69-305(3)) - practice without a Utah licence** (el becerisi: hayır): Not a licence. A dentist appointed to a faculty position at an accredited dental school may practise within that employment if licensed in another jurisdiction, has passed NBDE Parts I and II, holds a specialty degree, and 'is permitted to the work in the United States under federal immigration law'. Requires US work authorisation and employment, so irrelevant to this candidate.

## Teori-only açıklaması
Utah Code 58-69-302(1)(e) requires every dentist applicant to 'pass any regional dental clinical licensure examination approved by division rule', and Utah Admin. Code R156-69-302b implements this as: 'an applicant for licensure as a dentist shall pass the periodontics, endodontics, operative, class 2 restoration, class 3 restoration, and prosthodontics sections of any of the following regional dental clinical licensure examinations: (1) the WREB examination ... (2) the CDCA examination ... (3) the SRTA examination ... (4) the CRDTS examination ... or (5) the CITA examination'. Those six sections are the manikin/patient hand-skill components of ADEX/CRDTS/SRTA. There is no DLOSCE, OSCE, residency, portfolio or Utah-specific written alternative in the statute, rule, ADA summary (updated 20 Oct 2026) or the DOPL application form. A new graduate therefore cannot be licensed in Utah with computer-based examinations alone.

## DLOSCE notları
Utah does not accept the DLOSCE in any form. It is not on the JCNDE list of DLOSCE-accepting states, and R156-69-302b names only the hands-on sections (periodontics, endodontics, operative, class 2, class 3, prosthodontics) of WREB/CDCA/SRTA/CRDTS/CITA. Because Utah's rule is written in terms of those specific hand-skill sections rather than 'the ADEX exam' as a whole, an ADEX-reported DLOSCE pass (post-Aug 2026 integrated format) is irrelevant to Utah: the candidate must pass the six manikin sections regardless; whether the DLOSCE component of ADEX is also required by Utah is not stated (the rule does not list it). Earlier standalone DLOSCE scores have no standing in Utah.

PGY-1 kabul: False

## Göçmenlik / kimlik

- **citizenship_or_pr_required:** False
- **work_authorization_required:** False
- **lawful_presence_verification:** True
- **ssn_or_sin_required:** False
- **itin_or_no_ssn_alternative:** ITIN accepted. Dentist application form (last page): 'If you do not have a valid Social Security number, you must submit your Individual Taxpayer Identification Number (ITIN), Alien Registration Number (A-number), or a copy of an unexpired government issued passport from your country of residence and an intent-to-hire letter from a Utah based employer (Utah Code § R156-1-301). Submission of the above documents may require additional documents to demonstrate lawful presence (Utah Code § 63G-12-402 (3)(k)).' DOPL FAQ confirms this policy has applied since 23 April 2024 under R156-1-301, and adds: 'Providing this information ... does NOT replace the need to prove lawful presence ... If you are not lawfully present in the United States, your application will be DENIED.' Source: https://commerce.utah.gov/wp-content/uploads/2022/06/dentist-application.pdf and https://commerce.utah.gov/dopl/internationally-trained-applicant-information/frequently-asked-questions/
- **in_jurisdiction_address_required:** False
- **barrier_level:** medium
- **details:** Citizenship/PR: DOPL FAQ: 'Do I need to be a citizen to apply for a professional license in Utah? No. Citizenship is not a requirement, but to apply for any license, you will need to demonstrate lawful presence in the US.' Work authorisation is not a licence requirement (an intent-to-hire letter from a Utah employer is needed ONLY if the applicant has no SSN, ITIN or A-number; an ITIN avoids it). Lawful presence: Utah's state-wide public-benefit statute 63G-12-402 applies to Department of Commerce licences; per the FAQ, verification is waived under 63G-12-402(3)(k) only for applicants who give a Utah or other-state driver-licence/ID number (from a state that verifies lawful presence) plus an affidavit; 'Otherwise, a SAVE check is required to demonstrate lawful presence' with a copy of a listed document (the list includes immigrant documents but also non-immigrant ones such as 'Unexpired Foreign Passport with SEVIS ID (I-20 or DS2019)', 'Temporary I-551 Stamp (on passport or I-94)' and 'WT/WB Admission Stamp in Unexpired Foreign Passport', i.e. Visa Waiver visitor admissions). The application form asks the applicant to tick one of: 'I am a United States citizen or a non-citizen of the United States who is lawfully present' / 'I am a foreign national not physically present in the United States' / 'None of the above, please explain', and states 'If you do not hold a US Driver License or a US State ID, you must present a legible copy of your current and valid government issued document(s) showing evidence of lawful presence in the United States.' Interpretation: a B-1/B-2 visitor is a lawfully present non-immigrant and the FAQ's acceptance of WT/WB visitor stamps suggests visitor status can satisfy SAVE, and the form explicitly contemplates foreign nationals applying from outside the US; however DOPL has not published a rule saying a tourist-visa holder or an applicant abroad qualifies for a dental licence, so this is discretionary/unconfirmed. No Utah address or residency is required (form has generic address/state fields; no residency clause in 58-69-302). SSN: not mandatory (ITIN/A-number alternative).
- **citations:** Utah Code 58-69-302 (qualifications, no citizenship clause) via ADA summary; Utah Code 63G-12-402 and (3)(k) (lawful presence verification for state public benefits, Dept of Commerce licence exception) as quoted in DOPL FAQ; Utah Admin. Code R156-1-301 (SSN alternatives); DOPL Dentist Application form (commerce.utah.gov/wp-content/uploads/2022/06/dentist-application.pdf, v2025); DOPL Internationally-Trained Applicant FAQ (commerce.utah.gov/dopl/internationally-trained-applicant-information/frequently-asked-questions/); Utah Code 58-69-305(3) (faculty exemption requiring federal work authorisation - not applicable to licensure).

## Yüz yüze adımlar
(1) Hands-on regional clinical exam (ADEX/CRDTS/SRTA) must be taken in person at a US test site - unavoidable. (2) No fingerprinting or FBI background check is listed for the dentist application: the form only requires a 'Qualifying Questionnaire' and 'Medical Qualifying Questionnaire' with supporting documents for any 'yes' answers (no 'fingerprint' or 'background' wording found in the form or statute). (3) No jurisprudence/law exam exists: 58-69-302(1)(f) allows the division to require one by rule, but R156-69-302b lists only the clinical exam sections and the application form lists no law exam. (4) Interview only 'if requested by the board or division' (58-69-302(1)(h)). (5) The form has a signed 'Affidavit and Release'; whether notarisation is required could not be confirmed (not seen in the fetched text). (6) CPR certificate copy (CPR for local-anaesthesia classification; BLS for minimal sedation; ACLS/PALS for moderate/deep/general) must accompany the application; hands-on CPR skills sessions are typically needed for AHA/ARC cards. (7) Application is submitted online/by mail with transcripts sent directly by the school and score reports sent by JCNDE and the regional testing agency, so apart from the clinical exam and CPR course everything can be done from abroad or during a short visit.

## Diğer başlangıç şartları
Official transcripts from the CODA-accredited school sent directly to DOPL; INBDE score report released to Utah by JCNDE; regional exam score report/certificate from WREB/CDCA/CITA/CRDTS/SRTA; copy of current CPR/BLS (or ACLS/PALS depending on anaesthesia classification applied for); English: 58-69-302(1)(g) 'be able to read, write, speak, understand, and be understood in the English language and demonstrate proficiency to the satisfaction of the board if requested' (no specific test named); no jurisprudence exam; no fingerprint/background check listed; no insurance requirement; lawful-presence documentation and SSN/ITIN/A-number (see immigration). Anesthesia permits (local anaesthesia is a classification on the same form) need course-director letters per R156-69-301b. Timeline not published; DOPL communicates by email and sends licence copy 1-3 business days after approval (renewals).

## İlk ücretler
$110.00 non-refundable application processing fee (initial dentist licence, per DOPL dentist application form); $100.00 for licensure by endorsement. Plus exam costs: ADEX full series $2,995 (known fact), INBDE fee, and CPR course. Renewal fee is set by the DOPL fee schedule (reported as $73 per 2-year cycle in a DOPL dentistry page summary; the fee-schedule page itself could not be read); controlled-substance licence renewal $78 (DOPL renewal page).

## Lisansı sürdürme

- **renewal_period:** Two years; all dental licences expire 31 May of even-numbered years (Utah Code 58-69-303; R156-69-303; DOPL: 'Dental license renewals are due on May 31st of even years').
- **renewal_fee:** Set by DOPL fee schedule (DOPL renewal page: 'Renewal Fee: See the DOPL Fee Schedule for the current fee'); reported as $73 per cycle (secondary/unverified - fee schedule page was not readable). Controlled-substance licence renewal $78 extra. Late fee (expired <=30 days) and reinstatement fee (31 days-2 years) apply under R156-1-308g; amounts not verified.
- **ce_hours:** 30 hours of qualified continuing professional education per two-year cycle (R156-69-304a(1)), including 2 hours controlled-substance management and 2 hours ethics (ADA summary of R156-69-304a); sedation permit holders need additional topic-specific hours. CPR/BLS/ACLS/PALS recertification hours do not count toward the 30. Prorated for first partial cycle.
- **ce_online_ok:** NO - not 100%. R156-69-304a(5)(c): 'a maximum of 15 hours per two-year period may be recognized for continuing education that is provided online or through home study with an examination and a completion certificate'. The remaining 15+ hours must be 'formally established classroom courses, seminars, lectures, conferences, or training sessions' (unlimited hours) sponsored by a government agency, recognised university/college or accredited dental programme, professional associations/societies whose objectives relate to dentistry, the ADA/ADHA or subgroups, a recognised health-care professional association or peer study club (R156-69-304a(5)(a)). The rule does not restrict these to the US, so conferences/courses run by dental associations or universities abroad (e.g. in Turkey) may plausibly qualify, and whether live interactive webinars count as 'lectures' rather than 'online' is not stated. Up to 10 hours for teaching CE; up to 3 hours practice/office management; up to 15% via volunteer direct patient care (4 volunteer hours = 1 CE hour).
- **cpr_required:** Yes. R156-69-304a(2): 'an applicant for renewal or reinstatement of licensure shall hold current CPR-BLS, ACLS, or PALS certification as required by the license and permit classification'. Rule does not name a provider or state whether an online-only course is acceptable; standard AHA/ARC BLS cards require a hands-on skills check. R156-69-502(7)(a) makes failing to maintain current CPR/BLS unprofessional conduct.
- **practice_hours_required:** None for renewal of an active licence (only licensure by endorsement requires 6,000 hours in 5 years). However, reinstatement of a licence expired more than two years relies on continuous practice under another US state licence or US government employ (R156-1-308g(3)(b)); practice abroad does not count.
- **address_or_residency_required:** No Utah address or residency requirement. Licensee must notify DOPL within 10 business days of any change of address or email (Utah Code 58-1-301.7); all notices go to the email on file.
- **insurance_required:** No mandatory professional liability insurance for licensure or renewal (none in 58-69-302, R156-69 or the renewal page).
- **inactive_status:** Available: R156-1-305(1)(q) lists 'dentist' among licences that 'active in good standing may be placed on inactive licensure status'. Apply with a verified application plus fee (amount not verified). R156-1-305(3): 'A license may remain on inactive status indefinitely except as otherwise provided in Title 58 ... or Title R156' - no dentist-specific limit found. Inactive licensees may not practise; CE is not required while inactive (current renewal requirements must instead be documented at activation). A separate 'Retired Volunteer' / Volunteer Dentist licence also exists (for charitable practice).
- **reactivation:** From inactive: R156-1-305(4): submit a verified application for activation with 'the appropriate fee; and ... documentation that the inactive licensee meets current renewal requirements' (i.e. current CE - 30 hours - and CPR); no clinical re-examination or refresher is specified, and no maximum inactive duration. From lapse (R156-1-308g): expired <=30 days - renewal fee + late fee; 31 days to 2 years - renewal fee + reinstatement fee (plus renewal requirements); more than 2 years - if not continuously active under another US state licence/US government employ, 'submit an application for licensure complete with supporting documents as is required of an individual making an initial application for license demonstrating the applicant meets current qualifications for licensure' and pay the new-applicant fee (i.e. potentially a new regional clinical exam); if continuously licensed and active in another US state, reinstate with renewal + reinstatement fee within six months of re-establishing Utah domicile.
- **remote_maintainability:** moderate
- **details:** Keeping the licence ACTIVE from Turkey indefinitely is feasible but awkward: pay the biennial fee online, keep a current CPR/BLS card (hands-on skills check usually needed, obtainable abroad), and complete 30 CE hours of which only 15 may be online/home-study - the other 15 must be live classroom/seminar/conference CE from an approved-type sponsor (foreign dental-association or university events may qualify, but this is not explicit). No practice hours, no Utah address, no insurance. Alternatively place the licence on INACTIVE status indefinitely (R156-1-305) and reactivate later by documenting current renewal requirements (CE + CPR) and paying a fee - no exam retake specified. Avoid letting the licence LAPSE for more than two years, since that forces a fresh initial application under then-current rules (clinical exam again) unless continuously licensed in another US state.
