# Iowa (USA) — ajan bulgusu

Düzenleyici: Iowa Dental Board (now housed within the Iowa Department of Inspections, Appeals, and Licensing, DIAL) — https://dial.iowa.gov/licenses/health-professions/dental/dentists

**Teori-only rota:** partial · **Göçmenlik engeli:** high · **Uzaktan sürdürme:** hard · **Güven:** medium

## Sınav yolları

- **Licensure by examination (board-approved regional clinical exam: CRDTS, CDCA-WREB-CITA/ADEX, or SRTA; patient-based or manikin/simulated)** (el becerisi: evet): Current rule 481—572.3(2)(d) requires 'Evidence of successful passage of a board-approved clinical examination pursuant to rule 481—572.14' completed within five years of application, plus graduation from a CODA-accredited school, INBDE (JCNDE) pass, jurisprudence exam (75%), hands-on CPR, DCI/FBI fingerprint background check, $200 application fee + $46 background fee. Rule 481—572.14(2): 'the board accepts patient-based and simulated clinical examinations administered by the testing agencies as follows: a. CRDTS; b. CDCA-WREB-CITA ...; c. SRTA'. DIAL web page: 'Beginning in May 2021, the Iowa Dental Board accepts the patient-based and manikin-based clinical examinations.' Every listed exam involves hands-on (patient or manikin) components. Second or subsequent failure triggers remediation requirements (572.14(5)).
- **DLOSCE (JCNDE computer-based OSCE)** (el becerisi: evet): NOT named anywhere in the current Iowa rule (481—572.14, IAC 9/30/26) or on the DIAL dentist page; JCNDE's candidate guide lists Iowa only with an asterisk (partial fulfilment), i.e. at most as a component alongside a hands-on regional exam. The JCNDE DLOSCE page now states the DLOSCE has been licensed to ADEX 'for incorporation into the ADEX Dental Examination' and 'All administrations are conducted through ADEX'; results for those who 'took the DLOSCE through the ADA on or before September 25, 2026' remain accessible via DENTPIN. There is no Iowa rule making a standalone DLOSCE sufficient, so there is no DLOSCE-only route.
- **PGY-1 residency in lieu of clinical exam** (el becerisi: evet): Not available to CODA graduates. A one-year GPR at an accredited college appears only in 481—572.4(1)(a)(2) as a way for graduates of NON-accredited foreign dental schools to establish educational equivalence; such applicants must still 'meet the other requirements for licensure specified in rule 481—572.3', i.e. the board-approved clinical exam. Residents practising in Iowa need a resident license (481—572.8), which is not convertible to a full license.
- **California portfolio examination** (el becerisi: evet): The pre-April-2025 rule (650—11.2(2)(d)(3)) accepted the 2014 California portfolio exam only for University of Iowa College of Dentistry students/recent graduates; it does not appear in the re-promulgated 481—572.14 (effective 4/9/2025). Irrelevant to a Yeditepe graduate in any case.
- **Licensure by credentials (Iowa Code 153.21; 481—572.3(3))** (el becerisi: evet): For dentists who have held another state's license for one year or longer: must show either three consecutive years of lawful practice immediately prior to application, or (if less than three years) passage within the previous five years of a board-approved clinical examination per 481—572.14 (hands-on). Fee $550 + $46. Not an initial route for a new graduate.
- **Licensure by verification (Iowa Code 272C.12; 481—572.7)** (el becerisi: hayır): Iowa Code 272C.12(1): a professional license 'shall be issued without an examination to a person if ... a. The person is currently licensed ... by at least one other issuing jurisdiction in the occupation or profession applied for with a substantially similar scope of practice and the license ... is in good standing' and 'd. The person previously passed an examination required by the other issuing jurisdiction ..., if applicable.' The residency condition in 272C.12(1)(b) applies only to chapter 103/105 (electrical/plumbing) licenses, not dentists. Rule 481—572.7 requires only: application fee ($550) + $46 background packet, DCI/FBI fingerprints, a verification form sent directly by the other licensing authority, the Iowa jurisprudence exam (temporary 3-month license available pending it), criminal/disciplinary disclosures and scope-of-practice statements; no clinical exam is listed. Rule 572.3(1)(b) opens this to applicants 'licensed in another jurisdiction and who are unable to satisfy the requirements for licensure by examination or credentials'. So an applicant who first obtains a licence in another US jurisdiction by theory-only means could, in principle, obtain Iowa licensure without any Iowa clinical exam. Whether 'issuing jurisdiction' includes a Canadian province is not confirmed (Iowa Code 272C.1 definition not checked); the SAVE lawful-presence requirement applies to this pathway as well.

## Teori-only açıklaması
Direct licensure by examination is NOT theory-only: 481—572.3(2)(d) requires 'Evidence of successful passage of a board-approved clinical examination pursuant to rule 481—572.14(147,153)', and 481—572.14(2) limits acceptable exams to 'patient-based and simulated clinical examinations administered by the testing agencies as follows: a. Central Regional Dental Testing Service, Inc. (CRDTS); b. CDCA-WREB-CITA ...; and c. The States Resources for Testing and Assessments (SRTA)'. 572.14(1)(b) adds 'The examinee must attain a passing score on each clinical and written portion of the examination.' The DLOSCE is not mentioned in the rule and is now only administered inside the ADEX exam. The only written-in-rules route with no Iowa clinical exam is licensure by verification: Iowa Code 272C.12(1) ('shall be issued without an examination to a person if ... currently licensed ... by at least one other issuing jurisdiction ... with a substantially similar scope of practice') and rule 481—572.7, which require only the Iowa jurisprudence exam, fingerprints and fees. That route needs 'something extra' (a prior licence from another jurisdiction, presumably obtained theory-only elsewhere), hence 'partial'. Independently of exams, the lawful-presence/SAVE requirement (below) blocks a visitor-visa applicant in practice.

## DLOSCE notları
Iowa's current rule 481—572.14 (IAC edition 9/30/26, effective since 4/9/2025) does not mention the DLOSCE at all; it accepts only CRDTS, CDCA-WREB-CITA and SRTA patient-based or simulated (manikin) exams, and 572.14(1)(b) requires a pass on 'each clinical and written portion'. The DIAL dentist page likewise lists only those agencies/ADEX. The JCNDE guide's 'Iowa*' (partial) therefore can only mean the DLOSCE may be used as a written/OSCE component in conjunction with a hands-on regional exam, never alone. The JCNDE DLOSCE page (read 2 Nov 2026) confirms the DLOSCE is licensed to ADEX 'for incorporation into the ADEX Dental Examination', that 'All administrations are conducted through ADEX', and that the 2026 Candidate Guide is 'for historical reference purposes only'; results are still retrievable for those who took it 'through the ADA on or before September 25, 2026'. No Iowa rule or board statement addresses the validity of earlier standalone DLOSCE scores; since a standalone DLOSCE never satisfied Iowa's clinical-exam rule, a candidate holding one would still need a full regional/ADEX pass within five years of application. DLOSCE-only route: not available in Iowa.

PGY-1 kabul: False

- **citizenship_or_pr_required:** False
- **work_authorization_required:** False
- **lawful_presence_verification:** True
- **ssn_or_sin_required:** True
- **itin_or_no_ssn_alternative:** None published. Iowa Code 252J.8(1): 'A licensing authority shall maintain records of licensees by name, current known address, and social security number.' The dental application is filed through the Dental Board online portal; the downloadable supplemental forms (Certification of Education, Authorization to Release Information) contain no SSN field, and no ITIN / 'no SSN' affidavit option is published on any DIAL dental page read. Treat SSN as presumptively required and unverified; confirm with the board (idb@iowa.gov, 515-380-7393).
- **in_jurisdiction_address_required:** False
- **barrier_level:** high
- **details:** Iowa Code 147.3 says an applicant 'is not ineligible because of age, citizenship, sex, race, religion, marital status, or national origin, although the application form may require citizenship information', so citizenship/PR/work authorisation are not per se required. HOWEVER, since Executive Order 15 (8 Oct 2025) and Senate File 2218 (signed 1-2 June 2026, effective 1 July 2026) every Iowa professional-licence applicant is run through the federal SAVE system. DIAL notice (posted 13 Feb 2026, updated 16 July 2026): 'In accordance with 8 U.S.C.A. § 1621 and Executive Order 15 (Oct. 8, 2025), any individual granted an Iowa professional license must be a U.S. citizen, have lawful presence in the U.S., or meet an exception found in 8 U.S.C.A. § 1621(c)(2) ... You are not required to provide your personal information for this purpose; however, failure to do so will prevent further processing of your application.' SF 2218 §21 creates Iowa Code 272C.15A: a licensing board 'shall not issue or renew a professional license to an individual who the licensing board is unable to verify is lawfully present in the United States, as determined by using the SAVE program clearinghouse'; §14 requires DIAL boards to 'use the SAVE program clearinghouse ... to verify the United States citizenship and immigration status of an individual who has applied for a professional license'; §20 makes 'unlawful presence in the United States, as determined by using the SAVE program clearinghouse' a ground for discipline; a failed check is appealable only to the DIAL director (§13, §14(b)) and results are forwarded to ICE. The DIAL dentist page carries the same banner ('Iowa licensure requires U.S. citizenship or lawful presence verification through SAVE'). Practical effect for a Turkish citizen on a B-1/B-2 visa: federally, a 'nonimmigrant under the Immigration and Nationality Act' is an eligible alien under 8 U.S.C. 1621(a)(2), and SAVE can verify a nonimmigrant from a passport/I-94 while in status, so an application lodged while physically in the US on a valid visitor admission might verify; but at each biennial renewal (expressly covered by 272C.15A) the person would be outside the US with no current I-94, so SAVE could not verify lawful presence. 8 U.S.C. 1621(c)(2)(C) excludes from the 'public benefit' definition 'the issuance of a professional license to, or the renewal of a professional license by, a foreign national not physically present in the United States', and DIAL's notice refers to 1621(c)(2) exceptions, but Iowa's own statute contains no such carve-out and no DIAL guidance applies it to dental licences. No in-state address or Iowa residency is required (272C.12's residency condition applies only to electrical/plumbing licences); the board uses the e-mail address of record for renewal notices. Barrier rated high because lawful-presence verification is mandatory at issuance and renewal and is discretionary/untested for visitors.

## Yüz yüze adımlar
(1) Clinical exam: hands-on CRDTS/ADEX(CDCA-WREB-CITA)/SRTA exam at a US test site (manikin or patient) - unavoidable for licensure by examination. (2) Jurisprudence exam (481—572.15, minimum 75%): three formats are approved - (a) board exam proctored at Iowa community-college testing sites (in person in Iowa; 'contact the testing site ... at least 10 days prior to the exam date. A proctor fee will be paid directly to the community college testing site'); (b) exams administered by accredited Iowa schools; (c) 'Board-approved continuing education courses that include a posttest examination' - DIAL lists jurisprudence course providers (CuspEd, Hu-Friedy, Impact Dental Training, Iowa Dental Association, University of Iowa College of Dentistry etc.), several of which are online, so the jurisprudence requirement can likely be met from abroad; under 572.7(4)/old 11.14(5) a 3-month temporary licence can be issued pending the jurisprudence exam for verification applicants. (3) Fingerprinting: 481—572.3(2)(g) requires 'A completed packet to facilitate a criminal history background check by the DCI and FBI' ($46); the board's materials call it a 'fingerprint packet'/'background check packet' and 572.1(2) contemplates resubmitting 'an additional completed fingerprint packet' if DCI/FBI reject the prints, which indicates ink-card (FD-258-type) fingerprinting rather than Iowa Live Scan - cards can normally be rolled at any police agency and mailed, but no DIAL page confirms whether prints taken outside the US are accepted (unverified). (4) CPR: certificate from a nationally recognised course 'that included a hands-on component' (572.1(1)(b)); DIAL lists ProCPR as including 'options to complete hands-on component remotely with the use of manikins'. (5) Interview: discretionary only ('The committee or board may require an applicant to appear for an interview', 572.2(4)). (6) Notarisation: none required; application is 'signed and verified' online (572.1(1)(e)). (7) SAVE verification: document data (passport/I-94) submitted with the online application; presence in the US during adjudication may matter for a nonimmigrant to verify. Everything except the clinical exam and (if chosen) the community-college jurisprudence sitting can plausibly be done remotely or during one short visit; the application must be completed within 180 days of receipt (572.1(4)).

## Diğer başlangıç şartları
Graduation with DDS/DMD from an 'accredited school' = 'a dental ... education program accredited by the American Dental Association Commission on Dental Accreditation' (481—570 definitions) - a CODA-accredited Yeditepe graduate qualifies as an accredited-school graduate, so 481—572.4 (foreign-school TOEFL/equivalency rules) does not apply and no English test is required; Certification of Education form completed by the dean confirming CODA accreditation at graduation and good standing; INBDE pass (JCNDE); board-approved clinical exam within 5 years; jurisprudence exam ≥75% (online CE-with-posttest option exists); valid hands-on CPR certificate; DCI/FBI fingerprint background check ($46); certification of all licences held; disclosure statement of discipline/claims/criminal charges; Authorization to Release Information form; SAVE citizenship/immigration-status verification (mandatory since 2026); fees non-refundable plus online service charge; application must be completed within 180 days or it is void and fees forfeited; no liability insurance requirement for licensure; CE exempt for the first (partial) renewal period after original issuance (573.5(2)(e)). Timeline: staff can issue administratively 'if the final review of an application indicates no questions or concerns'; referrals to the Licensure/Registration Committee or Board add time (board meets periodically); no published processing time.

## İlk ücretler
Dental licence by examination application $200 (481—571.2(1)(a)); fingerprint packet/criminal history check $46 (571.2(5)); online payment service charge (571.1(1)); jurisprudence exam proctor fee paid to the community college (amount not published) or cost of an approved jurisprudence CE course; CPR course cost; regional/ADEX clinical exam fees paid to the testing agency (not an Iowa fee). If applying within four months of the 31 Aug even-year expiry you may add the $315 renewal fee so the licence is renewed on issuance (572.1(3)). Alternatives: licence by credentials or by verification $550 + $46. All fees non-refundable; a one-time fee waiver exists for applicants whose household income is under 200% of the federal poverty level (initial applications only).

## Lisansı sürdürme

- **renewal_period:** Biennial: dental licences expire 31 August of every even-numbered year (481—572.17(1)(b)); CE compliance period 1 Sept–31 Aug of the two-year cycle (573.1(3)). Renewal notice is e-mailed to the address of record; renewal is online.
- **renewal_fee:** $315 per biennium (481—571.3(1)). Late renewal in September +$100, in October +$150 (571.4(1)); failure to renew by 1 November = licence lapses and is invalid for practice (572.19(3)). Reinstatement of a lapsed licence: $150 application + $315 past-due renewal + $46 background check = $511 (571.5; DIAL fee page).
- **ce_hours:** 30 hours per biennium (573.1(1)(a)), including minimum 1 hour infection control (CDC standards), 1 hour Iowa jurisprudence, CPR certification (credited up to 3 hours), 1 hour opioid CE only if the dentist prescribed opioids during the biennium (otherwise attest not applicable), mandatory reporter training only for licensees who 'regularly provide ... dental services to patients in Iowa'. Up to 15 excess hours may be carried forward (not CPR/infection control/jurisprudence/opioid hours).
- **ce_online_ok:** Largely yes. 'Self-study activities' (Internet-based coursework, video, correspondence, interactive programs) are capped at 12 hours per renewal period (573.3(2)(f)), BUT DIAL/rule define self-study to exclude 'Webinars that include instruction in real time and allow for real-time communication with the instructor', so live online webinars count without cap; the remaining 18 hours can therefore be done by live webinars from abroad. Courses approved by ADA/AGD/specialty organisations are automatically acceptable. The only components with any physical element are CPR (hands-on, see below) and, if applicable, mandatory reporter training (not required if not treating patients in Iowa). CE exemptions exist for periods practising in another US 'state, district, or territory' with a CE requirement and for 'Other periods of active practice and absence from the state approved by the board' (573.5(2)) - practice in Turkey would need discretionary board approval.
- **cpr_required:** Yes at every renewal: 572.18(4) allows nonrenewal for 'Failure to maintain current certification in CPR that includes a hands-on component'; 573.1(1)(f). DIAL: 'CPR certification courses must be from nationally-recognized sponsor, and include a hands-on, clinical component. A CPR course may include an online component provided there is an in-person portion prior to the certification being issued.' DIAL's resource list includes ProCPR, which 'Includes options to complete hands-on component remotely with the use of manikins', so a remote hands-on skills session appears acceptable. Only the certificate expiry date is reported at renewal; certificate kept for audit.
- **practice_hours_required:** None for renewal. Practice matters only on reactivation after lapse: the board 'may require a licensee ... who has not actively practiced clinically within five years immediately preceding the date of application, to successfully complete a board-approved examination or assessment' (572.20(2)).
- **address_or_residency_required:** No Iowa address or residency requirement; licensees must keep a current e-mail/mailing address of record (renewal notices sent 'to the most recent email address of record', 572.17(2)); name/address changes must be reported. However, SF 2218 / Iowa Code 272C.15A makes SAVE lawful-presence verification a condition of RENEWAL as well as issuance ('shall not issue or renew'), which is the real obstacle to renewing from Turkey.
- **insurance_required:** No professional liability insurance requirement found in Iowa Code ch. 153 or 481—Chapters 570-581.
- **inactive_status:** NONE. The Iowa Dental Board abolished inactive status effective 25 May 2022 (ARC 6303C rescinded the definition of 'inactive status', struck the 'may place the license on inactive status ... No continuing education hours are required' language from 650—14.1, and rescinded 650—14.8 reactivation of an inactive license); the re-promulgated 481—572 (eff. 4/9/2025) contains no inactive category (only a vestigial mention in 572.13(7)). Options are: keep the licence active (CE + CPR + $315 + SAVE check every two years), let it lapse and reinstate later, or a no-fee 12-month 'retired volunteer license' (572.13) for retirees providing unpaid care in Iowa, which 'is not considered to be an active license' and 'cannot be converted to any regular license type'.
- **reactivation:** Reinstatement/reactivation of a lapsed licence (481—572.20) is 'at the discretion of the board' and requires: $150 reactivation fee + one past-due renewal fee $315 + $46 DCI/FBI fingerprint background check; evidence of the 30 CE hours required for renewal not previously reported OR evidence of full- or part-time practice in another US state/district/territory for at least two years within the previous five years; certification of good standing from any other licensing authority; disclosure statement; and, if the applicant 'has not actively practiced clinically within five years immediately preceding the date of application', the board 'may require ... a board-approved examination or assessment' (potentially a hands-on clinical exam). Hands-on CPR and SAVE lawful-presence verification would also apply. There is no time limit after which reinstatement becomes impossible, but the 5-year clinical-currency trigger is the key risk for someone not practising in the US.
- **remote_maintainability:** hard
- **details:** Mechanically Iowa is fairly remote-friendly: online renewal, 30 CE hours achievable entirely online (12 self-study + live webinars), hands-on CPR satisfiable by remote manikin skills sessions (ProCPR, per DIAL), no practice hours, no in-state address, no insurance. The blockers are (1) no inactive/non-practising status, so the full $315 + CE + CPR cycle must be done every two years to stay licensed; (2) Iowa Code 272C.15A (SF 2218, eff. 1 July 2026) forbids the board to 'renew a professional license to an individual who the licensing board is unable to verify is lawfully present in the United States' via SAVE - a Turkish citizen living in Turkey has no current US immigration status to verify at renewal time, and DIAL has published no guidance applying the 8 U.S.C. 1621(c)(2)(C) 'foreign national not physically present' exception; (3) after a lapse, reinstatement requires a new background check and, after 5 years without clinical practice, a possible board-approved clinical assessment.
