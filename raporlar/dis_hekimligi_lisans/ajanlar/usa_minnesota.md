# Minnesota (USA) — ajan bulgusu

Düzenleyici: Minnesota Board of Dentistry — https://mn.gov/boards/dentistry/

**Teori-only rota:** no · **Göçmenlik engeli:** low · **Uzaktan sürdürme:** moderate · **Güven:** medium

## Sınav yolları

- **Licensure by examination (INBDE + board-approved hands-on clinical exam: ADEX via CDCA-WREB-CITA, CRDTS, SRTA/WREB)** (el becerisi: evet): Minn. Stat. 150A.06 subd. 1 + Minn. R. 3100.1100 subp. 1: applicant must provide (A) completed application, (B) 'a form of current government-issued identification', (C) fee, (D) evidence of graduation from a CODA-accredited school, (E) 'evidence of passing all parts of a national board examination for the practice of dentistry within the past five years' (INBDE), (F) 'evidence of passing a board-approved clinical examination within the past five years', (G) board jurisprudence exam within 5 years, (H) current CPR certification, (I) criminal background check under 214.075. ADA Licensure-by-State page (updated 9 Oct 2026, sourced from the Board website) lists the board-approved clinical exams as 'American Board of Dental Examiners (ADEX, CDCA-WREB-CITA), CRDTS+SRTA' and states 'Minnesota requires applicants to pass ALL components of the exams, including post anterior and anterior operative, except for periodontal component, within 5 years prior to application'. The Board's own 'Licensure by Exam to Practice Dentistry' form (Rev. 09/26) has check-boxes '____ WREB ____ CRDTS ____ CDCA(ADEX) ____ Other' and says 'Clinical exams require all components but perio; you cannot opt-out of any section'. All are manikin/patient hands-on exams. Two failures = must take CODA remedial education before retake (3100.1100 subp. 1a). Fee $343 total.
- **Licensure by examination with clinical-exam waiver for PGY-1 (CODA-accredited GPR/AEGD residency)** (el becerisi: hayır): Minn. Stat. 150A.06 subd. 3(b): 'The board shall waive the clinical examination required for licensure for any dentist applicant who is a graduate of a dental education program accredited by the Commission on Dental Accreditation, who has passed all components of the National Board Dental Examinations, and who has satisfactorily completed a postdoctoral general dentistry residency program (GPR) or an advanced education in general dentistry (AEGD) program after January 1, 2004. The postdoctoral program must be accredited by the Commission on Dental Accreditation, be of at least one year's duration, and include an outcome assessment evaluation assessing the resident's competence to practice dentistry.' No hands-on exam, but a one-year US residency (requires a visa) is unavoidable, so it is excluded by the candidate's profile. Jurisprudence exam, CPR, CBC still required.
- **Discretionary waiver of all or part of the examination (statutory, not used in practice)** (el becerisi: evet): Minn. Stat. 150A.06 subd. 3(a): 'All or any part of the examination for dentists ... except that pertaining to the law of Minnesota relating to dentistry and the rules of the board, may, at the discretion of the board, be waived for an applicant who presents a certificate of having passed all components of the National Board Dental Examinations or evidence of having maintained an adequate scholastic standing as determined by the board.' This is pure board discretion; the binding rule (3100.1100 subp. 1 F) still lists 'evidence of passing a board-approved clinical examination' as a mandatory application item and the Board's form offers only hands-on exams. No evidence the Board has ever used this to license an INBDE-only new graduate. Treat as theoretical only (hands-on still expected).
- **Licensure by credentials (already licensed in another US state or Canadian province)** (el becerisi: evet): Minn. Stat. 150A.06 subd. 4 and Minn. R. 3100.1400: requires NBDE/INBDE pass; 'active practice at least 2,000 hours within 36 months of the application date'; a current (or previously surrendered in good standing) licence in another state or Canadian province; 'evidence of passing a clinical examination for licensure in another United States jurisdiction or Canadian province'; board interview; jurisprudence exam; CPR; CBC; English test at board discretion. Not available to a new graduate with no other licence; and the prior licence must itself have been obtained via a clinical exam (hands-on in practice; whether a Canadian NDEB Virtual OSCE would count as 'a clinical examination for licensure in ... Canadian province' is untested). Fee $893 statutory ($928 total on the form incl. $35 CBC).
- **DLOSCE / OSCE-only route** (el becerisi: evet): NOT available for initial licensure. Minnesota is not among the DLOSCE-accepting states; neither Minn. Stat. 150A.06 nor Minn. R. 3100.1100 mentions the DLOSCE or any OSCE, and the Board's application form and the ADA summary list only ADEX/CDCA-WREB-CITA, CRDTS, SRTA/WREB. The only OSCE reference in Minnesota's rules is for REINSTATEMENT after 24+ months of termination (Minn. R. 3100.1850 subp. 3 E(1): 'a nationally recognized objective structured clinical examination for general dentists').
- **Other statutory licence categories (not applicable)** (el becerisi: hayır): Limited general license (150A.06 subd. 9) is only for graduates of NON-accredited programmes and still requires the clinical licensure exam plus 3 years supervised practice in Minnesota; resident dentist licence (subd. 1b) requires enrolment in a US advanced education programme and 'does not qualify a person for licensure under subdivision 1'; faculty licences (subd. 1a) require employment by a Minnesota dental school; guest licence (subd. 2c) requires a current licence in another US jurisdiction. None provides a theory-only path for this candidate.

## Teori-only açıklaması
Minn. R. 3100.1100 subp. 1 (published electronically July 24, 2024) makes a hands-on clinical examination a mandatory application item: 'A person seeking licensure to practice general dentistry in Minnesota must provide the board: ... E. evidence of passing all parts of a national board examination for the practice of dentistry within the past five years; F. evidence of passing a board-approved clinical examination within the past five years; G. evidence of passing the board's jurisprudence examination within the past five years ...'. The Board's 'Licensure by Exam to Practice Dentistry' application (Rev. 09/26) states 'Clinical Exam (Clinical exams require all components but perio; you cannot opt-out of any section) ____ WREB ____ CRDTS ____ CDCA(ADEX) ____ Other', and the ADA state summary (updated 9 Oct 2026) confirms the approved exams are 'American Board of Dental Examiners (ADEX, CDCA-WREB-CITA), CRDTS+SRTA' with 'ALL components ... including post anterior and anterior operative'. The only statutory escape from the clinical exam is Minn. Stat. 150A.06 subd. 3(b), which requires a CODA-accredited one-year GPR/AEGD residency. Subd. 3(a) gives the Board pure discretion to waive 'all or any part of the examination' for an NBDE passer, but this is not implemented in rule or on the application form and cannot be relied upon. DLOSCE/OSCE is not accepted for initial licensure. Therefore a new graduate cannot be licensed in Minnesota with computer-based exams alone.

## DLOSCE notları
Minnesota does not accept the DLOSCE (standalone or ADEX-integrated) for initial licensure: it does not appear in the JCNDE list of accepting states, in Minn. Stat. 150A.06, in Minn. R. 3100.1100, on the Board's application form, or in the ADA state summary. A DLOSCE score earned as a component of the ADEX Dental Examination is irrelevant on its own because Minnesota requires passage of ALL ADEX components except perio (hands-on operative/prosthodontic sections included). The one place an OSCE appears is Minn. R. 3100.1850 subp. 3 E(1): to REINSTATE a licence terminated for 24 months or more, the dentist must show 'evidence of passing ... within 24 months prior to the board's receipt of the application: (1) a nationally recognized objective structured clinical examination for general dentists' (plus 50 CE hours, CPR, jurisprudence exam within 12 months, and a new CBC). With standalone DLOSCE administrations having ended 9 Oct 2026, how the Board will treat this OSCE requirement (e.g. accepting the ADEX-integrated DLOSCE score, or another OSCE) is an open question.

The background check is a criminal-history check (BCA + FBI), not an immigration check.

## Yüz yüze adımlar
(1) Hands-on clinical exam (ADEX/CRDTS/SRTA) – in person at a US test site; unavoidable. (2) Fingerprint-based criminal background check: Minn. Stat. 214.075 subd. 3(a) – 'the applicant or licensee must submit a completed criminal history records check consent form and a full set of fingerprints to the respective health-related licensing board or a designee in the manner and form specified by the board'; the Health Licensing Boards' Criminal Background Check (CBC) Program Office emails instructions and forms to the applicant after the application and fee are received, the applicant is responsible for obtaining and submitting the fingerprints, private vendors are allowed but cards may be rejected for poor quality, average processing about 7 business days (mn.gov/boards/cbc, read via search snippet only – page is CAPTCHA-blocked). This indicates a mailed fingerprint-card process rather than in-state Live Scan, so it can likely be done abroad or during the exam trip, but the current card/vendor instructions could not be read directly – confirm with criminal.background.check@state.mn.us. The application 'will be stored until the criminal background check is brought to the Board'. (3) Jurisprudence exam: administered by PSI Services under contract with the Board; open-book since October 2021 (Board govdelivery bulletin 18 Oct 2021); the Board exams page links a PSI 'Online Proctoring Experience' video and the bulletin (snippet) says register via PSI's portal, fee $85, no eligibility requirements, unlimited retakes – i.e. remote online proctoring appears available, so it can be taken from abroad (bulletin PDF itself returned 404; fee/remote details not verified directly). (4) Application is paper, mailed: 'Signatures on the Attestation of Applicant must be original. Copies are not accepted.' No notarisation required; name-change documents 'do not need to be notarized'. (5) No interview for the exam route (interview exists only for licensure by credentials/specialty). (6) CPR: must be a hands-on healthcare-provider course (Minn. R. 3100.0100 subp. 9a) – can be completed abroad with an AHA/Red Cross-type provider. (7) Renewal is online (mn.gov eLicense) – no in-person step.

## Diğer başlangıç şartları
Jurisprudence exam: mandatory (150A.06 subd. 1: 'The examination shall include an examination of the applicant's knowledge of the laws of Minnesota relating to dentistry and the rules of the board'), passed within 5 years before application; PSI-administered, open book, ~$85 (snippet). CPR: 'documentation of current CPR certification' (3100.1100 subp. 1 H); defined in Minn. R. 3100.0100 subp. 9a as 'a course and certification for a health care provider that teaches basic life support for adults, children, and infants. The course must include both hands-on practice and written examination' covering assessment, FBAO, compressions/ventilations, AED, multi-rescuer; form: 'Include a copy of your current CPR certification for healthcare providers.' Criminal background check: state (BCA) + FBI fingerprint check under 214.075; results valid 1 year; $35 fee. INBDE: 'all parts of a national board examination' within 5 years. Exams: 'Exams may not be more than 5 years old' (150A.06 subd. 2b). Good moral character; disclosure questions (discipline, criminal, HPSP health/substance). Government-issued ID copy. Official e-transcript emailed by the school directly to dental.board@state.mn.us. No English test for the exam route (only 'at board discretion' for credentials/specialty). No liability insurance requirement. Email address required. Processing: two-person review, then queue in date-stamp order once CBC results arrive; no published processing time.

## Lisansı sürdürme

- **renewal_period:** Biennial. Initial licence runs from issue date to the last day of the licensee's birth month in the next even-numbered year (even birth year) or odd-numbered year (odd birth year); thereafter 24-month terms (Minn. R. 3100.1700 subp. 1a-1b). Must renew within 30 days after expiration or the licence is administratively terminated (subp. 3 B-C; termination is 'not ... a disciplinary action').
- **renewal_fee:** Set by the Board 'not to exceed' $475 for a dentist per biennium (Minn. Stat. 150A.091 subd. 5); late fee 25% of the biennial fee (subd. 7). The exact current Board-set amount could not be confirmed because the Board's renewal web pages are CAPTCHA-blocked. Renewal is online via mn.gov eLicense.
- **ce_hours:** 50 hours of 'professional development' per biennial cycle: at least 30 fundamental and no more than 20 elective (Minn. R. 3100.5100 subp. 2 B). Fundamental must include a mandatory infection-control course and at least two courses from: ethics, treatment planning, patient communications, management of medical emergencies, treatment and diagnosis, HIPAA, opioids and pain management. Licensee keeps a portfolio; random Board audits (3100.5300); failed audit penalties up to $1,000 (150A.091 subd. 16). Excess hours do not carry forward.
- **ce_online_ok:** Yes, in practice 100% online appears possible. Fundamental clinical subjects 'are covered through seminars, webinars, symposiums, lectures, college courses ... or programs' (3100.5100 subp. 3 A(1)); the core courses may be 'offered through seminars, webinars, symposiums, lectures, or programs' (A(2)); elective activities include 'professional reading of published articles or other forms of self-study directly relating to dentistry' (B(3)). No rule caps online/webinar hours or requires any live/in-person component. (Self-study/reading is capped only indirectly by the 20-hour elective maximum; webinars count as fundamental.)
- **cpr_required:** Yes. At each biennial renewal 'the application must require the licensee ... to certify compliance with maintaining a consecutive and current CPR certification' (3100.1700 subp. 2); failure 'subjects the licensee ... to disciplinary proceedings'. CPR is defined as a healthcare-provider BLS course that 'must include both hands-on practice and written examination' (3100.0100 subp. 9a), so a hands-on skills session is required (every ~2 years for AHA-type cards). Can be done abroad with an AHA/Red Cross-type international training centre; no Minnesota-specific provider list in rule.
- **practice_hours_required:** None for renewal of an active general dentist licence (3100.1700 contains no currency requirement). 2,000 hours/36 months applies only to licensure by credentials (150A.06 subd. 4) and specialty licences.
- **address_or_residency_required:** None. Renewal rules impose no Minnesota address, residency or practice-location requirement; the application only asks for a mailing address and a practice address 'if employed'.
- **insurance_required:** No mandatory professional liability insurance found in Minn. Stat. ch. 150A or Minn. R. ch. 3100.
- **inactive_status:** Minnesota has no ordinary reversible 'inactive' licence. Options: (a) Emeritus INACTIVE licence (150A.06 subd. 10; 3100.1340): one-time $50 fee, no renewal, no CE, but 'is not a license to practice' and 'Once an emeritus inactive license is issued by the board, the licensee cannot seek reinstatement of the licensee's prior license' – i.e. permanent retirement; (b) Emeritus ACTIVE licence (subd. 11; 3100.1350): $212 biennial, 25 CE hours (15 fundamental incl. infection control), limited to pro bono/volunteer practice, up to 500 hrs/yr paid supervision and 500 hrs/yr paid consulting; if terminated, 'the licensee cannot seek reinstatement of that license or of the licensee's prior license'; both emeritus categories are for a person who 'retires from active practice'. (c) Simply not renewing: licence is administratively terminated (non-disciplinary) and can later be reinstated under 3100.1850 with no time limit stated. (d) CE waiver for retired volunteers (150A.06 subd. 2d) – only for retired in-state volunteer practice, 5 CE hours + CPR.
- **reactivation:** Reinstatement of a terminated (non-renewed or voluntarily terminated) licence, Minn. R. 3100.1850: pay biennial renewal fee + $140 reinstatement fee (150A.091 subd. 10) and: terminated ≤6 months – 50 CE hours within prior 24 months + current CPR; >6 to <24 months – CE + CPR + jurisprudence exam within 12 months + CBC if terminated >1 year; ≥24 months – CE + CPR + jurisprudence exam within 12 months + new CBC + 'evidence of passing ... within 24 months ... a nationally recognized objective structured clinical examination for general dentists' (an OSCE, NOT a hands-on exam). Practising on a lapsed licence incurs penalties $250-$1,000 (150A.091 subd. 21). Emeritus licences cannot be converted back. A new fingerprint CBC is also required for 'reinstatement or relicensure ... if more than one year has elapsed' (214.075 subd. 1(a)(2)).
- **remote_maintainability:** moderate
- **details:** An active Minnesota licence can be kept active indefinitely from abroad: biennial online renewal, 50 CE hours obtainable entirely via webinars/self-study, no practice hours, no in-state address, no insurance. The awkward items are (i) a hands-on CPR/BLS course every cycle (rule-defined hands-on component) and (ii) the absence of a cheap reversible inactive status – the emeritus categories are irreversible retirement statuses, so the only 'pause' is letting the licence terminate and reinstating later (after 24+ months that requires a nationally recognised OSCE plus jurisprudence exam, CE, CPR and a fresh fingerprint CBC). Portfolio audits are random and document-based (can be handled by email/mail).
