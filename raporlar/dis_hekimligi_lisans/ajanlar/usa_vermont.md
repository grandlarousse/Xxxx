# Vermont (USA) — ajan bulgusu

Düzenleyici: Vermont Board of Dental Examiners, administered by the Vermont Secretary of State, Office of Professional Regulation (OPR) — https://sos.vermont.gov/dental-examiners

**Teori-only rota:** no · **Göçmenlik engeli:** low · **Uzaktan sürdürme:** moderate · **Güven:** high

## Sınav yolları

- **Licensure by examination: INBDE + hands-on regional clinical exam (ADEX / CRDTS / SRTA)** (el becerisi: evet): CVR 04-030-080 Rule 4-2 (eff. 15 Oct 2025): CODA/CDAC degree + pass NBDE I&II, INBDE, or the NDEB (Canada) written exam + 'passed every required part of at least 1 of the following clinical examinations: (A) ADEX administered by CDCA or CITA, not including the optional periodontal exam; (B) CRDTS; (C) SRTA; (D) WREB, if examined prior to July 1, 2020'. All of these are manikin/patient hands-on exams sat at US testing sites. ADA Vermont page (updated 20 Oct 2026) lists accepted exams as 'ADEX (CDCA-WREB-CITA), CRDTS, SRTA, PGY-1'.
- **PGY-1 residency in lieu of clinical exam** (el becerisi: evet): Rule 4-2(c)(2): 'completed at least 1 year of graduate dental training in a CODA-accredited clinical training program, provided that the applicant's supervising dentist attests to the applicant's competency in all areas tested on the ADEX.' Replaces the clinical exam but is itself hands-on clinical training requiring a US visa/residency position; candidate has ruled this out.
- **ADA-recognized specialty certifying board examination** (el becerisi: evet): Rule 4-2(c)(1)(F): 'the certifying examinations of a clinical dental specialty certifying board recognized by the ADA' satisfy the clinical-exam requirement. Not available to a new general-dentistry graduate (requires completed specialty residency and board certification).
- **'Other' Board-pre-approved regional/national clinical exam (theoretical DLOSCE slot) - NOT currently approved** (el becerisi: hayır): Rule 4-2(c)(1)(E): 'another regional or national clinical examination approved by the Board before the examination is taken'. The DLOSCE is not named anywhere in the Vermont statute or rules, Vermont is not on the JCNDE/ADA list of DLOSCE-accepting states, and no Board approval of DLOSCE was found (July 2026 Board minutes contain no exam-policy item). Because approval must precede the exam and standalone DLOSCE sittings ended 9 Oct 2026, this slot is not a usable theory-only route today.
- **Licensure by endorsement / fast-track (not for new graduates)** (el becerisi: hayır): 26 V.S.A. § 602 and Rule 4-3: (a) equivalency endorsement from a US/Canadian jurisdiction with substantially equivalent requirements (CODA/CDAC graduate; if licensed >2 yrs must show active practice per Rule 10-4(a)); (b) fast-track endorsement: any US licence + 3 years practice in good standing; (c) Canadian licence + 5 years practice at >=1,200 h/yr. Requires an existing licence obtained elsewhere; the underlying licence would itself have needed a clinical exam or NDEB certification, so this is only an indirect route (e.g. after 5 years of Canadian practice).

## Teori-only açıklaması
Vermont requires a hands-on clinical examination or a PGY-1. CVR 04-030-080 Rule 4-2 (Administrative Rules of the Board of Dental Examiners, 15 Oct 2025): 'To qualify by examination, an applicant must: ... (b) have passed: (1) the National Board Dental Examination (NBDE) Parts I and II; (2) the Integrated National Board Dental Examination (INDBE); or (3) the written examination of the National Dental Examining Board of Canada; and (c) have either: (1) passed every required part of at least 1 of the following clinical examinations: (A) the American Dental Licensing Examination (ADEX), administered by CDCA or CITA, not including the optional periodontal exam; (B) CRDTS; (C) SRTA; (D) WREB, if examined prior to July 1, 2020; (E) another regional or national clinical examination approved by the Board before the examination is taken; or (F) the certifying examinations of a clinical dental specialty certifying board recognized by the ADA; or (2) completed at least 1 year of graduate dental training in a CODA-accredited clinical training program, provided that the applicant's supervising dentist attests to the applicant's competency in all areas tested on the ADEX.' The enabling statute 26 V.S.A. § 601(3) requires applicants to 'meet the certificate, examination, and training requirements established by the Board by rule.' The NDEB written exam is accepted only as the substitute for INBDE (the written component); it does not replace the clinical exam, and the NDEB Virtual OSCE is not mentioned. No computer-based OSCE (DLOSCE or NDEB OSCE) is listed; sub-paragraph (E) would require a prior Board approval that does not exist. Therefore a new graduate cannot be licensed with computer-based exams only.

## DLOSCE notları
The DLOSCE is not mentioned in 26 V.S.A. ch. 12, in the 15 Oct 2025 Board rules, or in the March 2026 Dentist Application Instructions (which ask for 'Regional Board Scores or documentation of a completed clinical PGY1'). Vermont is not among the DLOSCE-accepting states in the ADA/JCNDE 2026 Candidate Guide. Rule 4-2(c)(1)(E) would allow 'another regional or national clinical examination approved by the Board before the examination is taken', so acceptance would need an affirmative Board decision made before the candidate sits the exam; no such decision was found in the posted Board minutes (most recent: 8 July 2026 meeting). Consequences: (1) an earlier standalone DLOSCE score cannot be used in Vermont (no prior Board approval); (2) a DLOSCE taken as the integrated component of the ADEX exam counts only as part of ADEX - Rule 4-2(c)(1)(A) requires 'every required part' of ADEX, so the manikin/hands-on ADEX components must also be passed; (3) the Board rules permit no waiver: Rule 3-6 says the Board 'will not grant routine waivers or variances from any provisions of its rules without amending the rules'.

PGY-1 kabul: True

## Yüz yüze adımlar
The Vermont Board/OPR process itself has no physical-presence step: (1) application is filed through OPR's Online Services System and the $285 fee paid online (Rule 3-1: 'Applications for licensure must be made through an online licensing system'); (2) official transcripts/Verification of Dental Education must be sent directly by the school 'by US mail or email'; (3) INBDE score report, EOP and CPR certificates are uploaded as scans; (4) the Vermont Dentist Jurisprudence Examination is an OPEN-BOOK, 30-question, multiple-choice take-home exam ('This is an open book examination... upload only the answer sheet to your online application... Six or more answers incorrect is a failing score') - no proctoring, no visit; (5) no fingerprinting or criminal background check for dentists (not listed in 3 V.S.A. § 123(j)); (6) no interview, no notarisation requirement found in the instructions or rules; (7) OPR offers walk-in service in Montpelier but it is optional. The only steps that require physical presence are external: the hands-on clinical exam (ADEX via CDCA-WREB-CITA, CRDTS or SRTA) must be sat at a US exam site (a short visitor-visa trip), and the CPR course must include a hands-on skills examination (Rule 1-9(a): 'provided by a DANB-approved CPR provider and requires a hands-on examination') - this can be done abroad through an international AHA/Red Cross training centre. Processing time 3-5 business days once complete; applications remain valid 6 months (Rule 3-1(d)).

## Diğer başlangıç şartları
Rule 4-1: age 18+; 'completed training in emergency office procedures and CPR within the previous 24 months' (EOP course = Board-approved course of at least 2 hours on medical emergencies in dental care, Rule 1-14; CPR = DANB-approved provider with hands-on examination, Rule 1-9); 'pass the Vermont Statutes and Rules Exam for dentists' (open-book jurisprudence exam, answer sheet uploaded, pass = no more than 5 wrong of 30; version May 2026); qualify by examination (INBDE + hands-on clinical exam or PGY-1). Education: DDS/DMD from a CODA- or CDAC-accredited school (instructions: 'You must hold a D.D.S or D.M.D degree from a CODA or CDAC accredited school'). No English-language test, no criminal background check, no liability-insurance proof, no residency. DEA number only 'if applicable'. 90-day provisional licence exists only for endorsement applicants awaiting licence verification. Timeline: OPR processing 3-5 business days after a complete file; application valid 6 months; licence expiry fixed at 30 September of odd years, with OPR's 90-day policy (an initial licence issued within 90 days before the renewal date runs through the next full 2-year period).

## İlk ücretler
Application fee $285.00 (26 V.S.A. § 662(a)(1)(A); OPR fee chart updated 7/1/2025), non-refundable; no separate licence-issuance fee. Jurisprudence exam: no fee (included). Clinical exam fees (ADEX/CRDTS/SRTA) and INBDE fees are paid to the testing agencies separately. Pro bono-only licence available at $0 (§ 662(b), Rule 3-7). Biennial renewal $655.00.

## Lisansı sürdürme

- **renewal_period:** Biennial, fixed cycle: all dental licences expire 30 September of odd-numbered years (OPR fee/dates chart; Rule 10-1 'Licenses are valid for fixed 2-year periods'). Renewal opens about 6 weeks before expiry, online only (Rule 10-2). First renewal after initial licensure requires no CE reporting (26 V.S.A. § 661(b)).
- **renewal_fee:** $655.00 per biennium (26 V.S.A. § 662(a)(2)(A)). Late/reinstatement penalty (3 V.S.A. § 127(d)): $100 if within 30 days of expiry; otherwise renewal fee plus $40 for every additional month or part-month, capped at $1,500.
- **ce_hours:** 30 hours per 2-year cycle (26 V.S.A. § 661(d)(1); Rule 10-4(a)(1)), which must include the emergency office procedures course (2 h minimum), the CPR course, and 2 credit hours of opioid prescribing 'where applicable' (i.e. if prescribing). Max 2 h each creditable for BLS/CPR and EOP. Must also complete the mandatory healthcare workforce census in the renewal application. Pre-approved providers: ADA, AGD PACE, CODA, DANB etc. (Rule 10-6). Retain records for 2 cycles (audit, Rule 10-5).
- **ce_online_ok:** Yes for all 30 hours in principle. Rule 10-9 accepts: '(b) distance education with continuous 2-way communication and observation between a qualified instructor and students; (c) distance education with asynchronous exchanges between a qualified instructor and students; or (d) a self-study course, if completion is contingent upon examination.' No in-person requirement; OPR site also notes synchronous virtual courses count as live/in-person where rules demand it (H.305, 2023). The single hands-on element is the CPR course (see cpr_required).
- **cpr_required:** Yes, every renewal cycle (Rule 10-4(a)(1) 'and the CPR course'). Rule 1-9 defines it as 'a program of education in cardiopulmonary resuscitation that is provided by a DANB-approved CPR provider and requires a hands-on examination' - so a skills session is required; purely online CPR does not qualify. Can be completed abroad through an international AHA/Red Cross/ERC-recognised DANB-approved provider.
- **practice_hours_required:** Yes, but substitutable with CE. Rule 10-4(a)(2): 'Active practice of at least 800 hours or 100 continuing education credits within the previous 5 years. A combination of practice hours and continuing education credits may be used, with 1 hour of continuing education equating to 8 hours of active practice.' 'Active practice' (Rule 1-2) means 'engaging in licensed activities, while lawfully authorized to do so in the jurisdiction of practice' - so practice in Turkey counts. 26 V.S.A. § 661(d)(2): a dentist not in active practice for 5+ years must meet Board renewal requirements; Rule 10-4(e): one who 'has not met minimum practice-hour requirements may be required to complete one of the clinical examinations required for initial licensure.' A fully-abroad licensee can satisfy this with 100 CE credits per rolling 5 years (about 20/yr), all online.
- **address_or_residency_required:** None. No in-state address or residency requirement in statute or rules; licensee must keep contact details current (Rule 11-1 duty to update; OPR change-of-address service). Renewal reminders go to the email on file.
- **insurance_required:** No. No professional liability insurance requirement found in 26 V.S.A. ch. 12 or the Board rules.
- **inactive_status:** No formal inactive, retired or non-practising category exists for Vermont dentists. Rule 10-1: 'A license becomes inactive if not renewed by midnight on the date of expiry' - i.e. 'inactive' simply means lapsed, with no reduced fee or CE waiver. The only reduced-fee variant is the $0 pro bono-restricted licence (§ 662(b), Rule 3-7) for practice limited to approved free clinics, which does not suit someone abroad.
- **reactivation:** Lapse of not more than one biennial period: reinstate 'by meeting renewal requirements for the profession, paying the profession's renewal fee, and paying the... nondisciplinary reinstatement penalty' ($100 within 30 days; otherwise renewal fee + $40/month, max $1,500) (3 V.S.A. § 127(d)(1)); penalty waiver only for 'exceptional circumstances or extreme hardship'. Lapse of more than one biennial period: neither the statute nor the dental rules spell out the path (open question; OPR practice generally requires a reinstatement application through the online system meeting current renewal requirements). Substantively, reactivation turns on the currency rule: 800 practice hours or 100 CE credits in the previous 5 years; if not met, the Board 'may' require one of the hands-on clinical exams (Rule 10-4(e)); § 661(d)(2) flags 5+ years out of active practice. Keeping CE at 100 credits/5 years avoids this.
- **remote_maintainability:** moderate
- **details:** Vermont can realistically be kept ACTIVE from abroad indefinitely: all 30 CE hours may be distance/self-study, there is no residency, address, insurance or compact requirement, renewal is online, fee $655 per 2 years. The two awkward elements are (1) the CPR course each cycle must include a hands-on skills examination (available abroad via DANB-approved international providers), and (2) the rolling 5-year currency rule of 800 practice hours or 100 CE credits (foreign practice counts as 'active practice'; otherwise about 20 CE credits per year, all online, substitutes). There is no cheap inactive status, so the full $655 renewal must be paid every cycle to avoid lapse; a lapse beyond one biennium has no defined reinstatement path and risks a hands-on clinical exam being required.
