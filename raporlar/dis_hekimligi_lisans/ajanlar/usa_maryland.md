## Sınav yolları

- **Licensure by examination: ADEX (ADLEX) full series via CDCA-WREB-CITA + INBDE + Maryland Law (jurisprudence) exam** (el becerisi: evet): The only initial-licensure route for a new graduate. ADA Licensure-by-State page (updated 9 Oct 2026): 'Completion of all sections of ADEX Examinations required. Maryland accepts the all-manikin or live patient exam for dentists.' COMAR 10.44.15.05: 'Applicants may not be licensed until they have successfully passed: A. All required sections of the ADLEX or ADHLEX examination; and B. The national board examinations.' Health Occ. §4-306(a)(4): 'ADLEX ... includes a written component and a clinical component.' Plus COMAR 10.44.15.06 written Maryland law/ethics/jurisprudence examination (score >=75 on each of two parts). Max 4 failures (§4-305(e)). Degree must be DDS/DMD or equivalent from a school authorized by a US state or Canadian province and recognized by the Board (COMAR 10.44.15.03) - Yeditepe is CODA-accredited but is NOT located in a US state/Canadian province; Board recognition of a CODA-accredited foreign school is unverified.
- **Licensure by credentials / endorsement (§4-306) - only for dentists already licensed in another US state** (el becerisi: evet): §4-306(b)(1): a dentist licensed in another state is issued a Maryland license if (i) has passed the ADLEX and the Maryland Dental Jurisprudence Exam; or (ii) for the 5 preceding years has actively practised >=850 hours/yr on average, has passed an examination with a clinical component for licensure in another state, has passed ADEX's comprehensive written exam on applied clinical diagnosis and treatment planning, and passed the Maryland jurisprudence exam. Both branches presuppose a prior hands-on clinical exam; not available to a new graduate.
- **DLOSCE-only / OSCE-only** (el becerisi: hayır): NOT accepted. Maryland statute/COMAR make no reference to the DLOSCE; Maryland is not on the JCNDE list of DLOSCE-accepting states; COMAR 10.44.15.05 requires 'all required sections' of the ADLEX (manikin components included). A pass of the ADEX-integrated DLOSCE component alone would not satisfy the rule.
- **PGY-1 residency in lieu of clinical exam** (el becerisi: hayır): NOT provided for in Health Occ. Title 4 Subtitle 3 or COMAR 10.44.15; no residency/portfolio alternative exists in Maryland.
- **Limited / teacher's license (§4-303, §4-303.1, §4-309)** (el becerisi: hayır): Restricted licences tied to employment at a Maryland institution (limited licence expires on first anniversary; teacher's licence ends when faculty status ends). Not a full general licence and requires a Maryland employer - not suitable for the candidate.

## Teori-only açıklaması
COMAR 10.44.15.05 'Completion of All Sections of Examinations Required. Applicants may not be licensed until they have successfully passed: A. All required sections of the ADLEX or ADHLEX examination; and B. The national board examinations.' Health Occ. §4-306(a)(4)(ii): 'ADLEX includes a written component and a clinical component.' ADA summary (updated 9 Oct 2026): 'Completion of all sections of ADEX Examinations required. Maryland accepts the all-manikin or live patient exam for dentists.' No DLOSCE-only, residency, portfolio or written-only alternative exists in statute or regulation; the credentials route (§4-306) also requires a prior exam 'with a clinical component'. Additionally COMAR 10.44.15.07 lets the Board require the ADLEX clinical sections again if the applicant 'may have lost clinical skills because of an extended absence from clinical practice.'

## DLOSCE notları
Maryland rules never mention the DLOSCE or any OSCE. Licensure requires passing 'all required sections' of the ADLEX (ADEX) plus the national boards (COMAR 10.44.15.05). Since Aug 2026 the DLOSCE is a component of the ADEX series; Maryland will see it only as one of the ADEX sections, all of which (including the manikin hand-skill sections) must be passed. Neither earlier standalone DLOSCE scores nor an ADEX-reported DLOSCE pass alone has any standing under Maryland rules; the statute's 'or its successor examination' wording (§4-306(a)(4)) refers to the ADLEX as a whole.

## Yüz yüze adımlar
(1) ADEX/ADLEX clinical examination: in person at a US test site (manikin hand-skill sections) - unavoidable for initial licensure. (2) Notarised statement with the application photograph: COMAR 10.44.15.09(B) requires 'a recent photograph ... accompanied by a notarized statement stating that the photograph is a true photograph of me' - notarisation can presumably be done at a US notary during a visit or at a US consulate abroad (acceptance of foreign notaries unverified). (3) Maryland Law / jurisprudence examination (COMAR 10.44.15.06): a 'written examination on laws, regulations, and ethics given by the Board or its designee'; delivery format (take-home/online vs. proctored) not verified - a University of Maryland clinic-manual reference suggests the exam is enclosed with the application (i.e. take-home), but this is unofficial. (4) Criminal history records check: Maryland normally requires fingerprint-based State/FBI CHRC via Maryland CJIS; whether mailed fingerprint cards are accepted for applicants abroad was not verified. (5) No interview requirement found.

## Lisansı sürdürme

- **renewal_period:** Up to 2 years (Health Occ. §4-309(a)(1): 'A license may not be renewed for a term longer than 2 years'); dental licences renew on a biennial cycle with a 30-day late-renewal window (COMAR 10.44.10.02E) - a dentist 'who has failed to renew ... by July 31 of the year in which the license is due for renewal' may not practise (10.44.10.02F), implying a 30 June expiry.
- **renewal_fee:** Set in COMAR 10.44.20; exact amount not verified. Late renewal fee and reinstatement fee also apply per COMAR 10.44.20.
- **ce_hours:** 30 hours per renewal cycle (COMAR 10.44.22.04B) including 2 hours infection control; a 2-hour Board-approved Maryland abuse-and-neglect course every other renewal cycle; a 2-hour Board-approved course on proper prescribing/disposal of prescription drugs (dentists); all count within the 30 hours. Licensee verifies compliance on the renewal form (10.44.22.03B).
- **ce_online_ok:** Unclear. COMAR 10.44.22 requires courses from Board-approved sponsors (10.44.22.08) and the ADA summary excerpt showed no explicit cap on online/self-study hours, but the full regulation text on distance-learning limits was not read. CPR must be a current certification (AHA BLS for Healthcare Providers, ARC CPR for Professional Rescuers or Board-approved equivalent), which in practice involves an in-person skills session.
- **cpr_required:** Yes - COMAR 10.44.22.04A: licensee 'shall maintain cardiopulmonary resuscitation certification' from AHA BLS for Healthcare Providers, American Red Cross CPR for Professional Rescuers, or an equivalent Board-approved program. AHA/ARC certification requires a hands-on skills check (blended learning allowed), so a periodic in-person component is needed (obtainable at AHA/ARC international training sites).
- **practice_hours_required:** None for renewal of an ACTIVE licence (renewal requires only entitlement to be licensed, renewal fee, renewal form and CE/CPR). BUT: COMAR 10.44.15.07 allows the Board to require the ADLEX clinical sections of an applicant who 'may have lost clinical skills because of an extended absence from clinical practice', and reactivation from INACTIVE status requires either active practice within the preceding 36 months (notarised affidavit) or passing the ADEX exam (2019 reactivation form).
- **address_or_residency_required:** No Maryland address or residency requirement found; Board sends renewal notices to the 'last known electronic or physical address' (§4-309(c)).
- **insurance_required:** No mandatory professional liability insurance requirement found in statute/COMAR read.
- **inactive_status:** Inactive status exists (Board has an 'Application for Reactivation of an Inactive Dental or Dental Hygiene License'); fee, CE and maximum duration of inactive status not verified (statute page fetch failed). Reactivation fee for dentists $265 (2019 form).
- **reactivation:** Per the Board's reactivation form: (a) 30 hours CE incl. 2 hrs infection control, 2-hr abuse/neglect course, 2-hr prescribing course and current CPR completed 'during the two years prior to this reactivation'; AND (b) either 'actively practiced dentistry ... within the 36 months preceding application for reactivation' (notarised affidavit) OR, if not, 'passed the American Board of Dental Examiners (ADEX) ... examination in dentistry' with a certified exam report. So a dentist who has not practised anywhere for >36 months must re-take the hands-on ADEX exam to reactivate. Lapsed (non-renewed) licence: late renewal within 30 days with late fee; afterwards reinstatement on meeting renewal/reinstatement requirements plus reinstatement fee (COMAR 10.44.10.02E-F); practising on an expired licence is sanctionable.
- **remote_maintainability:** moderate
- **details:** An active Maryland licence can in principle be kept active from abroad by paying the biennial fee, completing 30 CE hours (approved sponsors; online acceptability not fully verified) and holding a current AHA/ARC CPR certification (hands-on skills component). There are no practice-hour, address, residency or insurance requirements for renewal. The weak points: (1) CPR needs periodic in-person skills sessions; (2) if the licence ever goes inactive or lapses and the dentist has not practised within 36 months, reactivation requires re-passing the hands-on ADEX exam; (3) the Board may invoke COMAR 10.44.15.07 (loss of clinical skills) for extended absence from practice. Exact renewal/inactive fees and the online-CE cap are unverified.
