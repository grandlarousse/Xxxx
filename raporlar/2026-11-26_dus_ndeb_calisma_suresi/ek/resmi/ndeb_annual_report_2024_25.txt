    About us
    We are a not-for-profit corporation established through an Act of Parliament.
    Our responsibility is to establish the national standard for beginning dental
    practitioners in Canada.

    We develop, administer, and evaluate fair, valid and reliable assessments that
    determine an individual’s readiness for NDEB certification.

    These examinations are a key component of the entry-to-practice
    pathway for both Canadian and international graduates. Provincial Dental
    Regulatory Authorities use NDEB certification to confirm that applicants have
    demonstrated the competencies required for a beginning dental practitioner.

    Our organization also specializes in the verification of educational and
    professional documentation confirming that individuals pursing certification
    and licensure in Canada hold the necessary qualifications to practice
    dentistry.

    By rigorously confirming both the legitimacy of credentials and the
    competencies of the practitioner, we are contributing to the oral health of
    Canadians and maintaining public trust.

        Sharing information
        to facilitate movement
        With our existing document requirements and
        credential verification processes, we are well
        positioned to develop a system that would house
        documentation accessible to other parties. Similar
        projects have been undertaken by other health
        professions to facilitate access to educational
        documents and credentials that individuals need to
        provide at multiple points during their licensure journey.

        Last year we engaged an external consultant,
        Dre Diane Legault, to spearhead the requirements
        gathering for a national repository of credentials.
        Dre Legault’s experience in regulation and law made
        her an ideal choice for this project. Over the year, she
        met with regulators across the country to learn about
        their requirements for issuing a license and spoke with
        organizations that have undertaken similar initiatives
        to discuss accomplishments and lessons learned.

        The final report provided an in-depth view of provincial
        needs and includes several recommendations for next
        steps. The report will serve as a jumping off point for
        further discussions with partners on the development
        of a national repository for credentials.

     standard of competence

     The Role of Examinations in Determining Competence
     With a lack of homogeneity in dental education around the world, we rely on a series of
     examinations to determine if an individual pursuing licensure has the knowledge, skills and
     abilities of a beginning dental practitioner in Canada. Unlike in accredited dental education
     programs where an independent body evaluates the programs against defined standards for
     the purposes of quality assurance and continuous enhancement the breadth and depth of
     dental programs in many parts of the world are unknown. This is where examinations come in.

     Examinations serve as an important mechanism for objectively determining an individual’s
     competence in relation to established standards. Through fair, valid and reliable examinations
     we can uphold the integrity of credentials, support informed decision-making, and maintain
     public confidence.



           At the NDEB, we support the integration of dentists trained outside of Canada
           into the profession through examination processes that assess the competencies
           required of Canadian dentists before they enter independent practice.
