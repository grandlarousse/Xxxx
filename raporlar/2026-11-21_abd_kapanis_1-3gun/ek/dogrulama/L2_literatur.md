I checked all 20 rows against the cited sources, mostly the PDF or the abstract page. Result: 17 confirmed, 3 corrected, none wrong and none left unverifiable. The corrections that affect the report: (1) R7-03: the Boyarchenko-Larsen-Whelan sample runs 1998–2020, not 1998–2019. (2) R7-09: the 16/17 bp drop in Harvey-Mazzoleni-Melone is the effect of a one-standard-deviation rise in the rebalancing signal on next-day S&P futures returns. It is not a fixed effect of stocks simply being overweight. (3) R1-17: in May 2018 Cheng says expected profits on shorting VIX futures had only started climbing back and were still low by historical standards, not fully recovered. Points to note: the overnight drift is reported near zero since 2021 (Liberty Street Economics, July 2026). Valcarcel et al. say out-of-sample forecasting ability is modest. The SqueezeMetrics GEX and DIX notes are self-published and not peer reviewed. R7-26 rests only on a secondary source, and the quote in the ledger does not appear on the page, so I replaced it. The QuantConnect post is still a draft. The 'VIX/100' sizing in R1-21 was not seen in its exact form.
