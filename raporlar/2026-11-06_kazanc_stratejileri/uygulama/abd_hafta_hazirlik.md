**Pine Screener betiği (TradingView > Products > Screeners > Pine; göstergeyi favorilere ekleyip "plot" değerlerine filtre uygulayın):**

```pine
//@version=6
indicator("TT_RS_EP_Screen", overlay=false)
spx      = request.security("SP:SPX", "D", close)
s50      = ta.sma(close, 50)
s150     = ta.sma(close, 150)
s200     = ta.sma(close, 200)
hi52     = ta.highest(high, 252)
lo52     = ta.lowest(low, 252)
rsLine   = close / spx
rsRaw    = 0.4*ta.roc(close,63) + 0.2*ta.roc(close,126) + 0.2*ta.roc(close,189) + 0.2*ta.roc(close,252)
tt       = close > s50 and s50 > s150 and s150 > s200 and s200 > s200[21] and close >= 1.3*lo52 and close >= 0.75*hi52
dv50M    = ta.sma(close*volume, 50) / 1e6
gapPct   = (open / close[1] - 1) * 100
rvol     = volume / ta.sma(volume, 50)[1]
plot(tt ? 1 : 0,                     "TT (1=ok)")
plot(rsRaw,                          "RS raw (IBD-weighted ROC)")
plot(rsLine / ta.highest(rsLine,252),"RS line / 52w max")
plot((close/hi52 - 1)*100,           "Dist to 52wH %")
plot(dv50M,                          "50d $Vol (M)")
plot(gapPct,                         "Gap %")
plot(rvol,                           "RVOL vs 50d")
```
